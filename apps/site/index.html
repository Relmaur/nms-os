Inside ATLAS

Close the tabs. Start the run.

No more juggling the wiki, Reddit threads, spreadsheets and screenshots of slot layouts. Everything ATLAS shows comes from your own log or a cited wiki page, so it never has to guess.

Privacy

Your log is yours.

No account.

Nothing to sign up for, no email to give. Your log lives on your device.

No ads. No tracking.

No analytics, no trackers, nothing loaded from anyone else's servers while you play.

Encrypted sync.

Between your devices if you want it, encrypted before it leaves, so the server can't read a thing.

Never touches the game.

ATLAS doesn't read your save. You log what you want to keep, so it works everywhere and can never break anything.

Install

Install it like an app.

ATLAS runs in your browser and installs from there. No app store, no download, about a minute.

IPHONE · IPAD

Safari

  1. Open ATLAS in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from your home screen. Alerts need it installed this way.
ANDROID

Chrome

  1. Open ATLAS in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app.
  3. Allow notifications so timers can ping you.
PC · MAC

Chrome or Edge

  1. Open ATLAS.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. It opens in its own window, next to the game.
