# Wiki mirror & Codex (`wiki/`)
The Codex is a verified mirror of the **game-data pages** of the No Man's Sky Wiki (`nomanssky.fandom.com`), nothing else: no hand-written or community-guide content is mixed in.
- **Scope:** main-namespace pages that use one of these infoboxes: Resource, Product, Technology, Technology NEXT, POI, Space Encounter, Mission, Milestone, Galaxy, Update (`SCOPE` in `packages/wiki/src/wikitext.ts`). Player discoveries (planets, creatures, bases, player-found ships/tools) have other infoboxes and are out of scope. Legacy Technology pages whose type is "provides N slots" are player-found multi-tools and are excluded.
- **Rendering:** every article is generated from the page's wikitext at a recorded revision (`revId`), not from rendered HTML: infobox → "Quick Facts", recipe templates (`Craft`, `CraftObsolete`, `PoC-Refine`, `Cook`, `PoC-Repair`, `PoC-Dismantle`) → recipe lines + structured `data.recipes`, links → `[[Target]]` / `[[Target|label]]`. Dynamic wiki queries (`UseQueries`, `Query Cooking`, navboxes) render as nothing; the Codex computes "Used in" from the recipes instead.
- **Matching:** wiki revisions are immutable, so archived `revId` == live `revId` means the copy matches the wiki's source exactly. `pnpm verify:wiki` is a dry-run sync: it rebuilds every article from the live wiki and reports anything that would change (exit 1 if any). `pnpm sync:wiki` applies it; a second sync writes nothing. Both write `wiki/verification.json`.
- **Schema guard:** API responses are parsed with Zod (`packages/wiki/src/api-schema.ts`) before anything is written; so is every article's frontmatter when the archive is read (`ArticleMetaSchema`). An unexpected shape stops the sync, names the page, writes nothing, and records `status: "invalid-response"` in `verification.json`; an unreachable wiki records `unreachable`. Either way the committed archive ships unchanged.
- **Flags, never silent deletes:** pages the wiki marks `{{Obsolete}}` or keeps for an older version ("Blaze Javelin (Atlas)") get `obsolete: true` and are hidden by default. A page that leaves the wiki or loses its game-data infobox keeps its last copy with `status: "removed"`.
- **Currency:** each page's `version` is the wiki's own `{{Version|…}}` stamp ("up to date as of the X update"). The mirror can claim to match the wiki; only this stamp speaks to whether the wiki matches the current game.
- Frontmatter: `title`, `category`, `kind`, `summary` (the in-game description when the page quotes it), `pageId`, `revId`, `revTimestamp`, `version`, `obsolete`, `status`, `source` (live URL), `license`, `data` (`{ fields, value?, recipes }`). Strings and objects are JSON, which is valid YAML.
- Folders come from the infobox (`categoryFor()`): resources, products, cooking, fish, building, technology, places, missions, galaxies, updates. Do not edit mirrored files by hand: the next sync overwrites them. Text is CC BY-SA 3.0 (No Man's Sky Wiki contributors).
- `pnpm index:wiki` rebuilds `index.json` (light: what the main app's header search shows), `offline-bundle.js` (bodies + recipes + aliases, for the Codex) and `game-data.json` (one record per wiki page with fields, value and recipes, the dataset for deterministic tools).
- Your own articles live in `wiki/custom/<category>/`, show as "Your articles", and are never part of the match.
- Codex page (`apps/web/wiki/index.html`, served at `wiki/`): verification panel, recently edited pages, faceted category/source/A–Z filters, "Include no longer in game" toggle (`old=1`), quick look (↑/↓, Space, Enter), and an article view with the wiki revision (linked to that exact revision), TOC, "Used in", outgoing links, "Mentioned in" and category neighbours. Below 1024px that index sits right under the article header as a bar of counts that opens it in a bottom sheet, and a strip under the header keeps it one tap away while reading; from 1024px it's the sticky right rail. The page is React (`apps/web/src/ui/codex/`, logic in `packages/core/src/codex.ts`); `index.json`, `offline-bundle.js`, `verification.json` and drafts are parsed with `packages/core/src/wiki-files.ts` before use. The main app's header search uses `index.json`, skips obsolete/removed pages, and deep-links into `wiki/#/a/<path>`.
