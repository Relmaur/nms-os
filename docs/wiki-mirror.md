# Wiki mirror & Codex (`wiki/`)
The Codex is a verified mirror of the **game-data pages** of the No Man's Sky Wiki (`nomanssky.fandom.com`), nothing else: no hand-written or community-guide content is mixed in.
- **Scope:** main-namespace pages that use one of these infoboxes: Resource, Product, Technology, Technology NEXT, POI, Space Encounter, Mission, Milestone, Galaxy, Update (`SCOPE` in `packages/wiki/src/wikitext.ts`). Player discoveries (planets, creatures, bases, player-found ships/tools) have other infoboxes and are out of scope. Legacy Technology pages whose type is "provides N slots" are player-found multi-tools and are excluded. Since 3.16.0, the game-mechanics pages named in `MECHANICS` (Inventory, Supercharged Slot, Starship, Multi-Tool, Exosuit, Exocraft, Freighter, Starship Outfitting Terminal, and Biome since the redesign) are mirrored by title too (kind `mechanic`, folder `mechanics/`), for the rules the cited facts quote. Since the dump import (2026-10-30), the scope also takes the game's own pages from infoboxes shared with players (General, Flora, Mineral; Starship and Multitool only for the pre-order bonuses in `GAME_SHIPS`), kept only when they fill no discovery or civilization field, plus pages with no infobox in the game categories of `CATEGORY_KINDS` (Mechanics, Lore, Biomes, Space Anomaly, the technology and element categories…). Community titles, categories and named pages are refused first. All of it is `gameKind()` in `packages/wiki/src/wikitext.ts`. The Biome page gives the planet marker its colour families: one cited fact per biome whose section says what it's like (`biome-*` in `packages/wiki/src/facts.ts`); a biome without one draws slate.
- **Rendering:** every article is generated from the page's wikitext at a recorded revision (`revId`), not from rendered HTML: infobox → "Quick Facts", recipe templates (`Craft`, `CraftObsolete`, `PoC-Refine`, `Cook`, `PoC-Repair`, `PoC-Dismantle`) → recipe lines + structured `data.recipes`, links → `[[Target]]` / `[[Target|label]]`. Dynamic wiki queries (`UseQueries`, `Query Cooking`, navboxes) render as nothing; the Codex computes "Used in" from the recipes instead.
- **Source: database dumps only (ADR-0009).** Fandom asked that tools wait for a new dump of the wiki (Special:Statistics) rather than call its API. Download it, keep it outside the repo (it's the whole wiki, community pages included), and run `pnpm sync:wiki --from-dump <file>` (`.xml` or `.xml.gz`; extract a `.7z` first). `packages/wiki/src/dump.ts` streams it page by page. Run without a dump, the sync refuses; nothing calls the wiki's API, and CI only indexes the committed archive. Commit the changed pages with `wiki/verification.json`.
- **Matching:** wiki revisions are immutable, so archived `revId` == the dump's `revId` means the copy matches the wiki's source as of the dump. `pnpm verify:wiki --from-dump <file>` is a dry-run sync: it rebuilds every article from the dump and reports anything that would change (exit 1 if any). `pnpm sync:wiki --from-dump <file>` applies it; a second run writes nothing. Both write `wiki/verification.json` with `source: "dump"`, the dump's file name and `dumpDate` (its newest revision), which the Codex shows as "Wiki copy from <date>".
- **Widening the scope:** `pnpm sync:wiki --discover <dump> --out <report.md>` lists every infobox (pages using it, samples, how many fill a discovery field) and every large category of pages without one, each with a proposed verdict: game, community or mixed. It writes nothing in `wiki/`; the scope widens only after the owner approves the list.
- **Hostile pages (3.15.0):**
  - A page over 256 KB of wikitext is skipped ("page too large").
  - Comments, refs and galleries are removed in one pass, and link and tag patterns exclude `[` and `<` from their insides, so a page of unclosed brackets costs linear time.
  - After entities are decoded, any `<` that doesn't open a bare `br`, `code`, `pre` or `blockquote` tag is escaped, so "<Crew name>" shows as written and no page can reach the Codex as markup.
  - The Markdown renderer (`apps/web/src/ui/parts/safe-html.ts`) escapes raw HTML the same way. A strict DOMPurify allowlist stays as the backstop.
- **Schema guard:** every page read from the dump is parsed with Zod (`DumpPageSchema` in `packages/wiki/src/dump.ts`) before anything is written; so is every article's frontmatter when the archive is read (`ArticleMetaSchema`). A malformed page, a dump of another wiki, or a file that isn't a dump stops the sync, names the problem, and writes nothing.
- **Flags, never silent deletes:** pages the wiki marks `{{Obsolete}}` or keeps for an older version ("Blaze Javelin (Atlas)") get `obsolete: true` and are hidden by default. A page that leaves the wiki or loses its game-data infobox keeps its last copy with `status: "removed"`.
- **Currency:** each page's `version` is the wiki's own `{{Version|…}}` stamp ("up to date as of the X update"). The mirror can claim to match the wiki; only this stamp speaks to whether the wiki matches the current game.
- Frontmatter: `title`, `category`, `kind`, `summary` (the in-game description when the page quotes it), `pageId`, `revId`, `revTimestamp`, `version`, `obsolete`, `status`, `source` (live URL), `license`, `data` (`{ fields, value?, recipes }`). Strings and objects are JSON, which is valid YAML.
- Folders come from the infobox (`categoryFor()`): resources, products, cooking, fish, building, technology, places, missions, galaxies, updates, mechanics, world (creature genera, flora, minerals, biomes), lore (story, races, NPCs, guilds, Sentinels). Do not edit mirrored files by hand: the next sync overwrites them. - `pnpm index:wiki` rebuilds `index.json` (light: what the main app's header search shows), `offline-bundle.js` (bodies + recipes + aliases, for the Codex) `game-data.json` (one record per wiki page with fields, value and recipes, the dataset for deterministic tools) `facts.json` (the cited facts whose quote is in the archive, and `game`: the current game version, from the newest Update page, the one with no next update) and `galaxies.json` (the 256 numbered galaxies, for the system editor's picker).
- **Cited facts (3.16.0, ADR-0003):** `packages/wiki/src/facts.ts` lists rules the wiki states in prose (supercharged slots per class, technology grid maximums, the Exosuit's fixed slots), each as `{ page, quote, value }`. Sync and verify check every quote against its page; a missing one is reported in `verification.json` as `staleFacts`, fails `verify:wiki`, and leaves the fact out of `facts.json`. To fix one, reread the page, update the quote (and the value if the rule changed), and rerun `pnpm index:wiki`. The unit test that checks every quote against the archive fails too, which stops a deploy of that archive until the quote is updated: a rule the wiki no longer states shouldn't ship silently. Values are typed by `packages/core/src/facts.ts`.
- **Converter fix (3.16.0):** a list item ending in bold (`'''Glass''' `) no longer runs into the next item; 135 articles re-rendered with no new revisions.
- Your own articles live in `wiki/custom/<category>/`, show as "Your articles", and are never part of the match.
- Codex page (`apps/web/wiki/index.html`, served at `wiki/`): verification panel, recently edited pages, faceted category/source/A–Z filters, "Include no longer in game" toggle (`old=1`), quick look (↑/↓, Space, Enter), and an article view with the wiki revision (linked to that exact revision), TOC, "Used in", outgoing links, "Mentioned in" and category neighbours. Below 1024px that index sits right under the article header as a bar of counts that opens it in a bottom sheet, and a strip under the header keeps it one tap away while reading; from 1024px it's the sticky right rail. The page is React (`apps/web/src/ui/codex/`, logic in `packages/core/src/codex.ts`); `index.json`, `offline-bundle.js`, `verification.json` and drafts are parsed with `packages/core/src/wiki-files.ts` before use. The main app's header search uses `index.json`, skips obsolete/removed pages, and deep-links into `wiki/#/a/<path>`.
