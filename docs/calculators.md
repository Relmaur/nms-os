# Calculators (`apps/web/src/ui/calculators/`, `packages/core/src/planner.ts`, `packages/core/src/calc.ts`)
- **Layout:** the recipe planner is its own screen, `#plan/<item>` (design 13, `Planner.tsx`), opened from Best crafts, from Calculators' planner card (the plan in progress: "×1 Stasis Device · 3/14 gathered") or by "Change item" on the planner. Calculators keeps a tool dock (ARIA tabs, `ToolDock.tsx`) for the small tools: Best crafts (the default), Base value, Extractors, Registers. Every tool stays mounted, so switching keeps what was typed; the chosen tool lasts while the page is open (`tool-store.ts`, not saved).
- Built only on the wiki mirror: `wiki/game-data.json` is fetched when the Calculators section first opens (precached by the service worker) and parsed with `GameDataSchema` (`packages/core/src/game-data.ts`) before use. Every number shown is wiki data, arithmetic on it, or an input the player sets; never a hand-typed game fact.
- **Recipe planner, one path in play order** (`buildPlan`, `planPath` in `packages/core/src/plan-path.ts`): expands crafting and cooking recipes (not refining, which is a way to obtain raw materials) down to items with no such recipe, and lays them out as the player works through them:
  - **Gather**: raw totals, one row each, ticked off as they land in the cargo;
  - **rounds** of crafts: round 1 needs only raw materials, round N needs something from round N−1; a round is marked crafted whole (`toggleRound`);
  - the **final craft**: the product. Above the path, its worth at base value against the raw inputs at base value, and the quantity.
  The first step not done is "now". Every item name opens the **Codex peek** (design 34, `CodexPeek.tsx`): the Source section of the item's mirrored article (`sourceLines` in `packages/core/src/item-source.ts`; prose kept as written, recipe lines keyed by machine), "Closest for you" (`closestFor`: logged planets or systems whose name, tags or notes name the item, nearest the system last visited, distances by the cited `region-light-years` fact), what the plan needs of it and at which step, the recipe switch for a crafted item, and Open in Codex. The article file is fetched when the peek opens and kept by the service worker; unread offline, the peek points to the Codex, whose bundle has every page.
  Each crafted item appears once, with its total demand batched once (`ceil(total / output per batch)`), as it is crafted in game; the old tree batched every branch separately. Self-multiplying recipes are skipped, cycles are cut where an item would need itself, and the player can cycle alternative recipes per item. Values are the wiki's base values.
- **Ticks** (`planProgress`): a step is ready when all its inputs are ticked, a round when all its steps are. Each tick names an amount (`raw:Radon@500`). After another quantity or recipe, `carryTicks` keeps it wherever what was gathered or crafted still covers the new need (500 Radon covers 300) and lets it lapse where the need grew; the old keys stay, so a tick comes back if the plan does. A different item starts fresh. Ticks are kept on this device only (`localStorage` key `atlas_plan_progress`, parsed with Zod), never in `nms_state.json` or exports.
- **Compare and check value:** each alternative-recipe switch (in the peek) shows the next recipe's inputs and how it changes the raw inputs' base value before you switch; the worth card states the product's worth against its raw inputs (×147.9), arithmetic on wiki base values only.
- **Best crafts** (3.18.0, `packages/core/src/profit.ts`, `BestCrafts.tsx`), the first recommendation: every current item with a wiki value and a crafting or cooking recipe, ranked by the value it gains over what its raw materials would sell for instead. It runs the planner's own `buildPlan` for one batch, values the raw totals at their wiki base values, and subtracts. Ranked by total gain or by gain per crafting step (which favours short chains), and filterable by category and length. An item with a raw material the wiki gives no value is left out and counted, never guessed. A blueprint badge marks a chain with a step that needs one. "Plan it" opens the planner for the item (`openPlan` in `plan-bits.ts`). The MCP server's `best_crafts` tool runs the same ranking.
- **Base value** of a haul, **mineral extractors** (the hourly rate is the player's input) and **Harmonic Camp registers** (`evaluateRegister`: plain arithmetic parsed by hand, never evaluated as code).
