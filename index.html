The Golden Rule of Cross-Play (Patch Parity):

You cannot open a save file saved on a newer game patch using a device running an older patch. Because Nintendo Switch and iOS App Store certifications occasionally take 2–5 extra days compared to Steam, always check that your version numbers match at the bottom-left of the title screen after a major update!

  • 3. Multiplayer Toggle (`Options → Network`) 100% Safe to toggle anytime! Flip Multiplayer OFF when claiming crashed S-Class ships or Multi-Tool cabinets so another player doesn't grab your spawn; flip it ON inside the Space Anomaly!

3. The "First 5 Hours" Optimal Roadmap

Troubleshooting: Locked Freighter Hangar Bays

After 3 cumulative hours and your 5th warp jump, you trigger a Freighter pirate rescue battle. If the Freighter bay doors stay red and locked after the fight:

  1. Zero Friendly Fire (#1 Culprit): If even a single stray bullet hits the friendly Freighter or its cargo pods during the dogfight, the Freighter flags you as hostile and locks its doors red! Hold fire when pirates cross the hull.
  2. Answer Your Ship Communicator (`X` / `Down D-Pad`): Right when the last pirate explodes, answer the Captain's radio call—the hangar doors stay red until he invites you aboard, turning them blue!
  3. Free Capital Ship Upgrade Trick: Your 1st battle is always a smaller System Freighter. Choose "Request Payment Instead" (or skip it)—every subsequent battle (3 hrs + 5 jumps) spawns a massive Capital Freighter that is still 100% FREE!

This repository (`nms-atlas-master-os`) hosts a local-first, offline-capable web application and structured knowledge base for a multi-platform No Man's Sky playthrough (PC/Mac, Nintendo Switch, and iOS).
