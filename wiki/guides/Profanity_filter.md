# Profanity filter

Alert of a name containing profanity

**Profanity filter** is a game mechanic.

## Summary

No Man's Sky has a profanity filter for names of the species and planets that players get to rename before uploading.

## Query range

Besides typical profanities in English language, No Man's Sky profanity filters include references to genitalia, slangs and slurs in other main languages (not included in this article because of profanity filter).

## Results of query failure

When players include the words in any part of the renaming, the filter will inform players and deny the name.
