# Project Elskyvin

A bloody red sky box can be found in systems with the number **5** (e.g. F5, G5p, E5f, O5pf)

**Project Elskyvin** is a science-focused research.

## Summary

**Note**: This section is under development.
**Project Elskyvin** is science-focused research. Its aim is to document star sky colors for all spectral classes. In binary and trinary systems, two and three skybox colors unique to the spectral number are mixed to form a new color. This page is under construction as documentation is ongoing.

## Methodology

Take two pictures in space with one showing at least half of the star. Take a picture of night sky in a non-dead planet with clear or normal weather. Exotic planets are best due to them having being always storm-free.
