# Cronus's Grill System

**Cronus's Grill System** refers to the advanced culinary functionality provided by the Nutrient Processor aboard the Space Anomaly. While identical in mechanics to planetary processors, its placement next to Iteration: Cronus gives it narrative and functional importance within the Anomaly.

## Overview

The system includes a fully-functional Nutrient Processor located near Iteration: Cronus, the master chef of the Anomaly. Players can use this processor to prepare meals from ingredients found throughout the universe.

Unlike portable units, Cronus's processor also provides access to a built-in Ingredient Storage, allowing for easy retrieval of cooking components without requiring inventory space.

Once a dish is prepared, it can be submitted to Iteration: Cronus for evaluation. He will assign a rating based on its complexity and ingredients, rewarding the player with Nanites for higher-quality meals.

## Usage

- Access the Nutrient Processor near Iteration: Cronus to combine ingredients.
- Use the integrated Ingredient Storage to quickly select from stored components.
- Create single-ingredient or multi-step recipes for higher value.
- Submit the final dish to Iteration: Cronus for judgment.
- Receive a reward of Nanites based on Cronus's satisfaction.

## Features

- No cost to use the system.
- Integrated Ingredient Storage for seamless cooking access.
- Located in a persistent and safe hub environment.
- Convenient proximity to Iteration: Selene and other crafting terminals.
- Immersive roleplaying value as part of No Man's Sky’s cooking mechanics.

## Tips

- Experiment with multi-layered recipes for better evaluations.
- Rare ingredients such as Vortex Cube-based jellies may yield greater rewards.
- Meals judged as "Delicious" or "Incredible" provide the most Nanites.
