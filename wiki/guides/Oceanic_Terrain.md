# Oceanic Terrain

**Oceanic Terrain** is a geographical feature.

## Summary

Achi Eta, A Mountainous Ocean World in Ommund

**Oceanic Terrain**, refers to a very rare occurrence of planet surfaces that have very small amounts of land. Exotic and dead planets will never be oceanic. Moons will never be oceanic.

A planet with oceanic terrain will feature most of the surface covered in water. They consist nearly uniformly of a down topography of which the tops are the only thing breaking through the surface of water. Many small islands with huge caveats of water have no waypoints at all and those that do form small plateaus of flatland rather uncommon for this planet type. Travel here is highly improbable and a Nomad is mandatory for portal travellers. Due to their lack of spawn points, bases are rare here and finding one from a portal can literally take hours if one spawns at all.

## Types

No specific variation has been noticed that would allow type categories at this time.

## Game application

In No Man's Sky, all features are procedurally generated, so any type of archetype can be present in the game are by definition undetermined.

A game example of an oceanic planet is Ommund.
