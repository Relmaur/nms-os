# Exploration

**Exploration** is a player role.

## Summary

**Exploration** is a player main role, you can if you wish only enjoy exploring new star systems or discovering new planets and new species of fauna and flora, even with the basic equipment.

## Planet Discovery

There are officially 18,446,744,073,709,551,616 planets and moons in No Man's Sky, so there are so many worlds to explore that it's simply impossible to visit them all. Each planet, although it may have similarities, is all unique, and everything can be played out on small details that vive a "personality" to the planet

All planets, moons and star systems are procedurally generated. Exploring worlds can result in the discovery of original things, in the long run, you may encounter very rare and beautiful things.

## Learning Languages

See: Language for more information.

Each species (Geks, Korvax and Vy'keen) has its own language. At the beginning of the game you are not able to translate these languages. As you explore, you'll learn different words in different ways so that you'll be able to translate these languages. There is also the Atlas language which allows you to understand it through its Atlas Stations.

There are 771 Gek words, 761 Korvax words, 781 Vy'keen words, and about 220 Atlas words.

### Word Learning Methods

- Activating Knowledge Stones
- Ask NPCs for help with the language.
- Work on the language with NPCs.
- Complete Monolith problems.

It is possible to improve your Exosuit to learn new words more easily from translation technologies.
