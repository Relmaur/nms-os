# Wonders Catalogue

**Wonders Catalogue** is a game mechanic.

## Summary

The **Wonders Catalogue** allows players to keep track of information concerning some of their rarest & strangest discoveries. It is part of the Catalogue section, inside Catalogue & Guide, one of the 4 main parts of the in-game menu.

These discoveries do not trace back further than update 3.30 (Expeditions) or June of 2021. Every record related to before this date has not been recorded by the game and has to be revisited.

### Guidelines

- 5 entries are listed per category.
- The catalogue only documents the encountered values, not the real one. For planets, this means weather has to be encountered, and for temperatures, it means a midnight blizzard or a midday firestorm has to be encountered for the maximum value to be correctly recorded.
- Glitched and modded values are not accepted (i. e. deepest ocean on collided planets or when falling through the floor).
- Every entry must include a screenshot of the wonders catalogue entry for verification. If an entry cannot be verified by other players in the vanilla game, it will be removed.

## Categories

These discoveries are classified into 7 different categories:

### Treasures

Keeps track of the **most valuable** version of any of the 13 different variations of Artifacts the player can find throughout the universe; expressed in *Years, Lifetimes* or *Mutated Genes.*

Check the Artifact Database to see other players records.

### Collected Glitches

Allow the player to check their collection of Stabilised Reality Glitches, found on exotic planets. It also displays the number of items collected.

### Flora Records

Registers various details regarding plants:

- Strongest Photoactivity
- Longest Lived
- Most Invasive
- Deepest Roots
- Strongest Cold Resistance
- Highest Drought Tolerance
- Greatest Radiation Resistance
- Most Toxic If Eaten

Sadly, there is currently no precise value linked to each different type of Flora records. Hence there is no way for players to compare their finds. The only four available facts, common to all plant records and unrelated to them specifically, are Age, Root Structure, Nutrient Source and Notes.

### Fishing Records

Keeps track of a variety of fish caught using the Fishing Rig or Automated Traps. Data contains information about the rarity of the fish, the specific conditions and planetary climate where it can be caught, the weight, in kg, and the number of times it was caught.
