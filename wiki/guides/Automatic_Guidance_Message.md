# Automatic Guidance Message

**Automatic Guidance Message** is an in-game notification.

## Summary

**Automatic Guidance Message** is a feature that helps players deal with immediate problems.

They have five type of automatic guidance message: Exosuit, Starship, Exocraft, Anomaly and VR. The message will show as the mission HUD alert.

## Exosuit Guidance Message

### Sentinel Deployment

When all Sentinels are destroyed and the wanted level is increased, the Guidance Message will warn the player that the stronger Sentinel forces are being deployed.

### Sentinel Investigation

When using the mining beam or other weapon to destroy a plant or the small rock, Sentinels will investigate and a message will alert the player. If the player is still breaking the law while in sight, the Sentinel will attack the player.

### Recharge Life Support

When Life Support is low, the message will advise you to find Oxygen or make Life Support Gel in your inventory.

### Recharge Hazard Protection

When Hazard Protection is low, the message will advise the player to find Sodium or craft Ion Battery in their inventory.

### Recharge Hazard Protection (derelict freighter variant)

When Hazard Protection is low and the player is aboard a derelict freighter, the message will advise the player to find nearby Emergency Heater.

### Planetary Pirate Raid Event

The message will inform the player of the remaining pirates, and instruct the player to launch their ship into space to fight the pirates.

### Extreme Storm Event

The message will guide the player to find shelter during the extreme storm event.

### Restore Inventory

After dying in normal mode, the player's inventory is stored in a grave and the player must find it to restore their inventory.

## Starship Guidance Message

### Sentinel Interceptor

The Sentinel Interceptor message is triggered when Sentinel Interceptors are deployed. It will tell you how many Sentinel Interceptors are left.

In the Outlaws update, it will tell you about the autopilot.

In the Echoes update, it will tell you about power diverting.

### Pirate

The pirate guidance message will trigger when pirates are:

- Scanning the player: When pirates are scanning the player, the remaining time of the scan and the time left for the player to communicate will be displayed, as well as instructions how to open the quick menu.
- Attacking the player: When pirates warp to the player's location, the number of pirates remaining will be displayed.

### Launching and Exiting

The Launching and Exiting message will trigger when you're in your Starship while landed on a planet. It will guide you to launch your Starship using the Launch Thruster, or exit your Starship.

If the Launch Thruster was destroyed, it will show that the said technology has been critically damaged and guide you to exit your Starship.

### Recharge Pulse Engine

The Pulse Engine may run out of fuel during long pulse jumps. In that case, the message will guide you to use your Scanner to scan for Asteroids and mine them for Tritium to recharge the engine.

### Passive Starship Attack

This message will trigger if you attack a non-hostile starship. Similar to the "Pirate" message (because it will make that starship turn hostile), except the message has one more additional information: if you destroy that starship, it will alert Sentinel Interceptors.
