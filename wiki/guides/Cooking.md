# Cooking

How the Nutrient Processor works

**Cooking** is a process of combining ingredients.

## Summary

**Cooking** is a process of combining various Raw Ingredients, Edible Products, Fish, and/or Harvested Agricultural Substances into Consumables with the use of a Nutrient Processor.

Foods can be acquired from:

- Processing foods.
- Killing faunas.
- Tame faunas, then extract their food.
- Through crafting (baits).
- From extractable plants.
- From floras.

Foods can be used to:

- Get bonus when consumed.
- Supercharge Jetpack or Sprint when consumed.
- Get when consuming a Chewy Wires
- Get when bring food to Iteration: Cronus.
- Tame a creature or distract a predator.
- Increase chance of rare or big fish when inserted in the Fishing Rig's bait box.

## Recipes

### 01 - Prepare Baits - Baits

The only product this creates is Creature Pellets, which can be fed to fauna allowing you to harvest Animal Byproducts from them.

- NOTE: For mechanical fauna, an Ion Battery can be fed to them in order to either harvest Chewy Wires, ride them, or make them companions. Ion batteries must be crafted or refined rather than cooked.
- NOTE: The recipe name is slightly misleading. The vast majority of references to "bait" by the game, since the Aquarius update, are for fishing bait. Animal Pellets can be used as fishing bait.

## Additional Information

Cooking can be a good way to farm Nanite Clusters, however, if you just want to record a specific product into your catalogue without having to cook it, all you need to do is arrange the necessary ingredients in the Nutrient Processor such that they are at a certain ratio and one step away. Once you have the desired final ingredient display in your output box, it will be recorded without any pop-ups or sound effects.
