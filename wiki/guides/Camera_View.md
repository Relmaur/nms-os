# Camera View

The camera view can be toggled between first-person and third-person.

| | First-Person View | Third-Person View |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pros** | Aiming and mining are easier. | You see your avatar and can take selfies. |
| **Cons** | You don't see your avatar. | You have to aim slightly to the side. |

To toggle the camera view, press X, select Utilities (a gear icon), and select Toggle Camera View.
