# Multi-Tool Research Terminal

**Multi-Tool Research Terminal** is a research terminal aboard the Space Anomaly.

## Summary

**Multi-Tool Research Terminal** is a terminal used to unlock Multi-Tool upgrade blueprints in exchange for Salvaged Data. It is located aboard the Space Anomaly, alongside other research terminals such as the Starship Research Terminal and Exosuit Research Terminal.

The terminal offers blueprints for a variety of Multi-Tool technologies that expand mining, scanning, and combat capabilities. The unlocks are arranged in branching trees where certain modules require prior research.

Available categories include:

- Mining Beam Upgrades
- Terrain Manipulator Modules
- Scanner Modules
- Combat Weapons (e.g. Blaze Javelin, Scatter Blaster)
- Utility Modules (e.g. Optical Drill, Survey Device)

## Differences from NEXT

In earlier versions such as NEXT, Multi-Tool technologies were mostly acquired through blueprint vendors, specialist NPCs, or missions. With the introduction of the Multi-Tool Research Terminal in Beyond, these upgrades were consolidated into a structured tech tree that uses Salvaged Data as currency.

## Additional information

The Multi-Tool terminal offers access to upgrades that enhance resource collection, increase scanning rewards, or add high-powered combat options. Some blueprints, like the Optical Drill or Survey Device, significantly change gameplay and are valuable for exploration or industrial base building.

## Release History

- Beyond – Introduced alongside the other research terminals in the revamped Space Anomaly.
- Relics – Additional Multi-Tool blueprint adjustments and balance changes.
