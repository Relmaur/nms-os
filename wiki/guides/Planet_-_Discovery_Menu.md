# Planet - Discovery Menu

**Planet - Discovery Menu** is a game mechanics page.

## Summary

The Discovery Menu section on a planet/moon discovery page is composed of several sections providing information about the celestial body.

This menu is accessed by clicking on a planet or moon graphical icon on the Star System Discovery Menu.

Ignoring the left side of the screen (which shows the star system visit history), these sections include:

- **Name** - the planet/moon name (or rename if applicable)
- **Edit/Upload Icon** - a pencil to the left of the name.
- **Discovered** - the name of the player that discovered the planet/moon, and when
- **Planet Data** - descriptive info associated with: Weather, Sentinels Flora, Fauna
- **Resources** - List of three or four resources on the planet/moon
- **System Conditions Icons** - a set of three icons will be under the Planet data.
- **Graphical Image** - a visual image of the planet/moon with rings and moons if appropriate
- **Waypoints** - Graphic of most recent six Waypoints visited, and upload status
- **Flora** - Number of flora discovered
- **Fauna** - Number of fauna discovered/maximum fauna count
- **Minerals** - Number of minerals discovered
- **Discovery Services Status** - Status of connection to Hello Games Discovery Servers

## Name Generation

The names are initially procedurally generated with certain customized terms and patterns recurring. It is completely possible to observe repeated procedurally generated planet/moon names across the universe, however they are unlikely to occur in the same region of a galaxy based on observations collected by travellers.

If the planet has been previously discovery and uploaded, then the player applied name will be displayed.

### Name Edit/Upload

The icon is visible in white when the name has not yet been edited/uploaded to the discovery servers of Hello Games. After it is edited and uploaded, it will be shaded out. Only one traveller can update the planet/moon name, afterwards it is normally fixed and uneditable for other travellers. Clicking on the icon to upload the name will reward the traveller with a small number of nanites.

However, there are many discovery server glitches which sometimes mean that names get reset. See Discoveries - Known Bugs.

## Waypoints

The waypoints section will show the last six waypoints visited by the current traveller in the recent past, and their upload status. If the upload icon is white, then it has not been uploaded yet.

Clicking on the icon to upload the waypoint will reward the traveller with a small number of nanites.

There is no discovery marking of a waypoint, and all travellers can re-discover a waypoint and receive the nanite award.

## Flora

Clicking on the Flora icon in the bottom center of the will bring the traveller to the Flora Discovery Menu.

## Fauna

Clicking on the Flora icon in the bottom center of the will bring the traveller to the Creatures Discovery Menu.

## Minerals

Clicking on the Minerals icon in the bottom center of the will bring the traveller to the Minerals Discovery Menu.

## Resources

Resources are biome specific. In general, the following will have three resources:

- Dead
- Exotic
- Marsh
- Mega Exotic
- Volcanic

All other biomes will have four resources.

## System Status Icons

Three icons under the planet data will indicate the star system wide conditions for the dominant race, the economy and the Conflict Level of the system.

## Discovery Services

The icon will be green if the game is currently connected and receiving the current conditions of the planet/moon from the Hello Games discovery servers. The text will say *Online Discovery Services Active*.

The icon will be red if there is no connection and the text will say *Discovery services offline*.

Note that even if the icon is green, there is a possibility the information displayed on this screen is inaccurate. This occurs when you visit a system that has been visited in the past, and the planet/moon has been discovered/uploaded in a prior era (eg. Atlas Rises, Visions etc.) but the biome has been reset or changed (See Universe Evolution).
