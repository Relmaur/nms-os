# Starship Research Terminal

**Starship Research Terminal** is a research terminal aboard the Space Anomaly.

## Summary

**Starship Research Terminal** is a terminal used to unlock Starship upgrade blueprints in exchange for Salvaged Data. It is located aboard the Space Anomaly, alongside other research terminals such as the Exosuit Research Terminal and Multitool Research Terminal.

The terminal provides blueprints for various starship technologies that improve combat, mobility, fuel efficiency, and utility. The technologies are organized in a tech tree format, with unlocks gated by prerequisite research.

Available categories include:

- Launch System Upgrades
- Pulse Drive Upgrades
- Hyperdrive Modules
- Shield Systems
- Weapon Systems (Photon Cannon, Phase Beam, etc.)
- Utility Modules (e.g. Economy Scanner, Conflict Scanner)

## Differences from NEXT

Before the overhaul in the Beyond update, starship upgrades were typically acquired through missions, specialist NPCs, or random vendor stock. With the introduction of the Starship Research Terminal, blueprints are now consistently accessible via Salvaged Data, making ship progression more structured.

## Additional information

Some key upgrades such as the Economy Scanner, Conflict Scanner, or advanced weaponry like the Positron Ejector may also be found through faction vendors, but unlocking them here ensures reliable availability. Total Salvaged Data required depends on chosen upgrade paths.

## Release History

- Beyond – Introduced with the revamped Space Anomaly.
- Relics – Balance changes and new blueprints incorporated into the tech tree.
