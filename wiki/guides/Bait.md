# Bait

- This article is about items that can be added to a Bait Box. For the consumable that can be fed to Fauna, see Creature Pellets.

An empty Bait Box

**Bait** is an item that can be added to a Bait Box.

## Summary

**Bait** is any item that can be added to the Bait Box of an angling Rig.

## Use

**Bait** can add a bonus to the player's chance of catching larger and rarer fish, and fishes that can only be caught under certain planetary conditions. This bonus will vary depending on the bait used.

The Bait Box can be accessed by navigating to the Multi-tool inventory and selecting your installed Fishing Rig or Lost Angler's Rig, or from the Quick Menu (//) by selecting 'Utilities' then 'Access Bait Box'.

## Source

The following categories of items can be used as bait:

- Any crafted Fishing Bait
- Any Fish
- Any 'foodstuff' - Ingredients that can be added to a Nutrient Processor, or Cooking Products.

## Additional Information

- Somewhat confusingly, "Fishing Bait" is the name used by the game for a specific type of consumable.
- Creature Pellets are also referred to as 'bait' in their description. They can be added to a bait box, but are also the only item that can be fed to certain fauna, or added to an Automated Feeder.
- Many foodstuffs can serve double duty as both a high quality lure and a useful consumable. For instance, Icey Marrow, Mucal Doughnuts, and Cake of the Lost all provide Jetpack overloads in addition to their fishing benefits. Conversely, for those seeking large fish, Starpollen Surprise and Creamy Clouds of Nectar provide significant size bonuses and when consumed replenish Hazard Protection and Health, respectively.
