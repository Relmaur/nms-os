# Making Discoveries

**Making Discoveries** is an information panel.
There are multiple game-release versions of the Making Discoveries page.

- For the current version, visit Making Discoveries
- For the ExoMech version, visit Making Discoveries (ExoMech)
- For the Synthesis version, visit Making Discoveries (Synthesis)
- For the Beyond version, visit Making Discoveries (Beyond)

## Summary

**Making Discoveries** is a display page referenced by the Game Content page.

## The Analysis Visor

The Analysis Visor

- Scan animals, plants and minerals with the Analysis Visor (Key F). Once the Visor is activated, use LPM to analyse your target. Units are rewarded for **each unique scan**.
- Every discovery is logged on the **Discoveries Page** (Esc). Systems and planets are automatically discoveres when visited.
- Visit the Discoveries Page to **rename or upload** your discoveries and earn nanites.
- Discovering **all animal life** on a planet rewards a **large number of units**.
- Scanning plants and minerals with the Analysis Visor (Key F) reveals additional information and may yield extra resources when harvesting.
- **Rare and / or large creatures** are worth more when discovered than common or small creatures.
- Upgrades can be installed in your Multi-Tool to **increase the reward** from scanning creatures or plants.

## Renaming and Uploading Discoveries

Remaining and Uploading Discoveries

- Every creature, plant or rock you discover can be *renamed'* and **uploaded**. You may also rename your **base, Multi-Tool, and starship**.
- Uploading shares your discovery with other Travellers. Any traveller who finds that object will see you as the original finder. Uploading rewards Nanites.
- Discover creatures, plants and rocks with the Analysis Visor. Enter visor mode with (Key F). Analyse your targets with.
- Systems and planets are discovered automatically when visited.
- Track, rename and upload your discoveries from the **Discoveries Page** of the Options Menu.

## Finding Buildings

Finding Buildings

- The Cartographer on Space Stations will supply Planetary Charts for specific buildings. Using these charts will mark a site on your exosuit's HUD.
- Collect Navigation Data from the beacons outside planetary buildings. Exchange this data with the Cartographer for additional maps. The Cartographer will also charts for nanites.
- larger buildings create detectable signals, either through direct transmission or via their radar signature. When exploring on foot, use the Analysis Visor to search for nearby buildings, or build (Key Z) a Signal Booster to mark the closest structure to your position.
- Specialist navigation data reveals the location of Data Pods, which contain powerful Exosuit Upgrades. These should be decoded in a Signal Booster.

## Smaller Points of Interest

Smaller Points of Interest

- Your **compass** is located at the top of the HUD. It will highlight nearby **points of interest** and **mission objectives**.
- Use the Analysis Visor to identify and pinpoint planetary objects. Identified points of interest can be pinned to the Exosuit HUD with (Key E) for ease of navigation.
- Learn how to make Beacons from the Base Scientist. Beacons can be placed anywere using (Key Z) and will be marked on your compass.
- The Signal Booster can be constructed to search for neraby points of interest.
- The Cartographer on Space Stations will supply Planetary Charts for specific buildings,
- Collect Navigation Data from the beacons outside planetary buildings, and from modules inside buildings.
- Exchange data with the Cartographer.
- Some points of interest, like your **ship** and **base** will always be tracked.
- In space or atmospheric flight, follow the **trails** left by other ships to locate planetary trading posts.

## Extreme Weather

Extreme Weather

- Most planets suffer from occasional storms. During a storm, your hazard protection will **drop much faster** than usual. Take shelter during storms if possible.
- Some planets have **extreme wheater**. Your exosuit will warn you when you set foot upon such a world. hazard levels are much higher on these worlds, and storms are more frequent. Hazard Protection Upgrades are strongly recommended before exploring such planets.
- However, such worlds offer rewards to the brave explorer. **Resource deposits** are worth considerably more.
- **Activated** forms of the stellar metals sell high prices, and can refined into vast stores of Chromatic Metal.
- Storm Crystals can be found on extreme weather planets. These are highly valuable rare resources, and can only be harvested during an active storm. Search for them using the Analysis Visor.

## Feeding Creatures

Feeding Creatures

- All creatures will eat Creature Pellets. creatures can be fed directly, or via the Utilities section of the Quick Menu (Key X).
- Special abilities are unlocked with more advanced bait. Scan creatures with the Analysis Visor to discover their bait prefernce. Deploy cpecific bait from the Quick menu.
- Advanced bait allows creatures to be **mounted** of farmed. Create advanced bait in the Nutrient Processor. Bait is typically processed from a base of Faecium or Mordite, blended with local ingredients. Experiment mixing a base with plants or meat to create new bait types.
- bait thrown from the Quick Menu may placate **predators** or other angry creatures, unlocking new farming or trawersal opportunities.

## Portals

Portals

- Portals create **connections between worlds**. Visit a Monolith to learn the location of a planet's portal.
- Portals require theis glyphs to be powered before they can be used. Interact with the Portal Terminal to power the glyphs.
- Once active, a portal can reveal the **Glyph Sequence** for their planet. Other travellers with this glyph sequence can enter it into their own portals and visit your planet.
- The ability to input a Glyph must be **earned**. Use the Scanner and Analysis Visor to locate the graves of fallen Travellers. extract knowledge of glyphs from their logs.
- travellers may rosk entering **random Glyph Sequences** into a Portal, but the accuracy of such unattuned transport is not guaranteed.
