# Galactic Commission Station

The **Galactic Commission Station** provides quests within No Man's Sky.

## Summary

The **Galactic Commission Station**, located inside Freighters, allow players to take on very dangerous versions of Mission Board missions for the different races, and guilds. Upon completion, a player may be rewarded with units, nanite clusters, blueprints, and other items. If a mission would reward a duplicate blueprint, units will be awarded in the blueprint's place. Reputation is awarded for completion based on the race or guild giving the mission.

Missions from the Galactic Commission Station are designed to be completed in Multiplayer play. Four missions, all without rank requirements, are always available at this terminal. One of the missions awards standing with the current star system's dominant lifeform; the other three missions award standing with the Mercenaries, Explorers, and Merchants guilds.

## Mission Types

### Destroy Sentinels

The player is tasked with destroying Sentinel units for the client. Variants offered by the Galactic Commission Station always task the player with destroying Sentinel Walkers.

### Hunt Pirates

The player is tasked with taking down a pirate for the client. Galactic Commission Station variants always require the player to take down Dangerous Pirates, which are much tougher and deadlier than standard Pirates.

### Raid a Facility

The player is tasked with attacking a point of interest for the client. Galactic Commission Station variants usually have a higher Sentinel presence than usual at the point of interest.

- *Raid a Depot*: The player must travel to the marked Depot and destroy it.
- *Raid a Planetary Harvester*: The player must travel to the marked Operations Centre, force its door open, and use its terminal to obtain key information.

### Scan Objects

The player is tasked with scanning unique fauna for the client using an Analysis Visor. Variants offered by the Galactic Commission Station require many more objects to be scanned for completion than those offered by the Mission Board.

### Collect Item

The player is tasked with acquiring the required items, then delivering them to a Galactic trade terminal located at a Trading Post. Galactic Commission Station variants ask for bundles of advanced Resources, such as Ionised Cobalt and Chlorine.

Completion of missions of this type grants ~60,000 Units and 1 standing.

## Additional Information

- Unlike missions from the Mission Board, missions from the Galactic Commission Station are only kept for the duration of the current play session.
- The player can only have one mission from the Galactic Commission Station at a time --- accessing the station while a mission is already active will merely display the details of that mission.
- Missions accepted from a Galactic Commission Station must also be handed in to a Galactic Commission Station to receive rewards.
