# Debris

Red Canister Debris AtlasPass V1 required

**Debris** is a lootable resource.

## Summary

**Debris** is an object found on every planet. It is locked, but if the player possesses an AtlasPass v1 in their inventory they can unlock and take the contents of the Debris. The player may be required to repair the lock before opening.

## Resources

- Suspension Fluid
- Electron Vapour
- Antimatter
- Warp Cell
