# Polymorphic Fauna

**Polymorphic Fauna** are a theoretical type of glitched fauna.

## Summary

**Polymorphic Fauna** are species of glitched creatures found on the same world in multiple forms which all share the same species name and listed characteristics. This is in contrast to monomorphic fauna, normal creature species with only one form.

This phenomenon is currently theoretical but could be potentially found on any world with a large variety of fauna species and should not be limited to any particular biome.

The name Polymorphic is taken from biology where it is used to describe species with two or more clearly different morphs or forms. The name is chosen here to similarly describe glitched fauna as it visualizes them as a single species with multiple differing morphologies or forms.

## Polymorphic Fauna Subcategories

Polymorphic Fauna can be further categorized by the specific number of distinct forms each can be found in.

### Dimorphic Fauna

Polymorphic Fauna having two distinct forms would be classified as Dimorphic Fauna. Theoretically if found, species with three forms would be Trimorphic, and those with four would be Tetramorphic.

#### Sexual Dimorphism

**Sexual dimorphism** is the condition where the two sexes of the same species exhibit distinctly different characteristics, most noticeably in their height, weight, and appearance. Sexual dimorphism amongst fauna species in the No Man's Sky universe is quite common and while a form of Dimorphism, should not be documented as Polymorphic Fauna. The opposite of dimorphism is **monomorphism**, which is creatures with different genders of identical form.

## Documenting Polymorphic Fauna

Despite the multiple species taking two or more separate lines in the Discoveries tab Fauna list, it is suggested they be envisioned as a single species and documented as such on a single creature page.

Species documented as Polymorphic must include pictures clearly showing each of their forms' matching species names and attributes.

## Additional information

Despite each form having identical scientific names and listed characteristics, it is important to remember this phenomena is caused by a bug.

While this theoretical bug seems somewhat similar to the Divergent Species bug affecting some fauna due to species affected by either bug both having identical statistics, they could otherwise be seen as completely opposite situations. Polymorphic Fauna are truly multiple species of creature with distinctly different forms which happen to have identical names, while Divergent Species are a single monomorphic species which somehow spawn under two or more different scientific names.

This phenomenon is different than potentially finding species of fauna on different worlds, with different statistics but the same scientific name. Given their differing statistics and locations, they would truly be distinct species. When differing species are given the same scientific name, they are considered **Homonymous**.
