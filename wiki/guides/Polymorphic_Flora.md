# Polymorphic Flora

**Polymorphic Flora** are a type of glitched flora.

## Summary

**Polymorphic Flora** are species of glitched plant found on the same world in multiple forms which all share the same species name and listed characteristics. This is in contrast to monomorphic flora, normal plants species with only one form.

The pattern of Polymorphic Flora is usually the same, a pair of species (rarely more) comprised of a large plant, like a tree, and a small kind of plant. In a few cases, an aquatic plant may replace the large plant of the pair. Both plant species will share the same scientific name and have identical properties. This phenomenon can be found on any world with a large variety of flora species and is not limited to any particular biome.

The name Polymorphic is taken from biology where it is used to describe species with two or more clearly different morphs or forms. The name is chosen here to similarly describe glitched flora as it visualizes them as a single species with multiple differing morphologies or forms.

## Polymorphic Flora Subcategories

Polymorphic Flora can be further categorized by the specific number of distinct forms each can be found in.

### Dimorphic Flora

Most polymorphic flora are found having two distinct forms, making them Dimorphic Flora.

### Trimorphic Flora

To date, seven multi-form species have been documented with three morphologies:

- M. Wiltputorea
- U. Limekardia
- T. Jamhieum
- Z. Dreamhedosa
- T. Gasrucium
- F. Bindmissia
- R. Spongeemmiae

It is currently unknown how common Trimorphic Flora species may be, or if species could be found with even more forms. Theoretically if found, species with four forms would be Tetramorphic, and those with five would be Pentamorphic.

## Documenting Polymorphic Flora

Despite the multiple species taking two or more separate lines in the Discoveries tab Flora list, it is suggested they be envisioned as a single species and documented as such on a single flora page.

Players discovering a Polymorphic Flora species may use the Polymorphic macro to tag its documentation page. By adding a number as a parm, a specific number of forms may be specified. Species documented as Polymorphic must include pictures clearly showing each of their forms' matching species names and attributes.

For an example, please see K. Culbirea.

## Additional information

Despite each form having identical scientific names and listed characteristics, it is important to remember this phenomena is caused by a bug.

While this bug is somewhat similar to the Divergent Species bug affecting some Fauna due to species affected by either bug both having identical statistics, they could otherwise be seen as completely opposite situations. Polymorphic Flora are truly multiple species of plant which happen to have identical names, while Divergent Species are a single monomorphic species which somehow spawn under two or more different scientific names.

The most Polymorphic Flora species discovered on a single world has been four pairs, as found on the Lush world Blodh-Vollr mid'Mildr.

This phenomenon was once believed to be limited to lush, frozen, and toxic biome worlds, but has been since discovered in all other biomes with significant amounts of flora species, such as scorched, barren, and lush-like mega exotic worlds.

This phenomenon is different than potentially finding species of flora on different worlds, with different statistics but the same scientific name. Given their differing statistics and locations, they would truly be distinct species. When differing species are given the same scientific name, they are considered **Homonymous**.
