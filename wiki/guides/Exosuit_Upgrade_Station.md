# Exosuit Upgrade Station

The **Exosuit Upgrade Station** is an installation aboard the Space Station and the Space Anomaly that allows Travellers to expand their personal inventory capacity.

## Summary

The Exosuit Upgrade Station is a static terminal found:

- On **Space Stations**, to the left side of the interior (opposite the appearance modifier), near the Exosuit merchant.
- On the **Space Anomaly**, directly across from the Exosuit vendor **Ena Zuzzi**, between the Starship and Multi-Tool research areas.

It features a holographic display of an exosuit and a small interface panel.

Each time the station is used, the Traveller may purchase **one** new inventory slot for Units. The location of the unlocked slot (General, Technology, or Cargo tab) may be selected manually.

The cost of the upgrade increases with each slot purchased, scaling progressively higher:

- General inventory slots start cheap and become more expensive with each upgrade.
- Technology and Cargo slots follow their own individual cost curves.

## Availability

- Only **one upgrade slot per star system** is allowed. The upgrade may be redeemed at either the Space Station or the Space Anomaly — not both.
- Once an upgrade has been purchased in a given system, both terminals (Station and Anomaly) will display a "No Upgrade Available" message until the player travels to a new system.
- Returning to the same system will not refresh the station. A jump to a new star system is required for each additional slot.

## Usage

- Approach the Exosuit Upgrade Station aboard a Space Station or the Space Anomaly.
- Interact with the panel to open the upgrade UI.
- Select the inventory tab to expand (General, Technology, or Cargo).
- Pay the displayed Units cost to unlock the slot.
- The slot is added immediately, without any crafting or materials.

## Tips

- To unlock many slots efficiently, visit new star systems and alternate between using the Space Station and Anomaly in each one.
- The **first Cargo slot** upgrade is especially valuable due to its high capacity (500 per stack vs. 250 in General).
- The **Exosuit Merchant** nearby also sells upgrade modules to enhance the Exosuit's core systems (e.g. Life Support, Jetpack).
- If you receive a "No Upgrade Available" message, jump to a new star system and try again there.
