# Flora - Discovery Menu

**Flora - Discovery Menu** is a game mechanics page.

## Summary

The Discovery Menu section on a Flora discovery page. This page is accessed by clicking on a flora graphical icon on the Planet Discovery Menu.

The page is composed of several text sections describing a plant. These sections include:

- **Name** - the flora name (or rename if applicable)
- **Discovered** - the name of the player that discovered the flora, and when
- **Age** - text descriptor of the age of the plant
- **Root Structure** - text descriptor for the root structure of the plant
- **Notes** - text descriptor

## Name Generation

The names are procedurally generated with certain customized terms (such as the name of the planet) being inserted in the proper spot.
