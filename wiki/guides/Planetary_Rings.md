# Planetary Rings

**Planetary Rings** are a planetary feature.

## Summary

**Rings** are objects that surround certain planets in No Man's Sky.

Space stations and moons may appear in them and NPCs can fly through them.

## Appearance

You can see a planet's rings from the surface and they range in size and width. Asteroids spawn within the rings, but they are very hard to collect because of the low visibility within rings.

## Additional Informnation

Star systems with Giant planets will never have rings.
