# Guild Envoy

Envoy Station

**Guild Envoy** is an NPC that provides rewards.

## Summary

The **Guild Envoy** is an NPC located inside space stations on the far right corner of the upper level, or in Colossal Archives. They allow players to receive free rewards based on their standing with the Envoy's guild and allow you to increase your standing with the guild by gifting them various items. The guild represented will be the same in all stations of a given region, regardless of the star system you are in.

## Engaging An Envoy

The envoy is only there to provide rewards to a traveller based upon their standing with their represented guild, be it Mercenaries, Explorers or Merchants, each guild gives different kinds of rewards to the player.

## Explorers Guild Envoy

Explorers guild rewards typically follow the following structure, though there are some variations:

- 5× Ion Battery - Free for Initiate and above
- 1× Warp Hypercore - Free for Apprentice and above
- 500× some kind of resource - 60% discounted (Status Required: Journeyman)
- 1× An A-Class Upgrade Modules or other rare items such as Hypnotic Eye- Free for Associate and above
- 1× an item such as Exosuit Expansion Unit or a Salvaged Frigate Module, or various Planetary Charts - Free for Master and above
- 1× an S-Class (or rarely A-Class) Hazard Protection Upgrade or occasionally other types of Upgrade Modules - Free for Exalted and above

You can donate items to improve your standing (by 1 per item). You can donate up to 3 of the following items per station per day:

- Glowing Mineral
- Storm Crystal
- Exosuit Upgrade Chart
- Hadal Core
- Ancient Key
- Unearthed Treasure

## Merchants Guild Envoy

Merchant guild rewards typically follow the following structure, though there are some variations:

- 3× Starship Launch Fuel - Free for Initiate and above
- 1× Suspicious Packet (Goods) - Free for Apprentice and above
- 500× some kind of resource - 60% discounted (Status Required: Journeyman)
- Craftable Products such as Fusion Accelerant or Semiconductor - 50% discounted (Status Required: Associate)
- Craftable Products such as Fusion Accelerant or Semiconductor - 50% discounted (Status Required: Master)
- Upgrade units such as **Exosuit Expansion Unit**, Cargo Bulkhead or a **Salvaged Frigate Module** - Free for Exalted and above

You can donate items to improve your standing (by 1 per item). You can donate up to 3 of the following items per station per day:

- Salvaged Data
- Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)
- one of the smuggable items, e.g. GrahGrah, Stolen DNA Samples
- a 5th tier tradeable, e.g. Superconducting Fibre, Neural Duct
- an upgrade unit such as Factory Override Unit, Cargo Bulkhead
- a high-tier craftable, e.g. Living Glass, Circuit Board

These items can be the same as the ones you can redeem.

## Mercenaries Guild

Mercenary guild rewards typically follow the following structure, though there are some variations:

- 2500x Projectile Ammunition- Free for Initiate and above
- 1× Unstable Plasma - Free for Apprentice and above
- 500× some kind of resource - 50% discounted (Status Required: Journeyman)
- 1× An A-Class **Hazard Protection Upgrade** - Free for Associate and above
- 1× an item such as Exosuit Expansion Unit or a Salvaged Frigate Module, or various Planetary Charts - Free for Master and above
- 1× an S-Class (or rarely A-Class) Hazard Protection Upgrade or occasionally other types of Upgrade Modules - Free for Exalted and above

You can donate items to improve your standing (by 1 per item). You can donate up to 3 of the following items per station per day:

- Salvaged Glass
- Pirate Transponder
- Quad Servo
- Crystallised Heart
- Hardframe Engine
- Vile cortex

## Guild Standing

To achieve a higher standing, the traveller needs to complete missions specific to the guild from the mission board, or to trade in items to the envoys to increase the standing of the presented guild, Not all missions provide a guild standing increase so selecting a focus plan may be required to target improved standing with a specific guild.

### Standing

- Unknown: 0
- Initiate: 3
- Apprentice: 8
- Journeyman: 14
- Associate: 21
- Senior: 30
- Master: 40
- Elder: 60
- Exalted: 100
