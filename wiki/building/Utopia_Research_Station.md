# Utopia Research Station

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Portable Blueprint Research Station
- **Value:** 10
- **Used for:** Building
- **Added in:** Breach

**Utopia Research Station** is a portable base building product.

## Summary

**Utopia Research Station** is a portable [[Base building|base building product]] for discovering new [[blueprint|blueprints]]/[[technology]], it can be acquired by completing some patch of the [[Expedition 9: Utopia]].

## Source

It can be found in the Build Menu under Tech/Portable. Once deployed, it provides access to the following construction research trees (there are 5 types of station):

- [[Exosuit]] technology
- [[Multi-Tool]] technology
- [[Exocraft]] technology
- [[Starship]] technology
- Base Construction blueprint

These trees can change depending on the station type. Like [[Construction Research Station]] or [[Construction Research Unit]], low-lvl blueprint must be known before you can use high-lvl blueprint.

The Utopia [[Overseer|Overseers]] wish for their brave settlers to be strong and well-equipped!
