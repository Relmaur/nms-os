# Storage Container - Yellow

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Storage Container** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Storage Container** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which is essentially the buildable, yellow version of the [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Container]].

Once placed, they take 1 hour in real time to fabricate a random container item. Once the item is fabricated, the container's glow lines turn from red to green, and any player will be able to claim the item and restart the fabrication process.

## Game description

Buildable ornament to beautify your base.

## Source

The blueprint can be acquired from the [[Blueprint Analyser]].

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Pure Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] → 1 × Storage Container - Yellow
