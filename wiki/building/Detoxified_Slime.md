# Detoxified Slime

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Placeable Sludge
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Detoxified Slime** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Detoxified Slime** is a customisation part available from the Scrap Dealer.

## Game description

Gelatinous and slowly pulsing, this puddle of living slime smells sweeter than would be imagined.

Though there is no evidence to suggest it would be unsafe to do so, avoid treading on the slime where possible.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Faecium]] → 1 × Detoxified Slime

It can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 500 [[Tainted Metal]]. After learning the blueprint, it will be listed under Exotic Parts.
