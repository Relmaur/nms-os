# Large Artifact Crate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Artifact
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Large Artifact Crate** is a container.

## Summary

**Large Artifact Crate** is a container found at [[Ruin|Ancient Ruins]]. It rewards the player with a random [[Artifact]] after all three [[Ancient Lock|Ancient Locks]] are repaired.

It can revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Additional information

- It is most likely located underground and will require the [[Terrain Manipulator]] to excavate it.
- Five or six [[Artifact Fragment|Artifact Fragments]] can be found at an Ancient Ruin, therefore the player does not need to carry additional [[Ancient Key|Ancient Keys]].
