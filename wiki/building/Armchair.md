# Armchair

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Furniture
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Armchair** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Armchair** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and can be found in the Decoration/Interior Decoration building category.

The blueprint is acquired from the [[Construction Research Unit]] or [[Construction Research Station]].

## Game description

A comfortable chair designed to accommodate most bipedal races, with only mild numbing of the posterior.
