# ByteBeat Cable

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** ByteBeat Extension
- **Value:** 0

**ByteBeat Cable** is a base building product.

## Summary

**ByteBeat Cable** is a [[base building]] [[product]].

## Game Description

Advanced cabling, designed to link one or more [[ByteBeat Device|Bytebeat Generators]] or to connect a [[Bytebeat Switch]] to the network.

The signal will synchronize connected lights and device to the generated waveform.

## Crafting

Does not require any materials to build.

## Additional information

- The blueprint can be acquired from the [[Construction Research Station]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for [[Salvaged Data]].
