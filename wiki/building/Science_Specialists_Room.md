# Science Specialist's Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Freighter Construction Module
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Science Specialist's Room** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Science Specialist's Room** is a [[Freighter Base Building]] module accomodating [[Scientist]] terminal.

## Game description

Integrated freighter research equipment tailored to the needs of scientists, and equipped for research and development of scientific blueprints.

This module is placed as an entire room and comes with its functional units pre-installed.
