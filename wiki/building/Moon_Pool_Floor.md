# Moon Pool Floor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Aquatic Construction Module
- **Added in:** Synthesis

**Moon Pool Floor** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Moon Pool Floor** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which creates a "hole" in the bottom of a room, allowing the player to swim up into it from below.

The object's preview only auto-snaps to the bottom surfaces of the following underwater structures: [[Cylindrical Room]], [[Square Room]], [[Deepwater Chamber]], and [[Square Deepwater Chamber]].

## Game description

A holographic moon pool hatch. Allows easy access into and out of aquatic environments.

Pressurisation systems are automatically and discreetly hidden around the room.

## Source

The blueprint is awarded as part of [[Dreams of the Deep]]; however, it can also be unlocked early using a [[Blueprint Analyser]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 80 × [[Pure Ferrite]] + 3 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 2 × [[Living Pearl]] → 1 × Moon Pool Floor

## Additional information

(PS4) Due to a recent bug, it creates an air pocket, however a [[Nautilon]] can still drive underneath.
