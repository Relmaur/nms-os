# Orange Pustule

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Organic decoration
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Orange Pustule** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Orange Pustule** is a customisation part available from the Scrap Dealer.

## Game description

The flesh of this sinister squash is always warm, and slightly luminous. Despite these strange qualities, it is quite edible, and releases a comforting aroma wherever it grows.

Provides decorative landscaping opportunities for bases.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 10 × [[Carbon]] + 10 × [[Copper]] → 1 × Orange Pustule

Blueprint can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 500 [[Salvaged Data]] modules.
