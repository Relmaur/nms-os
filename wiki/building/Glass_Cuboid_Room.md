# Glass Cuboid Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Structure
- **Base complexity:** 15
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Glass Cuboid Room** is a base-building component.

## Summary

**Glass Cuboid Rooms** are a [[Base building]] structure designed for the construction of transparent glass rooms.

## Summary

A Glass Cuboid room is similar to other cuboid rooms in that it fits and stacks with other cuboid rooms. It can also be attached to corridors.

## Game description

A cuboid room module which is created almost entirely out of glass. Excellent for observing areas of natural beauty.
