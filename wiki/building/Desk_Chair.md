## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Desk Chair** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and can be found in the Decoration/Interior Decoration building category.

The blueprint is acquired from the [[Construction Research Unit]] or [[Construction Research Station]].

A comfortable chair designed to accommodate most bipedal races, with only mild numbing of the posterior.
