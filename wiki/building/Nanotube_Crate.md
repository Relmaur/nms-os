# Nanotube Crate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Construction)
- **Value:** 1,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Nanotube Crate** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Nanotube Crate** is a 1st tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A packed crate of carbon nanotubes.

These tiny but incredibly strong tubes are required in the construction of tiny but incredibly strong scientific components.

## Source

Nanotube Crate can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all [[Advanced Materials Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Nanotube Crate can be sold for a profit in any [[Scientific Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].

## Release History

- [[Atlas Rises]] - Added as a trade commodity named Carbon Nanotubes.
- [[NEXT]] - Can be stacked in inventory. It has the same icon as [[Projectile Ammunition]].
- [[Update 1.60|NEXT 1.60]] - Renamed from Carbon Nanotubes to Nanotube Crate.
- [[Waypoint]] - Type changed, formerly "Advanced Construction Materials".
