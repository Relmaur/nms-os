# Flarecap Toadstool

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Organic decoration

**Flarecap Toadstool** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Flarecap Toadstool** is a customisation part available from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Game description

This species of fungus is notable for its dense capillary network and bioluminescent qualities. A circulating chain of chemical reasions produces the toadstool's nocturnal glow.

Provides decorative landscaping opportunities for bases.
