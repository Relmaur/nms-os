# Living Wall

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Corvette Building
- **Type:** Corvette Interior Furnishing
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Living Wall** is a corvette building product.

## Summary

**Living Wall** is a building component specifically designed for [[Corvette|Corvettes]].

## Game description

A specialist hydroponics unit, allowing cultivation of planetary crops while travelling through deep-space.

Note that the Hydroponics feature requires the [[Agricultural Terminal|farmer]] from the [[Expanding the Base]] mission. The sections can then have crops added to them.

This module is installed along the entire wall of a Corvette Hab.

## Build

- **Crafting**: 30 × [[Silver]] + 10 × [[Silicate Powder]] + 10 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Living Wall
