# T-Shaped Corridor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Structure
- **Base complexity:** 40
- **Added in:** Desolation

**T-Shaped Corridor** is a base building component.

## Summary

**T-Shaped Corridors** are a [[Base building]] structure designed to serve as junction points in corridors.

They can have [[window|windows]] built into the top.

## Game description

A corridor module with a branching, t-shaped form, for joining multiple rooms.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × T-Shaped Corridor

## Additional Information

When viewing the schematic at the [[Space Anomaly|Anomaly's]] [[Construction Research Station]], the T-Shaped Corridor's subtype will be listed as "AAST."
