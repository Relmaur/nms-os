# Hexplate Bush

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Stabilised Reality Glitch
- **Used for:** Building
- **Value:** 1,000.0
- **Added in:** Synthesis

**Hexplate Bush** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Hexplate Bush** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and it can be acquired from [[Hexagon]] type [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] planets.

Picking it up gives 1 piece of the product, but it does not unlock the [[blueprint]].

It can be found in the [[Build Menu]] under the Decoration/Glitches.

## Game description

A rare [[Stabilised Reality Glitch|stabilised glitch]], retrieved from a malfunctioning planet. Safe for rehousing in habitable bases.

## Additional information

Hexplate Bush has a blueprint ([[Pure Ferrite]] x10), but it is not possible to unlock it through normal gameplay.

However, if the player has one specimen in their inventory, they can build as many in a row as their supply of Pure Ferrite allows.

Finding the Hexplate Bush can be challenging, since it looks very similar to the shrubs found on Hexagon type planets. However, its branches are a deeper orange than the normal shrubs. This is most noticeable at night.
