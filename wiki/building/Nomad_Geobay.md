# Nomad Geobay

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Construction module
- **Description:** Parking facility for Nomad Exocraft
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Nomad Geobay** is part of the Exocraft system.

## Summary

**Nomad Geobay** is used to provide a safe parking lot for the Nomad [[Exocraft]]. Crafting the Geobay for the first time will automatically craft the Exocraft, which is immediately useable.

[[Geobay|Geobays]] can be placed anywhere, inside or outside of a claimed base. When a geobay is destroyed, the resources used to build it are returned.

All geobays have two terminals. One terminal, with a blue screen, is used to respawn the geobay's Exocraft; the other terminal, with an orange screen, grants access to Exocraft customisation of the vehicle's cosmetics, but not custsomization of the vehicle's performance upgrades.

## Game description

An underground secure parking facility for the Nomad light Exocraft.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 50 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Nomad Geobay
