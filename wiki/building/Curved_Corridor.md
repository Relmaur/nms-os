# Curved Corridor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Structure
- **Base complexity:** 20
- **Added in:** NEXT

**Curved Corridor** is a base building component.

## Summary

A **Curved Corridor** is a [[Base building]] structure designed to connect larger structures together.

As compared to [[Straight Corridor|Straight Corridors]], they are curved in a semi-circular fashion, with windows on the outer side of the curve.
