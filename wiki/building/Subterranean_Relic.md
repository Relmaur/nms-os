# Subterranean Relic

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Artifact
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Subterranean Relic** is a container.

## Summary

**Subterranean Relic** can be found in caves and it contains a [[Vortex Cube]].

It can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Additional information

Picking one up will not impact the player's [[wanted level]], and therefore will not attract [[Sentinel]] attention.
