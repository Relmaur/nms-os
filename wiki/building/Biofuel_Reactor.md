# Biofuel Reactor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Power Distribution Module
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Biofuel Reactor** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Biofuel Reactor** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that generates [[power]] and uses [[organic elements]] as fuel. It can be connected to various base building products with [[Electrical Wiring]] to supply power to them.

## Game description

A small power generator. Burns carbon-based fuel placed in its [[Furnace Tank|furnace tank]] and converts it into usable power.

Connect power generators to base structures and technologies with electrical wiring. Many parts require power to function.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be obtained from the [[Construction Research Unit]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Metal Plating]] + 25 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Biofuel Reactor

## Power generation

A Biofuel Reactor will output 50kP of power for up to 50 hours.

Interacting with a Biofuel Reactor displays power grid information, including total power currently generated by all connected generators; total power currently consumed by all connected base products; amount of power currently stored in all connected Batteries; and the amount of time left before fuel runs out.

## Additional information

- Full charge requires 100 [[Carbon]], 34 [[Condensed Carbon]], or 50 [[Oxygen]]

## Release history

- [[Beyond]] - Added as a base building product.
- [[Living Ship]] - Blueprint changed to reduce Oxygen requirement, formerly 40.
- [[Endurance]] - Operation time on full power increased from one to 50 hours; charging resource requirements drastically reduced.
