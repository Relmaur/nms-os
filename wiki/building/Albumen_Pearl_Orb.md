# Albumen Pearl Orb

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Orb Plant
- **Added in:** Origins

**Albumen Pearl Orb** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

## Summary

**Albumen Pearl Orb** seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], or purchased from the Construction Research Station in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 3 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Game description

A medium-sized, farmable Crystal Orb that yields an occasional harvest of valuable [[Albumen Pearl]]s.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] indoor planting. Cannot be planted outdoors.

Approximate growing time: 2 hours

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 60 × [[Indium]] + 20 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Albumen Pearl Orb

## Additional information

- Growing the plant will produce 1 [[Albumen Pearl]] in roughly 2 hours of real time.
