# Internal Stairway

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Corvette Building
- **Type:** Corvette Interior Furnishing
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Internal Stairway** is a corvette building product.

## Summary

**Internal Stairway** is a building component specifically designed for [[Corvette|Corvettes]].

## Game description

A vertical accessway, allowing traversal between floors of the corvette-class starship.

This module is installed into the centre of a Corvette Hab.

## Build

- **Crafting**: 50 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Internal Stairway
