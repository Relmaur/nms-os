# Artifact Fragment

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Artifact
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Artifact Fragment** is container.

## Summary

**Artifact Fragment** is a container found at [[Ruin|Ancient Ruins]]. It contains one [[Ancient Key]] that can be used to open [[Ancient Lock|Ancient Locks]] on [[Large Artifact Crate|Large Artifact Crates]].

It can revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Additional information

- It can be located underground and will require the [[Terrain Manipulator]] to excavate it.
- Five or six Artifact Fragments can be found at an Ancient Ruin.
