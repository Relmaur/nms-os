# Crate Fabricator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Added in:** Visions

**Crate Fabricator** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Crate Fabricator** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which is essentially the buildable version of the [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Container]].

Once placed, they take one hour in real time to fabricate a random container item. Once the item is fabricated, the container's glow lines turn from red to green, and any player will be able to claim the item and restart the fabrication process.

Moving a moderate distance away, or traveling to another location will reset the container as ready to claim again.

## Game description

Buildable ornament to beautify your base.

## Source

The blueprint can be acquired from the [[Blueprint Analyser]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 10 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] → 1 × Crate Fabricator

## Additional information

- All [[freighter|freighters]] have one Crate Fabricator by default in the open section of the build area.
- See [[Barrel Fabricator]] for similar fabricating container.
