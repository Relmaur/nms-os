# Decals

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Base complexity:** 1
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Decals** are a base building component.

## Summary

**Decals** are used in personalizing and decorating a player's home base or [[freighter]]. The [[blueprint]] provides 13 different varieties, including numbers and the No Man's Sky logo. One of the more common usages is labeling rooms and hallways for identification purposes.

## Game description

Placeable decorative decals.

## Build

- **Crafting**: 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Decals
