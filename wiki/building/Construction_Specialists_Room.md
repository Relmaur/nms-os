# Construction Specialist's Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Freighter Construction Module
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Construction Specialist's Room** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Construction Specialist's Room** is a [[Freighter Base Building]] module accomodating [[Overseer]] terminal.

## Game description

Integrated freighter research equipment tailored to the needs of construction overseers, and equipped with architectural and engineering design software.

This module is placed as an entire room and comes with its functional units pre-installed.
