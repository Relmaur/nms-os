# Stealth Starship Trail

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship Exhaust Override
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Stealth Starship Trail** is a Starship enhancement.

## Summary

**Stealth Starship Trail** is a Starship Exhaust modifier.

It provides a dark purple color to the output of the [[Pulse Engine]].

The product can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 2,100  Quicksilver.

## Game description

An exclusive Starship Exhaust Modifier.

Baryon suppressors alter the Pulse Engine's output, giving a distinct black tint to its signature trail.

Install this technology in your starship inventory to change the appearance of the engine exhaust trails.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 10 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Stealth Starship Trail

## Additional information

- Upgrade Properties have been extracted from the 3.75 version of the **NMS_REALITY_GCTECHNOLOGYTABLE.MBIN** game file.
- Currently, starship trails have a multiplayer based bug where a player would notice the color change on his/her own ship if the corresponding trail is equipped, but other players do not see any change in the trail color from the traditional blue.
