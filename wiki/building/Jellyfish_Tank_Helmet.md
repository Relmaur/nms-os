# Jellyfish Tank Helmet

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Unlockable Helmet
- **Added in:** Xeno Arena

**Jellyfish Tank Helmet** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Jellyfish Tank Helmet** is an unlockable helmet available via the [[Appearance Modifier]].

## Game description

An exclusive Exosuit Appearance Override.

Transport evidence of your fishing achievements around in your own personalised cranium/aquarium interchange.

Transform the appearance of your Exosuit at an Appearance Modifier.

## Source

Can be obtained as a reward for defeating the [[Deepwater Guardian]].
