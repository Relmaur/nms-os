# Red Canister

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Description:** Lootable container
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Red Canisters** are a type of storage unit.

## Summary

**Red Canisters** are base decoration items. They act as lootable containers, just like those found throughout the game as [[Debris]] or storage containers at Trading Posts. Looting them will procure either 1 [[Suspension Fluid]], [[Electron Vapour]], [[Antimatter]] or [[Warp Cell]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 10 × [[Voltaic Cell]] + 5 × [[Glass]] + 2 × [[Dynamic Resonator]] → 1 × Red Canister

## Additional information

- Loot respawn time = about 1h30 minutes?
- Once placed, Red Canisters will always provide the same item when looted. You can delete and move them around to try to improve the item obtained.
