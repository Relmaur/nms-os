# Automated Trap

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Building Part
- **Type:** Self-Sustaining Auto Creel
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Automated Trap** is a building part

## Summary

**Automated Trap** is a building part and a deployable technology which can be placed on water and will automatically [[fishing|catch]] [[fish]] over time.

## Game description

An autonomous fishing device, designed for long-term deployment in a wide-range of water conditions.

Once activated, the system will lure and trap any nearby aquatic creatures and store them for later retrieval.

## Use

**Automated Trap** can be placed on any [[Biome Subtype - Underwater|body of water]], and up to three can be placed on each planet. Over time, the four inventory slots will fill up with stacks of fishes that can spawn in the planetary [[biome]]. Other [[Fishing|conditions]] such as weather and water depth do not seem to limit what an Automated Trap can catch, but rarer fishes are less likely to be caught.
