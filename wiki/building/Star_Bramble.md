# Star Bramble

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Plantable Seed
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Star Bramble** is a farmable agricultural product.

## Summary

**Star Bramble** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

Star Bramble seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], or purchased from the Construction Research Station in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 3 [[Salvaged Data]],

## Game description

A medium-sized, farmable plant that yields an occasional harvest of valuable Star Bulbs.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] indoor planting. Ensure [[Biome - Lush|Lush]] climate before planting outdoors.

Approximate growing time: 4 hours

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Star Bulb]] + 25 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Star Bramble

## Additional information

- Growing the plant will produce 25 [[Star Bulb|Star Bulbs]] in roughly 4 hours of real time.
- It can be found on Lush planets along with its planting source, Paraffinium.
