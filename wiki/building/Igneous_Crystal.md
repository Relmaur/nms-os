# Igneous Crystal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Organic decoration
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Igneous Crystal** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Igneous Crystal** is a customisation part available from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Game description

A quiet roar sounds from within this natural crystal formation, lava and gas dancing in a perpetual cycle of heating and solidifying. Mesmerising to the eye, but despite the ultrahot core, only slightly warm to the touch.

Provides decorative landscaping opportunities for bases.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Igneous Crystal

Blueprint can be purchased from the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion for 500 [[Quicksilver]].

## Additional information

- The info panel for this object in the catalog shows it costs 500 [[Salvaged Data]] modules, however the blueprint can only actually be obtained using Quicksilver at the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion and not using the [[Construction Research Unit]].

- [[Expeditions]] - Added as a customisation part.
