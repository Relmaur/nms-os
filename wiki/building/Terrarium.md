# Terrarium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Interior Decorations
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Terrarium** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Terrarium** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and can be found in the Interior Decorations category.

The product can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 800  Quicksilver.

## Game description

A small observatory unit for studying animal behaviours.

Completely self contained, complete with inbuilt nutrient processor, air purifier and faecal disposal system.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Mordite]] + 50 × [[Faecium]] + 2 × [[Glass]] → 1 × Terrarium
