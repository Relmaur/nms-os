# Bytebeat Switch

## Summary

**Bytebeat Switch** is a [[base building]] [[product]].

## Game Description

A musical switching device, designed to interpret waveform signals generated by a [[Bytebeat Device|Bytebeat Generator]] and output electrical impulses to connected [[light|lights]] and other devices.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 25 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 15 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Bytebeat Switch
