# Horrific Flesh-Helmet

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Anomalous Face Transformation
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Horrific Flesh-Helmet** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Horrific Flesh-Helmet** is an unlockable helmet available via the [[Appearance Modifier]].

## Game description

Naturally transform your appearance via the application of a flesh-hungry parasite. This living being partially fuses with the brainstem of its host, sealing their symbiosis.

Cautiously transform the appearance of your [[Exosuit]] at an Appearance Modifier.

## Source

It can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 3200 [[Tainted Metal]].

## Additional information

This face transformation resembles the fauna seen in derelict freighters.
