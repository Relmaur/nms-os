# Tentacled Decal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Tentacled Decal** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Tentacled Decal** is a customisation part available from the Scrap Dealer.

## Game description

An exclusive placeable decal to add a touch of macabre decoration to bases.

This decal depicts a many-armed sentience, found drifting in the dark places of the universe.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Tentacled Decal

It can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 400 [[Salvaged Data]] modules.
