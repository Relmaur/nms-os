# Roamer Geobay

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Construction module
- **Description:** Parking facility for Roamer Exocraft
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Roamer Geobay** is part of the Exocraft system.

## Summary

**Roamer Geobay** is used to provide a safe parking lot for the Roamer [[Exocraft]].

[[Geobay|Geobays]] can be placed anywhere, inside or outside of a claimed base. When a geobay is destroyed, the resources used to build it are returned.

All geobays have two terminals. One terminal, with a blue screen, is used to respawn the geobay's Exocraft; the other terminal, with an orange screen, grants access to Exocraft customisation.

## Game description

An underground secure parking facility for the Roamer medium Exocraft.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 100 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Roamer Geobay
