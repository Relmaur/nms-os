# Atlas Figurine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship Interior Adornment

**Atlas Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment.

## Summary

**Atlas Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment, also known as a bobblehead.

The product can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 3200  Quicksilver.

## Game description

A synthetic polymer adornment for your starship's cockpit, fashioned in the likeness of an Atlas Interface.

Install to an Inventory Slot to decorate your ship's interior.
