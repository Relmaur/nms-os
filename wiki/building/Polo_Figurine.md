# Polo Figurine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship Interior Adornment
- **Type:** Bobblehead
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Polo Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment.

## Summary

**Polo Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment, also known as a bobblehead.

The product can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 3200  Quicksilver.

## Game description

A synthetic polymer companion for your starship's cockpit, fashioned in the likeness of Specialist Polo.

Install to an Inventory Slot to decorate your ship's interior.

## Upgrade properties

- *Maneuverability*: +5%
- Synergises with **[[Artemis Figurine]]** when placed next to each other
- Synergises with **[[Tentacled Figurine]]** when placed next to each other
- Synergises with every Starship trail, increasing maneuverability.
