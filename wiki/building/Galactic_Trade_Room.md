# Galactic Trade Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Freighter Construction Module
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Galactic Trade Room** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Galactic Trade Room** is a [[Freighter Base Building]] module replacing [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Game description

Integrated freighter equipment providing instant local access to the galactic trade market for resources and commodities.

This module is placed as an entire room and comes with its functional units pre-installed.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 60 × [[Silver]] + 35 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × Galactic Trade Room

- **Dismantle** returns: 60 × [[Silver]] + 35 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]]
