# Button

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Power Distribution Module
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Button** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Button** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that is used to toggle [[power]] to buildings using [[Electrical Wiring]].

## Game Description

A power management device, the switch will toggle the flow of power when used.

Switches require both power and signal wires to control a device.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Button

## Additional Information

- The blueprint can be acquired from the [[Construction Research Station]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for 1 [[Salvaged Data]].
- The product activated in the same way as a [[Wall Switch]] by a player using it (default E key). The difference is that this one will deactivate automatically after 1 second of being active, while a [[Wall Switch]] needs to be used by a player one more time to be deactivated.
