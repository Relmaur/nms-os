# Artemis Figurine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship Interior Adornment
- **Type:** Bobblehead
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Artemis Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment.

## Summary

**Artemis Figurine** is a Starship Interior Adornment, also known as a bobblehead.

The product can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 3200  Quicksilver.

## Game Description

A synthetic polymer companion for your starship's cockpit, fashioned in the likeness of the Traveller Artemis.

Install to an Inventory Slot to decorate your ship's interior.

## Upgrade Properties

- *Boost*: +5%
- Synergises with **[[Polo Figurine]]** when placed next to each other
- Synergises with **[[Tentacled Figurine]]** when placed next to each other
- Synergises with every Starship trail, increasing boost speed.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 10 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Artemis Figurine
