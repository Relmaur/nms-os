# Atmosphere Harvester

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Autonomous Gas Compressor
- **Value:** 1.0
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Atmosphere Harvester** is a portable base building product.

## Summary

**Atmosphere Harvester** is a portable [[base building]] [[product]] for extracting [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas|gases]] from a planet's atmosphere. It can extract the planet's gases without the need for constant supervision.

## Game description

An automated siphon unit for the collection of valuable gases. Once activated, the harvester condenses and compresses substances from the air over time. Compressed gas is then stored safely until collected.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Ammonia]] + 2 × [[Metal Plating]] + 2 × [[Hermetic Seal]] → 1 × Atmosphere Harvester

## Fuel sources

The Atmosphere Harvester has a [[Fuel Inverter]], which requires 80 [[Carbon]] or 27 [[Condensed Carbon]] to be fully charged.

## Additional information

- Its blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and continue the [[Scientist]] quest line.
- It is possible to store up to five (5) Atmosphere Harvesters in a single regular inventory slot (Ten (10) for a Cargo/Freighter slot). However, a maximum of three (3) Atmosphere Harvesters may be setup per planet. (Tip: Mark your harvester array with a colored [[Save Beacon]], and activate a [[Waypoints]] on your galactic map for the system as a safety measure against losing track of them).
- A fully charged device will harvest a maximum of 250 units over a time period of roughly 60 minutes.
- As of [[Update 6.06|Version 6.06]], removing an harvester will destroy any remaining fuel and harvested gas.
- As of [[Update 6.06|Version 6.06]], the harvester *can* keep working when player quit the game.
- The type of gas harvested  depends entirely on the type of planetary environment they're set up in:
  - [[Sulphurine]] on Hot and [[Biome - Barren|Barren]] planets
  - [[Radon]] on [[Biome - Irradiated|Irradiated]] and [[Biome - Frozen|Frozen]] planets
  - [[Nitrogen]] on [[Biome - Lush|Lush]] and [[Biome - Toxic|Toxic]] planets
  - [[Oxygen]] on [[Biome - Exotic|Exotic]] planets
- Although Atmosphere Harvesters must be built on outside terrain, they can clip through base walls if placed close, allowing access to them from inside. This is useful for safely collecting resources on inhospitable planets.

## Gas Crafting

One or more of these three gases make it possible to craft some of the rare intermediary materials (sometimes worth 100k to 2m), which in turn can be used to create various advanced (and highly valuable) commodities.

**NOTE:** The information below refers to product creation using a blueprint. A refiner can be used to create these products using various different recipes.

- Radon is combined with [[Condensed Carbon]] to make [[Enriched Carbon]]
- Sulphurine is combined with Condensed Carbon to make [[Thermic Condensate]]
- Nitrogen is combined with Condensed Carbon to make [[Nitrogen Salt]]

Here is one, non-exhaustive, example of a gas crafting chain:
- **Thermic Condensate** is combined with Nitrogen Salt to produce a [[Semiconductor]]
  - **Semiconductor** is combined with Enriched Carbon to produce a [[Superconductor]] (value = 400k)
    - **Superconductor** is combined with a [[Circuit Board]] (value = 1.2m) to create a [[Quantum Processor]] (value = 5.2m)
      - **Quantum Processor** is combined with [[Portable Reactor]] (value = 5m) and [[Geodesite]] (value = 150k) to make a [[Fusion Ignitor]] (value = 18m)

This is just an example of the things you can discover as you progress in the game by exploring planets and bartering with aliens.
