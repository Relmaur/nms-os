# Vertical Glass Tunnel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Aquatic Construction Module
- **Added in:** Visions

**Vertical Glass Tunnel** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Vertical Glass Tunnel** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which has no comparable corridor. A [[ladder]] can be constructed within it for ease of ascent.

This structure's preview auto-snaps to many vertical anchors.

## Game description

Pressurised, reinforced glass tunnels for deep-sea construction.

Advanced nano-mineral coating ensures high performance at depth, without compromising visibility.

## Source

The blueprint is awarded as part of [[Dreams of the Deep]]; however, it can also be unlocked early using a [[Blueprint Analyser]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Glass]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 25 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Vertical Glass Tunnel
