# Oxygen Harvester

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Atmosphere Processing Device
- **Value:** 10.0
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Oxygen Harvester** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Oxygen Harvester** is a [[base building]] [[product]] for extracting [[Oxygen]] from the air.

## Game description

An autonomous atmosphere extractor, this device can be left to collect and purify oxygen from the air. User must monitor fuel levels to ensure continued operation.

The resulting gas is compressed and processed, ready for use in all standard life support systems.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 5 × [[Life Support Gel]] + 3 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] → 1 × Oxygen Harvester

When [[dismantle|dismantled]] - Oxygen Harvester returns no resources held inside (i.e. any Oxygen and Carbon are lost), however construction ingredients are fully refunded.

## Fuel sources

The Oxygen Harvester has a [[Generator Coupling]], which requires 100 [[Carbon]] or 34 [[Condensed Carbon]] to be fully charged.

## Additional information

- A fully charged device will harvest a maximum of 250 units over a time period of roughly 12 minutes.
- The blueprint is acquired from the Base Computer Archives mission.
- Despite the fact that constructing the Oxygen Harvester looks, sounds, and gives a portable base part impression, the Oxygen Harvester is not considered portable and can only be built within the normal base limits, with a "zone of control" of 30u where no other oxygen extractors may be built in its immediate area.
