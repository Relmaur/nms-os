# Horrific Decal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Horrific Decal** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Horrific Decal** is a customisation part available from the Scrap Dealer.

## Game description

An exclusive placeable decal to add a touch of macabre decoration to bases.

This decal depicts the 'exotic' fauna discovered breeding in the remains of an unlucky intergalactic [[freighter]].

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Horrific Decal

The blueprint can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 400 [[Salvaged Data]] modules.

- [[Origins]] - Added as a customisation part.
