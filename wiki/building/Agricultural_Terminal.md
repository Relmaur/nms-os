# Agricultural Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Specialist terminal
- **Description:** Research station for Farmers
- **Added in:** Origins

The **Agricultural Terminal** is a terminal.

## Summary

The **Agricultural Terminal** is a [[base building]] [[product]] used for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] farming research.

[[Terminal|Terminals]] of various types can be crafted, each with their own special purposes. The Agricultural Terminal is used for farming of various resources such as Sac Venom and Gravitino Balls. It is used by a hired NPC known as a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]]. These NPCs can be recruited in [[Space station|Space stations]] within [[Gek]] controlled systems.

Seeds/DNA/[[blueprint|blueprints]] for the following items can be acquired at an Agricultural Terminal, based on specific quests from the **Farmer**. The crop produced by each plant follows in parenthesis.

## Game description

A research station tailored to the needs of Farmers, and equipped for the analysis and cultivation of farmable crops.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 1 × Agricultural Terminal

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Condensed Carbon]]

## Additional information

- The [[blueprint]] for an Agricultural Terminal is acquired after completing the first few quests from the [[Construction Terminal]].
- It can be built in a [[Cylindrical Room]], a [[Square Room]], or a [[Cuboid Room]], and each [[terminal]] related to [[Hirable NPC|Hirable NPCs]] needs to be placed in a separate room.
