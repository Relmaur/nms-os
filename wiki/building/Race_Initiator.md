# Race Initiator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 10
- **Type:** Race Part
- **Added in:** Origins

**Race Initiator** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Race Initiator** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and it is required to set up a [[racetrack]] for the various types of [[Exocraft]].

The blueprint is acquired from the [[Blueprint Analyser]].

## Game description

This platform acts as the start and finish line for Exocraft field tests, allowing [[Traveller|Travellers]] to practice manoeuvring and speed.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] → 1 × Race Initiator

## Additional information

- The Race Initiator must be placed on terrain, it cannot be constructed underwater. [[Nautilon]] racetrack creators can get around this restriction by carefully placing their Race Initiators on terrain, whether natural or generated with a [[Terrain Manipulator]], which is just above the water line.
