# NipNip

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Plantable Seed
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**NipNip** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

## Summary

**NipNip** seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]].

## Game description

This medicinal herb is known for its pungent buds, which are harvested to produce [[GekNip|Gek Nip]]. NipNip thrives in dank environments, and often grows as a weed.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic planting]] indoors. Needs a tropical or humid climate before planting outdoors.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[NipNip Buds]] + 20 × [[Faecium]] → 1 × NipNip

## Additional information

- Growing the plant will produce 1 [[NipNip Buds]] in roughly 4 hours of real time.
- Although it says "needs a tropical or humid climate before planting outdoors," NipNip cannot be planted outdoors, unless they are glitch-built.
- Users have glitched multiple plants into one spot to create [[NipNip - Hybrid Strains|hybrid strains]] of NipNip.
