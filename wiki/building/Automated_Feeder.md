# Automated Feeder

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Agricultural Robot
- **Power:** 20 kPs
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Automated Feeder** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Automated Feeder** is a [[base building]] [[product]]. It requires power to operate.

## Game Description

A robotic [[farming]] unit, designed to automatically supply nearby creatures with food.

When powered, will attract nearby animal life. Keep [[Creature Pellets|bait]] tanks full in order to supply lifeforms with desired food.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from the [[Construction Research Station]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for 10 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 4 × [[Metal Plating]] + 1 × [[Amino Chamber]] + 10 × [[Creature Pellets]] → 1 × Automated Feeder

## Additional Information

- Works in combination with the [[Livestock Unit]] for a fully automated feeding and harvesting cycle.
- [[Power]] consumption is 20kP
- Limited to ten (10) Automated Feeders per base.
