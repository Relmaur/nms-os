# Nutrition Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Corvette Building
- **Type:** Corvette Interior Furnishing
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Nutrition Unit** is a corvette building product.

## Summary

**Nutrition Unit** is a building component specifically designed for [[Corvette|Corvettes]].

## Game description

A functional suite of nutrient-processing tools, allowing for the creation of 'meals' while travelling through deep-space.

This module is installed along the entire wall of a Corvette Hab.

## Build

- **Crafting**: 30 × [[Silver]] + 50 × [[Gold]] + 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] → 1 × Nutrition Unit
