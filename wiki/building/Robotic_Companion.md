# Robotic Companion

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Exotic Collectibles
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Robotic Companion** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Robotic Companion** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and can be found in the Exotic Collectibles category.

## Game description

A perfectly-programmed and error free drone.

This miniature robot will be a lively presence in your base, bringing joy, laughter and a constant low level humming to your home.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 30 × [[Pugneum]] + 30 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Robotic Companion

## Acquisition

The blueprint can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 800  Quicksilver.
