# X-Shaped Glass Tunnel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Aquatic Construction Module
- **Value:** 3
- **Added in:** NextGen

**X-Shaped Glass Tunnel** is a base building product.

## Summary

**X-Shaped Glass Tunnel** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which is the underwater-exclusive equivalent of [[X-Shaped Corridor]].

This structure's preview auto-snaps to many horizontal anchors.

## Game description

Pressurised, reinforced glass tunnels for deep-sea construction.

Advanced nano-mineral coating ensures high performance at depth, without compromising visibility.

## Source

The blueprint is awarded as part of [[Dreams of the Deep]]; however, it can also be unlocked early using a [[Blueprint Analyser]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Glass]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 25 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × X-Shaped Glass Tunnel
