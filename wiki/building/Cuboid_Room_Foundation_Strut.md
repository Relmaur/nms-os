# Cuboid Room Foundation Strut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Cuboid Rooms
- **Base complexity:** 5
- **Added in:** Origins

**Cuboid Room Foundation Strut** is a base-building component.

## Summary

**Cuboid Room Foundation Struts** are used in the construction of [[Cuboid Room|Cuboid Rooms]].

When Cuboid Rooms are built, struts can be used to provide better support for their construction and flooring foundation..

## Game description

A sturdy leg for supporting cuboid rooms.
