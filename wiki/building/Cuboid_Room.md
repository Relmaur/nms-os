# Cuboid Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Cuboid Rooms
- **Base complexity:** 10
- **Added in:** Origins
- **Power:** 5 kPs

**Cuboid Rooms** are a base building component.

## Summary

Cuboid rooms can be used to increase the size of existing rooms. They can be stacked, or linked side by side.

If rooms are stacked on top of each other, a [[ladder]] can be installed from one to the other as long as [[Cuboid Room Flooring]] is used between the two levels.

They can also be used as housing locations for [[Storage Container|Storage Containers]].

## Game description

A small, cuboid construction module that can be stacked or linked to form larger structures.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Cuboid Room

- [[Large Window]]
- [[Small Window]]

## Possible bugs

If modifications are made to a cuboid room that contains a [[Hydroponic Tray]], the tray may glitch and disappear, leaving the plants hovering in mid-air.

## Additional Information

- They can be used for [[Hirable NPC|Hirable NPCs]] and their [[terminal]]. Adjacent Cuboid Rooms will appear as a single large room, but are considered separate for placing terminals.
- [[Power]] consumption is 5kP
