# Optical Solvent

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Construction)
- **Value:** 15,000.0
- **Description:** Several bottles of optical solvent
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Optical Solvent** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Optical Solvent** is a 3rd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Several bottles of optical solvent.

The liquid in these bottles is used to wash away surplus light that may otherwise cause decay in scientific components.

## Source

Optical Solvent can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Advanced Materials Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Optical Solvent can be sold for a profit in any [[Scientific Economy]] star system.

It is occasionally needed to fulfil missions given out by the mission agent on space ststions.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
