# Ancient Bones

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Artifact
- **Added in:** Visions

**Ancient Bones** is a container.

## Summary

**Ancient Bones** is a container found at [[Natural Burial Site|Natural Burial Sites]]. It rewards the player with a random [[Excavated Bones]] after excavating and interacting with it.

In space after scanning a planet the information panel indicates it with the  icon, if present.

On the planet once the player reaches a Natural Burial Site, it can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] looking for the same icon.

## Additional information

- It is located underground and will require the [[Terrain Manipulator]] to excavate it.
