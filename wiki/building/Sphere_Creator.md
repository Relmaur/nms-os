# Sphere Creator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 10
- **Type:** Technology module
- **Added in:** Cosmos

**Sphere Creator** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Sphere Creator** is a [[base building]] [[product]]. It requires power to operate.

Pressing the Interact button next to a Sphere Creator creates a spherical physics object that will freely roll when collided with.

The sphere's size will depend on the size of the Sphere Creator. Only one sphere per Sphere Creator can be active at a time, and interacting with the Sphere Creator again will instantly return its sphere. However, multiple spheres can be active with multiple Sphere Creators.

## Game Description

A highly-specific fabrication device, developed with the intention of finding the universe's most perfect sphere.

Requires power to operate. Will create a large sphere, ready for whatever experiments are deemed necessary.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from the [[Construction Research Station]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for 10 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 4 × [[Ion Battery]] + 100 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Sphere Creator

## Additional Information

- [[Power]] consumption is null, but sending an electrical pulse to it will generate a sphere.
- There is a possible bug associated with the interplanetary "star-gate" portals. If you delete the sphere creator while its created sphere is inside the cylindrical warp zone, you will be instantly transported to the inputted planetary coordinates even though you are at a far distance away from the star-gate. Further research required.
