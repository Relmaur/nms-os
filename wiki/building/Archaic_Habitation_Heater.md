# Archaic Habitation Heater

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Organic decoration
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Archaic Habitation Heater** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Archaic Habitation Heater** is a customisation part available from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Game description

Although primitive in design, this habitation heater is nonetheless a popular home furnishing.  Its hypnotic flames and gentle crackling lend a comforting ambience to any room.

Provides interior decoration opportunities for bases.
