# Construction Research Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Technology Recovery Device
- **Value:** 10
- **Used for:** Building
- **Added in:** Origins

**Construction Research Unit** is a portable base building product.

## Summary

**Construction Research Unit** is a portable [[base building]] [[product]] for discovering new [[blueprint|blueprints]] in exchange for [[Salvaged Data]]. The Construction Research Unit provides access to a basic selection of construction components and parts, necessary for setting up a simple base. More blueprints can be obtained later from the [[Construction Research Station]] in the [[Space Anomaly]].

It can be found in the [[Build Menu]] under Tech/Portable. Once deployed, it provides access to the following construction research trees and blueprints:
- Core habitation Tech
  - [[Base Teleport Module]]
  - [[Biofuel Reactor]]
  - [[Save Beacon]]
  - [[Electrical Wiring]]
  - [[Solar Panel]]
  - [[Battery]]
  - [[Health Station]]
  - [[Hazard Protection Unit (base building)|Hazard Protection Unit]]
- [[Build Menu|Timber Shelters]] (Formerly Wooden Shelters)
  - Includes floor panels, wall panels, roof panels, doorways and variants thereof.
- [[Build Menu|Stone Shelters]] (Formerly Concrete Shelters)
  - Includes floor panels, wall panels, roof panels, doorways and variants thereof.
- [[Build Menu|Alloy Shelters]] (Formerly Metal Shelters)
  - Includes floor panels, wall panels, roof panels, doorways and variants thereof.

Similar to the [[Construction Research Station]], parts further down the tree can only be researched after all parts preceding them have been researched.

## Game description

Portable blueprint decryption device.

This field-deployable memory salvage module allows the user to convert [[Salvaged Data]] technology modules into advanced construction blueprints.

## Source

The blueprint for the Construction Research Unit can be acquired from the base building tutorial which you will access from the [[Base Computer]] after starting your first base.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] → 1 × Construction Research Unit
