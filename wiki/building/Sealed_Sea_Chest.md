# Sealed Sea Chest

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Artifact
- **Added in:** Visions

**Sealed Sea Chest** is a container.

## Summary

**Sealed Sea Chest** is a container found at [[Submerged Ruins]]. It rewards the player with a random [[Aquatic Treasure]] after [[Trident Key (Damaged Component)|Trident Key]] and [[Pearl Offering]] are repaired.

It can revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Additional information

- It is located underground and will require the [[Terrain Manipulator]] to excavate it.
- One [[Trident Key]] can be received from the Refracted Light located at a Submerged Ruin.
- Three Sealed Sea Chests can be found at a Submerged Ruin, therefore the player will not have enough Trident Keys to unlock all of them.
