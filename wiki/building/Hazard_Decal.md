# Hazard Decal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Decoration
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Hazard Decal** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Hazard Decal** is a customisation part available from the Scrap Dealer.

## Game description

An exclusive placeable decal to add a touch of macabre decoration to bases.

The decal serves as a warning to all those who would venture past it.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Hazard Decal

It can be purchased from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 400 [[Salvaged Data]] modules.
