# Save Point

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Building Part
- **Type:** Construction module
- **Value:** 2.0
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Save Point** is a buildable technology.

## Summary

**Save Point** is a [[Buildable Technology]] that allow you to save your current game status. They are very useful when playing in [[Game mode|Survival Mode]], as they allow you to save your progress quickly and easily in harsh conditions.

## Game description

A buildable save point for managing your progress.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Metal Plating]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] → 1 × Save Point

## Trivia

The tiny spinning planet which pops up when you've saved your progress appears similar to Earth, but upside down.
