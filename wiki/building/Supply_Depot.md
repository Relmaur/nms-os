# Supply Depot

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Substance Distribution Module
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Supply Depot** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Supply Depot** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that can be used to store extracted [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas]] and [[resource|resources]] from [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit|Deep-Level Mineral Deposits]].

- Supply Depots are connected to [[Mineral Extractor|Mineral Extractors]] or [[Gas Extractor|Gas Extractors]] using [[Supply Pipe|Supply Pipes]].
- Resources are automatically transferred to the Supply Depot/s in timed batches. The player must interact with the Depot to transfer resources into their inventory.
- Each Supply Depot adds 1000 units of storage capacity (the extractors themselves store 250). Connecting more Supply Depots (to the Extractor or a connected Supply Depot) increases the total storage capacity.

## Game description

Automated storage unit. Connect to a Supply Grid using Pipelines.

Automatically stores the output of various Mineral and Gas Extractors, allowing for increased capacity and more efficient siting. Can be located a long distances  from the extraction site.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be obtained from the [[Construction Research Station]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]] for 10 [[Salvaged Data]].
