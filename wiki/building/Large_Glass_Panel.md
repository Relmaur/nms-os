# Large Glass Panel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Construction item
- **Added in:** Visions

**Large Glass Panel** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Large Glass Panel** is a [[base building]] [[product]] which replaces a large structure panel with a window.

The window object is only useful for changing the appearance of [[Cylindrical Room|Cylindrical Rooms]] and [[Square Room|Square Rooms]]; [[Deepwater Chamber|Deepwater Chambers]] and [[Square Deepwater Chamber|Square Deepwater Chambers]], though they are also valid targets, come equipped with Large Glass Panels by default, making placement redundant.

## Game description

A window made from self-cleaning, UV-treated, hermetically sealed glass.

## Source

The blueprint is awarded as part of [[Dreams of the Deep]]; however, it can also be unlocked early using a [[Blueprint Analyser]].

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Glass]] → 1 × Large Glass Panel
