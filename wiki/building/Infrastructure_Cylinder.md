**Infrastructure - Cylinder** is an item used in base building.

## Summary

Items in the Infrastructure category are used to build furniture, panels, and other small items inside rooms inside a base or [[freighter]].

This particular component is shaped liked a **cylinder** with a division round the middle, and each half subdivided into four pie-shaped wedges.
