# Fungal Cluster

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Plantable Seed
- **Added in:** Origins

**Fungal Cluster** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

## Summary

**Fungal Cluster** seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], or purchased from the Construction Research Station in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 3 [[Salvaged Data]],

## Game description

A spherical fungus with internal gills, found only in [[Biome - Toxic|toxic biomes]]. Fungal Clusters can be transplanted for cultivation, yielding a bountiful harvest.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] indoor planting. Ensure Toxic climate before planting outdoors.

Approximate growing time: 4 hours

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Fungal Mould]] + 25 × [[Ammonia]] → 1 × Fungal Cluster

## Additional information

- Growing the plant will produce 50 [[Fungal Mould]] in roughly 4 hours of real time.
