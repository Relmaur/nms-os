# ByteBeat Device

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building

## Summary

**ByteBeat Device** is a [[base building]] [[product]].

## Game Description

An advanced audio generator, allowing the user to synthesise complex musical arrangements.

Multiple devices may be placed in sequence or connected to [[Light|lights]] to generate spectacular displays.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 3 × [[Metal Plating]] + 50 × [[Gold]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] → 1 × ByteBeat Device

## Additional information

- The blueprint can be acquired from the [[Construction Research Station]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for [[Salvaged Data]].
