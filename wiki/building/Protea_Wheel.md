## Summary

**Protea Wheel** is an item used in base building.

## Game description

This unusual lifeform, dominated by a wheel of photopore glands, is difficult to classify neatly into flora or fauna The thick columns of thorns help protect the flavoursome glands from predators.

Provides decorative landscaping opportunities for bases.
