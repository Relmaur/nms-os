# Door

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Construction item
- **Base complexity:** 2
- **Added in:** WorldsOne
- **Power:** 1 kPs

**Door** is a Base building structure.

## Summary

A **Door** a [[Base building]] structure that provides room entry while controlling temperature and atmosphere to the adjoining rooms.

A Door may be installed in any exterior wall of a [[Cuboid Room]], in the four cardinal walls of a prefabricated [[Cylindrical Room|Cylindrical]] or [[Square Room]], a blank or doorway-equipped wooden, metal or concrete wall, or at the end of any corridor.

Doors require power to close; the default/unpowered state is open.

## Game Description

A compression-sealed, standard door suitable for all rooms and corridors.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Door

## Additional Information

- [[Power]] consumption is 1kP
- The door blueprint is obtained from the space anomaly in exchange for 1 Salvaged Data
- When powered, a door cannot be manually opened. Power must be removed for the door to open. This can be accomplished by connecting the door to a [[Power Inverter]] controlled by a switch. A [[Proximity Switch]] near the door will work from either side.
