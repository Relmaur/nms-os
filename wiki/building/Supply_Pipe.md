# Supply Pipe

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Power Distribution Module
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Supply Pipe** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Supply Pipe** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that can be used to connect silos and harvesters.

## Game description

Flexible high strength piping. Able to withstand a highly pressurised internal environment.

Used for connecting various [[Mineral Extractor|Mineral]] and [[Gas Extractor|Gas Extractors]] to [[Supply Depot|Silos]].

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be obtained from the [[Construction Research Station]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]] for 1 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 25 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 10 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Supply Pipe

## Additional information

The maximum length of a single piece of pipe is 240u. However, pipes can be placed in consecutive sections to bridge longer distances between the extractor and a more distant [[Supply Depot]].
