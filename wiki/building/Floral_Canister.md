# Floral Canister

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Floral Canister** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that can be found on [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]].

A containment vessel for alien flora, recovered from the wreckage of a derelict freighter.

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired by searching salvage crates found inside derelict freighters.
