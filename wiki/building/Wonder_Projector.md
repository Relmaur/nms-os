# Wonder Projector

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Interior Decorations
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Wonder Projector** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Wonder Projector** is a [[base building]] [[product]] and can be found in the Interior Decorations category.

The product can be researched from the [[Construction Research Station]] for 12 [[Salvaged Data]] in the Technology Modules section.

## Game description

A buildable holographic display, designed to visualise any discovery currently listed in your Personal Records.

Use the projector to manage these records directly, or view them from the Wonders section of the Catalogue.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] → 1 × Wonder Projector
