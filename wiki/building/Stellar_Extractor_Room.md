# Stellar Extractor Room

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Freighter Construction Module
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Stellar Extractor Room** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Stellar Extractor Room** is a [[Freighter Base Building]] module allowing gathering of stellar resources.

## Game description

Advanced integrated freighter equipment. Gathers resources directly from the interstellar medium, via a complex system of suction intakes and condensing tubes. The gathered substance varies with the local star classification.

This module is placed as an entire room and comes with its functional units pre-installed.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 60 × [[Silver]] + 45 × [[Gold]] + 40 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 1 × Stellar Extractor Room

- **Dismantle** returns: 60 × [[Silver]] + 45 × [[Gold]] + 40 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]]

## Additional Information

The Stellar Extractor Room allows the automatic production of materials based on the [[System colours|classification]] of the current [[Star system]]:
- Yellow Stars yield [[Chromatic Metal]]
- Red/Orange Stars yield [[Nitrogen]]
- Green Stars yield [[Sulphurine]]
- Blue Stars yield [[Radon]]
- [[System colours|Purple Stars]] yield [[Methane]]
- Additionally, if any of these materials are within the freighter's cargo containers, any produced material will be automatically deposited into the present stacks upon jumping the Freighter to another Star System.
