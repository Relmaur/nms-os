# Fossil Display

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Decorative Component
- **Added in:** Relics

**Fossil Display** is a base building component.

## Summary

**Fossil Display** is a [[Base building]] decorative structure used to reassemble and showcase [[Ancient Bones]] excavated from [[Fossil Bed|Fossil Beds]]. Fossil Displays allow Travellers to arrange bones into complete animal forms for display within their bases.

A Fossil Display can be deployed on any planetary surface or inside a [[Freighter Base]].

## Game description

A display stand designed to showcase the recovered remains of ancient lifeforms. Once placed, ancient bones can be arranged to re-create countless animal forms. A complete fossil requires a skull, a ribcage, two sets of limbs, and a tail.

Create a museum of assembled fossils at your base, or package your exhibits for trade on the open market, or with other Travellers. Find alien [[Fossil Collector|Fossil Collectors]] to trade with aboard [[Space Station|Space Stations]] or at planetary [[Palaeontological Dig|Palaeontological Digs]].

## Additional Information

- The blueprint is learned from a [[Construction Research Station]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]] after starting the [[Collecting Fossils]] mission.
