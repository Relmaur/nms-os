# Frostwort

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Plantable Seed
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Frostwort** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

## Summary

**Frostwort** is a wild plant found naturally only on icy planets and seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], or purchased from the Construction Research Station in the [[Space Anomaly]] for three [[Salvaged Data]]. Harvesting requires [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet|Haz-Mat Gauntlets]].

## Game description

A large crystal flower with radiant blue blooms, found only in [[Biome - Frozen|snowy]] biomes. Frostwort can be farmed and makes a reliable crop, and its adaptation to harsh conditions results in highly frequent harvests.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] indoor planting. Ensure Frozen climate before planting outdoors.

Approximate growing time: 1 hour

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 25 × [[Dioxite]] → 1 × Frostwort

## Additional information

- Growing the plant will produce 50-100 [[Frost Crystal]] in roughly 1 hour of real time.
- With [[Optical Drill]] installed on the [[Multi-tool]] and planted in a [[Bio-Dome]], each plant will produce 81 [[Frost Crystal|Frost Crystals]].
