# Race Force Amplifier

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 10
- **Type:** Race Part
- **Added in:** Origins

**Race Force Amplifier** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Race Force Amplifier** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that can be used when constructing a [[racetrack]].

It provides a temporary speed boost when driven over.

The blueprint can be acquired for 10 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Game description

Provides a boost to your [[Exocraft]] during races.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 2 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] → 1 × Race Force Amplifier
