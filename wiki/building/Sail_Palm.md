# Sail Palm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Organic decoration
- **Value:** 500
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Sail Palm** is a base-building component.

## Summary

**Sail Palm** is an item used in base building. It is a tall tree.

## Game description

A large-leafed, heat resistant tree.

Its broad frons are laced with minerals, forming a protective barrier against the harsh sunlight of its native conditions.
