# Gas Extractor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Value:** 1
- **Type:** Power Distribution Module
- **Power:** 50
- **Added in:** Relics

**Gas Extractor** is a base building product.

## Summary

**Gas Extractor** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that can be used to automatically harvest atmospheric gases.

## Game description

An automated gas harvester. Identify a [[Concentrated Gas Cloud]] site with the [[Survey Device|Surveying]] upgrade for the [[Analysis Visor]]. Gas extraction rates and produced gases will vary from site to site.

Requires [[power]] to function. Connect to [[Supply Depot|Supply Depots]] with [[Supply Pipe|Supply Pipes]] to allow collection from more convenient sites.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be obtained from the [[Construction Research Station]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]], costing 10 [[Salvaged Data]] to unlock.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Metal Plating]] + 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Gas Extractor

## Additional information

Available gases for extraction vary from planet to planet and biome to biome.
- [[Nitrogen]]
- [[Oxygen]]
- [[Radon]]
- [[Sulphurine]]
- [[Methane]]
