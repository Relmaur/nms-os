# Self-Repairing Heridium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Construction)
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Self-Repairing Heridium** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Self-Repairing Heridium** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A hopper of self-repairing Heridium. It looks fresh.

Used in the creation of scientific components. Self-repairing heridium is particularly useful for deployment in remote facilities, where maintenance opportunities may be limited or deadly.

## Economic Demand

The base value for Self-Repairing Heridium is 6,000.0  Units.

Self-Repairing Heridium is in high availability (lower buy value) in Advanced Materials (Purple) economy systems that are marked with a DNA Symbol :
- Material Fusion
- Metal Processing
- Processing
- Alchemical

Self-Repairing Heridium is in high demand (higher sell value) in Scientific (Dark Blue) economy systems that are marked with a beaker Symbol :
- Experimental
- Research
- Mathematical
- Scientific

## Source

Self-Repairing Heridium can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Advanced Materials Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Self-Repairing Heridium can be sold for a profit in any [[Scientific Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
