# Echinocactus

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Base Building
- **Type:** Plantable Seed
- **Added in:** Origins

**Echinocactus** is a [[farming|farmable]] agricultural [[product]].

## Summary

**Echinocactus** is a drought-resistant succulent, whose seeds can be acquired from a hired [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], or purchased from the Construction Research Station in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 3 [[Salvaged Data]].

## Game description

Native to [[Biome - Barren|barren]] biomes and capable of withstanding long periods of drought, Echinocactus is an edible succulent that responds well to cultivation, yielding a large but occasional harvest.

Suitable for [[Hydroponic Tray|hydroponic]] indoor planting. Ensure Barren climate before planting outdoors.

Approximate growing time: 16 hours

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Cactus Flesh]] + 25 × [[Pyrite]] → 1 × Echinocactus

## Additional information

- [[Farming]] the plant will produce 100 [[Cactus Flesh]] in roughly 16 hours of real-world time.
- The original name for Echinocactus, *Cucrassula*, likely comes from the real-world [Crassula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crassula) genus of succulent plants.
