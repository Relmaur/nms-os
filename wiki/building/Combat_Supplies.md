# Combat Supplies

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Added in:** Visions

**Combat Supplies** is a container.

## Summary

**Combat Supplies** is a container dropped by different types of [[sentinel|sentinels]] after their destruction and its type depends on the type of sentinel destroyed.

It needs to be destroyed by [[multi-tool]] or [[starship]] weaponry and it may recharge the player's [[Exosuit|shield]].
