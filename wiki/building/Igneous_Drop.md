# Igneous Drop

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Customisation Part
- **Type:** Organic decoration
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Igneous Drop** is a customisation part.

## Summary

**Igneous Drop** is a customisation part available from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Game description

This hybrid of egg and rock is known to form around the larvae of certain species, keeping the vulnerable creature warm and protected as it develops over the course of several decades. Membranous splits in the rock surface expose the ever-shifting molten heart.

Provides decorative landscaping opportunities for bases.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Igneous Drop

Blueprint can be purchased from the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion for 500 [[Quicksilver]].

## Additional information

- The info panel for this object in the catalog shows it costs 500 [[Salvaged Data]] modules, however the blueprint can only actually be obtained using Quicksilver at the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion and not using the [[Construction Research Unit]].
