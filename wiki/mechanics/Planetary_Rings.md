# Planetary Rings

**Planetary Rings** are a planetary feature.

## Summary

**Rings** are objects that surround certain planets in No Man's Sky.

[[Space station|Space stations]] and [[moon|moons]] may appear in them and NPCs can fly through them.

## Appearance

You can see a planet's rings from the surface and they range in size and width. [[Asteroid|Asteroids]] spawn within the rings, but they are very hard to collect because of the low visibility within rings.

## Additional Informnation

[[Star system|Star systems]] with [[Giant planet|Giant planets]] will never have rings.
