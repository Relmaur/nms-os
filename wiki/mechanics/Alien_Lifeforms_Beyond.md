# Alien Lifeforms (Beyond)

**Alien Lifeforms** is an information panel.

## Summary

**Alien Lifeforms** is a display page referenced by the [[Game Content]] page.

## Faction & Standing

- [[Faction|Standing]] in a measure of your [[Faction|reputation]] with alien races and factions.
- Track your standing on the **Milestones** page of the options menu (Key ESC).
- The [[Mission Board]] is the most efficient way of raising standing.
- Missions reward standing with the system's dominant lifeforms or one of the three [[guild|guilds]]: the [[Merchants Guild]], the [[Explorers Guild]] and the [[Mercenaries Guild]].
- [[Faction|Offering gifts]] to aliens on space stations or planetary outposts is a great way to increase your standing.
- Different lifeforms prefer different gifts.
- Higher standing with the Guilds grants access to [[[[Faction|higher level missions with improved rewards]].
- Hunting and destroying pirate [[Pirate|bounties]] will increase your standing with the system's dominant lifeforms.

## The Mission Board

- The [[Mission Board]] in found in every [[Space Station]]. Always check in at the mission board in each station to see what jobs are alvaible.
- Jobs reward units, nanites, pieces of technology and more.
- The Mission Board is found near the [[Galactic trade terminal|Trade Terminal]] on the right-hand side of the station.
- Multiple missions can be active at once. View them in the Log (Key ESC).
- Missions reward [[Faction|Standing]] with their faction. They are a good way to improve your relationship with the system's dominant lifeforms, or one of three guilds: the [[Merchants Guild]], the [[Explorers Guild]] and the [[Mercenaries Guild]].
- Higher standing with the Guilds grants access to higher level missions with improved rewards. Visit the [[Guild Envoy]] aboard the space atations for access to rewards.

## Learning Words

- Alien lifeforms encountered on [[planets]] and [[Space Station|Space Stations]] speak in a variety of languages, none of which are initially understood.
- Words can be deciphered through a variety of means.
- [[Automatic translation device|Automatic translation devices]] allow the realtime translation of a limited number of words. The plans to create translation devices can be learned from the [[Space Anomaly|Exosuit Research Specialist]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].
- Words can be learned via encounters with alien lifeforms. Making friendly [[Space station|request to the lifeforms]] upon the space station will often result in [[Space station|learning new words permanently]].
- Words may also be learned from , such as [[Plaque|Plaques]], [[Ruin|Ruins]] and [[Knowledge Stone|Knowledge Stones]].
- Wander planet surfaces and use the [[Analysis Visor]] to locale [[Knowledge Stone|Knowledge Stones]]. Larger sites can be found the same way, but can also be spotted from the air or pinpointed via a map from the [[Cartographer|Space Station Cartographer]].
