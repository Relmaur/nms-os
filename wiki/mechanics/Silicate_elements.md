# Silicate elements

**Silicate elements** are one of the five divisions of the periodic table in [[No Man's Sky]]

## Summary

Silicate elements can be combined with other elements, such as [[Plutonium]], for [[crafting]] purposes.

All elements in the Silicate family have an icon with a blue background when shown in the players inventory, or on the No Man's Sky periodic table.

When a [[Scanner|scan]] is taken of an area, locations with Silicate elements are tagged with a blue diamond shaped icon with a white flask in the centre ().

## Mining

These elements can be found in rock formations, caves, and certain types of flora. At least one member of this family of Elements ([[Heridium]]) can be scavenged from destroyed [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

## Members

Known members of the  family are [[Heridium]] (Hr), [[Platinum]] (Pt), and [[Chrysonite]] (Ch).
