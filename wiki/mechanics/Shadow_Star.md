# Shadow Star

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Star systems
- **Description:** Partially Inaccessible Stars
- **Added in:** Cosmos

**Shadow stars** are partially inaccessible stars.

## Summary

Unlike [[phantom star|phantom stars]], **shadow stars** are a unique anomaly of a region and are accessible through normal gameplay. They describe the first unreachable star system of the portal network after a sequence of IDs which is not hidden from the travellers and accessible via hyperjump. Since there are two portal sequences, the base and the purple sequence, there are two **shadow stars**.
This misalignment between the portal network and system count may be related to the star systems denial of any 0 addresses, which prompts the portal network to also enforce a start at 1 and autocorrect the 0 to a 1. However, the star systems just start at 1 and go through their entire iteration, effectively ending one ID later than the portal network that ignored one of their own entries, the 0.

### Tracking the regular Shadow Star

With the addition of the purple star systems in the [[Autophage]] questline, both the measurement of a region's standard size as well as the identification of their **Shadow Star** has become trivial.

To find the normal Shadow Star all that is needed is to:
- Enter the region
- Record the regional coordinates
- Replace the entrance system coordinates with system 3E8
- Go to orbit and attempt to jump out of the system
  - Due to the [[Reverse Shadow Star]] being an eligible navigation system it defaults to the prior system in line, which is the Shadow Star of the region
- Jump to a nearby system and back to the fake exit system to discover the Shadow Star officially

This method was pioneered by the [[Forgotten Colonies]].

Prior to this finding a **Shadow Star** was an excruciating task as it could only be found by hand. This meant that a **Shadow Star** inside of the biggest known regions was the most rare occurence. Nowadays finding the **Shadow Star** has the same difficulty, regardless of the size of a region.

### Tracking the purple Shadow Star

After the purple drive has been unlocked and the systems have become manifest, the only way to track the purple **shadow star** is too visit all of them. Unlike the ancient method of identifying the regular **shadow star** with only 65 potential purple targets, this is a much more reasonable endeavour.

## Additional information

The initial discovery of the **shadow star** anomaly was done in three parts. First the [[Sokoli Mass]] was proposed as the smallest known region by [DHarhanWulf](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/User:DHarhanWulf|), after which [CorvusCorax668](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/User:CorvusCorax668|) used a discovery software to confirm the claim from afar - due to it being in the Hilbert Dimension. After this led to contradictory results, [Thamalandis](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/User:Thamalandis|) of the Forgotten Colonies started a galaxy dive expedition to find out the truth.

The anomaly was found and physically confirmed as of the 28th May 2021 as [[Delta Majoris Hilbert Umbra]].
