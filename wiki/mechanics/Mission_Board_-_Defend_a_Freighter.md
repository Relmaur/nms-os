# Mission Board - Defend a Freighter

**Mission Board - Defend a Freighter** is a type of mission board.

## Summary

**Mission Board - Defend a Freighter** is a type of [[Mission Board|mission board]].

## Walkthrough

After accepting the mission, you'll be given a location in space where the [[freighter]] is located. Use your [[starship]] to travel to this location.

Once you arrive at the location, you'll find the freighter under attack by a group of [[pirate]] starships. Your task is to destroy these pirate. While engaging the enemy, be careful not to hit the freighter with the weapons, or the freighter's turret will turn against you.

After all the pirate starships has been destroyed, the mission will be complete. You can return to any Mission Board to gain reward.

## Additional information

- The difficulty and number of enemy starships can vary depending on the mission's rank. Higher rank missions meaning more enemy will spawn.

- In the [[Echoes]] update, the normal freighter can be destroyed, result in mission failure and cannot complete.

If this situation is happened, you need to travel to the Mission Board, abandon this mission (or open the log and abandon them) and restart them. When abandon this mission, all the mission will not updated, meaning this mission is restartable.
