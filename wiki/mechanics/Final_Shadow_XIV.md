# Final Shadow XIV

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Pistol
- **Slots:** 0
- **Platforms:** PS4
- **Added in:** Release

**Final Shadow XIV** is a multi-tool.

## Summary

Provided as a pre-order upgrade bonus, the **Final Shadow XIV** [[Multi-tool]] comes with the plasma upgrade pre-installed, helping the player to "stay safe and mine resources faster as you explore the infinite unknown frontier."

It is orange and silver in color, with a black handgrip.

## Acquirement

Final Shadow XIV is an exclusive Multi-Tool available only for players who pre-ordered [[No Man's Sky]] on Playstation 4 from Amazon and GameStop.

## Additional information

In the pre-game fliers released in the United States, this tool was originally referred to as the **Rezosu AZ65** or the **Rezosu Z65**.
