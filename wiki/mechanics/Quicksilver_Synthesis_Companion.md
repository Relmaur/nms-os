# Quicksilver Synthesis Companion

**Quicksilver Synthesis Companion** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Quicksilver Synthesis Companion** is a merchant NPC, who resides in the [[Space Anomaly]], and offers exotic items in exchange for [[Quicksilver]]. The Quicksilver Synthesis Companion has its own stall on the Space Anomaly, found near the Expedition Terminal, next to the ramp leading to the upper level, to the right of [[the Nexus]].

This NPC is also colloquially referred as the **Q.S.C.** or by the nickname "Quincy" (by players, not in-game text).

In the [[Appearance Modifier]], the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion is also available as a customisation option, under the name **Iteration X17**, found under the "Traveller" species.

[[Specialist Polo]] made the following comment about its creation: *A being fashioned from metal and soul, by my own paws!* This seems to imply that that the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion is actually a [[Traveller]] [[Unknown Grave|soul]] inserted into a metal shell.

## Interactions

- Create exotic items from Quicksilver (These items cannot be obtained elsewhere)
  - This includes [[Build Menu|base building]] ornaments, emotes/gestures, customisation pieces for the players and starships, [[ByteBeat Catalogue|ByteBeat]] music tracks, [[Player Titles]] and fireworks
- Purchase a [[Void Egg]] to begin the [[Starbirth]] quest, which eventually gives a [[Living Ship (Starship)|Living Ship]] at the end (This is the only way to start this mission)
- Collect [[List of Expeditions|expedition]] rewards
  - Free to purchase. Once unlocked, they will be available on all saves made on a given account.
- Collect [[Twitch Drops|Twitch rewards]]
  - Varies depending on Twitch Campaign. Can include special [[Multi-Tool|Multi-Tools]], [[Starship|Starships]], player and starship customisation pieces, base building ornaments, emotes/gestures and [[Companion Pets|Companions]]

## Additional Information

- The Quicksilver items were extracted from the 3.75 version of the **PURCHASEABLESPECIALS.MBIN** game file.
- The table lists the versions when the items first appeared in that file, not when they were actually accessible in game.
- Whenever the player purchases something from the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion, it will say "Collectible received", in a unique voice that is not heard anywhere else in the game.
