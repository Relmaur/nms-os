# Multi-Tool Decommissioning Terminal

**Multi-Tool Decommissioning Terminal** is a type of terminal.

## Summary

**Multi-Tool Decommissioning Terminal** is a computer terminal found on the [[Space Anomaly]] next to [[Iteration: Tethys]].

It can be used to scrap [[multi-tool|multi-tools]] for units and [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]].

## Scrapping a multi-tool

Multi-tools can be scrapped at the terminal by selecting the option to do so. All upgrades are lost, as is the multi-tool. This cannot be undone in the same game session (although if a savegame prior to tool being scrapped is still available, it can be loaded to "undo" multi-tool scrapping). The value in units of the multi-tool as scrap will be shown before you confirm the option. You must confirm the choice twice, and the value will be added to your inventory in the form of units and upgrade modules. The quality of modules received and their value depends in part on the value and class of the multi-tool.

Damaged technology slots **reduces** the value of the scrap received from a multi-tool.

The upgrade modules can be sold to any [[Technology Merchant]] for  Nanites, or installed and then dismantled to sell their parts for  Units. You must have enough free inventory slots in your [[Exosuit]] to be able to hold all the items you will receive or you cannot scrap a tool. Upon completion of scrapping, another multi-tool from your pool will be selected.
