# Iteration: Selene

**Iteration: Selene** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Selene** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

Selene's head has an elongated shape like a horse or a lizard, they frequently stick out their forked tongue indicating a reptilian heritage, specifically from a warm climate considering their clothing and the all around look of their head.

Facing the Nexus from where you park your ship, head to the left side of the Anomaly, and then go all the way to the back. There are several vendors here — Iteration: Selene is in the back left.

## Interactions

Selene sells [[Exosuit]] upgrade blueprints, such as the [[Neural Stimulator]].

The metal tube next to them also offers frequent exosuit slot upgrades.

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Selene Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Selene may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, this can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
