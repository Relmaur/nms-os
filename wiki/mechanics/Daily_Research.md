# Daily Research

** Daily Research** is a mission type.

## Summary

**Daily Research** missions are introduced as part of the [[Community Research]].

During [[Community Research (mission 9)]] the player can only choose one type of mission per day and rewarded with 250 Quicksilver on completion (10,500 Quicksilver in total).

The variant 1 type missions are available on the same day, while the variant 2 type missions are available on the following day.
