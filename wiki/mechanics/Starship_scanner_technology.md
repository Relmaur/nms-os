# Starship scanner technology

This page is an overview of starship scanners.

## Summary

The Starship comes with Scanner Technology installed that can determine the qualities of a system or planet. The scanner can be upgraded to scan the wealth or [[Conflict Level]] of other systems that the player is not currently located within.
