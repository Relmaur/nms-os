# Iteration: Hyperion

**Iteration: Hyperion** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Hyperion** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Interactions

Hyperion sells [[Starship]] upgrade blueprints on the [[Space Anomaly]] for  Nanites. This might include, for example, blueprints for the [[Cadmium Drive]], [[Emeril Drive]], and [[Indium Drive]].

Starship upgrade tree

## Additional Information

A [[Iteration: Hyperion Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Hyperion may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
