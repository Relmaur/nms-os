# Iteration: Hesperus

**Iteration: Hesperus** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Hesperus** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

Hesperus appears to be an owl-like creature, their head possessing many feathers which form a beard-like structure along with a large eyebrow. They also wear shaded goggles which might be for protection from certain types of high energy light considering their avian heritage.

## Interactions

Hesperus is Gemini's best friend, they both will always greet the player in a friendly matter if spoken to.

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Hesperus Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Hesperus may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
