# Community Research

**Community Research** is a community driven event.

## Summary

**Community Research** is a community driven event that consists of a series of missions. Its purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo]] determine how the universe changed recently.

Polo contacts the player and asks for their help with research. Of course, due to their Gek nature it is not simply a favor, but a trade business, and rewards every bit of research data with [[Quicksilver]].

This currency can be spent at [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]], a construct created by Polo to help them explore synthesis and creation, transforming Quicksilver into exotic goods.
