# Planetary Settlement - Population

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Description:** Population Guide
- **Added in:** Relics

**Planetary Settlement - Population** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - Population** contains a detailed description of the characteristics of the population aspect of a [[Planetary Settlement]].

The population count is a record of the number citizens in a planetary settlement, up to the maximum of 200.

By building more residences and accepting new people in the settlement, it is possible to increase its population.
