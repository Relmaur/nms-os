# Price Catalogue - Multi-Tools

**Price Catalogue - Multi-Tools** lists the possible prices for Multi-tools.

## Summary

**Price Catalogue - Multi-Tools** consist of several tables that indicate the price of [[Multi-tool|Multi-tools]] according to archetype and slots count.

Be aware that all possibilities do not happen in every system.

The prices are in thousands of [[units]].
