# Planetary Settlement - New Arrivals

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Description:** New Arrivals
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Planetary Settlement - New Arrivals** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - New Arrivals** contains a detailed description of opportunities presented by new arrivals to the settlement. The Settlement Overseer must oversee a decision on the fate of each new arrival at the [[Planetary Settlement|Settlement Administration Terminal]] in a [[Planetary Settlement]].

Meeting strangers usually result in gaining a positive or negative feature.
