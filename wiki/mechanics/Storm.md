# Storm

**Storms** are a type of game event.

## Summary

**Storms** are planetary weather phenomenon that will drain the [[Hazard Protection]], some planets will have **extreme storms** that will consume more of the Hazard Protection.

Storms usually last about 5 minutes. In those 5 minutes you must seek shelter as soon as possible if you do not have [[Sodium]], [[Sodium Nitrate]] or [[Ion Battery]] in your inventory.

## Storm Types

All storm types are listed below:
- Blizzard
- Firestorm
- Radioactive Supercell
- Toxic Rainstorm

## Storm Events

These events can occur during storms:
- Extreme Wind Event (Tornadoes)
- Electrical Disturbance (Lightning)

## Storm Benefits

Certain storm types can provide benefits to travellers brave enough to venture out during one.
- Blizzard - Increased [[Mining Beam]] heat dispersion
- Firestorm - Increased [[Jetpack]] duration
- Radioactive Supercell - Increased [[Terrain Manipulator]] yields
- Toxic Rainstorm - Increased sprint duration

## Avoiding Storms

To avoid storms, you must take shelter. You can hide in caves, your [[starship]], or any structure. Generally, if there's a roof overhead, the storm will be avoided. Hiding in the [[Minotaur]] can also restore the Hazard Protection.

## Storm Crystals

[[Storm Crystal|Storm Crystals]] are a valuable item that can be harvested on planets with extreme storms. During an extreme storm, Storm Crystals will appear as a white crystal with the underside hidden in the rock. To harvest a Storm Crystal, walk up to it and hold  to add it to your [[exosuit]] inventory.

If no storm is active, storm crystals will exist as a non-harvestable rock. They can't be mined with a mining beam in this state. It can be destroyed with the [[Geology Cannon]], however. Doing this will not harvest the crystal.

## Gravitional Anomaly

A gravitational Anomaly is a form of storm, which occurs on a planetary scale, with a frequency comparable to any other type of storm, depending on the conditions of the world in question.

During the anomaly, the planet's gravity becomes much weaker, allowing for larger [[Jetpack]] jumps. This is accompanied by the occasional appearance of glowing purple orbs, rising from the ground and rising into the air before disappearing.

Unlike other types of storms, they do not pose any particular danger. However, returning to normal gravity at the end of the storm can be a potential risk if the player is in the air at the time and is unable to cushion the fall (jetpack and/or shield not sufficiently improved).

For a gravitational anomaly to occur on a given planet, the following conditions must be met:

- The planet must be [[Dissonant Planet|dissonant]].
- The planet must have a [[Biome - Lush|lush biome]] or an [[Biome - Exotic|anomaly biome]].
- There must be storms, more or less frequent. Planets with [[Extreme Planet|extreme climate]] will have more frequent gravitational anomalies.

## Trivia

- Of all the boons provided during storms, the Sprint bonus from toxic weather makes the least sense, as increased strain on air filtration would in fact make breathing harder from exercise into a luxury.
- More perplexing is the justification of ionizing radiation somehow making digging up terrain easier. That boon is also the least useful as recharging the Terrain Manipulator with mined Silicate is already fairly generous.
