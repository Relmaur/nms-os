# Expedition Terminus

## Expedition Terminus

The **Expedition Terminus** is a terminal that allows players to begin an [[Game mode|Expedition]] with an existing normal save. This is also where they can pause an expedition and resume their normal save. This terminal was introduced in the [[Update 4.50|4.50 Omega]] update.

When all expedition objectives are completed, a player can end their expedition to transform their save into a normal save.

**Note**: You will not obtain rewards for optional objectives such as [[Community Research|Community Objectives]] when finishing an expedition **before they are completed**. However, you can still buy the community research milestone rewards from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] when they are unlocked.

### If the expedition was started from the Expedition Terminus

Finishing the expedition will unlock the storage slots for transferring inventory from expedition mode to the normal save. This allows the transfer of up to 8 stacks of tech items and 24 stacks of general inventory items at a time.

If you want to transfer more than fits into the slots, you can:
1. fill the slots,
1. save (e.g. by entering then exiting your ship),
1. return to your main save *via the Options menu*,
1. collect the items (and do whatever else you want in your normal save), then
1. return to expedition mode via the terminal.

You can repeat this process as often as needed.

**Using the "End Expedition" button is final.** You will not be able to return to expedition mode without starting a new expedition.
