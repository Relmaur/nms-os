# Iteration: Perses

**Iteration: Perses** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Perses** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]]. They sell [[Exocraft]] upgrade blueprints.

## Interactions

Perses sells [[Exocraft]] upgrade blueprints on the [[Space Anomaly]] for  Nanites.

Exocraft upgrade tree<br>(Sentinel Update)

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Perses Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Perses may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
