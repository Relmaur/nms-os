# Exocraft Research Terminal

**Exocraft Research Terminal** is a research terminal aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Summary

**Exocraft Research Terminal** is a terminal used to unlock [[Exocraft]] upgrade blueprints in exchange for [[Salvaged Data]]. It is located aboard the [[Space Anomaly]], alongside other research terminals such as the [[Starship Research Terminal]], [[Exosuit Research Terminal]], and [[Multitool Research Terminal]].

The terminal provides blueprints for various Exocraft technologies that enhance mobility, scanning, defense, and mining capabilities. The technologies are organized in a tech tree format, with some upgrades requiring prerequisites to unlock.

Available categories include:
- Engine and Acceleration Modules
- Radar and Signal Booster Systems
- Mining Lasers
- Weapon Systems (e.g. Mounted Cannon)
- Utility Upgrades (e.g. Exocraft Summoning Station)

## Differences from NEXT

Prior to the [[Beyond]] update, Exocraft upgrades were typically obtained through Technician NPC missions at planetary bases. With the Exocraft Research Terminal, these upgrades became more centralized and accessible via Salvaged Data, allowing for clearer progression and availability.

## Additional information

While some Exocraft upgrades—like the [[Exocraft Signal Booster]] or [[Exocraft Acceleration Module]]—may still appear from missions or planetary vendors, acquiring them through this terminal provides guaranteed access. Total Salvaged Data cost varies depending on the desired upgrade path and number of Exocraft types unlocked.

## Release History
