# Starship Catalogue - Interceptor

## Summary

The following is a list of various Interceptor [[starship|starships]] discovered by different players during the Echoes through current eras.

(For a list of all ship types, refer to the [[Starship Catalogue]])

## Description

These Sentinel ships have their own set of unique base technologies, but share upgrades with normal starships.

- [[Aeron Shield]] - equivalent of [[Shield|Shields]]
- [[Anti-Gravity Well]] - equivalent of [[Launch Thruster|Launch Thrusters]]
- [[Crimson Core]] - equivalent of [[Hyperdrive]]
- [[Luminance Engine]] - equivalent of [[Pulse Engine]]
- [[Sentinel Cannon]] - equivalent of [[Photon Cannon]]

## Inventory

The inventory for Interceptor Ships are 32-40 Cargo and 22-28 Tech.
