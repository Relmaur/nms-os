# Engineering Control

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Derelict Freighter
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** provides Salvaged Fleet Unit, Cargo Bulkhead, Nanites
- **Added in:** Desolation

**Engineering Control** is a terminal.

## Summary

**Engineering Control** is a terminal found on [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]]. It gives the player a choice of fabricating a [[Salvaged Fleet Unit]], a [[Cargo Bulkhead]], or [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]] (500-700). It is located in the final room of a derelict, after the [[Records Terminal]] and the [[Secure Mainframe]].

## Rewards

While the amount of  NanitesNanites received is random, the Salvaged Fleet Unit is always the same class (C, B, A, S). Also, all derelicts will yield one Cargo Bulkhead. Only one choice can be given, as the terminal will be drained of power after it is finished.
