# Dangerous Creature

A **dangerous creature** is a classification of creature.

## Summary

**Dangerous creatures** are a classification of [[creature]]. Any type of [[fauna]] could be considered a dangerous creature.

To be considered dangerous, a creature must be hostile towards players without provocation. Not all carnivores are considered dangerous creatures.
