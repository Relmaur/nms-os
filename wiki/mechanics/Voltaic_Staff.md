# Voltaic Staff

**Voltaic Staff** (a.k.a. "Autophage Staff") are a special Multi-Tool.

## Summary

A **Voltaic Staff** is a class of [[Multi-tool]] associated with the robotic species [[Autophage|Autophages]]. These Multi-tools work similarly to other Multi-tools, but have a staff shape and have the particularity of being custom-made by assembling the parts.

## Description

These weapons have a traditional importance among the Autophages. They are made up of three rooms:

- The head (or voltaic component): constitutes the upper part of the staff.
- The heart: constitutes the intermediate part of the staff.
- The structure: constitutes the handle of the pole.

All of these parts can be purchased from [[Autophage Camp]] via the [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal]], using [[Void Mote|Void Motes]], and then assembled through the Autophage Synthesis Terminal. A terminal never offers all the existing parts, so it may be necessary to visit several to find specific parts.

They are the same height as the player, these remain visible in third-person view even when not in use (this does not apply to the first-person view). Because of this, other players can see each other's Voltaic Staff in the [[Space Anomaly]] if they have one, but cannot be used in that location.

## Features

Voltaic Staves are assembled with three pieces from each of the three categories, namely a head, a heart, and a structure. The difference between these components is purely visual, and they can be mixed in any way they want.

Void Motes are required to purchase individual parts before assembly (with the exception of three specific parts). The assembly is done via the Autophage Synthesis Terminal after that.

### Assembly

The assembly makes it possible to create the staff from the three pieces chosen. The staff can be previewed before adding it to the multi-tool collection, and until it has been claimed, the parts remain in the [[inventory]].

The color of the staff is not selected by the player, however: each terminal chooses a unique color combination associated with each head, and the color defined by the stick head is applied to all the pieces. If you are not satisfied with the color of a staff, simply go to another terminal (including the same [[planet]]), which will offer a different color for the same combination of pieces. The class is also determined by the terminal, which can range from C to S.

## Additional Information

- All crafted Voltaic Staves have a [[Polyphonic Core]] pre-installed as standard.
