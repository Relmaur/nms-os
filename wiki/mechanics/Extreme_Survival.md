# Extreme Survival

**Extreme Survival** is one of the Journey Milestones.

## Summary

In order to achieve any of the Extreme Survival [[Journey Milestone|milestones]], the player must be on a [[planet]] with extreme conditions. Any planet where the [[weather]] status is displayed in red font counts as an extreme planet. They are also distinguished by the word "Extreme" appearing above the [[Hazard Protection]] bar. Additionally, the planet must drain your hazard protection. Extreme lush planets often have normal conditions during clear weather. These periods do not count towards the milestone.

## How to achieve

The best known method to achieve this milestone to the maximum tier (48 Sols) with the least amount of effort is to sit in any land [[exocraft]] except for the minotaur to achieve the maximum tier. If the exocraft hazard protection upgrades are installed, you don't even need to recharge your hazard protection.

You cannot just let the game run in the background to achieve this milestone while you're in singleplayer. Technically, the game runs, but most of the features and functionalities stop if no other players are in the system. This means that you will need to be tabbed into the game to achieve progress towards the Sol counter for the Milestone.

Extreme worlds are more common in Green Star Systems (which require an [[Emeril Drive]] to be accessed).

## Additional Information

As of the [[Update 4.00]] this milestone does not show Sols anymore, but minutes on the milestone itself. The counter counts up in in-game minutes rather than in real-time minutes. One in-game minute equals to one real-time second. This equates to 12 hours of real-time played.

You do not need to be in the [[Game mode|Survival]] game mode in order to achieve the Steam milestones that reference Extreme Survival.

It might be a good idea to bring extra [[resource|resources]] to recharge [[Exosuit]] hazard protection. Alternatively, you can use the Roamer or other exocraft, or Terrain Manipulator to burrow just under the surface of an extreme planet, just far enough to escape the effects of the weather, and stop your oxygen from depleting. Stay in the hazardous weather long enough to drop your red hazard protection bar below 100%, then head back underground. Your hazard protection will not recharge automatically while under the surface, so you will continue to earn time towards your survival milestones, even after the storm passes. This method is boring but will not require resources for recharging.
