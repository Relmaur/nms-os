# Hazard Protection Unit

**Hazard Protection Units** are exo-suit extensions.

## Summary

**Hazard Protection Units** are extensions for the players' [[exo-suit]] to survive longer in extreme environments. Unlike the [[Hazard Protection]], these do not recharge by themselves and need to be recharged with [[oxide elements|Oxide]] or with Shielding Products such as [[Shielding Shard]], [[Shielding Sheet]] and [[Shielding Plate]].
