# Egg Sequencer - Growth Hormone

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Genetic Sequencer
- **Description:** Growth Hormone
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Egg Sequencer - Growth Hormone** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Growth Hormone ** is a modification capability, used in the [[Egg Sequencer]] to modify the traits of a [[Companion Egg]].

A growth hormone, when applied to the egg sequencer can modify the potential companion creature size, either larger or smaller.  The items selected to be used in the growth hormone slot of the Egg Sequencer will result in different size adjustments.

## Additional Information

Offspring from unmodified eggs will gain or lose a multiple of 0.1 meters (enhancing growth and then reducing growth will prevent this). This mutliple is likely determined by the size of the parent. Eggs hatched from fauna that were 3.5m gained/lost 0.3m, 2.5m fauna gained/lost 0.2m, and 1.5m fauna gained/lost 0.1m. This can be used to create an offspring of a more exact desired size. This only applies to unmodified eggs/eggs that were not put through the egg sequencer, also all unmodified eggs from a specific parent will gain/lose the same amount.
