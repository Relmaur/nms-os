# Earth elements

**Earth elements** are one of the eight divisions of the [[resource|resources]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

**Earth elements** are commonly used for [[base building]], [[crafting]] various products and charging technologies.

Also [[glyph 3 / 16]], [[glyph 6 / 16]], [[glyph 9 / 16]], [[glyph 12 / 16]] and [[glyph 15 / 16]] need to be charged with these type of elements, before a [[portal]] could be activated.

## Sources

These elements can be found in [[flora]], [[mineral|minerals]], and [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]]. Can also be refined.
