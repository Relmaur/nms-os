# Special elements

**Special elements** are one of the eight divisions of the [[resource|resources]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

**Special elements** are mainly used for refinement, most of them directly or indirectly can be refined into [[Nanite Cluster]]. Chromatic Metal and Pugneum can be used to craft various technologies and products. Some were introduced to play a special role in [[Community Research]] missions.

## Sources

These elements can be obtained from various sources, some of them can purchased at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
