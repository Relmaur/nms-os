# Price Catalogue - Starships

**Price Catalogue - Starships** lists the possible prices for starships.

## Summary

**Price Catalogue - Starships** consist of several tables that indicate the price of [[starship|starships]] according to archetype and slots count.

Notes:
- all possibilities do not happen in every system
- analysis visor will not update your ship price after a slot augmentation until the next game session
- These tables were made for Normal mode, prices may vary on other difficulties

The prices are in thousands of [[units]].

## Calculation

The formulas defining the value of starships are not known from the game itself but can be derived from the data tables of the next sections.

Ship value is &lt;math>a\times S^b\times c&lt;/math> where S is the number of cargo slots and a, b and c are given in the table below. Then, under 3100k [[units]] the game rounds the value to the nearest 5k, between 3100 and 31000k the value is rounded to the nearest 50k and over 31000k to the nearest 500k.

*Scraping value is the rounded ship value times &lt;math>0.7\times (1 - D/S)&lt;/math> where D is the number of damaged cargo slots.*
