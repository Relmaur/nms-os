# Iteration: Cronus

**Iteration: Cronus** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Cronus** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]], and is the resident chef.

## Interactions

Cronus pays 0-300 Nanites for any food sample presented. Higher end foods tend to get higher scores. The table below lists his possible responses, where every blank is the name of the submitted food item. As of Worlds Part II, he now has a Food Market, where he requests specific food donations that change daily, along with a new standing system called "Esteem in the eyes of  Cronus," that is represented by a percentage value, which starts at zero and will incrementally increase the more the player donates.

Cronus has been described as a "temperamental food critic." One of their top-rated foods is [[Iced Screams]].
