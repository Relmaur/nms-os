# Found

All discoveries in No Man's Sky can be found using different methods.

## Summary

Most things in [[No Man's Sky]] can be found by the use of different [[Scan technology]], such as: [[Resource|Resources]], [[Fauna]], [[Damaged Machinery]], and [[Shelter|Shelters]].

Some things can be found visually that don't show up on scans such as [[Debris]] and containers that can be looted. [[Blueprint|Blueprints]] can be found randomly in buildings after solving puzzles or given as [[Terminal|quest]] and [[Journey Milestone]] rewards.

## Technology upgrades

Scan technology and upgrades are available for [[Multi-tool|Multi-tools]] and [[Exocraft|Exocrafts]]. [[Starship|Starships]] have built in scanners that cannot be upgraded.
