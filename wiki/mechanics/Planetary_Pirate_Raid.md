# Planetary Pirate Raid

**Planetary Pirate Raid** is a type of game event.

## Summary

**Planetary Pirate Raid** is a game event that can be indentified by a HUD alert:

*Pirate Attack Vector Detected // Nearby Structure Targeted*

Includes the alarm sound that can occur at any structure. Once it occurred, [[pirate|pirates]] will target the structure and shoot at it. The player can launch their ship into space to destroy the pirate ships. There can be up to 3 hostile ships. Once destroyed, you will receive a reward such as a [[Starshield Battery]] or increased [[Faction]] standing.

## How to find them

They can randomly appear near a structure. For example, if you stand on a [[Trade Outpost]] long enough and the alarm sounds, it indicates the arrival of a pirate raid.

There's no other way to call the raid. It happens more often in high conflict systems.

## Additional Information

- To save the structure, you must fight. However, you can keep a safe distance from the structure if you don't feel like fighting pirates.
- The pirate will not only attack the structure. Sometimes, when you do something but not in the structure, it will target and attack you instead.
- If you do nothing, the pirate attack will eventually stop.
