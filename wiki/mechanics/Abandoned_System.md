# Abandoned System

**Abandoned system** is a type of star system.

## Summary

**Abandoned systems** were once inhabited by sentient life, but an unknown event forced them to leave. It still has everything any inhabited [[star system|system]] has but without NPCs and few or no [[starship|starships]] of any kind.

It is NOT an [[Uncharted system]], although they are similar in some ways.

## Characteristics

Abandoned systems used to be inhabited by sentient life, but something forced them to either leave in a hurry or disappear from existence.

A heavily damaged [[Space Station]] is present, based on the original space station design. There is no [[Station Core]]. However, a room on the left ledge has a still-functioning [[Teleporter]] and on the right ledge a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] but with no market. On planets, waypoints like [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]] and [[Transmission Tower|Transmission Towers]] are still present and functional, but buildings are infested by [[Whispering Egg|Whispering Eggs]] and [[Biological Horror|Biological Horrors]]. Item crates and [[Buried Technology Module|Buried Technology Modules]] can still be found on planets and moons.

The Adrift expedition introduced Abandoned [[Game mode|Game Mode]], where all systems are Abandoned. New custom games can enable this mode. Except in Abandoned Game Mode, [[Pirate|Pirates]] can still attack you in Abandoned systems if you stay in space long enough. [[Space battle|Space battles]] can still occur if you warp here at certain times. NPC Ships will very rarely land at the space station.

## Where to find

Abandoned systems will only show up as (abandoned) once you visit them, otherwise they will look like ordinary systems in the [[Galactic Map]].

An [[Economy Scanner]] will still show a lifeform type and low economy rating but have "data unavailable" above the stats.

A [[Conflict Scanner]] will show conflict rating of "Not Available". The conflict filter may reveal that there is still conflict, though it will always be low.

## Additional Information

- "AbandonedSystemProbability" is a property related to the probability of which [[System colours|coloured system]] will be abandoned. The probabilities are as follows (GCSOLARGENERATIONGLOBALS.GLOBAL.MBIN WorldsTwo 5.53):

| Yellow | Red | Green | Blue | Purple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 0.1 | 0.1 | 0.35 |

- The presence of whispering eggs at buildings implies that biological horrors were responsible for driving out and/or killing the previous inhabitants. This is odd, as while certainly dangerous, biological horrors are quite easy to kill with the proper equipment, and are likely no match for an organized military force. This continues the trend of implying biological horrors are much, much stronger than they seem, or at least used to be. Other examples include the strange infestations found on certain derelict freighters. This is supported by the fact that abandoned space stations play the same alien sounds as derelicts.
