# Egg Sequencer - Dye Injector

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Genetic Sequencer
- **Description:** Dye Injector
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Egg Sequencer - Dye Injector** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Dye Injector ** is a modification capability, used in the [[Egg Sequencer]] to modify the traits of a [[Companion Egg]].

A Dye Injector, when applied to the egg sequencer can modify the color of your creature. All injectable items give different color and color schemes from that specific item, and are not random, but will be random on where it appears on that creature.

## Color Group by Genus

The columns below include all color outcomes, including the color outcomes for species that don't follow the standard coloring scheme.

<blockquote>**Color Group 1** Outcomes cover [[Mogara]], [[Diplo]], [[Felidae]], [[Theroma]], [[Tetraceris]], [[Reococcyx]], [[Felihex]], [[Bos]], and [[Tyranocae]], which are largely all creatures first introduced with the base game.

**Color Group 2** Outcomes cover [[Osteofelidae]]

**Color Group 3** Outcomes cover [[Rhopalocera]]

**Color Group 4** Outcomes cover [[Mechanoceris]]
</blockquote>
