# Cargo Drop

**Cargo Drop boxes** are abandoned objects that can be found on [[planet|planets]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

There are six types of Cargo Drop boxes that can be found in No Man's Sky. Colors and sizes differ, making them easy to identify. They can be found everywhere on a planet except for [[cave|caves]] and underwater. They can also be found scattered inside [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]], or neatly stacked in other rooms within certain [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] that requires an [[AtlasPass v2]].

## Cargo Drop (Damaged Container)

This box spawns in groups of 1-2. Once opened, will pop up a GUI with an option to either dismantle or transfer [[Rusted Metal]] to the player's inventory, which always comes in a quantity of 50-99. It will then open the box and will give the player a [[curiosity]] or [[consumable]]. Some of them are required to get a [[portal]] location from a [[monolith]].

This can also be built and placed in a [[base]] and is known as [[Crate Fabricator]].

## Jettison Pod

This cargo box is round and yellow, which can only spawn once per group. Opening it will give a random item from its loot table, but it also have an 8% chance of being empty. Effective for refueling [[Multi-tool]] equipment.

## Cargo Drop (barrel type)

This cargo box is shaped like a barrel, tall, a dark shade of red. It can spawn in groups of 1-2. It requires an [[AtlasPass v1]] to open. This can also be built and placed in a base and is known as [[Barrel Fabricator]].

| [[Navigation Data]] | 1 | 46% |

## Health Restorative

This cargo box is square and white and can spawn in groups of 1-2. Once opened, it will restore the player's health. Useful on Extreme hazard planets.

## Additional information

- It is possible to destroy these boxes with [[Plasma Launcher]] or [[Terrain Manipulator]].
- They can spawn in cave openings, but not in caves themselves or underwater.
