# Xbox Booster Pack

The **Xbox Booster Pack** is a pre-order setup for Xbox users.

## Summary

The **Xbox Next Generation Booster Pack** is a pre-order package offered to Xbox users prior to the release of No Man's Sky on the Xbox. There are two editions - each contains a special [[exo-suit]] and visor, a new [[multi-tool]], and 10,000  Units.

## Full Description

Pre-order No Man's Sky to receive the following items:

- X.O. Suit - Your suit is your protection and shield against the hazardous environments of the alien worlds you will discover. Stand out from your fellow travellers with the X.O. suit's unique visor cross as you trade, fight and survive together.
- [[Vertic-LX]] - On hostile planets, self defense and powerful offense is important. The Vertic-LX multi-tool comes with plasma upgrade pre-installed, helping you to stay safe and mine [[resource|resources]] faster as you explore the infinite unknown frontier.
- Starting Boost - Your journey will be filled with danger but 10,000 units will give you a head start in your race to the center of the universe. Use your units to buy faster ships, upgrade your weapons or purchase exotic resources.

## X.O. Suit

Despite being called a suit, the [X.O. suit](https://store-images.s-microsoft.com/image/apps.3707.68586748594120772.fde7f41d-2f83-4e2b-bb8f-95fa56cd1568.906d4908-50a2-4567-8617-735a161f103f?mode=scale&q=90&h=1080&w=1920&format=jpg) is actually just a helmet, this item can only be used in the customisation menu on the space station, the helmet takes up helmet slot number 14.

The X.O. suit is not commercially available anymore but can be obtained on console and PC by using save editors.

ID: SPEC_XOHELMET

## Additional information

The second booster pack contains the same X.O. Suit and Starting Boost, but features a different multi-tool, the [[Artios-VI]].

These items are no longer available officially, the only way to get them now is through a [save editor](https://github.com/goatfungus/NMSSaveEditor).
