# Life Pod

**Life Pod** is a starting starship.

## Summary

The **Life Pod** is a [[starship]] the player starts with at the beginning of a new game.

It has no [[hyperdrive]], but it may be possible that one can be added through an upgrade. The Life Pod acts as the basic cockpit of the starship. If it is destroyed, the player starts again with a new Life Pod.

## Death results

- If the player [[Death|dies]] in space, they will respawn on a [[space station]] with a new Life Pod.
- If the player dies on a [[planet]], they will respawn next to their current starship, minus any discoveries they had not yet reported at a planetary [[Beacon (Building)|beacon]].

## Additional information

- If the player pre-purchased [[No Man's Sky]] they can claim a [[Starship|pre-order starship]] as their starter ship, [[Horizon Omega]] on PC or [[Alpha Vector]] on PS4.
