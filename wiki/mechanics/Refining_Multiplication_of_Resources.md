# Refining Multiplication of Resources

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Guide
- **Description:** Resource Multiplication
- **Added in:** Fractal

**Refining Multiplication of Resources** is a guide. Refining is a process where you run different resources (or combinations of resources) through a [[Refiner]] to create new resources. Some of these refining processes can be combined with others to multiply existing stocks of resources, giving you more resources than what you initially had.

## Summary

This guide will provide instructions and steps for maximum production of resources without having to collect or farm them (where possible).

The objective is to obtain the maximum types and amounts of resources using only a [[Portable Refiner]], [[Medium Refiner]], [[Large Refiner]].

Some of these recipes use two or three ingredients, so a large refiner is required in most cases, but having another one- and two-input refiner nearby will help speed up some of the simpler processes. The more refiners that are available, the better, as that allows for multiple processes to be run in parallel.

## Starting Point

A basic collection of these resources is required to get started:

- [[Oxygen]]
- [[Nitrogen]]
- [[Di-hydrogen]]
- [[Pugneum]]

In addition, it is required to have at least 2 units each of [[Copper]], [[Cadmium]], [[Emeril]], [[Indium]], and [[Quartzite]] to use the [[Chromatic Metal]] multiplication process.

Once the process is set up, the only ongoing resources required are a continuous supply of Oxygen and Nitrogen. The former is readily obtainable from [[Oxygen Harvester|Oxygen Harvesters]] while the latter is readily obtainable from [[Atmosphere Harvester|Atmosphere Harvesters]] on [[Biome - Lush|Lush]] or [[Biome - Toxic|Toxic]] planets (or by refining the output from Atmosphere Harvesters on other planet types with Oxygen). Lastly, both are also obtainable from [[Gas Extractor|Gas Extractors]] above an associated [[Concentrated Gas Cloud|deposit]].

## Di-Hydrogen Cycling & Sourcing

Basic Principles: <br>
Crafting one unit of [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] requires 40 units of [[Di-hydrogen]], while *refining* it only requires 30 units of Di-hydrogen. And since Di-hydrogen Jelly breaks down into 40 units of Di-hydrogen in a refiner, this yields a net gain of 10 units of Di-hydrogen per unit of Jelly used in a two-part cycle. This is the only "reverse reaction" refining recipe that results in *more* of a given resource than the initial starting amount without needing additional resources (such as Oxygen) to maintain the cycle process.

While this theoretically infinite multiplication process sounds like an amazing boon on paper, it has two major drawbacks specific to refining Di-hydrogen Jelly from Di-hydrogen. The first drawback is that it is very time-consuming: it takes a full 60 seconds to produce just one unit of Di-hydrogen Jelly. The second drawback is that Di-hydrogen Jelly has a relatively small max stack size of 20, preventing large batches from being made in a single cycle. Technically speaking, the best results you can attain from a single refiner is a net of 200 units of Di-hydrogen per two-part cycle that takes approximately 22 minutes (approx. 545/hr). Scaling up production using multiple refiners running simultaneously can make this process somewhat more viable, but it is still limited by the batch sizes and need to manually set multiple refiners for any significant yield.

Sourcing Methods:<br>
Di-hydrogen is readily found on all planets as blue [[Mineral|crystals]] that can be manually harvested like any other mineral, but it does not occur as either a [[Resource Deposit]] nor [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit]]. Meaning that unlike most resources in the game, you cannot set up a single mining/extraction point for consistent farming. An alternative source would be [[Tritium]] mined from asteroids, as Tritium can be refined into Di-hydrogen at a rate of 5:1. Manual harvesting via either of these methods would undoubtedly be more productive with respect to time than the aforementioned cycling method. That said, a good strategy to employ would be to run a few refiner cycles *while* harvesting to increase production even further.

It should also be noted that both Di-hydrogen and Tritium can be purchased directly from vendors aboard the space stations of [[Outlaw System|Outlaw Systems]]. Additionally (and perhaps more importantly), Di-hydrogen Jelly can occasionally be found for sale at certain [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and offered up by [[Trader (NPC)|NPC Pilots]] wherever they might be found. Since breaking down Di-hydrogen Jelly into Di-hydrogen is significantly faster than creating it (80 seconds to break down a full stack of jelly into 800 units of Di-hydrogen), sourcing it directly through purchase and then breaking it down is arguably the fastest way to acquire Di-hydrogen in bulk. Just be sure to note which terminal(s) offer Di-hydrogen jelly for sale so you can regularly return to restock.

## Salt Cycling

Basic Principles:<br>
2 units of [[Salt]] directly refines into 1 unit of [[Chlorine]].  1 unit of Chlorine directly refines back into 2 units of Salt. So it is a conversion that can be made at any time without loss or additional resources. Adding 2 units of Oxygen to 1 unit of Chlorine yields 6 units Chlorine, which in turn can yield 12 units of Salt. Therefore, Oxygen enables a theoretically infinite multiplication of Salt and Chlorine, provided you begin with a trivial amount of salt and have a reliable source of Oxygen in bulk, e.g. extractors.

If no Salt is available, besides directly mining aquatic minerals and/or setting up extractors on deposits, it can be produced in a refiner by combining 1 unit of Di-Hydrogen with 1 unit of Oxygen to produce 1 unit of Salt.

## Carbon Cycling

Basic Principles: 2 Carbon makes 1 [[Condensed Carbon]] and 1 Condensed Carbon makes 2 Carbon. Add 2 Oxygen to 1 Condensed Carbon to get 6 Condensed Carbon, which then yields 12 Carbon. Thus, adding Oxygen allows infinite multiplication of Carbon and Condensed Carbon.

Carbon can be obtained by refining Oxygen or harvesting it from plants with the [[Mining Beam|Mining beam]] or from [[Standing Planter|Standing Planters]].

Alternatively, each point of percentage of fuel in a [[Portable Refiner]] returns 1 Carbon upon dismantling. By placing 40 Condensed Carbon as fuel into a Portable Refiner and then dismantling it, 100 Carbon is returned, more than refining Condensed Carbon back into Carbon and without needing to wait for the refining process. By using better refineries that don't use fuel along with a Portable Refinery to turn Condensed Carbon back into Carbon, Carbon can be multiplied without the use of Oxygen.

## Sodium Nitrate Cycling

Basic principles: Ferrite Dust/Pure Ferrite/Magnetised Ferrite and Nitrogen can be refined to make [[Sodium Nitrate]], with the higher-tier Ferrite types yielding more.

The next step is to refine Oxygen with Sodium Nitrate to multiply the Sodium Nitrate.

Sodium Nitrate can be refined to make Sodium as needed, then converted back to Sodium Nitrate for storage or multiplication.

## Cobalt Cycling

it is first necessary to refine Di-Hydrogen and Condensed Carbon into Mordite. Then refine Mordite with Sodium Nitrate to Make [[Marrow Bulb]]. Next, add Oxygen to said Marrow Bulb to make [[Ionised Cobalt]].

Ionised Cobalt can be multiplied with Oxygen, or converted to [[Cobalt]] at a 1:2 ratio. Adding 2 Oxygen to Ionised Cobalt provides 6 times as much Ionised Cobalt.

Ionised Cobalt can be converted to twice as much Cobalt, then converted back again for storage or multiplication as needed.

## Oxygen Multiplication

Basic Principles: 1 Salt + 1 Nitrogen produce 1 [[Kelp Sac]]. 1 Kelp Sac, 1 Condensed Carbon and 1 of any Farmable Plant material* make 10 Oxygen.

(**Any farmable plant except for Faecium and Mordite can be used, but Fungal Mould is preferred  as it is easy to create using the Ammonia-Fungal Mould production cycle further below*).

Step 1: 1 Salt and 1 Nitrogen make 1 Kelp Sac

Step 2: Said 1 Kelp Sac and 1 Plant Material* and 1 Condensed Carbon make 10 Oxygen.

It's a bit of a drawn out process, but if you lack a ready supply of Oxygen, this allows you to turn 1 Salt, 1 Nitrogen, 1 Condensed Carbon and 1 Farmable Plant into 10 Oxygen to top up your supplies.

Alternate solutions are to have a [[Oxygen Harvester]] nearby and fuel it with Carbon or Condensed Carbon, or to set up Gas Extractors at an Oxygen hotspot.

## Gas Cycling

There are three atmospheric gases in No Man's Sky: [[Nitrogen]], [[Sulphurine]] and [[Radon]].

In a Refiner, if you add Oxygen to any of these gases, you can convert it into the next gas in an endless cycle, at a 1:1 ratio.

It is not possible to create any of these from other elements in refiners. The gases can usually only be harvested by [[Atmosphere Harvester|Atmosphere Harvesters]] or [[Gas Extractor|Gas Extractors]] on biome-specific planets. With this solution, if one type of gas is available, it is possible to convert it into the other types by adding Oxygen in the cycle.

Using this approach it is possible to build a base on your preferred type of world (which allows access to one type of gas) and you will not need leave the planet to collect the other gases. The one exception to this rule are [[Biome - Dead|Dead]], [[Biome - Exotic|Exotic]] and [[Biome - Mega Exotic|Mega Exotic]] planets, as they do not feature any of these gases in any form, whether from Harvesters or Extractors.

## Ammonia/Fungal Mould

Basic Principles: 1 Di-Hydrogen and 1 Nitrogen can be refined to make 1 [[Ammonia]].

Then, 2 Ammonia + 1 Oxygen makes 1 Fungal Mould after which 1 Fungal Mould + 1 Ammonia makes 2 Fungal Mould.

Lastly, if you need to top up your supplies, 2 Fungal Mould and 1 Salt makes 1 Ammonia.

## Mordite and Faecium

Basic Principles: Make 1 [[Mordite]] from 1 Di-Hydrogen and 1 Condensed Carbon. Then add 1 Condensed Carbon to 1 Mordite to produce 4 times as much [[Faecium]] (Optionally, 2 Carbon gives 3 Faecium).

Multiply Faecium by adding 1 Oxygen to 1 Faecium to get 3 Faecium. Then, it is possible convert 3 Faecium back to 2 Mordite if needed to restart the process.

## Silver and Gold Cycling

**Note:** *These two processes are also useful for multiplying Chromatic Metal, shown further below!*

Before multiplying stocks of [[Gold]], [[Silver]] and [[Platinum]], a supply of [[Uranium]] and [[Pure Ferrite]] is required. Two methods for obtaining them are shown here:

### (Preparation) Di-Hydrogen and Radon for Uranium

Refine Di-Hydrogen and Radon into Uranium.

This may not be the most efficient way to make Uranium (see later), but this is a simple method using the materials produced so far with the earlier processes.

### (Preparation) Pure Ferrite Refining

Refine Di-Hydrogen and Nitrogen into Ammonia.  Ammonia can then be refined directly into Ferrite Dust, and in turn Pure Ferrite.

There will be other ways of building up Pure Ferrite reserves below.

Another source of Ferrite (all variants) is to build a [[Mineral Extractor]] on a [[Rusted Metal]] deposit (found on Dead worlds) and refine that into Ferrite Dust.

### Gold Cycling

Basic principles: 10 Gold, 30 Uranium and 60 Pure Ferrite (or 120 Ferrite Dust) can be refined into 1 [[Lemmium]]. 1 Lemmium can then be refined into 125 Gold.

By using the earlier created Uranium and Pure Ferrite reserves, it is possible to start multiplying Gold reserves.

### Silver Cycling

The first step is to create [[Pyrite]] by combining Oxygen with Gold, which can be obtained through the above processes.

The second step is to make [[Dirty Bronze]] by refining Pyrite with Gold and Pure Ferrite

The third step is to refine Dirty Bronze to make Silver. 1 Dirty Bronze produces 250 Silver.

It is possible to cycle Silver and Gold around to multiply stocks in this way, or combine this with the next process to create infinite supplies of Pure Ferrite and Magnetised Ferrite to make the process even more efficient, as the two processes directly feed into each other.

## Magnetised Ferrite and Platinum

### Magnetised Ferrite Cycling

The first step is to refine Gold and Silver into Platinum.

The second step is to refine Platinum and Oxygen to make 10 [[Magnetised Ferrite]].

Once there is a supply of Magnetised Ferrite, it can be readily refined to convert it into Pure Ferrite and back again. Magnetised Ferrite is a more convenient storage medium, as each unti can be refined into 2 Pure Ferrite, making it more space-efficient.

If Ferrite Dust is needed for Metal Plates, Add 1 Oxygen to 1 Pure Ferrite to make 2 Rusted metal, then refine 1 Rusted Metal to get 2 Ferrite Dust.

Note that Magnetised Ferrite can also be refined from Pure Ferrite and Condensed Carbon at a ratio of 2:3. While this process is less efficient compared to refining Platinum and Oxygen, it is more economical, as only a supply of Oxygen is needed for multiplying the Condensed Carbon, which can be accomplished with just an [[Oxygen Harvester]]. Also note, that this is a much more efficient way to make Pure Ferrite compared to making it from Ferrite Dust.

### Platinum Cycling

If you use [[Platinum]] instead of Gold or Silver to produce Lemmium or Dirty Bronze then this makes the above processes even more efficient, as that Lemmium and Dirty Bronze can then be used to make more Gold and Silver, which can then be used to make more Platinum, to repeat the cycle.

1. Refining 5 Platinum and 30 Uranium/Pyrite and 60 Pure Ferrite makes 1 Lemmium/Dirty Bronze.
1. Refining 1 Lemmium makes 125 Gold and 1 Dirty Bronze makes 250 Silver.
1. Refining 1 Silver and 1 Gold makes 1 Platinum.
  1. 1 Lemmium and 1 Dirty Bronze thus gives you 125 Platinum and 125 leftover Silver in the end. (This means you need to make twice as much Lemmium as Dirty Bronze, since Dirty Bronze has twice the yield)

## Biome-Dependent Resources

Each of the biome-dependent resources can be converted into the next at a ratio of 2:1 with the addition of Ferrite Dust or Pure Ferrite, in a continuous cycle of resources. However, this process has a 2:1 ratio, meaning that each cycle will consume half of its input while yielding its output.

Listed in the infographic is the most efficient way to make each of the resources. This works for all of them except [[Phosphorus]], which can only be refined from [[Dioxite]] or [[Solanium]] (Solanium can be refined from Di-Hydrogen and Sulphurine).

*[Outdated: Phosphorus can now be refined by combining [[Methane]] and Di-Hydrogen at a 1:1 ratio]*

Any of these resources can be refined into Ferrite Dust at a ratio of 1:1.

There are other ways to make Ferrite Dust (such as refining Rusted Metal), but a resource deposit mine on any planet will yield one or another of these biome-dependent resources, which can then be converted to Ferrite Dust.

## Biome-Specific Plants and Resources

Each biome type has an associated resource, and a type of plant which can grow there in the wild and these resources and plants are linked in refining processes:

- Adding Oxygen to any of the resources creates the associated plant.
- Adding Salt to any of the plants gives the associated resource.

However, these processes both have a 2:1 ratio, meaning that each cycle will consume half of the input while making the output. Using the plant itself as a catalyst can allow a 1:1 conversion from the associated resource to the associated plant (e.g. 1 Ammonia + 1 Fungal Mould makes 2 Fungal Mould), but it is not possible to convert with 1:1 efficiency in the other direction.

[[Solanium]] is unique in that it can also be refined from 1 Di-hydrogen and 1 Sulphurine. This can be useful for generating extra "fodder material" to overcome the 2:1 ratios mentioned above, as Solanium can be used to make Phosphorus, which can then be cycled to the other materials.

## Chromatic Metal and Stellar Metals

The final process is how to make [[Chromatic Metal]], then use it to multiply supplies of the colored Stellar metals of [[Copper]], [[Cadmium]], [[Emeril]], [[Indium]] and [[Quartzite]]. Chromatic Metal has many uses, and is likely to be scarce and in high demand until you can construct a Mineral Extractor on an appropriate mineral deposit. As such, this process is quite useful.

Any of these coloured metals, when combined with Silver and Gold, will refine to Chromatic Metal at a much better ratio than if simply refined by themselves. The more valuable the colored Stellar metal used, the more Chromatic Metal that will result.

The values of the colored Stellar metals are as follows (from least valuable to most valuable):

1. Copper
1. Cadmium
1. Emeril
1. Indium
1. Quartzite (not pictured in diagram)

There is no way to create these colored Stellar metals from base materials but starting with only a small amount, it is possible to infinitely multiply supplies using this refining process.

Chromatic Metal added to any colored metal will refine to double the amount of colored metal.

This process will ensure infinite supplies of Copper, Cadmium, Emeril, Indium, and Quartzite: as long as you begin with a minimum of 2 units of each metal, it is possible infinitely multiply these metals.

Lastly, whenever you refine colored Stellar Metals to make Chromatic Metal (without the intent to multiply it); add [[Pure Ferrite]] to it, as this improves the output ratio (e.g. refining Copper goes from a 2:1 ratio to a 1:1 ratio, doubling the output).

## Note on Activated Stellar Metals

[[Activated Quartzite]] can be created with 1 Quartzite, 1 Crystalised Helium/Lithium, and 1 Methane, producing 1 Activated Quartzite.

The only resources that cannot be created by refining (or crafting) in any way are:

- [[Activated Copper]]
- [[Activated Cadmium]]
- [[Activated Emeril]]
- [[Activated Indium]]

These resources can be refined to Chromatic Metal using the processes described above, and will have a higher yield compared to their non-activated counterparts, but the Chromatic Metal created through this cannot be used to create more Activated stellar metals; it can only be refined to make regular stellar metals.

The only renewable way to obtain Activated stellar metals is through a [[Mineral Extractor]] on an appropriate [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit|deposit]].
