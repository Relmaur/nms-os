# Chameleon Planet

**Chameleon Planet** is a classification page.

## Summary

**Chameleon Planet** is a [[planet]] classification page.

They are rare planets that have an **anomalous** atmospheric filter which moves with the player - objects and surfaces that appear at a distance and objects and surfaces that are close to the player appear to have different colors. This makes all of the surroundings around a player shift their color as the player moves. Sometimes, planets with such effects are called "Sentient" as they appear to react to player movement.

## Planetary Atmosphere Filter Specifics

The "Chameleon Effect" Filter is a sphere of a few dozen units around the player (**Area A**) that has a different color filter than areas outside of this sphere (**Area B**).

Surface and Objects in both areas may have varied color filters applied to them:

- modification of original colors hue, saturation, value
- more rarely, the color modification is applied only to specific RGB channels
- more visible and extreme changes often applied to area B - some planets exhibit extreme filters that render distance in Duo-chromatic pallets.
- in rare cases the effect / area split works only on particular types of objects or surfaces - for example a filter applied only on water, or in more rare cases - only on flora.

## Appearance

Chameleon planets are a rare find, and there are some player communities that dedicate time to finding this type of planets.

It is impossible to spot Chameleon Planet from space, but initiating planetary approach and breaching a planet atmosphere will determine if the planet is a Chameleon.
