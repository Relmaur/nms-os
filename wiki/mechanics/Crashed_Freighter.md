# Crashed Freighter

**Crashed Freighter** is a Point of Interest.

## Summary

**Crashed Freighter** is a [[Shelter]] type of planetary [[Point of Interest]].

Crashed freighters can be located after solving a puzzle at a [[Transmission Tower]], upon activating a [[Planetary Chart (distress signal)|Planetary Chart (Emergency Cartographic Data)]], or by normal exploration. Through the use of tools such as the [[Terrain Manipulator]], [[Plasma Launcher]], or [[Geology Cannon]], you can uncover storage units that contain [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]], [[units]], or valuable [[Product|products]], with the exact possible contents varying based on whether abandoned mode is active for the current save. Each crashed freighter has a [[Distress Beacon]] that will grant a [[blueprint]] and start the secondary mission. Note that the containers can emit deadly radiation that goes past any protection measures, although it can be cut off by entering a [[Minotaur]] power suit or retreating to a safe distance.

Also, [[Minotaur]] Distress signal scans with the [[Minotaur Radar Array]] can some times locate a crashed Freighter.

They can often be spotted on the surface without any hints due to their giant silhouette and appear in average frequency.

They can also be found underwater, with slightly different cargo pod locations, by utilising a [[Nautilon]]'s [[High-Power Sonar]].

## Repair

The cargo pod has a set of randomly chosen [[Junk|junk resources]] which will need to be removed to open the pod (formerly a randomly chosen [[Damaged Component]] which would need to be repaired to open the pod). All cargo pods located at the same Crashed Freighter will have the same set of junk resources (formerly the same Damaged Component).

## Additional Information

- Sometimes a secondary mission will appear in your log to search for six containers at a crash site. It can be removed from the log by opening all six containers or manually abandoning the mission.
- Different from a [[Crashed Ship]]; you can't recover a crashed freighter.
- One or more of the storage containers may be inaccessible without excavation. As well, some containers may be inaccessible without breaking through weakened walls of Reinforced Steel with melee or weaponry.
- The first sighting of a crashed freighter was on the planet [[Quidar II]], feature in one of the [[Pre-release]] videos.
- In a tutorial-enabled save, the player learns the [[Hyperdrive]] blueprint from a Crashed Freighter's distress beacon during the [[Awakenings]] mission.

## Bugs

- Sometimes the marker for a Crashed Freighter that is found through a Transmission Tower or with the [[Signal Booster]] will not be cleared when you access the distress beacon.  Using the Signal Booster to find a [[Drop Pod]] will clear the marker.  However, this may take you back to a Drop Pod you've already found.
- Sometimes you can not access all the cargo-pods the freighter contains because the one at the far end of the freighter gets stuck in indestructable terrain.
