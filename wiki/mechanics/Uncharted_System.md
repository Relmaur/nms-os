# Uncharted System

**Uncharted system** is a type of [[star system]].

## Summary

**Uncharted systems** are systems devoid of any sapient life aside from [[sentinel|sentinels]]. They do not have native [[space station|space stations]] or ships to encounter. Various planetary [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] are not present, like [[Habitable Base]] or [[Ruin|Ruins]], and no [[Buried Technology Module|Buried Technology Modules]] can be found. Before the [[NEXT]] update all systems were inhabited, thus their former inhabitants were wiped from existence.

It is NOT an [[Abandoned System]], although they are similar in some ways.

## Information on Galaxy Map

**Uncharted** systems show their name, the star type and [[region]]. The number of planets is not hidden even though it is supposed to be uncharted. At first glance, racial, security or economic data does not exist. But with a [[Conflict Scanner]] and an [[Economy Scanner]] installed, switching to the conflict and economy filters reveals that these systems actually have conflict and economy which can vary from system to system. There is no higher life here.

## Bases

[[Signal Booster|Signal Boosters]] will fail to find Habitable Bases on this type of system. If you wish to build a base, you'll need to build your own [[Base Computer]] to get started.

## Additional information

- 'EmptySystemProbability' is a property related to the probability of which coloured system will be uncharted. The probabilities are as follows (GCSOLARGENERATIONGLOBALS.GLOBAL.MBIN WorldsTwo 5.53):

| Yellow | Red | Green | Blue | Purple |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.95 | 0.4 | 0.4 | 0.2 |

- [[Mechanoceris]] (Robot Antelopes) and [[Structurae]] (Living Buildings) can be encountered in uncharted systems.
- The Discovery map of an Uncharted system will show three X's in the upper-left corner
