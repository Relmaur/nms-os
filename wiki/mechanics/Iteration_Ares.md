# Iteration: Ares

**Iteration: Ares** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Ares** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

This traveller's head depicts a large but seamingly empty translucent head, Through the membrane six fleshy lines can be seen leading to four devices and it's two eyes. The devices probably serve a sensory purpose such as hearing considering their position.

## Interactions

As an "avid collector of exotic goods," Ares will pay 50  Nanites for each [[Journey Milestone]] or some small amount of  Nanites for a chosen product (changed daily) and sells basic [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]]. These modules can be installed and dismantled for many types of [[Resource]], including rare ones such as [[Cadmium]].

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Ares Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Ares may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
