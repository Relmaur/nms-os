# Planetary Settlement - Happiness

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Description:** Happiness Guide
- **Added in:** Relics

**Planetary Settlement - Happiness** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - Happiness** contains a detailed description of the characteristics of the population's happiness or approval aspect in a [[Planetary Settlement]].

It is displayed as a percentage (%), Happiness measures the overall mood state of the citizens. The higher the percentage, the more content citizens of the settlement have become, and conversely for a lower percentage.  Happiness does not seem to be capped at 100%.

A settlement with low Happiness will often have citizens with negative thoughts, including about the Overseer, while settlements with high Happiness will often have positive thoughts among its citizens.
