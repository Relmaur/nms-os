# Swamp Terrain

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Geographical feature
- **Description:** Landlocked Planet with lakes
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Swamp Terrain** is a geographical feature.

## Summary

**Swamp Terrain**, refers to a occurrence of [[planet]] surfaces that have no oceans or seas but feature small to medium size lakes of water the fill shallow or deep ravines, craters and MESA depressions. [[Moon|Moons]],  and  [[planet|planets]] will never have swamp terrain.

A planet with swamp terrain will feature very limited water.

The swamp planets will often (but not always) appear similar to pangean worlds from space in many cases.

Swamps worlds typically have less than 20% of their surface covered with water.

## Types

No specific variation has been noticed as a repeating pattern of pangean generation on planets that would allow type categories at this time.

## Game application

In No Man's Sky, all features are procedurally generated, so any type of [[Terrain Archetype|archetype]] can be present in the game are by definition undetermined. This terrain *typically* occurs in combination with Craters, MESA, Hills or Honeycomb [[Terrain Archetype|Terrain Archetypes]], but it is not limited to those.
