# Localised Earth Element

**Localised Earth Element** is a type of resource.

## Summary

**Localised Earth Element** (or LEE) is a type of resource in [[No Man's Sky]], grouped under the [[earth elements]] division.

Resources in this type are commonly used for [[base building]], [[crafting]], charging technologies and upgrading.

They can be mined as a secondary resources from some [[mineral|minerals]] in specific [[biome|biomes]], which must be scanned to reveal their secondary resources.
