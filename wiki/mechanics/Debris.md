# Debris

**Debris** is a lootable resource.

## Summary

**Debris** is an object found on every planet. It is [[Superconductive Lock|locked]], but if the player possesses an [[AtlasPass v1]] in their inventory they can unlock and take the contents of the Debris. The player may be required to repair the lock before opening.
