# Space Station

**Space Station** is an orbiting platform found in most star systems.

## Summary

**Space Station** is a large alien spaceport able to be docked with a starship, providing a trading centre, mission hub, and means to travel to [[Base building|habitable bases]].

Aside from [[uncharted system|uncharted systems]], these stations can be found in every [[star system]] throughout the game, giving players a safe place to land. The exterior appearance of a space station can vary greatly, the most notable being a damaged appearance in [[Abandoned System|abandoned systems]].

## Details

Visiting a space station in any populated system provides access to a Station Terminus [[Base Teleport Module|Teleporter]], as well as the opportunity to [[Starship|buy a new starship]], create your own starship (currently limited to Fighters, Explorers, Haulers, and Solar ships), upgrade or scrap starships, upgrade [[Multi-tool|Multi-tools]], purchase or sell [[resource|resources]] and [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]], [[NPC conversation|talk]] to or hire [[Hirable NPC|NPC characters]], and obtain missions.

Docking at a space station involves flying into the station tractor beam, a blue shaft of light coming out of the space station's entrance. This will automatically pull the ship inward and through a hangar that can vary in length before landing it on one of eight landing pads. Landing will set a player's SWL (Space Wanted Level) to zero, making them useful to escape sentinel starships. The length of the hangar approaching the landing pads for regular ships is reserved for four [[Corvette]] landing pads, which double as [[Corvette Workshop|Corvette Workshops]], dedicated [[Corvette Construction Parts]] storage, and the only place you can build corvettes. As these are distantly placed to accommodate corvettes of the largest size available, a teleport pad is provided to quickly reach the station core.

Once you land at a space station and turn towards the core, there will be two floors in the interior and two amenities on each floor (see the [[#Amenities|Amenities]] section for more details). Though the interior of each Space Station does have randomly generated assets, the placement of amenities is always the same.

When leaving the space station, the [[Pulse Engine]] will not work until the player's starship is a safe distance away. Each space station has a randomly generated length of the hallway. Shorter hallways will have traders arriving more quickly. System conflict level determines how many traders will arrive overall before petering out.

### Amenities

The following amenities can be found in a Space Station:

- **First floor** (from left to right when entering from the landing bay):
  - [[Mission Board]] – Offers procedural missions from the Mission Listings Agent.
  - [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] – Access to local economy for buying and selling goods.
  - [[Teleportation Terminal]] – Functions like a [[Base Teleport Module]], allowing instant travel to discovered locations.
  - [[Cartographer]] – Sells planetary charts in exchange for [[Navigation Data]].

- **Second floor** (from left to right):
  - [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] – Sell upgrade modules for the [[Exosuit]], [[Multi-Tool]], [[Starship]], and [[Exocraft]].
    - [[Exosuit Upgrade Station]] – Located next to the Exosuit merchant; used to expand inventory slots for  Units.
    - [[Multi-Tool Purchase Station]] – Located on either side of the Multi-Tool merchant; used to purchase a new Multi-Tool
    - [[Multi-Tool Upgrade Station]] – Located next to the Multi-Tool merchant; used to improve tool class or stats.
  - [[Guild Envoy]] – Offers standing rewards and accepts faction donations.
  - Cargo Area – A decorative corner space with crates and equipment, which may occasionally contain random materials,  Nanites, or  Units.
  - [[Station Core]] – Positioned behind the merchants; allows interaction to check standing with races and factions. A [[Forged Passport]] may be used here to improve or restore standing.

- **Back of the station** (behind the landing pads):
  - [[Starship Outfitting Terminal]] – Used to scrap ships for inventory slots or upgrade their class.
  - [[Starship Fabricator Terminal]] – Allows fabrication of new starships using components.
  - [[Appearance Modifier]] – Used to customize player appearance.
  - [[Scrap Dealer]] – Hidden beneath the stairs; sells [[Emergency Broadcast Receiver|Emergency Broadcast Receivers]] and purchases recovered [[Derelict Freighter]] loot.
  - [[Fossil Collector]] – Located across from the Scrap Dealer; offers trades for [[Ancient Bones]].

## Claiming

As of [[Update 7.00]] players can become the Station Director and claim a system's [[Space Station]], which allows you to customise the interior and exterior decorations of the station.

Note that players cannot claim the following station types; [[Atlas Interface]], [[Space Anomaly]], Abandoned Station, and Outlaw Stations.

In order to claim a [[Space Station]], the player must interact with the [[Station Core]] and select 'Claim Station'. From here it will show you the prerequisites for claiming.

The above screenshot is missing the 1 billion credits needed to purchase a station in a non-customized savestate.

Ownership of a space station, contrary to what the quest claims with the 1 billion to fund its operations, does not unlock an economic minigame like the settlement does. Ownership of an official station is a pure alliance claim.

### Wormhole Network

An alliance system will only be catalogued for the Alliance Station subtab inside the wormhole teleport network if it has been revisited once after being claimed. Otherwise the station will remain inside the visited stations tab and be unavailable for easier selection. If the system has never been visited it will not appear in the alliance tab. The only exception for this rule is the founding system of the alliance. This system is always available to teleport to.

### Current Issues

Despite allowing the star base to be decorated, none of these are shown to other players. The only one to see these, even if the base was uploaded after placing them, is you.

Space stations cannot be unclaimed, but their between 150-300 parts count towards a save's base part limit. Claiming too many stations will permanently prevent the player on that save from changing freighter layouts, building Corvettes or new bases on planets or in space, and will even block the construction of signal boosters or save points. Until this issue is fixed by excluding space station parts from the base part limit, people are advised not to claim more than the original 20 station limit if they plan to use their save for any further base building.

#### "Construction Limit Reached"

Until the station parts are excluded - if ever - a player has to manually deactivate each and every base through deletion once it has been claimed. The base parts are "fake", deleting the base doesn't remove them ingame as the station will return to default visuals, which includes the parts. However, the base part objects are not inside your save anymore after deletion.

The station remains linked to your account and any new player instance (secondary saves of the same account) can return to reopen the base for editing. The base will then be linked to the secondary save permanently instead. You can not lose your claim and rights to the station this way.

To summarize, the "hotfix" for the construction limit bug is to disable all claimed stations immediately upon claim and reopen them with a secondary station governor save to officially retain them. A governor savestate should only manage about 20 stations or they can't edit them effectively either.

### Former Limitations

Prior to the 25th September 2026, a player was limited to an absolute number of 20 stations. Once this number had been reached the game would yield the response "Nothing Found" on further attempts to obtain a station. After the update of the same day, this limit has been removed. In return the maximum score a player can grant to their alliance has been hardcapped to 60,383 points instead. This was likely done to limit the grinding of a few players leading to distortion of the leaderboard. Now only alliances with many members will dominate the list, regardless of territory claimed.

Thanks to this hardcap, the station limitation seems to have been considered redundant and was removed.

## Special

There are special space stations that show up as a anomalies in space, they appear in addition to the regular one.
- [[Atlas Interface]]: Will open up Alien Anomalies, you can ask Polo for Atlas Interface coordinates.
  - This space station has a long walkway to an altar where you can talk with the Atlas; some Atlas Interfaces will have two [[Warp Cell|Warp Cells]] at the foot of the altar. You can also learn some new Atlas words by walking around the station onto the glowing dots.

- [[Space Anomaly]]: this space station includes the NPCs named [[Polo]], [[Nada]] and [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]], along with many others. They all speak the player's language rather than that of their race.
  - The station also offers research for most researchable blueprints, has it’s own mission station ([[Nexus]]) and many other features.

### Abandoned Station

Abandoned stations can be found in abandoned systems. This station can be identified from the outside by having pieces of metal broken and floating in space, and having a red tractor beam instead of a blue one. Inside, it is devoid of any life except it has purple goo with small purple tumors similar to what is found inside and around [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]], although there are no [[Whispering Egg|Whispering Eggs]] in abandoned stations.

In contrast to a normal space station with its open marketplace architecture, abandoned stations features the architecture and the layout prior to the [[NEXT]] update, i.e. two ramps leading to one room with an attached room each. The attached room requires an [[AtlasPass v3]] key and seems to be without damage or goo.

There are no NPCs aboard this space station (apart from the NPC traders who will still dock in the landing area), and no Appearance Modifier, however, the following basic amenities are still available:

- Hangar:
  - Corvette Workshops and module storage, but with no teleporter pads available
  - Starship Outfitting Terminal
- Left-hand side:
  - Station Terminus Teleporter (looks and works like a **Base Teleport Module**)
  - Exosuit Upgrade Station
  - Multi-Tool Upgrade Station
- Right-hand side:
  - Galactic Trade Terminal

### Outlaw Station

Outlaw stations can be found in any system previously inhabited by any of the races. Like an abandoned station, this station can be identified from the outside by having pieces of metal broken and floating in space, and having a red tractor beam instead of a blue one. Inside, a number of NPCs and vendors occupy the landing area.

Like abandoned stations, outlaw stations feature the architecture and the layout of space stations prior to the NEXT update. Like the former architectural layout of the space station, outlaw stations feature two ramps on either side of the core; however, unlike normal space stations prior to the update, these ramps only lead to blocked-off doors and don't lead into any actual rooms (though you can still see [[Cargo Drop|Cargo Drops]] behind the inaccessible doors with the [[Analysis Visor]]).

Like a normal space station, this station will have an **Appearance Modifier**, Multi-Tool and Exosuit upgrade cabinets, **Starship Outfitting Terminal**, and a **Scrap Dealer** booth. In addition, the following amenities are available:
- Left-hand side:
  - Station Terminus Teleporter (looks and works like a **Base Teleport Module**). Cannot transport the player to "normal" space stations.
- Right-hand side:
  - [[Contraband Agent]] dealing mostly in prohibited and suspicious goods.
  - [[Salvage Dealer]] trading suspicious upgrade modules.
  - [[Bounty Master]] offering pirate missions in exchange for  Units, prohibited goods and upgrades, [[Forged Passport|Forged Passports]], and regular rewards.

## Buying / Trading / Conversation

One of the many things you can do in space stations is trade with the NPC pilots that land their ships on the station's landing pads. You can trade items for [[units]] or buy items, ships, etc. from them. Space stations with smaller runways will have more traders than ones with longer runways.

Words can be learned via encounters with alien lifeforms. Making friendly requests while in a space station may result in learning new words permanently.

## Attacking

While you can attack a station, it cannot actually be destroyed. Attacking will initially trigger a 1-star Wanted Level. If attacked long enough, a 4-star wanted level is triggered followed by an endless wave of [[Sentinel Interceptor|Sentinel Interceptors]]. There will be 3 initial waves, each consisting of 3 Interceptors. Every Interceptor destroyed will be replaced ad infinitum. This applies to normal and abandoned stations, but not to Outlaw Stations.

As an integral part of the game, it will always remain in orbit around its host star system.

Attacking is disabled while inside the space station.

## Name generation

The name of space station is procedurally generated by combining the name of the [[star system]] (&lowast; marks its location) in which the space station is located, and one of the following suffixes.

The suffixes are generated on a per-region basis. One region uses one suffix (This was observed in the [[Apiacosyr]] [[Region]]).

## Additional information

- The name generation and the sequence are extracted from the 1.77 version of **NMS_UPDATE3_ENGLISH.MBIN** game file.
- Pre-release information indicated that there would be five classes of space stations in No Man's Sky; the actual number is unknown. Each space station is marked with a set of [[Space Station Symbols|symbols]] somewhere on the outside walls.

While inside a Space Station:

- You do not suffer fall damage
- You cannot draw your [[Multi-Tool]] (you can on the PS4 VR, but cannot fire any weapon or beam), but you can switch tools.
- You can use passive secondary multi-tool modes like the [[Cloaking Device]] or the [[Combat Scope]].
- There is no drain on your [[Life Support]].
- Upon entering, your [[Sentinel]] wanted level is reset to zero.
- Entering will stop any [[pirate|pirates]] from pursuing you. (Note: Pirate dreadnought attacks against freighters continue while you're inside a space station.)
- Player jetpack recharges at a much faster rate.
- You can use the Gravitino Coil but not the rest of your Multi-Tool weapons/tools. (Can somebody properly write this down?)
Atlantideum can be used at [[Korvax]] Space [[Station Core|Station Cores]] to get an 8-bit binary code that can be translated. More information and some Examples are under [[Atlantideum|Atlantideum: Station Cores]].
