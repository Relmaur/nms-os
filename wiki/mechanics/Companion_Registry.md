# Companion Registry

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** User Interface
- **Description:** Companion Registry
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Companion Registry** is a reference article.

## Summary

The **Companion Registry** is a user interface element which allows the player to access a list of their companions.

The player initially starts with two slots in their registry, and can add up to 28 additional slots, for a grand total of 30 slots.

## Game Usage

Companion Registry can be used to preview currently available pets, rename, and abandon them. Each pet has a listing detailing its characteristics (similar to regular [[Fauna|Animals]]) as well as pet-specific stats influencing their behavior, in percent:

- Playfulness or Usefulness
- Aggression or Gentleness
- Devotion or Independence

## Adding Companion Slots

Every player starts with a Registry of two slots. The number of slots can be increased by purchasing additional slots in the Registry menu with [[Nanite Cluster|Nanites]], up to a maximum of thirty (30). Each new slot is increasingly expensive to obtain. Generally, the cost for each next slot is double: Starting at 500 Nanites for the first new slot, and ending at 10,000 for the sixth.

Once all currently available slots are occupied, a player can't tame new creatures as pets until they free a slot by abandoning a pet they already own. Unhatched [[Companion Egg|Companion Eggs]] residing in the [[Inventory]] do not require Companion Slots until they are hatched.

## Interface

It can be accessed from the [[Controls|On Foot Quick Menu]] by selecting "Creatures" and then "Companion Register".
