# Planetary Settlement - Construction Opportunities

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Description:** Construction Opportunities
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Planetary Settlement - Construction Opportunities** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - Construction Opportunities** contains a detailed description of the construction opportunities which the Settlement Overseer must oversee at the [[Planetary Settlement|Settlement Administration Terminal]] in a [[Planetary Settlement]].

## Navigating

Planetary Settlement buildings are arranged haphazardly around a Settlement Administration Terminal. While building or upgrading, players can select the Settlement mission to show active steps, or place a [[Save Beacon]] or [[Exocraft]] bay to easily find the correct location.

### Player benefits

Besides the settlement bonuses listed above, some buildings can provide more tangible benefit to players at the settlement:

| Type | Player benefits |
| --- | --- |
| Farm Module | Enables Agricultural products. |
| Marketplace | Provides a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]], a [[Technology Merchant]], a [[Guild Envoy]], and enables Commercial products |
| Saloon | A radio can be found inside and its music can be changed. Contains a functional [[Nutrient Processor]] and enables Leisure products |
| Starship Dock | When you warp to the settlement, your starship will be parked in the first dock. NPC ships will also land at any empty docks, which provides an opportunity to recruit them to your [[Freighter\|freighter squadron]], [[Buying a starship\|buy their ship]], or [[Units\|trade]]. If your ship is in the dock, you can also upgrade or scrap the ship. Also enables Stardock products. |
| Tower | Provides a high vantage point from which to see an overview of the settlement, as well as providing the player [[Navigation Data]] (available per 24 real-life hours) and allowing players to scan for [[Point of Interest]] (specific to A-Class) as well as scanning for [[Space Encounter]] (specific to S-Class). These features can be accessed from the Tower Calibration Terminal. |
| Warehouse | Enables Industrial products. |
| Utility Module | Enables Industrial products. |
| Fishing Pond | Provides a small water source viable for [[Fishing]]. Rarity of high-class fishes varies depending on the pond's class, and will provide flotsams during construction progress. |
| Factory | Provides a [[Multi-Tool]] box that can be interacted with to buy/exchange a Multi-Tool. |

The Residence buildings have no known player benefits but increase maximum Settlement population.

## Upgrade Opportunities

As of the [[Update 5.70|Beacons]] update, the player can also upgrade individual buildings within a settlement to increase their capabilities.  Only one construction project can occur at once, either upgrading any one building, or setting up a new building.

Upgrading buildings does not appear to affect their Upkeep Cost. Residences also do not have their productivity impacted by upgrades.
