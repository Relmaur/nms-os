The **Exocraft Terminal** is an item used for research.

## Summary

Terminals of various types can be crafted, each with their own special purposes. The Exocraft Terminal is used for specialized research, and for the creation of [[Exocraft]]-related blueprints through the [[Exocraft Technician]] missions. The blueprint for this terminal itself is received as part of the [[Base Computer Archives|base]] missions.

The [[blueprint|blueprints]] for the following items can be acquired at an Exocraft Terminal based on specific quests from the [[Technician]].

## Game description

A research station tailored to the needs of Mechanics, and equipped with bespoke vehicular design software.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Exocraft Terminal

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Di-hydrogen]]

## Additional information

- It can be built in a [[Cylindrical Room]], a [[Square Room]], or a [[Cuboid Room]].  Each [[terminal]] related to [[Hirable NPC|Hirable NPCs]] needs be placed in a separate location (e.g. square room can snap-to-fit 4 terminals).
- Each Exocraft has its own [[Geobay]].
