# Extreme Planet

**Extreme Planet** are a hazardous type of [[planet]].

## Summary

**Extreme Planet** is a type planet that is particularly inhospitable with a very turbulent climate. Each [[Biome]] (except [[Biome - Dead|dead planets]]) can have very difficult [[weather]]. On these planets, [[Hazard Protection]] is consumed very quickly (about a minute and even less during very frequent [[Storm|Storms]]).

When a player lands on an inhospitable planet, the weather setting is written in red to indicate a dangerous climate. This type of planet has the particularity that stellar metal; [[Copper]] in yellow [[Star system|Star systems]], [[Cadmium]] in red systems, [[Emeril]] in green systems, and [[Indium]] in blue systems - are in an activated state. When scanning a planet in a [[Starship]], if you find [[Activated Copper]], [[Activated Cadmium]], [[Activated Emeril]] or [[Activated Indium]] in the resources, that planet is extreme. These activated resources have the particularity of producing more shiny metal and having a higher commercial value.

## Protection

In [[Space Station|Space Stations]], it is possible to buy additional shields against specific dangers, which allows you to last longer on these planets. The shields must be recharged either with [[Sodium]], [[Sodium Nitrate]], or with [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]], made from [[Cobalt]].

## Characteristics

- [[Biome - Scorched|Scorched]]: Very high temperature (about 100°C), sandstorms during which it can be up to 350°C.
- [[Biome - Frozen|Frozen]]: Intense snow, temperature of about -90 to -100°C, blizzards are very frequent. During which, the temperature drops to -130°C.
- [[Biome - Toxic|Toxic]]: Much higher toxicity than normal, intense toxic precipitation. Toxic storms can go up to 120 Tox (toxicity unit).
- [[Biome - Irradiated|Irradiated]]: Much higher radiation levels (about 10 Rad), radioactive dust storms cause the radiation level to rise to 30 Rad.
- [[Biome - Barren|Barren]]: Temperature of about 50 to 70°C during the day, -100°C at night, with very frequent scorching sandstorms, where the temperature can reach 150°C.
- [[Biome - Lush|Lush]]: Permanent intense rainfall, scorching rainstorms (over 100°C) very frequent.
- [[Gas Giant]]: Permanent storms, high temperatures (over 100°C) and frequent tornadoes.

## Trivia

- Although extreme weather does not appear to have an effect on flora and fauna, high radiation and extreme temperatures would in reality preclude most organic forms of life as the fundamental molecules of cells would denature and fragment.
- Radiation damage would be as dangerous to electronic beings as organic ones as circuitry malfunctions due to interference from free radicals, and burn up.
