# Multi-tools - Circle Blaze (Pathfinder)

The distinguishing features of this model of [[multi-tool]] is the large circular blaze on the side, either near the front or by the trigger area. This may be simply decorative, or actually be part of the tool mechanics.

These usually fall in the Rifle category.
