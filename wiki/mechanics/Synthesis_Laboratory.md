# Synthesis Laboratory

**Synthesis Laboratory** is a research terminal at the Space Anomaly.

## Summary

**Synthesis Laboratory** is a product recipes research [[terminal]] at the [[Space Anomaly]] unlocking product recipes in exchange for 250  Nanites each. It has the same research trees as ones available from [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]].

### Locating the Laboratory

To locate the synthesis lab, use the ramp on the right to reach the area with the market-style booths such as the **Cronus Grill System** and **Egg Sequencer**. Then take a left and through two doors. At the junction with a circular core structure take a right and a large floating orb can be seen rotating within a forcefield, this is also where the Multi-Tool Research, Exocraft Research, Exosuit Research, etc. can be found. The synthesis lab console is the unoccupied booth on the right between the Multi-Tool Research and Exocraft Research.

## Additional Information

Eight of the trade-good recipes (plus several other base construction items) can be unlocked for free by completing the [[Scientist]]'s questline via the [[Science Terminal]], saving up to 2000  Nanites.
