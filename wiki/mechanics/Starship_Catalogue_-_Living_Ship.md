# Starship Catalogue - Living Ship

## Summary

The following is a list of various [[Living Ship (Starship)|Living Ship]] starships discovered by different players during the Living Ship through current eras.

(For a list of all ship types, refer to the [[Starship Catalogue]])

## Description

These biological ships have their own set of unique organic technologies, all procedurally generated for a customised, evolved loadout.

- [[Neural Assembly]] - very similar to [[Launch Thruster|Launch Thrusters]]. The difference is that S class upgrades give you both guaranteed -20% launch cost plus automatic recharging per upgrade, while A class ones give you a guaranteed -20% launch cost. Sadly, no boost values are granted like in the more recent [[Launch Thruster Upgrade|Launch Thruster Upgrades]].
- [[Pulsing Heart]] - equivalent of [[Pulse Engine]]
- [[Spewing Vents]] - equivalent of [[Photon Cannon]]
- [[Scream Suppressor]] - equivalent of [[Shield|Shields]]
- [[Singularity Cortex]] - equivalent of [[Hyperdrive]]
- [[Grafted Eyes]] - equivalent of [[Phase Beam]]

## Inventory

The inventory for Living Ships are 36 Storage Sacs (Cargo), 30 Organ Chambers (Tech). To increase the capacity of your Living Ship, [[Spawning Sac|Spawning Sacs]] are needed. They can be obtained from Organic Frigates expeditions.
