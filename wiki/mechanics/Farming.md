# Farming

**Farming** is one of the major methods of fund-raising in No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Farming** provides the ability to plant farmable agricultural [[product|products]] and then harvest [[resource|resources]] from them after a certain period of time. [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet|Haz-Mat Gauntlets]] are generally required, except for Echinocactus. The harvested products can then be sold at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in their raw form, used as ingredients for crafting, or transformed into other products that can be sold. Most of these resources are referred to in-game as [[Harvested Agricultural Substance|Harvested Agricultural Substances]].

Crops may only be grown after learning their [[blueprint]], which can be acquired for free via missions from after a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]] has been hired for your [[Base building|base]] or [[freighter]], or at a cost of 3 [[Salvaged Data]] each from the [[Construction Research Station]] in the [[Space Anomaly|Nexus]]. The Farmer NPC will require an [[Agricultural Terminal]] to provide research and mission capabilities. Although some of the farmable plants may be grown outdoors in the proper conditions, most farms are built inside [[Bio-Dome|Bio-Domes]], as they provide slots for [[Hydroponic Tray|Hydroponic Trays]] and more convenient harvesting.  Crops can also be grown on freighters using the dedicated [[Cultivation Chamber]] and [[Double Cultivation Chamber]].

An index of player farms can be found at [[Community Farms]].

## Crafting products

Here are the minimum number of resources required to set up a self-sustaining farm for one of each of the higher-end , with highest possible yield per hour:

The best value is Living Glass as it grows much more rapidly as both Liquid Explosive and Circuit Board grow in roughly 24 hours.

- [[Living Glass]] (1 hour / 4 hours)
  - Harvesting once every **1 hour** (partial harvest -> high cost, high yield): 1600 [[Gamma Root]], 800 [[Uranium]], 200 [[Frost Crystal]], 100 [[Dioxite]] and 320 [[Faecium]] => 32 [[Gamma Weed]], 4 [[Frostwort]] and 8 [[Gutrot Flower]]
  - Harvesting once every **4 hours** (full harvest -> low cost, medium yield): 400 [[Gamma Root]], 200 [[Uranium]], 200 [[Frost Crystal]], 100 [[Dioxite]] and 80 [[Faecium]] => 8 [[Gamma Weed]], 4 [[Frostwort]] and 2 [[Gutrot Flower]]

- [[Liquid Explosive]] (1 every 16 hours)
  - Harvesting once every **4 hours** (minimum resources): 150 [[Fungal Mould]], 75 [[Ammonia]], 200 [[Cactus Flesh]], 50 [[Pyrite]], and 40 [[Mordite]] = 3 [[Fungal Cluster]], 2 [[Echinocactus]] and 1 [[Mordite Root]]
  - Harvesting once every **16 hours** (full harvest): 600 [[Fungal Mould]], 300 [[Ammonia]], 200 [[Cactus Flesh]], 50 [[Pyrite]], and 40 [[Mordite]] = 12 [[Fungal Cluster]], 2 [[Echinocactus]] and 1 [[Mordite Root]]

- [[Circuit Board]] (1 every 16 hours)
  - Harvesting once every **4 hours** (minimum resources): 100 [[Star Bulb]], 50 [[Paraffinium]], 200 [[Solanium]], 100 [[Phosphorus]], 50 [[Frost Crystal]], 25 [[Dioxite]], 50 [[Cactus Flesh]] and 25 [[Pyrite]] = 3 [[Solar Vine]], 1 [[Star Bramble]], 1 [[Frostwort]] and 1 [[Echinocactus]]
  - Harvesting once every **16 hours** (full harvest): 400 [[Star Bulb]], 200 [[Paraffinium]], 200 [[Solanium]], 100 [[Phosphorus]], 100 [[Frost Crystal]], 50 [[Dioxite]], 100 [[Cactus Flesh]] and 50 [[Pyrite]] = 4 [[Solar Vine]], 8 [[Star Bramble]], 2 [[Frostwort]] and 1 [[Echinocactus]]

## Additional information

- Resources can be harvested by anyone finding a working farm, not just the player who created and planted it.
