# Ferrite

**Ferrite** is a general resource category. It can refer to any of the following forms, listed in their order of purity:

- [[Ferrite Dust]] - a raw mineral element found in the wild (Fe)
- [[Pure Ferrite]] - a processed metal, free of impurities (Fe+)
- [[Magnetised Ferrite]] - a highly process metal created in a [[Refiner]] (Fe++)
