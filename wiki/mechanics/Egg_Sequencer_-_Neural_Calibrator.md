# Egg Sequencer - Neural Calibrator

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Genetic Sequencer
- **Description:** Neural Calibrator
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Egg Sequencer - Neural Calibrator** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Neural Calibrator ** is a modification capability, used in the [[Egg Sequencer]] to modify the traits of a [[Companion Egg]].

There are different temperaments for Companion creatures, each giving the player a more or less desirable trait they need in a creature.  The use of different items in the egg sequencer will change the resulting creature temperament.

## Temperaments

**Gentleness** controls how aggressive it is towards other creatures. Increasing it makes them more docile while decreasing it makes them more aggressive. A more aggressive Companion will be marked with **Aggression** in the [[Companion Registry]]. Gentleness will make a creature scan for resources more often, while aggression will suppress this action more often. Higher aggression also increases the creature's want for attention from you. Aggressive creatures tend to randomly hunt for food, somewhat decreasing the need to feed them (but randomly slaying nearby docile creatures in the process).

**Independence** controls how often the companion needs attention from you. **Devotion** increases it, but Independence decreases it. Independence will increase the radius of which the creature will listen to commands from you while the opposite decreases it.

**Helpfulness** controls how often Companions help you with their unique abilities. Increasing it will make them do these abilities more often while decreasing them will make them goof around more. A less helpful Companion will be marked **Playful** in the Companion Registry. Playfulness increases how often a creature will do a 'playful' action. A creature with complete playfulness will not help you out whatsoever.

## Temperament Modification

A single egg can be repeatedly treated with mutagens before hatching, each cycle stacking on top of the previous one (even if the tooltip doesn't show it explicitly, currently listing only the *latest* cycle). Every cycle of modification causes a 20% shift in temperament, provided the mutagen is present in a 100% dose. This means that, for example, if there is a 100% aggressive creature egg at the start, five cycles of 100% dosage decreasing its aggressiveness will yield a "purely neutral" creature, while ten cycles will result in a 100% gentle creature.

Neural Calibrator shows shifts referencing the **initial** trait of the egg. When an original egg has certain trait (for example, "Aggressive"), mutagens increasing Gentleness will be shown to "Decrease Aggressiveness" instead. This is essentially the same, as temperaments in corresponding pairs are polar opposites and thus mutually exclusive. If after a successful mutation 2nd generation is Gentle, the Calibrator will switch to indicating changes in relation to "Gentle" (the same applies if a current egg already shifted its characteristics due to previous sequencing cycle).

Using low-tier raw materials as mutagens is generally not very effective. Materials that belong to the same element tend to give similar results in the Neural Calibrator, but refined varieties are more effective (i.e. both Ferrite Dust and Magnetized Ferrite increase Helpfulness / decrease Playfulness, but one requires a smaller amount of refined materials to achieve the same result). Materials that belong to the same type (i.e. "Localized Earth Element": Pyrite, Phosphorus, Dioxite, Uranium etc) tend to be equal in potency (meaning you need the same amount to get a 100% dose) but differ in effects.

Using highly refined products, both crafted and cooked, tends to make modification even faster (but still varies from item to item).

Amount of mutagens required to get a 100% dosage seems to differ between different companion eggs. Determining the reason requires more research (weight?).
