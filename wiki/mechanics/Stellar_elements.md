# Stellar elements

**Stellar elements** are one of the eight divisions of the resource.

## Summary

**Stellar elements** are [[resource|resources]] commonly used in [[refinement]] and they are the essential ingredients for refining [[Chromatic Metal]].

There are two types, activated and non-activated.  Activated stellar elements can only be used by refinement.  The non-activated elements can be used to craft various technologies and products.

## Sources

These elements are mainly obtained from [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] and [[Hotspot|Hotspots]].  The elements available in such cases depending on the [[System colours]] of the current [[star system]].  There are however also other ways to obtain non-activated stellar elements such as [[Copper]] in cave minerals, [[Emeril]] from certain tamed fauna, and [[Cadmium]] by dismantling pulse engine or warp drive upgrades.
