# Protection technology

**Protection technology** is [[technology]] that upgrades a player's [[exosuit]]. There are four types of Hazard Protections: Thermic Layer (coldness), Coolant Network (overheat), Radiation Deflector (Radiation) and Toxin Supressor (Toxication).
