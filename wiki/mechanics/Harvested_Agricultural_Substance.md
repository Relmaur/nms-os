# Harvested Agricultural Substance

**Harvested Agricultural Substance** is a type of resource.

## Summary

**Harvested Agricultural Substance** is a type of resource, grouped under the [[flora elements|flora]] division.

## Source

- Can be harvested in the wild. Most require [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet|Haz-Mat Gauntlets]] to handle.
- The Kelp Sac and Marrow Bulb can only be harvested wild, the rest can also be [[Farming|farmed]].
- Before they can be farmed, the assistance of the [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]] is needed.

## Use

- [[Crafting|Crafted]] into [[tradeable|tradeables]].
- [[Refining|Refined]] into other [[resource|resources]].
- [[Cooking|Cooked]] into [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]], [[Edible Product|Edible Products]], or [[Creature Pellets]].

## Members

- [[Cactus Flesh]]
- [[Faecium]]
- [[Frost Crystal]]
- [[Fungal Mould]]
- [[Gamma Root]]
- [[Kelp Sac]]
- [[Marrow Bulb]]
- [[Solanium]]
- [[Star Bulb]]

## Additional information

- Cactus Flesh and Kelp Sac don't require the Haz-Mat Gauntlet to harvest.
- All the farmable members of this item type are used in the making of valuable trade items such as [[Living Glass]], [[Liquid Explosive]] and [[Circuit Board]].
