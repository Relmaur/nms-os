# Junk

**Junk** is a type of resource.

## Summary

**Junk** is a type of resource in [[No Man's Sky]], grouped under the [[special elements]] division.

Resources in this type are mainly used for refinement, most of them directly or indirectly can be refined into [[Nanite Cluster]].

They can be purchased and sold at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and NPCs for a profit.

## Members

- [[Living Slime]]
- [[Residual Goop]]
- [[Rusted Metal]]
- [[Runaway Mould]]
- [[Viscous Fluids]]

## Additional information

- This resource type was introduced in [[Atlas Rises]].
- All of these resources have the same symbol (Jn) rather than their own.
