# Egg Sequencer - Gene Splitter

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Genetic Sequencer
- **Description:** Gene Splitter
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Egg Sequencer - Gene Splitter** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Gene Splitter ** is a modification capability, used in the [[Egg Sequencer]] to modify the traits of a [[Companion Egg]].

A gene splitter, when applied to the egg sequencer can modify the potential companion creature body parts.  The items selected to be used in the growth hormone slot of the Egg Sequencer will result in different size adjustments.

## Considerations

This part of the anomaly service changes body parts that a companion has. Use it at your own risk!

- Outcome will always be **Unstable**. If overdosed, an "Overdosed!" message will show up.
- Appendages, heads, minor body parts and even patterns can be affected.

In addition, the following information is based on testing from multiple sources, so they might not be accurate:

- Companions can turn into extremely rare variants of creatures if the same body plan. (Confirmation needed.)
- The eggs from these creatures if overdosed once more can change back to the grandparent's original form. (Confirmation needed.)
- In some cases, overdosing a companion will result in a completely different variation while giving a regular dose will sometimes result in minor changes.

### Results Catalog

Body and part changes follow a seemingly strict change ruleset depending on a specific variable (might be the type of creature, the individual appearance or something else, needs confirmation).

No matter the companion type, input material or dosage, the same changes will happen every time you use the Gene Splitter. Some companions, no matter the input material or dosage, will not change at all.

For example, a [[Mogara]] with a teddy head will get a turtle head, while a [[Diplo]] with a cobra hood will get a rat head.

For a community database of base creatures and results, see [[Egg Sequencer - Gene Splitter Results]].
