# AI Ship

**AI Ships** are a type of starship.

## Summary

**AI Ships** are an important part of the game. AI ships can be interacted with in many ways, these include: space battles, trading and buying the [[starship]] (small ships only).

## Types of AI ships

### Cargo ships

Cargo ships are ships owned by natural AI [[Faction|Factions]], and are similar to [[Freighter|Freighters]]. If attacked and damaged, cargo ships will drop cargo; however watch out as they will fight back and call the [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

### Pirates

[[Pirate|Pirates]] are AI ships that will attack players or cargo ships at random.

### Traders

Traders are small ships found flying around and they land on [[space station|space stations]] and any structures with a [[Landing Pad]]. Players can walk up to a ship and trade with it while it is landed and will have the option to buy the ship at that time. They also sometimes land on normal ground and sell illegal upgrade modules.

### Sentinels

Sentinels are also in space. They appear when a player receives a [[Wanted level]] while in space.

## Interacting with AI ships

### Trading

Players can trade with ships by talking with the owner.

### Space battles

[[Space battle|Space battles]] are massive battles players can fight in and join the side of one of the factions.

### Buying ships

Players can [[Buying a starship|purchase]] a small ship by talking to the owner.

## Release History

- [[Release]] - Introduced to the game.
- [[Origins]] - Can now land anywhere on the planet, not just trade outposts.
- [[Endurance]] - Can now be encountered mining asteroids.
