# Blueprint Trader

**Blueprint Traders** are vendors who allow you to purchase [[technology]] with [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]].

## Summary

Blueprint Traders can only be found in [[Minor Settlement|Minor Settlements]]. The Blueprint Trader themselves can be [[Gek]], [[Vy'keen]], or [[Korvax]], depending on the [[faction]] in that system. They can be found in the same room with a Trade Terminal. They provide a wide range of technologies and upgrades which can only be purchased with Nanite Clusters.

## What they sell

Blueprint Traders sell a wide variety of technology for your [[Multi-tool]], [[Exosuit]],  [[freighter]], and [[starship]]. Each Blueprint Trader will have 2 pages of technologies, allowing up to 10 items. However, the list can contain items you've already purchased, even if you are not in the same system you purchased it in. In order to buy the more expensive items, you must have a high standing with the faction that owns the system.

Vy'keen traders tend to have more multi-tool technology, Gek have more starship technology, and Korvax will have more exosuit technology.

### Starship

- [[Economy Scanner]] (Only in **Gek** systems)
- [[Conflict Scanner]] (Only in **Vy'keen** systems)
- [[Cyclotron Ballista]] (And upgrades)
- [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]] (and upgrades)
- [[Positron Ejector]] (And upgrades)
- Any other technology that you have not gained yet (including Warp Drive upgrades)

### Multi-tool

- [[Blaze Javelin]] (And upgrades)
- [[Pulse Spitter]] (And upgrades)
- [[Scatter Blaster]] (And Upgrades)
- Any other technology that you have not gained yet.

### Exosuit

- Any technology that you have not gained yet.

## Additional Information

- Only place where you can spend Nanite Clusters, beside [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]].
- Some of these upgrades can be obtained through other game mechanics.
