# Technology Merchant

**Technology Merchant** is a merchant NPC.

## Summary

**Technology Merchant** is a merchant NPC that can be found in some of the small planetary buildings with [[Landing Pad|Landing Pads]] and on [[space station|space stations]].
They sell technology blueprints for [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]] and products for [[units]].

## Space Station Technology Merchant

There are four different Technology Merchants that can be found on the space station based on what type of blueprint they sell:
- [[Exocraft]]
- [[Exosuit]]
- [[Multi-tool]] - Also can be used to upgrade existing multi-tool class and slot sizes. See [[Multi-Tool Upgrade Station]]
- [[Starship]]

Their blueprint list can contain items the player might have already purchased in another [[star system]].

## Planetary Technology Merchant

On planets, Technology Merchant can be found in some of the small planetary buildings with Landing Pads and [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]]. They can sell:
- Multi-tool, exosuit or starship [[blueprint|blueprints]]
- Various [[product|products]], such as [[component|components]], [[consumable|consumables]], [[tradeable|tradeables]] or certain [[curiosity|curiosities]] (like [[Navigation Data]], [[Drop Pod Coordinate Data]])
