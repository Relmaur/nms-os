# Making Discoveries (Beyond)

**Making Discoveries** is an information panel.

## Summary

**Making Discoveries** is a display page referenced by the [[Game Content]] page.

## The Analysis Visor

- Scan animals, plants and minerals with the [[Analysis Visor]] (**Key F**). Once the Visor is activated, use LPM to analyse your target. [[Units]] are rewarded for each unique scan.
- Every discovery is logged on the [[Discoveries]] page (**Key ESC**). [[[Star system|Systems]] and [[planet|planets]] are automatically discovered when visited.
- Visit the Discoveries page to rename or upload your discoveries and earn [[Nanite Cluster|nanites]].
- Discovering all animal life on a planet rewards a large number of units.
- Scanning plants and minerals with the Analysis Visor (**Key F**) reveals additional information and may yield extra [[resource|resources]] when harvesting.
- Rare and/or large creatures are worth more when discovered than common or small creatures.
- [[Multi-Tool Upgrades|Upgrades]] can be installed in your [[Multi-tool]] to increase the reward from scanning creatures or plants.

## Finding Buildings

- The [[Cartographer]] on [[Space station|Space stations]] will supply [[Planetary Chart|Planetary Charts]] for specific buildings. Using these charts will mark a site on your Exosuit's HUD.
- Collect [[Navigation Data]] from the [[Beacon (Building)|beacon]] outside planetary buildings. Exchange this data with the cartographer for additional maps. The Cartographer will also sell charts for nanites.
- Large buildings create detectable signals, either through direct transmission or via their radar signature. When exploring on foot, use the Analysis Visor to search for nearby buildings, or build (**Key Z**) a [[Signal Booster]] to mark the closet structure to your position.
- Specialist navigation data reveals the location of [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]], which contain powerful [[Exosuit|Exosuit Upgrades]]. These should be decoded in a Signal Booster.

## Smaller Point of Interest

- Your compass is located at the top of the HUD. It will highlight nearby [[Point of Interest]] and [[Mission Board|mission objectives]].
- Use the Analysis Visor to identify and pinpoint planetary objects. Identified point of interest can be pinned to the Exosuit HUD with (**Key E**) for ease of navigation.
- Learn how to make [[Beacon (Building)|Beacons]] from the [[Scientist|Base Scientist]]. Beacons can be placed anywhere using **Key Z** and will be marked on your compass.
- The Signal Booster can be constructed to search for nearby point of interest.
- The Cartographer on Space Stations will supply Planetary Charts for specific buildings.
- Collect Navigation Data from the beacons outside planetary buildings, and from modules inside buildings.
- Exchange data with the Cartographer.
- Some point of interest, like your [[ship]] and [[base]], will always be tracked.
- In space or atmospheric flight, follow the trails left by other ships to locate planetary [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]].
