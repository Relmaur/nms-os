# Iteration: Mercury

**Iteration: Mercury** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Mercury** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Interactions

Mercury is a Teleportation Terminal guardian, and can assist with [[Featured Base|Featured Bases]].

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Mercury Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Mercury may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
