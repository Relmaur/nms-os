# Artifact Exchange Vault

**Artifact Exchange Vault** is a terminal at a point of interest.

## Summary

**Artifact Exchange Vault** is a [[terminal]] at a [[Colossal Archive]].

## Function

The traveller is invited to exchange an existing artifact, with the potential reward of an exchanged artifact of unknown value.

The traveller can provide a [[Biological Sample]], [[Fossil Sample]], [[Historical Document]] or a [[Lost Artifact]] for an exchange: a [[Biological Sample]], [[Fossil Sample]], [[Historical Document]], [[Lost Artifact]] or an [[Unearthed Treasure]] of equal or greater quality.

## Additional Notes

- There are 3 Vaults located within a Colossal Archive - Two on the ground floor on hallways, underneath the landing pads and one that visually appears as two on the upper catwalk.
- Each building has specific artifacts that it can take (not type), so if you see the type of artifact box greyed out on a dialogue despite having the required type of artifact in the inventory, then that building cannot take that artifact. Instead, search for a new building to see if it takes it.
