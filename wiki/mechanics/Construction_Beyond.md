# Construction (Beyond)

**Construction** is an information panel.

## Summary

**Construction** is a display page referenced by the [[Game Content]] page.

## Portable technology

- Technology deployed on the planet's surface from the [[Build Menu]] (**Key Z**) may be recovered by approaching and holding **Scroll Mouse**. This places the technology in user's Exosuit [[Inventory]].
- [[Dismantle|Dismantling]] technology offers a convenient way for the user to transport and redeploy the technology at a location of their choosing.
- Redeploying portable technology incurs no additional costs. The unit only needs to be constructed once.
- **WARNING:**: Do not forget location of your deployed technology.

## Powering the Base

- Many pieces of base technology require [[Power]] Modules to run. They must be wired up to a grid of Generators and [[Battery|Batteries]]. Large bases with lots require several generators to stay online.
- Wiring is free to place. Select [[Electrical Wiring]] in the Build Menu (**Key Z**) and place on a plug socket to begin laying wires. Snap wires directly to another plug socket or place along the ground to lay out interim paths.
- Wires can be connected to large structure using a Transformer. Large structures will distribute power to all technology placed inside them.
- Different generators have different capabilities. The [[Biofuel Reactor]] requires manual fueling. [[Solar Panel|Solar Panels]] generate power automatically, but only during the day. The [[Electromagnetic Generator]] generates power continuously, but must  be sited on special power [[hotspot|hotspots]] located with the [[Survey Device]] upgrade for the [[Analysis Visor]].
- Batteries store surplus power from the grid and redistribute in them required. They are particularly efficient when used with [[Solar Panel|Solar Panels]].

## Base Construction

- Any site can be claimed for construction using the [[Base Computer]]. Multiple sites can be claimed across many or one world.
- Expand your base with the Build Menu (**Key Z**). New base parts require resources to build.
- [[Dismantle|Dismantling structures]] within your base refunds the resources.
- Most structures can only be built in the vicinity of your base. Some, such as [[Signal Booster|Signal Boosters]], can be built anywhere.
- Use the Analysis Visor (**Key F**) and the [[Terrain Manipulator]] to find [[Buried Technology Module|Buried Technology Modules]]. [[Salvaged Data]] extracted from these devices can be spent on new Construction [[Blueprint|Blueprints]].
- [[Specialist]] Terminals can be built in your base. Workers can be recruited from space stations to operate the terminal. They offers missions to research advanced base parts and equipment.
- Whilst onboard a space station, locate a Terminus [[Teleporter]] for teleportation to and from your base. The Terminus is on the left side of the space station, near the Mission Agent.
