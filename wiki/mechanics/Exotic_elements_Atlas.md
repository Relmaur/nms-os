# Exotic elements (Atlas)

**Exotic elements** are one of the original five divisions of the [[periodic table]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

Exotic elements (formerly known as Precious elements) are very rare, and extremely lucrative when sold in [[Galactic trade terminal|Galactic markets]].

All elements in the Exotic family have an icon with a purple background when shown in the players inventory, or on the No Man's Sky periodic table.

When a [[Scanner|scan]] is taken of an area, locations with Exotic elements are tagged with a purple diamond-shaped icon.

## Members

Known members of the  family are [[Omegon]] (Om), [[Radnox]] (Ra), [[Murrine]] (Mu), and [[Calium]] (Ca).

The [[Update 1.10|Foundation Update]] added the elements [[Cymatygen]] (Cy), [[Kelp Sac]] (Ks), [[Rubeum]] (Ru), and [[Viridium]] (Vi).
