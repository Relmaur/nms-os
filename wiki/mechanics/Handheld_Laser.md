# Handheld Laser

**Handheld Laser** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

The Handheld Laser, which is a part of the [[multi-tool]], was first seen during a gameplay reveal on [[Illicies|Illicles]]. Since then, it has been widely speculated to be the primary weapon of No Man's Sky, though the developers have been relatively tight-lipped concerning the weapon. It has also been suggested as a mining tool, though there is little evidence to back up this claim.

The laser option of the multi-tool is known as *LAZEUS*.
