# Multi-tools - Alien (Pathfinder)

**Multi-tools - Alien (Pathfinder)** is a catalogue page.

## Summary

Most Alien multitools fall into four sub-categories, all based on the appearance of the muzzle.

One of the distinguishing features of alien [[multi-tool]] are the claw-like protrusions that sprout from the weapon. The claw may be part of Vy'keen heritage, as one of the passages in the [[Saga of the Vy'keen]] refers to "*the claws of tim*e".
