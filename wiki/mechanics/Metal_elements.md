# Metal elements

**Metal elements** are one of the eight divisions of the [[resource|resources]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

**Metal elements** are mainly used as an ingredient for [[refining]] and they may also be used for [[crafting]] various products.

## Sources

These elements can be found as a primary/secondary element in many [[mineral|minerals]]. Can also be refined, collected from [[pirate|pirates]], or found in [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]].
