# Damaged Machinery

**Damaged Machinery** is an [[Point of Interest|artificial resource]].

## Summary

**Damaged Machinery** can be found in various locations in [[No Man's Sky]]. These mechanical pods are often located near [[Crashed Ship|Crashed Ships]], [[Crashed Freighter|Crashed Freighters]], and [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]].

They are also commonly found scattered around the planet surface. If you look through your Analysis Visor, they will show up on your [[Heads-Up Display]] with an icon shaped like a blue cog. You can also spot them by their blue glow and the dark smoke spiraling into the air. Note that fixing them removes the black smoke.

## Contents

Repaired Damaged Machinery will contain this loot-table regardless of component type and will pull from it once repaired:

| Item | Quantity | Chance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [[Nanite Cluster\|Nanite Clusters]] | 25-34 | 82% |
| [[Starship Launch Fuel]] | 1 | 10% |
| [[Upgrade Module]] | 1 | 4% |

Living slime, viscous fluids, residual goop and rusted metal can also be found in some damaged machinery and need to be removed.

## Repair

Damaged Machinery has one, randomly chosen [[Damaged Component]] which need to repaired.
