# Deepwater Guardian

**Deepwater Guardian** is a miniboss.

## Summary

**Deepwater Guradian** is a miniboss that takes the form of a jellyfish. These boss creatures do not spawn naturally, and can only be summoned by picking up and releasing a [[Child of Aquarius]] or [[Spawn of Arcadia]] back into the water; the [[Child of Aquarius]] can be caught anywhere.

## Summoning

Releasing a Child of Aquarius or Spawn of Arcadia summons the Deepwater Guardian, which will then emerge from the water and immediately begin attacking the player.

The Child of Aquarius can be caught on any planet in any conditions and can be released in any body of water.

## Combat Behaviour

The Deepwater Guardian features a two-part HUD, indicating the status of the Guardian's primary health in red, and the countdown until it spawns more jellyfish and/or fires projectiles in yellow.

It looms around summoning smaller jellyfish called **Child of the Waves**, which slowly approach the player and proceed to explode, dropping [[Shattered Qualia]] upon death. As the Guardian attacks, it sends a barrage of 6-8 electrical projectiles which lock onto the player, however these projectiles are relatively slow, so they can be outrun using the jetpack.

The Deepwater Guardian can be damaged at any time as it does not possess any sort of armour.

## Additional Information

- Upon defeating the Deepwater Guardian, the player will receive an exclusive [[Jellyfish Tank Helmet]] for the [[Anomaly]] race, and a unique **Child of Aquarius** title as rewards.
