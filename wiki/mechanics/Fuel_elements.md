# Fuel elements

**Fuel elements** are one of the eight divisions of the [[resource|resources]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

**Fuel elements** are mainly used as an ingredient for crafting or refining into various [[product|products]].

Also [[glyph 1 / 16]], [[glyph 4 / 16]], [[glyph 7 / 16]], [[glyph 10 / 16]] and [[glyph 13 / 16]] are need to be charged with these type of elements, before a [[portal]] could be activated.

## Sources

These elements can be found as a primary/secondary element in [[flora]], or as [[Mineral|crystal minerals]]. They can be acquired through refinement as well.
