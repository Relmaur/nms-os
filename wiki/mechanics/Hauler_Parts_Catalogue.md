# Hauler Parts Catalogue

**Hauler Parts Catalogue** is an index page.

## Summary

This page contains a list of wing types and parts for the [[Starship Catalogue - Hauler|Hauler]] version of the [[starship]].

In total, there are 14 wing shapes, six cockpits, and three tails. Depending on the model, one ship can display multiple parts. The names used are not game based, but are generally accepted by the community.

**Note:** the items in each list are sorted in order by size.

The six cockpits provide the customized appearance of each starship model.

### Necks

The cockpit can be mounted to these two necks:

- Pelican/Goose Neck
- Long Neck

## Tails

Hauler starships are powered by one of three different tail types:

- Short Tail
- Box Tail
- Long Tail

### Tail Wings

The tail can have one of these two wing types attached:

- Aftershock
- V-Wing

## Inventory Sizes

- Any hauler with D-Flect or W-Wings has a medium inventory.
- Any hauler with Fan Wings has a large inventory.
- Box and Ball wings have large inventories, except if they have shields or split shields attached (V-Wings don't fall into this category). In that case they become small.
- Any hauler that does not fit any of the previous cases will have a small inventory.
