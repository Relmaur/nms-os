# Spawn point

A **Spawn point** mark the beginning of a new game.

## Summary

**Spawn points** play a major decision on your game play. Every spawn point is different and you cannot spawn in the same place as another player.

## Differences in spawn points

Each spawn point is different. There is no way for your initial spawn to be on the same planet as a different user. You may spawn on a beautiful tropical rich-in-resources planet, however another player may spawn on a barren harsh planet with barely any resources!
Spawn Points are usually around 700,000 LY from the Galaxy centre, but can be anywhere in the galaxy!

## What you get when you spawn

When you spawn you receive: a [[Life Pod]], a Basic [[Multi-tool]] and no [[Units]] or [[Hyperdrive technology|hyper-drive]]. You must gather resources and trade and work your way to getting better upgrades to progress in the game.
