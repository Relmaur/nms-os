# Egg Sequencer

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Genetic Sequencer
- **Description:** Allows editing of Companion eggs
- **Added in:** Frontiers

The **Egg Sequencer** is a Space Anomaly service.

## Summary

The **Egg Sequencer** is a [[Space Anomaly]] service for editing the genes of creature [[Companion Egg|Companion Eggs]]. Four different aspects can be edited at a given time: Weight/Height, Color, Temperament, and the option to Splice Genes. These changes can be used to make more desirable creatures, or even new ones.

Items are used in the process of editing the eggs genes. Different items have different effects in each of the four slots, and the scale/intensity of these effects is different depending on the item and amount of that item used.

## Types of Modifications

Companion Eggs can be modified as many times as the player wishes, and each modification stacks on previous modifications. This can allow a player to set personality attributes to 100% (achieving desired stats as fast as the first creature generation), change body styles and parts (unpredictable results), change colours, or change the potential creature size.

## Additional Information

The small phrase written on the bottom right of the sequencer reads "Korvet 2000", and the sentence on the floor reads "Treat your egg to a fresh pair of genes".
