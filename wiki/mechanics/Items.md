# Items

**Items** is a collective term for the ingredients and technologies .

## Summary

**Items** is a collective term for all the possible ingredients and technologies the player can collect during their exploration of the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Ingredients

### Resources

[[Resource|Resources]] are the primary materials for [[multi-tool]], [[starship]], [[exosuit]] and [[exocraft]] upgrades, as well as [[base building]].

### Products

[[Product|Products]] are useful or valuable materials. Some of these can be crafted using various ingredients in the player's inventory using [[blueprint|blueprints]].
