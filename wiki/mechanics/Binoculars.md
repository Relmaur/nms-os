# Binoculars

**Binoculars** are a basic level of [[Scan technology|scanner technology]].

## Summary

Binoculars are more than a simple scanner. They can zoom in, show the distance between the player and objects and lock on to a building or [[landmark]]. If the item turns out to be an object of interest, it can be marked as a [[Waypoints]] for later study. It will then appear on the compass semicircle at the top of the view screen.

## Scanning lifeforms

Binoculars may also be used for scanning new plants and animals [[species]]. Once the red crosshairs are centred on the object and it shows between the two brackets, the analyzing process begins, followed by a white-line scan. Once the creature has been identified, the player is notified if this is a new discovery. If so, they may chose to [[Naming rights|rename]] it, within the game's naming restrictions.

## Speculation

The fact that other scanner technology attachments for the [[multi-tool]] can be upgraded, makes it likely that binoculars can be as well.

It is unknown if the player will be given a basic set of Binoculars by default or it will be available only through [[blueprint|blueprints]] or by trading with one of the existing NPC [[alien]] races.
