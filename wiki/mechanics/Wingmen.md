# Wingmen

**Wingmen** are a type of NPC.

## Summary

**Wingmen** are AI controlled [[starship|starships]] that automatically warp to your location during space combat, and can be summoned to fly into formation at any time.

## Access

They can be unlocked at the [[Fleet Command Room|Manage Fleet]] terminal in the [[Freighter]] by spending [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]]. A maximum of 4 Wingmen can be recruited.

## Additional information

This feature was intended for the initial release, but was not included at launch.
