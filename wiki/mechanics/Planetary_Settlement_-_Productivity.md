## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - Productivity** contains a detailed description of the characteristics of the [[Planetary Settlement]]'s Productivity.

It is measured in  Units per (real life) day. Productivity is the passive income generated by the settlement.

The net gain is reduced by Maintenance Cost.
