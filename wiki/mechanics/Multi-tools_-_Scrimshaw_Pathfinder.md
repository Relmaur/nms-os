# Multi-tools - Scrimshaw (Pathfinder)

&lt;hr>

## Scrimshaw

The primary distinguishing feature of this type of [[multi-tool]] is the decorative engravings and fanciful patterns on the body of the tool. Many of these tools were found in the [[Hilbert Dimension]] galaxy.

These usually fall into the Experimental category.
