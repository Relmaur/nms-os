# Fighter Parts Catalogue

**Fighter Parts Catalogue** is an index page.

## Summary

This page contains a list of body shapes and parts for the [[Fighter]] version of the [[starship]].

In total, there are nine body shapes, 18 auxiliary parts, and three engines. Depending on the model, one ship can display multiple parts. The names used are not game based, but are generally accepted by the community.

The 19 Auxiliary parts provide the customized appearance of each starship model. They can be combined in many ways, and most starships will feature multiple items from this list.
