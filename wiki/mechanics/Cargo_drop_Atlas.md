# Cargo drop (Atlas)

**Cargo Drop** boxes are items that can be found on [[planet|planets]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Summary

There are six types of drop boxes that can be found in *No Man's Sky.* Colors and sizes differ, making them easy to identify. They can literally be found everywhere on a planet except for [[cave|caves]] and underwater. It's easiest to find them by looking for a [[Waypoints]], as cargo boxes will always spawn nearby, and in bigger groups.

## Damaged Container

This box, once opened, will pop up a GUI with an option to either dismantle or transfer to your inventory 50-100 units of [[Rusted Metal]]. It will then open the box and may give you a commodity item, such as a [[Gek Relic]] or [[Korvax Convergence Cube]].

Effective in trade and also some are required to get a [[Portal]] location from a [[Monolith]].

## Jettison Pod

This cargo box is round, and yellow. Once opened it will give you a [[Power Canister]].

Effective for refueling [[multi-tool]] equipment.

## Cargo Drop

This cargo box is square, and yellow. Once opened it can give you a [[Shielding Shard]], a random [[Alloy]] (rare), and sometimes up to 50 [[Titanium]].

Effective for refueling [[Hazard Protection|hazard protection]] technology. The alloys are also effective in trade.

## Isotope

This cargo box is the smallest and can come in two colors: red is more common, while yellow is rare. Even though it is called [[Isotope elements|Isotope]], once opened it will always give you up to 50 [[Plutonium]].

Effective for multi-tool equipment and [[Launch Thruster|Launch Thrusters]].

## Search

This cargo box is tubular, tall, and a dark shade of red. It requires an [[AtlasPass v1]] to open. It is probably the most useful as it contains ingredients for a [[Warp Cell]], and sometimes even a Warp Cell itself.

Can contain [[Suspension Fluid]], [[Electron Vapour]], [[Antimatter]], or a Warp Cell (rare).

## Health Restorative

This cargo box is square, and white. Once opened it will raise your health levels.

## Damaged Container Items

Damaged Containers can contain one of the following items: (+ up to 100 Rusted Metal)
- [[Vy'keen Dagger]] (common)
- [[Grahgrah|GrahGrah]] (uncommon)
- [[Korvax Casing]] (rare)
- [[Korvax Convergence Cube]] (common)
- [[Gek Charm]] (common)
- [[GekNip]] (uncommon)
- [[Gek Relic]] (rare)
- [[Fascination Bead]] (uncommon)
- [[Night Crystals]] (very rare)
- "None Found" (very rare)

## Additional information

- The green cargo box has been renamed *Damaged Container* in the [[Atlas Rises]] update, and you can now obtain Rusted Metal upon opening it.
- It is possible to destroy these boxes with your [[Plasma Launcher]].
- Can spawn in cave openings, but not in caves themselves or underwater.
