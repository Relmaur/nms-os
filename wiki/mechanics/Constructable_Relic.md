# Constructable Relic

**Constructable Relic** is a type of Base Building component.

## Summary

**Constructable Relic** are building recipes introduced in World Parts II. They can be discovered on [[Biome - The Reliquary|Reliquary]] planets. Use the Visor and look for a [[Memory Stone]]. Interact with it to absorb ancient knowledge: It gives you a building blueprint or Nanites.

To build one relic in your base, you need the blueprint, 25 [[Magnetised Ferrite]], and 25 [[Polished Stone]] harvested from [[Ruined Structure|Ruined Structures]].
