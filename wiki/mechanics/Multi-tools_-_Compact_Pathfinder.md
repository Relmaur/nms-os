# Multi-tools - Compact (Pathfinder)

The most distinguishing feature of this type of [[multi-tool]] is size, as they are noticeably smaller than most other weapons. Many of the [[Vy'keen]] multi-tools fall into this category, as their warlike leanings tend to produce more exotic and efficient weapons. One of the more unusual multi-tools is the globular [[R/0 Strange Riposte]].

These usually fall into either Pistol or Experimental categories.
