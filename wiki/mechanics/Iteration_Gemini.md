# Iteration: Gemini

**Iteration: Gemini** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Gemini** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Gemini Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Gemini may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
