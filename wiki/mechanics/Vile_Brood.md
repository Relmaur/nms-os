# Vile Brood

**Vile Brood** is a game mechanic.

## Summary

**Vile Brood** is a property that appears on some planets. The affected planets contain [[Juicy Grub|Juicy Grubs]] which can be picked up and crushed to summon a [[Brood Mother]] miniboss.

## Locating

Planets with vile brood display "Vile Brood Detected" in their discovery menu and when scanned from space.
