# Technology Trees

**Technology Trees** is a game mechanics page.

## Summary

**Technology trees** are the path that you can unlock technology and types of buildings.

## Where Found

They are displayed when when talking to certain vendors, the [[Construction Research Station]] in the [[Space Anomaly]], and the [[Construction Research Unit]] you can build in a base.

The anomaly can be unlocked by progressing the Artemis path quest.
