# Bounty

**Bounties** are a fund-raising opportunity.

## Summary

**Bounties** are opportunities to make units by destroying fugitive [[starship|starships]]. They are random events with three levels of difficulty and reward.

## Bounty levels

- Mostly Harmless: A single 3 star pirate. Rewards 100,000 units
- Dangerous: A single skull rank pirate. Rewards 200,000 Units
- Elite: A skull rank pirate and a 0 star pirate. Rewards 300,000 Units

## Faction standings

Destroying ships with bounties on them also increases the standing with the current [[star system|star systems']] [[faction]].
