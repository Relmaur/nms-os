# Freighter Base Building

**Freighter Base Building** describes base building aboard a freighter.

## Summary

**Freighter Base Building** describes [[base building]] aboard a [[freighter]]. Seen from inside the freighter's bridge, opposite the captain's seat, beyond the glowing green steps, is an editable area for the player. Facilities and structures can be rebuilt. The system is very different from the normal [[Base building]]. The building of the freighter base can only be assembled in a predetermined grid shape. The height of the first floor is also predetermined. The specifications are also different in terms of power.

In addition, at the freighter depot, many equipment is prepared as a type of room part. Each installation occupies one square of the grid, and it is treated like a dedicated room or built-in furniture.

## Atmosphere

Overall, it is easy to create a shape that seems to be in the image of a lunar base or an undersea base, where each block grows horizontally from the main passage. Of course, it is not limited to that, you can create a three-dimensional enclosed space like a ship, or conversely, you can create a shopping mall-like structure that emphasizes a sense of freedom.

Some of the building parts are treated as [[Exterior Platform]] and have an exterior look. There, under the starry sky, you can stand and walk outside. Although it is in outer space, it is not subject to abnormalities such as rapid wear and tear of [[life support]] systems and low gravity of operability. However, there is a risk that you will not be able to return to the freighter if you deviate too much. In that case, you can restore it by "Reset Freighter Position" in "Option → General".

Lifeforms roam around in the base you have built. Not only the crew of the freighter, but also the captain of the [[frigate]], the scouted squadron, and other persons invited by the player walk around the base. It's a function that emphasizes visuals, and it doesn't mean that you can do anything, but you can enjoy watching the inside of the freighter become bustling as your army expands. Changing the base structure also changes the range and number of people who move around. Unfortunately, you can't call a companion.

## Relationship

A freighter base is a base "on a freighter". However, systematically, it is tied to "save data" rather than "to ship".

The edit range is the same for any ship. The base can be transferred to a new freighter. However, there can be troubles. When a freighter is replaced, the base can be taken over by a new freighter. However, as of v4.08, this is not "automatically relocated", but "automatically relocated when accepted". Therefore, if you choose not to relocate on the confirmation screen, it will not be handed over.

When you talk to the captain of a freighter and "purchase a freighter", you will be taken to a screen where you can see the performance of the cargo ship.

- If you press the "Compare" button, you will see a screen where you can transfer items from the current ship to the new ship.
- If you select "Exchange" there, you will be asked if you do not need to transfer the item.

If you accept the confirmation, you will be asked if you want to relocate the freighter base. If this second check is wrong, it will not be relocated. When the replacement is completed (regardless of what you did with the item or base), a manual save (overwriting the restore point) is automatically performed.

Even if the design is unexpected, it may not be relocated. If you answer "Yes/No" with the cancel button. It's unconfirmed, but v5.29 Cross Save, it may not be a problem as of now. At the time of the replacement, the original freighter was not loaded with any luggage. In some cases, the screen to confirm whether to relocate the cargo ship base was not displayed and the relocation was not carried out. If you're worried, you may want to back up your save data from just before the cargo ship was replaced.

## Editing range

Starting from one square right next to the green glowing doorway, you can build up to 21 squares in the stern direction, up to 10 squares in the port direction, and up to 10 squares in the starboard direction (21 squares wide × 22 depths in total). However, one square that corresponds to the edge of the limit cannot be built "except for rooms, stairs, and decorative parts" (catwalks and passages cannot be placed). Catwalks and walkways can only be built within a width of 19 × a depth of 21).

The starting point is always on the first floor. From there, it can be extended to 15 steps only upward, for a total of 16 floors (stairs and ladders to the 17th floor cannot be installed).

There is no concept of a basement floor downward from the first floor. However, there is a small space under the floor at the bottom of the first floor. You can use it to "put a light fisher at your feet" even on a single floor.

If it's a portable device or a decorative part, it can also be placed near a glowing green [[Bulkhead Door]]. It can be displayed up to the bridge side of the entrance/exit, but its horizontal range is quite small, only a few steps. However, there seems to be no restriction on the top and bottom, and you can put things on the second floor of the bridge as long as it is near the entrance.

## Major Bugs

As of v4.10, there are still some unstable parts in the freighter base.

### Terrain Stuck Bug

If you place a short-range teleporter at the edge of a room, you may get stuck in the wall when teleporting. You will be stuck, but you can escape by going to "Options → General → Other → Reset position in freighter". In v4.08-4.09, this was the most dangerous bug because there was no way to escape as described above.

### Disappearance of Refiner/Nutrient Processor Contents

Even with the exact same equipment in the exact same base, this may or may not occur depending on the situation: As of v4.08, there is no known solution to the problem of the contents of [[Refining|Refiner]]/[[Nutrient Processor]] disappearing.

On the other hand, there is a way to check in advance whether the contents will disappear later if you use it now. Open the equipment screen, put in something you don't care about, and cancel the equipment screen. When you open the equipment screen again, check whether the previously inserted items have disappeared. If they have not disappeared, then it is okay for now. However, if you load a save data or restart the game, you will need to retest (confirmed in v4.12). Although it has not been verified, it may be the same for inter-system travel.

### Stellar Extraction Efficiency Instability

When there are multiple machines, some machines do not accumulate properly, while others seem to accumulate normally, and for some reason the results are inconsistent. It seems that depending on the installation location, number of machines, and base structure, some machines may not function properly.

### Save Points Hidden

After opening and closing the [[frigate]] dispatch screen, the save points in the base are hidden. (They are just hidden, not disappeared, so going elsewhere and returning and reloading will fix it.) In rare cases, when the above conditions are met, the base itself may almost disappear. There is a possibility that the edit results will be overwritten while the bug remains, so it is recommended to immediately reload the previous save data.
The color of the freighter base parts may not change.

If you move away from the freighter base parts after changing their color, the change may not be reflected. It is only reflected visually, but it is reflected internally, so it will be reflected by reloading.

### Insufficient Power

This can be fixed by saving, waiting for the save completion message to appear, and reloading the data from the options screen. This seems to happen more often right after performing a base reset.

## Functions

The freighter base is a way to add functions to your freighter. The main ones are as follows.

### Storage Box

This corresponds to a [[Storage Container]]. While on a freighter (within the same star system if a material transport beam is installed), a storage container tab will be added to your [[Inventory]]. This makes resource management much easier. Also, while standing inside the freighter base, you will be able to directly select a storage container as the item transfer destination.

If you install the technology [[Matter Beam]] in your freighter's Inventory, it will be even more convenient, so you should get it as a top priority.

### Fleet Command Room

A facility required to launch frigates and obtain deliverables. Used by the player to communicate with frigate squadrons that have already departed. Note that the squadrons do not depart from here, but are instead initiated by talking to the navigator on the freighter bridge. After acquiring a freighter, you will build one in the tutorial mission "[[Freighter Command]]" and try using it.

The first fleet command room you built at that time can be dismantled and rebuilt later without any problems. The work done during the deployment will be properly carried over to the new location. (There have been reports of a bug where the deployment will not be completed if you remove it while it is deployed, so it is recommended to remove it when no one is deployed.) When you have all the materials, prepare a total of five. Depending on the player, you can build up to six to eight, but you may not use that many. If you consider the layout, six is easy to handle.

### Galactic Trade Room

Equivalent to a "[[Galactic Trade Terminal]]". If you have one on board, you can quickly convert the results of the frigate fleet into cash. It is also useful for replenishing small resources and parts.

### Teleport Chamber

Equivalent to a "[[Base Teleport Module]]". After placing this, the freighter will gradually become an important base of movement.

In the past, many players would use one of the planetary bases as their main base and go from there to do various errands (the freighter was just one of the "errands"). However, since v3.94, you can now fly directly to freighters using teleporters in various places, so freighter bases can now be considered as useful bases, or even more useful, than planetary bases. They can be used as a relay point for movement and item management in many ways.

Note that this freighter base is facing diagonally, so if you make just one of these rooms in a small area and connect it, it will be difficult to use. If possible, place it as part of a large room made up of 4 to 6 or more blocks. If you want to save space, make the room 4 blocks and place a scanner room or galactic trade room next to it to improve access. In that case, you can use the expansion parts at the entrance to make it easier to enter and exit.

### Orbital Exocraft Materialiser

If this is currently in the [[star system]], players will be able to call up their [[Exocraft]] from the [[Quick Menu]] as many times as they like.

This saves you the trouble of having to build a geobay or [[Exocraft Summoning Station]] every time you land on a [[planet]] (and then remember to retrieve it afterwards). You no longer need to carry materials to create a geobay, and it also frees up a small amount of item slots. As of v4.08, installing this device does not allow you to operate the exocraft's inventory from inside the freighter. It does not free you from the ownership check for each exocraft (i.e. whether or not you have left one geobay on the ground for each one). Therefore, a vehicle depot on the planet is not necessary.

### Scanner Room

A device that allows you to view the outline of the discovery information currently in the star system. In simple terms, it is the same as "having landed once on all planets and moons in the current star system". This is very helpful when searching for specific environments, resources, mechanical [[Fauna|creatures]], glitches, etc. However, since you cannot see what the scenery will look like when you actually land on the planet, it may not be very useful when searching for a potential base.

### Specialist Room

Equivalent to a "specialist terminal." You can now carry out planetary base-related missions in a corner of your freighter without having to worry about expanding your planetary base. After the mission is over, it will also help liven up your freighter base.

There are other facilities that can be placed. However, as of v4.08, the freighter base version of the refiner and nutrient processor may suddenly lose items that are being processed (this can happen even if [[multiplayer]] is disabled). Until this bug is fixed, it is better to process important items at a ground.

## Freighter Base Transfer And Reset

After obtaining a new freighter, the pre-generated base within is automatically inherited and you will lose any previous freighter base. Similar to the newly added base reset option (available at the Upgrade Terminal within the freighter), no materials will be refunded--not even to the [[Base Salvage Capsule]], wherein most deleted bases' materials are found. As such, it is recommended to dismantle your base entirely before moving freighters. Note that items left in cargo containers can be retrieved post-reconstruction.

Starting in [[Prisms]], freighter bases became transferable, and in fact were automatically transferred upon purchase of a new freighter.
