# Combat (Beyond)

**Combat** is an information panel.

## Summary

**Combat** is a display page referenced by the [[Game Content]] page.

## Ground Combat

- Fire your [[Multi-tool]] with LPM. When out of ammo or charge, use Key Q to [[Controls|meele strike]].
- [[Controls|Aim]] (Key F) and [[Controls|Zoom]] (RPM) to target enemies with precision.
- Cycle between **weapon modes** by using Key G.
- **Reload** your current weapon with Key R. Ensure you have crafted [[Amplified Cartridges|sufficient ammunition]] before entering a combat situation.
- Damage received while on-foot will first deplete your [[shield]]. Your shield will recharge over time. **Take cover** and avoid damage while the shield is low.
- Purchase plans for a [[Personal Forcefield]] at [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]]. This is a powerful combat shield deployed from the Multi-Tool.
