# Corvette

**Corvette** is a type of starship.

## Summary

The **Corvette** is a class of [[Starship]], introduced in [[Update 6.00|Voyagers Update]]. They are bigger than standard spacecrafts, but smaller than [[Freighter|freighters]] and [[Frigate|frigates]].

The key difference compared to other player operated starship is the corvette is nearly completely customisable. The operator/captain (you or a friend) can ferry other players as passengers to other [[planet|planets]], [[star system|star systems]], [[space station|space stations]], or freighters, etc.

Corvettes take up a starship slot. They can be exchanged or added to the player collection.

Unlike all other starships, the corvette cannot be found for acquisition in any other way in the game.

## Creating A Corvette

You can modify your corvette(s) at any time at the space station, so don't worry if your first one is not perfect, you can improve it.

### 1 Obtain modules

To build a corvette, you first need modules. Visit any populated space station, walk through the warp points on the left to be transported to the corvette platforms near the space station's exit, and interact with the Corvette Workshop console.

You have several options to get [[Corvette Construction Parts|Corvette Modules]]:
- Buy basic modules at the Corvette Workshop console.<br>They are expensive, only basic ones are available, but it's easy if it's your first.
- Trade modules at the Corvette Workshop console.<br>You can choose which modules you'll get, but you need many (1-3) spares to trade for one. Basic modules cannot be traded.
- Explore [[Salvaged Scrap]].<br>The modules are free, all types can be found, but it's random.
- Loot from pirate battles, frigate expeditions, missions, crashed freighters and derelict freighters.<br>The modules are free, all types can be found, but it's random.
- Additionally, [[Expedition 19: Corvette]] and [[Expedition 20: Breach]] provide unique module plan rewards.

At minimum, you must obtain:
- landing gear
- habitation module
- access module
- cockpit
- reactor
- main engine
- Weapon System

Optionally, obtain shields, windows, platings, hull attachments, flight stabilisers.

### Aside: Design Styles

Note: The modules have different names which imply different design styles. The functionality of the modules will be the same. Over time, as you collect more, you can swap them out and try different ones, and decide which style you like.
- Thunderbird - pragmatic and gritty with exposed pipework
- Ambassador - elegant, round, soft, clean, retro-futuristic
- Titan - militaristic, with many monitoring panels, buttons, gauges, screens
- Legion - sterile, sharp, high-tech with a lot of blinking electronics
At first, don't worry about it, just use which ever parts that you have, you can swap them out later.

Thunderbird-class corvette parts seem to be directly inspired by the [Frontier](https://starfield.fandom.com/wiki/Frontier) ship from Starfield with the interior strongly resembling such of a Millenium Falcon from Star-Wars, and the Legion-class interiors also seem to be a legally-distinct version of Imperial interior designs, while the Ambassdor and Titan class interiors seem to be directly inspired by various interiors from the Alien franchise, like the [USCSS Nostromo](https://avp.fandom.com/wiki/USCSS_Nostromo).

Some interior designs have typography similar to [Semiotic Standard](https://avp.fandom.com/wiki/Semiotic_Standard), with repeating signs that mean "WARNING/HAZARD", "RADIATION HAZARD" and an unknown sign that does not seem to exist in the symbol list. Some signs even appear to have different color, though the image itself is not changed.[https://avp.fandom.com/wiki/USCSS_Nostromo](https://avp.fandom.com/wiki/USCSS_Nostromo)

### 2 Create the Corvette

To build a corvette, return to the Corvette Workshop console in the space station and click Create New Corvette.
1. The console will switch you to a build mode.
1. Press Z to get the build menu.
1. Select and place the landing gear first.
1. On top of it place larger modules, such as the habitation.
1. Attach the access module to the habitation back.
1. Attach the cockpit module to the habitation front.
1. Add main engines and reactor where ever it looks good.
The ship will fly equally well whether it's symmetrical or asymmetrical.

As of [[Update 6.03]] there is a 160 part maximum.

Apply your changes. You can also store and continue later. Other players only see applied changes. A name for the ship will automatically be generated, which you can change later.

### 3 Decorate the interior

After the outer shape is finished and saved, enter the parked corvette and press Z inside. This opens an interior build menu. Continue to add a bed, refinery, cargo containers, or windows, these are corvette specific modules. Players can also add most buildable items that they'd use on a planetary base. These parts require materials, just as when you build a base. There is no part maximum for inside. (Other than the save-wide [[Base building|16.000 placeable objects limit]] )

Just as with a base, you can change the colour of some Corvette parts.

Note: The interior parts can look different in style, too, depending on whether you place them in a Thunderbird, Ambassador, or Titan habitation module.

## Mechanics

The simplest ship is just one habitation with landing gear, cockpit, thruster, weapon, and access ramp. You will not be able to finalise a build without these required modules, but the more modules you find, the bigger you can build!

Habitation modules have an impact on cargo size and outer Corvette size.
You can build several habitations, one after the other (horizontal), or one next to each other (horizontal), or on top of each other (vertical) to build larger corvettes.
- for horizontally aligned habitations, the game will insert passages for players to walk through.
- for vertically aligned habitations, enter the bottom floor and open the build menu, and select a ladder module, and place it to go upstairs.

You can only have one cockpit.

Reactor parts and the cockpit impact manoeuvrability. A maximum of 3 appear in the corvette technology slots. For example, in a new build add 3 Medusa Reactor before adding a cockpit and only the Medusa Reactors show up in the technology slots. With these reactor boost bonuses and maximum conventional Pulse Engine upgrades, an S-class corvette can have several thousand manoeuvrability.

Engines have an impact on your speed, you can have several, even a mix of types.

You can have a mix of landing gears.

You can have a mix of weapons but regardless of which parts you use a [[Photon Cannon]] will always appear in the technology slots.

Shields have an impact on shield strength.

Each part gives slightly different stat boosts depending on if it's the first, second or third of that type you've placed. If you plan accordingly, you can get the most boost for each part by choosing the parts that have generated with the biggest bonuses, for example using one of each of the 3 shield modules, but placing them in the order that gives the best boost for each. Any more than 3 provide no additional bonuses.

[[Supercharged Slot]] placement is randomised at time of build. One way to check: finalise a basic build and upgrade the class at the [[Starship Outfitting Terminal]]. If you do not like the placement, reload a restore point and repeat the process.

## Accessing the Corvette & beam-down

Corvettes can be accessed two ways, either through the access module e.g. landing bay ramp, or via the teleport. The latter allows the player to travel to and from the ship without needing to land the ship. The landing bay internally has two buttons, the one on the right toggles the bay open/closed and the one on the left activates the teleport.

When the teleport is activated, the player will beam down to a location below the corvette on the planet. To return to the ship, they can use the teleport point and interact with the console.
