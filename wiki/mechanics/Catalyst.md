# Catalyst

A **Catalyst** is a material used in the Refining process.

## Summary

A Catalyst is a material used to aid the reaction process of a [[resource]] in a [[Refiner]].

By putting a specific resource in one slot and a Catalyst in the two other slots, the reaction will yield a specific output, depending on the primary input resource.

Catalysts are not consumed by the refining reaction.

## Catalyst example

The following is an example of the catalyst process:
- 50 x [[Condensed Carbon]] + Catalyst + Catalyst → [[Carbon Crystal]]
