# Planetary Settlement - Policy Decisions

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Description:** Policy Decisions
- **Added in:** Relics

**Planetary Settlement - Policy Decisions** is a reference article.

## Summary

**Planetary Settlement - Policy Decisions** contains a detailed description of opportunities presented by policy decisions to the settlement. The Settlement Overseer must oversee a decision on each policy decision at the [[Planetary Settlement|Settlement Administration Terminal]] in a [[Planetary Settlement]].

## Improving our Lives

Improving our Lives is the most common Policy Decision. It always offers two choices from the table below.

| Choice | Adds Feature With | Removes Feature With |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Research additional facilities | Increases productivity | Decreases productivity |
| Research camouflage measures | Decreases Sentinel attention |   |
| Research commercial innovation | Increases productivity | Decreases productivity |
| Research farming technology | Decreases maintenance costs | Decreases productivity |
| Research housing tech | Increases population |   |
| Research industrial tech | Increases productivity |   |
| Research leisure technology | Improves citizen happiness | Reduces citizen happiness |
| Research maintenance tech | Decreases maintenance costs |   |
| Research new industrial units | Decreases maintenance costs<br>or Increases productivity | Decreases productivity |
| Research public goods | Improves citizen happiness |   |
| Research efficient security systems | Decreases maintenance costs |   |
