# Guild

**Guilds** are intergalactic NPC organisations.

## Summary

**Guilds** are intergalactic NPC organisations composed of individuals and groups with shared goals.

A player can pick up different missions from the guilds via the [[Mission Board]]. Successfully completing a guild mission raises their standing with that guild, while failing or abandoning a mission results in a loss of standing. Just like [[faction|factions]], guild ranks are independent of each other.

- **Note:** Players should be careful to only accept missions they intend to complete, as abandoning unwanted missions negatively affects standing.

A player can also gain standing with a guild by donating items to a [[Guild Envoy]] at a [[Space Station|space station]]. Each space station hosts a [[Guild Envoy]] who offers free supplies or heavily discounted items to members of good standing. Donating supplies to these envoys increases standing with their respective guilds and grants access to higher-level missions with improved rewards.

## Main Guilds

There are three main guilds, each with different goals. Each guild is mostly associated with one of the three main factions, but not exclusively.

- The [[Explorers Guild]]: Focused on exploration, discovery, and research. Associated with the [[Korvax]].
- The [[Merchants Guild]]: Focused on trading and delivery. Associated with the [[Gek]].
- The [[Mercenaries Guild]]: Focused on combat and bounty hunting. Associated with the [[Vy'keen]].

## Additional Information

There are smaller groups affiliated with each guild, showcased as "clients" when picking up missions.

Tip: Guilds are region-dependent. When looking for a different guild, see the "REGION" of star system descriptions in the galaxy map. Highlight the star and expand the description to see more information. (It is not guaranteed that a different guild will be next to your current region.)
