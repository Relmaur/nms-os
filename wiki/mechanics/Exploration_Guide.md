# Exploration Guide

**Exploration Guide** is a Location Information Register from the in-game guide.

## Summary

This screen appears on the in-game guide, under the LOG tab. It will appear when the user selects the **Exploration Guide** under **Secondary Missions** on the left hand side of the display.

This page comes in two different format, depending on the location of a traveller when it is accessed - a [[star system]] information guide, or a [[planet]]/[[moon]] information guide.

## Exploration Guide - Star System

This screen will display when the Exploration Guide is accessed while
- In a [[starship]] in the upper atmosphere of a planet
- In a [[Space Station]]
- on the [[Space Anomaly]]
- On a [[Frigate]]
- On a [[Freighter]]
- In an [[Atlas Interface]] space station
- Free floating in space if ejected from your starship, freighter or a space station due to an in-game glitch

The guide displayed will contain the following information:
- [[Spectral class|Stellar class]] useful for knowing the [[System colours|star colour]]
- Age of the star system. Note: All star systems in a given region are the same age
- Stellar Region name. Note: [[Phantom star|Phantom stars]] in a glitched region will display as [[???????]].
- Planets Discovered: A count of planets AND moons discovered out of the total in the system
- Dominant lifeform
- Conflict Level
- Economy: the Economy Type and the economic wealth level
- A note to remind travellers that the wealth of a system influences how many goods they have available to sell. High-wealth systems are also more likely to have high-quality starships. High-conflict systems carry a risk of greater pirate interference.

## Exploration Guide - Planet/Moon

This screen will display when the Exploration Guide is accessed while
- In a [[starship]] in the lower atmosphere of a planet/moon
- On the surface or in a building on the surface of a planet/moon
- Under the surface of a planet/moon
- Underwater on a planet/moon

The guide displayed will contain the following information:
- Name of planet and the planet's description label related to its [[biome]]
- Creatures discovered progress indicator: X out of total number of creatures on the planet/moon
- Flora documented progress indicator: X out of total number of flora on the planet/moon
- Minerals documented progress indicator: X out of total number of minerals on the planet/moon
- Weather
- Atmosphere: Composition of atmosphere in terms of % for a primary and secondary gas, followed by the phrase *Trace Others* (See list below)
- Primary Core Element: (See list below)
- Geology: (See list below)
- Other Notes: (See list below)
