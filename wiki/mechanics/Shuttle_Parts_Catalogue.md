# Shuttle Parts Catalogue

**Shuttle Parts Catalogue** is an index page.

## Summary

This page contains a list of wing types and parts for the [[Starship Catalogue - Shuttle|Shuttle]] version of the [[starship]].

In total, there are three hull types, nine wing shapes, two thrusters, five chins, six fins, 12 attachments, and 15 accessories. Depending on the model, one ship can display multiple parts. The names used are not game based, but are generally accepted by the community.

**Note:** the items in each list are sorted in order by size.

This provides the general appearance of the starship, known as the silhouette. There are three general types:
