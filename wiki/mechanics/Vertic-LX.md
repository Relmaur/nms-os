# Vertic-LX

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Rifle
- **Category:** Rail Gun
- **Class:** C
- **Slots:** 7
- **Platforms:** Xbox
- **Added in:** NEXT

The **Vertic-LX** is a multi-tool.

## Summary

The **Vertic-LX** is an exclusive [[Multi-tool Catalogue - Rifle|Rifle]] type [[Multi-tool]] in the universe of No Man's Sky.

## Location

This tool is available only through a [[Xbox Booster Pack|pre-order package]] available to Xbox users. It may be transferred to any other platform through [[Cross-save|Cross-Save]] functionality.

## Additional information

This multi-tool came with a [[Plasma Resonator]] pre-installed.

Although this multitool is unattainable today, it can still be acquired through save editing.

The seed is as follows: **0xDEB7E483C1A3F7F8**

## Release History

- [[NEXT Update|NEXT]] - Added as an exclusive Xbox [[Xbox Booster Pack|pre-order package]].
- *Hidden changes -* [[Plasma Resonator]] removed from the game. Colors of tool shifted to pastel blue and yellow.
