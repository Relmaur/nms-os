# Pre-release

## Quick Facts

- **Description:** Game version prior to release
- **Platforms:** PS4
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Pre-release** refers to the various demos and game builds of [[No Man's Sky]] made before the actual release of the game.

## Summary

Prior to the release of No-Man's Sky in August 2016, there were a number of pre-release videos released as teasers for the game. Various [[planet|planets]], [[star system|star systems]], elements, and [[fauna|creatures]] were referenced in these videos, and have been logged as "pre-release" entries.

## Banner indicator

Pages tagged with the Pre-release banner and category were never present in the actual game, but are logged here for historical purposes.

## Additional information

Some planets, especially from the earlier builds, may have had certain objects added to them by hand.
