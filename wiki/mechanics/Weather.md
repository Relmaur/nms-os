# Weather

**Weather** is an environmental modifier.

## Summary

The **Weather** influences the intensity of the present environmental hazard. Weather conditions can be sorted into several general categories (Note that all environmental hazard values are approximate).

- For a list of weather types, see [[Property:Planet weather|weather types]].
- For a list of weather types for each [[biome]], refer to the specific biome pages.
- For further information on storms, see [[storm]].

## Protection against hazards

There is insulation in your [[Exosuit|suit]] that protects you from these dangers; once it wears out you will start to take damage.

To recharge it you must go to a location sheltered from the specific hazard (for example, a cool place on a hot planet). Caves often serve this purpose if they can be found. Exosuit insulation can also be upgraded to last longer.

## Weather Types

The Weather reading on a planet's discoveries page varies between each planet, and depends on a planet's biome and how frequently it experiences storms. They are separated into three categories, with many names for each status. With some specific exceptions, worlds can either have no storms, occasional storms or extreme storms. Most biomes will have a passive hazard presence when they are not experiencing a storm, primarily Scorched, Frozen, Toxic and Radioactive worlds. Most worlds will also feature storms occasionally occurring, which will drain hazard protection somewhat quickly. Extreme worlds that are not currently experiencing a storm have the same hazard intensity as a non-extreme world that is experiencing a storm. Extreme worlds that are experiencing a storm will drain hazard protection the fastest, as well as having more frequent storms. The amount of time that the player lasts in each of these conditions varies depending on technology installed and the player's chosen Survival Difficulty option.

With no hazard protection technology installed, the player will survive for this long on each setting of Survival Difficulty:

| Survival Difficulty | No Storm | Regular Storm/Extreme | Extreme Storm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relaxed | 25:15 | 5:39 | 1:55 |
| Standard | 8:02 | 1:48 | 0:37 |
| Challenging | 2:41 | 0:36 | 0:13 |

These values do not change based on slight variations in hazard strength, like temperature or toxicity.

Installing the four Hazard Protection Unit blueprint technologies and/or the Illegal Hazard Protection Upgrades will increase how long the Hazard Protection meter lasts against the specified hazard. Installing the four types of Hazard Protection upgrade modules purchasable from Space Stations will add an extra bar when exposed to that hazard that depletes at a fixed rate, regardless of hazard strength.

#### Exotified Planet Weather

Some lush planets have different weather descriptors. These planets never have storms, apart from ones with extreme weather, shown by the presence of activated stellar metals. The storms on those planets do not drain hazard protection. Planets with these weather descriptors show signs of exotification, either through bubbles on the surface, or by having minerals or flora that's typical for  planets on their surface. These weather types are also found on  planets.

#### Dead & Exotic

Dead and Exotic worlds will always lack storms and have randomly assigned weather readings names.

#### Mega Exotic

Mega Exotic worlds are separated into Red, Green and Blue biomes, that are all identical in their possible variations besides their biome names, weather names, resources present and star colors they appear in. All of them can have no storms, occasional storms or extreme storms but their weather reading is randomly assigned and does not indicate storm status. Their storms will always be super-heated rainstorms, similar to lush worlds.

#### Waterworld

Waterworlds are able to have no storms, occasional storms or extreme storms but will always display "Coastal Storms" regardless of their storm frequency. Waterworlds can have any one of the four primary hazards present on them, and therefore they can have hot, cold, toxic or radioactive storms on them.

#### Gas Giant

Gas Giants will always have heat storms that resemble the effects of extreme storms (tornadoes, active storm crystals), but their hazard intensity is only that of a regular planetary storm, or an extreme world that is not experiencing a storm.

## Extreme Hazard Planets

As of [[Update 1.03|1.03]], extreme planets are added. These planets contain dangerous, turbulent climate. When landed on, these types of planets will drain [[Hazard Protection]] quicker than normal. Extreme Planets also contain extreme storms. These storms are very powerful and drain Hazard Protection even quicker. The weather descriptor is written in red to warn the player about the extreme climate. Hazards on these planets depend on the biome.

These planets also contain activated stellar metal (always, such as [[Activated Copper]]) - if a planetary scan reveals these metal, the planet will be extreme.

## Atmospheric Events

As of [[Update 3.00|Origins]], there are five atmospheric events that can occur in conjunction with an ongoing storm. With this update, the Exosuit can now take advantage of the extreme conditions: superheated temperatures can be processed into improved jetpack efficiency; freezing temperatures prevent Mining Beam overheating; high radioactivity increases mining yield; dense toxic gases can be recirculated for additional stamina.

- Firestorms
  - Occur on extreme temperature worlds
  - Fire will damage players if they get too close
  - Fire dissipates after a storm subsides
- Gravitational Event
  - Occur on anomalous planets
  - Will exhibit sudden low-gravity shifts on the planet's surface
- Lightning
  - Can occur with any storm
  - Can occasionally strike the ground near the player and cause damage
- Meteor Showers
  - Can occur independently of a storm at the horizon, or as its own event close to the player
  - Meteoroids can damage the player, with the strike zone being indicated with a red circle on the planet's surface
- Tornadoes
  - Can rarely occur with storms, and is indicated with an "extreme wind event" Exosuit warning
  - Will pick up nearby players and creatures

## Localized Weather Events

As of World Part II update, there are some weather events that acts as "mini-storms". This occurs directly on the surface of a planet and for a short time.

- Toxic Spores
  - Appears as a green clouds with some green particles
  - Will induce extreme toxicity if inside it, draining [[Hazard Protection|Toxic Protection]] faster
- Radiation Fallout
  - Appears as a yellow clouds with some yellow particles
  - Will induce extreme radiation if inside it, draining Radiation Protection faster
- Geothermal Geysers
  - If that happens, some waters steams will burst out from the ground
- Electrical Disturbance
  - Appears as a field of energy bolts
  - Damages players that walks inside it. Other players see the influenced player being electroduced with some energy bolts on the player.
  - The effects stays even when leaving the energy bolts, for a few seconds.
- Gravity Inversion
  - Appears as a steam with floating stones
  - Similar to gravity storms, will launch the player high up if [[Jetpack]] is used inside it
