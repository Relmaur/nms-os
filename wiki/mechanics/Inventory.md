# Inventory

**Inventory** is a general term for items that can be carried or stored by a player.

## Summary

A player's **inventory** is divided into six sections: [[exosuit]], [[multi-tool]], [[starship]], [[exocraft]], [[Storage Container|storage container]], and [[freighter]].

There are two types of inventory slot: Cargo and Technology.

## Inventory Slots

### Cargo

Cargo slots are used for storing items, and cannot have technologies installed into them. They can hold any of the following stacks:

- up to 9999 (Normal or Creative modes), or up to 500 (Survival or Permadeath modes) of any basic resource
- up to 200 supply items, including **Ion Batteries**, **Life Support Gels** and **Creature Pellets**
- up to 100 items of all **food** items and **Starshield Batteries**
- up to 50 items of **Glass**, all **trade goods**, certain curiosities and **Emergency Signal Scanners**
- up to 40 tiny items, including **Navigation Data**
- up to 30 small items, including **Salvaged Data**
- up to 20 medium items, including **Metal Plating**, **Di-hydrogen Jelly** and **Antimatter**
- up to 10 large items, including uninstalled **Upgrade Modules** and all manufactured **trade goods**
- A single item, mostly **Artifacts** and archived **Upgrade Modules**

### Technology

Technology slots are, as the name implies, limited to containing installed technology; they cannot hold any other items. Specifically, they can hold any of the following:

- A single installed **Technology**
- A single installed **Upgrade Module**

Note that technology slots cannot store uninstalled upgrade modules.

## Exosuit

The base Exosuit has 24 **Cargo Inventory Slots** and 10 **Technology Inventory Slots** available. Additional slots can be unlocked by interacting with suit upgrade terminals in [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]] and [[Space Station|Space Stations]].

### Cargo Inventory

This inventory begins with 24 Cargo slots installed. The maximum number of inventory slots for this category is 120 (12x10). The first upgrade costs 5,000 units with varying costs for subsequent upgrades. The price is capped at 200,000 units.

The total amount of units needed to gain the maximum number of **Cargo Inventory** slots is 36,750,000 [[unit|units]].

### Technology Inventory

This inventory begins with 10 Technology slots installed. The maximum number of inventory slots for this category is 60 (6x10). They are the cheapest slots to purchase. The first upgrade costs 1,000 units with varying costs for subsequent upgrades. The price is capped at 125,000 units.

The total amount needed to gain the maximum number of **Technology Inventory** slots is 1,456,000 units.

## Multi-tool

The starting multi-tool has eight (8) inventory slots. The maximum number of inventory slots for a multi-tool is 30 (10x3) for a C or B-class multi-tool, 45 (10x4 + 5) for an A-class multi-tool, and 60 (10x6) for an S-class multi-tool. Players can exchange their multi-tool for other versions to get additional slots. Multi-tools with upgraded inventories can be found in outposts (planetary bases with [[Landing Pad|Landing Pads]]), space anomalies, and randomly as rewards from successfully completing events. Additionally, players can add slots to existing multi-tools at [[Multi-Tool Upgrade Station|Multi-Tool Upgrade Stations]] present on every space station using  Units (starting at 100,000) or [[Multi-Tool Expansion Slot|Multi-Tool Expansion Slots]].

All multi-tools have technology slots only; it is not possible to store items in a multi-tool.

## Starship

Since the [[Update 4.00|Waypoint]] update, the starting [[starship]], [[Radiant Pillar BC1]], has 25 cargo inventory slots and 13 technology slots. The pre-order starship, [[Horizon Omega]], has 16 cargo inventory slots and 21 technology slots. (note: possibly unintentional, check number of cargo and tech slots for Horizon Omega as of most recent update)
- The maximum number of cargo and technology slots for a starship depends on its class and type.
- Each type of S-class ship can be upgraded to, in ascending order:
  - Fighter: 100 cargo slots (10x10)
  - Exotic: 105 cargo slots (10x10 + 5)
  - Shuttle, Explorer, Solar: 110 cargo slots (10x11)
  - Hauler, Living Ship, Salvaged: 120 cargo slots (10x12)
- Any S-class ship can be upgraded to have 60 technology slots (10x6).

**Additional slots** can be added to a ship by:

- Purchasing slots using units from the [[Starship Outfitting Terminal]], next to the [[Appearance Modifier]] in most space stations.
- [[Storage Augmentation]] curiosity for a single-slot expansion.

Otherwise, a player may purchase a different starship with more initial inventory slots; this may be cheaper than buying inventory slots.

## Exocraft

Each of the four Exocraft has its own inventory.

## Base containers

Up to 10 [[Storage Container|Storage Containers]] may be constructed at your base, or on your freighter. Each one contains 50 Cargo slots, each capable of storing 9999 (normal), or 1000 (survival) resources, or twice as many items of a given size (e.g. 60 small items instead of 30). These inventory slots are shared with like-numbered storage containers in other bases or on a freighter.

## Freighter

[[Freighter|Freighters]] may also be purchased to expand your storage capabilities.

## Mechanics

You can move items from your Exosuit to your Freighter and vice versa without a distance limit, but only when a Matter Beam is installed. Without one, you must be aboard your Freighter to move an item between Freighter and your Exosuit or Starship. On the contrary, moving an item to or from your Ship and Exosuit is limited to 25-seconds walking distance between you and your ship (walls do not block the limit).

Elements can stack up to 9999 in each of the slots in the Exosuit **General** inventory in Normal mode, and 250 per slot in other modes.

Elements can stack up to 9999 in any inventory in Normal mode, and 500 in each of the slots in the Exosuit **Cargo** inventory, Ship inventory, up to 1000 in the Freighter inventory or in a **Storage Container** inventory in other modes.

A player may also transfer inventory to another member of their [[Multiplayer]] team. When the Transfer Items panel is displayed, the names of the other players in the group are included for selection if desired.
