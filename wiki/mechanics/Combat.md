# Combat

**Combat** is an information panel.

## Summary

**Combat** is a display page referenced by the [[Game Content]] page.

## Sentinels

- [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] are robotic entities that guard most worlds. **Harvesting resources** in view of the Sentinels will cause them to turn **hostile**.
- Harvesting some resources, such as [[Gravitino Ball|Gravitino Balls]], will immediately alert Sentinels forces.
- Sentinels may approach and **scan you** or any resources you recently harvested. Avoid harvesting or attacking non-hostile creatures while being watched.
- Some worlds host **extremely hostile Sentinels**. These drones will attack on sight. Pay attention to the planet information in the [[Analysis Visor]] (Key F) to learn Sentinels status.
- The [[Sentinel|Security Level]] in the top right of the HUD shows the current Sentinels status. This will increase as you fight Sentinels.
- Eliminate Sentinels Drones quickly to avoid [[Sentinel|more powerful Sentinels]] being summoned.
- Destroying Sentinel Drones is a good source of **exotic materials**.

## Ammunition

- Most Multi-Tool [[weapon|weapons]], such as the [[Boltcaster]] require **ammunition**. Craft ammunition in empty slots in your [[Inventory]].
- While in combat, **Reload** your current weapon with Key R. this will automatically draw ammo from your inventory.
- Ensure you have crafted sufficient ammunition **before** entering a combat situation.
- All projects based weapons use the same ammunition: the **Boltcaster**, the [[Pulse Spitter]] and the [[Scatter Blaster]].
- Energy based weapons, such as the [[Blaze Javelin]], are charged with [[carbon]] directly from the Quick Menu or the Inventory. The Mining Beam is not use in combat.
- When out of ammo or charge, use Key Q to **melee strike**.

## Weapon Management

- Fire your [[Multi-tool]] with LPM. When out of ammo or charge, use Key Q to **melee strike**.
- Reload your current weapon with Key R. Some weapons require **charge** instead of ammo.
- By default, the Multi-Tool is only equipped with a [[Mining Beam]]. To increase combat power, install new **Weapon Types**.
- Cycle between **weapon modes** by using Key G.
- The Multi-Tool can be upgraded with a [[Terrain Manipulator]], allowing the user to create and remove planetary terrain.
- Find new weapon blueprints and other [[Multi-Tool Upgrades|Multi-Tool Upgradess]] in buildings or at a [[Technology Merchant]] aboard [[Space Station|Space Stations]].
- Some planetary buildings may host brand-new Multi-Tools to purchase. Each Multi-Tool has **unique bonuses** to boost its effectiveness.

## Defensive Systems

- Damage received while on-foot (from combat, hostile fauna or environmental damage) will first deplete your [[shield]].
- Your shield will recharge over time. Take cover and avoid taking damage while the shield is low. Damage taken while shields are down will damage your [[health]].
- Purchase plans for a [[Personal Forcefield]] at [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]]. This is a powerful combat shield deployed from the Multi-Tool.
- Exosuit defensive and life support systems can be upgraded with **new technology**. Visit **technology merchants** on space stations to upgrade.

## Ground Combat

- Fire your **Multi-Tool** with LPM. When out of ammo or charge, use Key Q to **melee strike**.
- **Aim** (Key F) and **Zoom** (RPM) to target enemies with precision.
- Cycle between **weapon modes** by using Key G.
- Reload your current weapon with Key R. Ensure you have crafted **sufficient ammunition** before entering a combat situation.
- Damage received while on-foot will first deplete your [[shield]]. Your shield will recharge over time. Take cover and avoid taking damage while the shield is low.
- Purchase plans for a **Personal Forcefield** at **Technology Merchants**. This is a powerful combat shield deployed from the Multi-Tool.

## Space Combat

- Fire **ship weapons** with LPM. Cycle weapon modes with Key G.
- if attacked by [[Pirate|Pirates]], answer them on the Communicator with Key X. They can be **bribed** to leave you alone.
- Alternatively, use a [[Defence Chit]] to summon the **System Authorities** to aid in combat.
- Purchase **Defence Chits** from merchants on Space Stations.
- **Stay behind your target** to deal more damage and avoid enemy fire.
- Manage your speed with **Boost** (L Shift) and **Brake** (Key S) to stay behind your target.
- **Turning while braking** is much faster.
- Your [[Starship Shield Module|Starship Shields]] will recharge over time.
- Change your **current target** with {,} and {.}.
- Aim at the **target leading reticle** for increased accuracy.
