# Brood Mother

**Brood Mother** is a miniboss.

## Summary

**Brood Mother** (also known as **Insect Queen**) is a miniboss that looks similar to [[Arthropodae]]. These boss creatures do not spawn naturally, and can only be summoned by picking up and crushing a [[Juicy Grub]], which can be found on planets marked with 'Vile Brood Detected'.

Brood Mothers also sometimes spawn automatically at Settlements ([[Update 5.70]]) when an infestation event occurs.

## Summoning

Crushing a Juicy Grub summons the Brood Mother, which will then emerge from the ground and immediately begin attacking the player.

While Juicy Grubs only spawn on some planets, they can be crushed and the brood mother be summoned on any planet, regardless of whether that planet has vile brood or not.

## Combat Behaviour

The Brood Mother features a two-part HUD, and uses ranged attacks. Its smaller broodlings use exclusively close range attacks. The HUD indicates the status of the Queen's primary health in red, and the countdown until it spawns more broodlings in yellow.

During its primary attack phase it spits 4-5 balls of purple acid at the player, however these orbs are relatively slow, do not lock on, and can be dodged. The broodlings chase the player and launch at them when in range.

Each area of the Brood Mother has defensive plating that prevents damage to the Brood Mother's health until it the plating has been damaged sufficiently to remove it, exposing vulnerable areas.

## Additional Information

- Once the Brood Mother has been killed, it will explode, and all remaining broodlings will burrow into the ground and despawn.
- Upon defeat of a Brood Mother, there is a chance that the player will receive 1 of 4 vile brood-themed helmets exclusive to the [[Anomaly]] race. The brood-themed helmets are:
  - Broodling Skull (Helmet Type 22)
  - The Hunter's Jaws (Helmet Type 24)
  - Bioluminescent Headpiece (Helmet Type 25)
  - Brood Mother's Maw (Helmet Type 26)
- Using [[Paralysis Mortar]] on the Brood Mother will reset the broodling spawn timer at the end of the stun duration.
