# Iteration: Ariadne

**Iteration: Ariadne** is an NPC.

## Summary

**Iteration: Ariadne** is a [[Traveller]] that can be found in the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Interactions

Ariadne introduces the player to the Space Anomaly.

## Lore

During the 2020 [[Weekend Missions - Lore|Weekend Missions]], the story revealed that Ariadne crashed their ship on a planet and was replaced by an unknown entity taking their appearance. The imposter has been standing in Ariadne's place ever since. It is still unknown what they are, what they want or what happened to the real Ariadne.

Interacting with [[Sentinel Pillar|Sentinel Pillars]] to read the log entries always start with a text saying "IMPOSTER DETECTED [OVERRIDE CODE: 4R1ADN3 ]", implying a link between the imposter Ariadne and the pillar logs.

## Additional information

A [[Iteration: Ariadne Visage|Silicone/biological helmet]] with the appearance of Iteration: Ariadne may be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

Once purchased, these can be used in the [[Appearance Modifier]].
