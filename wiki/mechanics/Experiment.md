# Experiment

**Experiment** is a multi-tool naming prefix.

## Summary

One of the more common [[multi-tool]] name prefixes is the word **Experiment**.

## Models

In the [[Release|early days]] of the No Man's Sky wiki, many multi-tool pages were created with little more than the name of the tool listed

Nine of these begin with the word "experiment":
- Experiment 1-E
- Experiment 2/Q
- Experiment 5/OB2
- Experiment 6-94J
- Experiment 8-UF8
- Experiment 9-D
- Experiment N42-KG5
- Experiment Q66-117
- Experiment TJ6-5

## Additional information

The initial multi-tool granted to a new player is also an Experiment model called the [[Experiment C6/4]].
