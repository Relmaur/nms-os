# Sentient Plant

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Resource Node
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Sentient Plant** is a type of resource node.

## Summary

**Sentient Plant** has the appearance of a special [[Point of Interest]] and can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

Depending on the system, a Sentient Plant can produce one of two types of [[tradeable|tradeables]] when harvested, [[Gravitino Ball|Gravitino Balls]] or [[Albumen Pearl|Albumen Pearls]]. The player can expect a level 3 [[Sentinel|Sentinel alert]] for collecting these items.
