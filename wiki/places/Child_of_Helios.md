# Child of Helios

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Use communicator
- **Rewards:** Living Water during Starbirth mission, nothing otherwise
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Child of Helios** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Child of Helios** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Children of Helios appear to be a massive, glowing jellyfish-like creature.

## Behavior

Upon approach, a Child of Helios will attempt to communicate with a [[starship]] pilot. If communicated with, they will broadcast feelings of pure emotion, then leave. If the player is currently undergoing the [[Starbirth]] mission, they will first provide them with the [[Living Water]] needed to complete the mission.

Firing upon a Child of Helios will have no effect on them, nor will they become aggressive.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
