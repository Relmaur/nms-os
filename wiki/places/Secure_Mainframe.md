# Secure Mainframe

## Quick Facts

- **Rewards:** Captain's Log
- **Added in:** Desolation

**Secure Mainframe** is a terminal.

## Summary

The **Secure Mainframe** is a terminal aboard a [[Derelict Freighter]]. It is always located in the second-to-last room in the derelict, the [[Engineering Control]] being the last.

## Interactions

The player is tasked with accessing the Secure Mainframe by the [[Lost in Space]] mission. The player must follow these steps:

1. Collect [[Security Credentials]], found by reading [[Log Snippet|Log Snippets]] to access the terminal.
1. Log in as a duty officer of the freighter to bring up the [[Captain's Log]].
1. Read the last significant entry in the log before receiving a download of the log.
