# Harmonic Camp

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Abandoned Outpost
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Harmonic Camp** is a type of planetary Point of Interest.

## Summary

**Harmonic Camps** are one of the various types of planetary [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. They are found exclusively on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]] and are represented by a yellow symbol with an Autophage head on it.

## Layout

Harmonic Camps typically include a large crashed pod (a wrecked space station core), with a [[Harmonic Interface]] in front of the pod. Two reinforced tents are located on each side of the pod, with the right one containing the remains of an [[Harmonic Scrap|Autophage robot]], and the left one containing a cabinet with a [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Sentinel|Sentinel Multi-Tool]]. Two pairs of tarp-covered cylinders decorate the front of the camp.

## Harmonic Interface

The Harmonic Interface is the main point of interest in a Harmonic Camp. Unlocking it will grant access to the [[Multi-Tool]] cabinet and will allow the player to locate a crashed [[Interceptor (Starship)|Sentinel Interceptor]]. To unlock it, the player should select the "Scan Memory Registers" dialogue option, which will present a series of 3 simple arithmetic equations.  The player should then select the first option, "Input Override Glyphs", and input the 3 glyphs that correspond to the answers to the 3 equations (order does not matter). Upon successful completion, the player will be presented with options to unlock the multi-tool cabinet, and to "Locate Dissonance Spikes". Choosing the latter will display a navigational marker to a Sentinel Interceptor [[Crashed Ship|crash site]]. This option can be repeated indefinitely to find multiple crashed ships, though it will only point to a new ship location once the prior one it located has been salvaged. In addition, as the design of sentinel interceptors is tied to the system they spawned in, all interceptors located by the camp will be the same model, differing only in ship stats.

The first time a player unlocks a Harmonic Camp interface, they will be awarded with the [[Aeron Turbojet]] jetpack customisation.

## Additional information

- Harmonic camps can be located using an [[Echo Locator]]. These items can be found as a random drop from [[Corrupted Sentinel Quadruped]] and [[Dissonance Resonator|Dissonance Resonators]].
- Each camp contains 7 wheelbarrows full of "Harmonic Scrap", which will reward the player with various items when picked up, including expansion slots.
- Only found within [[Dissonant System|Dissonant Systems]] on dissonant planets.
- With the Echoes update, 3 Autophage NPCs can be uncloaked and the camp's interface includes the same functions as [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal|Autophage Synthesis Terminals]], given the player has progressed [[They Who Returned]] questline.
