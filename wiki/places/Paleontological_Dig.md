# Paleontological Dig

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Interaction Location
- **Type:** Interaction Location
- **Added in:** Breach
- **Description:** Interaction Location

A **Paleontological Dig** is a type of inhabited outpost.

## Summary

A Paleontology Digsite is a structure established upon many worlds except [[Uncharted System|Uncharted Systems]] or [[Abandoned System|Abandoned Systems]].

## Contents

The digsites will always contain one buried fossil that can be exhumed. The structures of the site is similar to an [[Autophage Camp]], except it is inhabited by normal aliens.

In the center of the camp, a large skull decoration can be found, however it cannot be collected.

Discarded dirt items are often scattered around the digsite, and can be collected for [[Polished Stone]].

## Interaction

Scattered around the digsite there is often, but not always, an interactable NPC that a player can learn their language, give them human language skills, or gift them for alien rank improvements.

A bartender can also be found. Players that interact with the bartender can browse the archive, in which all kinds of fossils are presented. These bones can be collected by making an offer, however they may like it or not. The chance of a successful order depends on the item presented by the player, which is shown in the near-bottom of the interface.

A player can trade up to three items, in which it will contributes to the increment of the chance. For example, if a player give the bartender a 11% item, and an another 24% item, the total chance will be 11+24%, which is 35%. If a player trades an another 57%, the total chance will be increased since there is more items, in which the chance will use this expression: (11+24)+57% or an excellent 92%. If the chance is high enough, then the trade will be accepted, and the player will receive the selected fossil, but the offered items will be lost. In contrast if the chance are very low, then the trade will be declined, but the player keeps their offered items.

A player can only trade up to three fossils, or the player can no longer trade that fossils. Failed trades won't count upon the three fossils limit.

## Locating

These camps  can be found among some "abandoned" points of interest.
