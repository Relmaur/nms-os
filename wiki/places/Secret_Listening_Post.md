# Secret Listening Post

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Inhabited
- **Frequency:** 2
- **Interaction:** Use communicator
- **Rewards:** none
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Secret Listening Post** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Secret Listening Post** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Secret Listening Post appear as structures built on asteroids that eject a red beam. Upon interaction with Communicator they will give a message in the language of the faction to which the system belongs to.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any inhabited [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
