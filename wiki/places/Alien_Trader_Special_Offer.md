# Alien Trader Special Offer

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Inhabited
- **Frequency:** 4
- **Interaction:** Trade
- **Rewards:** Rare artifact for purchase / barter
- **Added in:** Relics

**Alien Trader Special Offer** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Alien Trader Special Offer** is a [[Space Encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Alien Trader Special Offer appear as an NPC-piloted [[starship]]. They can have the appearance of any starship normally found in the current [[Star system|system]].

## Behaviors

Upon dropping out of pulse, an Alien Trader Special Offer will attempt to communicate with a starship pilot. If communicated with, they will offer to trade the player a relic in exchange for several payment options including specific [[resource|resources]], a specific racial item, or a large amount of [[Unit|Units]].

Should [[Pirate|Pirates]] attack the player when one is near, they will assist the player. Firing upon an Alien Relic Trader will cause them to become hostile.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any inhabited system and have a low frequency of appearance.
