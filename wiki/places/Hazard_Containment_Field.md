# Hazard Containment Field

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** Very Low
- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Terrifying Sample, Biological Sample, Magnetised Ferrite
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Hazard Containment Field** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Hazard Containment Field** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Hazard Containment Fields appear as four pairs of cubical cells made enclosed by glowing force fields stacked around a central axis. Imprisoned creatures reside inside three of these paired cells, lying dormant in stasis. The fourth pair of cells will generally be empty, as if awaiting another large threat to be encountered and captured.

## Behavior

Hazard Containment Fields will not respond if fired upon, but can be damaged and destroyed by a starship's weapons.

## Rewards

- [[Terrifying Sample]] (50%)
- 900-1300 [[Magnetised Ferrite]] (33.3%)
- [[Biological Sample]] (16.7%)

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
