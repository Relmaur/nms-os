# Shelter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Shelters** are a types of planetary Point of Interest.

## Summary

**Shelters** are one of the various types of planetary [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. They are represented by a blue symbol with a square house on it.

## Composition

Shelters typically include two long semi-cylindrical buildings, with a nearby piece of [[Damaged Machinery]].  There is usually a small outdoor canopy structure for quick access to shelter and a [[Waypoints]] for saving progress.  Occasionally there may be non-functional decorative structure such as a transmission tower present.  Shelters are sometimes stand alone and sometimes adjacent to one of the following:
- [[Minor Settlement]]
- [[Manufacturing Facility]]
- [[Observatory]]
- [[Transmission Tower]]
- [[Holographic Comms Tower]]
