# Drop Pod

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Shelter
- **Added in:** NextGen
- **Description:** Inventory upgrade location

**Drop Pod** is a Point of Interest.

## Summary

**Drop Pod** is a [[Point of Interest]] which allows the player to upgrade their [[Exosuit]] [[inventory]]. Unlike the [[Exosuit Upgrade Station]], which requires  Units to unlock slots, Drop Pods offer free upgrades after repair. The Exosuit will gain one additional slot every upgrade up to a maximum of 48 for Inventory, 48 for Cargo, and 14 for Technology. Each Drop Pod may be used only once.

Drop Pods allow a player to choose one of three types of inventory upgrade:
- General - the standard inventory space, which can be re-purposed for Technology if needed
- Cargo - an additional space that permits stacking of products. Due to Cargo upgrades being much more expensive on a station, it is better to upgrade them using a Drop Pod instead.
- Technology - Dedicated specifically for Exosuit technology.

## Strategy

Use a [[Signal Booster]] and [[Exosuit Upgrade Chart]] to locate the first pod. Make sure to keep an eye out for the "Destination Reached" message. Repair and use the Drop Pod, then put down a Signal Booster and use another Drop Pod Coordinate Data. If the current Drop Pod's marker has been cleared, the game will automatically find a nearby, fresh Drop Pod. Repeat the process as many times as required.

An alternative is to use the [[Advanced Signal Booster]] from an [[Exocraft]], which is cheaper.

- Drop Pods also act as [[shelter|shelters]] and provide protection from the [[weather]], hostile [[fauna]] and [[sentinel|sentinels]].
