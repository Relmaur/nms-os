# Dyson Lens

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 4
- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Nanites
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Dyson Lens** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Dyson Lens** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

A Dyson Lens will appear as a circular anomaly like starships, adrift in space.

## Behavior

A Dyson Lens has small rotating and flashing objects and is unresponsive to player hails or other actions until destroyed.

Destroying a Dyson Lens will automatically set your [[Sentinel|wanted level]] to 5 and trigger an immediate response by System Authority Combat Ships.

Dyson Lens can (very rarely) award an upgrade module for the [[Living Ship archetype|Living Ship]].

Update August 2026: Dyson Lenses now appear to be completely unresponsive to any interaction - they take no damage, make no communications, and you can fly right through them with no response.

CONFIRMED.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a low frequency of appearance.
