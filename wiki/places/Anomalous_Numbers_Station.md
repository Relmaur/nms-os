# Anomalous Numbers Station

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Communicate
- **Rewards:** Anomalous Timepiece
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Anomalous Numbers Station** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Anomalous Numbers Station** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Anomalous Numbers Stations have the appearance of a circular mass of smaller circular metal loops covered in small rings.

## Behavior

Upon approach, an Anomalous Numbers Station will show no sign of being bothered by a pilot.

They will not respond if fired upon, nor will it damage them.

If the player answers their communication, they will be given a choice to either accept or reject the message. A message will be received that is a series of five number sequences. No other behavior or result appears to occur.

After receiving the numbers from the station, the player will acquire the [[Anomalous Timepiece]] [[Space Station]] [[Base building]] decoration.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any system, inhabited or not, and have a very low frequency of appearance.
