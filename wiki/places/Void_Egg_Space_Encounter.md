# Void Egg (Space Encounter)

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 7
- **Interaction:** Shoot down
- **Rewards:** A Living Ship upgrade
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Void Egg** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Void Egg** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Void eggs appear as an oval mass of tendrils wrapped around a glowing orb of light. The light they emit can be one of several different colors.

They will not appear if you have any parts needed to create a [[Starship Catalogue - Living Ship|Living Ship]].

## Behavior

Void eggs are nonresponsive and unmoving, other than slowly rotating. If destroyed, they will grant the player a living ship upgrade.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a moderate frequency of appearance.

They do not seem to appear when using [[Anomaly Detector|Anomaly Detectors]].
