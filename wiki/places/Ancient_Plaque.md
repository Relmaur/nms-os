# Ancient Plaque

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Interaction Location
- **Type:** Interaction Location
- **Added in:** Outlaws
- **Description:** Interaction Location

A **Plaque** is a type of relic.

## Summary

The Plaque is one of the various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] on planets. They are one of the [[Waypoints|relics]] made by sentient alien species.

## Contents

The plaques contain information about the species that created them (i.e. the dominant species of the current [[star system]]), and can be categorized as follows:
- [[Gek]] plaques document [[First Spawn|Gek the First Spawn]]'s treacherous encounters and volatile behaviours toward other species
- [[Vy'keen]] plaques document [[Vy'keen|Vy'keen Alliance]] war lore, saga, and stories of [[Hirk the Great]]
- [[Korvax]] plaques document [[Korvax]] research, [[Korvax Echoes|echoes]] and worship toward the [[Sentinels|Aerons]]

## Interaction

Each Plaque has a circular or pyramidic cavity placed in the side of the obelisk structure. Upon player interaction, a portion of the [[faction|faction's]] lore will be revealed. Players are then given a choice between two mutually-exclusive options: "Seek help with language" or "Seek knowledge of the past".

- Choosing to seek help with language will teach the player a word of the dominant lifeform and raise their standing with the lifeform's faction.
- Choosing to seek knowledge of the past will reveal the location of an Ancient [[Ruin]] where the player can dig for a valuable [[Artifact]] buried underneath the surface.
  - There appears to be a bug with seeking knowledge of the past that results in "Nothing Found."  In this case, the player can interact with the plaque again and seek help with language.

The lore is continuous for each species; it will continue at the point where the lifeform's last lore had been revealed.

## Locating

- Using a [[Planetary Chart (ancient artifact site)]], which can be purchased from the [[Space Station]] [[Cartographer]].
- Plaques can be located by using an [[Radar Power Resonator]] with the "Scan for Ruins" option.
