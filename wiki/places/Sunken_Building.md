# Sunken Building

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Shelter
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Sunken Building** is a planetary [[Point of Interest]].

## Summary

**Sunken Buildings** are abandoned building that can be located underwater with a [[High-Power Sonar]] scan.

## Appearance

Upon arrival, the construct appears to be a typical planetary [[shelter]], but with a busted-down door and strange substances growing inside. Many of these are [[Carbon]]-based plants.

Inside the building there is a working terminal, but [[Junk]] type resources must be removed from the terminal before it can be used. The player can choose to keep the material and sell it or simply discard it. The terminal provides the player with a [[Abandoned Building|log]] from a random long-dead [[Traveller]], which together form the story of the Traveller.

[[Abyssal Horror (stationary)|Abyssal Horrors]] have often take root outside the building.

## Terminal types

These are the possible names for the terminal found inside Sunken Buildings. One of the following is randomly chosen when an Sunken Building is procedurally generated.

- Corrupted Terminal
- Sunken Terminal
- Haunted Terminal
- Deserted Terminal
- Forgotten Terminal
- Forsaken Terminal

## Additional information

- The appearance and features of the fungus growing in the buildings are also randomly generated.
