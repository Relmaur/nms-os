# Ancient Ruin

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Interaction Location
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Ancient Ruin** is a point of interest.

## Summary

**Ancient Ruin** is one of the various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] on [[planet|planets]]. Ruins are one of the relics created by sentient alien [[species]].

Ruins can be located by the following means:
- Discovering an [[Observatory]] and decryption of the research data
- Scanning with an [[Exocraft]] with an [[Radar Power Resonator]] installed
- By pure exploration.

Ruins with Artifact can be located by the following means:
- Using a [[Artifact Chart]], which can be purchased from the [[Space Station]] [[Cartographer]] for 3 nanites or a **Local Information Repository** inside a [[Colossal Archive]] for 15 [[nanites]].
- Using the artifact scanner of a [[Frigate|frigate]] with the Exploration Specialist [[Frigate Traits|trait]].
- Selecting "Seek knowledge of the past" from Plaques.
- By pure exploration.

## Additional information

Ruins typically have 2 or 3 [[Knowledge Stone|Knowledge Stones]] accompanied by a memoir device similar to the ones placed in front of a [[Plaque]] and about 2 or 3 [[Autophage]] wandering about. The memoir includes partial [[lore]] that depends on which of the [[faction|factions]] built the ruin. Interaction with the memoir device grants players a new word. The lore is continuous by faction; it will continue where the faction's last lore had been revealed.

Each Ruin is unique but sometimes systems will present similar or identical ruins if systems are in close proximity to each other. Ruins come in different designs; some look like two-floor brick houses while others are similar to a mosque.

Ruins with Artifact will lack the Knowledge Stones and Plaque, and usually much of it will be buried. To unearth it, a [[Terrain Manipulator]] or [[Geology Cannon]] is needed. Using the visor can reveal 5 to 6 [[Artifact Fragment|Artifact Fragments]] and a [[Large Artifact Crate]] buried within the Ruin, and various [[Gravitino Ball|Gravitino Balls]] which will cause [[Sentinel]] alert when picked up.

## Additional Information

- Some ruins have the remains of a [[Gek]] statue, which can be a heritage from the domination period of the [[Script of the Gek|Gek First Spawn]].
- If fully excavated to the maximum depth, much of the original ruin structures may sink and become unreachable.
- Constructed items cannot be placed directly on ruin surfaces.
