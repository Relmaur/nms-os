# Beacon (Building)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Interaction Location
- **Added in:** Origins

**Beacon** is a Point of Interest.

## Summary

**Beacon** is a [[Point of Interest]] that can be found on planets. It can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by looking for the  icon.

It allows to scan for nearby Advanced Lifeforms, such as [[Gek]], [[Korvax]], or [[Vy'keen]], inhabiting a [[Minor Settlement]].

## Discovered beacons (pre-release)

- Beacon HYNE.0E3.06A.0AC on [[Colbert Prime]]
- Beacon MOCI-033.0E4.087 on [[Sugas-Uomi]]
- Unnamed beacon on [[Notogertasi]]
