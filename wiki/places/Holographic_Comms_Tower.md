# Holographic Comms Tower

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Communication
- **Type:** Planetary
- **Added in:** Adrift
- **Description:** Used to communicate with other travellers

**Holographic Comms Tower** is a point of interest.

## Summary

The **Holographic Comms Tower**, also referred to as **Holo-Terminus**, is a planetary [[Point of Interest]].

Holo-Terminus can be used for communicating with other projected Travellers.

## Usage

Its [[terminal]] is used to activate the tower by giving it 30 [[Sodium]]. In the [[storyline]], [[Artemis]], [[Apollo]] and [[-null-]] are the Travellers which can be projected, based on the current mission. Outside of the storyline, it will just transmit dialogue in the system's faction's language.
