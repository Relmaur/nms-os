# Alien Trader

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Inhabited
- **Frequency:** 12
- **Interaction:** Trade
- **Rewards:** Items for Purchase
- **Added in:** Relics

**Alien Trader** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Alien Trader** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Alien Traders appear as an NPC-piloted [[starship]]. They can have the appearance of any starship normally found in the current [[star system|system]].

## Behaviors

Upon dropping out of pulse, an Alien Trader will attempt to communicate with a starship pilot. If communicated with, they will offer a variety of basic [[resource|resources]] and items for purchase.

Should [[Pirate|pirates]] attack the player when one is near, they will assist the player. Firing upon an Alien Trader will cause them to become hostile.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any inhabited system and have a very high frequency of appearance.

## Additional Information

- This space encounter is, by far, the most common encounter ever.
