# Gaseous Sentience

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Use communicator
- **Rewards:** 100-300 Nanites
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Gaseous Sentience** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Gaseous Sentience** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Gaseous Sentiences appear to be a massive spherical ball of energy. They can be encountered in different colors, which does not otherwise affect their behavior.

## Behavior

Upon approach, a Gaseous Sentience will attempt to communicate with a [[starship]] pilot. If communicated with, they will speak cryptically, then give the pilot 100-300 [[nanites]].

Firing upon a Gaseous Sentience will have no effect on them, nor will they become aggressive. However, if you fire upon a Gaseous Sentience, you will take slight damage.

Can be pushed by the player's ship.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
