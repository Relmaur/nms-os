# Autophage Camp

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Planetary
- **Description:** A primary location where some Autophage reside.
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Autophage Camp** is a point of interest.

## Summary

**Autophage Camp** is a type of [[Point of Interest|point of interest]]. These camps are hidden and can be found among some "abandoned" points of interest, including [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]], [[Beacon (Building)|Beacons]], and [[Crashed Freighter|Crashed Freighters]]. When installed in a [[Multi-Tool]], the [[Polyphonic Core]] can be used to locate them. Both the Polyphonic Core and the [[Scan Harmoniser]] can be used to decloak the camps and [[Autophage]] NPCs with a local scan, once the site has been reached. Camp locations are marked by a yellow Autophage icon.

Sufficient progress must be made in the [[They Who Returned]] questline to obtain the necessary technologies for detection, and to interact with any Autophage entities or the [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal|Autophage Synthesis Terminals]].

The camp does not exist at inhabited points of interest.

## Features

- Autophage lifeforms: There are generally three to six Autophage NPCs found at each camp.
- Autophage missions: By interacting with a given Autophage, the player can accept missions in exchange for [[Void Mote|Void Motes]], words of their [[Language]], and reputation gains.
- Autophage Synthesis Terminals: Staff components can be purchased and crafted into [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Voltaic Staffs]]. Unique Autophage themed cosmetics (equipped at the [[Appearance Modifier]]) and [[Rebuilt Exosuit Module|Rebuilt Exosuit Modules]] can also be bought here.
- Exchanging [[Void Mote|Void Motes]]

## Additional Information

After completion of the They Who Returned quest, one can find further Autophage Camps by jumping into the system with Dissonant tag, then using the [[Echo Locator]] to find the camp.

Post-They Who Returned the way to exchange [[Void Mote|Void Motes]] and assemble a staff might be confusing for some. After approaching the [[Harmonic Interface]] for the first time in the camp and unlocking it through the override glyphs only two options will appear: 1. Deactivate Multi-Tool seal and 2. Locate Dissonance Spikes. Pick either of them, then interact with the Harmonic Interface again. Now you will get to see the Exchange void motes and Staff assembly features along with either of the previous options that you did not pick.
