# Grave of the Ocean King

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Enter and exit
- **Rewards:** Fossil Sample or Ancient Skeleton
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Grave of the Ocean King** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Grave of the Ocean King** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Graves of the Ocean King appear to be a massive skull from an unknown space creature.

## Behavior

Graves of the Ocean King are static objects, unable to respond to hails or being fired upon. If a pilot flies close enough to one, they will be "bestowed" a reward of either a [[Fossil Sample|fossil sample]] or [[Ancient Skeleton|ancient skeleton]], both with a 50% chance.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
