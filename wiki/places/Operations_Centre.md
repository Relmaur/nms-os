# Operations Centre

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Building
- **Added in:** NextGen

An **Operations Centre** is a [[Point of Interest]] found on planets.

## Summary

**Operations Centres** are planetary locations with locked steel doors. As of version 1.37, Operation Centres give the same rewards as [[Manufacturing Facilities]], but they have a higher chance to reward technology [[blueprint|blueprints]]. They can occasionally reward a blueprint for [[Frigate|Frigate consumable upgrades]].

Operations Centres can be found by using [[Planetary Chart]] (secure site) or by following "raid planetary harvester" missions from the [[Mission Board]]. These missions, however, will not give any blueprint rewards.

## Structure

It is a three-block single-structure location with a parking beacon. It can be easily confused with an [[Observatory]], which has a satellite dish on top of it. The front door of the structure is locked and will require the player to blast it open with the [[Plasma Launcher]], [[Boltcaster]] or [[Scatter Blaster]] which will alarm [[sentinel|sentinels]].

Behind the door in the centre of the room is a terminal with which the player will have to interact to solve a randomly-generated [[problem]]. The structure will have 0-3 extensions that require an [[AtlasPass v2]] to enter. The operations centre is guarded closely by three [[Sentinel Drone|sentinel drones]] and two [[Sentinel Quad|Sentinel Quads]].
