# Waypoints

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Save Location point
- **Added in:** Origins

A **Waypoint** is a Point of Interest structure.

## Summary

Waypoints enable saving one's game, and logging of one's discovery of a [[Point of Interest]]. They can be found as standalone Points of Interest, but also alongside the following, listed below.

### Points of Interest:

1. [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]]
1. [[Boundary Failure|Boundary Failures]]
1. [[Crashed Freighter|Crashed Freighters]]
1. [[Crashed Ship|Crashed Ships]]
1. [[Depot|Depots]]
1. [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]]
1. [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]]
1. [[Manufacturing Facilities]]
1. [[Holographic Comms Tower|Holographic Comms Towers]]
1. [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]]
1. [[Observatories]]
1. [[Shelter|Shelters]]
1. [[Transmission Tower|Transmission Towers]]
Waypoints are the sole means of claiming discovery of specific regions of a [[planet]]. [[Discoveries]] of this nature can be uploaded for 3 [[Nanite Clusters]] per discovered region.

## Name generation

The names are procedurally generated using one of the following methods, where the Sector and Numeral terms are procedurally generated as well.

The headers indicate the landscape according to the game file. The sequence is also taken from the game file.

## Additional Information

- The name generation is extracted from the NMS_UPDATE3_ENGLISH.MBIN (1.77) game file.
- Waypoints can be detected using a [[starship]]'s scanner. A detected Waypoint displays as an **Unknown Building**.
- When one nears a Waypoint, typically a message will appear on the HUD with the message: "Waypoint Detected".
- In [[Pre-release]] versions of the game, Waypoints were a larger structure called [[Beacon (Building)|Beacons]], and were suggested to be one's method of uploading all discoveries, instead of the discoveries menu found in the release version of the game. While Beacons remain in the present version of the game, they have since changed in functionality.
