# Living Metalloid

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** Very Low
- **Interaction:** None
- **Rewards:** Salvaged Scrap, Starship Upgrade
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Living Metalloids** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Living Metalloids** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Living Metalloids can appear in a variety of geometric shapes with a metallic surface.

## Behavior

Living Metalloids will not respond if fired upon, but can be damaged and destroyed.

## Rewards

- [[Salvaged Scrap]] (50%)
- Starship Upgrade (12.5% each):
  - [[Pulse Engine]]
  - [[Hyperdrive]]
  - [[Deflector Shield]]
  - [[Photon Cannon]]

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
