## Summary

**Monolith Type 02** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 02 is a tapered column with circular "eye" near the top and cylinders in a groove at the very top.
