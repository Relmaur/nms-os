# Harmonic Scrap

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Planetary
- **Description:** Wreckage of an unbuild Autophage.

**Harmonic Scrap** is a planetary point of interest.

## Summary

**Harmonic Scrap** is one of various planetary [[Point of Interest|points of interest]]. They are damaged, partially constructed [[Autophage]] shells found on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]]. Their goal is probably to rebuild an entire carapace and thus be able to move. They are often found in the wild, but they are also found near the wreckage of [[Sentinel Interceptor|Sentinel Interceptors]] and in [[Harmonic Camp|Harmonic Camps]].

## Description

These machines can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] (Autophage icon). The machine has a screen displaying a corrupted image (red glitched circle containing four symbols). These four symbols are actually characters from the coded [[Alphabet]], meaning the word "Void".

## Interactions

By interacting with the machine, you must first remove the corruption. This "corruption" is a recoverable resource, often [[Pugneum]], but also sometimes more interesting resources such as [[Walker Brain|Walker Brains]] (x10), [[Quad Servo|Quad Servoes]] (x10) or [[Inverted Mirror|Inverted Mirrors]] (x10).
