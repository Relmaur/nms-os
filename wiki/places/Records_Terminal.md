# Records Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Rewards:** Crew Manifest
- **Added in:** Desolation

**Records Terminal** is a freighter terminal.

## Summary

The Records Terminal is a terminal aboard a Derelict Freighter. It is always located in the third-to-last room in the derelict, the [[Engineering Control]] being the last.

## Interactions

The player is tasked with accessing the Crew Manifest from this terminal during the [[Lost in Space]] mission.

Interacting will show three records: two will be encrypted, and the accessible one will be the Crew Manifest. After performing a bio-scan and reading the manifest, the player will be given a portable digital download.
