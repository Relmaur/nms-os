## Summary

**Monolith Type 11** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 11 is an elongated octahedron with circular indentation near top. Long end of diamond points toward the ground.
