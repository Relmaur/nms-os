# Mutant Plant

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Resource Node
- **Added in:** Beyond

A **Mutant Plant** is a type of resource node.

## Summary

A **Mutant Plant** is a type of [[resource]] node that appears on various [[biome|biomes]] and it can be harvested with the [[Mining Beam]]. They can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Types by Biome

Mutant Plants have different looks depending on the biome they live in and offer different resources:
- In an [[Biome - Irradiated|irradiated biome]], they yield [[Radon]]. They are vegetative carbuncles that appear as short and squat throbbing blobs which belch fumes.
- In a [[Biome - Frozen|frozen biome]], they yield [[Sulphurine]]. They appear as mushrooms with floral tentacles which float in the air.

## Release History

- The Mutant Plants on irradiated worlds yielded [[Calium]] in the past.
