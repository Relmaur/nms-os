# Colossal Archive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Type:** Trade Outpost
- **Added in:** WorldsOne
- **Description:** Trade Outpost

**Colossal Archive** is a point of interest.

## Summary

A **Colossal Archive** aka Planetary Archive is one of the various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

## Colossal Archive

A type of habitable outpost with a series of multiple physical [[Landing Pad|Landing Pads]], and a range of different terminal and NPC interaction possibilities.

### Features

These inhabited structures are equipped with several items of interest:

- Multiple NPC lifeforms which may interact with the player in a similar manner as those in a [[Trading Post]].
- A [[Guild Envoy]] which functions similar to an envoy on a [[Space station]].
- A **Local Information Repository** terminal which, when interacted with, grants a [[Artifact Chart]] for 15 [[nanites]].
- A [[Technology Merchant]] which also sells goods not sold by other merchants (such as [[Antimatter Housing|Antimatter Housings]] and [[Drop Pod Coordinate Data]])
- A [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] which, like terminals at Trading Posts, offers superior prices for [[Tradeable|marketed tradeables]] compared to terminals on [[space station|space stations]]
- Five or Six landing pads which can be visited by NPC or human travellers similar to a trading post.
- At least three [[Artifact Exchange Vault|Artifact Exchange Vaults]] (one on the skywalk, two at ground level) which allows the operator to exchange an [[artifact]] for one of potentially higher or equivalent value.
- A Planetary Archive (aka Celestial Archive) [[terminal]] providing race-specific lore. Requires some knowledge of the race language to operate.
  - [[Colossal Archive Lore - Gek]]
  - [[Colossal Archive Lore - Korvax]]
  - [[Colossal Archive Lore - Vy'keen]]

## Additional Information

- It can be discovered by using [[Planetary Chart (inhabited outpost)]]. Upon discovering it, the chart will say "planetary archive" located.
- Multiple instances per planet/moon are possible.
- You can repeatedly find colossal archives by using the planetary chart multiple times. Once a chart has found a colossal archive, keep using planetary charts (the game will give an error saying that a place has been already been found) until you receive the global scan and the search will always guarantee a colossal archive. Repeat this in the same solar system.
- The guild envoy assigned to the Space Station will hold the same inventory as those envoys found on Planetary Archives.
