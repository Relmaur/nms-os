# Resource Depot

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Resource Depot
- **Description:** 5 silos containing different materials
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Resource Depot** is a [[Point of Interest]].

## Summary

**Resource Depot** is a resource storage that usually consists of 5 containers. When the player destroys one, a [[Wanted level]] of 1 star will be placed upon the player and one [[Sentinel]] will arrive at the scene and an additional one per container up to total of three.

## Rewards

Each container yields one of the following [[resource|resources]] or [[product|products]] when destroyed:
- [[Alloy|Alloys]]: 3-5 [[Grantine]], [[Lemmium]], [[Magno-Gold]], [[Aronium]], [[Dirty Bronze]], [[Herox]]
- Atmospheric Gases: 100-250 [[Nitrogen]], [[Radon]], [[Sulphurine]]
- Gas Products: 3-5 [[Nitrogen Salt]], [[Enriched Carbon]], [[Thermic Condensate]]
- Asteroid Metals: 100-250 [[Silver]], [[Gold]], [[Platinum]]

## Additional Information

- When destroyed it immediately triggers a sentinel response on level 3.
  - An exception is when a depot is destroyed on a dissonant planet, where it only triggers a response on level 1.
- It can also be located with the [[Signal Booster]] by scanning the area, or from [[Planetary Chart|Planetary Charts]] - Secure Frequencies, but this option can provide locations for [[Manufacturing Facilities]] or [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]] as well.
- It can be detected by scanning with an [[Exocraft]] that is outfitted with an [[Exocraft Signal Booster]]. The option to scan for it can be accessed through the [[Quick Menu]] by pressing  (PS4/PC/Xbox).
