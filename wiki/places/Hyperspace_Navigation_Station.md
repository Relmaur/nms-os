# Hyperspace Navigation Station

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Use communicator
- **Rewards:** Unknown
- **Added in:** Living Ship

**Hyperspace Navigation Station** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Hyperspace Navigation Station** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Hyperspace navigation stations appear to be a two massive thrusters pointing in opposite directions with a cylindrical plasma coil connecting them.

## Behavior

Upon approach, a hyperspace navigation station will attempt to communicate with a [[starship]] pilot. If communicated with, they send an encoded message of 16 hexadecimal digits which can be used as galactic coordinates or as a portal code.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.

### Systems Regularly Encountered In

The following systems have regularly encountered hyperspace navigation stations.
- 1 min. 55 sec., [[Strom-Perth ei'Folr]]

## Additional information

- The transmitted 16 hexadecimal digit code is the [[Universal Address]] address for the current system.
  - To translate to a [[portal address]] remove digits 1,2,7,8 from the code; digits 7,8 will be the hex representation for the current galaxy.
- All Hyperspace Navigation Stations found in the same system as each other will always broadcast the same signal/code.
- Speculation of purpose:
  - They were established by the ancients to allow galactic travelers the ability to determine their position and galaxy without needing to first land on a planet and use a [[Signal Booster]] or a [[portal]].
  - An old theory was that by removing the first or last four digits they would point to another system as part elaborate maze of systems that led to a yet undiscovered secret.  Possibly confused with [[Anomalous Numbers Station|Anomalous Numbers Stations]]
