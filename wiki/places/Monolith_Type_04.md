## Summary

**Monolith Type 04** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 04 is a square column with circular hole near top. There is a sloped ramp that wraps up around the sides of the column to access the elevated interaction point.
