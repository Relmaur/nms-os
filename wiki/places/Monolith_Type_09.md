## Summary

**Monolith Type 09** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 09 is a cuboid monolith with circular features running front-to-back, potential rotating cylinders inside circular features.
