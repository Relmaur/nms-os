## Summary

**Monolith Type 05** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 05 is a Half-circle column with one flat front and rounded back, circular centre-piece either hollow or filled, with an inclined top.
