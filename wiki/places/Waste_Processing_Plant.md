# Waste Processing Plant

## Quick Facts

- **Added in:** Remnant

**Waste Processing Plant** is a point of interest.

## Summary

**Waste Processing Plant** is a location where [[Industrial Waste]] can be reclaimed and recycled. They can be located on planets where [[Salvageable Scrap]] is present.

There are four disposal units where waste products can be deposited in. They are color categorized into four categories: Scrap, Toxic, Radioactive, and Explosive. Scrap brought to the Processing Plant must be sorted manually using a [[Gravitino Coil]] to pick up and place them into the proper disposal unit. Common metallic Scrap are placed into a fire furnace. Green Toxic waste are dropped into a green toxic silo at the top on the left side. Radioactive yellow units are placed into a yellow turbine unit on the middle. The red Explosive containers are placed into a red crushing unit on the right end side.

Upon placing scrap into the correct unit, the salvager is compensated with a small sum of [[Nanite Cluster|Nanites]]. Sometimes when depositing waste products, other resources may be reclaimed in the process. Scraps must be placed into the correct disposal unit, as placing them into the wrong unit will result in loss of the waste product and no payout.

At the the center of the processing plant is a control panel hosting a Valuation Scale Operation where the Processing Plant may auto-sort waste products. To use, a [[Colossus]] exocraft with a flatbed chassis loaded with scrap must be present near the control panel. Upon verification, the Processing Plant will collect all scrap in the Colossus and exchange them with default Nanites. This process has a less amount of Nanite payout as the scrap processing is done by the Plant instead.

## Additional information

- The Toxic disposal unit has an inner forcefield on top that can catch toxic scrap thrown from the ground.
- The processing machinery can be entered on foot, which will inflict rapid amounts of damage while within the machine.
