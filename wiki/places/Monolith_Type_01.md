## Summary

**Monolith Type 01** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 01 is a tapered square column with engraved obelisk and various circular designs on sides and back.

There are two sub-variations, one with a staircase to the interaction point, and one with the interaction point on the ground.
