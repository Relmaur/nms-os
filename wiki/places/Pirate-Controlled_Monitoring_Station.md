# Pirate-Controlled Monitoring Station

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Shoot down
- **Rewards:** Increased standing after defeating pirates
- **Added in:** ExoMech

**Pirate-Controlled Monitoring Station** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Pirate-Controlled Monitoring Station** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Pirate-controlled monitoring stations have the appearance of a rectangular mass of tangled pipes covered in protrusions resembling copper padlocks, sometimes with a small bronze antenna on top.

## Behavior

Once a pirate-controlled monitoring station is destroyed, a group of [[pirate|pirates]] will appear to attack the pilot. If they are destroyed, it will increase the player's standing with the system's local [[faction]].

## Location

They can be randomly found in any inhabited [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.

### Systems Regularly Encountered In

The following systems have regularly encountered pirate-controlled monitoring stations.
- 3 min. 40 sec., [[Othala fi'Gronn]]
- [[Ischel-Althu]]
