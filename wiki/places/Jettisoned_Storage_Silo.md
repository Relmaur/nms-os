# Jettisoned Storage Silo

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Shoot down
- **Rewards:** Resource (varies per encounter)
- **Added in:** ExoMech

**Jettisoned Storage Silo** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Jettisoned Storage Silo** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Jettisoned storage silos are large cylindrical shipping containers. Looking at one will show what type of cargo is stored inside.

## Behavior

Jettisoned storage silos are static objects and have no behavior. Firing upon and destroying one will allow the attacker to acquire the cargo inside.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
