# Unknown Grave

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Interaction Location
- **Type:** Interaction Location
- **Description:** Glyph collection
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Unknown Grave** is a point of interest.

## Summary

**Unknown Grave** is the grave of a fallen [[Traveller]] and can be found on the surface of planets.

Finding a grave will prove challenging without the help of a [[Traveller]], who can be found on space stations or (very occasionally) in planet-side structures such as [[Observatory|observatories]], [[Minor Settlement|Minor Settlements]], or [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]]. When asked where they are from and in exchange for 100 [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]], Travellers will provide the location of a grave on one of the planets in the system.   While many graves can be found randomly while walking on most planets, they only show up on radar if the player is within 50u of them, making them hard to find despite being plentiful. Travellers will typically only send players to the same one or two graves per star system despite the plethora of them on many planets.

### Rewards

Activating the grave will trigger a broken message from the Traveller itself, and provide a [[Glyph]] that can be used in a [[Portal]]. The player will also receive a random technology [[Blueprint]] in the form of [[Memory Fragment|Memory Fragments]]. Players may sometimes alternatively receive Nanite Clusters.

Using the [[Analysis Visor]] shows them as **Unknown Grave** indicated with the  icon.

## Appearance

Traveller Graves are white with yellow glowing edges and bear a strong resemblance to [[Atlas Station|Atlas Stations]]. Upon the death of the player, a similar grave is spawned, although it is black with red edges.

## Additional Information

You may have to talk to the Travellers a second time to get the option to pay for the grave location.

## Release History

- [[Atlas Rises]] - A glitch on PS4 allowed players to collect all 16 Glyphs from one Grave. This glitch has been patched.
- [[NEXT]] - A glitch on Xbox One allowed players to collect all 16 Glyphs from one Grave. This glitch has since not been patched.
- [[Waypoint]] - Added Memory Fragment as a reward after activating the grave.
