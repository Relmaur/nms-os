# Emergency Containment Device

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** Low
- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Terrifying Sample
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Emergency Containment Device** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Emergency Containment Device** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Emergency Containment Device has the shape of a glowing box surrounded by four triangular mega-structures.

## Behavior

Destroying the Emergency Containment Device can drop a [[Terrifying Sample]].

In some cases, keeping this item in your inventory will cause this Space Encounter to stalk you / keep occurring; pulling you out of pulse drive without the usual warning.

Can also drop [[Delicate Flora (PLNT)|Delicate Flora Artifacts]] and [[Biological Sample|Biological Samples]].

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a low frequency of appearance.
