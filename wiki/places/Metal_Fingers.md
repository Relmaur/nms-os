# Metal 'Fingers'

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Resource Node
- **Added in:** Visions

**Metal 'Fingers**' are a type of resource node.

## Summary

**Metal 'Fingers**' are a type of rare [[resource]] node and mined with the [[Mining Beam]]. They can be found on any random planet.

They can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

They are extremely resistant to damage, and without upgrades it can take over a minute to finish mining one. However, due to the fact that they only give their resources in one large burst at the end, [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] will not become hostile during the mining process.

There are two types of 'fingers':
- [[Gold]]
- [[Uranium]]
