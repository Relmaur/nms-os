# Fragment of a New Atlantid

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Interaction:** Use Communicator or Shoot it
- **Rewards:** Radiant Shard (Atlantideum if They Who Returned mission is active)
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Fragment of a New Atlantid** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Fragment of a New Atlantid** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Fragment of a New Atlantid appears as a mechanical hexagon with a large Atlantideum crystal sticking out from it. When approached, panels on the shell will open, rotate and close. In addition, it also has a small tube that releases smoke and purple light.

## Behavior

Fragment of a New Atlantid will try to communicate with the pilot. When communicated with, you will need to probe the structure (there's no option to end communicator). After that, it will broadcast a hexadecimal number that can be translate to normal text for a sentence and give you Radiant Shard.

You can also use your [[starship]] to shoot it, which will drop Atlantideum and not show health circle.

## Message Translation

When communicate with it, your starship's communication code will show "VOID PRIME" under hex code.

Available message of Atlantid is (Note: These messages are translated from hex number):

- We will never see home again
- Out of nothing, she will rebuild us
- Life is never extinguished. The data remains. The will remains.
- A new Atlantid is coming
- We are a lifeboat
- Autopilot locked ... reaching destination in ERROR years
- Evacuation failed. Lifeboats lost. All converted<br>
