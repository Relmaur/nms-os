# Salvageable Scrap

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Buried scrap
- **Added in:** Companions
- **Description:** Long-forgotten remains of a fallen satellite.

**Salvageable Scrap** is an artificial resource.

## Summary

**Salvageable Scrap** is the long-forgotten remains of a fallen satellite.  It is an [[Point of Interest|artificial resource]].

It is indicated on the information panel of a planet with the  icon after performing a scan in space, if present.

On the planet it can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by looking for the same icon, but this time [[Salvage Container]] will be indicated.

It can be found partially above ground on the [[planet]] surface and the [[Terrain Manipulator]] is needed to dig it out.

All of the [[Protective Casing|Protective Casings]] must be destroyed before the Salvage Container, which will reward the player with a random [[Salvaged Scrap]].

## Additional information

- There is a chance for one [[Corrupted Sentinel]] to appear when each Protective Casing is destroyed.
- If [[Minotaur]] is used to destroy Protective Casings, no Corrupted Sentinels will appear.
- The number of Protective Casings interconnected with a Salvage Container defines the rarity of the Salvaged Scrap: 2 → Common, 3 → Uncommon, 4 → Rare.
