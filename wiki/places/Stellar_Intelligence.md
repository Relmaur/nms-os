# Stellar Intelligence

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Use communicator
- **Rewards:** Possibly coordinates to an Ancient Ruin
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Stellar Intelligence** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Stellar Intelligence** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Stellar Intelligences appear to be a massive entity resembling a cluster of neurons, with small electrical sparks flickering at the tips of its branches.

Stellar Intelligences can come in a variety of color pairs for its central core and its branches. The tips of the branches share the color of the core. Their core will most often be red paired with white, yellow, or red branches. The color of the branches on red Stellar Intelligences seems to occur at equal frequency. There is a less common variant with a dark green core and cyan branches. This rarer variant will always have these two colors.

Their core color seems to most commonly be red, and their branches seem to most commonly be either white, red, or yellow. A rare variant with a dark green core and cyan branches has been observed, but it is unknown if this difference is caused by external variables or simply random chance.

## Behavior

Upon approach, a Stellar Intelligence will attempt to communicate with a [[starship]] pilot. If communicated with, they will speak cryptically, and possibly give coordinates to an ancient [[ruin]].

Firing weapons at Stellar Intelligence will cause damage to the attacker of 0 points. Ramming the Stellar Intelligence will cause it to move; pushing the Stellar Intelligence into a planet's atmosphere will cause it to despawn with no effects or rewards.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.

### Systems Regularly Encountered In

The following systems have regularly encountered Stellar Intelligences.
- 0 min. 35 sec., [[Strom-Perth ei'Folr]]
- [[Rycempler Travellers Foundation Star]]
- [[Kyomei]] (Done using the [[Anomaly Detector]])

## Additional information

When in close proximity to a Stellar Intelligence (about 1,500u), the player will start to hear static and crackling caused by the electrical emissions at the tips of the neuron-like branches of the entity. The volume of this static increases as you get closer to it.
