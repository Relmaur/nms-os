# Natural Burial Site

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Ancient Bones
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Natural Burial Site** is an artificial resource.

## Summary

**Natural Burial Site** can be found less than 100u under the [[planet]] surface. It is an [[Point of Interest|artificial resource]].

It can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by looking for the  icon. The [[Terrain Manipulator]] is needed to dig the [[Ancient Bones]] out of the ground.

After the Ancient Bones are uncovered, they can be opened and the player will be rewarded with a random [[Excavated Bones]].

## Additional information

- If present, Ancient Bones will be indicated on the information panel of a planet after performing a scan in space.
- There is a chance for [[Vigilant Stone|Vigilant Stones]] to spawn in Natural Burial Sites. Once disturbed, a Stone Guardian spawns and is immediately hostile. Be prepared for a fight, or to make a quick escape. Having your ship nearby is recommended.
