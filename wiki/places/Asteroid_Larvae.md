# Asteroid Larvae

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Shoot
- **Rewards:** None
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Asteroid Larvae** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Asteroid Larvae** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]].

## Appearance

Asteroid Larvae appear to be a massive [[Abyssal Horror (stationary)|Abyssal Horror]]. It's cylindrical in shape with a orange-red, lizard-like eye on the front. It also has small tendrils in the same colour. It's about the size of 2 [[Space Anomaly|Space Anomalies]] next to each other.

## Behavior

Upon approach, an Asteroid Larvae will show no sign of being bothered by a pilot.

Firing upon an Asteroid Larvae will cause it to immediately attack and deal damage per 0.5 second, although it will only fire straight forward. This makes them easy to avoid merely by staying to the side.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
