## Summary

**Monolith Type 10** is a type of [[Point of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]]. There are 17 different types of [[Monolith|Monoliths]], but all interaction and results are similar.

Type 10 is a floating elongated diamond with potential diamond or circular holes through the depth of the structure.
