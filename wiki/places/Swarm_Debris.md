# Swarm Debris

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Buried scrap
- **Added in:** Cosmos
- **Description:** The buried, ruined remnants of crashed swarmer ships.

**Swarm Debris** are an artificial resource.

## summary

**Swarm Debris** are the buried, ruined remnants of crashed swarmer ships. It is an [[Point of Interest|artificial resource]].

It is indicated on the information panel of a [[planet]] with the  Icon after performing a scan in space, if present.

On the planet it can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by looking for the  icon.

It can be found underground just below the planet surface and it can be excavated by using the [[Terrain Manipulator]].

The player can then interact with the Swarm Debris, which will reward the player with [[Corrupted Ichor]].

## Additional Information

- There is a chance for Planetary Swarmers to appear, they will attack the player to protect the **Swarm Debris**.
