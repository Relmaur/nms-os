# Autophage Synthesis Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** Used to make purchases with void motes.
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

An **Autophage Synthesis Terminal** is used to purchase and craft Autophage themed items.

## Summary

An **Autophage Synthesis Terminal** is an interactive object used to purchase [[Autophage]] themed appearance customisations for the [[Exosuit]], as well as purchasing themed components and assembling them into [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Voltaic Staff]] [[Multi-Tool|Multi-Tools]]. Autophage terminals and [[Harmonic Interface|Harmonic Interfaces]] are the only source of [[Rebuilt Exosuit Module|Rebuilt Exosuit Modules]], which are used to obtain [[Salvaged Upgrade Components]] for the Exosuit. [[Void Mote|Void Motes]] are the currency used to make all purchases.

## Locations

These terminals are found at [[Autophage Camp|Autophage Camps]] on systems are inhabited by sentient [[Species]]. The [[Polyphonic Core]] and [[Scan Harmoniser]] technologies (acquired throughout the [[They Who Returned]] questline) are also needed to find and/or reveal Autophage camps and NPCs. Locations are marked by a yellow Autophage icon. Camps found on [[Biome - Dead|Dead]] or [[Biome - Exotic|Exotic]] biomes will have a terminal and infrastructure, but no NPCs.

26 Exosuit customisations, 19 unique staff components, repair kits, and Rebuilt Exosuit Modules may be available for purchase. The terminal may also be used to sell items for [[Units]]  Units.<br>

## Additional Information

- Harmonic Interfaces offer the same pool of items to purchase as Autophage Synthesis Terminals.
- Reclaimed is the only style set with two staff head options. It is assumed the Recycled Pole component belongs to this set despite having a different name.
- If the staff color and class rating at one terminal is undesirable, crafting can be canceled by exiting the terminal. A new terminal on the same planet may be located to find a more desirable option.
- Crafting blueprints for the three Voltaic components (Concentric Transducer, Atlantideum Chamber, and Fiberglass Grip) are awarded in the Autophage questline and don't have to be purchased.
- Progress must be made in the They Who Returned questline to find Autophage Synthesis Terminals, or to access the purchasing and staff crafting options in Harmonic Interfaces.
- Every interface has a set rarity, slot layout, and color scheme of any staff crafted at it, if you don't like the staff it offers you can simply decline to get the parts back and try again at another camp. Multiple camps with interfaces can be found on a planet. If you are willing to spend a few hours and a lot of launch fuel it's doable to get an S-class staff with four adjacent supercharge slots in the exact color you prefer.
