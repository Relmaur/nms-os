# Trade Outpost

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Type:** Trade Outpost
- **Description:** Trade Outpost
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Trade Outpost** is a planetary Point of Interest.

## Summary

The **Trading Posts** or **Trade Outpost** are a type of planetary [[Point of Interest]].  Similar to [[space station|space stations]], trading posts feature a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] and seven [[Landing Pad|Landing Pads]] where [[starship|starships]] can land. In the main section, the player can find 3-6 NPCs of that [[Star system|system's]] dominant [[faction]], and, in rare occasions, a [[Traveller]].

[[Cargo Drop|Cargo Drops]] can be found near the landing pads and in the lower sections of the Trading Post.

## Locating

Trading posts can be found by:
- Using the [[Starship|starship's]] [[Economy Scanner]]
- Asking an alien NPC for directions
- Using a [[Planetary Chart (inhabited outpost)]] for finding inhabited outposts (may reveal any type of structure, not just a trading post)
- Using a [[Signal Booster]] to locate nearby structures. (may reveal any type of structure, not just a trading post)
- By pure exploration

## Trade routes

Following [[Trade route|Trade routes]] or starships in space or in a [[planet]]'s atmosphere can lead the player to a trading post.

## Prices and tradeables

Prices and tradeables at trading posts can differ from the system's space station. Trading posts seem to offer far fewer resources (Ex: [[Ferrite Dust]]) than Space stations, but more rare items for a cheaper price.

## Missions

Delivery missions will ask the player to deliver a certain [[resource]] or [[tradeable]] to a trading post in that system. Once there, the player has to enter the post's Galactic Trade Terminal in order to complete the mission.

Another mission that includes a trip to a trade outpost is the high-rank variant of the [[Mission Board|Find Missing Person]].

## Additional Information

- In [[Pirate-Controlled]] [[Star system|Star systems]], all trading posts on planets will be called a *Black Market Terminal* when viewed through the [[Analysis Visor]].

## Release history

- [[Beyond]] - Staircases were introduced so that [[jetpack]] access is no longer required
- [[Outlaws]] - Added Black Market Terminal variants which are only found in Pirate-Controlled systems.
