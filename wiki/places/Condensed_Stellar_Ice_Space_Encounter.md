# Condensed Stellar Ice (Space Encounter)

## Quick Facts

- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Quicksilver, Nanites, Uranium, Condensed Carbon, Di-hydrogen, Dioxite
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Condensed Stellar Ice** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Condensed Stellar Ice** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]].

## Appearance

Condensed Stellar Ice appears as a glowing trail of large ice crystals.

## Behavior

Approaching the ice shows its contents, but currency rewards will only display "Condensed Stellar Ice". This can be used to know in advance, prior to completing its destruction, which drop the ice may contain.

Destroying the Condensed Stellar Ice will drop one of the following items:
- [[Condensed Carbon]] 900-1500
- [[Di-hydrogen]] 300-900
- [[Uranium]] 800-1200
- [[Quicksilver]] x 100
- [[Nanite Cluster|Nanites]] 100-300
- [[Dioxite]] 800-1200
