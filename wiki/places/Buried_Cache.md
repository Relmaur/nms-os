# Buried Cache

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Buried cache
- **Added in:** Breach

**Buried Cache** is an artificial resource.

## Summary

**Buried Cache** is a [[Point of Interest|artificial resource]] with long-forgotten buried contents.

## Locating

On a [[planet]], it can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by looking for the  icon. Alternately, its  icon can be added to the Heads-Up Display by pressing the scan button in the resource's vicinity while piloting an [[Exocraft]] with a radar [[technology]] installed.

It has the same icon as [[Cargo Drop]] and typically is one buried yellow container that can be excavated from the shallow surface by using the [[Terrain Manipulator]].

## Contents

After opening, the container can have a Suspicious Module or other high value loot, depending on whether abandoned mode is active for the current save.

## Additional Information

- Buried Caches have different contents in abandoned mode. In particular, [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]] for Exocraft other than the [[Minotaur]] and [[Nautilon]] can only be obtained from these points of interest on abandoned mode saves.
- On occasion, a Buried Cache can appear at the same location as a [[Subterranean Organic Structure]], a [[Buried Mineral Formation]], a lone [[Buried Technology Module]], or another Buried Cache.
