# Emergency Civilisation Shelter Pod

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 2
- **Interaction:** Use Communicator
- **Rewards:** None
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Emergency Civilisation Shelter Pod** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Emergency Civilisation Shelter Pod** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] composed of a large space city.

## Appearance

Emergency Civilisation Shelter Pod appears as a huge sphere protected by a flashing hexagonal shield and attached to a base composed of tubes and various structures that appear to be generators.

## Behavior

Emergency Civilisation Shelter Pod is a space city where a large population was embarked, and has various purposes. So far the following missions have been documented:
- Seeding a new society
- Fleeing a technological failure
- Fleeing a planetary failure
- Fleeing a Stellar Collapse
- Fleeing conflict
- Fleeing nano-catastrophe
- Gene Bank
- Haven from Sentinels

The population is in Cryogenic Stasis, as one Pod encountered in [[Nugsfo OR-Gamma]] reported a Cryogenic Failure.

Upon encountering it, a Ship Communicator message is prompted, and a dialogue option without choices gives you information about the Pod current state, including:
- Homeworld
- Mission
- Status
- Days since contact
- Surviving population

In those encountered, it seems that the population is not tied to a particular faction and that several decades or centuries have passed since launch.  Firing on the superstructure has no discernable effect.

## Location

They can randomly be found in any [[star system]] and have a low frequency of appearance.
