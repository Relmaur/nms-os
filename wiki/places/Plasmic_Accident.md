# Plasmic Accident

## Quick Facts

- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Mordite and Terrifying Sample
- **Added in:** Orbital

A **Plasmic Accident** is a space encounter.

## Summary

A **Plasmic Accident** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

A Plasmic Accident appears as nebulous, glowing mass with tendrils at its poles. It can be destroyed through being fired at from your starship.

## Behavior

Destroying the Plasmic Accident will drop significant amounts of [[Mordite]] and one [[Terrifying Sample]]. After destruction, the message "Extreme Sub-Audible Resonance Detected" will be displayed.
