**Rubble of the First Spawn** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Rubble of the First Spawn** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

Rubble of the First Spawn appear to be a massive, shattered statue with the head of a Gek.

## Behavior

Rubble of the First Spawn are static objects and have no behavior. They respond to neither communicator hails nor being fired upon.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a very low frequency of appearance.
