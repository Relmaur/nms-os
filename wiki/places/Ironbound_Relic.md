# Ironbound Relic

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** Low
- **Interaction:** Fire to Destroy
- **Rewards:** Platinum, Gold, Silver, Ancient Skeleton, Fossil Sample, Terrifying Sample
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Ironbound Relic** is a space encounter.

## Summary

**Ironbound Relic** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

## Appearance

An Ironbound Relic looks like an enormous chest with glowing green lights floating in space.

## Behavior

Destroying the Ironbound Relic will drop one of the following items: [[Platinum]] / [[Gold]] / [[Silver]] / [[Ancient Skeleton]] / [[Fossil Sample]] / [[Terrifying Sample]]

In some cases, keeping **Terrifying Sample** in your inventory will cause this Space Encounter to stalk you / keep occurring; pulling you out of pulse drive without the usual warning.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any [[star system]] and have a low frequency of appearance.
