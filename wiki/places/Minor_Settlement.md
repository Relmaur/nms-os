# Minor Settlement

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Inhabited Outpost
- **Type:** Trade Outpost
- **Added in:** Origins
- **Description:** Trade Outpost

**Minor Settlement** is a point of interest.

## Summary

**Minor Settlements** are one of the various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

There are two flavors: Regular and Abandoned.

## Minor Settlement

A type of habitable outpost with a single physical [[Landing Pad]] which can be reliably located with a [[Beacon (Building)|Beacon]].

### Features

These inhabited structures are equipped with several items of interest:

- A case holding a [[Multi-tool]] for sale
- A lifeform which may send the player on a mission
- An object which, when interacted with, grants either [[Navigation Data]] or [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]]
- A [[Technology Merchant]] which also sells goods not sold by other merchants (such as [[Antimatter Housing|Antimatter Housings]] and [[Drop Pod Coordinate Data]])
- A [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] which, like terminals at [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]], offers superior prices for [[Tradeable|marketed tradeables]] compared to terminals on [[space station|space stations]]

Find Missing Person missions from the [[Mission Board]] will eventually direct the player toward a Minor Settlement for completion of the mission's objective.

## Abandoned Settlements

Abandoned Minor Settlements can be found on planets in [[Abandoned system|Abandoned systems]], or on abandoned planets in otherwise inhabited systems.

### Features

These uninhabited, infested structures may be equipped with several items of interest:
- Two rooms instead of one
- [[Landing Pad]]
- Corrupted terminal
- Working Galactic trade terminal
- Case holding a Multi-tool for sale
- [[Damaged Machinery]]
- [[Whispering Egg|Whispering Eggs]] outside
- Two shelter buildings
- No NPCs
