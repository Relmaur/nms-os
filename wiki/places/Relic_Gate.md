# Relic Gate

## Quick Facts

- **System types:** Any
- **Frequency:** 1
- **Interaction:** Fly through
- **Rewards:** Long-distance travel
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Relic Gate** is a space encounter.

## Summary

A **Relic Gate** is a [[Space Encounter|space encounter]] that can be encountered in [[No Man's Sky]]. Flying through the Relic Gate will safely transport the player's ship to a random distant star somewhere in the player's galaxy.

## Appearance

A relic gate has a similar visual center as a [[Base Teleport Module]] but much larger, and with four large rocks suspending it in space.

## Location

They can be randomly found in any star system and have a very low frequency of appearance.
