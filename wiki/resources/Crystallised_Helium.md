# Crystallised Helium

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Localised Earth Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Symbol:** He
- **Value:** 82
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Crystallised Helium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Crystallised Helium** (**He**) is a [[resource]].

## Game description

A supercooled chemical extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from smaller mineral formations after inspection with an Analysis Visor.

Extracted from deposits formed in the hostile conditions of gas giants.

## Source

It can be found on planets orbiting [[System colours|purple stars]], specifically on gas giants.

- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Lithium]] + 1 × [[Quartzite]] → 1 × Crystallised Helium
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.18 s per unit: 1 × [[Lithium]] + 1 × [[Activated Quartzite]] → 2 × Crystallised Helium

## Additional Information

Is used for upgrading certain settlement buildings.
