## Summary

**Corrupted Ichor** (**∞**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[special elements]].

## Game Description

When swarmer shells are completely obliterated, this syrupy metal remains. It has a cloying, coppery smell that clings to the throat.

Pure corrupted ichor is always flowing, sluggishly folding its molecules over and over.

## Source

Corrupted Ichor can be acquired in a variety of ways:
- Dismantling swarmer wrecks on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]] and destroying their guardians
- Excavating buried [[Swarm Debris]] on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]] with [[Salvageable Scrap]]
- Destroying swarms in space during [[Expedition 22: Swarm]]
