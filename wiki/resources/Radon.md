# Radon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Compressed Atmospheric Gas
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Crafting, Charging
- **Symbol:** Rn
- **Value:** 20.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Radon** is a resource.

## Summary

**Radon** (**Rn**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

A colourless, odourless gas with a relatively short half-life.

Found in both [[Biome - Irradiated|Radioactive]] and [[Biome - Frozen|Frozen]] atmospheres, this gas is produced by the decay of other high-energy elements.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[flora]].
- From [[Mutant Plant|Mutant Plants]] in Irradiated biomes.
- From [[Atmosphere Harvester|Atmosphere Harvesters]] built on planets with [[Biome - Irradiated|Irradiated]] or [[Biome - Frozen|Frozen]] biomes.
- Using [[Gas Extractor]] at a Radon [[Concentrated Gas Cloud]].
- It can be found at [[Resource Depot|Resource Depots]].

- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 3 × [[Sulphurine]] → 1 × Radon
- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sulphurine]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Radon
- **Refiner** · *Gas Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sulphurine]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Radon
