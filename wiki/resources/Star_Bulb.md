# Star Bulb

**Star Bulb** is a resource.

## Summary

**Star Bulb** (**Sb**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Harvested Agricultural Substance]] [[flora elements]].

## Game Description

A beautiful gemstone, this prized product is the engorged, luminescent berry of the [[Star Bramble]].

The host plant requires [[Biome - Lush|lush]] conditions to thrive in the wild but can be farmed in all environments with the aid of a [[Hydroponic Tray]].

## Source

This element can be harvested from wild plants, but requires the use of [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]].

It can also be found mixed with [[carbon]] in carbon-based plants or trees. For easy discovery, scan the surrounding flora with the [[Analysis Visor]].

Please note that the spawn rate for the "classic" star bulb plants is really low and is easier to find the element mixed with other flora found on lush planets.

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Star Bulbs can also be grown as a product of Star Bramble with a harvest time of approximately 4 hours and yielding 25 Star Bulbs.

- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Star Bulb]] + 1 × [[Paraffinium]] → 2 × Star Bulb
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Paraffinium]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Star Bulb
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Paraffinium]] + 1 × [[Nitrogen]] → 1 × Star Bulb
- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Paraffinium]] + 1 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Star Bulb
