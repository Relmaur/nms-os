# Oxygen

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fuel
- **Type:** Concentrated Liquid Fuel
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** O2
- **Value:** 34.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Oxygen** is a resource.

## Summary

**Oxygen** (**O2**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[fuel elements]].

## Game description

Integral to the atmospheric filtering technology at the heart of the [[exosuit]] [[Life Support|life support]] system.

Exosuit material processing allows oxygen to be harvested directly from appropriate planetary flora.

Other plantlife may be scanned with the [[Analysis Visor]] to reveal alternative oxygen supplies.

## Source

- Can be harvested from red plants in the wild. (These red plants are what used to be [[Thamium9]])
- Can be found as a secondary element in some [[flora]].
- Can be found as a primary element in [[Underground Toxic Hazard]].
- Can always be bought from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] or from pilot NPCs on [[Space Station|Space Stations]] (only on "Goods Availability->Abundant" setting).
- Obtained from an [[Oxygen Harvester]].
- Obtained from an [[Atmosphere Harvester]] on exotic planets.
- Using [[Gas Extractor]] at an Oxygen [[Concentrated Gas Cloud]].

- **Refiner** · *Extract Micro-Bubbles* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] → 1 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Release Captured Oxygen* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Carbon]] → 2 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Release Captured Oxygen* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 2 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Fungal Mould]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Cactus Flesh]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Solanium]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Gamma Root]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen
- **Refiner** · *Artificial Photosynthesis* · 3.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Star Bulb]] + 1 × [[Kelp Sac]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 10 × Oxygen

## Additional information

- Other than not being a power source, Oxygen has largely replaced [[Thamium9]] in game requirements.
- Oxygen rich plants can be found in at least two kind of types. In larger groups or separate. Same as with [[Sodium]] rich plants.
