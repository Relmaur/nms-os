# Heridium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Silicate
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Value:** 27.5
- **Symbol:** Hr
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Heridium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Heridium** (**Hr**) is a flammable member of the [[Silicate elements]] [[resource]] family.

## Game description

Element used in the creation of components vital to space exploration.

Found in planetary terrain. Often highlighted by holographic cubes in user's visor after local scans.

Use [[Signal Booster]] to locate large resource deposits.

## Mining

This element is found in planetary terrain and [[cave|caves]]. Often highlighted by holographic cubes in user's visor after local scans. Their normal spawn appearance is a tall square column jutting out of the ground. In rare cases extremely larger deposits can be found as frozen "lakes".

## Use

Heridium is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Selling Pre-release

Like other resources, Heridium can be traded for [[units]], as seen on [No Man's Sky - Gameplay Trailer | PS4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nmwG6Sj1Yfg). In the trailer the player can be seen landing their ship on a platform on the planet [[Achaia]], exiting their ship, and shortly after the notification "-3.2 HERIDIUM" appears on screen. Moments later the notification "+1357 UNITS" appears on screen, indicating that the resource has been exchanged for currency. It is likely that the player visited a [[Galactic trade terminal]] to make this transaction.

## Additional information

- It is possible to salvage Heridium from destroyed [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].
- Can be bought at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- It can be given to a [[Korvax]] NPC in exchange to learn a word in the [[Korvax (language)|Korvax language]].
- It is also the only [[Silicate elements|Silicate element]] found in every single [[planet]] and [[moon]].
