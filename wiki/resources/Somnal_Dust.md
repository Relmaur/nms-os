# Somnal Dust

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Decayed Spacetime Remnant
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Value:** 1,616.0
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Somnal Dust** is a resource.

## Summary

**Somnal Dust** is a [[resource]] and one of the [[special elements]].

## Game description

Grey, dead, sparkling with life and colour, singing as it shimmers. This impossibly fine dust is the accumulated decay of time and memories.

Collected from the fine layers it forms around hotspots in the loop. Of great value to [[Specialist Polo]] as they research the loop.

## Source

Can be found on [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]] and are used in Expedition 7: Leviathan.

Can be gathered on any derelict freighter via using Q to bash-mine the onboard pustules.

### Use

Somnal Dust is used in Expedition Seven: Leviathan in the **Loop Research** mission.

Other than this, it appears to have little purpose beyond its value.
