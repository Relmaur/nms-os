# Runaway Mould

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Junk
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Value:** 20.0
- **Used for:** Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Jn
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Runaway Mould** is a resource.

## Summary

**Runaway Mould** (**Jn**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[junk]] [[special elements]].

The [[British spelling]] of the word is used in the wiki.

## Game Description

An out of control mould growth that builds up in poorly-maintained machinery.

Likely to be several different species of mould, merged together into one super-colony.

## Source

- This item can be received as a gift from the Guild representatives in space stations.
- Runaway Mould can be purchased and sold at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and NPCs for a profit.
- This item can be received from encrypted missions at the [[Nexus]].
- This item can also be obtained from [[Curious Deposit|Curious Deposits]] on some planets, appearing as a sphere that rolls when mined.

- **Refiner** · *Recycle Waste Materials* · 0.6 s per unit: 1 × [[Living Slime]] → 1 × Runaway Mould
- **Refiner** · *Reality Pollution* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 3 × Runaway Mould

## Use

Through multiple steps of refining, [[Residual Goop]] can be converted to [[Viscous Fluids]], to [[Living Slime]], to [[Runaway Mould]] and then to [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]].

## Additional Information

- Junk type resources have the same symbol (Jn) rather than their own.
- It has the same icon as [[Containment Failure]]
