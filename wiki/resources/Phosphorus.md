# Phosphorus

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Localised Earth Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** P
- **Value:** 62.0
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Phosphorus** is a resource.

## Summary

**Phosphorus** (**P**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Localised Earth Element|localised]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common minerals after inspection with an [[Analysis Visor]].

Typically found on planets with a [[Biome - Scorched|scorched]] environment.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] or in [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] or [[Hotspot|Hotspots]] on planets with Scorched [[biome|biomes]].
- Found in Hotspots on Mega Exotic planets located in [[System colours|red]] stellar systems.
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Recycling Explosive [[Industrial Waste]] at a [[Waste Processing Plant]].

- **Refiner** · *Floral Titration* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Solanium]] + 1 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Phosphorus
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Phosphorus
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Phosphorus
- **Refiner** · *Organic Fusion* · 0.16 s per unit: 1 × [[Methane]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Phosphorus

## Trivia

- In real life, Phosphorus is almost never found freely in nature; except for minerals such as apatite. Phosphorus is found organically in sources of bone ash and guano and is also produced in supernovae as a byproduct of supernova nucleosynthesis, where the phosphorus-to-iron ratio from the supernova remnant is believed to be 100 times higher than Milky Way supernovae byproducts in general.
