# Rubeum

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Neutral/Exotic
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Symbol:** Ru
- **Value:** 288.8
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Rubeum** is a resouce.

## Summary

**Rubeum (Ru)** is a [[resource]], and a member of the [[Exotic elements (Atlas)|Exotic elements]] class.

This element is found only in [[System colours|Red Star systems]], and commonly located in large terrain piles or rimming circular openings in [[cave]] roofs.

If present, the element will show on the results of a Planet Scan.

## Source

As described in the Summary, this element can be found on [[planet|planets]] located in Red Star systems. The Red Star systems will be shown on the Warp Map with a K# or M# classification. Rubeum is mined using the [[Multi-tool]] and is commonly found as a bright red surface mound (similar morphology to [[gold]], [[emeril]], etc.) or as a coating rimming circular surface openings within cave roofs.

## Use

Rubeum is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

- Rubeum can be sold in the [[Galactic trade terminal|Marketplace]], and is a good source for acquiring [[unit|units]].
