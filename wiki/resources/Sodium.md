# Sodium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Catalyst
- **Type:** Unrefined Catalytic Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Value:** 41.0
- **Symbol:** Na
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Sodium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Sodium** (**Na**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[catalyst elements]].

## Game description

A powerful catalyst, useful for charging defensive technologies such as shields or [[Hazard Protection|hazard protection]].

[[Exosuit]] material handling technology allows automatic harvesting from appropriate sodium-rich flora.

A vital ingredient in the creation of many advanced devices, products and components.

## Source

- From yellow flowers visible on the [[Scanner]].
- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]].
- As a primary element in [[Underground Toxic Hazard]].
- In [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]].
- Can always be bought from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] or from pilot NPCs on [[Space Station|Space Stations]] (only on "Goods Availability->Abundant" setting).

- **Refiner** · *Free Sodium* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 2 × Sodium
- **Refiner** · *Extract Organic Sodium* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] → 1 × Sodium
- **Refiner** · *Extract Organic Sodium* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] + 1 × [[Carbon]] → 2 × Sodium
- **Refiner** · *Extract Organic Sodium* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 2 × Sodium

## Additional information

- A [[Portable Refiner]] can be used to change Sodium into [[Sodium Nitrate]]
- Note that a unit of [[Sodium Nitrate]] is six times as good at charging technologies and requires only two sodium to produce. This makes it both more storage and energy efficient.
- The flower models from which Sodium is harvested used to be sources of [[Zinc]] before the NEXT update.
- Sodium-rich plants can be found by themselves or, rarely, in large groups. Same as with [[Oxygen]]-rich plants.
