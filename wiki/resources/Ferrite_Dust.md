# Ferrite Dust

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Metal
- **Type:** Metallic Mineral Extract
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Fe
- **Value:** 14
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Ferrite Dust** is a resource.

## Summary

**Ferrite Dust** (**Fe**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[metal elements]]. It is obtainable on the surface of planets, from various rocks.

## Game description

An abundant mineral element, found in rock formations and other areas of geological interest. Easily extracted using a [[Mining Beam|Mining Laser]].

Typically requires processing in a Refiner before use in construction or technology manufacture.

## Source

- As a primary element in most [[mineral|minerals]].
- From any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Trivia

In real-world terms, Ferrite Dust is analogous to rock dust, primarily consisting of iron with trace amounts of zinc, copper and other minerals.

## Additional Information

- It is possible to also collect ferrite dust by using a [[Starship]] weapon. Since a starship [[Starship Weapons technology|weapon]] has a high power capability normally used for mining [[asteroid|asteroids]], the player can alternatively mine large source of ferrite dust and other basic secondary raw materials ([[Di-hydrogen]], [[oxygen]], [[sodium]], etc.) on a planet surface by flying low and strafing it with a sufficiently powerful starship weapon. Note the starship weapon is incapable of 'mining' big rocks which require the [[Advanced Mining Laser]].  Due to the larger damage capacity of starship weaponry, it is capable of quickly stripping and mining both organic and inorganic material much quicker as compared with using mining laser. In a special case, the Sentinel interceptors perform even better due to their capability to hover in place.
