# Ferrite Dust

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Metal
- **Type:** Metallic Mineral Extract
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Blueprint Value:** 14
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** **Fe**
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Ferrite Dust** is a resource.

## Summary

**Ferrite Dust** (**Fe**) is a resource and one of the metal elements. It is obtainable on the surface of planets, from various rocks.

## Game description

An abundant mineral element, found in rock formations and other areas of geological interest. Easily extracted using a Mining Laser.

Typically requires processing in a Refiner before use in construction or technology manufacture.

## Source

- As a primary element in most minerals.
- From any Galactic Trade Terminal.

Ferrite Dust can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Ammonia ×1 → Ferrite Dust ×1 (*"Ferrite Dust Extraction"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Dioxite ×1 → Ferrite Dust ×1 (*"Ferrite Dust Extraction"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)

## Trivia

In real-world terms, Ferrite Dust is analogous to rock dust, primarily consisting of iron with trace amounts of zinc, copper and other minerals.

## Additional Information

- It is possible to also collect ferrite dust by using a Starship weapon. Since a starship weapon has a high power capability normally used for mining asteroids, the player can alternatively mine large source of ferrite dust and other basic secondary raw materials (Di-hydrogen, oxygen, sodium, etc.) on a planet surface by flying low and strafing it with a sufficiently powerful starship weapon. Note the starship weapon is incapable of 'mining' big rocks which require the Advanced Mining Laser. Due to the larger damage capacity of starship weaponry, it is capable of quickly stripping and mining both organic and inorganic material much quicker as compared with using mining laser. In a special case, the Sentinel interceptors perform even better due to their capability to hover in place.
