# Pure Ferrite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Metal
- **Type:** Processed Metallic Minerals
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Fe+
- **Value:** 28
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Pure Ferrite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Pure Ferrite** (**Fe+**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[metal elements]].

## Game description

Processed metal, free of dust and pebbles. Used to construct a wide variety of structures and technologies.

Typically purified from Ferrite dust using a Refiner, but can occasionally be found in large deposits. Collection may require an [[Advanced Mining Laser]].

## Source

- As a primary or secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]]. These minerals usually require Advanced Mining Laser to harvest.
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Refiner** · *Matter Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 3 × Pure Ferrite
- **Refiner** · *Demagnetise Metal* · 0.16 s per unit: 1 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 2 × Pure Ferrite
- **Refiner** · *Extract Metallic Elements* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Pure Ferrite
- **Refiner** · *Recycle Stone* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Polished Stone]] → 2 × Pure Ferrite

## Repair

Pure Ferrite can be used for repairing damaged [[Launch Thruster|Launch Thrusters]].
