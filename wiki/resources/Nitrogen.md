# Nitrogen

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Compressed Atmospheric Gas
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Crafting
- **Symbol:** N
- **Value:** 20
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Nitrogen** is a resource.

## Summary

**Nitrogen** (**N**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

A reactive atmospheric gas found wherever there is organic life.

Nitrogen is readily harvested from the atmospheres of [[Biome - Toxic|Toxic]] and [[Biome - Lush|Lush]] planets.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[flora]].
- From [[Atmosphere Harvester|Atmosphere Harvesters]] built on planets with Lush and Toxic [[biome|biomes]].
- Using [[Gas Extractor]] at a Nitrogen [[Concentrated Gas Cloud]] on Toxic or Lush biomes.
- It can be found at [[Resource Depot|Resource Depots]].
- From [[Stellar Extractor Room]] while being in solar systems with Red/Orange stars.

- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 3 × [[Radon]] → 1 × Nitrogen
- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Radon]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Nitrogen
- **Refiner** · *Gas Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Radon]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Nitrogen
- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 3 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Nitrogen
- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Methane]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Nitrogen
- **Refiner** · *Gas Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Methane]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Nitrogen
