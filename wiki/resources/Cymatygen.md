## Summary

**Cymatygen (Cy)** is a [[resource]], and a member of the [[Exotic elements (Atlas)|Exotic elements]] class.

It is a sky-blue element found only in [[System colours|Blue Star systems]], and commonly located in large terrain piles.

## Source

As described in the Summary, this element can be found on [[planet|planets]] located in Blue Star systems. Cymatygen is usually found in large deposits reaching deep into the ground.  Found in O class systems.
