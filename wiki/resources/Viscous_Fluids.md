# Viscous Fluids

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Junk
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Blueprint Value:** 20.0
- **Used for:** Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** **Jn**
- **Updated:** Origins

**Viscous Fluids** is a resource.

## Summary

**Viscous Fluids** (**Jn**) is a resource and one of the junk special elements.

## Game description

An unnaturally thick liquid of dubious colour. Its origins are impossible to fathom.

Appears to have some mild magnetic properties.

## Source

Viscous Fluids can be purchased and sold at Galactic Trade Terminals and NPCs for a profit.
Viscous Fluid frequently appears as a removed item to repair Damaged Machinery.

Viscous Fluids can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Residual Goop ×1 → Viscous Fluids ×1 (*"Recycle Waste Materials"*, 0.6 sec./unit output)

## Use

Through multiple steps of refining, Residual Goop can be converted to Viscous Fluids, to Living Slime, to Runaway Mould and then to Nanite Cluster.

Viscous Fluids can also be used to charge the Pulsing Heart of a Living Ship (Starship).

### Crafting

**Viscous Fluids** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Viscous Fluids** is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

- Living Slime -- Viscous Fluids x1 → Living Slime x1 (*"Recycle Waste Materials"*, 0.6 sec./unit output)

### Cooking

**Viscous Fluids** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

- Junk type resources have the same symbol (Jn) rather than their own.
