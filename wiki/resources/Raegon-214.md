# Raegon-214

## Quick Facts

- **Description:** Fuel for ion engines
- **Symbol:** Rg
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Raegon-214** (**Rg**) is a resource.

## Summary

**Raegon-214** (**Rg**) is a [[resource]]. It is only available in [[pre-release]] builds of [[No Man's Sky]].

## Game description

Standard high-yield fuel for small to medium spacefaring ion engines. Raegon sublimates violently with little provocation and should be handled with caution.

## Additional information

The information about Raegon was first shown during the **No Man's Sky - 18 Minute Gameplay Demo** released on July 6, 2015. A purple icon for this element is displayed when scavenging space debris.

Based on its description, Raegon-214 may have been intended as fuel for the [[starship]]'s [[Pulse Engine]], before being replaced with [[Thamium9]] for the final release.
