# Aluminium

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Neutral
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Symbol:** Al
- **Value:** 165
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Aluminum** is a resource.

## Summary

**Aluminium (Al)** is a [[resource]], and a member of the [[Neutral elements]] class.

The [[British spelling]] of this word is used in the wiki.

## Game description

A hugely useful, ductile and versatile metal.

A highly desired element in trading markets and cargo manifests throughout the known galaxy.

Can be combined with other elements to create valuable [[alloy|alloys]] if [[Blueprint|recipe]] is available.

## Source

Aluminium can be mined from nodes, crystals, and monoliths on [[planet|planets]] as well as [[asteroid|asteroids]].
- node form appears metallic-white and is smaller and wider than a [[Heridium]] node with a rounded top (shaped like a half-disc)
- crystal form is green in colour
- monolith form are column-shaped with a square facing
- sub-surface, spotted via two adjacent holes with light-blue metallic edges. The node is a U-shaped tube.

Nodes are sub-surface and the cores are hollow. They appear as small vertical caverns, and maintain their white/silvery appearance. Nodes can be located when flying above the surface, as long as you are flying at a low to medium height, and slowly.

## Use

Aluminium is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

- Aluminium can be sold in the [[Galactic trade terminal|Marketplace]].

- In rare cases, gigantic monoliths of aluminium can be found on planets with [[Sentinels]] under threatening behaviour. The monoliths have an above-ground height 1.5x greater than a typical [[Heridium]] column, a square face, and an endless depth. The core is aluminum but cannot be mined (it will remain reflective). Mining this monolith yields a tremendous amount of Aluminium. In one specific case, more than 3000 units of Aluminium were harvested.

- When mining sub-surface aluminium, the nodes will bottom out, often after yielding 3000-5000 units of Aluminium..

## Trivia

This element is also called Aluminum in the US English localisation.
