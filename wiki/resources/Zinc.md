# Zinc

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Oxide
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Value:** 41.3
- **Symbol:** Zn
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Zinc** is a resource.

## Summary

**Zinc** (**Zn**) is an uncommon metal and a member of the [[Oxide elements]] class.

## Game description

Powerful element used to recharge defensive technologies, and to create and maintain many components and devices.

Found in yellow flowering vegetation and planetary flora.

## Sources

- Found in yellow flowering vegetation and planetary flora.
- Acquired via the [[Dismantle|dismantling]] of various technologies.
- Bought at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- Salvaged from destroyed [[Sentinel]] drones.

## Use

Zinc is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

When looking for Zinc on a planetary surface it is useful to scan the surroundings (PC: <code>C</code> key) and look for yellow shield-like icons. Zinc is especially important as an ingredient in crafting Antimatter, which in turn is a component of [[Warp Cell|Warp Cells]].
