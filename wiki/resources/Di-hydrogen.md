# Di-hydrogen

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** High Energy Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Blueprint Value:** 34.0
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** **H**
- **Updated:** Orbital

**Di-hydrogen** is a resource.

## Summary

**Di-hydrogen** (**H**) is a resource and one of the earth elements. It is a main ingredient required to craft fuel for both starships and frigates.

## Game description

A stable, crystalline form of hydrogen remarkable both for its extreme energy density and its stability at atmospheric pressures.

Common to all worlds, Di-hydrogen is found in the form of large blue crystals. It is a key component of Starship Launch Fuel.

## Source

Harvesting Di-hydrogen crystals

- Found as blue crystal minerals on the surface of every planet. The largest crystals require an Advanced Mining Laser to be mined. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a blue (H) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the Scanner.
- Found as a secondary element in some individual minerals (must be identified with the Analysis Visor first).
- Also available for purchase from Galactic Trade Terminals in pirate controlled systems.

Di-hydrogen can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Di-hydrogen Jelly ×1 → Di-hydrogen ×40 (*"Di-hydrogen Cycling"*, 4 sec./unit output)
- Tritium ×5 → Di-hydrogen ×1 (*"Tritium Cycling"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)

### Alternative Recommendation

Some Galactic Trade Terminals found on Space Stations, Trading Posts and Colossal Archives will sell Di-hydrogen Jelly in bulk. Unlike the method previously described, refining Di-hydrogen directly from sourced Di-hydrogen Jelly is simpler, faster and results in a higher yield. One stack of Di-hydrogen Jelly (20) can be refined into 800 Di-hydrogen in 80 seconds. Using multiple refiners can speed up this process considerably.

Provided you can locate/make note of any terminals selling Di-hydrogen Jelly (and have sufficient units), this method is both renewable and the fastest method to acquire Di-Hydrogen in bulk.

### Crafting

**Di-hydrogen** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Di-hydrogen Jelly -- Di-hydrogen x40 → Di-hydrogen Jelly
- Exocraft Specialist's Room -- Silver x50 + Di-hydrogen x25 + Chromatic Metal x20 → Exocraft Specialist's Room
- Exocraft Terminal -- Chromatic Metal x40 + Di-hydrogen x25 → Exocraft Terminal
- Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes) -- Di-hydrogen x100 + Tritium x100 → Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes)
- Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes) -- Di-hydrogen x200 + Tritium x200 → Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)
- Frigate Fuel (50 Tonnes) -- Di-hydrogen x50 + Tritium x50 → Frigate Fuel (50 Tonnes)
- Hydraulic Wiring -- Carbon Nanotubes x2 + Salt x20 + Di-hydrogen x40 → Hydraulic Wiring
- Minotaur Laser -- Chromatic Metal x80 + Di-hydrogen x65 + Wiring Loom x1 → Minotaur Laser
- Resonance Amplifier -- Di-hydrogen x160 + Gravitino Ball x3 + Radiant Shard x1 → Resonance Amplifier
- Spicy Chum -- Mordite x80 + Phosphorus x25 + Di-hydrogen x90 → Spicy Chum x30
- Starship Launch Fuel -- Di-hydrogen x40 + Metal Plating x1 → Starship Launch Fuel
- Trade Rocket -- Wiring Loom x2 + Tritium x120 + Di-hydrogen x90 → Trade Rocket

### Refining

**Di-hydrogen** is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

- Ammonia -- Nitrogen x1 + Di-hydrogen x1 → Ammonia x1 (*"Organic Fusion"*, 0.16 sec./unit output)
- Condensed Carbon -- Uranium x1 + Di-hydrogen x1 → Condensed Carbon x2 (*"Harness Energy"*, 0.04 sec./unit output)
- Deuterium -- Di-hydrogen x1 + Silicate Powder x1 → Deuterium x1 (*"Di-hydrogen Cycling"*, 0.24 sec./unit output)
- Deuterium -- Di-hydrogen x1 + Tritium x1 → Deuterium x1 (*"Di-hydrogen Cycling"*, 0.24 sec./unit output)
- Di-hydrogen Jelly -- Di-hydrogen x30 → Di-hydrogen Jelly x1 (*"Condense Di-hydrogen"*, 60 sec./unit output)
- Faecium -- Di-hydrogen x1 + Carbon x1 → Faecium x1 (*"Assisted Decomposition"*, 0.08 sec./unit output)
- Life Support Gel -- Oxygen x10 + Condensed Carbon x10 + Di-hydrogen x10 → Life Support Gel x1 (*"Advanced Oxygen Capture"*, 45 sec./unit output)

- Phosphorus -- Methane x1 + Di-hydrogen x1 → Phosphorus x1 (*"Organic Fusion"*, 0.16 sec./unit output)
- Salt -- Di-hydrogen x1 + Oxygen x1 → Salt x1 (*"Rapid Formation / Evaporation"*, 0.08 sec./unit output)
- Solanium -- Di-hydrogen x1 + Sulphurine x1 → Solanium x1 (*"Sulphur Injection"*, 0.16 sec./unit output)

- Uranium -- Radon x1 + Di-hydrogen x1 → Uranium x1 (*"Catalyse Radiation"*, 0.16 sec./unit output)

### Cooking

**Di-hydrogen** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

- Blue crystals used to be a source of Chrysonite before the NEXT update.
- Prior to the Fractal update, you could also gain Di-hydrogen by crafting Di-hydrogen Jelly, then refining it back to Di-hydrogen. This method yielded 10 Di-hydrogen per cycle.
