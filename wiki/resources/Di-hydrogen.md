# Di-hydrogen

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** High Energy Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** H
- **Value:** 34.0
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Di-hydrogen** is a resource.

## Summary

**Di-hydrogen** (**H**) is a  [[resource]] and one of the [[earth elements]]. It is a main ingredient required to craft fuel for both [[starship|starships]] and [[frigate|frigates]].

## Game description

A stable, crystalline form of hydrogen remarkable both for its extreme energy density and its stability at atmospheric pressures.

Common to all worlds, Di-hydrogen is found in the form of large blue crystals. It is a key component of [[Starship Launch Fuel]].

## Source

- Found as blue [[Mineral|crystal minerals]] on the surface of every planet. The largest crystals require an Advanced Mining Laser to be mined. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a blue (H) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the Scanner.<br>

- Found as a secondary element in some individual minerals (must be identified with the [[Analysis Visor]] first).<br>

- Also available for purchase from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in pirate controlled systems.

- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Cycling* · 4 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] → 40 × Di-hydrogen
- **Refiner** · *Tritium Cycling* · 0.36 s per unit: 5 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Di-hydrogen

### Alternative Recommendation

Some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] found on [[Space Station|Space Stations]], [[Trade Outpost|Trading Posts]] and [[Colossal Archive|Colossal Archives]] will sell Di-hydrogen Jelly in bulk. Unlike the method previously described, refining Di-hydrogen directly from sourced Di-hydrogen Jelly is simpler, faster and results in a higher yield. One stack of Di-hydrogen Jelly (20) can be refined into 800 Di-hydrogen in 80 seconds.  Using multiple refiners can speed up this process considerably.

Provided you can locate/make note of any terminals selling Di-hydrogen Jelly (and have sufficient units), this method is both renewable and the fastest method to acquire Di-Hydrogen in bulk.

## Additional Information

- Blue crystals used to be a source of [[Chrysonite]] before the NEXT update.
- Prior to the Fractal update, you could also gain Di-hydrogen by crafting Di-hydrogen Jelly, then refining it back to Di-hydrogen. This method yielded 10 Di-hydrogen per cycle.
