# Radnox

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Precious/Exotic
- **Rarity:** Very Rare
- **Value:** 303.0
- **Symbol:** Ra
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Radnox** is a resource.

## Summary

**Radnox (Ra)** is a Very Rare [[resource]], and a member of the Precious/[[Exotic elements (Atlas)|Exotic elements]] class.

## Game description

Mysterious and valuable chemical resource.

## Source

Found in green rock-like deposits on the surface of extreme [[planet|planets]] or in floating creature-like deposits (see pictures).

One deposit usually contains 2-5 Radnox. Can be mined with a standard mining laser

## Use

Radnox is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

Spawns on extreme planets.
