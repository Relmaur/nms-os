# Ammonia

- **Symbol:** NH3

**Ammonia** is a resource.

## Summary

**Ammonia** (**NH3**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Localised Earth Element|localised]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common materials after inspection with an [[Analysis Visor]].

Typically found on planets with a [[Biome - Toxic|toxic]] environment.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] or [[Hotspot|Hotspots]] on planets with Toxic [[biome|biomes]].
- Found in Hotspots on Mega Exotic planets located in green stellar systems.
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Recycling Toxic [[Industrial Waste]] at a [[Waste Processing Plant]].

- **Refiner** · *Floral Titration* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Fungal Mould]] + 1 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Ammonia
- **Refiner** · *Organic Fusion* · 0.16 s per unit: 1 × [[Nitrogen]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Ammonia
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 2 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Ammonia
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Pure Ferrite]] + 2 × [[Paraffinium]] → 1 × Ammonia
