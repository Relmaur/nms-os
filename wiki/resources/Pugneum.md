# Pugneum

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Anomalous Material
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Symbol:** Pg
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Value:** 138.0
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Pugneum** is a resource.

## Summary

**Pugneum** (**Pg**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[special elements]].

## Game description

This oily, unsettling liquid leaks from the circuitry of [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

Prized by the [[Vy'keen]], Pugneum is thought to share some essential properties with [[Nanite Cluster|Nanites]]. It hums in a sinister fashion upon contact with organic matter.

## Source

Drops from destroyed Sentinels; the received amount depends on what type of sentinel is destroyed, see more information [[Sentinel|here]].

Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Refiner** · *Restore Harmony* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Atlantideum]] → 2 × Pugneum

## Additional information

- Pugneum can be sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- Pugneum can be requested for a bribe in the rank 4 find missing persons mission from the [[Mission Board]]
- Needed to open cargo containers on crashed freighters.
- Needed in small amounts (10-25 per component) to repair [[Damaged Component|damaged components]] in crashed starships and damaged multi-tools. (Example: [[Scorched Plating]])
