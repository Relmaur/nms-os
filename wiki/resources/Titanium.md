# Titanium

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Oxide
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Symbol:** Ti
- **Value:** 61.9
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Titanium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Titanium** (**Ti**) is a member of the [[Oxide elements]] class.

## Game description

Extremely powerful oxide that can be used to recharge defensive technologies.

A vital ingredient in the creation of many advanced products and components.

Found in yellow crystal clusters during planetary exploration.

## Source

Titanium crystals can most commonly be found on hot [[planet|planets]], as well as on lush planets with normal temperature. The [[Advanced Mining Laser]] is required for mining.

It can also be obtained from destroyed [[sentinel|sentinels]].

## Use

Titanium is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.
