# Hexite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Unrefined Organic Element
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Symbol:** X
- **Value:** 654.0
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Hexite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Hexite** (**X**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[special elements]].

## Game description

An odourless, mineral-rich substance with a hexagonal crystalline structure, harvested from the faecal matter of a creature.

## Source

Hexite can be acquired in a variety of ways:
- During the Starbirth mission, a set amount of Hexite is awarded at a nearby (marked) [[Crashed Freighter|crashed freighter]], for use in creating the [[Consciousness Bridge]].
- Rewarded from [[Guild Envoy|Guild Envoys]] of the [[Merchants Guild]].
- Can be purchased directly from vendors at some [[Outlaw Station|Outlaw Stations]].
- Awarded from [[The Nexus|Nexus]] missions (3.5%).
- Can be obtained at a [[Multi-Tool Decommissioning Terminal]] at the [[Space Anomaly]] when the player chooses to scrap an Atlantidean Multi-Tool for [[Void Mote|Void Motes]].

## Additional information

- It was possible to get 12-18 pieces of Hexite as a reward for picking up one [[Coprite|Faecium]] Crystal during the [[Community Research (week 2)]] mission.
