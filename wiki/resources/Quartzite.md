## Summary

**Quartzite** (**Qu**) is a [[resource]].

## Game description

A chromatic mineral, generated by fusion in the heart of a star.
Such stellar material ends up forming deposits in the crust of local planets.

Quartzite is found on planets orbiting purple stars, and can be placed in a Refiner to create purified Chromatic Metal for use in the manufacture of advanced technologies.

## Source

It can be found on planets orbiting [[System colours|purple stars]], where it can be mined with the Terrain Manipulator attachment for the [[Multi-tool]].

- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Alchemy* · 0.1 s per unit: 1 × [[Activated Quartzite]] + 1 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Quartzite
- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Expansion* · 0.18 s per unit: 1 × [[Quartzite]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 2 × Quartzite
