# Faecium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Flora
- **Type:** Harvested Agricultural Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Upgrading, Cooking
- **Symbol:** Fa
- **Value:** 30
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Faecium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Faecium** (**Fa**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Harvested Agricultural Substance]] [[flora elements]].

## Game Description

A foul-smelling compound that can be harvested from the [[Gutrot Flower]], or less desirably from the faecal matter of some species.

Creatures can be fed to encourage the formation and deposition of faecium crystals.

## Source

- This resource can be obtained by feeding creatures on [[planet|planets]] and then collecting their dropped dung samples. This process can be rather slow, however.
- With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Faecium can also be grown as a product of the Gutrot Flower, yielding a base product of 25 with a harvest time of 4 hours.
- It can be found on some planets as a by-product of some carbon-based plants much like some gases.
- Sometimes it can be found in resource deposits on [[Biome - Marsh|marsh]] planets.
- It can also be found as junk in some [[Damaged Machinery|damaged machinery]].

- **Refiner** · *Assisted Decomposition* · 0.24 s per unit: 3 × [[Mordite]] → 1 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Assisted Decomposition* · 0.08 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Oxygenate Microbes* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 3 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Nitrogen]] + 1 × [[Sulphurine]] → 1 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Runaway Decomposition* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Mordite]] + 2 × [[Carbon]] → 3 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Runaway Decomposition* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Mordite]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 4 × Faecium
- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Mordite]] + 1 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Faecium

## Additional Information

Base harvest amount for Faecium in a bio-dome is 25.
