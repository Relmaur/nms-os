## Summary

**Contaminated Metal** (**Fe(O)**) is a [[resource]] that can be found in abandoned [[Space Hulk|Space Hulks]].

## Game Description

Torn fragments of old industrial components. Years of decay have left it pocked by exotic oxides and faintly irradiated. This material requires deep processing before it can be recycled into new components.

Salvaged from the creaking wreck of an abandoned space hulk.
