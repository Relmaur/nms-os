# Activated Quartzite

## Quick Facts

- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Activated Quartzite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Activated Quartzite** (**Qu+**) is a [[resource]].

## Game description

A chromatic metal, generated by fusion in the heart of a star.

Quartzite is found on planets orbiting purple stars, and can be placed in a Refiner to create purified Chromatic Metal.

This Quartzite has been activated by the extreme conditions in which it was formed, and is highly valuable.

## Sources

It can be found on planets orbiting [[System colours|purple stars]], **(typically extreme weather planets)** where it can be mined with the Terrain Manipulator attachment for the [[Multi-tool]]. It's easy to spot because of its specific purple color.

- **Refiner** · *Loosen Bonds* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Methane]] + 1 × [[Lithium]] + 1 × [[Quartzite]] → 1 × Activated Quartzite
- **Refiner** · *Loosen Bonds* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Methane]] + 1 × [[Crystallised Helium]] + 1 × [[Quartzite]] → 1 × Activated Quartzite
