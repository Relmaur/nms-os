# Frost Crystal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Flora
- **Type:** Harvested Agricultural Substance
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting
- **Symbol:** Fc
- **Value:** 12
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Frost Crystal** is a resource.

## Summary

**Frost Crystal** (**Fc**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Harvested Agricultural Substance]] [[flora elements]].

## Game Description

A beautiful blue crystal, this organic compound possesses both great hardiness and a shimmering translucence. It is harvested from the leaves of [[Frostwort]].

Frostwort requires [[Biome - Frozen|frozen]] conditions to thrive in the wild, but can be farmed in all environments with the aid of a [[Hydroponics Tray]].

## Source

This element can be harvested from wild plants, but requires the use of [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]].

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Frost Crystals can also be grown as a product of the Frostwort plant, providing a base yield of 50 with a harvest time of approximately 60 minutes.

- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 1 × [[Dioxite]] → 2 × Frost Crystal
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.24 s per unit: 2 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Frost Crystal
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Radon]] → 1 × Frost Crystal
- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Frost Crystal

## Additional Information

- The base yield for Frost Crystal in a bio-dome is 50.
