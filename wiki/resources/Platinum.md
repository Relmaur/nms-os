# Platinum

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exotic
- **Type:** Valuable Asteroid Mineral
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** Pt
- **Value:** 505.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Platinum** is a resource.

## Summary

**Platinum** (**Pt**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[exotic elements]].

Platinum is the most expensive single element.

## Game description

Extremely valuable interstellar trading mineral, typically mined from asteroids. Occasionally found in underground planetary deposits.

Universally prized for its hardiness and versatility.

## Source

- Can be bought from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] or Ship Traders.
- Can be mined from crystalline/amorphous [[asteroid|asteroids]].
- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] in [[cave|caves]].
- By destroying freighter cargo

- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Geodesite]] → 250 × Platinum
- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Iridesite]] → 250 × Platinum
- **Refiner** · *Transmutation* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Silver]] + 1 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Platinum
- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Alchemy* · 4.8 s per unit: 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] + 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 10 × Platinum
