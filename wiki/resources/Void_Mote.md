# Void Mote

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Essence of Atlantid
- **Description:** Used as currency with the Autophage.
- **Category:** Special
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Value:** 380
- **Symbol:** V
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Void Mote** is a resource.

## Summary

**Void Motes** are a [[resource]] and constitute a [[currency]] of exchange with the [[Autophage]].

They are required to purchase unique [[Appearance Modifier]] cosmetics, [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Voltaic Staff]] crafting components, and [[Rebuilt Exosuit Module|Rebuilt Exosuit Modules]] from [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal|Autophage Synthesis Terminals]] and [[Harmonic Interface|Harmonic Interfaces]].

## Game Description

A purified drop of [[Atlantideum]], refined to its very essence. This precious material is acquired by earning favour with and assisting the Autophage.

Visit the Autophage Synthesis Terminal found at all [[Autophage Camp|Autophage Camps]] to exchange Void Motes for unique customisation options.

## Source

- By completing Autophage missions (200-600 Void Motes).
- By scrapping an [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Atlantid|Atlas Multi-Tool]] at the [[Multi-Tool Decommissioning Terminal]] (100-5400 Void Motes).
- Void Motes can occasionally be found in wheelbarrows around [[Harmonic Camp|Harmonic Camps]].
