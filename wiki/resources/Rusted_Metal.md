# Rusted Metal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Junk
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Value:** 20.0
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Symbol:** Jn
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Rusted Metal** is a resource.

## Summary

**Rusted Metal** (**Jn**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[junk]] [[special elements]].

## Game description

Handfuls of corroded metal, distorted by years of exposure to the harsh environment.

Handle with care.

## Source

- Upon opening a Damaged Container, a GUI will pop up with an option to either discard or keep some Rusted Metal. Each container can contain between 50 and 100 Rusted Metal. Once the Rusted Metal is either discarded or moved to your inventory, the Damaged Container will open and you can receive a reward.
- Rusted Metal can be purchased from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and pilot NPCs.
- Rusted Metal is a rare planetary resource to be mined akin to Ferrite and its variants. Seen on [[Biome - Dead|Dead Worlds]].
- May be acquired when recycling metallic [[Industrial Waste]] in the furnace at a [[Waste Processing Plant]].

- **Refiner** · *Oxidise Metal* · 0.4 s per unit: 1 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Rusted Metal
- **Refiner** · *Oxidise Metal* · 0.4 s per unit: 1 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 2 × Rusted Metal

## Progression

The following progression is used for Ferrite refinement via a Portable Refiner:
- Rusted Metal
- [[Ferrite Dust]]
- [[Pure Ferrite]]
- [[Magnetised Ferrite]]
