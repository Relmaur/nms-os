# Rusted Metal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Junk
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Blueprint Value:** 20.0
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Symbol:** **Jn**
- **Updated:** Endurance

**Rusted Metal** is a resource.

## Summary

**Rusted Metal** (**Jn**) is a resource and one of the junk special elements.

## Game description

Handfuls of corroded metal, distorted by years of exposure to the harsh environment.

Handle with care.

## Source

Obtaining Rusted Metal from a Damaged Container

- Upon opening a Damaged Container, a GUI will pop up with an option to either discard or keep some Rusted Metal. Each container can contain between 50 and 100 Rusted Metal. Once the Rusted Metal is either discarded or moved to your inventory, the Damaged Container will open and you can receive a reward.
- Rusted Metal can be purchased from some Galactic Trade Terminals and pilot NPCs.
- Rusted Metal is a rare planetary resource to be mined akin to Ferrite and its variants. Seen on Dead Worlds.
- May be acquired when recycling metallic Industrial Waste in the furnace at a Waste Processing Plant.

Rusted Metal can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Oxygen ×1 + Ferrite Dust ×1 → Rusted Metal ×1 (*"Oxidise Metal"*, 0.4 sec./unit output)
- Oxygen ×1 + Pure Ferrite ×1 → Rusted Metal ×2 (*"Oxidise Metal"*, 0.4 sec./unit output)

## Use

### Crafting

**Rusted Metal** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Rusted Metal** is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

- Ferrite Dust -- Rusted Metal x1 → Ferrite Dust x2 (*"Recycle Waste Materials"*, 0.64 sec./unit output)

### Cooking

**Rusted Metal** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Progression

The following progression is used for Ferrite refinement via a Portable Refiner:

- Rusted Metal
- Ferrite Dust
- Pure Ferrite
- Magnetised Ferrite

## Additional Information

- Junk type resources have the same symbol (Jn) rather than their own.
