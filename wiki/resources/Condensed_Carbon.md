# Condensed Carbon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fuel
- **Type:** Refined Organic Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** C+
- **Value:** 24
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Condensed Carbon** is a resource.

## Summary

**Condensed Carbon** (**C+**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[fuel elements]].

## Game description

Advanced material, processed from regular carbon in a [[Refiner]].

The increased molecule density offers a substantially improved energy-per-gram ratio.

Occasionally found in crystalline form. Harvesting large crystals may require an [[Advanced Mining Laser]].

## Sources

- Found as red [[Mineral|crystal minerals]] on the surface of every planet. The largest crystals require an Advanced Mining Laser to be mined. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a red (C+) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the [[Scanner]].<br>
- Also found as a secondary element in some plants (must be identified with the [[Analysis Visor]] first).
- Can be collected from Support Frigates in player's fleet via the Communication Panel (by landing pads).

- **Refiner** · *Condense Carbon* · 0.18 s per unit: 2 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Oxygenate Carbon* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Carbon]] + 2 × [[Oxygen]] → 5 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Oxygenate Carbon* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 2 × [[Oxygen]] → 6 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Feed Microbes* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 1 × [[Mordite]] → 2 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Feed Microbes* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 2 × [[Carbon]] → 2 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Feed Microbes* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 3 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Algal Processing* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Cyto-Phosphate]] → 1 × Condensed Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Harness Energy* · 0.04 s per unit: 1 × [[Uranium]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 2 × Condensed Carbon

## Infinite Growth

There is a method to grow Condensed Carbon (and Mordite) infinitely. You will need 1x Condensed Carbon, 1 Faecium, and 1 Mordite to start:

1. Faecium x1 + Condensed Carbon x1 => Condensed Carbon x3 (Plus 2 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Faecium)
1. Condensed Carbon x1 + Mordite x1 => Faecium x4 (Minus 1 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Mordite, Plus 4 Faecium)
1. Faecium x3 => Mordite x2 (Minus 3 Faecium, Plus 2 Mordite)

You end up with +1 Mordite and +1 Condensed Carbon

## Additional information

- If a Traveller NPC asks for [[Plutonium]], the option is to give a "plutonium substitute", generally condensed carbon.
