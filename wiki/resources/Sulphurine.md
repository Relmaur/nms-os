# Sulphurine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Compressed Atmospheric Gas
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Crafting, Charging
- **Symbol:** Su
- **Value:** 20.0
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Sulphurine** is a resource.

## Summary

**Sulphurine** (**Su**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

A pungent atmospheric gas. High concentrations are extremely hazardous to organic life.

With the aid of specialised machinery, can be extracted from the atmospheres of , [[Biome - Barren|Desert]] and [[Biome - Volcanic|Volcanic]] planets.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[flora]]
- Rarely as a primary resource in some minerals
- From [[Atmosphere Harvester|Atmosphere Harvesters]] built on planets with Scorched, Barren or Volcanic [[biome|biomes]]
- Using [[Gas Extractor]] at a Sulphurine [[Concentrated Gas Cloud]]
- Raiding some [[Resource Depot|Resource Depots]]
- From [[Mutant Plant|Mutant Plants]] in Frozen biomes
- From a freighter's [[Stellar Extractor Room]] when in a Green [[Star system|Star System]]

- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 3 × [[Nitrogen]] → 1 × Sulphurine
- **Refiner** · *Gas Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Nitrogen]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Sulphurine
- **Refiner** · *Gas Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Nitrogen]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Sulphurine
