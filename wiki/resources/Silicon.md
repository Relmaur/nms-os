# Silicon

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Silicate
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** Found in various silicates. Used for construction and recharging shields.
- **Symbol:** Si
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Silicon** (**Si**) is a resource.

## Summary

**Silicon** (**Si**) is a member of the [[Silicate elements]] [[resource]] family. It is only available in [[pre-release]] builds of [[No Man's Sky]].

This element can be combined with other elements to construct tools, or used to recharge shields.

## Known locations

- Pre-release planet [[Balari V]]

## Source

This element can be found in blue bulbous flora. The silicate is located in the head of the plant and is removed when the silicate is harvested.

## Use

60 units of Silicon was one of the ingredients used for crafting the [[Bypass Chip]] during the pre-release, along with 25 units of [[Plutonium]].

## Additional information

Silicon can be used to recharge shields.
