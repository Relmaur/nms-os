# Sulfur

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Oxide
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** Abundant yellow non-metal element
- **Symbol:** S
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Sulfur** is a resource.

## Summary

**Sulfur** (**S**) is a common yellow non-metal [[resource]] of the [[Oxide elements|Oxide]] family. It is only available in [[pre-release]] builds of [[No Man's Sky]].

## Game description

Abundant yellow non-metal, most commonly found in the foodstuff of the [[Gek]] due to its use in scent gland processes. Occurs naturally with a reactivity that leads to a wide range of use in galactic  [[Manufacturing Facilities]]. Often seen adding a splash of colour to a Gek Trader's beak.
