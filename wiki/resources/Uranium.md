# Uranium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Localised Earth Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** U
- **Value:** 62
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Uranium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Uranium** (**U**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Localised Earth Element|localised]] [[earth elements]]. It is used as fuel for the [[starship]]'s [[Launch Thruster]].

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common minerals after inspection with an [[Analysis Visor]].

Typically found on planets with a [[Biome - Irradiated|radioactive]] environment.

## Source

- In [[Mineral]] deposits on Irradiated [[biome|biomes]].
- Can be found in [[Metal 'Fingers']].
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]], but the purchase price is much higher than the nominal value.
- Recycling Radioactive [[Industrial Waste]] at a [[Waste Processing Plant]].
