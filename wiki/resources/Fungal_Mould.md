# Fungal Mould

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Flora
- **Type:** Harvested Agricultural Substance
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting
- **Symbol:** Ml
- **Value:** 16
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Fungal Mould** is a resource.

## Summary

**Fungal Mould** (**Ml**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Harvested Agricultural Substance]] [[flora elements]].

The [[British spelling]] of this phrase is used in the wiki.

## Game Description

An oddly-glowing substance, harvested from the gills of [[Fungal Cluster|Fungal Clusters]].

The host plant requires a [[Biome - Toxic|toxic climate]] to thrive in the wild, but can be farmed in all environments with the aid of a [[Hydroponics Tray]].

## Source

As described in the Summary, this element can be obtained from specific reddish-brown mushrooms on toxic [[planet|planets]]. Safely harvesting this resource requires the use of [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]].

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Fungal Mould can also be grown as a product of the Fungal Cluster plant, with a harvest time of 4 hours yielding 50 Fungal Mould.

- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Fungal Mould]] + 1 × [[Ammonia]] → 2 × Fungal Mould
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Ammonia]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Fungal Mould
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Ammonia]] + 1 × [[Nitrogen]] → 1 × Fungal Mould
- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Ammonia]] + 1 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Fungal Mould

## Additional Information

- Fungal Mould can be used to make [[Fibrous Stew]] without needing any other ingredients. After cooking the mould to create Non-Toxic Mushrooms, placing the product back into the Nutrient Processor will create Fibrous Stew.
