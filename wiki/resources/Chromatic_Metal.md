# Chromatic Metal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Highly Refined Stellar Metal
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Symbol:** Ch
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Upgrading
- **Value:** 88
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Chromatic Metal** is a resource.

## Summary

**Chromatic Metal** (**Ch**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[special elements]]. It is used for construction of advanced technologies.

## Game description

A processed metal alloy, created in a [[Refiner]] from the stellar metals: [[Copper]], [[Cadmium]],[[quartzite]], [[Emeril]] and [[Indium]].

The rarer the stellar metal, the more Chromatic Metal it will produce.

This valuable metal is used in the creation of many advanced technologies.

## Source

- Available for purchase from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Secondary element in some minerals.
- Can be requested from freighter's captain for rescuing his [[Freighter]] from pirates, up to 256 Chromatic Metal.
- Drops from destroyed [[Pirate]] ships and NPC ships (standing will decrease) in small amounts, 30-50 Chromatic Metal per ship destroyed.
- Drops from Freighter cargo pods ([[Sentinel|Sentinels]] will attack back) or accompanying free floating cargo pods (standing will decrease), 200-250 Chromatic Metal per pod destroyed.

## Infinite Chromatic Metal - Beyond

**NOTE:** this exploit is only possible with [[Indium]] or [[Quartzite]] as of the [[WorldsTwo]] release.

It is possible to get an infinite amount of Chromatic Metal using 1-2 [[Medium Refiner|Medium Refiners]]/[[Large Refiner|Large Refiners]], Indium or Quartzite, and Chromatic Metal.

- Start with 4x Indium or Quartzite and 4x Chromatic Metal.
- In the first Refiner put 4x Indium or Quartzite as the first resource and 4x Chromatic Metal as the second resource. This will generate 8x Indium or Quartzite, depending on the material used.
- In the second Refiner put 4x Indium or Quartzite into the first slot. This will give you a 8x Chromatic Metal, with 4x Indium or Quartzite leftover from step 1.
- Repeat.

There is also a faster, but more costly method involving a [[Large Refiner]], Indium or Quartzite, Chromatic Metal, Gold, and Silver

- Start with 10x Indium or Quartzite, 30x Chromatic Metal, 40x Gold, and 40x Silver
- Place the 10x Indium or Quartzite and 10x Chromatic Metal in the Refiner, generating 20x Indium or Quartzite, depending on the material used.
- Repeat that step once more with the 20x Indium or Quartzite and remaining 20x Chromatic Metal, ending with 40x Indium or Quartzite, depending on the material used.
- Place the 40x Indium or Quartzite, 40x Gold, and 40x Silver into the Refiner. After it is complete you will have 1200x Chromatic Metal
The Chromatic Metal at the end of this process doubles every time you repeat steps 2 and 3, so doing it once more will have you end with 2400, but this increases the amount of Chromatic Metal, Silver, and Gold needed as well.

Important notes:
- Technically only one refiner is needed, but having two makes it quicker to be doing both conversions at the same time.
- Make sure not to use up all of your starting Indium while converting into Chromatic Metal.
- This is also a way to get infinite Copper, Cadmium, Emeril, or Indium.
- This does not work with [[Activated Indium]].
- You can add [[Pure Ferrite]] to the second refiner to jumpstart the process.
