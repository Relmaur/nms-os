# Comet Dust

## Comet Dust

**Comet Dust** is a resource.

## Sumary

Comet Dust (H4) is a [[resource]] found on *comet fragments* located within deep-space [[Asteroid Belt|asteroid belts]].

## Game description

An incredibly fine powder derived from the blasted remains of comet fragments. No evidence of its original crystalline matrix remains.

Comet fragments are located within deep-space asteroid belts.

## Source

- It can be mined on marked comet fragments with your [[Multi-Tool]] Laser.
