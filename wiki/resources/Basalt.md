## Summary

**Basalt** (**B**) is a [[resource]].

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common minerals after inspection with an [[Analysis Visor]].

Typically found on planets with a volcanic environment.

## Source

- In [[Resource Deposit|resource deposits]] on Volcanic planets, though not all eligible planets will present deposits.
- As a secondary element in some [[Mineral|Minerals]] on Volcanic planets.

- **Refiner** · *Volcanic Transmutation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Pyrite]] + 1 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 1 × Basalt
- **Refiner** · *Volcanic Transmutation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Phosphorus]] + 1 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 2 × Basalt
