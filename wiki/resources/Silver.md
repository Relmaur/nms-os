# Silver

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exotic
- **Type:** Valuable Asteroid Mineral
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Ag
- **Value:** 186.0
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Silver** is a resource.

## Summary

**Silver** (**Ag**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[exotic elements]]. It is one of the main ingredients for crafting [[freighter]] related [[base building]] products.

## Game description

Valuable interstellar trading mineral, typically mined from asteroids. Occasionally found in underground planetary deposits.

Prized by people in all systems for its lustre and shine.

## Source

- Can be mined from [[asteroid|asteroids]].
- Can be found in [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]].
- Can be extracted from [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit|Deep-Level Mineral Deposits]].
- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] in [[cave|caves]].
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1 s per unit: 1 × [[Herox]] → 250 × Silver
- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Aronium]] → 250 × Silver
- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Dirty Bronze]] → 250 × Silver
- **Refiner** · *Crystal Processing* · 0.36 s per unit: 5 × [[Comet Dust]] → 1 × Silver
