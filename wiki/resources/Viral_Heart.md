# Viral Heart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exotic Goods
- **Type:** Semi-Sentient Protein Strand
- **Value:** 190,000
- **Added in:** Cosmos

**Viral Heart** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Viral Heart** is a [[curiosity]]. It can be found at [[Infested Outpost|Infested Outposts]] and brought back to [[Deep-Space Outpost|Deep-Space Outposts]] to be harvested.

## Game description

A folded mass of pathogenic proteins, harvested from a deep-space biohazard.

This has been processed at a [[deep-space outpost]], and can be sold on the open market.

## Source

Using a Gravitino coil and a Corvette with a tracker beam to bring a Viral Heart from an [[Infested Outpost]] to a [[deep-space outpost]] where it can be processed.
