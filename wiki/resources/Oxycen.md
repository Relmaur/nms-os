# Oxycen

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Gas
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** Common breathable gas and is most likely based off of, and has the same properties as, oxygen.
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Oxycen** is a pre-release element.

## Summary

**Oxycen** is a common breathable gas and is most likely based off of, and has the same properties as, [[wikipedia:Oxygen|oxygen]]. [[Planet]] atmospheres are generally made up of a varying mixture of **Oxycen** and [[Hyglese]].

An in-game panel description of the game does not appear exist.

## Additional Information

This element has only been seen in the pre-release versions of the game, and MAY have a chemical symbol of **Ox**.
