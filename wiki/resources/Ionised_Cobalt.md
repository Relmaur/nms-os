# Ionised Cobalt

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Processed Subterranean Mineral
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Co+
- **Value:** 162.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Ionised Cobalt** is a resource.

## Summary

**Ionised Cobalt** (**Co+**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[earth elements]].

The [[British spelling]] of this word is used in the wiki.

## Game description

A highly energised form of [[Cobalt]], this processed metal can be used to create [[Advanced Ion Battery|Advanced Ion Batteries]], a conveniently portable source of energy for the [[Exosuit]].

In widespread use as a key component in the creation of advanced technologies and structures.

## Source

- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Refiner** · *Ionise Mineral* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Cobalt]] → 1 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Efficient Cobalt Ionisation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 2 × [[Oxygen]] → 6 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Efficient Cobalt Ionisation* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Cobalt]] + 2 × [[Oxygen]] → 5 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Pugneum Washing* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 2 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Bonded Cobalt Extraction* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 2 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Ionise Bulbs* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] → 2 × Ionised Cobalt
- **Refiner** · *Ionise Bulbs* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Marrow Bulb]] → 2 × Ionised Cobalt

## Additional information

- 13 Ionised Cobalt are required to charge a [[Portal]] [[Glyph]]. It is actually less efficient than using standard Cobalt, as 20 Cobalt are used instead and the ratio of Cobalt : Ionised Cobalt is 2:1.
- The description refers to Advanced Ion Batteries, although they were removed from the game in Origins.
