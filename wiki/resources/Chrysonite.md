# Chrysonite

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Silicate
- **Category:** Technology
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** Blue crystals often found on cold planets
- **Symbol:** Ch
- **Value:** 82.5
- **Added in:** Origins

**Chrysonite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Chrysonite** (**Ch**) is a member of the [[Silicate elements]] family.

## Game description

A very powerful element used in many advanced technology [[blueprint|blueprints]], components and product formulas. Found in blue crystalline structures.

## Source

Chrysonite can be mined from blue crystalline structures on [[planet|planets]] or bought from merchants. Commonly found on the surface of [[Biome|cold planets]]. Chrysonite crystals were also found on paradise type planets.

An [[Advanced Mining Laser]] is required to extract the crystals.

## Use

Chrysonite is an obsolete resource and no longer has a purpose in the game.
