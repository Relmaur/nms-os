# Carbon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fuel
- **Type:** Unrefined Organic Element
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** C
- **Value:** 12
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Carbon** is a resource.

## Summary

**Carbon** (**C**) is a [[resource]] used in recharging and one of the [[fuel elements]] for refining.

## Game description

The building block of all organic life. Of critical importance to space travellers, as Carbon is used to recharge mining equipment.

The [[Mining Beam|Mining Laser]] will harvest Carbon from trees, plants, and all other forms of vegetable.

## Source

- Can be mined from [[flora]].
- Can be found as a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]].
- Can be farmed by constructing a [[Standing Planter]] in a base.
- Can be harvested from the walls of agricultural type freighter base building modules, such as the [[Biological Room (Expansion)]].

- **Refiner** · *Burn Plantlife* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Cactus Flesh]] → 2 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Release Carbon* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 2 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Burn Plantlife* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Gamma Root]] → 2 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Burn Plantlife* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Fungal Mould]] → 2 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Inefficient Burn* · 0.08 s per unit: 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Burn Plantlife* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Solanium]] → 2 × Carbon
- **Refiner** · *Burn Plantlife* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Star Bulb]] → 2 × Carbon

### Exploit

Once the player has a [[Portable Refiner]] and [[Medium Refiner]]/[[Large Refiner]], they can produce Carbon and Condensed Carbon for free.

This can also be done with only a Portable Refiner, but with less yield.

1. Refine 68 Carbon in Medium Refiner/Large Refiner to produce 34 Condensed Carbon.
1. Place down the Portable Refiner and recharge its [[Fuel Inverter]] with the 34 Condensed Carbon.
1. Pick up the Portable Refiner to receive 100 Carbon.
1. Repeat steps 1 to 3, to produce 32 Carbon per cycle.

### Exploit 2

1. Get a [[Freighter]] (you get a free one from the story; they're all the same inside excluding the one page of inventory.)
1. Place as many [[Standing Planter|Standing Planters]] as you want in your freighter. Requires two [[Metal Plating]] and 25 [[Condensed Carbon]] per planter. (They refresh with new carbon every time your freighter base loads.)
1. Walk into the base area of your [[freighter]] and collect carbon. One planter yields 62-120 **Carbon**.
1. Walk into the hanger area hallway and immediately turn around to go back into the base area.
1. Repeat steps 3-4. All the **Carbon** will be refreshed letting you harvest it again in seconds.

### Exploit 3

1. Place a [[Standing Planter]] in your base or freighter.
1. Harvest the carbon from the planter - yields 62-120 Carbon.
1. Open the edit base menu and move the planter.
1. The planter will be refreshed. Repeat steps 2-4.
