# Condensed Carbon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fuel
- **Type:** Refined Organic Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Blueprint Value:** 24
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** **C+**
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Condensed Carbon** is a resource.

## Summary

**Condensed Carbon** (**C+**) is a resource and one of the fuel elements.

## Game description

Advanced material, processed from regular carbon in a Refiner.

The increased molecule density offers a substantially improved energy-per-gram ratio.

Occasionally found in crystalline form. Harvesting large crystals may require an Advanced Mining Laser.

## Sources

- Found as red crystal minerals on the surface of every planet. The largest crystals require an Advanced Mining Laser to be mined. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a red (C+) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the Scanner.
- Also found as a secondary element in some plants (must be identified with the Analysis Visor first).
- Can be collected from Support Frigates in player's fleet via the Communication Panel (by landing pads).

Condensed Carbon can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Carbon ×2 → Condensed Carbon ×1 (*"Condense Carbon"*, 0.18 sec./unit output)
- Carbon ×2 + Oxygen ×2 → Condensed Carbon ×5 (*"Oxygenate Carbon"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Condensed Carbon ×1 + Oxygen ×2 → Condensed Carbon ×6 (*"Oxygenate Carbon"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Faecium ×1 + Mordite ×1 → Condensed Carbon ×2 (*"Feed Microbes"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Faecium ×1 + Carbon ×2 → Condensed Carbon ×2 (*"Feed Microbes"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Faecium ×1 + Condensed Carbon ×1 → Condensed Carbon ×3 (*"Feed Microbes"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Cyto-Phosphate ×1 → Condensed Carbon ×1 (*"Algal Processing"*, 0.24 sec./unit output)
- Uranium ×1 + Di-hydrogen ×1 → Condensed Carbon ×2 (*"Harness Energy"*, 0.04 sec./unit output)

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Condensed Carbon gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%

## Infinite Growth

There is a method to grow Condensed Carbon (and Mordite) infinitely. You will need 1x Condensed Carbon, 1 Faecium, and 1 Mordite to start:

- Faecium x1 + Condensed Carbon x1 => Condensed Carbon x3 (Plus 2 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Faecium)
- Condensed Carbon x1 + Mordite x1 => Faecium x4 (Minus 1 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Mordite, Plus 4 Faecium)
- Faecium x3 => Mordite x2 (Minus 3 Faecium, Plus 2 Mordite)

You end up with +1 Mordite and +1 Condensed Carbon
