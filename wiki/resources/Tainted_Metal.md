# Tainted Metal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Special
- **Type:** Salvaged Scrap
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Blueprint Value:** 380.0
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Symbol:** **Un**
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Tainted Metal** is a resource.

## Summary

**Tainted Metal** (**Un**) is a resource found in Derelict Freighters. It is used to trade for suspicious items and cosmetics with the Scrap Dealer, or for converting into Nanites via refining.

## Item description

Shredded fragments of a soft, grey metal. Its surface is porous, and when touched it oozes ever-so-slightly.

Found in the creaking wreck of a derelict freighter. The space station Scrap Dealer may find this interesting...

## Source

- Resource found in Derelict Freighter space encounter.
- Received from Scrap Dealer and Guild Envoy in exchange for Crew Manifest and Captain's Log.

## Use

### Crafting

**Tainted Metal** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Tainted Metal** is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

- Nanite Cluster -- Tainted Metal x1 → Nanite Cluster x2 (*"Doom Cycling"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)

### Cooking

**Tainted Metal** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional information

The Scrap Dealer on the space station sells contraband in exchange for Tainted Metal.
