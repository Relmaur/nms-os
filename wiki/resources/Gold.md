# Gold

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exotic
- **Type:** Valuable Asteroid Mineral
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** Au
- **Value:** 353.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Gold** is a resource.

## Summary

**Gold** (**Au**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[exotic elements]].

## Game description

Highly valuable interstellar trading mineral, typically mined from asteroids. Occasionally found in underground planetary deposits.

Universally prized for its beauty and versatility.

## Source

- As a primary (very rare) or secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] in [[cave|caves]].
- Can be mined from [[asteroid|asteroids]].
- Can be mined from deposits on [[Biome - Exotic|Exotic]] planets.
- Can be extracted from [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit|Deep-Level Mineral Deposits]] on exotic planets.
- Acquired as loot dropped by [[pirate|pirates]].
- Can be found in [[Metal 'Fingers']].
- Can be received from [[Gold Nugget|Gold Nuggets]] after they are analysed.
- Also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Lemmium]] → 125 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Organic / Anomaly Synthesis* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Mordite]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 1 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Alchemical Construction* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 1 × [[Residual Goop]] → 1 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Pugneum Alchemy* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 2 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Alchemy* · 4.8 s per unit: 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Emeril]] → 10 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Magno-Gold]] → 125 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Extreme Alloy Separation* · 1.2 s per unit: 1 × [[Grantine]] → 125 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Calcite Transmutation* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Living Pearl]] → 100 × Gold
- **Refiner** · *Alchemical Construction* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Hexite]] → 1 × Gold
