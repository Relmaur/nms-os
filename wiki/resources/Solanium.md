# Solanium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Flora
- **Type:** Harvested Agricultural Substance
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting
- **Symbol:** So
- **Value:** 70
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Solanium (So)** is a resource.

## Summary

**Solanium** (**So**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Harvested Agricultural Substance]] [[flora elements]].

## Game Description

A powerful chemical that has resisted attempts at synthesis. It can be harvested from the bark of the [[Solar Vine]].

The vine requires a [[Biome - Scorched|hot climate]] to thrive in the wild, but can be farmed in all environments with the aid of a [[Hydroponics Tray]].

## Source

This element can be harvested from wild plants, but requires the use of [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]], once every 21 hours (real time).

It can also be found mixed with [[carbon]] in carbon-based plants or trees. For easy discovery, scan the surrounding flora with the [[Analysis Visor]].

Please note the spawn rate for the "classic" solanium plants is really low and it is easier to find the element on other flora found on [[Biome - Scorched|scorched planets]].

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Solanium can be grown as a product of the Solar Vine plant, providing a base yield of 50 with a harvest time of 16 hours. It requires the use of [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]] to safely harvest.

- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Solanium]] + 1 × [[Phosphorus]] → 2 × Solanium
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Phosphorus]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Solanium
- **Refiner** · *Encourage Growth* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Phosphorus]] + 1 × [[Sulphurine]] → 1 × Solanium
- **Refiner** · *Organic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Phosphorus]] + 1 × [[Methane]] → 1 × Solanium
- **Refiner** · *Sulphur Injection* · 0.16 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Sulphurine]] → 1 × Solanium

## Additional Information

- Base harvest amount for Solanium in a bio-dome is 50.
