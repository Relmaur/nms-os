# Deuterium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** High Energy Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** D
- **Value:** 34.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Deuterium** is a resource.

## Summary

**Deuterium** (**D**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[earth elements]].

Blue [[Deuterium Rich Plant|Deuterium Rich Plants]] gives a short-term boost to the player's [[jetpack]] when interacted with. This can help the player travel greater distances using the "jetpack + melee" combo.

## Game Description

A stable hydrogen isotope used heavily in both small-scale fusion reactors and neutron-scattering shield technology.

Deuterium very rarely occurs naturally. Most of the galaxy's supply comes from the processing of [[Di-hydrogen]] and [[Tritium]] in [[Refiner|Refiners]].

## Source

Deuterium can be obtained in a variety of ways:
- purchased from terminals or NPCs
- refining process
- dismantling Deuterium-based technology, such as [[Pulse Engine Upgrade|Pulse Engine Upgrades]].

Refining it requires a [[Medium Refiner|medium]] or [[Large Refiner|large]] refiner.

- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Cycling* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Deuterium
- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Cycling* · 0.24 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Silicate Powder]] → 1 × Deuterium

## Additional Information

The Deuterium-rich plants mentioned above originally produced deuterium when harvested, but this was patched out soon after the initial NEXT release.

Because of this, Deuterium has to be obtained using other means.
