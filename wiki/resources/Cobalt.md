## Summary

**Cobalt** (**Co**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[earth elements]].

## Game description

Mined from underground minerals, cobalt is found in plentiful supply in [[cave|caves]] and caverns. Useful in the manufacture of [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]].

It is used in the creation of several technologies and structures, and can be processed in a [[Refiner]] to make the most of its inherent charge.

## Source

- From any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- As a primary element in stalagtite and stalagmite [[mineral|minerals]] found in caves.
- In [[Resource Deposit|resource deposits]].
- Can be found randomly within [[Hotspot|Hotspots]] on any planet, but are guaranteed on Irradiated Planets.

- **Refiner** · *Deionise Mineral* · 0.12 s per unit: 1 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 2 × Cobalt

## Trivia

- Cobalt (derived from German Kobalt, from Kobold, which means goblin) is an element only found in Earth's crust in chemically combined forms, save for small deposits of alloys from natural meteoric iron. Cobalt's name origin was superstitious with early cobalt miners and since cobalt's primary ores always contain arsenic, smelting the ore would oxidize the arsenic into highly-toxic and volatile arsenic oxide. Like in No Man's Sky, Cobalt is commonly used widely in lithium-ion battery cathodes in the form of lithium-cobalt oxide but nickel-cadmium and nickel-metal hydride batteries also utilize cobalt to improve nickel oxidation in batteries.
