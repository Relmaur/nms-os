## Summary

**Indium** (**In**) is a [[resource]].

## Game description

A chromatic metal, generated by fusion in the heart of a star. Such stellar material ends up forming deposits in the crust of local planets.

Indium is found on planets orbiting blue stars, and can be placed in a [[Refiner]] to create purified [[Chromatic Metal]] for use in the manufacture of advanced technologies.

## Source

It can be found on planets orbiting [[System colours|blue stars]]. It's easy to spot because of its specific blue color.

- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Indium]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 2 × Indium

### Exploit

NOTE: prior to Synthesis update the Indium output was twice as high.

Once the player has  [[Medium Refiner]]/[[Large Refiner]], they can use it to produce limitless Indium:

1. Start with x6 Indium.
1. Refine x2 Indium to create x4 Chromatic Metal.
1. Refine x4 Indium and x4 Chromatic Metal to create x8 Indium.
1. Repeat steps 2-3, creating 8 Indium for every 6 Indium started with.

Convert 1/3 of your Indium to Chromatic Metal, then combine with remaining 2/3rds of Indium to make 33% more.

Note:  Adding Pure Ferrite to this equation doubles the yield

1. Refine x1 Indium + x1 Pure Ferrite to create x4 Chromatic Metal.
