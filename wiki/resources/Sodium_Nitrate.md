# Sodium Nitrate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Catalyst
- **Type:** Refined Catalytic Element
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Blueprint Value:** 82.0
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** **Na+**
- **Updated:** Endurance

**Sodium Nitrate** is a resource.

## Summary

**Sodium Nitrate** (**Na+**) is a resource and one of the catalyst elements.

## Game description

An advanced catalyst, useful for charging defensive technologies such as shields or hazard protection.

Created in a Refiner, or occasionally found in crystalline form on a planet's surface.

Offers a substantially improved energy-per-gram ratio over regular Sodium.

## Source

- Found as yellow crystal minerals sporadically on the surface of every planet. All Sodium Nitrate crystals require an Advanced Mining Laser to be mined, regardless of their size. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a yellow (Na+) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the Scanner.
- Also available for purchase from some Galactic Trade Terminals and NPC Pilots.

### Cooking

**Sodium Nitrate** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

- Sodium Nitrate is roughly 2.5 times more efficient at recharging technologies than Sodium. Therefore, refining Sodium into Sodium Nitrate, despite reducing the quantity at a 2:1 rate, effectively boosts your catalyst reserves by 50%.
- 10 Sodium Nitrate is needed to repair a drop pod.
