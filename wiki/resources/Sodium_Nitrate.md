# Sodium Nitrate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Catalyst
- **Type:** Refined Catalytic Element
- **Rarity:** Rare
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Na+
- **Value:** 82.0
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Sodium Nitrate** is a resource.

## Summary

**Sodium Nitrate** (**Na+**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[catalyst elements]].

## Game description

An advanced catalyst, useful for charging defensive technologies such as shields or [[Hazard Protection|hazard protection]].

Created in a Refiner, or occasionally found in crystalline form on a planet's surface.

Offers a substantially improved energy-per-gram ratio over regular Sodium.

## Source

- Found as yellow [[Mineral|crystal minerals]] sporadically on the surface of every planet. All Sodium Nitrate crystals require an [[Advanced Mining Laser]] to be mined, regardless of their size. All crystals within a short radius of the player are shown as a yellow (Na+) symbol on the HUD for a short period of time after activating the [[Scanner]].<br>

- Also available for purchase from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and [[Trader (NPC)|NPC Pilots]].

### Single Input

- **Refiner** · *Process Sodium* · 0.4 s per unit: 2 × [[Sodium]] → 1 × Sodium Nitrate

### Basic Input

- **Refiner** · *Catalyse Carbon* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Sodium Nitrate

- **Refiner** · *Oxygenate Sodium* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 1 × Sodium Nitrate
- **Refiner** · *Oxygenate Sodium* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 1 × [[Oxygen]] → 2 × Sodium Nitrate
- **Refiner** · *Sentinel Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 1 × Sodium Nitrate
- **Refiner** · *Sentinel Catalysation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 3 × Sodium Nitrate

## Additional Information

- Sodium Nitrate is roughly 2.5 times more efficient at recharging technologies than [[Sodium]]. Therefore, refining Sodium into Sodium Nitrate, despite reducing the quantity at a 2:1 rate, effectively boosts your catalyst reserves by 50%.
- 10 Sodium Nitrate is needed to repair a drop pod.
