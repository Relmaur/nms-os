# Ioxinite

## Quick Facts

- **Description:** A hard, heavy metal that is red-silver in colour.
- **Symbol:** Ix
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Ioxinite** (Ix) is a resource.

## Summary

**Ioxinite** (**Ix**) is a hard, heavy metal [[resource]] that is red-silver in colour. It is only available in [[pre-release]] builds of [[No Man's Sky]].

Tough and durable, this element is found in a wide range of technological assemblies that need to survive in harsh conditions.

## Known locations

It is found in dirty-yellow rock formations on the pre-release planet [[Konangko]] in the system [[Re-Dhah]]. An aqua icon for this element is also displayed in the 18-minute gameplay video when scavenging space debris.

## Mining

[[Plasma Core Casing V1]] upgrade is necessary to mine Ioxinite.

## Additional information

The information about Ioxinite was first shown during the **No Man's Sky - 18 Minute Gameplay Demo** released on July 6, 2015.

## Speculation

Based on it's known properties, Ioxinite is likely to be a member of the [[Silicate elements|Silicate]] family of elements.
