# Dioxite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Localized Earth Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Blueprint Value:** 62.0
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** **CO2**
- **Updated:** Omega

**Dioxite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Dioxite** (**CO2**) is a resource and one of the localised earth elements.

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common minerals after inspection with an Analysis Visor.

Typically found on planets with a frozen environment.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some minerals or in Resource Deposits or Hotspots on planets with Frozen biomes.
- Found in Hotspots on Mega Exotic planets located in blue stellar systems.
- Available from some Galactic Trade Terminals.
- Dismantling Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)

Dioxite can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Condensed Carbon ×1 + Sodium Nitrate ×1 → Dioxite ×2 (*"Advanced Carbon Processing"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Carbon ×1 + Sodium Nitrate ×1 → Dioxite ×1 (*"Advanced Carbon Processing"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Frost Crystal ×2 + Salt ×1 → Dioxite ×1 (*"Floral Titration"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Ammonia ×2 + Ferrite Dust ×1 → Dioxite ×1 (*"Environmental Element Transfer"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Ammonia ×2 + Pure Ferrite ×1 → Dioxite ×1 (*"Environmental Element Transfer"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)

### Crafting

**Dioxite** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Cyclotron Ballista -- Dioxite x200 + Wiring Loom x2 → Cyclotron Ballista
- Frostwort -- Frost Crystal x50 + Dioxite x25 → Frostwort
- Grantine -- Ionised Cobalt x50 + Dioxite x50 → Grantine

- Thermic Layer -- Dioxite x150 + Silver x60 + Copper x75 → Thermic Layer

### Cooking

**Dioxite** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

### Charging

Dioxite charges Life Support by 2% per item used. It charges Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold) by 1.5% per item used.

## Additional information

- Due to it's periodic symbol being CO2, Dioxite may be implied to be some form of carbon dioxide. This is supported by the fact that Dioxite is only naturally found on cold planets and carbon dioxide solidifies into dry ice in extreme cold temperatures of at least −78.5 °C (−109.3 °F). Incidentally, some cold planets are cold enough in No Man's Sky to reach that temperature.
