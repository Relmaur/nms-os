# Dioxite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Localized Earth Element
- **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading
- **Symbol:** CO2
- **Value:** 62.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Dioxite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Dioxite** (**CO2**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[Localised Earth Element|localised]] [[earth elements]].

## Game description

Local mineral extract, typically found in large deposits or extracted from common minerals after inspection with an [[Analysis Visor]].

Typically found on planets with a [[Biome - Frozen|frozen]] environment.

## Source

- As a secondary element in some [[mineral|minerals]] or in [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] or [[Hotspot|Hotspots]] on planets with Frozen [[biome|biomes]].
- Found in Hotspots on Mega Exotic planets located in blue stellar systems.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Dismantling [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)]]

- **Refiner** · *Advanced Carbon Processing* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 2 × Dioxite
- **Refiner** · *Advanced Carbon Processing* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Carbon]] + 1 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 1 × Dioxite
- **Refiner** · *Floral Titration* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 1 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Dioxite
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Ammonia]] + 1 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Dioxite
- **Refiner** · *Environmental Element Transfer* · 0.36 s per unit: 2 × [[Ammonia]] + 1 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Dioxite

## Use

### Charging

Dioxite charges [[Life Support]] by 2% per item used. It charges [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)]] by 1.5% per item used.

## Additional information

- Due to it's periodic symbol being CO2, Dioxite may be implied to be some form of carbon dioxide. This is supported by the fact that Dioxite is only naturally found on cold planets and carbon dioxide solidifies into dry ice in extreme cold temperatures of at least −78.5 °C (−109.3 °F). Incidentally, some cold planets are cold enough in No Man's Sky to reach that temperature.
