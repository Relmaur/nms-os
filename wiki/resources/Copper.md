# Copper

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Refined Stellar Metal: Yellow
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Symbol:** Cu
- **Description:** A component for Chromatic Metal.
- **Value:** 41.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Copper** is a resource.

## Summary

**Copper (Cu)** is a [[resource]].

## Game description

A chromatic metal, generated by fusion in the heart of a star. Such stellar material ends up forming deposits in the crust of local planets.

Copper is found on planets orbiting yellow stars, and can be placed in a [[Refiner]] to create purified [[Chromatic Metal]] for use in the manufacture of advanced technologies.

## Source

Copper sources include:

- [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] and [[Hotspot|Hotspots]] on planets orbiting [[System colours|yellow stars]].
- [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Secondary element in cave [[mineral|minerals]] (rare).

### Mining

A [[Terrain Manipulator]] or [[Autonomous Mining Unit|Autonomous Mining Units]] are needed to mine copper deposits. A [[Mineral Extractor]] is required to harvest copper located using the [[Survey Device]] upgrade to the [[Analysis Visor]].

### Refining

- **Refiner** · *Chromatic Expansion* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Copper]] + 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 2 × Copper
