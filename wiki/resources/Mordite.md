# Mordite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Harvested Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** Mo
- **Value:** 40
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Mordite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Mordite** (**Mo**) is a [[resource]] and one of the [[earth elements]].

## Game description

Despite much study, little is known about this haunting, crystalline compound.

It is released from the cadavers of dying creatures, but can also be specifically cultivated and harvested from the [[Mordite Root]] plant.

## Source

- It can be recovered from killing [[fauna]] in the game.
- It can be harvested from [[Mordite Root]] (yields 25 every eight hours of real time).
- It can also be found in [['Organic' Rock|'Organic' Rocks]].
- It can be found in Mineral Deposits on [[Biome - Marsh|Marsh]] planets.

- **Refiner** · *Organic Reassembly* · 0.72 s per unit: 3 × [[Faecium]] → 2 × Mordite
- **Refiner** · *Anomaly / Organic Hybridisation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Carbon]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 1 × Mordite
- **Refiner** · *Anomaly / Organic Hybridisation* · 0.36 s per unit: 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Pugneum]] → 2 × Mordite
- **Refiner** · *Organic Reassembly* · 0.08 s per unit: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 1 × Mordite

## Infinite Growth

There is a method to grow Mordite (and Condensed Carbon) infinitely. You will need 1x Condensed Carbon, 1 Faecium, and 1 Mordite to start:

1. Faecium x1 + Condensed Carbon x1 => Condensed Carbon x3 (Plus 2 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Faecium)
1. Condensed Carbon x1 + Mordite x1 => Faecium x4 (Minus 1 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Mordite, Plus 4 Faecium)
1. Faecium x3 => Mordite x2 (Minus 3 Faecium, Plus 2 Mordite)

You end up with +1 Mordite and +1 Condensed Carbon

## Additional information

Mordite should not be confused with the [[mineral]] [[Notmordite]], as they are two distinct items.
