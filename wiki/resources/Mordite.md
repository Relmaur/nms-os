# Mordite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Earth
- **Type:** Harvested Substance
- **Rarity:** Common
- **Blueprint Value:** 40
- **Used for:** Building, Crafting, Charging, Upgrading, Refining
- **Symbol:** **Mo**
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Mordite** is a resource.

## Summary

**Mordite** (**Mo**) is a resource and one of the earth elements.

## Game description

Despite much study, little is known about this haunting, crystalline compound.

It is released from the cadavers of dying creatures, but can also be specifically cultivated and harvested from the Mordite Root plant.

## Source

- It can be recovered from killing fauna in the game.
- It can be harvested from Mordite Root (yields 25 every eight hours of real time).
- It can also be found in 'Organic' Rocks.
- It can be found in Mineral Deposits on Marsh planets.

Mordite can be refined using a Refiner with the following ingredients:

- Faecium ×3 → Mordite ×2 (*"Organic Reassembly"*, 0.72 sec./unit output)
- Carbon ×1 + Pugneum ×1 → Mordite ×1 (*"Anomaly / Organic Hybridisation"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Condensed Carbon ×1 + Pugneum ×1 → Mordite ×2 (*"Anomaly / Organic Hybridisation"*, 0.36 sec./unit output)
- Di-hydrogen ×1 + Condensed Carbon ×1 → Mordite ×1 (*"Organic Reassembly"*, 0.08 sec./unit output)

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Mordite gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%

## Infinite Growth

There is a method to grow Mordite (and Condensed Carbon) infinitely. You will need 1x Condensed Carbon, 1 Faecium, and 1 Mordite to start:

- Faecium x1 + Condensed Carbon x1 => Condensed Carbon x3 (Plus 2 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Faecium)
- Condensed Carbon x1 + Mordite x1 => Faecium x4 (Minus 1 Condensed Carbon, Minus 1 Mordite, Plus 4 Faecium)
- Faecium x3 => Mordite x2 (Minus 3 Faecium, Plus 2 Mordite)

You end up with +1 Mordite and +1 Condensed Carbon

## Additional information

Mordite should not be confused with the mineral Notmordite, as they are two distinct items.
