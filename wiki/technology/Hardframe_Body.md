# Hardframe Body

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Stats:** Fuel Usage: -11%, Boost Tank Size: +25%
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Hardframe Body** is a technology.

## Summary

**Hardframe Body** is a weapon which can be mounted on a Minotaur.

## Game description

A collection of salvaged components from a [[Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech|Sentinel Hardframe]], reconditioned to fit the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid.

Will function as a semi-autonomous artificial pilot plugin. Installation allows the Minotaur to traverse planets itself and engage in combat or mining activities to support its primary operator.

The AI pilot will always cede control to the primary operator when they enter the cockpit.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Radiant Brain]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Life Support Gel]] → 1 × Hardframe Body

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Radiant Brain]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Life Support Gel]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Radiant Brain]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Life Support Gel]]

## Additional Information

There are adjacency bonuses with the AI pilot and the [[Liquidator Body]], but the bonuses are unknown.
