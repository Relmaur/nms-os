# Singularity Cortex

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Type:** Lightspeed Warp Drive
- **Description:** FTL propulsion drive for warp speed
- **Recharge:** Chromatic Metal
- **Added in:** Echoes

The **Singularity Cortex** is a Living starship propulsion method.

## Summary

The **Singularity Cortex** is the Living [[starship]]'s faster-than-light propulsion drive to aid players travel across galaxies. By default, each warp between [[star system|star systems]] consumes 20% of the Singularity Cortex's fuel supply. It requires 60 [[Chromatic Metal]] or 96 [[Living Slime]] to fully charge.

The base range of a Singularity Cortex is 101 light-years (ly) and the player can warp to any systems.

## Game description

A complex bio-metallic stem plugged directly into an antimatter heart. Registers high activity during interstellar jumps. It appears to delight in the complex calculations required for FTL travel.

Access long-distance navigation systems through Galactic Map. Keep the ship happy by regularly jumping between systems.

## Crafting

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Larval Core]] + 20 × [[Viscous Fluids]]

## Upgrades

- **Singularity Cortex Node** - Potential improvements include warp-drive efficiency and jump distance (see info in gallery below).
- **Singularity Cortex Node** can evolve for  Nanites: C to B - 210  Nanites, B to A - 310  Nanites, A to S - 430  Nanites.
- The total number of nanites required to evolve a **Singularity Cortex Node** from C to S is 950  Nanites.

## Additional information

- Upgrade Nodes can be obtained by shooting [[Void Egg (Space Encounter)|Void Eggs]] in [[Space Encounter|Space Encounters]]. They are more common in [[Uncharted system|uncharted]] and [[Abandoned system|abandoned]] systems.
