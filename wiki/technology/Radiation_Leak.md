# Radiation Leak

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Type:** Damaged Starship Component
- **Added in:** Abyss

**Radiation Leak** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Radiation Leak** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found on a [[Crashed Ship]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

Starship subsystem is critically damaged.

Slot cannot be used for storage or technology until damage is repaired. Use / (PC/PS4) to begin repair process.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 410 × [[Paraffinium]] + 25 × [[Pugneum]]
