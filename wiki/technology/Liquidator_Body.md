# Liquidator Body

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Stats:** Allows the Minotaur to assist the player in combat when not in use
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Liquidator Body** is a technology.

## Summary

**Liquidator  Body** is a limb replacement which can be mounted on a Minotaur.

## Game description

An upgrade to the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid, the Liquidator is a cutting-edge heavy duty combat unit, designed to operate in the harshest of environments.

Will function as a semi-autonomous artificial pilot plugin. Installation allows the Minotaur to traverse planets itself and engage in combat or mining activities to support its primary operator.

The AI pilot will always cede control to the primary operator when they enter the cockpit.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Walker Brain]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Liquidator Body

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Walker Brain]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Walker Brain]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional Information

There are adjacency bonuses with the AI pilot and the [[Hardframe Body]], but the bonuses are unknown.

The Liquidator Body is only available after being unlocked during [[Expedition 14: Liquidators]].
