# Oxygen Rerouter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit - Life Support
- **Type:** Emergency Life Support System
- **Stats:** Oxygen Rerouting: Enabled
- **Updated:** Expeditions

**Oxygen Rerouter** is a technology.

## Summary

**Oxygen Rerouter** is a technology that allows the player to use their Life Support system as an emergency oxygen supply, if their other oxygen sources have been depleted.

It needs to be installed in an exosuit inventory slot for it to take effect.

## Game description

Upgrades the Aeration Membrane of the Exosuit to harvest oxygen from the Life Support system in the event of a breathing emergency, allowing the user to resurface and seek conventional fuel supplies.

Note: Long-term conversion of life support energy to breathable gas is not recommended, and may result in life no longer being supported.

## Source

The blueprint can be acquired from a Technology Merchant for 240.
The blueprint can be acquired from Iteration: Selene on the Space Anomaly.

## Crafting

Oxygen Rerouter can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Chromatic Metal ×100 + Quantum Computer ×1 + Crystal Sulphide ×6 → Oxygen Rerouter

Oxygen Rerouter can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Chromatic Metal ×50 + Quantum Computer ×1 + Crystal Sulphide ×3

Oxygen Rerouter can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Oxygen Rerouter → Chromatic Metal ×50 + Crystal Sulphide ×3

## Additional information

Oxygen Rerouter does not trigger adjacency bonuses with the other life support upgrades and is considered a lone blueprint upgrade. Even though this upgrade simply states that it upgrades the Aeration Membrane, this is not the case for direct purposes.
