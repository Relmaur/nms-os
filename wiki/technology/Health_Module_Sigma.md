# Health Module Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Exosuit Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases user's health once primary systems have failed.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Health Module Sigma** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

**Health Module Sigma** is a first-level [[health technology]] upgrade for the [[exosuit]].

The Health Module increases user's health once primary systems have failed. It is automatically consumed when [[Life Support]], Shield and [[Hazard Protection]] systems are offline.

## Function

After the player dies while they have this technology in their inventory, the player will gain 1 health.

## Location

Blueprints for this upgrade are only found in Abandoned Buildings.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Iron]] + 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 10 × [[Zinc]] → 1 × Health Module Sigma
