# Personal Forcefield

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Shield
- **Type:** Defensive Shield Technology
- **Added in:** NEXT

**Personal Forcefield** is a technology.

## Summary

**Personal Forcefield** is a [[technology]] which adds a deployable shield as an alt-fire function to [[Multi-tool]] weapons. This shield nullifies all ranged attacks coming from the front, but disables weapon usage (except in virtual reality; see Additional Information) and incurs a heavy drain on [[Life Support]] while active (the percentage value associated with the shield in the heads-up display indicates how much Life Support is left).

## Game Description

On activation, this module draws power from Exosuit Life Support systems to overload the Multi-Tool, distorting the electromagnetic field around the user and creating a powerful personal shield.

While this field offers substantial protection against incoming laser fire, the user is advised that the Multi-Tool cannot be used for other functions while powering the shield.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Personal Forcefield

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Ion Battery]]

## Release history

- [[NEXT]] - Added as a technology.
- [[Update 1.57|NEXT 1.57]] - Fixed an issue where it could be deployed on 0% charge. Fixed an issue where it would remain in-world forever when changing weapon modes.

## Additional Information

- In VR mode, you can use the Multi-Tool's primary weapon even while the shield is active, but the beam or bolt will be blocked by the shield. This is likely a bug and may be patched in a future update.
