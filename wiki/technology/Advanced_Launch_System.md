# Advanced Launch System

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Type:** Bespoke Landing Gear
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Advanced Launch System** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Advanced Launch System** is an alternative [[technology]] first introduced in [[Expedition 3: Cartographers]].

## Game Description

A custom-made launch control system for high-performance starships. Sensitive gyroscopes and specialised control circuits plot custom take-off routines for increased launch speed and efficiency.

Specialised, deuterium-derived fuel is required to recharge thruster. Launch procedures require sufficient fuel levels before activation.

Hold [W] to take off from planet. Hold [Circle/Shift/B] to initiate in-flight speed boost.

- **Repair**: 3 × [[Storm Crystal]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]]
