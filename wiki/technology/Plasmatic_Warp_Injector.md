# Plasmatic Warp Injector

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 300 ly
- **Added in:** Origins

**Plasmatic Warp Injector** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Plasmatic Warp Injector** is a level 2 [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be traveled by a freighter.

## Game description

An augmentation for the colossal hyperdrive engine of an interstellar freighter.

Hyperdrive benefits from advanced fusion that brings starship to higher warp speed and intensity. User's craft will become able to leap deeper into space, and into more distant planetary systems.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for eight [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Emeril]] + 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 5 × [[Antimatter Housing]] → 1 × Plasmatic Warp Injector

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Emeril]] + 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 2 × [[Antimatter Housing]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
