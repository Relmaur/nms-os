# Indium Drive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Blue Systems
- **Added in:** Synthesis

**Indium Drive** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Indium Drive** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] and it is the third upgrade to the [[Hyperdrive]]. It allows warping to [[System colours|blue coloured]] [[star system|star systems]].

The Indium Drive acts as a direct upgrade to the [[Emeril Drive]] in that it allows travel to Red and Green stars without needing to also have an [[Emeril Drive|Emeril]] or [[Cadmium Drive]] installed.

## Game description

Upgrades the hyperdrive's computational matrix, preventing temporal anomalies when attempting to reach rare-class blue stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on [[Space Anomaly]] for 200  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Emeril]] + 5 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Indium Drive

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Emeril]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Emeril]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Upgrade progression

- [[Hyperdrive]]
- [[Cadmium Drive]]
- [[Emeril Drive]]
- [[Indium Drive]]
- [[Atlantid Drive]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- Gives a bonus to hyperdrive range if the upgrade module is installed adjacent to the ship's hyperdrive or other drive modules.
