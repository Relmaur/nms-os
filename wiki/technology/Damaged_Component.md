# Damaged Component

**Damaged Component** is a broken technology.

## Summary

**Damaged Component** is a technology that suffered external or internal damage that prevents it from working properly, but it can still be repaired.

It can be found at various Points of Interest and fixing it allows access to the previously prevented function.
In case of the Multi-tool and the starship, the repair restores functionality to the slot where the Damaged Component used to be.

## Release history

- Atlas Rises - Introduced to the game.
- NEXT - New damaged components introduced, while the blueprint changed for previously added ones.
- Abyss - Added two damaged components related to the Sealed Sea Chest.
- Beyond - Added more damaged components.
- Expeditions - New repair recipes.
