# Master Circuit (Frigate)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Frigate
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Master Circuit** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Master Circuit** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at a [[frigate]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The control circuit for all the other components in the device. Damage to this circuitry is fatal.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 5 × [[Platinum]]
