# Nautilon Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Nautilon Cannon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Nautilon Cannon Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Nautilon Cannon Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Nautilon Cannon Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Nautilon]] [[Exocraft]]'s [[Nautilon Cannon]].

## Upgrade properties

Nautilon Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Rate of Fire*
- *Damage*

Up to three Nautilon Cannon Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Nautilon submarine --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

| Nautilon Cannon<br>Upgrade | Stats given: | Module Improves: | C-class | B-class | A-class | S-class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C/B: 1<br>A: 1-2<br>S: 2 | Damage | +1-3% | +3-6% | +6-9% | +9-12% |   |
| Fire Rate | +2-3% | +4-5% | +6-7% | +7-10% |   |   |

## Crafting

Nautilon Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Exocraft Cannon Modules.

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
