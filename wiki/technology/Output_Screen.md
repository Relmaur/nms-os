# Output Screen

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Output Screen** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Output Screen** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A display terminal designed to communicate the device's status to the user. As the terminal is broken, the user cannot be made aware that the terminal is broken.

- **Repair**: 8 × [[Chromatic Metal]]
