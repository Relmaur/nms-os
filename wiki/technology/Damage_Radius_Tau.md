# Damage Radius Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Plasma Launcher Companion Unit
- **Description:** Plasma Launcher grenades have widened impact craters
- **Stats:** Explosion Radius = +60%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Damage Radius Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Damage Radius Tau** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade for the [[Plasma Launcher]] portion of the [[Multi-tool]]

This technology modifies the Plasma Launcher to give its projectiles widened impact craters, increasing destructive power.

## Game description

Plasma Launcher grenades have widened impact craters. User will observe an increased destruction area upon ignition. User is advised to stand at a distance from targeted land mass to avoid damage to personal appendages.

Operates automatically once constructed within the user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.
