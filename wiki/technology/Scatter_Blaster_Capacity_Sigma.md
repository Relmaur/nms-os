# Scatter Blaster Capacity Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scatter Blaster
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases amount of ammunition held by the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster
- **Stats:** Clip Size = 20%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Scatter Blaster Capacity Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Scatter Blaster Capacity Sigma** is a first-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Game description

Increases amount of ammunition held by the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon mode before a reload is required by a small amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 20 × [[Titanium]] + 2 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] → 1 × Scatter Blaster Capacity Sigma

## Additional information

- Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory
- Needs no fuel or recharging
