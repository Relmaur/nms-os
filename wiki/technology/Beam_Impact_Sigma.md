# Beam Impact Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Strengthens Starship weapons.
- **Stats:** Damage = +40.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Beam Impact Sigma** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

The Beam Impact Sigma upgrade improves the [[Phase Beam]]'s capabilities, increasing it's ability to destroy [[starship|starships]] and [[asteroid|asteroids]] rapidly.

## Game description

Phase Beam destroys starship and asteroid targets at an increased rate.

Operates automatically when constructed within user's Starship inventory.
