# Land Disruptor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Type:** Explosive Energy Grenades
- **Description:** Excavation tool using remotely charged plasma shells.
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Land Disruptor** is a grenade technology upgrade.

## Summary

**Land Disruptor** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade that uses plasma shells. If used recklessly, this grenade tool can also increase the player's [[wanted level]].

It has at least two upgrade levels.

This was made redundant by the [[Terrain Manipulator]] and is no longer part of the game.

## Game description

Excavation tool that operates through the distribution of remotely charged plasma shells. Careless use can result in disrupted alien ecosystems, [[Sentinel]] attention, and damage to the user.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Silicon]] + 50 × [[Sulfur]] → 1 × Land Disruptor

## Uses

Land Disruptors can be used to create craters in the ground and bore holes in and out of [[cave|caves]]. It is handy for use on [[planet|planets]] like [[Balari V]] (pre-release) where the surface is extremely cold and there are warm underground caves that provide protection. Like most crafted upgrades, it can be dismantled to regain resources or an expansion slot.
