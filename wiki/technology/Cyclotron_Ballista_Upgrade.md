# Cyclotron Ballista Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Cyclotron Ballista Upgrade** is a [[starship]] [[technology]].

## Summary

**Cyclotron Ballista Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Cyclotron Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to the [[starship]]'s [[Cyclotron Ballista]].

## Upgrade Properties

Cyclotron Ballista Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Heat Dispersion*- *Fire Rate*- *Damage* (up to 2%)

Up to three Cyclotron Ballista Upgrades can be installed in each inventory of the starship. Installing four or more upgrades in a given inventory will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.
