# Large Rocket Tubes

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Rocket Launcher Upgrade
- **Stats:** +20% Heat Recovery
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Large Rocket Tubes** is a [[starship]] [[technology]].

## Summary

**Large Rocket Tubes** is a technology that augments the [[Rocket Launcher]], a starship weapon. Though there are no other Rocket Launcher upgrades, installing this technology next to Rocket Launcher will trigger an [[upgrade bonus]], further increasing its benefits.

## Game description

Expands the loading chamber of the starship Rocket Launcher, improving exhaust performance and allowing for significantly faster heat recovery.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 4 × [[Unstable Plasma]] → 1 × Large Rocket Tubes

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 2 × [[Unstable Plasma]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 2 × [[Unstable Plasma]]

## Bugs

The Heat Recovery stat appears to be not working. All it does is give an adjacency buff to the Rocket Launcher's damage output. The overall cooldown after one shot remains the same.
