# Liquidator Right Arm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Liquidator Right Arm** is a technology that can be purchased for 2400 [[Quicksilver]] at the [[Space Anomaly]] or claimed as a reward from [[Expedition 14: Liquidators]].

## Summary

**Liquidator Right Arm** is a weapon which can be mounted on a [[Minotaur]].

## Game description

An upgrade to the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid, the Liquidator is a cutting-edge heavy-duty combat unit designed to operate in the harshest of environments.

This limb override also functions as its own dedicated combat system - a flamethrower for extreme damage at close range.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 60 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 3 × [[Unstable Plasma]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Liquidator Right Arm

- **Repair**: 30 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 2 × [[Unstable Plasma]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 30 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 2 × [[Unstable Plasma]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
