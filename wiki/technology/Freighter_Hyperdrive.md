# Freighter Hyperdrive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Type:** Lightspeed Warp Drive
- **Recharge:** Warp Cell
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Freighter Hyperdrive** is a a freighter propulsion method.

## Summary

**Freighter Hyperdrive** is the [[freighter]]'s lightspeed propulsion drive to aid players travel across galaxies. It has a base range of 100 light-years.

By default, each warp between systems consumes 20% of the hyperdrive's fuel supply. A freighter hyperdrive is fully charged with 5 [[Warp Cell|Warp Cells]] or one [[Warp Hypercore]].
Installing hyperdrive upgrade modules found in [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]] will increase a range and may reduce fuel consumption just like in a regular [[Starship]].

## Game description

FTL propulsion drive that allows starship to attain warp speed and jump between neighbouring [[star system|systems]].

Charge equipment with Warp Cell or Warp Hypercore.

## Crafting

Freighter Hyperdrive is a pre-installed technology on the freighter.

- **Repair**: 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Antimatter]] + 2 × [[Antimatter Housing]]

Freighter Hyperdrive cannot be [[Dismantle|dismantled]].
