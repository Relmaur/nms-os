# Shell Greaser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Upgrade
- **Stats:** -15% Reload Time
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Shell Greaser** is a technology.

## Summary

**Shell Greaser** is a [[Projectile technology]] that augments the [[Scatter Blaster]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Scatter Blaster Upgrade|Scatter Blaster Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

The blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]].

## Game Description

Advanced self-lubricating components decrease friction within the Scatter Blaster, offering significant improvements to reload times.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Shell Greaser

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
