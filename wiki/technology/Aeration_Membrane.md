# Aeration Membrane

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Life Support
- **Type:** Aquatic Respiration Aid
- **Description:** Extends duration of underwater exploration.
- **Added in:** Expeditions

The **Aeration Membrane** system is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

The **Aeration Membrane** system is a [[Stamina technology]] upgrade for the [[Exosuit]].

Aeration Membranes increase a user's ability to swim underwater longer, and can be used alongside other Aeration Membrane upgrades.

## Game Description

User is able to swim underwater for extended periods.

Absorbs gases dissolved in water through a membrane contractor that, if user is of a conventionally structured species, is placed over the mouth or facial breathing hole.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[exosuit]] inventory.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Selene]] on the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 150 × [[Oxygen]] + 60 × [[Silver]] + 75 × [[Copper]] → 1 × Aeration Membrane

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Oxygen]] + 30 × [[Silver]] + 35 × [[Copper]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Oxygen]] + 30 × [[Silver]] + 35 × [[Copper]]

## Additional information

- Aeration Membrane does not provide any adjacency bonuses to the Underwater Protection Modules, nor can any of the other hazard protection upgrades do the same. It does, however, share an adjacency bonus with the other hazard protection upgrades.
