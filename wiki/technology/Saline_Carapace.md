# Saline Carapace

## Quick Facts

- **Description:** Allows aquatic landing
- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Launch Thruster
- **Type:** Aquatic Landing System
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Saline Carapace** is a living starship technology.

## Summary

**Saline Carapace** is a [[Living Ship (Starship)|Living Ship]] technology and an upgrade for the [[Neural Assembly]].

This technology is the living ship's equivalent to the [[Aqua-Jets]] upgrade, allowing a living ship to land and take-off on water.

## Game Description

A salty membrane encases the ship, dramatically increasing its natural buoyancy.

The carapace will allow the vessel to float, making water landings possible.

## Sources

This technology can be acquired by opening [[Psychonic Egg|Psychonic Eggs]] obtained by sending [[Organic Frigate|Organic Frigates]] on expeditions.

## Crafting

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Hadal Core]] + 1 × [[Hypnotic Eye]]
