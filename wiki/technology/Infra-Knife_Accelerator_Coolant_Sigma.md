# Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Infra-Knife Companion Unit
- **Description:** Reduces Infra-Knife cool-down times
- **Stats:** Heat Dispersion = +4%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Sigma** is [[weapons technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]].

This upgrade reduces the Infra-Knife Accelerator cool-down times. It is the first of three upgrade levels.

## Game description

Reduces time between shots when using the Starship's Infra-Knife Accelerator weapon mode by a small amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 30 × [[Thamium9]] + 30 × [[Chrysonite]] + 50 × [[Iron]] → 1 × Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Sigma

## Additional notes

- Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
