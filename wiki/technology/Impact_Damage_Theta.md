# Impact Damage Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Stats:** Damage = +5.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Impact Damage Theta** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Impact Damage Theta** is a third-level [[projectile technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

## Game description

Boltcaster unleashes more powerful rounds due to improved internal ion circulation speeds.

Allows user to deal critical damage levels on chose targets.

Works automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 200 × [[Iridium]] + 80 × [[Radnox]] + 200 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Impact Damage Theta
