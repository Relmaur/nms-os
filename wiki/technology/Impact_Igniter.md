# Impact Igniter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Pulse Spitter Upgrade
- **Stats:** +80 Fire Damage 4s Impact Fire Duration
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Impact Igniter** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

Impact Igniter is a Multi-tool upgrade which causes every projectile from the Pulse Spitter to set its target on fire, as well as deal damage on impact. It is acquired as a [[blueprint]], which blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]] for 180  Nanites.

Impact Igniter needs to be crafted and then stored in a Multi-tool [[inventory]] slot for it to have its effect. Unlike [[Pulse Spitter Upgrade|Pulse Spitter Upgrades]], this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for enhancing [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] on other upgrades.

## Game Description

A powerful combat upgrade for the Pulse Spitter. This module installs series of delicately calibrated fuel-injection nozzles within the fireing chamber, which are used to initiate a controlled burn within its plasma projectiles

Causes targets hit by the Pulse Spitter to burn for a short while, causing additional damage.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 200 × [[Phosphorus]] + 4 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Impact Igniter

- **Repair**: 100 × [[Phosphorus]] + 2 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 100 × [[Phosphorus]] + 2 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
