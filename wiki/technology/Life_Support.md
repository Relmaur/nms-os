# Life Support

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Core Systems
- **Type:** Suit Survival Power Pack
- **Description:** Provides basic life support from environmental hazards.
- **Recharge:** Isotope
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Life Support** is a core component of the [[exosuit]].

## Summary

Provides general life support as part of the general exosuit functionality. The level of current support is displayed on one of the various HUD bars, marked with the same "heartbeat" symbol as is displayed on the suit icon.

Unlike [[Hazard Protection]], Life Support only drains due to [[Travel|movement]] outside of an [[Exocraft]], [[space station]] or other habitable structure.

- walking (low drain)
- sprinting (medium drain)
- using the [[Jetpack]] (high drain)
- using [[Personal Forcefield]] (high drain)
- being in interplanetary space (extremely high drain)

These drains are increased while on a [[Biome - Dead|Dead]] [[planet]].

## Game description

The core of the Exosuit, the **Life Support System** continually filters and pumps breathable Oxygen.

In addition to its role in the continued existence of the Exosuit user, the Life Support Unit uses surplus oxygen to generate power for secondary systems, such as the Jetpack, Speed Augmentation and Torch.

Charge equipment with Oxygen, Life Support Gel or Dioxite.

## Recharge

The Life Support can be recharged using the following items (% values on Normal Difficulty):
- [[Oxygen]] (~2.5% per unit)
- [[Dioxite]] (~6.25% per unit)
- [[Cactus Nectar]] (5% per unit)
- [[Fresh Milk]] (20% per unit)
- [[Processed Sugar]] (20% per unit)
- [[Steamed Vegetables]] (20% per unit)
- [[Flavoursome Sauce]] (20% per unit)
- [[Spicy Fleshballs]] (20% per unit)
- [[Sweetened Butter]] (40% per unit)
- [[Honey Butter]] (40% per unit)
- [[Life Support Gel]] (100% per unit)

Note: On Survival/Permadeath Difficulty the resource demand for equipment recharging is increased by 50%, so these values would all be reduced by a third of the value on Normal Difficulty.  For instance, full life support recharge on Normal costs 40 Oxygen/16 Dioxite/1 Life Support Gel, while on Survival/Permadeath those costs increase to 60 Oxygen/24 Dioxite/2 Life Support Gel.

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger if Life Support is adjacent to [[Oxygen Recycler]] or Life Support Upgrades, though this only indirectly augments Life Support by improving the effects of its upgrades.
- Despite being impossible to Craft, a placeholder [[Blueprint]] exists within the [[Space Anomaly]], with a suggested build cost of 100 Ferrite Dust.
- For some odd reason, in the event of Life Support being damaged (ex. Galactic Travel), the power gauge never drains and always stays at the same percentage until it is repaired (ongoing bug).
