# Toxin Suppressor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Hazard
- **Type:** Blueprint
- **Description:** Protection from Toxins
- **Added in:** Origins

**Toxin Suppressor** is a type of exosuit technology.

## Summary

User with a toxin suppressor installed will have some protection from toxic damage that may be inflicted by the atmospheric environment on  [[planet|planets]] or from [[Cave]] [[Hazardous Flora]] which typically distribute toxins.

Reactive compounds within the Suppressor remove toxic liquids that fall on the user, thereby reducing peril and choking hazards.

The technology is charged by mineral extracts found in toxic environments. It operates automatically once constructed within user's exosuit inventory.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Selene]] on the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 150 × [[Ammonia]] + 60 × [[Silver]] + 75 × [[Copper]] → 1 × Toxin Suppressor

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Ammonia]] + 30 × [[Silver]] + 38 × [[Copper]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Ammonia]] + 30 × [[Silver]] + 37 × [[Copper]]

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's [[Exosuit]] inventory
