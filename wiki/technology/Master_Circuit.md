# Master Circuit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Master Circuit** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Master Circuit** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The control circuit for all the other components in the device. Damage to this circuitry is fatal.
