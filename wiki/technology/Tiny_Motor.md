# Tiny Motor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Tiny Motor** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Tiny Motor** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A very small motor, designed to power very small parts. It has corroded, and is full of very small rust.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 20 × [[Sodium]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]]
