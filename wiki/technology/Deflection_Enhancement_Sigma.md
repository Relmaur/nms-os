# Deflection Enhancement Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Deflector Shield Companion Unit
- **Description:** Starship energy shield is strengthened. Provides added protection from laser fire, in-flight projectiles and accidental space debris interaction.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Deflection Enhancement Sigma** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Deflection Enhancement Sigma** is [[health technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Deflector Shield]].

It improves the Deflector Shields capabilities, and is a level one upgrade.

## Game description

Starship energy shield is strengthened.

Provides added protection from laser fire, in-flight projectiles, and accidental space debris interaction.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 6 × [[Carite Sheet]] + 500 × [[Heridium]] → 1 × Deflection Enhancement Sigma

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
