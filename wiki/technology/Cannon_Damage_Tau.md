# Cannon Damage Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Cannon Damage Tau** is starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Cannon Damage Tau** is the second-level [[weapons technology]] upgrade for the [[starship]] [[Photon Cannon]].

The Cannon Damage upgrade increases the damage of the Photon Cannon.

## In-Game Description

Photon Cannon fires at an increased rate.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory

### Note

The Cannon Damage upgrades does not have any impact on rate of fire. The description appears to be incorrect, and may be an accidental inclusion of text data from the [[Accelerated Fire Sigma]] panel.
