# Radiation Protection Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Radiation Protection
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Recharge:** Uranium, Ion Battery, Advanced Ion Battery
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Radiation Protection Upgrade** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

**Radiation Protection Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Radiation Protection Modules]], are upgrades granting supplementary protection against radiation.

## Upgrade properties

Radiation Protection Upgrades function similarly to [[Hazard Protection]] but only protect the player from radiation damage. Protection duration depends on upgrade class (S-class upgrades offer the best protection).

| **Module Name** | **Module Improves** | B-class | A-class | S-class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Power Duration** | **Power Duration** | **Power Duration** |   |   |
| [[Radiation Protection Module]] | *Radiation Protection* | 200-300 | 240-320 | 280-360 |

The protection offered by Radiation Protection Upgrades, unlike that offered by the [[Exosuit]]'s built-in radiation protection, does not recharge automatically while in locations without a significant radiation hazard. Radiation Protection Upgrades must be manually recharged as needed using [[Uranium]] (6.25%/unit) or [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]] (100%/unit).

Up to three protections upgrades can be installed in the technology section of the Exosuit. Installing four or more upgrades in the technology section of the Exosuit will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Radiation Protection Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Radiation Protection Modules.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Uranium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Uranium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- It is not clear as to what percentages of Radiation Protection and Radiation Shielding are given. All that has been observed is that the depletion rate is the same regardless of whether it is a little too irradiated or way too irradiated, and it is also variable amongst the pool of upgrades by an adjustable time-frame.
- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Radiation Protection Upgrades if they are adjacent to any of the following:
  - Hazard Protection
  - Shield Lattice
  - Aeration Membrane
  - Suspicious Hazard Protection Upgrades
  - [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Heat)|Thermal Protection Upgrades (Heat)]]
  - [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)|Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold)]]
  - [[Toxic Protection Upgrade|Toxic Protection Upgrades]]

These only apply in actuality to basic upgrades with a visible percentage.
