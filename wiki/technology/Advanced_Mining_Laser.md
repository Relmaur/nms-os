# Advanced Mining Laser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Mining Beam Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Advanced Mining Laser: Enabled, +15% Mining Speed
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

The **Advanced Mining Laser** is a [[Multi-Tool]] upgrade.

## Summary

The Advanced Mining Laser is an upgrade for the [[Mining Beam]] that allows it to mine stronger resource deposits. This allows the Mining Beam to extract resources like [[Pure Ferrite]] and [[Sodium Nitrate]] without having to refine them. It also slightly increases mining speed.

Additionally, the Advanced Mining Laser's effects stack with those provided by [[Mining Beam Upgrade|Mining Beam Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game Description

Replaces standard Mining Beam optics with high-grade [[Living Glass]], allowing the beam to be effective against large and/or rare resource deposits.

The lens adapts automatically to minute fluctuations in the beam waveform, minimising scatter and increasing device mining potential.

## Crafting

The Advanced Mining Laser can be obtained in several ways:
- From the [[Base Computer Archives]] quest.
- Can be obtained as a reward from the final stages of the [[Awakenings]] main quest.
- Blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]] on the [[Space Anomaly]] for 75 [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]].
- Can appear as an attachment on Multi-Tools found in [[Space Station|Space Stations]], [[Sentinel Pillar|Sentinel Pillars]], [[Harmonic Camp|Harmonic Camps]], etc.
- Can be bought from technology merchants in [[Minor Settlement|Minor Settlements]].
- Can be acquired as a random upgrade through through communication with the [[Korvax]].

- **Crafting**: 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Advanced Mining Laser

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
