# Geology Cannon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Type:** Terrain Destruction Device
- **Recharge:** Unstable Plasma
- **Added in:** Frontiers

The **Geology Cannon** is a component of the multi-tool.

## Summary

The **Geology Cannon** component of the [[multi-tool]] causes massive damage to terrain compared to the [[Terrain Manipulator]].

Projectiles from the cannon will not detonate midair but will instead detonate immediately after hitting the ground or any obstacle.

## Game description

Large-bore projectile launcher. Propels plasma shells designed for rapid ground excavation.

Charged with [[Unstable Plasma]]. Warning: Explosive hole creation may also damage nearby organic matter.

Press [Right Mouse Button] to activate.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Geology Cannon

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- It can store maximum 20 charges and can be recharged with [[Unstable Plasma]]

## Warning

- Always be careful when firing this weapon, as it can instantly kill players, including the shooter, if they are too close to the explosion.
