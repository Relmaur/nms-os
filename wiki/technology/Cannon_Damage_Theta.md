# Cannon Damage Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship - Weapons
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Updated:** Atlas Rises

**Cannon Damage Theta** is a 3rd-level weapons upgrade.

## Summary

**Cannon Damage Theta** is the third-level weapons technology upgrade for the starship Photon Cannon.

This upgrade increases the damage of the Photon Cannon.

## In-Game Description

Photon Cannon fires at an increased rate.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory

#### Note

The Cannon Damage upgrade does not have any impact on rate of fire. The in-game description appears to be incorrect, and may be an accidental inclusion of text data from the Accelerated Fire Sigma panel.

## Crafting

Cannon Damage Theta cannot be crafted.

Original formula:

Omegon x100 + Gold x50 + Zinc x50 → Cannon Damage Theta

## Upgrade progression

- Cannon Damage Sigma
- Cannon Damage Tau
- Cannon Damage Theta

## Release history

- Release - Added as a technology.
- NEXT - Removed from the game. If it was installed, it has turned into Obsolete Technology.
