# Hyperdrive technology

**Hyperdrive technology** is technology that provides propulsion for a player's starship and allows them to jump from one star system to another.

## Summary

Hyperdrive is not provided with the original Life Pod systems, so will require crafting with the proper blueprints and resources. Videos of gameplay indicate that at least one type of hyperdrive can be crafted with a combination of Silicon and Iridium. The Alpha Vector starship (*PS4 pre-orders*) and the Horizon Omega starship (*PC pre-orders*) will both contain hyperdrive technology.
