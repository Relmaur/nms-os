# Osmotic Generator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Type:** Humboldt Drive Upgrade
- **Stats:** Fuel Efficiency = +20%
- **Added in:** Origins

**Osmotic Generator** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Osmotic Generator** is a propulsion upgrade for the [[Nautilon]] submarine. It lowers the fuel drain of all movement within the [[Exocraft]], extending the distance it can cover before the [[Humboldt Drive]] requires recharge with a [[Hydrothermal Fuel Cell]].

## Game description

Installed into the drive system aboard the Nautilon Submarine.

Captures energy by exploiting the saline gradient in deep water environments, allowing a slow recharge of the Humboldt Drive in the event of fuel emergencies.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 150 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 6 × [[Living Pearl]] + 2 × [[Solar Mirror]] → 1 × Osmotic Generator

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 3 × [[Living Pearl]] + 1 × [[Solar Mirror]]

## Additional information

- Osmotic Generator triggers [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] when installed adjacent to the Humboldt Drive or any of its procedurally generated upgrades, further increasing fuel efficiency.
- It does not actually allow recharge of the Nautilon, instead it simply reduces fuel burn.
