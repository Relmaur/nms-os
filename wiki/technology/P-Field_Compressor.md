# P-Field Compressor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Neutron Cannon Upgrade
- **Stats:** +2.0 Ion Spheres Created +27% Ion Sphere Speed -11% Charging speed
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**P-Field Compressor** is an energy technology.

## Summary

**P-Field Compressor** is a technology that augments the [[Neutron Cannon]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Neutron Cannon Upgrade|Neutron Cannon Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades. Supercharging its unique +2.0 spheres stat provides more per-shot damage than supercharging an S-rank upgrade, though less charge speed.

The blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]] for 180  Nanites.

## Game Description

A powerful upgrade to the Neutron Cannon. Additional resonance compressors allow significantly more energy to be routed through the cannon's central core, dramatically increasing operating power.

Installation will allow a fully-charged Neutron Cannon to launch additional projectiles at higher speeds.
