# Reactor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Subcomponent
- **Added in:** Cross-Save

**Reactor** is starship assembly part.

## Summary

The **Reactor** is a [[starship]] subcomponent that it is required to assemble a new starship in the [[Starship Fabricator Terminal]].

## Game Description

A starship subcomponent - the reactor core. The exceptional generator unit is capable of powering an S-class-Class starship.

Acquire starship components by breaking down existing ships at the Starship Outfitting unit aboard any space station. Visit the Starship Fabricator to design and assemble a new vessel.

## Additional Information

S-class [[Reactor]] costs around 12.000  Nanites

Reactors can only be sold for units, not Nanites.
