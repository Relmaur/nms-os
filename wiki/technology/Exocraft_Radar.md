# Exocraft Radar

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Communications
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Exocraft Radar** is an exocraft technology.

## Summary

**Exocraft Radar** is an [[exocraft]] [[technology]] that provides a large subspace radar mounted on the exocraft. It can be used by pressing the scan button, or with the [[Quick Menu]] to scan for individual points of interest, including [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] and [[Depot|Depots]]. This method of scanning does not require [[Navigation Data|Navigational Charts]] of any kind.

[[Radar Amplifier]] and [[Radar Power Resonator]] can be added to increase its functionality.

## Game description

A large and complex field-based subspace radar.

Due to their large power consumption and inherent subatomic instability, they are found only installed on Exocraft.
