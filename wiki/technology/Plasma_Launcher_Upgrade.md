# Plasma Launcher Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Plasma Launcher Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Plasma Launcher Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Plasma Launcher Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Plasma Launcher]].

## Upgrade properties

Plasma Launcher Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Projectile Velocity:* increases travel speed of explosive
- *Damage:* increases the maximum damage to other targets
- *Bounce Potential:* increases the maximum height of bounces
- *Explosion Radius:* increases the maximum radius of the explosion

Up to three Plasma Launcher Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a [[Multi-tool]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or fewer.

## Crafting

Plasma Launcher Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Plasma Launcher Modules.

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Unstable Plasma]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Unstable Plasma]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Plasma Launcher Upgrades if they are adjacent to [[Plasma Launcher]] or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- When using a save editor to compare a Plasma Launcher Upgrade with a Geology Cannon Upgrade of the same class, any given five digit Procedural ID will display the same name for both types, but they will always have stats different from each other.
