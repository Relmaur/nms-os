# Solar Ray

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Alchemical Laser
- **Recharge:** Catalytic elements
- **Added in:** Outlaws

The **Solar Ray** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Solar Ray** is a [[Multi-tool]] "weapon" acquired in [[Starbirth]] which allows conversion of [[Resource|metal deposits]] into [[Liquid Sun]]. It has the same general functionality as the [[Terrain Manipulator|Terrain Manipulator's]] Mine mode; however, the differences are as follows:

- The weapon's internal fuel supply is drained the same amount at all sphere sizes: 1%/tick in Survival and Permadeath [[Game mode|game modes]] and 0.5%/tick in Normal mode.
- Non-deposit terrain yields nothing when mined by the Solar Ray.
- The Solar Ray's small/medium/large sphere sizes have a maximum yield per tick of 1/2/8 units of Liquid Sun in Survival and Permadeath game modes and 2/4/16 units in Normal mode.
  - On flat deposits (50% sphere fill), the first tick of excavation yields 1/1/8 units of Liquid Sun in Survival and Permadeath game modes and 2/2/16 units in Normal Mode.
- [[Catalyst elements|Catalytic elements]], rather than [[Metal elements|ferrite elements]], are used to recharge the weapon.

The Solar Ray only has one firing mode, allowing it to be paired with secondary weapons such as [[Plasma Launcher]].

## Game Description

A highly specialised addition to the Multi-Tool's mining capabilities, transforming terrain deposits into liquid sun.

A powerful laser array amplifies local solar radiation, allowing for the rapid reconfiguration of nearby mineral ores.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 50 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 50 × [[Cobalt]] → 1 × Solar Ray

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 25 × [[Cobalt]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 25 × [[Cobalt]]

## Upgrades

The Solar Ray has no upgrades.

## Recharge

The Solar Ray is rechargeable using the following items:

- [[Sodium]] - 2%/unit
- [[Sodium Nitrate]] - 5%/unit

## Additional information

- The Solar Ray is the second Multi-Tool primary weapon in the history of *No Man's Sky* which is unable to deal damage at all (the first one being the Terrain Manipulator, which was initially added in [[Atlas Rises]]).
  - Including secondary weapons makes the Solar Ray the fourth non-damaging weapon which was introduced into the game by a major update (behind [[Combat Scope]] and [[Personal Forcefield]], which both made their first appearance in [[NEXT]]).
- The technology icon of the Solar Ray is similar to that of the [[Cyclotron Ballista]].
- The Solar Ray and Liquid Sun currently serve no purpose outside of the Starbirth quest and the Leviathan expedition. The upgrade can be safely disassembled after completing the relevant objective.
