**Deflection Enhancement Tau** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Deflection Enhancement Tau** is [[health technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Deflector Shield]].

It improves the Deflector Shields capabilities, and i a level-two upgrade.

## Game description

Starship energy shield is strengthened. Provides added protection from laser fire, in-flight projectiles, and space debris interaction.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.
