# Spewing Vents

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Spacecraft Projectile Weapon
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Spewing Vents** are weapon pseudo-technology for a Living starship.

## Summary

**Spewing Vents** are the default weapon installed on all [[Living Ship (Starship)|living starships]].

## Game description

A mess of sacs and sphincters, these vents generate and expel globules of plasma. When fused to the weapon systems of the ship, these are super-heated and then expelled at high-pressure.

## Usage

The Spewing Vents can be used both in direct combat and in clearing [[asteroid|asteroids]] for traversal.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 5 × [[Sac Venom]] + 40 × [[Living Slime]] → 1 × Spewing Vents

- **Repair**: 3 × [[Sac Venom]] + 20 × [[Living Slime]]

## Combat Properties

Spewing Vents have a typical range for a starship weapon, having the ability to hit targets from up to 1500 distance units (1500u) away (for reference, fugitive [[pirate]] starships will attack the player if they get within 1500u). By default, the weapon fires at a rate of ~7 shots per second and can fire 56 shots before overheating, resulting in a time to overheat of ~8 seconds, Upon overheating, Spewing Vents takes 4 seconds by default to recover from overheating.

Other than its bigger overheat tolerance, Spewing Vents behave identical to [[Photon Cannon|Photon Cannons]], having the same hit-scan mechanics with high lock-on potential.

Spewing Vents deal 120 base damage on direct hits to the following targets: [[fauna]], starships, [[Space Fleets]], and destructible obstacles such as Reinforced Doors ([[Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]]). Against starships in particular, the starship weapon deals 4x damage to the rear (resulting in 480 effective base damage).

Spewing Vents deals 24 base damage on direct hits with a full volley to all planetary [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

## Mining Properties

When used to harvest a planet's [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]], Spewing Vents have a base mining power of 24 for each shot.

## Upgrades

- **Spewing Vents Node** - Potential improvements include Damage, Fire Rate and Heat Dispersion  (see info in gallery below).
- **Spewing Vents Node** can evolve for  Nanites: C to B - 210  Nanites, B to A - 310  Nanites, A to S - 430  Nanites.
- The total number of nanites required to evolve a **Spewing Vents Node** from C to S is 950  Nanites.

## Additional information

- Upgrade Nodes can be obtained by shooting [[Void Egg (Space Encounter)|Void Eggs]] in [[Void Egg (Space Encounter)|Space Encounters]]. They are more common in [[Uncharted system|uncharted]] and [[Abandoned system|abandoned]] systems.
- In previous versions of the game, the installation location of the weapon would affect its projectile color. If the weapon was installed in the Storage Sacs, the plasma fire was green. If it were installed in the Organ Chamber, the weapon fire was blue.
