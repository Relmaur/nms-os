# Amplified Warp Shielding

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 50 ly
- **Added in:** Origins

**Amplified Warp Shielding** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Amplified Warp Shielding** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that allows warping to red [[Star system|Star systems]].

## Game description

Upgrades the hyperdrive's core bounding magnets, preventing rapid disassembly when attempting to reach rare-class red stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the Galactic Map.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for four [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 2 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 250 × [[Cadmium]] → 1 × Amplified Warp Shielding

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 125 × [[Cadmium]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
