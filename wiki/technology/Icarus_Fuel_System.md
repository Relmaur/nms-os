# Icarus Fuel System

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Solar Recharge Unit
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Icarus Fuel System** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Icarus Fuel System** is a solar recharging upgrade for Exocraft.

## Game description

The Icarus system takes advantage of existing metal panelling, upgrading them into photovoltaic surfaces. This reactive coating generates energy during daylight hours, offsetting fuel consumption.
