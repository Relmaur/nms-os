# Path of Deyneseg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**Path of Deyneseg** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is red in color with a black crystalline muzzle and a silver crystalline hand grip. The body contains vein-like patterns; all operating lights are green.

## Acquirement

Path of Deyneseg was found for sale in an [[camp|Outpost]] on an unknown planet, and is very likely of [[Vy'keen]] construction. The number of slots is currently unknown.
