# Pearl Offering

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Offering to the Sea
- **Added in:** Abyss

**Pearl Offering** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Pearl Offering** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found on [[Sealed Sea Chest|Sealed Sea Chests]] at [[Submerged Ruins]].

## Game Description

The chest requires a Living Pearl, harvested from the giant mother-molluscs that grow on the ocean floor.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Living Pearl]]
