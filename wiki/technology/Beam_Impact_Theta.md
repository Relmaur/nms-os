# Beam Impact Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Strengthens Starship weapons.
- **Stats:** Damage = +100.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Beam Impact Theta** is  [[weapons technology]] for the [[starship]] [[Phase Beam]].

## Summary

The Beam Impact upgrade improves the Phase Beam capabilities, increasing its ability to rapidly destroy ships and [[asteroid|asteroids]].

## Game description

Phase Beam destroys starship and asteroid targets at an increased rate.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[Starship]] inventory.
