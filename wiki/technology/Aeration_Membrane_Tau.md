# Aeration Membrane Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Life Support
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Extends duration of underwater exploration.
- **Stats:** Oxygen Tank = 75.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Aeration Membrane Tau** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

Aeration Membranes upgrades a user's [[Life support]] system, increasing the ability to swim underwater 75 seconds longer. It is the second level in the series.

This effect is not increased by additional Aeration Membranes in the user's inventory, although they will automatically take over when this upgrade runs out.

## Game description

Provides an increased ability to [[Travel|swim]] underwater for extended periods.

Can can be used alongside other Aeration Membrane upgrades.

Device is charged by [[Oxide elements]]. Operates automatically once constructed within user's exosuit inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Zinc]] + 100 × [[Carbon]] + 1 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] → 1 × Aeration Membrane Tau

This identical formula is used to craft five separate [[hazard protection]] upgrades for the [[exosuit]]:
- Aeration Membrane Tau
- [[Coolant Network Tau]]
- [[Radiation Deflector Tau]]
- [[Thermic Layer Tau]]
- [[Toxin Suppressor Tau]]
