# Crimson Freighter Trail

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Type:** Freighter Engine Flare Override
- **Description:** Changes the colour of the engines of your freighter.
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Crimson Freighter Trail** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Crimson Freighter Trail** is a [[Freighter]] [[Technology]] used to change your freighter trail color. The trail color can be changed using the [[Upgrade Control|Upgrade Control Terminal]].

## Game Description

An exclusive Freighter Engine Flare Modifier.

A harmonic generation device converts the output of the freighter engines to A T L A S frequencies, radiating a painfully crimson trail.

Transform the appearance of your freighter at the Upgrade Control Terminal on the bridge.

## Obtained

The **Crimson Freighter Trail** color could have been purchased at the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 800 Quicksilver. It was later rewarded for completing [[Expedition 10: Singularity]] - Phase 2.
