# Emergency Warp Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade
- **Added in:** Origins

**Emergency Warp Unit** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Emergency Warp Unit** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] that allows escape from combat by warping to the nearest  [[star system]]. It can be used anywhere other than a [[planet]]. Prior to [[Synthesis]] it was always possible to escape combat by warping.

## Game description

A significant upgrade to the [[Hyperdrive]] that allows an instantaneous warp even while conventional systems are blocked by starship combat. Access to the emergency warp system is via the [[Quick Menu]].

WARNING: Fast scramble systems mean the user is unable to plot a destination. Uncalculated lightspeed events will damage the unit and require frequent repairs.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on board [[Space Anomaly]] for 240  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Cell]] + 220 × [[Gold]] + 4 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × Emergency Warp Unit

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Warp Cell]] + 110 × [[Gold]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 110 × [[Gold]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]]

## Additional information

- Gives a bonus to hyperdrive range if the upgrade module is installed adjacent to the ship's hyperdrive or other drive modules.
- Has no organic counterpart, so it’s not possible to emergency warp on a [[Living Ship (Starship)|living ship]].
- Will be instantly damaged after every emergency warp.
