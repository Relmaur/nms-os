# Humboldt Drive Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Humboldt Drive
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Humboldt Drive Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Humboldt Drive Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Humboldt Drive Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Nautilon]] [[Exocraft]]'s [[Humboldt Drive]].

## Upgrade properties

Humboldt Drive Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Top Speed*- *Fuel Efficiency:* Reduces all mobility-related fuel costs
- *Boost Power*
Up to three Humboldt Drive Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Nautilon submarine --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Humboldt Drive Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Humboldt Drive Modules.

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
