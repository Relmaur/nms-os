# Forbidden Exosuit Module

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Procedural Upgrade
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Forbidden Exosuit Module** is an exosuit Upgrade Module.

## Summary

**Forbidden Exosuit Modules**, contained within [[Sentinel Exosuit Fragment|Sentinel Exosuit Fragments]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] that grant various bonuses to your [[exosuit]].

## Upgrade properties

Forbidden Exosuit Modules grant at least two to a maximum of four of the following six bonuses:
- *Shield Strength:* increases the durability of the Exosuit's shield.
- *Core Health:* adds 1 health square (the player initially has 3 health squares).
- **Life Support Tanks:** increases Life Support capacity
- **Solar Panel Power:** during the day, reduces the Life Support drain of all movement functions
- *Sprint Distance:* Sprint bonus: increases maximum sprint duration
- *Fuel Efficiency:* Jetpack bonus: decreases Jetpack's drain on [[Life Support]]

Up to three Forbidden Exosuit Modules can be installed in each inventory tab of the exosuit which can accept technology. Installing four or more upgrades of the same type in a given inventory tab will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

These Upgrades can also be installed alongside [[Salvaged Upgrade Components]].

Their stats are the same as those of the Salvaged Upgrade Components.

## Crafting

Forbidden Exosuit Modules can only be obtained from opening [[Sentinel Exosuit Fragment|Sentinel Exosuit Fragments]].

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional Information

- Core health units added by Forbidden Exosuit Modules start out depleted and will not protect the player from death until they have been replenished by a source of healing.
- Adjacency [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Forbidden Exosuit Module, [[Defence Systems Upgrade|Defence Systems Upgrades]], and [[Salvaged Upgrade Components|Salvaged Upgrade Componentss]]. If they have common stats they share such as Shield Strength, the stat bonus can be increased above listed values. This does not, however, apply adjacency bonuses to other upgrades such as Life Support and its upgrades or the Jetpack and its upgrades, despite sharing some of these stats.
- Note that extra core health granted by these modules may not be a beneficial stat late-game, as it is possible to max out with 9 core health units using only [[Defence Systems Upgrade|Defence Systems Upgrades]], [[Remembrance]], and [[Star Seed]]/[[Mark of the Denier]].  Further core health stat bonuses have no effect, so it may be better to choose a different 4th stat to pump.
