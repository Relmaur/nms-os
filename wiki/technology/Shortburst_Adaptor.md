# Shortburst Adaptor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 5
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Shortburst Adaptor** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Shortburst Adaptor** is a [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

The upgrade causes the Boltcaster to release short, rapid sequences of fire.

## Sources

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 60 × [[Copper]] + 100 × [[Heridium]] + 20 × [[Zinc]] → 1 × Shortburst Adaptor

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in Multi-tool inventory
- Needs no fuel or recharging
- Increases Magazine Size when installed

## Release history

- [[Release]] - Added as a technology.
- [[Pathfinder]] - Removed from the game. Replaced by [[Pulse Spitter]].
