# Cadmium Drive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Red Systems
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Cadmium Drive** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Cadmium Drive** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] and it is the first upgrade to the [[Hyperdrive]]. It allows warping to [[System colours|red coloured]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Game description

Upgrades the hyperdrive's core bounding magnets, preventing rapid disassembly when attempting to reach rare-class red stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on [[Space Anomaly]] for 80  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 3 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Cadmium Drive

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- Gives a bonus to hyperdrive range if the upgrade module is installed adjacent to the ship's hyperdrive or other drive modules.
