# Tamper Prevention Device

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Tamper Prevention Device** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Tamper Prevention Device** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A unit designed to destroy vital systems in the event that the device is tampered with by an unauthorised party. Unfortunately, it has itself malfunctioned and will continue to damage other systems until repaired.
