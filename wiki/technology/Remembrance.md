# Remembrance

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit - Health
- **Type:** A relic of another place
- **Updated:** Origins

**Remembrance** is an exosuit health technology.

## Summary

**Remembrance** is an exosuit health technology and its blueprint is a reward for finishing The Purge mission in the Artemis Path storyline.

Installing it grants one unit of core health and allows the player to operate Remembrance Terminals placed behind AtlasPass v2 and AtlasPass v3 doors.

## Game Description

Everything you ever knew is within.

## Crafting

Remembrance can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Heart of the Sun ×1 + Platinum ×16 → Remembrance

Remembrance cannot be repaired.

Remembrance can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Remembrance → Platinum ×8 + Heart of the Sun ×1

## Additional Information

This has an adjacency bonus with the Star Seed.

## Bugs

- Some players may have received the blueprint for Remembrance as soon as they entered the game after the Atlas Rises update.

The cause *might* be that these players had already completed The Atlas Path. However, such theory has been proven invalid in the current version of the game, at least when loading a pre-1.30 save.

- Some players may have forgotten the blueprint after first having it in their mission log. There is currently no known workaround. Re-do The Atlas Path to re-acquire the blueprint.

## Release history

- Atlas Rises - Added as a technology. It has the same icon as Atlas Stone.
- NEXT - Blueprint changed, formerly used Heart of the Sun and Nanite Clusters.
