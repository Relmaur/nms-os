# Remembrance

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Type:** A relic of another place
- **Added in:** Origins

**Remembrance** is an exosuit health technology.

## Summary

**Remembrance** is an [[exosuit]] [[health]] [[technology]] and its blueprint is a reward for finishing [[The Purge]] mission in the Artemis Path storyline.

Installing it grants one unit of core health and allows the player to operate [[Remembrance Terminal|Remembrance Terminals]] placed behind [[AtlasPass v2]] and [[AtlasPass v3]] doors.

## Game Description

Everything you ever knew is within.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Heart of the Sun]] + 16 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Remembrance

- **Dismantle** returns: 8 × [[Platinum]] + 1 × [[Heart of the Sun]]

## Additional Information

This has an [[Upgrade bonus|adjacency bonus]] with the [[Star Seed]].

## Bugs

- Some players may have received the [[blueprint]] for Remembrance as soon as they entered the game after the Atlas Rises update.

The cause *might* be that these players had already completed The Atlas Path. However, such theory has been proven invalid in the current version of the game, at least when loading a pre-[[Update 1.30|1.30]] save.

- Some players may have forgotten the blueprint after first having it in their mission log. There is currently no known workaround. Re-do The Atlas Path to re-acquire the blueprint.

## Release history

- [[Atlas Rises]] - Added as a technology. It has the same icon as [[Atlas Stone]].
- [[NEXT]] - Blueprint changed, formerly used Heart of the Sun and [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]].
