# Star Seed

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Type:** Cosmic Brand
- **Added in:** Omega

**Star Seed** is an exosuit health technology.

## Summary

**Star Seed** is an [[exosuit]] [[health]] [[technology]] that increases the player's core health by 1 unit.

## Game description

The smallest of sparks, housed within a containment field of unknown origins.

The seed glows with life, desperate to escape and fulfill its potential.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 16 × [[Platinum]] + 16 × [[Tritium]] + 16 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Star Seed

- **Repair**: 8 × [[Platinum]] + 8 × [[Tritium]] + 8 × [[Gold]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 8 × [[Platinum]] + 8 × [[Tritium]] + 8 × [[Gold]]

## Source

The blueprint is rewarded for finishing [[The Atlas Path]] mission, only if the player chose to birth a star.

## Additional Information

- [[Voyagers|As of Voyagers]] in September 2025, players who previously completed [[The Atlas Path]] before [[Atlas Rises]] won't automatically get the blueprint for the Star Seed. This means they won't benefit from the increased core health unless they redo The Atlas Path.

- As of [[Update 5.50]] the star seed’s installation allows an additional dialogue option in [[In Stellar Multitudes]].
