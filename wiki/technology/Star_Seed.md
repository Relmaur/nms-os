# Star Seed

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit - Health
- **Type:** Cosmic Brand
- **Updated:** Omega

**Star Seed** is an exosuit health technology.

## Summary

**Star Seed** is an exosuit health technology that increases the player's core health by 1 unit.

## Game description

The smallest of sparks, housed within a containment field of unknown origins.

The seed glows with life, desperate to escape and fulfill its potential.

## Crafting

Star Seed can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Platinum ×16 + Tritium ×16 + Gold ×16 → Star Seed

Star Seed can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Platinum ×8 + Tritium ×8 + Gold ×8

Star Seed can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Star Seed → Platinum ×8 + Tritium ×8 + Gold ×8

## Source

The blueprint is rewarded for finishing The Atlas Path mission, only if the player chose to birth a star.

## Additional Information

- As of Voyagers in September 2025, players who previously completed The Atlas Path before Atlas Rises won't automatically get the blueprint for the Star Seed. This means they won't benefit from the increased core health unless they redo The Atlas Path.
- As of Update 5.50 the star seed’s installation allows an additional dialogue option in In Stellar Multitudes.
