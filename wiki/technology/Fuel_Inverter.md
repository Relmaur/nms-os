# Fuel Inverter

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Chargable Power Unit
- **Recharged with:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon
- **Updated:** NEXT

**Fuel Inverter** is a power technology.

## Summary

**Fuel Inverter** is a power technology installed in various products to power them and it requires 100 Carbon or 34 Condensed Carbon to be fully charged.

## Game description

A piece of rechargeable power technology. The fuel inverter ensures an even flow of power to autonomous machines.

## Products

The following products are equipped with Fuel Inverter:

- Atmosphere Harvester
- Autonomous Mining Unit
- Portable Refiner

## Release History

- NEXT - added to the game
- Desolation - recharging values changed, used to be 150/50
