# Fuel Inverter

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Chargable Power Unit
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon
- **Added in:** NEXT

**Fuel Inverter** is a power technology.

## Summary

**Fuel Inverter** is a power [[technology]] installed in various [[product|products]] to power them and it requires 100 [[Carbon]] or 34 [[Condensed Carbon]] to be fully charged.

## Game description

A piece of rechargeable power technology. The fuel inverter ensures an even flow of power to autonomous machines.

## Products

The following products are equipped with Fuel Inverter:
- [[Atmosphere Harvester]]
- [[Autonomous Mining Unit]]
- [[Portable Refiner]]
