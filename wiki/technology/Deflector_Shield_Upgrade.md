# Deflector Shield Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Ship
- **Subcategory:** Shield
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Deflector Shield Upgrade** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Deflector Shield Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Starship Shield Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] granting bonuses to the [[Deflector Shield]] of the [[starship]].

## Upgrade properties

Deflector Shield Upgrades grant a varying bonus based on their class:
- *Shield Strength:* Increases the Shield Absorption, indirectly increasing the Deflector Shield's durability for each upgrade.

It is controversial to install B-Class shield upgrades, as they give a shield bonus that is no better than a C-Class upgrade. The only difference between the C and B classes is that the B-class upgrade gives a higher chance of obtaining a better percentage within their combined percentage range than the C-class upgrade. A-class upgrades, however, have a very wide percentage range to choose from, and occasionally can be as good as an S-class upgrade.

Up to three Deflector Shield Upgrades can be installed in each inventory tab of the starship. Installing four or more upgrades in a given inventory tab will disable all upgrades of that type on that tab until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Deflector Shield Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Starship Shield Modules.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 50 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 50 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
