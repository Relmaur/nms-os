# Rapidfire Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the boltcaster's rate-of-fire
- **Stats:** Fire Rate = +39% Recoil = +60%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Rapidfire Theta** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Rapidfire Theta** is a third-level upgrade for the [[Boltcaster]]. Improved charge functions allow user to take repeated shots in fast succession.

## Game Description

Provides shortest cool-down times possible between Boltcaster shots, and simultaneously increases internal plasma production speeds.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[multi-tool]] inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Aluminium]] + 200 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Rapidfire Theta

## Upgrade progression

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's Multi-tool inventory with the Boltcaster
- Needs no fuel or recharging
