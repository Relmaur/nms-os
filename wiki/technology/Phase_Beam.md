# Phase Beam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Starship Energy Weapon
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Phase Beam** is a starship weapon.

## Summary

**Phase Beam** is one of the primary [[starship]] weapons, and is an alternative to the more common [[Photon Cannon]]. Both can be used to mine [[asteroid|asteroids]] in space, which hold valuable resources.

## Game description

A high-energy beam weapon for ship-to-ship combat.

Though vulnerable to overheating, these highly accurate laser weapons can recharge the users's shields by leeching energy from target shield systems.

## Combat Properties

Phase Beam has an unusually long range for a starship weapon, having the ability to hit targets from up to 3000 distance units (3000u) away (for reference, fugitive [[pirate]] ships will attack the player if they get within 1500u). By default, the weapon deals damage over time to targets at a tick rate of 5 damage ticks per second, overheating after 10 seconds of fire whether that fire is done contiguously or in short bursts. Upon overheating, Phase Beam takes 2 seconds by default to recover from overheating.

As the visuals suggest, Phase Beams are a hit-scan weapon, but with no lock-on potential, making it heavily reliant on HUD's built-in aim assist.

Phase Beam deals 180 base damage per tick (pairs of ticks are visually combined to display 360 base damage per 0.4 seconds) to the following targets: [[fauna]], starships, [[Space Fleets]], and destructible obstacles such as Reinforced Doors ([[Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]]). Against starships in particular, the starship weapon does *not* deal any bonus damage to the rear (that is, it deals 1x damage to the rear).

Phase Beam deals 36 base damage per tick (pairs of ticks are visually combined to display 72 base damage per 0.4 seconds) to all planetary [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

## Mining Properties

When used to harvest a planet's [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]], Phase Beam has a base mining power per tick of 36 (pairs of mining damage ticks are visually combined to display 72 per 0.4 seconds). Update: It seems that the mining properties for metals on planets (like copper, gold, etc.) was removed recently.

## Pre-order bonus

The pre-order starships [[Alpha Vector]] and [[Horizon Omega]] come equipped with the Phase Beam whereas the regular starting ships need to craft it.
