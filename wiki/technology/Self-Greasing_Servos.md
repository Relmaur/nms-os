# Self-Greasing Servos

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Motor Efficiency Upgrade
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Self-Greasing Servos** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Self-Greasing Servos** is a fuel economy upgrade for [[Minotaur]].

## Game description

An automatically-regulated lubricant dispenser fitted to the leg joints of the Minotaur reduce friction and encourage fuel-efficient motion.

Significantly reduces fuel burn in the Minotaur's [[Daedalus Engine]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Acid]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Self-Greasing Servos

- **Dismantle** returns: 20 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]]

**Note:** Self-Greasing Servos cannot be dismantled due to a bug that makes it a duplicate main engine, even though this is not the case.
