# Warp Reactor Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Level:** 3
- **Description:** Increases length of Hyperdrive operation.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Warp Reactor Theta** is a level 3 [[hyperdrive technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Hyperdrive]].

## Summary

The Warp Reactor improves the Hyperdrives capabilities, producing higher warp speeds and increasing the distance that can be traveled by 400ly.

When placed adjacent to Hyperdrive or other technologies that align with Hyperdrive technology, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].

## In-game description

Uncapped, yet stabilized, fusion systems are threaded into user's hyperdrive. Allows Starship to jump ever longer distances in deep space. User discomfort through intense thermal distribution also limited through integration with craft's existing coolant systems. User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems through Galactic Map.

## How to acquire

- The [[blueprint]] for this technology is given to the player by [[Polo]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 3 × [[Dynamic Resonator]] + 1000 × [[Gold]] + 1000 × [[Solanium]] → 1 × Warp Reactor Theta

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's Starship inventory.
- Still consumes 20% of Warp Cell fuel
- Required for warp to **Class B Star System** (blueish white stars)
- Required for warp to **Class O Star System**  (blue stars)
