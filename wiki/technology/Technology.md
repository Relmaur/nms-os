# Technology

- This article is about the current version of Technology. For the Atlas Rises version, see Technology (Atlas).

**Technology** is used for upgrading player equipment.

## Summary

**Technology** is an item that upgrades a player's exosuit, multi-tool, exocraft, and starship. You learn how to install technologies from blueprints.

When installing and upgrading technology, there are specific domains that can be upgraded. Your current stats are shown on the right hand side of the panel of your multi-tool or starship (stats for the exosuit are not displayed in-game).

- In starships, you upgrade Damage Potential, Shield Absorb, Hyperdrive Range, and Maneuverability.
- For Multi-tools, you upgrade Damage Potential and Scanner Range.
- For the Exosuit you can upgrade Life Support, Hazard Protection, Movement, and Gloves.

How to use technologies:

- Loot, buy or otherwise learn blueprints.
- Craft Technologies and install them into slots (general slots or technology slots).
- Optionally buy Upgrade Modules and install them.
- If needed, dismantle Technologies and re-craft them in a different slot.

## Installation and Dismantle

Can only be installed in the "Technology" slot of the item case.
Technology Blueprint can be learned in the Space Anomaly. Then you can create them yourself. After learning them, select a technology slot and start the installation. When all the materials are inserted, the installation is complete. They cannot be installed unless there is an empty "technology" slot.

Each device in the technology slot can be dismantled by placing the cursor over it and holding down the delete button. Dismantling it will give you items. The breakdown of items obtained by dismatling is the same as items required for repair. Essential technologies and special equipment cannot be decomposed.

## Technology Damage and Repair

Technology will be critically damaged if it receives any damage or exceeds the Galactic Core. The likelihood of technology breaking down depends on the dfficulty setting: Technology Damage and the current shield remaining. They will break even if the shield is not exhausted.

The priority of what is likely to break depends on what has been damaged. For example, if you are attacked in battle, technology related to "Shields" will be more likely to break, and if you receive fall damage from a high altitude, technology related to "Jetpacks" will be more likely to break.

Broken technologies can be divided into those that do not function when broken and those that continue to function even when broken but are weakened. Those that do not function when broken are Normal Technology. Those that continue to function even when broken but are weakened are Upgrade Module and Core Technology. Performance is weakened only while broken, and returns to original performance after repair.

Broken technologies can be repaired with resources, items, and Repair Kit. The type and number of items needed for repair are the same as those that appear when dismantled. By using one Repair Kit, you can repair one slot. If there is something that is difficult to obtain in the repair area, use them instead.

The last remaining weapon installed on the spaceship will no longer break down in v4.2 Interceptor. This was mentioned again in the patch notes for v4.4 Echoes. In addition to the above, if multiple weapons are installed, the Rocket Launcher will no longer be selected as the last weapon when weapons break.

## Technology Storage

From v4.0 Waypoint onwards, it has become possible to "store" equipped technology in the item case while it is still in its completed state. Before that, you had to create and procure it again every time you changed Starship or Multi-Tool. After v4.0, it became possible to move and reuse technology by removing what was equipped on A and putting it on B. Core Technology cannot be saved.

If you hover the cursor over an installed device and select "Store Technology", it will change to an item with the name "<Technology Name> Package". The installed materials and status of its contents will be retained. By using this item, you can reinstall the device with the same status as before it was saved.
