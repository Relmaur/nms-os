# Photon Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Companions

**Photon Cannon Upgrade** is a starship technology Upgrade Module.

## Summary

**Photon Cannon Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Photon Cannon Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] granting bonuses to the [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

## Upgrade properties

Photon Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Heat Dispersion*: allows to fire the weapon continuously for longer before it overheats.
- *Fire Rate*:  decreases the time between each shot.
- *Damage:* increases the damage of each shot. Bug: S-Class displayed damage bonus is 6-8%, which lower than the bonus used in combat calculations: 20-28%.

Up to three Photon Cannon Upgrades can be installed in each inventory tab of the starship. Installing four or more upgrades in a given inventory tab will disable all upgrades of that type on that tab until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Photon Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Photon Cannon Modules.

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
