# Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrade** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Infra-Knife Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to the [[starship]]'s [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]].

## Upgrade Properties

Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Heat Dispersion*- *Fire Rate*- *Damage*

Up to three Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrades can be installed in each inventory of the starship. Installing four or more upgrades in a given inventory will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Infra-Knife Accelerator Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Infra-Knife Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Silver]] + 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Silver]] + 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
