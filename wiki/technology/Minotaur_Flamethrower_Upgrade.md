# Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur Flamethrower
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Minotaur Flamethrower Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Minotaur]]'s [[Liquidator Right Arm]] .

## Upgrade properties

Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Damage*
- *Weapon Power Efficiency*
- *Impact Fire Duration*

Up to three Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Minotaur; installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Minotaur Flamethrower Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Minotaur Flamethrower Modules.

- **Repair**: 80 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 80 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
