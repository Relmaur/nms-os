# Phase Coolant Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Phase Beam Companion Unit
- **Description:** Reduces Phase Beam cool-down times
- **Stats:** Heat Dispersion = +50%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Phase Coolant Sigma** is [[weapons technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Phase Beam]].

## Summary

Phase Coolant Sigma reduces Phase Beam laser cool-down times. It is the first of three upgrade levels.

## Game description

Phase beam laser cool-down times are significantly reduced.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 2 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] + 200 × [[Thamium9]] + 25 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Phase Coolant Sigma

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
