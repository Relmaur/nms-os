# Mark of the Denier

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit - Health
- **Type:** Cosmic Brand
- **Recharged with:** Nanite Cluster
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Mark of the Denier** is a technology.

## Summary

**Mark of the Denier** is an Exosuit technology that is the opposite to the Star Seed. The mark does not give you anything.

## Game Description

A tiny mote of stardust, no more than a speck. On closer examination, its surface is all crystalline shards and distorted crimson geometry.

It flickers with pent-up energy, demanding to be free.

Empower the mark as you discover systems, planets and objects across the universe.

## Crafting

Mark of the Denier can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Tritium ×16 + Gold ×16 + Platinum ×16 → Mark of the Denier

## Source

They can be acquired by completing The Atlas Path, but instead of birth a new star, you need to choose to walk away. After you do so, you will be given a chance to reconsider. If you reconsider, you won't earn this blueprint. The correct choice is to walk away (when given the chance).

## Recharge

Unlike other technologies, this requires earning Nanite Clusters to recharge.
