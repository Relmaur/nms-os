# Nonlinear Optics

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Upgrade
- **Stats:** +21% Heat Dispersion
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Nonlinear Optics** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Nonlinear Optics** is [[technology]] that augments the [[Photon Cannon]], a starship weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Photon Cannon Upgrade|Photon Cannon Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game description

Replaces the conventional Photon Cannon optics with an experimental glass array, boosting combat performance.

Significantly improves heat sink capacity.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 100 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Nonlinear Optics

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 50 × [[Tritium]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 50 × [[Tritium]]
