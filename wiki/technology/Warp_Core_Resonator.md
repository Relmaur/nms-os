# Warp Core Resonator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 200 ly
- **Added in:** Origins

**Warp Core Resonator** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Warp Core Resonator** is a level 1 [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be traveled by a freighter.

## Game description

An augmentation for the colossal hyperdrive engine of an interstellar freighter.

Quantum resonation  improves the robustness of the drive during FTL travel, granting the ability to leap to stars previously out of reach.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for four [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 4 × [[Antimatter Housing]] → 1 × Warp Core Resonator

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Antimatter Housing]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
