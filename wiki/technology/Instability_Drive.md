# Instability Drive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Interplanetary
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Pulse Engine Upgrade
- **Added in:** Synthesis

**Instability Drive** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Instability Drive** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] that increases [[Pulse Engine]] fuel efficiency.

## Game description

A highly advanced upgrade for the Pulse Engine, vastly increasing fuel efficiency.

Tritium supplies are redirected into a cycle of miniature black holes. Resulting micro-gravity fluctuations are harnessed to boost starship propulsion beyond conventional combustion-based approaches.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on board [[Space Anomaly]] for 460  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Cell]] + 3 × [[Tritium Hypercluster]] + 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Instability Drive

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Warp Cell]] + 2 × [[Tritium Hypercluster]] + 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Tritium Hypercluster]] + 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]]

## Additional information

- Gives a bonus to the speed and maneuverability if the upgrade is installed next to the Pulse Engine or other [[Pulse Engine Upgrade|Pulse Engine Upgrades]].
