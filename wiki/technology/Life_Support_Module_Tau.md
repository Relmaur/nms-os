# Life Support Module Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the capacity of a user's Life Support systems
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Life Support Module Tau** is a second-level upgrade for the [[Life support|Life Support System]].

## Summary

Life Support Modules increase the capacity of a user's [[Life support|Life Support Systems]], allowing for extended periods of operation.

## Game description

Increases available energy within user's Life Support systems.

Allows for extended periods of planetary exploration.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 150 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Life Support Module Tau

## Upgrade progression

- [[Life Support Module Sigma]]
- Life Support Module Tau
- Life Support Module Theta

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's [[Exosuit]] inventory alongside the existing [[Life support|Life Support]] unit.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
