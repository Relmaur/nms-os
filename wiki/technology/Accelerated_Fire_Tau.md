# Accelerated Fire Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the photon cannon's rate of fire
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Accelerated Fire Tau** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

The **Accelerated Fire Tau** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

This upgrade modifies the Photon Cannon's rate of fire, increasing its potential for target damage.

## Game description

Photon Cannon fires at an increased rate.

Rapid fire modification that increases potential of damage, death, and destruction on user's chosen target.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Nickel]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Accelerated Fire Tau
