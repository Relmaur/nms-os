# Exceptional Intuition VII

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Crack Nose
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

## Summary

**Exceptional Intuition VII** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is a light marble color, with a pink crystalline barrel grip, and dark veins throughout the body. This tool has claw extensions on the butt, and all operating lights are green.

## Acquirement

The Exceptional Intuition VII is a [[Vy'keen]] multi-tool discovered by player *Edgarsoft*.
