# Combat Amplifier Omega

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Mining Beam
- **Level:** 4
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Combat Amplifier Omega** is multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Combat Amplifier Omega** is the fourth-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Offensive capabilities of the [[Mining Beam]] are upgraded to their maximum potential.

User will find target survival rates diminished, allowing user to return to non-violent planetary exploration at faster rates.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 400 × [[Heridium]] + 200 × [[Iron]] + 100 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Combat Amplifier Omega
