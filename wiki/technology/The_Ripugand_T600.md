# The Ripugand T600

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Release

## Summary

**The Ripugand T600** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is peach, teal, and gray in color with white and orange strips on the barrel.

## Acquirement

The Ripugand T600 was found in a [[Space Anomaly]] in the [[Calypso]] galaxy. It was offered at a purchase price of 186,975 units.
