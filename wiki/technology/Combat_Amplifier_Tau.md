# Combat Amplifier Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Mining Beam
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Combat Amplifer Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Combat Amplifier Tau** is a second-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Further increases damage of Mining Beam laser during combat.

Focuses laser optics to allow for more effective beam damage during moments of violence.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's Multi-tool inventory.

Stats Damage +1.0
