# Minotaur Cannon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Heavy Mounted Cannon
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Minotaur Cannon** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Minotaur Cannon** is a [[Minotaur]] weapon which lobs explosives toward targets. It shoots two explosives per second and must periodically be recharged with [[Carbon]] (0.5%/unit), [[Condensed Carbon]] (1.5%/unit) or [[Oxygen]] (1%/unit).

## Game description

A mounted cannon specifically designed for the Minotaur Heavy Exosuit Hybrid.

Designed primarily for damage against biological entities and inefficient when deployed as a mining device.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 200 × [[Projectile Ammunition]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Minotaur Cannon

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Pugneum]] + 100 × [[Projectile Ammunition]]

## Additional Info

For any save editors that want to increase the actual fire rate of the cannon, duplicate copies of the Exocraft Mounted Cannon in the same exocraft increase the rate of fire indirectly due to an adjacency glitch similar to that of the Photon Cannon and Spewing Vents.
