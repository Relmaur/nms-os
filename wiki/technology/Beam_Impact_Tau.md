# Beam Impact Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Strengthens Starship weapons.
- **Stats:** Damage = +70.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Beam Impact Tau** is an upgrade for a starship's [[Phase Beam]].

## Summary

The **Beam Impact Tau** upgrade improves the [[Phase Beam]]'s capabilities, increasing it's ability to destroy ships and [[asteroid|asteroids]] rapidly.

## Game description

Phase Beam destroys [[starship]] and asteroid targets at an increased rate.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 200 × [[Thamium9]] + 50 × [[Copper]] + 50 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Beam Impact Tau
