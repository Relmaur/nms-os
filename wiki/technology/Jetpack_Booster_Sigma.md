# Jetpack Booster Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Aerial Propulsion Companion Unit
- **Description:** Allows the Jetpack to ascend higher
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Jetpack Booster Sigma** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

The **Jetpack Booster Sigma** is a first-level [[utilities technology]] upgrade for the [[exosuit]] [[Jetpack]].

It provides additional propulsion for the Jetpack. This is a level one upgrade.

## Game description

Jetpack is granted extended bursts of propulsion, allowing user ascent to higher locations.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's exosuit inventory, alongside existing Jetpack unit.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 1 × [[Carite Sheet]] + 15 × [[Platinum]] + 10 × [[Zinc]] → 1 × Jetpack Booster Sigma

## Additional Information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- Adds roughly 1 second of boost.
