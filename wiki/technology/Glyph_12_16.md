# Glyph 12 / 16

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Ancient Technology
- **Recharge:** 50 Di-hydrogen,, 50 Deuterium,, 50 Cobalt,, 13 Ionised Cobalt,, 50 Salt
- **Added in:** Visions

**Glyph 12 / 16** is glyph.

## Summary

**Glyph 12 / 16** is a [[glyph]] that can be found on [[Portal|Portals]] and [[Portal Terminal|Portal Terminals]]. It needs to be charged with [[earth elements]] before it can be used.

## Common Names

This glyph can be referred to as either Fish or [[Grave of the Ocean King|Ocean King]].

## Additional information

- It can be acquired from [[The Purge]] primary mission or from a [[Traveller Grave]].
