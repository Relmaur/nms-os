# Forbidden Multi-Tool Module

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-Tool
- **Type:** Procedural Upgrade
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Forbidden Multi-Tool Module** is an upgrade module.

## Summary

**Forbidden Multi-Tool Module**, contained within [[Sentinel Weapons Shard|Sentinel Weapons Shards]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] granting various bonuses to your [[multi-tool]].

## Upgrade Properties

Forbidden Multi-Tool Modules can grant two to a maximum of three of the following bonuses, which **apply only to the Boltcaster**:
- *Damage:* increases the damage of each shot.
- *Fire Rate:* decreases the time between each shot in a single burst.
- *Reload Time:* decreases the time to reload a new clip.

### Upgrade Modules

|   | Stats Drawn | Module<br>Improves | Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Forbidden Multi-Tool<br>Module** | 2-3 | *Damage* | +1% | +2% |
| *Fire Rate* | +1% | +20% |   |   |
| *Reload Time* | -1% | -15% |   |   |

Up to three Forbidden Multi-Tool Modules can be installed in each multi-tool. Installing four or more upgrades of the same type will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Forbidden Multi-Tool Modules can only be obtained from opening Sentinel Weapons Shards.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Pugneum]] + 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Pugneum]] + 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]]

## Additional Information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Forbidden Multi-Tool Modules and/or [[Boltcaster Upgrade|Boltcaster Upgrades]] if and only if they have common stats they share in adjacency such as Fire Rate bonuses. This does not, however, apply adjacency bonuses to other upgrades such as the [[Pulse Spitter]] and its upgrades or the [[Scatter Blaster]] and its upgrades, and nor do the stat bonuses - they exclusively affect the Boltcaster.
- The game files list the maximum amount of drawn stats as four, despite there only being three possible stats.
