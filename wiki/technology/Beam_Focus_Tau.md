# Beam Focus Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Mining Beam Companion Unit
- **Description:** Faster Mining Beam processes on resource-rich materials.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Beam Focus Tau** is a technology upgrade.

## Summary

The **Beam Focus Tau** is a second-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Faster Mining Beam processes on resource-rich materials. User will enjoy quicker element extraction from crystals, rocky outcrops, and planetary vegetation. Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 100 × [[Chrysonite]] → 1 × Beam Focus Tau

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- This upgrade replaced [[Beam Focus V2]] from the initial [[Release]] era.
