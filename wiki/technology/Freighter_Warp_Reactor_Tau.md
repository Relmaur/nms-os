# Freighter Warp Reactor Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 300 ly
- **Added in:** Visions

**Freighter Warp Reactor Tau** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Freighter Warp Reactor Tau** is a level 2 [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be travelled by a freighter.

## Game description

Hyperdrive benefits from advanced fusion that brings starship to higher warp speed and intensity. User's craft will become able to leap deeper into space, and into more distant planetary systems.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from a [[Technology Merchant]] for 400  Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 250 × [[Emeril]] + 1 × [[Sodium Diode]] + 5 × [[Antimatter Housing]] → 1 × Freighter Warp Reactor Tau

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
