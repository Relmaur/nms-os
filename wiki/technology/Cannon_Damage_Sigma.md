# Cannon Damage Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Cannon Damage Sigma** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Cannon Damage Sigma** is the first-level [[weapons technology]] upgrade for the [[starship]] [[Photon Cannon]].

### Note

The Cannon Damage upgrades appear to have no impact on rate of fire. The description appears to be incorrect, and may be an accidental inclusion of text data from the [[Accelerated Fire Sigma]] panel.
Most likely, this does what the **title** says it does; increase the per shot damage output.

## Summary

The Cannon Damage upgrade increases the damage of the Photon Cannon.

## In-Game Description

Photon Cannon fires at an increased rate.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Iron]] + 50 × [[Thamium9]] → 1 × Cannon Damage Sigma

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
