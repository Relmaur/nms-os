# Polyphonic Core

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-Tool
- **Subcategory:** Scanner
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Autophage Resonance Key
- **Description:** Reveals Autophage camps, NPCs, and sentience echoes.
- **Stats:** Echo Resonator: Enabled, +11% Scan Radius
- **Recharge:** Atlantideum
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Polyphonic Core** is a technology.

## Summary

**Polyphonic Core** is a [[Multi-Tool]] [[technology]] upgrade for the [[Scanner]]. It reveals nearby sentience echo sites, cloaked [[Autophage]] NPCs, locates [[Autophage Camp|Autophage Camps]], and gives +11% to scan radius.

To locate camps, the Polyphonic Core must be activated from the Multi-Tool technology inventory. It must be fully charged to use this ability.

While using the [[Analysis Visor]] on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]], an icon will appear for sentience echoes within scanner range. Once the site is reached, initiating a local scan pulse will reveal the sentience echo, which can then be mined to obtain an [[Echo Seed]].

A scan pulse at [[Autophage Camp|Autophage Camps]] and [[Harmonic Camp|Harmonic Camps]] will also reveal cloaked Autophage NPCs.

## Game Description

An attachment for the Multi-Tool Scanner that allows the device to detect and decode the cloaking signal of nearby Autophage units.

Use (//) (PC/PS/XB) to activate the Scanner. As its detector waveform passes over the local area, it will automatically reveal any nearby Autophage entities.

In addition, this device can be used to locate Autophage settlements. Use Locate Autophage Camp to pinpoint a planetary gathering of Autophage.

## Source

The blueprint is given in the [[They Who Returned|Autophage questline]] for free. A Polyphonic Core is also included on any [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Staff Multi-Tool]] crafted at [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal|Autophage Synthesis Terminals]] and [[Harmonic Interface|Harmonic Interfaces]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 190 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]] → 1 × Polyphonic Core

- **Repair**: 95 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 95 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]]

## Recharge

This scanner technology can be recharged using [[Atlantideum]] - 2.5%/unit

## Additional Information

- The [[Scan Harmoniser]] provides the same uncloaking ability and stat increase. However, it can't locate Autophage Camps.
- This technology does not count toward the 3-upgrade limit.
