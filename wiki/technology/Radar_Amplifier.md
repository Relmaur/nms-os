# Radar Amplifier

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Communications
- **Stats:** +100% Scan
- **Description:** Exocraft Signal Detection Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Radar Amplifier** is an Exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Radar Amplifier** is a level-1 upgrade to the standard [[Exocraft Radar]], or subspace radar. The radar amplifier will not operate unless the basic radar is also installed.

It can be used with the [[Quick Menu|quick menu]] to scan for additional points of interest, including [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]] and [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]] without the use of Navigational Charts of any kind.

## Game description

Allows for additional building types to be acquired by the Exocraft Radar.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × Radar Amplifier

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Microprocessor]]
