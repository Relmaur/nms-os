# Interstellar Scanner

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Scanner
- **Type:** Freighter Technology
- **Added in:** Endurance

## Summary

The **Interstellar Scanner** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that allows remote scanning of star systems.

## Game description

An upgrade to the freighter's galactic-scale sensors.

This device allows the user to access detailed economic and conflict data for a system, even without having previously visited.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for four [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 160 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 70 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Interstellar Scanner

- **Dismantle** returns: 80 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 35 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Release history

- [[Endurance]] - Added as a technology that can be researched at the Freighter Research Terminal.
