# Protection technology

**Protection technology** is technology that upgrades a player's exosuit. There are four types of Hazard Protections: Thermic Layer (coldness), Coolant Network (overheat), Radiation Deflector (Radiation) and Toxin Supressor (Toxication).

## Technologies

- Cold Protection Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)
- Thermic Layer
- Heat Protection Coolant Network
- Thermal Protection Module (Heat)
- Radiation Protection Radiation Deflector
- Radiation Protection Upgrade
- Toxication Protection Toxin Suppressor
- Toxic Protection Upgrade
