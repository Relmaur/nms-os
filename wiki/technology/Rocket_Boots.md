# Rocket Boots

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Movement
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Exosuit Upgrade
- **Stats:** Advanced Jump System: Enabled
- **Added in:** Origins

**Rocket Boots** is an exosuit technology.

## Summary

**Rocket Boots** is a [[technology]] that allows a player to fire their [[jetpack]] rapidly in effort to fly a greater distance and increase flight time.

It needs to be installed in an [[exosuit]] [[inventory]] slot for it to take effect.

## Game Description

Equips the Exosuit jetpack with advanced jump capabilities, allowing the user to take huge leaps with ease.

Activate jump systems by rapidly pressing // (PS4/PC/Xbox).

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from a [[Technology Merchant]] for 200  Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Tritium]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] → 1 × Rocket Boots

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Tritium]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Tritium]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger if Rocket Boots are adjacent to [[Neural Stimulator]] or [[Movement System Upgrade|Movement System Upgrades]], but the technology is not actually affected by these bonuses.
  - Rocket Boots does not count toward the three-upgrade limit; however, only one Rocket Boots technology can be installed at a time.
