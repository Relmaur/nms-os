# Liquidator Legs

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Stats:** Fuel Usage: -11%, Boost Tank Size: +25%
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Liquidator Legs ** is a technology.

## Summary

**Liquidator Legs** is a limb replacement which can be mounted on a [[Minotaur]].

An adjacency bonus is gained when installed next to the Minotaur's [[Daedalus Engine]].

## Game description

An upgrade to the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid, the Liquidator is a cutting-edge heavy duty combat unit, designed to operate in the harshest of environments.

Installation will significantly improve the Minotaur's engine and mobility systems.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Quad Servo]] + 2 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] → 1 × Liquidator Legs

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Quad Servo]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Quad Servo]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

## Additional Information

There is an adjacency bonus with the [[Hardframe Legs]], but the effect is unknown.
