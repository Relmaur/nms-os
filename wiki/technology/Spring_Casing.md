# Spring Casing

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Spring Casing** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Spring Casing** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]]. Fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The casing for a high-compression spring. The casing has ruptured, causing the spring to shoot out and damage the rest of the device.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 15 × [[Silicate Powder]]
