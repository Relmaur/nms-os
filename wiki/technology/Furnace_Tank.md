# Furnace Tank

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Fuel Incineration Device
- **Recharged with:** Organic elements
- **Updated:** Crossplay

**Furnace Tank** is a power technology.

## Summary

**Furnace Tank** is a power technology installed in Biofuel Reactors to power them.

## Game description

High-temperature burn unit, designed to consume a wide range of biological material. Insert materials to initiate burn.

When active, drives an internal turbine to generate **power**.

## Products

The following products are equipped with Furnace Tank:

- Biofuel Reactor
