# Mineral Processing Rig

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Colossus
- **Type:** Colossus Large Refiner
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

The **Mineral Processing Rig** is an [[Exocraft]] [[technology]] upgrade.

## Summary

The Mineral Processing Rig provides [[Large Refiner]] capabilities to the [[Colossus]], allowing it to refine resources on the move. However, much like the [[Personal Refiner]], it requires its own separate fuel source.

## Game description

A large and powerful refiner unit, designed to be housed within the belly of a Colossus Exocraft.

The unit is able to process the most complex recipes, but requires its own independent fuel source.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 2 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 4 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × Mineral Processing Rig

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]]
