# Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Mining Laser
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Exocraft Laser Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to an [[Exocraft]]'s [[Exocraft Mining Laser|Mining Laser]].

## Upgrade properties

Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Mining Laser Efficiency:* Reduces charge cost
- *Mining Laser Power:* Increases damage against all targets

Up to three Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of an Exocraft other than the [[Nautilon]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less. **Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrades cannot be installed within the Nautilon's inventory** (currently, there are no procedurally generated upgrades which are able to augment the [[Tethys Beam]], the Nautilon's equivalent to the Exocraft Mining Laser).

## Crafting

Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Exocraft Laser Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
