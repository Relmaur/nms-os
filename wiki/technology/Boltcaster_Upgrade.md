# Boltcaster Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Boltcaster Upgrade** is a [[projectile technology]].

## Summary

**Boltcaster Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Boltcaster Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]] granting bonuses to the [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Boltcaster]].

## Upgrade Properties

Boltcaster Upgrades grant up to four of the following bonuses:
- *Clip Size:* extends the capacity of the Boltcaster's clip, allowing to fire for longer before a reload is needed.
- *Damage:* increases the damage of each shot.
- *Fire Rate:* decreases the time between each shot in a single burst.
- *Reload Time:* decreases the time to reload a new clip into the Boltcaster.
- *Burst Cooldown*: decreases the time between each burst of shots. (added in [[Desolation]])
- *Shots Per Burst*: increases the number of shots per burst. (added in **Desolation**)
- *Ricochet Module:* causes missed shots to bounce off of surfaces they do not damage. (pre-**Desolation**)

Up to three Boltcaster Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of the Multi-tool. Installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Boltcaster Upgrades can only be obtained by installing Boltcaster Modules.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional Information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Boltcaster Upgrades if they are adjacent to Boltcaster, [[Boltcaster Ricochet Module]], [[Barrel Ioniser]], [[Boltcaster SM]], [[Forbidden Multi-Tool Module|Forbidden Multi-Tool Modules,]] or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- Boltcaster shots affected by the Ricochet Module bonus are unable to damage reinforced doors, supply depots or [[Sentinel Walker]] torsos on direct hits. (It is not yet known whether shots are able to damage armored targets after a bounce.)
