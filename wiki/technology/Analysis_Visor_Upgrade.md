# Analysis Visor Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Analysis Visor
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Analysis Visor Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Analysis Visor Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Scanner Modules]], are [[Procedural generation|procedurally generated]] upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Analysis Visor]] and [[Scanner]].

## Upgrade properties

Up to three **Analysis Visor Upgrades** can be installed in the inventory of a Multi-tool. Installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

Analysis Visor Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- **Scan Radius:** increases the scanning range of quick scan.
- **Flora Analysis Rewards:** increases [[Units]] received from scanning [[flora]].
- **Fauna Analysis Rewards:** increases Units received from scanning [[fauna]].
- **Mineral Analysis Rewards:** increases [[Units]] received from scanning [[mineral]]s.

## Crafting

Analysis Visor Upgrades can only be obtained by opening Scanner Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Analysis Visor Upgrades if they are adjacent to [[Scanner]], [[Waveform Recycler]] or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- Even though it is technically possible to extend scan radius by a considerable amount with multiple upgrades, the hard limit to the overall scan radius is currently confirmed to be at 750 u, regardless of the effects of other upgrades.
