# Generator Coupling

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Chargable Power Unit
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon
- **Added in:** Companions

**Generator Coupling** is a power technology.

## Summary

**Generator Coupling** is a power [[technology]] installed in various [[product|products]] to power them.

It requires 100 [[Carbon]] or 37 [[Condensed Carbon]] to be fully charged.

## Game description

The generator coupling is an important piece of the machine's rechargeable power systems. Without it, the generator would fruitlessly spin, pumping power out into the void.

## Products

The following products are equipped with Generator Coupling:
- [[Antimatter Reactor]]
- [[Oxygen Harvester]]
