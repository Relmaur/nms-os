# Impact Damage Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Stats:** Damage = +3.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Impact Damage Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Impact Damage Sigma** is a first-level [[projectile technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Boltcaster]].

It automatically increases the damage caused by Boltcaster shots.

## Game description

Boltcaster shots deliver added physical damage upon impact. Grants additional kinetic energy to the user's ionised plasma distribution system.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Iridium]] + 50 × [[Iron]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Impact Damage Sigma

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- This upgrade shares the same crafting requirements as **Boltcaster SM** but they are not the same. The former is a regular upgrade, while the latter was provided as a bonus to players that pre-ordered the game.
