# Mass Accelerator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Blaze Javelin Upgrade
- **Stats:** +20% Damage
- **Added in:** Origins

**Mass Accelerator** is an upgrade for the Blaze Javelin.

## Summary

**Mass Accelerator** is a [[Multi-tool]] upgrade which increases the damage of the [[Blaze Javelin]] at full charge (and, by extension, at partial charge as well). The blueprint can be purchased in [[Space Anomaly]].

Mass Accelerator needs to be crafted and then stored in a Multi-tool [[inventory]] slot for it to have its effect. Unlike [[Blaze Javelin Upgrade|Blaze Javelin Upgrades]], this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for enhancing [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] on other upgrades.

## Game Description

Extends the firing chamber of the Blaze Javelin, allowing the internal particle accelerators to reach both greater mass and speed.

Significantly improves weapon damage.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Mass Accelerator

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
