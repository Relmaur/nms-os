# The Rulsefren-Terq D600

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Crack Nose
- **Description:** provides 24 slots
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**The Rulsefren-Terq D600** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It varies in color from bright green at the muzzle to khaki green at the butt, with claw extensions on the butt and a crack-nose muzzle. All operating lights are green.

This tool is [[Vy'keen]] in origin.

## Acquirement

This 24-slot multi-tool was owned by player *rvlahn1978*.
