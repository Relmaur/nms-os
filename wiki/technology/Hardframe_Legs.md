# Hardframe Legs

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Stats:** Fuel Usage: -11%, Boost Tank Size: +25%
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Hardframe Legs** is a technology.

## Summary

**Hardframe Legs** is a weapon which can be mounted on a [[Minotaur]].

An adjacency bonus is gained when installed next to the Minotaur's [[Daedalus Engine]].

## Game description

A collection of salvaged components from a [[Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech|Sentinel Hardframe]], reconditioned to fit the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid.

Installation will significantly improve the Minotaur's engine and mobility systems.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 120 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hardframe Engine]] → 1 × Hardframe Legs

- **Repair**: 60 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hardframe Engine]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 60 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hardframe Engine]]

## Additional Information

There is an adjacency bonus with the [[Liquidator Legs]], but the effect is unknown.
