# Advanced Cooling Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Reduces cool-down time for the Photon cannon
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Advanced Cooling Sigma** is a Photon Cannon Companion unit.

## Summary

Ship-on-ship destruction becomes a more fulfilling endeavor through reduction in photon discharge interruptions. This is a first level upgrade.

## Game description

Photon Cannon cool-down times are significantly reduced.
Ship-on-ship destruction becomes a more fulfilling endeavor through reduction in photon discharge interruptions.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.
