# Mining Beam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Mineral Extraction Laser
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon, Phosphorus
- **Added in:** Omega

The **Mining Beam** is a core [[Multi-Tool]] component.

## Summary

The Mining Beam is the default weapon found on all Multi-Tools. While it can be used in combat, its primary purpose is to harvest resources from [[Mineral|minerals]] and [[flora]]. Using the Mining Beam also builds up heat over time, dealing more and more damage the closer it is to overheating.

Though the Mining Beam is an essential tool in all stages of the game, using it to harvest resources near [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] may result in them turning hostile.

## Game Description

Multi-Tool Mining Beam powered by Carbon-based Elements. Fire the beam with //.

Installs focusing optics within the Multi-Tool, allowing it to emit a high-energy beam. The beam is calibrated to excite molecules within common organic or geological objects, allowing it to operate as a highly effective mining device.

WARNING: Destruction of flora, fauna or minerals may attract Sentinel attention.

## Recharge

This directed laser mining tool can be charged using the following resources:
- [[Carbon]] - 0.5%/unit
- [[Condensed Carbon]] - 1.5%/unit
- [[Phosphorus]] - 2.5%/unit

## Additional Information

- Minerals and flora being mined by this weapon, in addition to granting resources upon their destruction, grant 2 units (1 unit in Survival and Permadeath [[Game mode|Game modes]]) of their primary resource on each mining tick.
- Though it has a listed base Damage Potential of 333.3, it deals greatly reduced damage to [[fauna]], sentinels and other players and it is unable to damage the reinforced doors of [[Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]].
- When adding upgrades for the Mining Beam, placing them next to each other or Mining Beam in the multi-tool inventory will trigger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]], increasing their bonuses above listed values.
