# Beam Amplifier

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Type:** Mining Extractoion Beam Enhancement
- **Description:** Strengthening and improving Mining Beam's capabilities
- **Added in:** Pre-release

A **Beam Amplifier** is a [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[MineLaser]].

## Summary

This technology is use to modify the [[MineLaser|Mining Beam Laser]] to improve its capabilities.

Operates automatically once constructed within the user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.

## Bonus

If Beam Amplifier Technologies are placed side by side, the player will receive additional bonuses for the Multi-tool Beam Laser Amplifier branch. Does not require the upgrades to be in direct contact with the Laser equipment.
