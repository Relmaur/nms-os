# Tethys Beam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Aquatic
- **Type:** Submarine Mining Laser
- **Recharge:** Organic Elements
- **Added in:** Visions

**Tethys Beam** is an [[exocraft]] technology.

## Summary

**Tethys Beam** is a variant of the [[Exocraft Mining Laser]] that can only be mounted on the [[Nautilon]].

Its internal fuel supply can be recharged with [[Carbon]] (0.5%/unit), [[Condensed Carbon]] (1.5%/unit) or [[Oxygen]] (1%/unit).

It is not a pre-installed technology and needs a blueprint to build it.

## Game description

A mounted laser system specifically designed for the Nautilon Submarine.

Fires a concentrated plasma beam towards the intended target, which is then ignited. Such a trigger mechanism allows the use of high-energy optical mining systems in a liquid medium.

## Source

The blueprint is acquired from Exocraft [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 100 × [[Chlorine]] + 10 × [[Living Pearl]] → 1 × Tethys Beam

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 50 × [[Chlorine]] + 5 × [[Living Pearl]]

## Mining properties

The Tethys Beam seems to behave similarly to the Exocraft Mining Laser without the [[Advanced Mining Laser]] upgrade, dealing 98 damage per apparent tick to [[mineral|minerals]] and [[flora]].

The Tethys Beam is not compatible with the upgrades available to standard Exocraft Mining Lasers.

It is currently unconfirmed if the Tethys Beam can ever gain an equivalent upgrade to the Advanced Mining Laser, in spite of some aquatic minerals requiring it.

## Combat properties

The Tethys Beam is very effective against [[fauna]], especially against the anglerfish type [[Abyssal Horror (anglerfish)|Abyssal Horror]], dealing 484 damage per apparent tick to these targets (though some creatures are not resilient enough to withstand a full apparent tick before dying). Damage dealt to weak spots on these targets is multiplied by a factor of 4.

## Additional Information

- The Tethys Beam can, under the right conditions, be used to mine [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]] that are above water (they do not currently appear to spawn underwater). For this to work, the Nautilon must still be submerged or else the laser's reticle cannot be aimed higher than sea level.
