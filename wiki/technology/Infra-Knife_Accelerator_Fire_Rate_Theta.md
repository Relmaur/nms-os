# Infra-Knife Accelerator Fire Rate Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Infra-Knife Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases Infra-Knife fire rate
- **Stats:** Fire Rate = +14%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Fire Rate Theta** is a weapons upgrade.

## Summary

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Fire Rate Theta** is [[weapons technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]].

This upgrade increases the rate of fire for the Infra-Knife Accelerator. It is the third of three upgrade levels.

## Game description

Increases rate of fire of the Starship's Infra-Knife Accelerator weapon mode by a large amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 300 × [[Thamium9]] + 300 × [[Iron]] + 3 × [[Dimensional Matrix]] → 1 × Infra-Knife Accelerator Fire Rate Theta

## Additional notes

- Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
