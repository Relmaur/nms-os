# Reload Accelerant Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Stats:** Reload Time = (-19%)
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Reload Acclerant Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Reload Accelerant Tau** is a second-level [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

The Reload Accelerant Tau upgrade automatically decreases the [[Boltcaster|Boltcasters]] ammunition reload times.

## Game description

Further improves Boltcaster reload times, ensuring that ammunition replenishment occurs swiftly and clearly.

Primed nerve endings in user's upper arm are complemented by magnetic clip guidance during reload procedures.

Operates automatically once constructed with user's multi-tool inventory.
