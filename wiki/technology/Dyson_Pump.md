# Dyson Pump

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Starship Cyclotron Upgrade
- **Stats:** +11% Heat Dispersion
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Dyson Pump** is a [[starship]] [[technology]].

## Summary

**Dyson Pump** is a technology that augments the [[Cyclotron Ballista]], a starship weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Cyclotron Ballista Upgrade|Cyclotron Ballista Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game Description

Replaces the heat sink of the Cyclotron Ballista by encompassing the entire unit with a heat-absorbing megastructure. Heat output is recycled to boost combat efficiency.

Significantly improves heat performance.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 3 × [[Amino Chamber]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Dyson Pump

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Amino Chamber]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Amino Chamber]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

The in-game description incorrectly refers to hint sink, instead of heat sink.
