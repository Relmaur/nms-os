# Minotaur Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur Cannon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Minotaur Cannon Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Minotaur Cannon Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Minotaur Cannon Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Minotaur]]'s [[Minotaur Cannon]] .

## Upgrade properties

Minotaur Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Damage*
- *Rate of Fire*
- *Weapon Power Efficiency: * reduces weapon charge cost of each shot

Up to three Minotaur Cannon Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Minotaur; installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Damage | +3-6% | +6-9% | +9-12% |   |   |
| Weapon Power Efficiency | +6-10% | +11+15% | +16-20% |   |   |

## Crafting

Minotaur Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Minotaur Cannon Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Minotaur Cannon Upgrades if they are adjacent to the **Minotaur Cannon** or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- Minotaur Cannon Upgrades share the same procedural IDs with the Exocraft Mounted Cannon Upgrades, including the names and stats.
