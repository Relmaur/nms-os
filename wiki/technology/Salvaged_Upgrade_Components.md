# Salvaged Upgrade Components

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Procedural Upgrade
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Salvaged Upgrade Components** is an Exosuit upgrade module.

## Summary

**Salvaged Upgrade Components**, contained within [[Rebuilt Exosuit Module|Rebuilt Exosuit Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] that grant various bonuses to your [[Exosuit]].

## Upgrade Properties

Salvaged Upgrade Components grant at least two to a maximum of four of the following six bonuses:
- *Shield Strength:* increases the durability of the Exosuit's shield.
- *Core Health:* adds 1 health square (the player initially has 3 health squares).
- **Life Support Tanks:** increases Life Support capacity
- **Solar Panel Power:** during the day, reduces the Life Support drain of all movement functions
- *Sprint Distance:* Sprint bonus: increases maximum sprint duration
- *Fuel Efficiency:* Jetpack bonus: decreases Jetpack's drain on [[Life Support]]

Up to three Salvaged Upgrade Components can be installed in the user's Exosuit. Installing four or more upgrades of the same type will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

These upgrades can also be installed alongside [[Forbidden Exosuit Module|Forbidden Exosuit Modules]] and [[Defence Systems Upgrade|Defence Systems Upgrades]] for adjacency [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]].

Their stats are the same as those of the Forbidden Exosuit Modules.

## Crafting

Salvaged Upgrade Components can only be obtained from opening [[Rebuilt Exosuit Module|Rebuilt Exosuit Modules]].

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]]

## Additional Information

- Core health units added by Salvaged Upgrade Components start out depleted and will not protect the player from death until they have been replenished by a source of healing.
- Adjacency [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Salvaged Upgrade Components, [[Defence Systems Upgrade|Defence Systems Upgrades]], and [[Forbidden Exosuit Module|Forbidden Exosuit Modules]]. If they have common stats they share such as Shield Strength, the stat bonus can be increased above listed values. This does not, however, apply adjacency bonuses to other upgrades such as Life Support technologies or Jetpack technologies, despite sharing some of these stats.
- Note that extra core health granted by these modules may not be a beneficial stat late-game, as it is possible to max out with 9 core health units using only [[Defence Systems Upgrade|Defence Systems Upgrades]], [[Remembrance]], and [[Star Seed]]/[[Mark of the Denier]].  Further core health stat bonuses have no effect, so it may be better to choose a different 4th stat to pump.
