# Photon Accelerator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** High speed photon energy modification
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Photon Accelerator** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

The **Photon Accelerator** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

It  modifies the weapon to allow a sustained rate of fire at a higher level of intensity. The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from an NPC [[armourer]].

## Game description

Photon cannon releases sustained rapid sequences of fire.

User is advised to limit bursts as overheating can occur very rapidly.

Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Nickel]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Photon Accelerator

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- 50 units of [[Coryzagen]] are required to start the production of a Photo Accelerator; once this is in hand the regular crafting can be completed.
