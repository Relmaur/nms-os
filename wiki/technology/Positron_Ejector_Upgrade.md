# Positron Ejector Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Positron Ejector Upgrades** are starship upgrades.

## Summary

**Positron Ejector Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Positron Modules]], are upgrades granting bonuses to the [[starship]]'s [[Positron Ejector]].

## Upgrade Properties

Positron Ejector Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Heat Dispersion*
- *Fire Rate*
- *Damage*

- [1] Technically X-Class upgrades can output 11% on either Fire Rate or Heat Dispersion, but there's only a 2/100000 (0.002%) chance of it happening.

Up to three Positron Ejector Upgrades can be installed in the tech inventory of the starship. Installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Positron Ejector Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Positron Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Silver]] + 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Silver]] + 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
