# Blaze Javelin

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Energy Beam
- **Type:** Weapon
- **Description:** The Blaze Javelin delivers instantaneous damage on targets
- **Stats:** Damage = ~1200 (full charge) Mining Power = ~240 (full charge)
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon
- **Added in:** Echoes

The **Blaze Javelin** is a component of the [[Multi-Tool|multi-tool]].

## Summary

The Blaze Javelin is a charge-fire weapon which instantaneously deals damage based on charge time --- a fully-charged shot, obtained by charging for at least 2 seconds, deals ~1200 damage and has ~240 mining power. 400 distance units is the maximum confirmed range of this weapon.

The Blaze Javelin draws energy from an internal fuel supply whenever it fires a shot: 4% fuel for fully-charged shots and 2% fuel for all other shots. This supply must be recharged using [[Carbon]] (2%/unit) or [[Condensed Carbon]] (6%/unit) in order to ensure continued function.

## Game description

Aggressive weapon attachment for the *Multi-Tool*. Access fire mode by pressing [Triangle/G] and fire weapon with [Left Mouse Button].

The Blaze Javelin delivers instantaneous damage on targets.

Plasma charge process creates an ionised gas arc to propel projectiles at great speed. User will perceive destructive results simultaneous to the pull of the trigger.

## How to acquire

- The [[blueprint]] for Blaze Javelin, as well as its upgrades, are purchased aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 200 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 150 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Blaze Javelin

- **Repair**: 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 75 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 75 × [[Condensed Carbon]]

## Additional notes

- This is a long-range weapon; the game equivalent of a sniper rifle. It has almost infinite charge and no reload. However, it is a poor choice for close range combat due to a long delay between shots.
- The Blaze Javelin may be a reference to Fallout 3's [Fire Lance](https://fallout.gamepedia.com/Firelance) alien blaster
- If installed on a [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Voltaic Staff]]-class multi tool, the tool will have a unique animation depending on the head piece when fully charged
- It does very high damage
- It is amazing against airborne [[Fauna]]
- It has a lot of recoil
