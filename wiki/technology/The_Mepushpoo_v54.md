# The Mepushpoo v5.4

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon

## Summary

**The Mepushpoo v5.4** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]], and has one of the odder names found so far.

It is sea-green in color, with black highlights. The barrel contains three wide decorative pink stripes. This tool has two muzzles, one above the other.
