# Boltcaster SM

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Stats:** +1% Damage, +21% Fire Rate, +8.0 Clip Size
- **Added in:** Visions

**Boltcaster SM** is a [[projectile technology]].

## Summary

**Boltcaster SM** is a technology that augments the [[Boltcaster]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Boltcaster Upgrade|Boltcaster Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game description

Boltcaster shots deliver added physical damage upon impact.

Grants additional kinetic energy to the user's ionised plasma distribution systems.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] is only available for players who bought the [[Limited Edition]] (PS4) version of the game, or got it from the day-one patch.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Boltcaster SM

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Pure Ferrite]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Pure Ferrite]]

## Additional Info

Boltcaster SM is the only upgrade that, while installed, changes the color of the shots from original cyan to a unique green.
