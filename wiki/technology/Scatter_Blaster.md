# Scatter Blaster

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Scatter Shot Projectile Weapon
- **Stats:** Damage Potential = 1070.7, Damage = 150 x8, Mining Power = 30 x8
- **Recharge:** Projectile Ammunition
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Scatter Blaster** is an upgrade for the multi-tool.

## Summary

**Scatter Blaster** is a [[technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool]] which uses [[Projectile Ammunition]].

This shotgun-style weapon fires 8 projectiles at once in a wide spread, allowing it to damage multiple targets at once, and making it most effective in close range encounters such as charging animals or attacks by [[Sentinel Quad|Sentinel Quads]].

It has a burst capacity of two shots by default (can be observed by holding down the attack button), which can be upgraded through S and X-rated [[Scatter Blaster Upgrade|Scatter Blaster Upgrades]].

## Game description

Specialist close combat weapon attachment for the Multi-Tool. Access fire mode by pressing // (PS4/PC/Xbox) and fire weapon with // (PS4/PC/Xbox).

The Scatter Blaster reconfigures the Multi-Tool to heat and shatter Metallic Elements, firing them in a wide cone of destruction. Range is limited, but the shrapnel is highly effective at close range.

## Additional notes

- The Scatter Blaster initially can hold 24 projectiles at a time.
- The Damage stat represents the Scatter Blaster's effectiveness against [[fauna]] and [[sentinel|sentinels]]; the Mining Power stat represents the weapon's effectiveness against [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]].
