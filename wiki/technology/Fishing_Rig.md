# Fishing Rig

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-Tool
- **Subcategory:** Angling Equipment
- **Type:** Deployable Angling Device
- **Description:** A versatile piece of angling equipment, enabling the user to fish in a variety of water conditions.
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Fishing Rig** is a technology.

## Summary

**Fishing Rig** is a Multi-tool upgrade that allows the player to catch [[fish]] from any body of water.

## Game Description

A versatile piece of angling equipment, enabling the user to fish in a variety of conditions.

Access the fishing rig by pressing // (PS4/PC/Xbox) and cast your line into a body of water with //. With a fish hooked, press and hold // to haul in your catch.

## Use

The Fishing Rig enables [[fishing]] by the player. Casting your line into any planetary body of water will attract fish, which can be seen swimming near the line after it is cast. After some time, the line will move and turn yellow, or green. If the player holds down their primary attack button when the line has turned green, a fish, or occasionally [[Collected Flotsam]], will be added to the player's [[inventory]].

[[Bait]] can be added to the Fishing Rig, by selecting it in the Multi-tool inventory. This increases the chances of catching a rarer, larger, or specific category of fish.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 3 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 60 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Fishing Rig

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 30 × [[Chromatic Metal]]

## Additional Information

- A blueprint for [[Lost Angler's Rig]], an alternative to the Fishing Rig, can be earned by completing the [[Expedition 15: Aquarius|Aquarius Expedition]].
