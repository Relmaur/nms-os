# Chloroplast Membrane

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Launch system
- **Type:** Neural assembly recharger
- **Added in:** Echoes
- **Description:** Recharges Neural Assembly during daylight

The **Chloroplast Membrane** is a piece of Living starship technology.

## Summary

The **Chloroplast Membrane** is a piece of [[Living Ship (Starship)|Living Ship]] [[technology]] and allows automatic recharging of Neural Assembly during daylight.

## Game description

An infusion of cyanobacteria fills the membrane, which rests just beneath the ship's translucent dermal layer.

The membrane will recharge the Neural Assembly automatically when exposed to sunlight.

## Sources

Grown from a [[Chloroplast Membrane Implant]].

## Crafting

- **Repair**: 8 × [[Processed Meat]] + 20 × [[Runaway Mould]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 8 × [[Processed Meat]] + 20 × [[Runaway Mould]]
