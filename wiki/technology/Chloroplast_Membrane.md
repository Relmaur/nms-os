# Chloroplast Membrane

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship - Launch system
- **Type:** Neural assembly recharger
- **Updated:** Echoes

The **Chloroplast Membrane** is a piece of Living starship technology.

## Summary

The **Chloroplast Membrane** is a piece of Living Ship technology and allows automatic recharging of Neural Assembly during daylight.

## Game description

An infusion of cyanobacteria fills the membrane, which rests just beneath the ship's translucent dermal layer.

The membrane will recharge the Neural Assembly automatically when exposed to sunlight.

## Sources

Grown from a Chloroplast Membrane Implant.

## Crafting

Chloroplast Membrane can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Processed Meat ×8 + Runaway Mould ×20

Chloroplast Membrane can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Chloroplast Membrane → Processed Meat ×8 + Runaway Mould ×20
