# Q-Resonator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Infra-Knife Upgrade
- **Stats:** Total Cannon Count: 3
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Q-Resonator** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Q-Resonator** is a technology that uses the [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]] icon. It adds two extra shots to each firing of Infra-Knife Accelerator, effectively tripling its base damage output. These extra shots have no effect on the rate at which the Infra-Knife overheats, and it fires volleys just as fast as the base weapon fires individual shots. Supercharging this module has no effect, despite the tooltip increasing to 4; there is no visible added bullet, or increase in damage.

## Game description

Upgrades the Infra Knife Accelerator, compressing internal waveforms to allow an extra shot to be weaved in to the conventional fire patterns.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 3 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Q-Resonator

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

This upgrade effectively replaces the [[Nonlinear Optics (Infra-Knife Accelerator)]] upgrade.
