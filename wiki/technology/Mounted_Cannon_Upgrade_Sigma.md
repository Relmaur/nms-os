# Mounted Cannon Upgrade Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Weapon Upgrades
- **Stats:** +83% Damage
- **Type:** Mounted Cannon Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Mounted Cannon Upgrade Sigma** is a an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Mounted Cannon Upgrade Sigma** is the sole [[weapons technology]] upgrade for the [[Mounted Cannon]].

This upgrade, despite what its listed stats would suggest, increases the damage of the Mounted Cannon against all targets by +250%. If placed in an adjacent inventory slot to the cannon, the [[Exocraft]] weapon's damage will be increased by another +12.5%, bringing the total damage boost up to +262.5% when the [[Upgrade bonus]] is active.

## Game description

Increases the power of the Mounted Cannon.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 100 × [[Copper]] → 1 × Mounted Cannon Upgrade Sigma

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Pugneum]] + 50 × [[Copper]]
