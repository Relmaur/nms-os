# Freighter Warp Reactor Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 200 ly
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Freighter Warp Reactor Sigma** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Freighter Warp Reactor Sigma** is a level 1 [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be travelled by a freighter.

## Game description

Hyperdrive is granted more robust deep space exploration capabilities and ability to leap to stars previously out of reach.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from a [[Technology Merchant]] for 300  Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 250 × [[Cadmium]] + 1 × [[Oxygen Filter]] + 4 × [[Antimatter Housing]] → 1 × Freighter Warp Reactor Sigma

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
