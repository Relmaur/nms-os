# Mounted Cannon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon, Oxygen
- **Type:** Exocraft Weapon Attachment
- **Added in:** Waypoint

The **Mounted Cannon** is an [[Exocraft]] upgrade.

## Summary

The Mounted Cannon is an Exocraft-based weapon that fires explosive energy projectiles at enemies. It fires twice per second and must periodically be recharged with [[Carbon]] (0.5%/unit), [[Condensed Carbon]] (1.5%/unit) or [[Oxygen]] (1%/unit). The cannon's high damage makes it great at dealing with [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] and hostile [[fauna]], though its explosive properties means it can accidentally kill players who aren't careful.

It has a dedicated [[Upgrade Module]] type, [[Exocraft Cannon Module]]. Upgrades from these modules grant significant improvements to the weapon's damage, rate of fire and charge efficiency.

-

## Game description

Due to energy requirements of this powerful weapon, they can only be fired from an Exocraft mount.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 100 × [[Copper]] → 1 × Mounted Cannon

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Pugneum]] + 50 × [[Copper]]

## Notes

- Each shot deals 1040 damage to [[fauna]] and [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] on critical hits and 260 damage on all other direct hits.
  - It deals 52 damage to [[mineral|minerals]] and [[flora]].
- For any save editors that want to increase the actual fire rate of the cannon, duplicate copies of the Exocraft Mounted Cannon in the same exocraft increase the rate of fire indirectly due to an adjacency glitch similar to that of the Photon Cannon and Spewing Vents.
- Being an explosive weapon, it is very much capable of accidentally killing the player using it. As such it should be treated with care, especially in a [[Game mode|Permadeath]] save file.
