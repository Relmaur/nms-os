# Radar Power Resonator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Communications
- **Stats:** +100% Scan
- **Description:** Exocraft Signal Detection Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Radar Power Resonator** is an Exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Radar Power Resonator** is a level-2 upgrade to the standard [[Exocraft Radar]].

It can be used with the [[Quick Menu]] to scan for additional points of interest, including [[Ruin|Ruins]] and [[Monolith|Monoliths]].

## Game description

Allows for additional building types to be acquired by the Exocraft Radar.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 150 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 50 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Radar Power Resonator

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 25 × [[Gold]]

## Upgrade progression

- [[Radar Amplifier]] - Allows the player to scan for [[Abandoned Building|Abandoned Buildings]] and [[Drop Pod|Drop Pods]].
- [[Radar Power Resonator]] - Allows the player to scan for Ruins and Monoliths.
