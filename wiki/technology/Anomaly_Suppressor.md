# Anomaly Suppressor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Hazard
- **Type:** Automated Breach Integrity Device
- **Description:** Protects the user from any boundary failure
- **Recharge:** Elixir of Glass

**Anomaly Suppressor** is an Exosuit technology.

## Summary

**Anomaly Suppressor** is an [[Exosuit]] technology that is only available on [[Expedition 16: The Cursed]]. It provides protection to planetary boundary failure and is indicated by a purple gauge. This technology will replace [[Hazard Protection]] during the expedition.

## Game Description

As the boundaries of reality begin to weaken, the risk of catastrophic breach anomalies increases.

This device monitors and maintains the local boundary strength, guarding against reality continuity errors. Supply the device with glass to maintain functionality.

## Usage

This technology works like a Hazard Protection. It protects the user from boundary failures and is indicated by a purple gauge. The strength in which the suppression decreases is indicated by arrows pointing at the gauge, and the failure strength is declared by the measurement units of "exB". The higher the unit is, the faster the supperession decrease.

As the power lower, time get faster. And if the power is at 50% or below, [[Boundary Horror]] will attack the user. They can also use ramming attack at 20%. Both attack decreases the power of the Anomaly Suppressor. They can be recharged using the [[Elixir of Glass]], the [[Anomaly Seal]] [[base building]] component. The nost effective way to recharge a suppresson is by going to a [[starship]] and launch into space which recover the supperession to 100% instantly.

Going to a starship or a structure stabilizes the power, but unlike the Hazard Protection, they cannot auto-recharge the power. If the Suppressor reaches 0% power, you did not take damage from hazards, but your shield will stop auto-recover. Incase of combat, it is important to keep the Anomaly Suppressor power full or above 1% because as stated before, the shield cannot recharge themselves when the power depletes, especially when fighting [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].
