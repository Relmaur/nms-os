# Reload Accelerant Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Stats:** Reload Time = (-10%)
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Reload Accelerant Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Reload Accelerant Sigma** is a first-level [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Boltcaster]].

It automatically decreases the [[Boltcaster|Boltcasters]] ammunition reload times.

## Game description

Boltcaster ammunition times are decreased.

Tendons in the user's upper arm are wired to boltcaster sensors, which prime nerve endings with electrical charge while the plasma chip is expended.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
