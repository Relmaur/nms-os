# Defence Systems Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Defence Systems
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Defence Systems Upgrade** is an exosuit Upgrade Module.

## Summary

**Defence Systems Upgrades**, contained within [[Shield Module|Shield Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] granting bonuses to the [[exosuit]]'s shield and [[health]].

## Upgrade properties

Defence Systems Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Shield Strength:* increases the durability of the Exosuit's shield.
- *Core Health:* adds one health square (the player initially has three health squares).

- **NOTE:** The max for X-class is the same as the current S-class stat; do not risk!

The likelihood of an upgrade granting both bonuses depends on its class: low (C-class), high (B-class), or guaranteed (A-class and S-class).

Up to three Defence Systems Upgrades can be installed in each inventory tab of the [[exosuit]] which can accept technology. Installing four or more upgrades of the same type in a given inventory tab will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

Note: The Shield strength buff has something wrong with it. Classes C, B, and S have a 5% range for shield strength stats, whereas the A-class has a 10% range for shield strength stats. Even worse, the X-class upgrades now share the same maximum shield strength stat with that of the S-class. Furthermore, this change should now discourage travelers from installing the Suspicious Shield Modules, as they give no more incentive for better stats. For a proper reference, theoretical stats are shown on the table.

## Crafting

Defence Systems Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Shield Modules.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Sodium]] + 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- Health squares added by a Defence Systems Upgrade start out depleted and will not protect the player from death until they have been replenished by a source of healing.
- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Defence Systems Upgrades if they are adjacent to any [[Forbidden Exosuit Module|Forbidden Exosuit Modules]] that have Shield Strength bonuses or each other, increasing their combined Shield Strength above listed values. This does not, however, apply to bonuses that try to match a mod with just a sprint distance buff with another mod with only a shield strength buff, like in the forbidden mods.

## Known Issues

- The maximum X-class Shield Strength stat equals that of the maximum S-class Shield Strength stat, which is a last minute change from the devs.
