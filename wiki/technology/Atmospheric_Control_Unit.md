# Atmospheric Control Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Life Support
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Atmospheric Control Unit** is freighter technology.

## Summary

The **Atmospheric Control Unit** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that serves as a freighter life support unit.

## Game description

A critical freighter subsystem, vital for maintaining a habitable and safe environment for all on-board organic lifeforms.

Capable of generated a limited breathable atmosphere even when damaged or unpowered, but only for a short period of time.
