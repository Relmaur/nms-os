# Hardframe Left Arm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Stats:** Mining Laser Power: +62%, Damage to stunned entities: +400%, Post-Impact Ignition: Enabled, Impact Fire Duration: 4 seconds
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Hardframe Left Arm** is a technology.

## Summary

**Hardframe Left Arm** is a weapon which can be mounted on a [[Minotaur]].

An adjacency bonus is gained when installed next to the [[Minotaur Laser]].

## Game description

A collection of salvaged components from a [[Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech|Sentinel Hardframe]], reconditioned to fit the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid.

When installed alongside a Minotaur Laser, it transforms the mining laser into a powerful combat upgrade with the ability to deal heavy damage to stunned entities.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 40 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] → 1 × Hardframe Left Arm

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 20 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 20 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]
