# Toxin Suppressor Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Level:** 2
- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Provides increased protection from toxic rain damage.
- **Stats:** Toxic Protection = 225.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Toxin Suppressor Tau** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

The **Toxin Suppressor Tau** upgrade protects the user from toxic damage.

## Game description

Provides increased protection from toxic rain damage.

Device is charged by [[Oxide elements]]. Operates automatically once constructed within user's exosuit inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Zinc]] + 100 × [[Carbon]] + 1 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] → 1 × Toxin Suppressor Tau

This identical formula is used to craft five separate [[hazard protection]] upgrades for the [[exosuit]]:
- [[Aeration Membrane Tau]]
- [[Coolant Network Tau]]
- [[Radiation Deflector Tau]]
- [[Thermic Layer Tau]]
- Toxin Suppressor Tau

## Upgrade progression

- [[Toxin Suppressor Sigma]]
- Toxin Suppressor Tau
- [[Toxin Suppressor Theta]]

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's Exosuit inventory
- Recharged with [[Oxide elements]] and Defensive Energy Sources ([[Shielding Shard]], [[Shielding Plate]], [[Shielding Sheet]])
