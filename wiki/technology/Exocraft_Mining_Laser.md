# Exocraft Mining Laser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Type:** Exocraft Mining Attachment
- **Recharge:** Carbon, Condensed Carbon, Oxygen
- **Added in:** NextGen

The **Exocraft Mining Laser** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

The **Exocraft Mining Laser** is a weapon which can be mounted on any Exocraft, but comes pre-installed on the [[Colossus]]. It can be fired continuously without ever overheating, dealing damage at an apparent tick rate of 5 ticks per second until its energy is depleted, at which point it must be recharged with [[Carbon]] (0.5%/unit), [[Condensed Carbon]] (1.5%/unit) or [[Oxygen]] (1%/unit).

## Game description

A mining laser too cumbersome for handheld operation, they are found only installed on Exocraft.

## Mining properties

The Exocraft Mining Laser initially behaves similarly to a [[Mining Beam]] without the [[Advanced Mining Laser]] upgrade, dealing 98 damage per apparent tick to [[mineral|minerals]] and [[Flora]].

It can also be used like a [[Terrain Manipulator]], although only on [[Resource Deposit|resource deposits]]. It is very inefficient and hard to use.

With the best possible upgrades installed for mining beam and this laser are compared, the beam does around 100 damage per tick while the exocraft laser can do around 170 damage per tick, making this laser very useful for mining expeditions.

## Combat properties

The Exocraft Mining Laser is mediocre at harvesting [[Fauna]] and fighting [[Sentinel|Sentinels]], dealing 98 damage per apparent tick to these targets. However, the weapon's laser properties make it much easier to use at long range than the [[Exocraft Mounted Cannon]].

*See also: [[Exocraft Mounted Cannon]]*

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 100 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 2 × [[Life Support Gel]] → 1 × Exocraft Mining Laser

- **Dismantle** returns: 12 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Life Support Gel]]

## Additional information

- The weapon actually deals damage 10 times per second, but pairs of damage ticks are automatically added together to reduce clutter for the purposes of damage number display.
- This technology has one blueprint upgrade, [[Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrade Sigma]], and a dedicated [[Upgrade Module]] type, Exocraft Laser Module.
- As it does not yield resources until it is finished harvesting, it will not attract [[Sentinel]] attention in the event of a misfire, so long as it does not finish destroying the object.
- Unlike small-arms [[Multi-tool]] weaponry, the mining laser can damage starships and will cause them to fire back on the player.
- A mining laser with the Upgrade Sigma is better at destroying Sentinels and fauna than a stock Exocraft Mounted Cannon
