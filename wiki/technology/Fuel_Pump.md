# Fuel Pump

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Fuel Pump** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Fuel Pump** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A high-capacity pump, ensuring that fuel reaches every part of the device in all gravitational conditions. The filter is clogged with a strange substance, causing it to lose power.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 10 × [[Pure Ferrite]]
