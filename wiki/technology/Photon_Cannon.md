# Photon Cannon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Starship Projectile Weapon
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Photon Cannon** is a starship weapon.

## Summary

**Photon Cannon** is the default [[weapons technology|weapon]] installed on all [[starship|starships]], and is more commonly used than the alternative [[Phase Beam]]. This weapon may occasionally be referred to as a Starship Cannon, although Photon Cannon is the preferred usage.

## Game description

A destructive starship projectile launcher, the weapons system for choice for the interstellar trader and pirate alike.

Use () to change weapon mode. Press () to fire.

## Usage

The cannon can be used both in direct combat and in clearing [[asteroid|asteroids]] for traversal.

### Combat Properties

| Rate of Fire | ~7 shots/sec |
| --- | --- |
| Time to Overheat | ~5.6 seconds |
| Overheat Cooldown Time | 4 seconds |

Photon Cannon has a typical range for a starship weapon, having the ability to hit targets from up to 1500 distance units (1500u) away (for reference, fugitive [[pirate]] ships will attack the player if they get within 1500u). By default, the weapon fires at a rate of ~7 shots per second and can fire 40 shots before overheating, resulting in a time to overheat of ~5.6 seconds, Upon overheating, Photon Cannon takes 4 seconds by default to recover from overheating.

Despite its visuals, the Proton Cannon is a *hit-scan* weapon with high lock-on potential, making it the easiest weapon to land hits with against starships.

Photon Cannon deals 120 base damage on direct hits to the following targets: [[fauna]], starships, [[Space Fleets]], and destructible obstacles such as Reinforced Doors ([[Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]]). Against starships in particular, the starship weapon deals 4x damage to the rear (resulting in 480 effective base damage).

Photon Cannon deals 24 base damage on direct hits with a full volley to all planetary [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

### Mining Properties

When used to harvest a planet's [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]], Photon Cannon has a base mining power of 24 for each shot.
