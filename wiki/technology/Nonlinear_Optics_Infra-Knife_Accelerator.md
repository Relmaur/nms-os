# Nonlinear Optics (Infra-Knife Accelerator)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Upgrade
- **Stats:** Total Cannon Count: 3
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Nonlinear Optics (Infra-Knife Accelerator)** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Nonlinear Optics (Infra-Knife Accelerator)** is a technology that uses the [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]] icon and has different crafting recipes, but is otherwise identical in appearance to the [[Photon Cannon]] upgrade [[Nonlinear Optics]]. It adds a third shot to each volley of Infra-Knife Accelerator, effectively increasing its base damage output by 50%.

## Game description

Replaces the conventional Photon Cannon optics with an experimental glass array, boosting combat performance.

Significantly improves heat sink capacity.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 3 × [[Destablised Sodium]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Nonlinear Optics (Infra-Knife Accelerator)

## Additional information

- The third shot added by Nonlinear Optics overlaps one of the two existing shots. Since damage numbers from Infra-Knife Accelerator shots in a volley do *not* visually combine to show volley damage (rather, damage numbers from individual shots tend to overlap), this makes it very difficult to confirm in-game that the upgrade is working as intended.

- One way to force the shots to have non-overlapping damage numbers is to shoot a [[fauna]] in a specific spot near its critical-damage area. If done correctly, only one of the two overlapping shots will land a critical hit (the other two shots will land non-critical hits, either directly or via splash damage), causing all damage numbers to become visually distinct.
