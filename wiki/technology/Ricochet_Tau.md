# Ricochet Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Boltcaster shots are granted deflective properties.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Ricochet Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Ricochet Tau** is a second-level upgrade for the [[Boltcaster]].

## Game Description

Boltcaster shots are granted deflective properties.

Environmental collision results in plasma diversion, unpredictable flight paths and potential victim impact.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 300 × [[Platinum]] + 150 × [[Zinc]] + 1 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] → 1 × Ricochet Tau

## Upgrade progression

- [[Ricochet Sigma]]
- Ricochet Tau
- [[Ricochet Theta]]

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's Multi-tool inventory with the Boltcaster
- Needs no fuel or recharging
