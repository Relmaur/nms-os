# Minotaur Laser Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur Laser
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Minotaur Laser Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Minotaur Laser Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Minotaur Laser Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Minotaur]]'s [[Minotaur Laser]] .

## Upgrade properties

Minotaur Laser Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Mining Laser Power*
- *Mining Laser Efficiency*

Up to three Minotaur Laser Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Minotaur; installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

| Minotaur Laser<br>Upgrade | Stats Given: | Module Improves | B-class | A-class | S-class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| B/A: 1-2<br>S: 2 | Mining Laser Power | +12-24% | +25-37% | +37-49% |   |
| Mining Laser Efficiency | +6-10% | +11-15% | +16-20% |   |   |

## Crafting

Minotaur Laser Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Minotaur Laser Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Minotaur Laser Upgrades if they are adjacent to the **Minotaur Laser**, [[Precision Minotaur Laser]], or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- Minotaur Laser Upgrades share the same procedural ID with the Exocraft Laser Upgrades, as well as the names and stats.
