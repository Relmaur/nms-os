# Launch Auto-Charger

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Launch Thruster
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Starship Launch System Upgrade
- **Stats:** Automatic Recharging: Enabled
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Launch Auto-Charger** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Launch Auto-Charger** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] and an upgrade for the [[Launch Thruster]].

The recharge rate is 4% per minute. Keep in mind that you need to have your starship grounded and set as your primary before the recharge can take place.

## Game description

Photovoltaic panels fitted to the starship Launch Thrusters allow for a slow trickle charge while the ship is not in operation.

Harvested light is used to fuse [[di-hydrogen]] from the atmosphere and over time will refuel the Launch Thrusters automatically.

## Sources

This technology can be acquired from the starship technology researcher on board the [[Space Anomaly]] for 460 Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Antimatter]] + 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Launch Auto-Charger

- **Repair**: 3 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 3 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
