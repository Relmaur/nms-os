# Jetpack Booster Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Aerial Propulsion Companion Unit
- **Description:** Provides an additional boost to the Jetpack
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Jetpack Booster Tau** is a jetpack upgrade.

## Summary

The **Jetpack Booster Tau** is a second-level [[utilities technology]] upgrade for the [[exosuit]] [[Jetpack]].

## Game Description

Jetpack is granted extended bursts of propulsion, allowing user ascent to higher locations.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's exosuit inventory, alongside existing Jetpack unit.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 150 × [[Chrysonite]] + 150 × [[Titanium]] + 150 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Jetpack Booster Tau

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
