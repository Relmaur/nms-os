# Phase Beam (Atlas)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Type:** Spacecraft Laser Device
- **Description:** One of the two primary ship weapons
- **Stats:** Damage = 200.0
- **Recharge:** Isotope
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Phase Beam** is a starship weapon.

## Summary

The Phase Beam is one of the primary [[starship]] [[weapon|weapons]], and is an alternative to the more common [[Photon Cannon]]. Both can be used to mine [[asteroid|asteroids]] in space, which hold valuable resources.

## Game Description

Spacefaring beam weapon and asteroid mining tool.

User is advised that [[Isotope elements]] are required to recharge device.

Press ... to activate.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 30 × [[Iron]] + 30 × [[Heridium]] + 30 × [[Thamium9]] → 1 × Phase Beam (Atlas)

## Pre-order bonus

The pre-order starships [[Alpha Vector]] and [[Horizon Omega]] come equipped with the Phase Beam whereas the regular starting ships need to craft it.

## Upgrades

The Phase Beam can be upgraded with the following items:
- Phase Coolant
  - [[Phase Coolant Sigma]]
  - [[Phase Coolant Tau]]
  - [[Phase Coolant Theta]]
- Beam Impact
  - [[Beam Impact Sigma]]
  - [[Beam Impact Tau]]
  - [[Beam Impact Theta]]

## Recharge

The Phase Beam is rechargeable using following items:
- 1x [[Carbon]] by 0,1%
- 1x [[Thamium9]] by 0.2%
- 1x [[Plutonium]] by 0,3%
- 1x [[Power Gel]] by 5%
- 1x [[Power Canister]] by 7%
- 1x [[Power Reservoir]] by 10%

The Phase Beam was featured in various pre-release trailers for *No Man's Sky*, including the PS4 Explore trailer (see below).
