# Fourier De-Limiter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** B
- **Type:** Phase Beam Upgrade
- **Stats:** +11% Heat Dispersion
- **Added in:** Origins

**Fourier De-Limiter** is a [[starship]] [[technology]].

## Summary

Fourier De-Limiter is a technology that augments the [[Phase Beam]], a starship weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Phase Beam Upgrade|Phase Beam Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game description

Rewires the Phase Beam with [[nanite]]-based circuitry, bypassing conventional reality limits on laser power.

Significantly improves heat performance.

## Crafting

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
