# Beam Coolant System

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Type:** Mining Beam Companion
- **Description:** Shorten Mining Beam cool-down periods
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Beam Coolant System** is a [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool]]'s [[MineLaser|mining beam]].

## Summary

This technology is use to modify the [[MineLaser|Mining Beam Laser]] to shorten Mining Beam cool-down periods. Adds additional coolant loops of liquid [[frenium]], placed around the laser's crystal excitement chamber, quickly and efficiently remove symptoms of overheating.

Operates automatically once constructed within the user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.

## Upgrade progression

The Beam coolant System has the following upgrades available:
- [[Beam Coolant System Sigma]]
- [[Beam Coolant System Tau]]
- [[Beam Coolant System Theta]]

## Bonus

If Beam Coolant Technologies are placed side by side, player will receive additional bonuses for the Multi-tool Beam Laser Coolant branch. Does not require they be connected to Laser the equipment.

## Release history

- [[Release]] - Added to the game.
- [[NEXT]] - Removed from the game. If any of the relevant technologies were installed, they have turned into [[Obsolete Technology]].
