# Combat Scope

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Precision
- **Description:** Weapon Precision Enhancement
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Combat Scope** is an element of technology that enables zoom while wielding a [[Multi-tool]] weapon.

## Summary

Combat Scope is a Multi-tool upgrade which adds zoom as an alt-fire function to Multi-tool weapons. It is activated by holding down the alt-fire input (Right Mouse Button on PC by default).

The Combat Scope [[blueprint]] can be purchased in the [[Space Anomaly]] for 80 [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]]. It can also be given by a Traveler at a Space Station for answering their question correctly, or by saying the word "tech" to a [[Korvax]] after attempting to practice your language skills.

Once the blueprint has been obtained, Combat Scope can be installed in the Multi-tool [[inventory]] to gain its benefits.

## Game Description

A recoil-stabilising weapon scope for precise combat.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Combat Scope

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Ion Battery]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Ion Battery]]
