# Thermal Buffer

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Type:** Exocraft Hazard Protection Upgrade
- **Added in:** ExoMech

**Thermal Buffer** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Thermal Buffer** is a hazard protection upgrade for the [[Exocraft]].

## Game description

Sublimation cooling vents and high-thermal mass buffers are installed in all structural panels, keeping the Exocraft pilot at non-lethal temperatures even in the most overwhelming of firestorms.
