# Pulse Spitter Accuracy Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Subcategory:** Pulse Spitter
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Pulse Spitter Companion Unit

**Pulse Spitter Accuracy Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Pulse Spitter Accuracy Sigma** is a first-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Pulse Spitter]].

## Game description

Increases accuracy of the Multi-tool's Pulse Spitter weapon mode by reducing the spread of the projectile by a small amount.
