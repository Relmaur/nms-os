# Photon Blast

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** High speed photon energy modification
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Photo Blast** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

The **Photon Blast** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

It modifies the spread of a Photon Cannon's target area to proved a wide range of damage. Best used on targets close to the user. The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from an NPC [[armourer]].

## Game description

Photon cannon shots are granted a wide cone of destruction. Reduces device's range and rate of fire, encouraging combat with targets in close proximity to user. Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Nickel]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Photon Blast

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- the NPC armourer will require a [[Vy'keen Dagger]] as an initial investment before providing the blueprint. Once this is in hand, the regular crafting can be completed.
