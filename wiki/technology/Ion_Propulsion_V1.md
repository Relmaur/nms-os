# Ion Propulsion V1

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Ship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Description:** Ship drive using ionized xenon gas for propusion
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**Ion Propulsion V1** is a [[ship]] [[hyperdrive technology]] upgrade.

## Summary

Ion propulsion uses ionized gas to propel a spaceship. When xenon ions are emitted at such a high speed as exhaust from a spacecraft, they push the ship in the opposite direction.
