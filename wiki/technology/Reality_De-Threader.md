# Reality De-Threader

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 800 ly
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Reality De-Threader** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Reality De-Threader** is a level 3 [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be traveled by a freighter.

## Game description

An augmentation for the colossal hyperdrive engine of an interstellar freighter.

Uncapped, yet stabilised, fusion systems are threaded into user's hyperdrive. Allows starship to jump ever longer distances in deep space.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for 12 [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Indium]] + 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 6 × [[Antimatter Housing]] → 1 × Reality De-Threader

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Indium]] + 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 3 × [[Antimatter Housing]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
