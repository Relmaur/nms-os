# Photovoltaic Panel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Photovoltaic Panel** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Photovoltaic Panel** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A small but efficient panel, designed to harvest light and convert it to power. However, the panel is now cracked and dusty, and can no longer be relied on.
