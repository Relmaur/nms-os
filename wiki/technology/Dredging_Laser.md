# Dredging Laser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Aquatic
- **Subcategory:** Nautilon
- **Type:** Nautilon Terrain Manipulator
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Dredging Laser** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Dredging Laser** provides [[Terrain Manipulator]] capabilities to the Nautilon's [[Tethys Beam]].

## Game description

Adds Terrain Manipulation capability to the laser systems of the Nautilon Submarine.

Cooled by specially designed water intakes, it is specifically calibrated to carve through complex geology of the sea floor.

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 45 × [[Lithium]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]]
