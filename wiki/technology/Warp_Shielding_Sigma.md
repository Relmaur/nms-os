# Warp Shielding Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Red Systems
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 50 ly
- **Added in:** Visions

**Warp Shielding Sigma** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Warp Shielding Sigma** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it allows warping to [[System colors|red colored]] [[star system|star systems]].

It also improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be travelled by a freighter.

## Game description

Hyperdrive computational matrix is upgraded, preventing rapid disassembly when attempting to reach rare-class red stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from a [[Technology Merchant]] for ?  Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 2 × [[Antimatter Housing]] + 2 × [[Superoxide Crystal]] + 250 × [[Cadmium]] → 1 × Warp Shielding Sigma

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
