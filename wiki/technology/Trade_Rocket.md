# Trade Rocket

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Exosuit Upgrade
- **Updated:** Waypoint

**Trade Rocket** is an exosuit technology.

## Summary

**Trade Rocket** is a technology that allows a player to sell Trade Goods from the planet surface.

It needs to be installed in an exosuit inventory slot for it to take effect.

## Game Description

A personal rocketry device with a direct link to the Galactic Trade Network. This unit allows the user to load a small spacecraft with goods. Upon launch, the goods will be transported to the nearest trade terminus and automatically sold.

## Source

The blueprint can be acquired from Iteration: Selene in the Space Anomaly for 90.

## Crafting

Trade Rocket can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Wiring Loom ×2 + Tritium ×120 + Di-hydrogen ×90 → Trade Rocket

Trade Rocket can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Wiring Loom ×1 + Tritium ×60 + Di-hydrogen ×45

Trade Rocket can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Trade Rocket → Wiring Loom ×1 + Tritium ×60 + Di-hydrogen ×45

## Additional information

- Trade Rocket has 25 slots but does not appear in Quick Transfer.
- It must be periodically recharged with Tritium to operate.
- System Economy modifiers are ignored, items are sold for around 99% of standard value (minus a fee of less than 1%).
- Be careful however, the Rocket does not trade for nanites, so pay attention, otherwise you will waste nanite related items like sentinel upgrades.
- Units are credited instantly after pressing the launch button. Telamon does not announce this, so some might miss it.
- It can be followed after launch, and flies at normal (non-boost, non-Pulse Drive) speeds in the direction of the Space Station. It is invulnerable to damage, but can damage your ship's shields if it flies into you.
