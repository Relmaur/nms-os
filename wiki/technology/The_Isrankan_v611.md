# The Isrankan v6.11

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**The Isrankan v6.11** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is steel-gray in color, with purple inside the trigger guard.

## Acquirement

The Isrankan v6.11 was been found on a sale mount in an outpost on the [[planet]] Nienst Puerlunab (his planet is part of the Ianationau [[Star system]] in the [[Ickjamatew]] galaxy).
