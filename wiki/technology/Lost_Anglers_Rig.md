# Lost Angler's Rig

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-Tool
- **Subcategory:** Angling Equipment
- **Type:** Unique Angling Device
- **Description:** A versatile piece of angling equipment, enabling the user to fish in a variety of water conditions.
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Lost Angler's Rig** is a technology.

## Summary

**Lost Angler's Rig** is a Multi-tool upgrade that allows the player to catch [[fish]] from any body of water.

## Game Description

A versatile piece of angling equipment, enabling the user to fish in a variety of conditions. This rig was once owned by the legendary Lost Angler

Access the fishing rig by pressing // (PS4/PC/Xbox) and cast your line into a body of water with //. With a fish hooked, press and hold // to haul in your catch.

## Use

The Lost Angler's Rig enables [[fishing]] by the player. Casting your line into any planetary body of water will attract fish, which can be seen swimming near the line after it is cast. After some time, the line will move and turn yellow, or green. If the player holds down their primary attack button when the line has turned green, a fish, or occasionally [[Collected Flotsam]], will be added to the player's [[inventory]].

[[Fishing Bait]] can be added to the Lost Angler's Rig, by selecting it in the Multi-tool inventory. This increases the chances of catching a rarer, larger, or specific category of fish.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired by completing the final phase of the [[Expedition 15: Aquarius|Aquarius Expedition]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 60 × [[Salt]] + 90 × [[Silver]] → 1 × Lost Angler's Rig

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] + 30 × [[Salt]] + 45 × [[Silver]]

## Additional Information

- This functions the same as the [[Fishing Rig]], which must be uninstalled from your Multi-tool before installing Lost Angler's Rig.
- Though visually distinct from Fishing Rig, and an expedition reward, it does not claim to offer any functional advantage over using Fishing Rig.
- As of the [[Aquarius]] update, this does not appear to have an entry in the [[Catalogue]], unlike its counterpart.

## Release History
