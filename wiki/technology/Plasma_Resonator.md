# Plasma Resonator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Mining Beam Upgrade
- **Stats:** +6% Mining Speed, +5% Heat Dispersion
- **Added in:** NEXT

**Plasma Resonator** is a [[multi-tool]] [[technology]].

## Summary

**Plasma Resonator** is a technology that augments the [[Mining Beam]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Mining Beam Upgrade|Mining Beam Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game Description

A significant upgrade for the Mining Beam.

Upgrades the Mining Laser's primary crystal chamber, boosting core beam energy while decreasing unwanted heat spill.
