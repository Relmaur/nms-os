# Damage Radius

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Plasma Launcher Companion Unit
- **Description:** Plasma Launcher grenades have widened impact craters
- **Stats:** Explosion Radius = +20%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Damage Radius** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade for the [[Plasma Launcher]] portion of the [[Multi-tool]]

## Summary

This technology modifies the Plasma Launcher to give its projectiles widened impact craters, increasing destructive power.

## Game description

Plasma Launcher grenades have widened impact craters.

User will observe an increased destructive area upon ignition. User is advised to stand at a distance from targeted land mass to avoid damage to personal appendages.

Operates automatically once constructed within the user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.
