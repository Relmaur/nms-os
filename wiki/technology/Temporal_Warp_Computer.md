# Temporal Warp Computer

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 50 ly
- **Added in:** Origins

**Temporal Warp Computer** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Temporal Warp Computer** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that allows warping to blue [[Star system|Star systems]].

## Game description

Upgrades the hyperdrive's computational matrix, preventing temporal anomalies when attempting to reach rare-class blue stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the Galactic Map.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for 12 [[Salvaged Frigate Module|Salvaged Frigate Modules]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 250 × [[Indium]] → 1 × Temporal Warp Computer

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 1 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 125 × [[Indium]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
