# Ablative Armour

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Ship
- **Subcategory:** Shield
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Starship Shield Upgrade
- **Stats:** +7% Shield Strength
- **Added in:** Origins

**Ablative Armour** is a shield technology for the starship.

## Summary

**Ablative Armour** is a shield [[technology]] for the [[starship]] which improves shield durability.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] in [[Space Anomaly]].

## Game description

Adds a layer of superheated shielding to the starship's defense array, vaporising incoming fire.

Significantly increases starship shield performance.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 100 × [[Gold]] + 50 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Ablative Armour

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Gold]] + 25 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Gold]] + 25 × [[Sodium Nitrate]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger if Ablative Armour is adjacent to [[Deflector Shield]] or [[Deflector Shield Upgrade|Deflector Shield Upgrades]], increasing its bonus above the listed value.
  - Ablative Armour does not count toward the three-upgrade limit; however, only one Ablative Armour technology can be installed at a time.
