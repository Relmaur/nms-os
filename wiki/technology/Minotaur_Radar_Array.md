## Summary

**Minotaur Radar Array** is a scanner technology upgrade for [[Minotaur]].

## Game description

A high-detail radar device, capable of upgrading the scanner technology aboard the Minotaur to [[Analysis Visor]] levels.

Press  to activate a radar sweep, revealing additional points of interest that cannot be located via a [[Multi-tool]] scanner.

Use the Quick Menu to tune the radar to specific buildings.
