# Rapidfire Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the boltcaster's rate-of-fire
- **Stats:** Fire Rate = +29% Recoil = +39%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Rapidfire Tau** is a second-level upgrade for the [[Boltcaster]].

## Game Description

Boltcaster rate-of-fire system upgrade.

Improved charge functions allow user to take repeated shots in fast succession.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's mult-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 200 × [[Titanium]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] + 100 × [[Aluminium]] → 1 × Rapidfire Tau

## Upgrade progression

- Rapidfire Tau
- [[Rapidfire Theta]]

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's Multi-tool inventory with the [[Boltcaster]]
- Needs no fuel or recharging
