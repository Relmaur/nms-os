# Beam Coolant System Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Mining Beam
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Further lengthens the time between Mining Beam cool-down periods.
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

The **Beam Coolant System Tau** is a second-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Further lengthens the time between [[Mining Beam]] cool-down periods.

Additional coolant loops of liquid frenium, placed around the laser's crystal excitement chamber, quickly and efficiently remove symptoms of overheating.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[Multi-tool]] inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 200 × [[Iridium]] + 100 × [[Heridium]] → 1 × Beam Coolant System Tau
