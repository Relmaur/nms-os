# The T400

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**The T400** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is sea-green in color, with black highlights. The barrel contains three wide decorative lime-green stripes. This tool has two muzzles, one above the other.

## Acquirement

The T400 is a 24-slot multi-tool offered in trade to player *chipzilla* for 193,725 [[units]].

## Trivia

There appears to be a minor bug in the naming of this multi-tool. Instead of a middle word between *The* and *T400*, there is simply an extra space, as if the name generator failed to provide one. The same issue can be seen in the [[The Totzerid T600|The T600]].
