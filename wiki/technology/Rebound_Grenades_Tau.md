**Rebound Grenades** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Rebound Grenades** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade to the [[multi-tool]] [[Plasma Launcher]].

## Game description

Plasma Launcher shells rebound from planetary structures before ignition.

Customized explosive grenades are fired on a revolving axis. User will observe unpredictable placement of ensuing blast zone.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
