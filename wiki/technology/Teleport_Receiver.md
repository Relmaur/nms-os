# Teleport Receiver

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Ship
- **Type:** Ship-Mounted Teleportation Device
- **Updated:** Beyond

**Teleport Receiver** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Teleport Receiver** is a starship technology, which increases ship material teleport range from **50u** to **150u**.

## Game description

A device that modulates standard starship communication signals to enable the transport of matter as well as data.

Starships fitted with such a device can send and receive products and substances to a user's exosuit over a long distance.

## Crafting

Teleport Receiver can be built using the following ingredients:

- Antimatter ×1 + Wiring Loom ×3 → Teleport Receiver

Teleport Receiver can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Wiring Loom ×1

Teleport Receiver can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Teleport Receiver → Wiring Loom ×1

## Additional information

The Teleport Receiver is a ship upgrade that once installed allows moving items to and from your ship from a longer range.

Default max 50u, with this upgrade max 150u. Thus tripling the distance, and the effective area coverage times 9 (Pi * radius2).

In practice this means you can now often reach the ship from anywhere in a base or Trade Outpost etc. and not just when right next to it.
