# Beam Intensifier Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Mining Beam laser is strengthened to speed up terrain destruction.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Beam Intensifier Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Beam Intensifier Sigma** is a first-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Mining Beam laser is strengthened to speed up terrain destruction.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's Multi-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Heridium]] + 25 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Beam Intensifier Sigma
