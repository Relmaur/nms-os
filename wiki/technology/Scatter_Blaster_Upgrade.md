# Scatter Blaster Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Scatter Blaster Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Scatter Blaster Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Scatter Blaster Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Upgrade properties

Scatter Blaster Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Reload Time*- *Damage*- *Clip Size:* Allows the Scatter Blaster to hold more than 96 projectiles (up to 120)
- *Fire Rate*- *Shots per Burst:* Adds extra shots for the burst (2 shots with 8 projectiles each are fired per burst initially [up to 5])
- *Burst Cooldown*

Up to three Scatter Blaster Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a [[Multi-tool]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Scatter Blaster Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Scatter Blaster Modules.

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
