# Pressure Chamber

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Pressure Chamber** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

A small pressurised chamber, designed to create the compressed gas necessary for the device to function. Cracks have appeared in the chamber, causing it to leak.
