# Barrel Ioniser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** C
- **Type:** Boltcaster Upgrade
- **Stats:** +31% Accuracy
- **Added in:** Synthesis

**Barrel Ioniser** is a [[projectile technology]].

## Summary

**Barrel Ioniser** is a technology that augments the [[Boltcaster]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Boltcaster Upgrade|Boltcaster Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades.

## Game Description

Inserts a powerful field ionisation device into the barrel of the Boltcaster, increasing projectile stability through the air.

Improves device accuracy and allows the user to fire for longer periods without degrading combat performance.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 4 × [[Glass]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Barrel Ioniser

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Glass]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Glass]]
