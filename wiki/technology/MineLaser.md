# MineLaser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Weapon
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Type:** Mineral Extraction Beam
- **Description:** Directed laser tool for mining.
- **Added in:** Pre-release

**MineLaser** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**MineLaser** is a [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool]].

This tool is used to expose and captures minerals and released gasses. Sustained user operation can result in [[Sentinel]] scrutiny and possible attack. Also used in the damage and destruction of alien flora, alien fauna and Sentinels. Sustained use can increase the player's [[wanted level]].

It is also referred to as a **Mining Beam**.
