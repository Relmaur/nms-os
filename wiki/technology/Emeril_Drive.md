# Emeril Drive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Green Systems
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Emeril Drive** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Emeril Drive** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] and it is the second upgrade to the [[Hyperdrive]]. It allows warping to [[System colours|green coloured]] [[star system|star systems]].

The Emeril Drive is a direct upgrade to the [[Cadmium Drive]] in that it allows travel to Red stars without needing to also have a Cadmium Drive installed.

## Game description

Hyperdrive shield matrix is upgraded, preventing temporal anomalies when attempting to reach rare-class green stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on [[Space Anomaly]] for 120 Nanites, or rarely for free from a random Korvax entity when practicing language skills. Also rarely from a distress signal when comforting dying pilot.

Crafting the Emeril Drive requires [[Cadmium]] which can be found by:
- by mining in red coloured star systems using the [[Cadmium Drive]]
- dissasembling purchased starship upgrades

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Cadmium]] + 4 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Emeril Drive

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Cadmium]] + 2 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- Gives a bonus to hyperdrive range if the upgrade module is installed adjacent to the ship's hyperdrive or other drive modules.
