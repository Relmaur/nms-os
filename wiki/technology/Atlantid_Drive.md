# Atlantid Drive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** ?
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Purple Systems
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Atlantid Drive** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Atlantid Drive** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] added in [[Update 5.50|Worlds Part II]] and is a further upgrade to the [[Hyperdrive]]. It allows warping to [[System colours|purple coloured]] [[star system|star systems]].

The Atlantid Drive acts as a direct upgrade to the [[Indium Drive]] in that it allows travel to Red, Green and Blue stars without needing to also have an [[Indium Drive|Indium]], [[Emeril Drive|Emeril]] or [[Cadmium Drive]] installed.

## Game description

Sideloads a resonant memory packet into your warp computer, allowing the ship to pass through the dissonant space surrounding purple stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired during the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] questline.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Indium]] + 200 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]] → 1 × Atlantid Drive

- **Repair**: 125 × [[Indium]] + 100 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 125 × [[Indium]] + 100 × [[Atlantideum]] + 1 × [[Living Glass]]

## Upgrade progression

- [[Hyperdrive]]
- [[Cadmium Drive]]
- [[Emeril Drive]]
- [[Indium Drive]]
- [[Atlantid Drive]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- Gives a bonus to hyperdrive range if the upgrade module is installed adjacent to the ship's hyperdrive or other drive modules.
- In order to unlock the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] quest, both [[They Who Returned]] and [[The Atlas Path]] must first be completed.
