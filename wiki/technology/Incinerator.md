# Incinerator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-Tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Short-Range Fire Delivery Unit
- **Added in:** Orbital

The **Incinerator** is a multi-tool blueprint.

## Summary

The **Incinerator** is a [[Multi-tool]] weapon which cannot be currently obtained by normal means.

It is significantly overpowered against all enemies in the game. Plus it also affects fauna.

- It is considerably weaker against shielded players, but destroys unshielded players.

Its mining capabilities are not great. It destroys all trees and rocks it comes into contact with, but produces significantly reduced yields of resources from them compared to other weapons.

## Game Description

Specialist high temperature weapon attachment for the Multi-Tool. Access fire mode by pressing CYCLEWEAPON and fire weapon with ATTACK.

The Incinerator focuses internal lasers onto the naturally-occuring exhaust gases generated by the Multi-Tool. Ignition results in a highly destructive, if short range, inferno.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 200 × [[Deuterium]] → 1 × Incinerator

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 100 × [[Deuterium]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 100 × [[Deuterium]]

## Upgrades

The Incinerator currently has no upgrades.

## Recharge

The Incinerator is rechargeable (after a long period of use), using the following items:

- 800 [[Projectile Ammunition]]
- If supercharged, it takes 1000 Projectile Ammunition

## Additional Information

- The Incinerator was released for two days before being removed. However, players who obtained a multi-tool with the technology pre-installed retained it.
- Alternatively, players using a save editor could directly give it to themselves or unlock the blueprint for themself.
- In derelict freighters, its damage is reduced significantly.
- If supercharged, it does more damage, fires faster, and has bigger clips
