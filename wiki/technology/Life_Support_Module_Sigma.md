# Life Support Module Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the capacity of a user's Life Support systems
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Life Support Module Sigma** is a life support upgrade.

## Summary

The **Life Support Module Sigma** is a first-level upgrade for the [[Life support]] system.

## Game description

Increases available energy within user's Life Support Systems. Allows for extended periods of planetary exploration.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 20 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Life Support Module Sigma

## Upgrade progression

- Life Support Module Sigma
- [[Life Support Module Tau]]
- Life Support Module Theta

## Additional information

- Works automatically when located in a player's [[Exosuit]] inventory alongside the existing [[Life support|Life Support]] unit.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
