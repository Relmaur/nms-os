# Sub-Light Amplifier

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Interplanetary
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Pulse Engine Upgrade
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Sub-Light Amplifier** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Sub-Light Amplifier** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] that increases [[Pulse Engine]] speed.

## Game description

A highly advanced upgrade for the Pulse Engine, vastly increasing interplanetary speed.

Naturally occuring frameshift effects within the Pulse Engine are amplified by tritium-based resonators, increasing its maximum sub-light speed.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on board [[Space Anomaly]] for 460  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 3 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] + 200 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Sub-Light Amplifier

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 50 × [[Platinum]] + 100 × [[Tritium]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 50 × [[Platinum]] + 100 × [[Tritium]]

## Additional information

- Gives a bonus to the speed and maneuverability if the upgrade is installed next to the Pulse Engine or other [[Pulse Engine Upgrade|Pulse Engine Upgrades]].
- All Sentinel starships reject this upgrade with the message: "Aeron Protocol rejects alien Technology".

## Release history

- [[Synthesis]] - Added as a technology.
- [[Origins]] - Blueprint changed, previously used [[Carbon Crystal]].
- [[Update 4.20|Interceptor]] - Sentinel starships disallow the upgrade from being installed.
