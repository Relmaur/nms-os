# Daedalus Engine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Power Unit
- **Added in:** ExoMech

**Daedalus Engine** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

The **Daedalus Engine** provides power for the [[Minotaur]] exocraft.

## Game description

A compact reactor unit, this engine is the heart of the Minotaur. It is mounted on a gyroscope to prevent fuel incidents during rapid movement.

Powered by conventional carbon-based fuels.
