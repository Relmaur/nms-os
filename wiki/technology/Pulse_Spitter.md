# Pulse Spitter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Type:** Weapon
- **Description:** The Pulse Spitter releases short, rapid sequences of fire.
- **Stats:** Damage = 34x3 Mining Power = 7x3
- **Recharge:** Projectile Ammunition
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

The **Pulse Spitter** is a component of the multi-tool.

## Summary

The Pulse Spitter is a [[multi-tool]] weapon which utilises [[Projectile Ammunition]] to produce a rapid-fire barrage of plasma. Each shot fires three pellets in a small spread, making the Pulse Spitter a good weapon for shooting ground and underwater [[fauna]].

## Game description

Specialist **rapid-fire** weapon attachment for the *Multi-Tool*. Access fire mode by pressing [Triangle/G] and fire weapon with [Left Mouse Button].

The *Pulse Spitter* redirects Multi-Tool optics to ionise inserted *Metallic Elements*. These projectiles are launched in a rapid-fire spread, bombarding potential targets with a **wave of high-energy plasma**.

## Additional notes

- Pulse Spitter can hold 80 rounds at a time.
- This weapon is ideal for mid to close-range skirmishes, particularly against fast-moving targets.
- It works like a heavy machine gun, with high recoil, high fire rate, high damage and medium accuracy
