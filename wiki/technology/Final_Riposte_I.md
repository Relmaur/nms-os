# Final Riposte I

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Crack Nose
- **Description:** provides 14 slots
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

## Summary

**Final Riposte I** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is red, gray and purple, with claw-like extensions on the butt of the tool. All operating lights are green.

## Acquirement

The Final Riposte I is a 14-slot multi-tool of [[Vy'keen]] origin.
