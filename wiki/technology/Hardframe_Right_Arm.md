# Hardframe Right Arm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Limb Replacement
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Hardframe Right Arm** is a technology.

## Summary

**Hardframe Right Arm** is a weapon which can be mounted on a [[Minotaur]].

## Game description

A collection of salvaged components from a [[Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech|Sentinel Hardframe]], reconditioned to fit the Minotaur Heavy Exocraft Hybrid.

This limb override also functions as its own dedicated combat system. It deploys high-velocity stun projectiles and serves as an excellent support weapon to the Minotaur's primary cannon.

Use <weapon select key> to cycle available weapon modes.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 30 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 40 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]] → 1 × Hardframe Right Arm

- **Repair**: 15 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 20 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 15 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 20 × [[Pugneum]] + 1 × [[Hydraulic Wiring]]

## Additional Information

There is an adjacency bonus with the [[Liquidator Left Arm]], but the effect is unknown.
