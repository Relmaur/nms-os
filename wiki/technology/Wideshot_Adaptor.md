# Wideshot Adaptor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 5
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Wideshot Adaptor** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Wideshot Adaptor** is a [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

## Game description

Boltcaster shots are granted a wide cone of destruction.

Reduces device's range and rate of fire, encouraging combat with targets in close proximity to user.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Sources

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 40 × [[Copper]] + 100 × [[Heridium]] + 50 × [[Iron]] → 1 × Wideshot Adaptor

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- Multiple bolts are used for each shot
- Reduces clip size while in use
- Can be combined with damage upgrades to kill [[sentinels]] in a single shot

- [[Release]] - initial game component
- [[Pathfinder]] - dropped from the game, replaced by [[Pulse Spitter]] technology
