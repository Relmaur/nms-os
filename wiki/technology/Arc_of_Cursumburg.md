# Arc of Cursumburg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Rail gun
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**Arc of Cursumburg** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is mostly brown, with green inside the trigger guard and claw-like protrusions on the barrel.

## Acquirement

The 14-slot Arc of Cursumburg was found while helping a [[Vy'keen]].
