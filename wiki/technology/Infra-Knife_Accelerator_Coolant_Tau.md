# Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Infra-Knife Companion Unit
- **Description:** Reduces Infra-Knife cool-down times
- **Stats:** Heat Dispersion = +8%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Tau** is a weapons upgrade.

## Summary

**Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Tau** is [[weapons technology]] for a [[starship]]'s [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]].

This upgrade reduces the Infra-Knife Accelerator cool-down times. It is the second of three upgrade levels.

## Game description

Reduces time between shots when using the Starship's Infra-Knife Accelerator weapon mode by a moderate amount.

## Sources

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 80 × [[Thamium9]] + 70 × [[Chrysonite]] + 120 × [[Iridium]] → 1 × Infra-Knife Accelerator Coolant Tau

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's starship inventory.
- Needs no fuel or recharging
