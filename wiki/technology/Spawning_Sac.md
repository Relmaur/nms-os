# Spawning Sac

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship - Inventory
- **Type:** Storage Module
- **Updated:** Echoes

**Spawning Sacs** are a Living starship technology.

## Summary

**Spawning Sacs** are a Living Ship technology. They allow the player to increase the inventory of a Living Ship, serving as the Living Ship equivalent of Storage Augmentation.

## Game Description

An additional storage module for Sentient starships. Upon hatching, the parasite inside this sac is grafted upon the vessel, exchanging the use of its cavernous innards for vital nutrients from the host.

Use // to trigger growth and begin the hatching process.

## Sources

- Brought by Organic Frigates from expeditions.

## Additional Information

Spawning Sacs share an icon with Seeds of Glass.
