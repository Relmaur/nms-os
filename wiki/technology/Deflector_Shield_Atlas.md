# Deflector Shield (Atlas)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Type:** Spacecraft Hull Protection
- **Description:** Energy shield that protects spacecraft from laser fire, in-flight projectiles and accidental space debris interaction.
- **Recharge:** Oxide elements, any Shielding object
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

A **Deflector Shield** is a [[health technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]].

## Summary

**Deflector shields** are the standard means of protecting a [[starship]] against enemy fire or collisions from space objects like [[asteroid|asteroids]] or [[space station|space stations]]. It will prevent both from making contact with the ship, but require [[oxide elements]] to keep them operating. If not recharged and permitted to deplete, the shield will drop, leaving the spaceship vulnerable to critical damage.

Shields are easily recharged with the [[Quick Menu|quick menu]], but at this time, only raw materials appear - no [[Shielding Shard|shielding shards]], etc.

## Game Description

Energy shield that protects spacecraft from laser fire, in-flight projectiles and accidental space debris interaction.
User is advised that Oxide elements are required to recharge device.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.

## Crafting

- **Repair**: 3 × [[Carite Sheet]] + 250 × [[Heridium]]
