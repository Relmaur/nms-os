## Summary

**Stable Iguenowah-Ropes** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is steel-grey in color with red vein-like streaks, and claw-like protrusions on the rear. All operating lights are green.

The Stable Iguenowah-Ropes is a 24-slot multi-tool available for trade or purchase.
