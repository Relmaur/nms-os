# Mounted Flamethrower

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Type:** Exocraft Weapon
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Mounted Flamethrower** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Mounted Flamethrower** provides Flamethrower capabilities to all Exocrafts.

## Game description

A mounted flamethrower specifically designed for the Exocraft. <br>Causes Extreme damage at close range

Use **G** to cycle through available weapon modes
