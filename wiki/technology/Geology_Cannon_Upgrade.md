# Geology Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Geology Cannon Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Geology Cannon Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Geology Cannon Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Geology Cannon]].

## Upgrade properties

Geology Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Projectile Velocity:* increases the travel speed of the explosive
- *Damage*
- *Explosion Radius:* increases the size of the explosion, allowing it to make bigger holes and damage enemies in a wider area

Up to three Geology Cannon Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a [[Multi-tool]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Geology Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Geology Cannon Modules.

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Geology Cannon Upgrades if they are adjacent to [[Geology Cannon]] or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- When using a save editor to compare a Plasma Launcher Upgrade with a Geology Cannon Upgrade of the same class, any given five digit Procedural ID will display the same name for both types, but they will always have stats different from each other.
