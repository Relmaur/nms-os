# Solenoid

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Solenoid** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Solenoid** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A relay switch, intended to aid the powering up of the device. The connectors have become corroded, and the solenoid is no longer functioning.
