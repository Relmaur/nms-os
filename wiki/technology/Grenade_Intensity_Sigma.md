# Grenade Intensity Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Plasma Launcher Companion Unit
- **Description:** Grenades cause heightened damage.
- **Stats:** Damage = +150.0

**Grenade Intensity Sigma** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade to the [[multi-tool]] [[Plasma Launcher]].

## Game Description

Plasma Launcher grenades cause heightened damage within their localized zone of impact.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
