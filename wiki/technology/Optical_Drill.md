# Optical Drill

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Material Gain Amplifier
- **Stats:** Resource Mined +50%
- **Added in:** Aquarius

The **Optical Drill** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Optical Drill** is a [[Multi-tool]] upgrade for the Mining Laser, allowing for more resources to be collected per click.

## Game Description

Wafer-thin lens arrays are arranged in a highly calibrated matrix, focusing the Mining Beam on an atomic level.

This extra precision allows for highly efficient mining. Elements are extracted more efficiently, increasing the yield from items harvested with the Multi-Tool.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Eos]] in the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 5 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 3 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 3 × [[Solar Mirror]] → 1 × Optical Drill

- **Repair**: 3 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 2 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Solar Mirror]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 3 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 2 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 2 × [[Solar Mirror]]

## Additional Information

Installing the Optical Drill will also increase the amount harvested from plants by 50%, with an extra 6% bonus from each connected mining laser module. If you surround the Optical drill on all four sides it ends up giving a bonus of 74%.
