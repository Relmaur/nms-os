# Thermic Layer Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Level:** 3
- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Utilities
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Description:** Further extended shielding from extreme cold damage on frozen worlds.
- **Stats:** Cold Protection = 490.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Thermic Layer Theta** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

The **Thermic Layer Theta** upgrade protects the user from cold temperatures.

## Game description

Further extended shielding from extreme cold damage on frozen worlds.

Flow of liquid calidium through suit capillaries is monitored and modified by a central temperature governance chip, allowing for targeted relief and more efficient usage.

Device is charged by [[Oxide elements]]. Operates automatically once constructed within user's [[exosuit]] inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 2 × [[Microdensity Fabric]] + 1 × [[Electron Vapour]] + 1 × [[Gravitino Ball]] → 1 × Thermic Layer Theta

This identical formula is used to craft four separate [[hazard protection]] upgrades for the exosuit:
- [[Coolant Network Theta]]
- [[Radiation Deflector Theta]]
- Thermic Layer Theta
- [[Toxin Suppressor Theta]]
