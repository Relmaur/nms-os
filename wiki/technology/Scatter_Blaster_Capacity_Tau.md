# Scatter Blaster Capacity Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scatter Blaster
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases amount of ammunition held by the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster.
- **Stats:** Clip Size = 29%

**Scatter Blaster Capacity Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Scatter Blaster Capacity Tau** is a second-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Game description

Increases amount of ammunition held by the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon mode before a reload is required by a moderate amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 20 × [[Zinc]] + 10 × [[Nickel]] → 1 × Scatter Blaster Capacity Tau

- Needs no fuel or recharging
