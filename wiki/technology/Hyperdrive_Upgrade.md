# Hyperdrive Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Hyperdrive Upgrade** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Hyperdrive Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Hyperdrive Modules]], are upgrades granting bonuses to the [[Hyperdrive]] of a [[Starship]].

## Upgrade Properties

Hyperdrive Upgrades grant variable magnitudes of the following bonus based on their class:
- *Hyperdrive Range:* Increases the maximum distance of a hyperjump by a fixed amount.
A-class and S-class Hyperdrive Upgrades also grant +100% of the following bonus:
- [[Warp Cell|Warp Cell Efficiency]]: Increases the fuel range of the ship by 100% each.
  - It has a stacking effect: 1 = 50% fuel cost / 2 = 33% fuel cost / 3 = 25% fuel cost (5% per jump!)

Up to three Hyperdrive Upgrades can be installed in the [[Starship]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Hyperdrive Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Hyperdrive Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 13 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 12 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional Information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Hyperdrive Upgrades if they are adjacent to [[Hyperdrive]] or each other, increasing their Hyperdrive Range bonuses above listed values. Installing [[Cadmium Drive]], [[Emeril Drive]], and [[Indium Drive]] as well as the [[Emergency Warp Unit]] adjacent to other Hyperdrive Upgrades also increases Hyperdrive Range bonuses.
- At the maximum three upgrades, a Hyperdrive can get 2,200 [S] - 2,500 [X] ly and ten jumps/warp cell
