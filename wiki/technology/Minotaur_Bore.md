# Minotaur Bore

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Minotaur
- **Type:** Minotaur Digging Laser
- **Added in:** Origins

**Minotaur Bore** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Minotaur Bore** provides [[Terrain Manipulator]] capabilities to the [[Minotaur Laser]]. It is, however, not very fuel-efficient. You refuel it with [[Carbon]], [[Condensed Carbon]], or [[Oxygen]]; so bring or harvest plenty.

## Game description

Adds Terrain Manipulation capability to the laser array of the Minotaur Heavy Exosuit Hybrid.

A special mount allows the rapid deployment of a high-energy terrain excavator, without clogging the Minotaur's delicate joints and servos with dirt.
