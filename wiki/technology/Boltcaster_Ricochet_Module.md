# Boltcaster Ricochet Module

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Boltcaster Upgrade
- **Stats:** Ricochet Module: Enabled
- **Added in:** Desolation

**Boltcaster Ricochet Module** is a [[projectile technology]].

## Summary

**Boltcaster Ricochet Module** is a technology that augments the [[Boltcaster]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon. Its effects stack with those provided by [[Boltcaster Upgrade|Boltcaster Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades. A blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]] for 90  Nanites.

## Game Description

Creates an antimatter field around the shots of the [[Boltcaster]], causing them to be repelled by non-damaged surfaces.

Enables ricochet, causing projectiles to bounce if they hit a target they do not damage.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 4 × [[Glass]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Boltcaster Ricochet Module

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Glass]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Glass]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
