# Flight Assist Override

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Interplanetary
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Pulse Engine Upgrade
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Flight Assist Override** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Flight Assist Override** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] that increases [[Pulse Engine]] maneuverability.

## Game description

This grey-market electronics module disables critical safety and pilot-assistance modules usually baked into the starship's fly-by-wire control system. Despite its dubious legal status and its implication in a series of high-speed asteroid impacts, this module remains popular with smugglers, mercenaries, and others who think of themselves as advanced pilots.

Specifically tuned to increase the ability of the starship to drift during spaceflight.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on board [[Space Anomaly]] for 460  Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 60 × [[Platinum]] + 200 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Flight Assist Override

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 30 × [[Platinum]] + 100 × [[Tritium]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 30 × [[Platinum]] + 100 × [[Tritium]]

## Additional information

- Gives a bonus to the speed and maneuverability if the upgrade is installed next to the Pulse Engine or other [[Pulse Engine Upgrade|Pulse Engine Upgrades]].
