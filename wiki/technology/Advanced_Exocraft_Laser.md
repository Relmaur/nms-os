# Advanced Exocraft Laser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Weapon Upgrades
- **Stats:** +20% Mining Laser Power, +100% Advanced Exocraft Mining Laser
- **Type:** Exocraft Mining Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Advanced Exocraft Laser** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Advanced Exocraft Laser** is the sole technology upgrade available for the [[Exocraft Mining Laser]]. The upgrade increases the weapon's mining power and grants it the ability to harvest tough [[mineral|minerals]].

## Game description

Increases power of the Exocraft Mining Laser.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pugneum]] + 50 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Advanced Exocraft Laser

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Pugneum]] + 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]]
