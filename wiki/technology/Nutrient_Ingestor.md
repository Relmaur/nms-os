# Nutrient Ingestor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Feeding Technology
- **Added in:** Worlds Part II

**Nutrient Ingestor** is a technology.

## Summary

**Nutrient Ingestor** is an [[Exosuit]] [[technology]] that improves exosuit performance by ingesting cooked items.

## Game Description

The nutrient ingestor improves exosuit performance via the automatic portioning and processing of various cooked items.

Select Begin Ingestion to commence automatic consumption of supplied ingredients.

## Source

This blueprint can be bought from the [[Exosuit]] upgrade merchant on the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 30 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Lubricant]] + 2 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × Nutrient Ingestor

- **Dismantle** returns: 15 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Lubricant]] + 1 × [[Microprocessor]]
