# Homingbolt Adaptor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 5
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Boltcaster rounds trace and follow target movement. Best used on lifeforms with unpredictable attack patterns, or those likely to attempt escape from user during combat. Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Homingbolt Adaptor** is a [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

## Summary

The Homingbolt Adaptor ensures that rounds from the [[Boltcaster]] trace and follow their target. This is useful when fighting opponents that move a lot, or when attacking a target that will likely try to escape.

## Game description

Boltcaster rounds trace and follow target movement.
Best used on lifeforms with unpredictable attack patterns, or those likely to attempt escape from user during combat.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Craft

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 5 × [[AquaSphere]] + 200 × [[Titanium]] + 200 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Homingbolt Adaptor

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's [[Multi-tool]] inventory alongside the [[Boltcaster]]
