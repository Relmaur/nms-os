# Beam Focus Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Laser
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Mining Beam Companion Unit
- **Description:** Faster Mining Beam processes and resource-rich materials
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Beam Focus Sigma** is a Multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Beam Focus Sigma** is a first-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Faster Mining Beam processes on resource-rich materials.
User will enjoy quicker element extraction from crystals, rocky outcrops, and planetary vegetation.
Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 20 × [[Iron]] + 20 × [[Heridium]] + 25 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Beam Focus Sigma

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- This upgrade replaced [[Beam Focus V1]] from the initial [[Release]] era.
