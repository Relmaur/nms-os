# Boltcaster (Atlas)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Type:** Energy Projectile Weapon
- **Description:** A powerful defensive weapon.
- **Recharge:** Isotope elements
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Boltcaster** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The Boltcaster is an [[projectile technology|attachment]] for the [[multi-tool]], and is considered a very powerful weapon. It's perfect for defending against hostile lifeforms and [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].
- To activate, switch from the [[Mining Beam]] by pressing the triangle button  (PS4) or the 'G' key (PC)
- To reload, press the square button  (PS4) or the 'R' key (PC)

## Game description

Combat-focused weapon. User is advised that [[Isotope elements]], such as [[Carbon]], are required to recharge device.

Use can result in targeted entity death.

## Recharge

The Boltcaster is rechargeable using following items:
- 1x [[Carbon]] by 0.5%
- 1x [[Thamium9]] by 1%
- 1x [[Plutonium]] by 1.5%
- 1x [[Power Gel]] by 25%
- 1x [[Power Canister]] by 37%
- 1x [[Power Reservoir]] by 50%

## Additional information

- The spread is quadratic and not circular like in most games.
- The "Shortburst Adaptor" and "Wideshot Adaptor" have been replaced by the "Pulse Spitter" and "Scatter Blaster" respectively
