# The Selmikka-Tosu v6.28

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Release

## Summary

**The Selmikka-Tosu v6.28** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is orange, blue, and grey in color, with white and grey stripes on the barrel. It appears to have two muzzles, one above the other.

## Acquirement

The Selmikka-Tosu v6.28 was offered for sale in a [[Space station]] of the Auobreioaar-Guca [[Star system]] (located in the [[Hyades]] galaxy). The player had answered a question of the [[Vy'keen]] Alien Entity.
