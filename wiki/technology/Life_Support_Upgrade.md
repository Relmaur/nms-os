# Life Support Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Life Support
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Life Support Upgrade** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

**Life Support Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Life Support Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to [[Life Support]].

## Upgrade properties

Up to three **Life Support Upgrades** can be installed in each inventory of the [[Exosuit]] which can accept [[Technology]] --- installing four or more upgrades in a given inventory will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

Life Support Upgrades have the following bonuses:
- **Life Support Tanks:** increases Life Support capacity
- **Solar Panel Power:** during the day, reduces the Life Support drain of all movement functions

The table shows the minimum and maximum values of the bonuses, while the number of bonuses indicates how many are granted randomly for each class.

## Crafting

Life Support Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Life Support Modules.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
