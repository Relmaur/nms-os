# Security Alarm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Security Alarm** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Security Alarm** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

A small light/klaxon hybrid, designed to warn of unauthorised access to the device. Damage to the alarm has caused it to overload and short other nearby circuits.
