# Shadow of Eharasaki

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Crack Nose
- **Description:** provides 12 slots
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**Shadow of Eharasaki** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is red and grey in color, with a pink crystalline barrel grip. This tool has claw extensions on the butt, and all operating lights are green.

## Acquirement

The Shadow of Eharasaki is a 12-slot [[Vy'keen]] multi-tool offered for sale to player *fathergascan* for 81,000 units.
