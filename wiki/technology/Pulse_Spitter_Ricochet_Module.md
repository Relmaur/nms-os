# Pulse Spitter Ricochet Module

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Pulse Spitter Upgrade
- **Stats:** +31% Accuracy
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Pulse Spitter Ricochet Module** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Pulse Spitter Ricochet Module** is [[projectile technology]] that augments the [[Pulse Spitter]], a [[Multi-tool]] weapon.

Its effects stack with those provided by [[Pulse Spitter Upgrade|Pulse Spitter Upgrades]], but this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for triggering stronger [[upgrade bonus|upgrade bonuses]] on those upgrades. A blueprint can be purchased from [[Iteration: Eos]] for 180  Nanites.

## Game Description

Creates an antimatter field around the shots of the Pulse Spitter, causing them to be repelled by non-damaged surfaces.

Enables ricochet, causing projectiles to bounce if they hit a target they do not damage.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Pulse Spitter Ricochet Module

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
