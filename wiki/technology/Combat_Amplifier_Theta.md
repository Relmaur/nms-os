# Combat Amplifier Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Mining Beam
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Combat Amplifier Theta** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Combat Amplifier Theta** is the third-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game description

Provides a markedly more destructive laser during Mining Beam combat.

Adds photon agitation technology to the Mining Beam's capacitor banks.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's Multi-tool inventory.

Stats Damage +1.5

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 400 × [[Heridium]] + 200 × [[Iron]] + 20 × [[Radnox]] → 1 × Combat Amplifier Theta
