# Daedalus Engine Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Daedalus Engine
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Daedalus Engine Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Daedalus Engine Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Minotaur Engine Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Minotaur]]'s [[Daedalus Engine]] .

## Upgrade properties

Daedalus Engine Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Boost Tank Size*- *Fuel Usage*
Up to three Daedalus Engine Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a Minotaur; installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Daedalus Engine Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Minotaur Engine Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
