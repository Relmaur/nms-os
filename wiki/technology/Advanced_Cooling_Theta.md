# Advanced Cooling Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Significantly reduces Photon Cannon cool-down times
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Advanced Cooling Theta** is a weapons upgrade.

## Summary

The **Advanced Cooling Theta** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

## Game description

Photon Cannon cool-down times are significantly reduced.

Ship-on-ship destruction becomes a more fulfilling endeavor through reduction in photon discharge interruptions.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.
