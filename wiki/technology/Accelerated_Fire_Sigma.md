# Accelerated Fire Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the photon cannon's rate of fire
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Accelerated Fire Sigma** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade.

## Summary

The **Accelerated Fire Sigma** upgrade modifies the rate of fire for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]]. This is a first level upgrade.

## Game description

Photo Cannon fires at an increased rate.

Rapid fire modification that increases potential of damage, death and destruction on user's chosen targets.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.
