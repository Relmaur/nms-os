# Chloroplast Membrane Implant

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Launch system
- **Type:** Neural assembly recharger
- **Added in:** Endurance
- **Description:** Recharges Neural Assembly during daylight

The **Chloroplast Membrane Implant** is a Living starship technology.

## Summary

The **Chloroplast Membrane Implant** is a [[Living Ship (Starship)|Living Ship]] [[technology]] and can evolve into [[Chloroplast Membrane|Chloropast Membrane]] which allows allows automatic recharging of Neural Assembly during daylight.

## Game description

A pre-grown bionic implant, designed to facilitate the fusion of bio-technology to sentient starships. Use  to begin the installation process.

An infusion of cyanobacteria fills the membrane, which rests just beneath the ship's translucent dermal layer.

The membrane will recharge the Neural Assembly automatically when exposed to sunlight.

## Sources

The Chloroplast Membrane Implant is grown from [[Psychonic Egg]].

## Additional information

The Chloroplast Membrane Implant shares an icon with the [[Fragile Shell]].
