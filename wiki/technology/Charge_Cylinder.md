# Charge Cylinder

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Power supply
- **Type:** Energy Source
- **Description:** specialized power storage unit
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Charge Cylinder** is a power storage unit.

## Summary

A **Charge Cylinder** is used to power devices such as the player's [[Multi-tool]].

## Game description

Specialized Power storage unit. Supplies instant power to targeted Laser and Plasma devices on user demand. Vital to mining, and sometimes self-defense.

## Additional information

Based on phrases datamined from [[Update 1.03]], a Charge Cylinder is strong enough to power up an entire Hospital facility.
