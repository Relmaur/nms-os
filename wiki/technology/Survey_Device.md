# Survey Device

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool - Scanner
- **Type:** Analysis Visor Upgrade
- **Updated:** Frontiers

**Survey Device** is a Multi-tool technology.

## Summary

**Survey Device** is a Multi-tool technology and an upgrade to the Analysis Visor. It enables the Surveying Mode, a real-time terrain analysis to the scanning array.

## Game description

A self-contained circuitry upgrade for the Analysis Visor, adding real-time analysis to the scanning array.

Toggle between Visor mode with 1/3. Use Surveying Mode to detect hidden terrain resources.

## Sources

The blueprint can be acquired from Iteration: Eos on the Space Anomaly for 320 Nanites.

## Build

Survey Device can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Magnetic Resonator ×3 + Quantum Computer ×1 + Wiring Loom ×2 → Survey Device

Survey Device can be repaired using the following ingredients:

- Magnetic Resonator ×2 + Quantum Computer ×1 + Wiring Loom ×2

Survey Device can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Survey Device → Magnetic Resonator ×2 + Quantum Computer ×1 + Wiring Loom ×2

## Surveying Mode

This device allows the player to look for Power Hotspots, which are shown by Electromagnetic radiation. Following the pulsing signals on the left and right of the screen will lead the player to these spots, which are marked by lightning bolt symbols.

### Usage

Use the following method to find a hotspot.

- Activate your Visor by Holding F/.../...
- Toggle between surveying different Hotspot types with 1 and 3
- Notice the pulsing signals. These will appear when the player is closer than 400u to a hotspot while the Visor is active. Turn around until the pulsing signals are both on the left and right of the HUD.
- Notice the distance value. Keep walking forward so that the distance value *decreases*. Keep the pulses centred.
- When the distance is less than **5** units, the analysis will start automatically.
- Click and hold the symbol to mark a waypoint.

You will need to purchase the blueprints for the respective device to harvest the different hotspot types. The blueprint can be obtained from the Construction Research Station aboard the Space Anomaly.

- Mineral Extractor
- Gas Extractor
- Electromagnetic Generator

### Usage warning

Walk carefully. While distracted, it is easy to fall into holes, step on hazardous flora, or walk into the open mouth of a dinosaur.

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to the Analysis Sensor or other Scanner technologies, the player will receive additional scanning bonuses, although not to the Survey Device itself.
- Building this upgrade is also one of the steps of the secondary mission Industrial Surveying
- It is possible to locate the direction of hotspot from a distance more than 400u (This requires listening to the in-game sounds for the "ringing". When this ringing can be heard in both ears you are facing towards the hotspot)
