# Transmission Box

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Transmission Box** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Transmission Box** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The gearing mechanism for the device. The lubricant that keeps it operational appears to have been replaced with an otherworldly green goo, and the gears are now stuck.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Mordite]] + 25 × [[Faecium]]
