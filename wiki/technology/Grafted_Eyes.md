# Grafted Eyes

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Spacecraft Laser Device
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Grafted Eyes** is a Living starship weapon.

## Summary

**Grafted Eyes** is one of the primary Living [[starship]] weapons, and is an alternative to the more common [[Spewing Vents]]. Both can be used to mine [[asteroid|asteroids]] in space, which hold valuable resources.

## Game description

A complex array of membranes and lens, fused atop a high-energy laser array. Incineration awaits those that meet their stare.

## Crafting

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Hypnotic Eye]] + 20 × [[Viscous Fluids]]

## Combat Properties

Grafted Eyes have an unusually long range for a starship weapon, having the ability to hit targets from up to 3000 distance units (3000u) away (for reference, fugitive [[pirate]] ships will attack the player if they get within 1500u). By default, the weapon deals damage over time to targets at a tick rate of 5 damage ticks per second, overheating after 3 second of continuous fire. Upon overheating, Grafted Eyes take 2 seconds by default to recover from overheating.

Other than its larger overheat tolerance, Grafted Eyes behave identical to [[Phase Beam|Phase Beams]], having the same hit-scan mechanic with no lock-on potential.

Grafted Eyes deal 180 base damage per tick (pairs of ticks are visually combined to display 360 base damage per 0.4 seconds) to the following targets: [[fauna]], starships, [[Space Fleets]], and destructible obstacles such as Reinforced Doors ([[Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]]). Against starships in particular, the starship weapon does *not* deal any bonus damage to the rear (that is, it deals 1x damage to the rear).

Grafted Eyes deal 36 base damage per tick (pairs of ticks are visually combined to display 72 base damage per 0.4 seconds) to all planetary [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].

## Mining Properties

When used to harvest a planet's [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]], Grafted Eyes have a base mining power per tick of 36 (pairs of mining damage ticks are visually combined to display 72 per 0.4 seconds).

## Upgrades

- **Grafted Eyes Node** - Potential improvements include Damage and Heat Dispersion (see info in gallery below).
- **Grafted Eyes Node** can evolve for  Nanites: C to B - 210  Nanites, B to A - 310  Nanites, A to S - 430  Nanites.
- The total number of nanites required to evolve a **Grated Eyes Node** from C to S is 950  Nanites.

## Additional information

- Upgrade Nodes can be obtained by shooting [[Void Egg (Space Encounter)|Void Eggs]] in [[Space Encounter|Space Encounters]]. They are more common in [[Uncharted system|uncharted]] and [[Abandoned system|abandoned]] systems.
