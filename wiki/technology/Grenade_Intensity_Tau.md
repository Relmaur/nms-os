# Grenade Intensity Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Plasma Launcher Companion Unit

- **Stats:** Damage = +300.0

*Grenade Intensity Tau*' is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Grenade Intensity Tau** is a [[grenade technology]] upgrade to the [[multi-tool]] [[Plasma Launcher]].

This technology increases damage of Plasma Launcher's fire upon impact of any surface.

## Game Description

Plasma Launcher grenades cause heightened damage within their localized zone of impact.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
