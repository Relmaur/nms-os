# Blaze Javelin Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Blaze Javelin Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Blaze Javelin Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Blaze Javelin Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Blaze Javelin]].

These were added as part of the new upgrade system introduced in the [[NEXT]] update.

## Upgrade properties

Blaze Javelin Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Time to Full Power:* Reduces charge time required to reach full damage (default: 2 seconds)

Up to three Blaze Javelin Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a [[Multi-tool]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Blaze Javelin Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Blaze Javelin Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Gravitino Ball]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 1 × [[Gravitino Ball]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
