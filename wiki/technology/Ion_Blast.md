# Ion Blast

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Non-guided energy blast modification
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Ion Blast** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

The **Ion Blast** is a [[weapons technology]] upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Photon Cannon]].

It fires a ball of charged particles, similar to a photon torpedo. Once fired, the charge can no longer be guided. The blueprint can be acquired from an NPC [[armourer]].

## Game description

Fires a large, concentrated ball of charged particles at forward targets, causing heavy damage. The charge is non-guided and will continue on its trajectory until it impacts with an obstacle.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Nickel]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Ion Blast

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
- A [[Science Terminal]] must be constructed before the [[blueprint]] for the Ion Blast becomes available.
