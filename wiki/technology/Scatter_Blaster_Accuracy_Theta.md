# Scatter Blaster Accuracy Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scatter Blaster
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases accuracy of the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Scatter Blaster Accuracy Theta** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Scatter Blaster Accuracy Theta** is a third-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Game description

Increases accuracy of the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon mode by reducing the spread of the projectile by a large amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 250 × [[Heridium]] + 100 × [[Zinc]] + 150 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Scatter Blaster Accuracy Theta

## Additional information

- Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory
- Needs no fuel or recharging
