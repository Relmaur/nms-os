# Resonance Matrix

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** ?
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 50 ly
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Resonance Matrix** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Resonance Matrix** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that allows warping to purple [[Star system|Star systems]].

## Game description

Sideloads a resonant memory packet into your warp computer, allowing the ship to pass through the dissonant space surrounding purple stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the Galactic Map.

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] is unlocked via progressing through the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] questline, without needing to research it at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Crystallised Heart]] + 160 × [[Atlantideum]] + 250 × [[Quartzite]] → 1 × Resonance Matrix

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Crystallised Heart]] + 80 × [[Atlantideum]] + 125 × [[Quartzite]]

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
