# Fleeting Daanseon-Uzan

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**Fleeting Daanseon-Uzan** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is orange, green, and grey in color, with green inside the trigger grip and yellow and grey stripes on the top of the barrel. The square barrel is split in half.
