# Mounted Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Mounted Cannon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Mounted Cannon Upgrade** is an exocraft upgrade.

## Summary

**Mounted Cannon Upgrade**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Exocraft Cannon Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to an [[Exocraft]]'s [[Exocraft Mounted Cannon|Mounted Cannon]]. They were introduced into the game in [[Update 1.63]].

## Upgrade properties

Exocraft Mounted Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Rate of Fire*- *Damage*- *Weapon Power Efficiency:* reduces weapon charge cost of each shot

Up to three Exocraft Mounted Cannon Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of an Exocraft --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

| Exocraft Mounted<br>Cannon Upgrade | Stats given: | Module Improves: | C-class | B-class | A-class | S-class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C: 1-2<br>B: 2-3<br>A/S: 3 | Rate of Fire | +2-3% | +4-5% | +6-7% | +8-10% |   |
| Damage | +1-3% | +3-6% | +6-9% | +9-12% |   |   |
| Weapon Power Efficiency | +1-5% | +6-10% | +11-15% | +16-20% |   |   |

## Crafting

Exocraft Mounted Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Exocraft Cannon Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
