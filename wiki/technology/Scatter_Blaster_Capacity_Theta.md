# Scatter Blaster Capacity Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scatter Blaster
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Companion Unit
- **Stats:** Clip Size = 39%
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Scatter Blaster Capacity Theta** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Scatter Blaster Capacity Theta** is a third-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Game description

Increases amount of ammunition held by the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon mode before a reload is required by a large amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 50 × [[Zinc]] + 150 × [[Nickel]] → 1 × Scatter Blaster Capacity Theta

## Additional information

- Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory
- Needs no fuel or recharging
