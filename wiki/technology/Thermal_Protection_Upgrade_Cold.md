# Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Thermal Protection
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Recharge:** Dioxite, Ion Battery, Advanced Ion Battery
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)** is an exosuit upgrade.

## Summary

**Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold)**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Thermal Protection Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting supplementary protection against low temperatures.

## Upgrade properties

Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold) function similarly to [[Hazard Protection]] but only protect the player from cold damage. Protection effectiveness depends on upgrade class (S-class upgrades offer the best protection).

| **Module**<br>**Name** | *Module*<br>*Improves* | B-class | A-class | S-class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Power Duration** | **Power Duration** | **Power Duration** |   |   |
| [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)]] | *Cold Protection* *Duration* | Short | Medium | Long |

The protection offered by Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold), unlike that offered by the [[Exosuit]]'s built-in thermal protection, does not recharge automatically while in locations without a significant cold hazard. Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold) must be manually recharged as needed using [[Dioxite]] (6.25%/unit) or [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]] (100.0%/unit).

Up to three protections upgrades can be installed in the technology section of the Exosuit. Installing four or more upgrades in the technology section of the Exosuit will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold) can only be obtained from opening Thermal Protection Modules offering protection against sub-zero temperatures.

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- It is not clear as to what percentages of Cold Protection and Cold Shielding are given. All that has been observed is that the depletion rate is the same regardless of whether it is a little too cold or way too cold, and it is also variable amongst the pool of upgrades by an adjustable time-frame.
- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Thermal Protection Upgrades (Cold) if they are adjacent to any of the following:
  - Hazard Protection
  - Shield Lattice
  - Aeration Membrane
  - Suspicious Hazard Protection Upgrades
  - [[Radiation Protection Upgrade|Radiation Protection Upgrades]]
  - [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Heat)|Thermal Protection Upgrades (Heat)]]
  - [[Toxic Protection Upgrade|Toxic Protection Upgrades]]

These only apply in actuality to basic upgrades with a visible percentage.
