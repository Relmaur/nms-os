# Pulse Spitter Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Pulse Spitter Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Pulse Spitter Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Pulse Spitter Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Pulse Spitter]].

## Upgrade properties

Pulse Spitter Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Clip Size:* allows the Pulse Spitter to hold more than 80 shots (each Pulse Spitter discharge consumes 3 shots)
- *Fire Rate*
- *Reload Time*
- *Damage*

Up to three Pulse Spitter Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of a [[Multi-tool]] --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Pulse Spitter Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Pulse Spitter Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Additional information

- [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] trigger for Pulse Spitter Upgrades if they are adjacent to Pulse Spitter, [[Amplified Cartridges]] or each other, increasing their bonuses above listed values.
- Pulse Spitter shots affected by Ricochet Module from Pulse Spitter Upgrades were always able to damage armored targets since the upgrade type's initial appearance in [[NEXT]]. [[Update 1.60]]'s ricochet fix primarily addressed issues with [[Boltcaster]] shots affected by Ricochet Module from [[Boltcaster Upgrade|Boltcaster Upgrades]].
