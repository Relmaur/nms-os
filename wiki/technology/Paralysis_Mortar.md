# Paralysis Mortar

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Grenade
- **Type:** Stun Grenades
- **Recharge:** Unstable Plasma
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

The **Paralysis Mortar** is a multi-tool component.

## Summary

The **Paralysis Mortar** is a [[technology]] component of the [[multi-tool]].

It temporarily stuns all entities caught in its blast radius, both living and robotic.

## Game description

Non-violent projectile weapon. Launched projectiles will incapacitate nearby targets with a burst of electrical energy. Effective against both biological and electronic entities.

Charged with [[Unstable Plasma]].

Press [secondary weapon fire control] to activate.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 2 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Paralysis Mortar

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Hermetic Seal]] + 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

## Upgrades

There are no associated upgrades with the Paralysis Mortar.

## Additional information

- It can store a maximum of 20 charges and can be recharged with Unstable Plasma.
