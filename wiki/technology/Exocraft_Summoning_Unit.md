# Exocraft Summoning Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Exocraft Technology
- **Added in:** Worlds Part II

The **Exocraft Summoning Unit** is an [[Exosuit]] technology upgrade.

## Summary

The Exocraft Summoning Unit is an [[Exosuit]] technology module that allows players to directly summon an [[Exocraft]] from **anywhere** in a [[galaxy]], bypassing the need for dedicated technologies like the [[Exocraft Summoning Station]] or the [[Orbital Exocraft Materialiser|Orbital Exocraft Materializer]]. However, while it is easy to obtain the blueprint for the summoning unit, the materials required to craft it can only be obtained after completing the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] side mission. As a result, it may be a long way off for some players.

## Game Description

A high-powered matter teleportation device, this personal translocator allows any connected Exocraft to be instantly transported to your location, from anywhere in the galaxy.

Interfaces with the Exosuit Quick Menu for ease of use.

## Source

This blueprint can be bought from the [[Exosuit]] upgrade merchant on the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 140 × [[Activated Quartzite]] + 3 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 90 × [[Crystallised Helium]] → 1 × Exocraft Summoning Unit

- **Dismantle** returns: 70 × [[Activated Quartzite]] + 2 × [[Quantum Computer]] + 45 × [[Crystallised Helium]]

## Notes

- If you plan on using Exocraft for planetary exploration, then the Exocraft Summoning Unit is a must.
