# Ariadne's Flame

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Type:** Exocraft Engine Upgrade
- **Added in:** Origins

**Ariadne's Flame** is a technology.

## Summary

**Ariadne's Flame ** is a propulsion upgrade for the [[Exocraft]].

Blueprint can be purchased for 180 nanites.

## Game description

A series of agile, high-powered jet thrusters mounted to the rear of the rear of the Minotaur. Precise use allows the Minotaur to quickly and powerfully traverse all manner of terrain.

Press and hold JUMP/IMG to activate. Limited in-flight control is possible.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Amino Chamber]] + 4 × [[Metal Plating]] + 10 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] → 1 × Ariadne's Flame

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Metal Plating]] + 5 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]]
