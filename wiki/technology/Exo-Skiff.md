# Exo-Skiff

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Type:** Deployable Fishing Platform
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Exo-Skiff** is a technology.

## Summary

**Exo-Skiff** is an [[Exosuit]] [[technology]] that gives the player access to a summoned vehicle. It deploys a static platform over any suitable [[Biome Subtype - Underwater|body of water]].

## Game Description

A deployable static platform, allowing the user to comfortably survey the seas in any weather. As well as providing a steady and easily accessible fishing spot, the Exo-Skiff also features abundant Cold Storage, granting additional space to store your catch.

Summon the Exo-Skiff to any suitable body of water using the Quick Menu ().

## Use

Can be placed from the summon vehicle tab of the [[Quick Menu]].
- Gives the player somewhere to stand over deep water.
- Has one local "Cold Storage" box with a shared inventory, which can store any [[fish]] or crafted [[Fishing Bait]], and a Portable Grill with the same function and shared Ingredients Storage as a [[Nutrient Processor]].
- The colour scheme can be customised.
- The skiff can move - use the summon menu to place it again, as you did when deploying it. It will slowly scoot toward that location instead of teleporting like the Exocraft do. You can even fish while it does so.

## Source

This blueprint can be bought from the [[Exosuit]] upgrade merchant on the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 120 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 30 × [[Salt]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] → 1 × Exo-Skiff

- **Dismantle** returns: 60 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 15 × [[Salt]] + 1 × [[Crystal Sulphide]]

## Additional Information

- Despite the description, the **Exo-Skiff** does not protect the player from hazardous [[weather]] unless in the exact center.
- The platform moves with any waves. The player can be knocked off the skiff in strong [[storm|storms]].
