# Complete Casting XIII

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon
- **Type:** Crystal nose
- **Description:** Basic brown with blue crystals
- **Added in:** Foundation

## Summary

**Complete Casting XIII** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is brown in color with dark brown vein-like striations and blue claw-like extensions on the back end. The muzzle and barrel grip are a light crystaline blue. All operating lights are green in color.

## Acquirement

Complete Casting XIII is [[Vy'keen]] multi-tool offered for sale to player *Mr_Master89*.
