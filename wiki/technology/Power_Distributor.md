# Power Distributor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Power Distributor** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Power Distributor** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The central power unit for the device. Damage to the unit means it is currently distributing all the power to one particular circuit, causing a dangerous short.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 15 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]]
