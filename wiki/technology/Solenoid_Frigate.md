# Solenoid (Frigate)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Frigate
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Solenoid** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Solenoid** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found on a [[frigate]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented functions.

## Game description

A relay switch, intended to aid the powering up of the device. The connectors have become corroded, and the solenoid is no longer functioning.

## Repair

- **Repair**: 25 × [[Silver]] + 10 × [[Ferrite Dust]]
