# Waveform Oscillator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Level:** S
- **Type:** Blaze Javelin Upgrade
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Waveform Oscillator** is an upgrade for the Blaze Javelin.

## Summary

**Waveform Oscillator** is a [[Multi-tool]] upgrade which applies a brief stunning effect to any target hit by the Javelin's beam. The stun is of the same duration regardless of the Javelin's charge level. The blueprint can be purchased in [[Space Anomaly]].

Waveform Oscillator needs to be crafted and then stored in a Multi-tool [[inventory]] slot for it to have its effect. Unlike [[Blaze Javelin Upgrade|Blaze Javelin Upgrades]], this technology does not count toward the three-upgrade limit, making it useful for enhancing [[Upgrade bonus|Upgrade bonuses]] on other upgrades.

## Game Description

Overloads the energy beam generated by the Blaze Javelin, causing it to deliver a power electrical charge at its poin of impact.

Will briefly stun any organic and inorganic entities that intersect the Blaze Javelin's beam.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 125 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 75 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Waveform Oscillator

- **Repair**: 64 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 37 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 64 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 37 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
