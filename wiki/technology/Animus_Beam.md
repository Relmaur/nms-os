# Animus Beam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapons
- **Type:** Soul Extractor
- **Added in:** Living Ship

The **Animus Beam** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Animus Beam** is a [[Multi-tool]] weapon which is obtained in the mission [[Starbirth]]. A blue variant of the [[Mining Beam]], its primary purpose is the extraction of [[Fragmented Qualia]] from [[fauna]], a lethal process which forfeits normal creature resources such as [[Mordite]] and [[cooking]] ingredients.

The Animus Beam deals ~100% more damage (and mining power) relative to the Mining Beam; additionally, it inflicts 4x damage to non-anomalous fauna weak spots. However, as the weapon does not benefit from [[Advanced Mining Laser]] or other [[Mining Beam Upgrade|Mining Beam Upgrades]], it cannot be modified to damage tough objects such as large [[mineral|minerals]] or Reinforced Doors, nor can its fuel efficiency (similar to that of the Mining Beam) or power be improved beyond initial values.

## Game Description

A unique overload to the Multi-Tool's standard mining laser. Beam frequency is modulated precisely in a feedback loop with the delta-waves generated by the impact target.

Recordings of the target's experiences are thus etched into tiny fragments of metal and rendered as **Fragmented Qualia**. Readability of collected fragments is not guaranteed.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 50 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 1 × Animus Beam

- **Repair**: 50 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 25 × [[Sodium Nitrate]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 25 × [[Sodium Nitrate]]

## Upgrades

The Animus Beam has no upgrades.

## Recharge

The Animus Beam is rechargeable using the following items:

- [[Di-hydrogen]] - 0.5% per unit
- [[Deuterium]] - 0.5% per unit
