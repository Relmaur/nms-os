# Advanced Cooling Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Photon Cannon Companion Unit
- **Description:** Reduces cool-down time for the Photon cannon
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Advanced Cooling Tau** is a weapons upgrade.

## Summary

The **Advanced Cooling Tau** is a [[Photon Cannon]] Companion unit.

Ship-on-ship destruction improves via a reduction in photon discharge interruptions. This is a level-two upgrade.

## Game description

Photo Cannon cool-down times are significantly reduced.

Ship-on-ship destruction becomes a more fulfilling endeavor through reduction in photon discharge interruptions.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's starship inventory.
