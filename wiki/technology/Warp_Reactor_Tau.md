# Warp Reactor Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Level:** 2
- **Description:** Increases length of Hyperdrive operation
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Warp Reactor Tau** is the level 2 upgrade for a [[starship]]'s [[Hyperdrive]].

## Summary

Warp Reactor Tau improves the [[Hyperdrive|Hyperdrive's]] capabilities, producing higher warp speeds and increasing the distance that can be traveled by 200ly.

When placed adjacent to Hyperdrive or other technologies that align with Hyperdrive technology, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].

## Game description

Hyperdrive benefits from advanced fusion that brings starship to higher warp speed and intensity. User's craft will become able to leap deeper into space, and into more distant planetary systems. Warp core output capped to avoid certain core meltdown, uncontained [[Thamium9]] fusion and death. User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems through Galactic Map.

## How to acquire

- The blueprint for this technology is given to the player by [[Polo]].
- It can be acquired from a [[Gek]] [[blueprint]] trader on a station within a Gek controlled system (occasionally)

## Craft

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 2 × [[Dynamic Resonator]] + 600 × [[Nickel]] + 1000 × [[Fungal Mould]] → 1 × Warp Reactor Tau

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when located in a player's [[Starship]] inventory.
- Does not need fuel or recharging
- Adds 200 Ly to Hyperdrive
- Required for warp to **Class E Star System** (Green Stars)
