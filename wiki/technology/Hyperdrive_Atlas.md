# Hyperdrive (Atlas)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Type:** Lightspeed Warp Drive
- **Description:** FTL propulsion drive for warp speed
- **Recharge:** Warp Cell
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Hyperdrive** is a starship propulsion method.

## Summary

**Hyperdrive** is the [[starship]]'s lightspeed propulsion drive to aid players travel across galaxies. It is also known as warp drive.

Basic hyperdrive has a specific range, which can be improved by the Warp Reactor upgrades (see below). [[Warp Cell|Warp Cells]] are used to charge this technology.

## Game description

FTL propulsion drive that allows [[starship|starships]] to attain warp speed and travel between neighbouring [[star system|star systems]]. User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems through [[Galactic Map]].

## How to Obtain

While flying in space for the first time, you’ll get a distress signal from another planet. Travel to that planet and find the [[Beacon (Building)|Beacon]], which will give you the coordinates to where the signal is coming from. Travel to that outpost and you’ll meet the alien. You probably won’t understand what they are saying, unless you have found enough [[Knowledge Stone|Knowledge Stones]], but don't worry, if you haven't yet you'll be able to figure out their request... The alien is in bad shape after fighting off the local animals and you are given three choices. Choose to recharge its [[exosuit]] with [[Carbon]] and heal him. In return the alien will give you the recipe for the Hyperdrive.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 1 × [[Dynamic Resonator]] + 200 × [[Heridium]] → 1 × Hyperdrive (Atlas)

## Pre-order bonus

The pre-order starships [[Alpha Vector]] and [[Horizon Omega]] came equipped with the Hyperdrive to give pre-ordered players a head start whereas the starting ships needed to craft it.
