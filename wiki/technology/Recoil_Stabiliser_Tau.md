- **Stats:** Recoil = (-39%)

**Recoil Stabiliser Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Recoil Stabiliser Tau** is the second-level [[weapon]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

## Summary

The Recoil Stabiliser Tau upgrade provides additional weapons accuracy by reducing kickback from the Boltcaster..

## Game description

Further reduces the Boltcaster's kick-back upon firing. User experience improves through increased stability, leading to more efficient combat.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.
