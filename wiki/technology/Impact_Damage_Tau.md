# Impact Damage Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Projectile
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Boltcaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Defensive weapon upgrade.
- **Stats:** Damage = +4.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Impact Damage Tau** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Impact Damage Tau** is a second-level [[projectile technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool|multi-tool's]] [[Boltcaster]].

This upgrade automatically increases the damage caused by Boltcaster shots.

## Game Description

Boltcaster shots deliver far more physical damage upon impact.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Iridium]] + 50 × [[Iron]] + 100 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Impact Damage Tau

## Additional information

- Needs no fuel or recharging
