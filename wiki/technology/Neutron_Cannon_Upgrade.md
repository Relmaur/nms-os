# Neutron Cannon Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Neutron Cannon
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Omega

**Neutron Cannon Upgrade** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**Neutron Cannon Upgrades**, contained within Neutron Cannon Modules, are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to the [[Neutron Cannon]].

## Upgrade properties

Neutron Cannon Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Damage:* Increases damage output
- *Ion Spheres Created:* Increases the maximum number of Ion Spheres at full charge (default is set to 1)
- *Ion Sphere Speed:* Increases the speed of Ion Sphere projectiles
- *Charging Speed:* Increases the charge speed to maximum burst capacity

Up to three Neutron Cannon Upgrades can be installed in the multitool --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Neutron Cannon Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Neutron Cannon Modules.

- **Repair**: 75 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 75 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
