# Aeron Shield

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Shield
- **Type:** Radiant Defensive Barrier
- **Recharge:** Sodium, Sodium Nitrate, Radiant Shard
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Aeron Shield** is a technology for the Sentinel Interceptor.

## Summary

**Aeron Shield** is the standard means of protecting a [[Sentinel Interceptor]] against incoming damage. It prevents objects and enemy fire from hitting the ship, but regenerates slowly, requiring manual recharging with [[Sodium]], [[Sodium Nitrate]] or [[Radiant Shard|Radiant Shards]] for a meaningful increase in durability in combat. If not recharged and allowed to deplete, the shield will drop, leaving the starship vulnerable to critical damage.

## Game Description

A defensive crimson cocoon, obfuscating the ship's physical form and providing protection from collision and laser fire.

The core self-heals over time, but may be quickly replenished with catalytic elements or radiant shards.
