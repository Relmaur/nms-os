# Fusion Engine Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Fusion Engine
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Fusion Engine Upgrade** is an Exocraft Upgrade Module.

## Summary

**Fusion Engine Upgrades**, contained within [[Upgrade Module|Exocraft Engine Modules]], are procedurally generated [[Upgrade Module|upgrades]] granting bonuses to an [[Exocraft]]'s [[Fusion Engine]].

## Upgrade properties

Fusion Engine Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Top Speed*: Increases maximum possible speed
- *Fuel Usage:* Reduces all mobility-related fuel costs (including [[Exocraft Acceleration Module]] use)

Up to three Fusion Engine Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of an Exocraft --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Fusion Engine Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Exocraft Engine Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
