# Health Module Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit
- **Subcategory:** Health
- **Level:** 3
- **Type:** Exosuit Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases user's health once primary systems have failed.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Health Module Theta** is a third-level [[health technology]] upgrade for the [[exosuit]].

## Summary

The Health Module increases user's health once primary systems have failed. It is automatically consumed when [[Life Support]], Shield and [[Hazard Protection]] systems are offline.

## Location

Blueprints for this upgrade are only found in Abandoned Buildings.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 200 × [[Aluminium]] + 250 × [[Plutonium]] + 300 × [[Thamium9]] → 1 × Health Module Theta
