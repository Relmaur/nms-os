# Warp Shielding Theta

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Upgrade: Blue Systems
- **Stats:** Hyperdrive Range: 50 ly
- **Added in:** Visions

**Warp Shielding Theta** is a freighter technology.

## Summary

**Warp Shielding Theta** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] and it allows warping to [[System colors|blue colored]] [[star system|star systems]].

It also improves the [[Freighter Hyperdrive]] capabilities by increasing the distance that can be travelled by a freighter.

## Game description

Hyperdrive protection matrix is upgraded, preventing reality glitches when attempting to reach rare-class blue stellar bodies.

User is advised to access Hyperdrive systems via the [[Galactic Map]].

## Sources

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from a [[Technology Merchant]] for ?  Nanites.

## Additional information

- Operates automatically when installed and needs no fuel or recharging.
- When placed adjacent to Freighter Hyperdrive or other freighter technologies, the player will receive additional [[Upgrade bonus|bonuses]].
- When originally introduced, the crafting formula for this item was:
  - [[Antimatter Housing]] 2
  - [[TetraCobalt]] 2
  - [[Indium]] 250
