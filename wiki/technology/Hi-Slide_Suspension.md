# Hi-Slide Suspension

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Vehicle
- **Subcategory:** Handling
- **Level:** A
- **Type:** Exocraft Handling Modification
- **Stats:** -50% Forwards Grip, -34% Angular Grip
- **Added in:** Beyond

**Hi-Slide Suspension** is a [[technology]].

## Summary

**Hi-Slide Suspension** is a technology which changes the handling characteristics of an [[Exocraft]], making it slide easily.

## Game description

Overhauls Exocraft handling values through a combination of software tweaks, pressure adjustments and the insertion of microgravity wells into key suspension components.

Specifically tuned to increase the ability of the Exocraft to slide.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 50 × [[Dioxite]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]] → 1 × Hi-Slide Suspension
