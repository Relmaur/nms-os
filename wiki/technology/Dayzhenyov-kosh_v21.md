# Dayzhenyov-kosh v2.1

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Weapon

## Summary

**Dayzhenyov-kosh v2.1** is one of the [[Multi-tool|Multi-Tools]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

It is a dark steel-grey in color with red vein-like streaks, and claw-like protrusions on the front and barrell. The butt of the tool is embedded with numerous bright pink crystals; all operating lights are green.
