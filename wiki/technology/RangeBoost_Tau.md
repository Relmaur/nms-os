# RangeBoost Tau

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scanner
- **Level:** 2
- **Type:** Scanner Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases the scanner's detection range
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**RangeBoost Tau** is a Multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

**RangeBoost Tau** is a second-level [[scan technology]] upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Scanner]].

## Game Description

Scanner's detection range is increased.

A powerful output modulator filters local information from increased distance into user's visualisation systems.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's multi-tool inventory.

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 120 × [[Thamium9]] + 120 × [[Chrysonite]] + 200 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × RangeBoost Tau
