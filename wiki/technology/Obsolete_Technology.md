# Obsolete Technology

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit, Starship
- **Type:** Old, unusable technology
- **Updated:** NEXT

**Obsolete Technology** is a technology.

## Summary

**Obsolete Technology** is a technology that replaced the outdated exosuit and starship Sigma/Tau/Theta type technologies, which were removed in NEXT.

## Game description

A piece of technology, corrupted by interference from the Atlas.

Can be recycled for usable components.

## Crafting

Obsolete Technology cannot be crafted.

Obsolete Technology cannot be repaired.

Obsolete Technology can be dismantled after construction, returning the following ingredients:

- Obsolete Technology → Wiring Loom ×2 + Nanite Cluster ×50

## Release history

- NEXT - Added as a technology.
- NEXT 1.51 - It also rewards the player with Nanite Cluster (beside Technology Module), when dismantling.
