# Obsolete Technology

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exosuit, Starship
- **Type:** Old, unusable technology
- **Added in:** NEXT

**Obsolete Technology** is a technology.

## Summary

**Obsolete Technology** is a [[technology]] that replaced the outdated [[exosuit]] and [[starship]] Sigma/Tau/Theta type technologies, which were removed in [[NEXT]].

## Game description

A piece of technology, corrupted by interference from the [[Atlas]].

Can be recycled for usable components.

## Crafting

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 50 × [[Nanite Cluster]]

## Release history

- [[NEXT]] - Added as a technology.
- [[Update 1.51|NEXT 1.51]] - It also rewards the player with Nanite Cluster (beside Technology Module), when dismantling.
