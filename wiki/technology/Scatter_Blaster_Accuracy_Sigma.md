# Scatter Blaster Accuracy Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Scatter Blaster
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Scatter Blaster Companion Unit
- **Description:** Increases accuracy of the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon.

**Scatter Blaster Accuracy Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Scatter Blaster Accuracy Sigma** is a first-level upgrade for the [[multi-tool]]'s [[Scatter Blaster]].

## Game description

Increases accuracy of the Multi-tool's Scatter Blaster weapon mode by reducing the spread of the projectile by a small amount.

## Crafting

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Thamium9]] + 50 × [[Titanium]] + 50 × [[Chrysonite]] → 1 × Scatter Blaster Accuracy Sigma

- Needs no fuel or recharging
