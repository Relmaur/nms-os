# Singularity Engine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Freighter
- **Subcategory:** Hyperdrive
- **Type:** Wormhole Propulsion
- **Recharge:** Di-hydrogen, Deuterium, Cobalt, Ionised Cobalt, Salt
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Singularity Engine** is freighter technology.

## Summary

The **Singularity Engine** is a [[freighter]] [[technology]] that generates and warps a freighter through a [[Black Hole]].

## Game description

An unstable and highly-experimental piece of [[Freighter Hyperdrive|freighter hyperdrive technology]]. This extreme gravity device generates a wormhole deep within the freighter's reactor core, inverting the entire vessel and transporting it across the universe.

Speak to the freighter's commander to engage the drive. Warning: destination is not controllable.

## Sources

- [[Expedition 8: Polestar]] Deep Sleep Cruise milestone.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 3 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 120 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Singularity Engine

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Warp Hypercore]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 60 × [[Ionised Cobalt]]

## Additional Information

Black holes that are generated by this powerful warp augmentation lead to a fixed location, determined by location seed where used. It is similar to those in black hole systems, if used in such a system, one arrives in the same location the black hole leads to.

This upgrade is only available in the normal save where the expedition that introduced the technology was played. This upgrade is not buyable or earnable outside of that expedition save.

However, if one obtains the upgrade outside of the expedition, they can install it on their freighter.
