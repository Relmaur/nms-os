# Combat Amplifier Sigma

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Multi-tool
- **Subcategory:** Mining Beam
- **Level:** 1
- **Type:** Companion Unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Combat Amplifier Sigma** is a multi-tool upgrade.

## Summary

The **Combat Amplifier Sigma** is the first-level [[laser technology]] upgrade for the [[Multi-tool|Multi-tool's]] [[Mining Beam]].

## Game Description

Mining Beam laser damage is increased during combat.

Existing laser functionality is improved by precision optics and beam amplification, resulting in more efficient user aggression.

Operates automatically once constructed within user's Multi-tool inventory.

Stats Damage +0.5

## Crafting
