# Safety Panel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Point of Interest
- **Type:** Damaged Component
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Safety Panel** is a Damaged Component.

## Summary

**Safety Panel** is a [[Damaged Component]] that can be found at various [[Point of Interest|Points of Interest]] and fixing it allows access to previously prevented function.

## Game description

The safety panel seals off potentially dangerous components in the event of a malfunction. An over sensitive trigger has caused it to activate, preventing the device from operating.
