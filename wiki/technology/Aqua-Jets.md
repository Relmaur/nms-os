# Aqua-Jets

## Quick Facts

- **Description:** Allows aquatic landing
- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Launch Thruster
- **Type:** Aquatic Landing System
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Aqua-Jets** is a starship upgrade.

## Summary

**Aqua-Jets** is a [[starship]] technology and an upgrade for the [[Launch Thruster]].

The jets allow the starship to land and takeoff on water. However, the jet does not allow the starship to land underwater, meaning the starship only capable of hover on the water surface.

## Game Description

An advanced upgrade for the starship's landing system, enabling the ship to land and take off from aquatic surfaces.

A mixture of bespoke jets and re-directed exhaust fans allow the ship to hover just above the water's surface.

## Sources

This technology can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] onboard the Space Anomaly for 240 Nanites.

## Crafting

- **Crafting**: 3 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 2 × [[Antimatter]] + 4 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] → 1 × Aqua-Jets

- **Repair**: 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 2 × [[Magnetic Resonator]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 2 × [[Crystal Sulphide]]
