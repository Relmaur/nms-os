# Exocraft Boosters Upgrade

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Exocraft
- **Subcategory:** Boosters Module
- **Type:** Generated Upgrade
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Exocraft Boosters Upgrade** is an exocraft Upgrade Module.

## Summary

**Exocraft Boosters Upgrades**, contained within [[Exocraft Boost Module|Exocraft Boost Modules]], are procedurally generated upgrades granting bonuses to the [[Exocraft]]'s boosters.

## Upgrade properties

Exocraft Boosters Upgrades grant at least one of the following bonuses:
- *Boost Power*: Increases vehicle speed bonus during boost
- *Boost Tank Size*: Increases duration of each boost

Up to three Exocraft Boosters Upgrades can be installed in the inventory of an Exocraft --- installing four or more upgrades will disable all upgrades of that type until the upgrade count is reduced to three or less.

## Crafting

Exocraft Boosters Upgrades can only be obtained from opening Exocraft Boost Modules.

- **Repair**: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Wiring Loom]]
