# High-Power Sonar

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Aquatic
- **Type:** Submarine Analysis Device
- **Added in:** Origins

**High-Power Sonar** is an exocraft technology.

## Summary

The **High-Power Sonar** is a variant of the [[Exocraft Signal Booster]] that can only be mounted on the [[Nautilon]].

It can be used by pressing the scan button, or with the [[Quick Menu]] to scan for individual [[Point of Interest|points of interest]] , including [[Crashed Ship]], [[Crashed Freighter]], [[Sunken Building]] and [[Submerged Ruins]].

It is not a pre-installed technology and needs a blueprint to build it.

## Game description

A high-detail sonar device, capable of upgrading the scanner technology aboard a [[Exocraft|submarine]] to [[Analysis Visor]] levels.

## Source

The blueprint is acquired during the [[Dreams of the Deep]] secondary mission.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 3 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 50 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × High-Power Sonar

- **Dismantle** returns: 25 × [[Ferrite Dust]]
