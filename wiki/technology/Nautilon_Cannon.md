# Nautilon Cannon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Aquatic
- **Type:** Submarine Weapon
- **Recharge:** Organic Elements
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Nautilon Cannon** is an exocraft technology.

## Summary

**Nautilon Cannon** is an [[exocraft]] weapon for the [[Nautilon]].

The weapon draws from an internal fuel supply to lob explosive projectiles which are affected by gravity. This fuel supply can be recharged with [[Carbon]] (0.5%/unit), [[Condensed Carbon]] (1.5%/unit) or [[Oxygen]] (1%/unit). Each shot drains 0.75% fuel when fired, and the weapon has a default fire rate of 2 shots per second, resulting in a default fuel drain rate of 1.5%/sec during continuous fire.

It has a dedicated [[Upgrade Module]] type, [[Nautilon Cannon Module]]. Upgrades from these modules may grant significant improvements to damage and rate of fire.

## Game description

A mounted cannon specifically designed for the Nautilon Submarine.

A high-energy weapon system, it superheats a small body of water before launching it rapidly towards the intended target.

Designed primarily for damage against biological entities and inefficient when deployed as a mining device.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Chlorine]] + 6 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 2 × [[Living Pearl]] → 1 × Nautilon Cannon

- **Dismantle** returns: 50 × [[Chlorine]] + 3 × [[Crystal Sulphide]] + 1 × [[Living Pearl]]

## Combat properties

By default, Nautilon Cannon deals 260 damage to [[fauna]], including all types of Abyssal Horrors. Direct hits on weak points on these targets deal 4x damage.

## Mining properties

By default, Nautilon Cannon has a mining power of 52 against [[flora]] and [[mineral|minerals]]. It is unable to damage objects which require [[Advanced Mining Laser]] functionality to harvest.

## Additional information

For any save editors that want to increase the actual fire rate of the cannon, duplicate copies of the Exocraft Mounted Cannon in the same exocraft increase the rate of fire indirectly due to an adjacency glitch similar to that of the Photon Cannon and Spewing Vents.
