# Cargo Scan Deflector

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Starship
- **Subcategory:** Interplanetary
- **Type:** Anti-Probe Countermeasures
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Cargo Scan Deflector** is a starship technology.

## Summary

**Cargo Scan Deflector** is a [[starship]] [[technology]] that jams remote cargo scans.

## Game description

A specially tuned resonance jammer, designed to interfere with the remote cargo scans deployed by system authorities in the search for smuggled goods.

Deploy from the Quick Menu while a scan in progress to deflect the probe. Note: jamming is not 100% effective.

## Source

The [[blueprint]] can be acquired from [[Iteration: Hyperion]] on board [[Space Anomaly]] for 240 Nanites.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Counterfeit Circuits]] + 2 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 220 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Cargo Scan Deflector

- **Repair**: 1 × [[Counterfeit Circuits]] + 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 110 × [[Platinum]]

- **Dismantle** returns: 1 × [[Counterfeit Circuits]] + 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 110 × [[Platinum]]

## Additional information

Despite the description, the Cargo Scan Deflector works both against sentinel cargo scans for smuggled goods AND Hostile Sub-Space Scans from pirates.
