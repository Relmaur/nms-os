# Sentinel Repair Drone

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Sentinel
- **Description:** Sentinel support units
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Sentinel Repair Drones** are a type of Sentinel.

## Summary

**Sentinel Repair Drones** are small, highly agile units that rapidly repair other [[Sentinel]] drones. While they themselves are weak and lightly armed, these mobile engineers represent a serious threat and should be quickly eliminated.

If the repair drone is in proximity of any damaged sentinel units, they will rush to their aid and progressively repair them until the unit or themselves is destroyed. If the unit is fully repaired, they will proceed to repair another unit that requires it.
