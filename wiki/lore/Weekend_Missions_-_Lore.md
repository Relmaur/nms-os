# Weekend Missions - Lore

**Weekend Missions - Lore** is a mission page.

## Summary

[[Weekend Missions]] are a weekly community-driven events, surrounding the investigation of mysterious disturbances around the [[Euclid]] galaxy.

They consist of weekly missions. Their purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo|Polo]] and [[Priest Entity Nada|Nada]] determine how the universe changed recently.

**Warning: This page may contain spoilers for Weekend Events that have not taken place yet based on data mined information.**

## Game Description

Nada's spacetime loom has detected a concerning pattern resonating across the universe, and they need help from their explorer friends to investigate. Readings indicate a disturbance in reality, where an anomalous – and possibly sinister – force has begun to leak through to this universe...

These weekend missions, which bring explorers together on a specific planet, will tell a story that continues to unfold.

## Additional information

- At the start of the mission the player has the option to speak with one of the Residents in the [[Space Anomaly]] for additional details (lore below) before seeking the [[Knowledge Stone|Knowledge Stones]].
- All missions require the player to interact with three Knowledge Stones to recover the [[Portal]] coordinates for the mission location.
- The player has to travel through the Portal to a specifically marked [[Point of Interest]], and is required to collect and turn in a specified amount of various resources, then return back through the Portal to the [[Nexus]] to collect their rewards.
  - Mission titles, descriptions, Knowledge Stone, and Portal Lore are randomly pulled from the pools found below.

- Where possible System/Planet are listed under their original names and will link to the current pages
- To prevent accidental spoilers, mission details and dialogue are hidden by default.
  - Data mined information about future missions have been presented in the manner they are suspected to appear in-game.
