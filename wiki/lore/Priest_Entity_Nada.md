# Priest Entity Nada

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Space Anomaly
- **Type:** Korvax NPC
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Nada** is an NPC character in No Man's Sky.

## Summary

[[Korvax|Priest Entity]] **Nada** currently resides in the [[Space Anomaly]] along with [[Polo]] and belongs to the [[Korvax]].

They are a non-compliant Korvax and do not engage in the Korvax Convergence. The Convergence constantly tries to "wipe Nada from existence" by regaining control over their shell. They become more paranoid and pessimistic as the player progresses in the game. Eventually Nada will ask you to continue to the [[Galaxy Centre|centre of the galaxy]], says that their work will be done, and that they will then return to the Convergence.

## Lore

Nada presumably first met Polo on a [[freighter]]. In a dimension where the [[sentinel|sentinels]] had destroyed all life, Nada saved Polo from being killed. They then went on to the centre of the galaxy in the freighter. Before they could travel to a new galaxy via the centre, the sentinels killed Polo, and Polo tells Nada that they will meet again in another universe.

## Rewards

Nada awards [[Quicksilver]] throughout the story if the player chooses to talk with them about the things happening with [[Artemis]], [[Apollo]] and [[-null-]] throughout these missions.

Choices may include help with exploration, help with Artemis (reward of nanites), or asking about the Crimson Liar, which opens another dialogue option to seek the Atlas. This reveals the atlas on your galaxy map.

## Additional Info

- Nada is wearing a red and blue cape with a formation of three starships and an Atlas symbol on it.
- When starting a new playthrough in Creative Mode, Nada will still ask the player about Artemis' whereabouts no matter whether the player met them before. Nada will also try to comfort the player, saying that the Anomaly is the same as before even if this is the player's first visit.
- During the [[Expedition 13: Adrift|Adrift Expedition]] (or when playing a campaign in Abandoned Mode) Nada's shell/carapace can still be found in the Space Anomaly in their usual position, albeit idle, lifeless and unresponsive. This, in a way, makes Nada the only NPC character the player will ever encounter in any form in Adrift/Abandoned Mode, aside from the lone [[Traveller]] the player briefly encounters when examining an [[Unknown Grave]] towards the end of the Adrift expedition.

## Release history

- [[Origins]] - Nada no longer offers three different awards based on the type of conversation. (These included nanites or locating objects in space)
- [[Prisms]] - multiple types of rewards for conversation have been returned to the game
