# Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Sentinel
- **Description:** High-level Sentinel unit
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mechs** are high-level Sentinel units.

## Summary

**Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mechs** are the second most advanced land-based [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] in No Man's Sky.

They look similar to the original player Exo Mech. Though heavily armoured, its high-powered jetpack gives it terrifying battlefield mobility. This fearsome unit is deployed alongside elite Sentinel forces, and comes equipped with a variety of weapons, including a plasma-powered flamethrower.

## Combat behaviour

The mech may have a weak spot, that being the battery on its back, though this is unconfirmed.

## Loot information

- [[Hardframe Engine]] can be harvested from a destroyed Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech.
