# Explorers Guild

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Guild
- **Description:** Explorers Guild
- **Added in:** NEXT

The **Explorers Guild** is one of six quest givers in No Man's Sky.

## Summary

The Explorers Guild is one of three quest-giving [[Guild|guilds]] released in the [[Atlas Rises]] update. Its basic information can be accessed in the user's menu under the Journey tab:<blockquote>A mutual association of researchers and explorers, the Explorers Guild serves to match up those who need data with those who would collect it.

Membership is largely split between institutions, geographical societies, and technology companies, with the occasional individual explorer. The Guild offers pooled resources, shared labs, lecture series and yearly medals.</blockquote>

## Standing Ranks

Standing ranks for the Explorers Guild are as follows:

1. **Unknown** (0 standing)
1. **Initiate** (3) - Player can now obtain gifts from Guild Envoys representing the Explorers Guild
1. **Apprentice** (8) - Player can now obtain Guild Missions from Guild Envoys representing the Explorers Guild
1. **Journeyman** (14)
1. **Associate** (21)
1. **Senior** (30)
1. **Master** (40)
1. **Elder** (60)
1. **Exalted** (100)

## Guild actions/quests

Once the player has reached Apprentice rank with the Explorers Guild, Guild Envoys which represent this guild will begin to offer special Guild Missions to the player. Guild Missions have objectives and rewards which normally only generate in high-rank [[Mission Board]] missions, and successful completion of a Guild Mission raises the player's standing with that guild by 5 points.

While standing with the guild can only be improved by completing its missions, other actions associated with the Explorers Guild include:
- Scanning rare creatures
- Warping

## Clients

While united on the surface, each client of a guild has its own agenda and their own area of expertise.<br>

### 23 Sceptics

They wish to research the more complex sentinels from the safety of a nearby location, but this includes their battle tactics and their demise. To achieve this goal others are asked to get in harms way for good credits, you.
Lowest Mission: Rank 9
Known Mission: Destroy Sentinel Quads (Gek)

### Bureau of Black Holes

They trade in big data and operate libraries for businesses to source and license.
Lowest Mission: Rank 4
Known Mission: Take Photo (Vy'keen)

### Client Untraceable

No info is given, it simply is urgent. Later missions may give more hints.
Lowest Mission: Rank 9
Known Mission: Emergency Repair (Gek)

### Omegon Institute

They operate planetside equipment and machines and ask for repairs.
Lowest Mission: Rank 9
Known Mission: Repair (Korvax)

### The Ever-Beating Drum

They reward people for their deeds and employ with special deliveries - no not bombs. They seem to actually care about loyalty and merit.
Lowest Mission: Rank 7
Known Mission: Delivery (Vy'keen)

## Additional information

- The warping statistic in the Explorers Guild section of the Milestones interface claims to track *distance traversed during warps*. However, the statistic actually tracks the *number of times the player has warped*.
- They are called "EGUILD" in the game files.
