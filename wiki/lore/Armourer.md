# Armourer

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Weapons Terminal
- **Type:** Vy'keen
- **Description:** Hireable NPC character
- **Added in:** Outlaws

The **Armourer** is a hireable Vy'keen NPC character.

## Summary

The **Armourer** is a hireable [[Vy'keen]] NPC character and is the mission-giver of the [[Weapons Research]] mission. They can mostly be found in Vy'keen systems, but can also randomly be found in systems controlled by other species.

The Armourer can be located by a weapon icon (pistol/multi-tool) One can be found in the space station and from there, choose the location with a small Erlenmeyer flask icon. The NPC will be standing on the right space station platform.

The [[British spelling]] of this word is used in the wiki.

## Requirements

Before hiring an Armourer, there are the following requirements:
- A Habitable Base must be claimed
- An [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]] and [[Scientist]] hired and having needed to progress enough to unlock the [[Weapons Terminal]] blueprint
- A Weapons Terminal needs to be constructed and placed in a cuboid/square room

## Blueprints

The Armourer gives the following blueprints in order:
- [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]]
- [[Cadmium Drive]]
- [[Starshield Battery]]
- [[Plasma Launcher]]
- [[Phase Beam]]
- [[Cyclotron Ballista]] & [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]]
- [[Oxygen Capsule]]
- [[Combat Scope]]

## Additional Information

After completing Weapons Research, the Armourer will create a new, random [[Multi-tool]] every 24-hour period and offer it for free. Multi-tools created with a high number of slots will often contain broken components, and a new tool will be offered each 24-hour period regardless of whether or not the previous tool was accepted or ignored.

## Bugs

- Giving [[Vy'keen Dagger]] to the Armourer may cause their next dialogue choice to bug out where the Vy'keen Dagger request will loop again, but instead with the give option greyed out. This possible bug has no outcome and speaking to them will cause them to resume with the next step of the mission.
