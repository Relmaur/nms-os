# Sentinel Summoner Drone

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Sentinel
- **Description:** Units that call in Sentinel reinforcements
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Sentinel Summoner Drones** are a type of Sentinel.

## Summary

**Sentinel Summoner Drones** are capable of beaming in reinforcements mid-battle. While of low threat on their own, these advanced [[Sentinel]] units can quickly turn the tide of a fight and overwhelm an unwary player.

Occasionally, they will start summoning [[Sentinel Heavy Combat Drone|Heavy sentinel drones]]. This process is slow, and can be stopped by destroying the summoner. If it's completed, the newly summoned drones won't disappear, even after the summoner's destruction.

It can also be stopped by stunning the summoner, for instance with the [[Waveform Oscillator]] upgrade for the [[Blaze Javelin]]. The summoning will be completely halted (all summoning progress stops and the half-summoned Sentinel backup will vanish) regardless of whether the drone is destroyed or not.

## Appearance

The Summoner Drone is a smaller, flying Sentinel. Its body is armoured and pyramid shaped and incorporates a radio antenna-like structure in the top center. A Wi-Fi symbol above its head indicates it is calling reinforcements.
