# Autophage (language)

**Autophage (language)** is a language page.

## Summary

**Autophage (language)** is a list of words from the [[Autophage]] [[language]]. The Autophage are a subrace of the [[Korvax]] introduced in the [[Update 4.40|Echoes]] update.

## Overview

Uniquely, when compared to the other languages, the Autophage language is written with a combination of Greek and Latin letters with numbers, instead of the Latin alphabet used by the other languages.

This language hasn't been datamined, therefore the word order is unknown, as several words are unlocked at once.

The entire language changed between updates as well.

## Order & Translation

As of [[Worlds Part I]], there are 1,135 Autophage words.

The exact order is currently unknown.
