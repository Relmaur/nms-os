# Script of the Gek

On Gek-inhabited planets, you can find Gek [[Plaque|Plaques]], which are monuments that contain Lore regarding the Gek the First Spawn. Interacting with the Plaques will present you with a passage from the Gek the First Spawn history and will also allow you to learn a random word from the Gek language.

There are 30 distinct messages you can receive from reading Gek Plaques.

Knowing all Gek words doesn't affect your interaction with the Gek Plaques.

- For a history from the [[Vy'keen]] viewpoint, see [[Saga of the Vy'keen]].
- For a history from the [[Korvax]] viewpoint, see [[Testament of the Korvax]].
- For a history from a different [[Gek]] viewpoint, see [[First Spawn]].
