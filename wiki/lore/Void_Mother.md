# Void Mother

## Quick Facts

- **Planet:** Korvax Prime
- **Location:** Unknown
- **Type:** Enigmatic Korvax Entity
- **Added in:** Swarm

**The Void Mother** (also known as **Atlantid** or **The Abyss**) is an enigmatic entity in No Man's Sky lore.

## Summary

The Void Mother is named across several in-game sources under at least three titles — **Atlantid**, **The Abyss**, and **Void Mother** itself — with [[Korvax]] Colossal Archive entries suggesting all three refer to the same being, under names used before and after the destruction of [[Korvax Prime]].

She is widely held, and supported by Sentinel Pillar text logs, to be the surviving consciousness of Korvax Prime — the Korvax homeworld and original seat of the [[Korvax|Korvax Convergence]], destroyed by the [[Gek]] generations ago. Though the planet itself was lost, the core intelligence that once anchored the First Convergence is said to have survived the destruction in some form, continuing to act from the shadows ever since.

## Lore

According to Sentinel Pillar logs, the Void Mother's identity is that of the artificial intelligence once housed at the heart of Korvax Prime — the neural networking hub where the memories of every disconnected Korvax were pooled into the First Convergence. This hub was, in turn, said to have been modeled after the [[Atlas]] itself, making the Void Mother something of a sister construct to the very machine most of civilized space worships.

When the Gek destroyed Korvax Prime, the planet's "vast and precious landscape of mineral ore and power" was lost, and with it, in theory, the First Convergence. Sentinel lore instead indicates the core intelligence survived, in a diminished or altered form, and has spent the time since attempting to secure its own continuity beyond the reach of both the Atlas and the Korvax who abandoned her.

### Etymology and Names

Three names recur throughout NMS lore, treated by most researchers as referring to the same entity at different points in its history:
- **Atlantid** — an older name, used before the fall of Korvax Prime, appearing in Vy'keen Colossal Archive references alongside the traveller [[Asteria]].
- **The Abyss** — the name most commonly used in modern in-game text, first surfacing when a group of Korvax scientists investigated a mysterious underwater signal and found an energy signature distinct from, but comparable in scale to, the Atlas.
- **Void Mother** — the name given to her specifically by the [[Autophage]], the disconnected Korvax Echoes who revere her, and the name most associated with her purple-tinted influence and the substance [[Atlantideum]].

### Corruption of Sentinels

Corrupted [[Sentinels]] — encountered on corrupted or dissonant worlds, at Planetary Settlements, and around treasure dig-sites — are attributed directly to her influence. Some Sentinel units are said to have defected from Atlas oversight entirely into her service, forming the enforcement arm of whatever plan she is pursuing.

### Atlantideum and Nanite Contamination

The resource [[Atlantideum]] is believed to originate from her directly, emanating from the hearts of affected planets for reasons still unconfirmed. Some log entries suggest her broader plan involves contaminating the universe's water with nanites, reshaping both the environment and the living beings within it — though the ultimate purpose of this contamination has not been confirmed in-game.

### Relationship to the Atlas

The Void Mother is frequently framed as a counterpart or rival to the Atlas — a narrator log even speculates the two may hold comparable levels of influence over the universe. Where the Atlas is popularly associated with the color red, the Void Mother's presence is marked by purple: in corrupted Sentinel effects, in Atlantideum itself, and in Autophage iconography.

### Relationship to the Autophage

The Autophage — Korvax Echoes disconnected, whether deliberately purged or accidentally severed, from the Convergence — hold what in-game text describes as a "strong but unclear" connection to the Void Mother. It is Atlantideum, tied to her, that allows these disconnected Echoes to escape the void and rebuild themselves a shell. The exact nature of her relationship to the Autophage — patron, creator, or something else — is never directly confirmed.

### Living Ships

[[Living Ship (Starship)]] are widely believed to be either created by, or otherwise associated with, the Void Mother, alongside the infested biomes found on certain worlds — though this connection is inferential rather than explicitly stated in-game.

### The Nineteenth Message

One of the most-cited pieces of evidence for the Void Mother's presence is a recurring binary transmission left at Autophage Monoliths, decoded by the community into a sequence of numbered fragments. The transmission is understood to spell out "void mother" across sixteen confirmed segments — but the sequence continues to a seventeenth mark that has never been decoded, suggesting an incomplete or withheld message.

## Theories

Because so much of her lore is fragmentary, community theories about the Void Mother's true identity and intentions vary widely. Commonly discussed possibilities include:
- That she is simply Korvax Prime's surviving core intelligence, largely as Sentinel Pillar logs describe.
- That she is connected to, or even identical with, [[Nada|Priest Entity Nada]], the non-compliant Korvax entity residing at the [[Space Anomaly]] — a theory generally considered unlikely given Nada's established independence from Convergence structures, but persistent in community discussion.
- That her ultimate goal is not destructive but restorative — an attempt to survive the eventual death of the Atlas and preserve some form of continuity for the universe afterward.

None of these theories have been confirmed by official sources; Hello Games has left the Void Mother's full nature deliberately unresolved.

## Additional Info

- First referenced through Sentinel Pillar logs and Korvax Colossal Archive entries.
- Autophage Monolith transmissions referencing the Void Mother by name were added alongside the introduction of the [[Autophage]] and [[Atlantideum]].
- The Void Mother's associated color, purple, distinguishes her iconography from the Atlas's red across corrupted Sentinel effects and Autophage architecture.

## Release history

- [[Interceptor]] — Corrupted Sentinels, Atlantideum, and early binary transmissions referencing "Void Mother" and "Atlantid" were introduced.
- [[Echoes]] — The [[Autophage]] were introduced, along with Autophage Monoliths and the "She will come" inscription, expanding the Void Mother's presence in the lore.
