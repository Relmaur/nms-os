# Alphabet

**Alphabet** is a catalogue page.

## Summary

**Alphabet** catalogues the common alphabet used for written [[language|languages]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] universe.

In many places throughout the game, alien alphabet characters can be seen on various devices, signs, and posters. These characters are used for spelling the words and phrases used by the sapient aliens in the game's universe. These characters are a 1:1 equivalent to the roman alphabet, and once translated, spell words in the English language.
