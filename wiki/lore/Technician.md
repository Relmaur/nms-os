# Technician

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Exocraft Terminal
- **Type:** Vy'keen
- **Description:** Hireable NPC character
- **Added in:** Frontiers

The **Technician** is a hirable NPC.

## Summary

The **Technician** is a hireable [[Vy'keen]] NPC character related to [[exocraft|exocrafts]] and is the mission-giver of the [[Exocraft Technician]] mission. They can be found in Vy'keen [[Star system|systems]],
but any Vy'keen on a space station can be a Technician.

## Requirements

Before hiring a Technician, there are the following requirements:
- A Habitable Base must be claimed.
- An [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]], [[Scientist]], [[Armourer]] and [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]] hired and having needed to progress far enough to unlock the [[Exocraft Terminal]] blueprint.
- An Exocraft Terminal needs to be constructed and placed.

## Blueprints

The Technician gives the following blueprints in order:

- [[Roamer Geobay]]
- [[Exocraft Signal Booster]]
- [[Exocraft Mounted Cannon]]
- [[Exocraft Acceleration Module]]
- [[Exocraft Mining Laser]]
- [[Nomad Geobay]]
- [[Advanced Signal Booster]]
- [[Colossus Geobay]]
- [[Exocraft Mining Laser Upgrade Sigma]]
- [[Exocraft Signal Booster Upgrade Tau]]

Other Rewards:

- [[Exocraft Acceleration Module Upgrade|Exocraft Boost Modules]]
- [[Exocraft Acceleration Module Upgrade|Exocraft Acceleration Modules]]

## Trivia

- The [[Exocraft Mining Laser]] is the only blueprint not unlocked directly via the Technician and is instead unlocked from a terminal during their mission.
