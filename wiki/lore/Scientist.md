# Scientist

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Science Terminal
- **Type:** Korvax
- **Description:** Hireable NPC character
- **Added in:** NEXT

The **Scientist** is a hireable NPC character.

## Summary

The **Scientist** is a hireable [[Korvax]] NPC character and is the mission-giver of the [[Scientific Research]] mission.

This NPC is required for essential tech and [[base building]] progression. They can be found in Korvax [[Star system|systems]], but can also randomly be found in systems controlled by other species. The Scientist is notable for requiring the player to wait an hour and a half in real time twice during their mission, for a total of three hours in real time.

After completing their mission, the player may give planetary data they've previously gathered to the Scientist for a reward of [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]].

## Requirements

Before hiring a Scientist, there are the following requirements:
- A Habitable Base must be claimed.
- An [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]] hired and having needed to progress enough to unlock the [[Science Terminal]] blueprint.
- A Science Terminal needs to be constructed and placed.
