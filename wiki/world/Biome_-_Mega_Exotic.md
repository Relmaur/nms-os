# Biome - Mega Exotic

**Mega Exotic** is one of the Biome varieties.

## Summary

Worlds with mega exotic [[biome|biomes]] are characterized by having mega-sized versions of objects found in other biomes, like giant circular rock formations, algae, smokestack plants, and trees. It has an atmosphere that refracts light into a certain colour, which can vary from a strong orange hue to a dismal black-and-white dystopia.

Though classified similarly to [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] planets, it seems to be on a class of its own. It has clear, normal and extreme weather with characteristics similar to lush planets. Flora and fauna here can be discovered. Waypoints are present if the system is inhabited.

In the game file (found [[User:Gorla/BIOMELISTPERSTARTYPE.MBIN|here]]), this biome is split into 3 subtypes, **Red Biome**, **Green Biome** and **Blue Biome**.
As implied by the names, these subtypes appear in red, green and blue star systems respectively; in purple and yellow star systems, any of the 3 types can be found. The subtype determines the available name generation and weather description options for the planet or moon.

From space, they look like any other planet. Look for them in any star system, other than yellow star systems if you are not in an Empty or Lush galaxy.
The easiest way to identify them is by the planet's information panel; as shown below, the name generation of worlds with this biome tends to be very strange, with names such as "Stellar Corruption Detected" and "Planetary Anomaly". To determine the specific subtype, you can look at which names are seen in which sub-types, along with the weather description if needed.

Some worlds with this biome don't have mega sized flora or minerals, but are instead more like worlds with the Lush Biome, having a thick layer of grass and many normal sized trees covering the surface.
A small portion of these worlds may also have normal colours.

## Additional Information

- The name generation and weather with their sequence are extracted from the 3.68 version of NMS_UPDATE3_ENGLISH.MBIN game file.
- Many planets with this biome contain mega-flora that can be both scanned with the [[Analysis Visor]] and harvested with a [[Mining Beam]]. Research on these flora can be found on the [[Mega Flora Botanical Research]] page.
- Unlike [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] biomes, mega exotic biomes can have water.
