# Cave

**Caves** are a type of geological structure.

## Summary

**Caves** are a type of geological structure generated on most if not all [[planet|planets]].

They can be used as a means of shelter against [[Weather]] and other planet hazards. The safety of cave systems vary, so be on your guard whenever entering a new cave. They also contain [[resource|resources]] and different [[Species]], some of which cannot be found on the surface. These are considered part of the Subterranean ecosystem.

Cave systems can also be found underwater; check your breathing apparatus before exploring these.

## Resources

Resources found in caves includes the following:

- [[Cobalt]] (mineral formations in all caves)
- [[Pure Ferrite]] (mineral formations in some caves)
- [[Silver]] (rarely replaces Cobalt in mineral formations)
- [[Gold]] (rarely replaces Cobalt in mineral formations)
- [[Platinum]] (rarely replaces Cobalt in mineral formations)
- [[Sodium]] ([[Underground Toxic Hazard]])
- [[Oxygen]] (Underground Toxic Hazard)
- [[Marrow Bulb]] ([[Cave Marrow]])
- [[Vortex Cube|Vortex Cubes]] ([[Subterranean Relic|Subterranean Relics]])
- [[Albumen Pearl|Albumen Pearls]] ([[Humming Sac|Humming Sacs]])

## Temperature Differences

In an early game video, a player is seen going into a cave on [[Balari V]] to recharge the anti-planet hazard insulation in his suit. It is notably warmer inside as the temperature rises at least 200 degrees Celsius. The player penetrates the cave by blowing a hole in the ground using the [[Land Disruptor]] upgrade for his weapon.

Currently, caves are all safer than the external environment.

## Additional Information

- Caves extending all the way down to "bedrock" will often have surface objects generate on the exposed "bedrock"
