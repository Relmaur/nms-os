## Summary

**Ventriculogeoditicus** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It is formed from vented mineral deposits and is rich in geodes.

### Appearance

A tall, upright, cylindrical mineral formation with a slightly tapered profile. Its surface is dark metallic brown to near‑black with prominent, irregular bands and veins of bright turquoise‑blue running horizontally and diagonally across the body, creating a striking layered appearance. The texture is rough and heavily striated, with visible horizontal banding and subtle cracking throughout. The top surface is flat and shows concentric layering with the same blue veining. Overall morphology indicates a dense, vented mineral rich in geodes, formed through hydrothermal processes.
