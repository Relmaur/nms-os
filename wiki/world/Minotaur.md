# Minotaur

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Exocraft
- **Description:** Mechanical walker
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

The **Minotaur** is a mechanical walker exocraft.

## Minotaur

**Minotaur** is a mechanical walker [[exocraft]] containing 28 slots that can be used to safely explore all planets, performing mining, combat, and exploration tasks.

It has an independent jetpack, and is with the pre-installed [[Environment Control Unit]] - it is immune to all planetary hazards. The Minotaur's standard mining laser can be upgraded to a [[Minotaur Bore]] if desired, enabling the alteration of terrain on planets similar to the function of a [[Multi-tool]]'s [[Terrain Manipulator]]. It can also shoot plasma projectiles in combat.

The Minotaur is also amphibious, allowing underwater navigation, combat and mining.

It can be summoned to any land surface or ocean on the planet or at a [[Minotaur Geobay]]. Once the [[Minotaur AI Pilot]] is installed, it will auto-summon to the player's location if it was summoned to the planet.

## Upgrading the Minotaur

### Other Minotaur Upgrades

The following upgrades could be earned by completing milestones in [[Expedition 14: Liquidators]]. Once earned during the expedition, they could be claimed at the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

| System | Technology/Upgrade | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
|   | Liquidator Left Arm | Fires stun projectiles |
|   | Liquidator Right Arm | Flamethrower |
|   | Liquidator Legs | Improves engine and mobility systems |
| AI Abilities | Liquidator Body | Semi-autonomous Minotaur operations |

## Additional information

With **Minotaur AI Pilot** installed, a Minotaur will engage enemies including predators and Sentinels in combat, though if it takes too much damage, the tech will be destroyed and require repairs; if still in combat, enemies will begin to ignore the Minotaur unless manual control is assumed.
