## Summary

**Novalith petraefactus** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It is a recently formed petrified stone with a pristine structure and high metal content.

### Appearance

A large, flattened, irregularly oval mineral mass with a low‑profile, disc‑like shape. Its surface is dark olive‑brown to grayish‑green with a rough, heavily pitted and cracked texture marked by numerous small depressions, shallow grooves, and irregular fissures. The overall form is compact and dense, with subtle layering visible along the edges and a slightly domed upper surface. No external lattice, rings, protrusions, or crystals are present. Overall morphology indicates a recently formed, petrified mineral with high metal content.
