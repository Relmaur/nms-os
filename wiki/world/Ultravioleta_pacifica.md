## Summary

**Ultravioleta pacifica** is a flora species found on [[Aevum Michaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It feeds on ultraviolet radiation and emits a calming scent.

### Appearance

An upright floral organism consisting of a thick, vertically oriented stem with prominent horizontal segmentation and a mottled greenish-brown woody integument. Surmounting the stem is a single massive terminal bloom forming a globose, bulbous corolla that tapers upward before flaring into a broad, open cup; the corolla surface displays deep vertical pleats and ridges, with a luminous gradient from deep magenta-purple at the base to vivid pink at the scalloped, frilled rim. Overall morphology indicates an erect, specialized floral structure adapted for elevated display.
