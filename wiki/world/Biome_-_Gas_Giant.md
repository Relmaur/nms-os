# Biome - Gas Giant

**Gas Giant** is one of the Biome varieties.

## Summary

The surface of Gas Giant [[biome]] worlds resemble [[Biome - Scorched|scorched]] worlds, and are mostly flat, but can have very tall mountains. There are almost no plants here, and some minerals appear to be crystals.

No [[Fauna|fauna]] ever live in this biome, but you can still [[Fishing|fish]] in the limited water here, and [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] can survive the heat and pressure.

The gravity here is higher than on regular sized planets, and as such the [[Jetpack|jetpack]] cannot be used as effectively. This is the opposite effect as is seen on [[Biome - Dead|dead worlds]].

No Gas Giant has been observed to have [[Planetary Rings]], unlike Gas Giants in real life.

Sentinels are observed to exist without issues.

## Name Generation

The biome type is indicated in the information panel when the player exits their starship. This is, for gas giants, always **Gas Giant**.

## Conditions

- These planets are characterized by constant electromagnetic storms, thick fog and rocks floating around tornadoes.
- [[Hazard protection]] is always on due to high temperatures. [[Coolant Network|Coolant network]] upgrades are recommended.
- Despite these worlds having constant storms, the temperature is always similar to the non-storm temperature on an extreme [[Biome - Scorched|scorched]] or [[Biome - Volcanic|volcanic]] world.
- These lack of additional temperature increases means that gas giants can be considered to be more habitable than such a world.
- The ground may self-ignite creating local high danger spots.

## Resources

The resources [[Activated Quartzite]] and [[Crystallised Helium]] can always be found here, and [[Storm Crystal|storm crystals]] are common.

The [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas]] [[Methane]] can be extracted using a [[Atmosphere Harvester]].

There are no plant resource unique to gaseous worlds.

## Additional Information

- The weather table is not extracted from any game file, but from encounters, and as such may not be comprehensive.
- Note that all Gas Giants are Giant planets, but not all Giant planets are Gas Giants.

## Trivia

- Real-world Gas Giants are comprised entirely of gases and semi-solid substrate. There are only the continuous storms of violently clashing fronts of vapors, gases, and particulate, but no surface on which to stand or construct buildings, nor any bodies of water. Any object would descend until reaching a crush depth at which point it would become semi-solid.
- The fantastical nature of a climate and surface on a Gas Giant may be a reference to the book 'Journey to the Center of the Earth' by Jules Verne and several theatrical reproductions, in which explorers traverse multiple unique biomes within the Earth's own interior and discover various creatures and civilizations thought lost to time.
