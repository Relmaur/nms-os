## Summary

**Aerovorus putrescis** is found on [[Spalek]] in the [[Omen]] system. A mature, terrestrial flora that leaches nutrients directly from the irradiated atmosphere, its tissues exhibit a slowly rotting appearance.

## Appearance

A mature terrestrial flora consisting of numerous thick, gnarled, upward-curving branches of mottled gray-brown coloration with a cracked, peeling surface texture indicative of slow decay. The branches emerge from a central base in a dense, radiating cluster, each terminating in blunt, finger-like tips. The overall morphology indicates an upright growth habit with a simple root structure specialized for air leaching and a slowly rotting appearance.

## Additional information

- The simple root structure indicates minimal below‑ground investment, consistent with a strategy of drawing nutrients directly from the atmosphere rather than from soil.
- Air leaching as a nutrient source allows the plant to bypass the heavily metallic, potentially toxic soil of Spalek, absorbing what it needs from the oxygen‑ and Radnox‑rich air.
- The slowly rotting appearance may be an adaptation to the planet's irradiated thunderstorms and electrical activity, with the peeling, cracked surface possibly shedding contaminants or reducing water loss.
