# Protocaeli

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Airborne
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** floating, spheres or disk shaped
- **Added in:** Breach

**Protocaeli** is a genus.

## Summary

**Protocaeli** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **PROTOFLYER** that appears in the game code.

Protocaeli is a floating creature, often showing as spheres, disks, or fish-like.

## Major Characteristics

- Floating/hovering a low to moderate distance from the surface
- Airborne
- Often appears aquatic
