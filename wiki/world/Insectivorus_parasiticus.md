## Summary

**Insectivorus parasiticus** is a flora species found on [[Terra Lorrainae I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It traps insects and serves as a host for parasitic organisms.

## Appearance

An upright floral organism consisting of a thick, vertically oriented, segmented stem with a rough, woody integument of mottled dark green and brown tones. At the apical junction, a whorl of five to six large, glossy, dark-blue bracts radiates outward in a star-like arrangement; each bract is lanceolate to ovate with prominent parallel venation and a metallic sheen. Surmounting this structure is a single massive terminal flower comprising a broad, funnel-shaped corolla formed by numerous overlapping petals that flare dramatically upward and outward into a wide, scalloped rim; the corolla wall displays fine longitudinal pleating and a luminous peach-pink to pale cream coloration with subtle translucency. Overall morphology indicates an erect, specialized floral structure adapted for elevated display and potential insect capture.
