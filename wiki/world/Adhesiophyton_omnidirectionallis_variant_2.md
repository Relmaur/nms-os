## Summary

**Adhesiophyton omnidirectionallis variant 2** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. An ancient terrestrial flora with an omni‑directionally spreading root structure, it parasitizes other plants using a sticky coating on its intricate, net‑like crown.

## Appearance

A tall, slender terrestrial flora consisting of a single twisted trunk of mottled brown, green, and magenta coloration with a gnarled, bark-like texture. The upper portion dramatically branches into a complex, open lattice-like crown of dark greenish-blue interwoven strands with a sticky coating. The base splits into several thick, root-like projections with pinkish accents. The overall morphology indicates an ancient omni‑directional growth habit specialized for capturing other plants through its intricate, net‑like structure.
