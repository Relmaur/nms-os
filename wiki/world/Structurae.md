# Structurae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Ground
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Living Building
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Structurae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Structurae** is a [[genus]] of [[species|fauna]]. They are synthetic constructs with a [[Colossal Archive]]-like building on their back.

These creatures can be found on planets with high anomaly field strength, namely those found within [[Uncharted System|uncharted]] systems.

## Additional Information

They produce the following [[Cooking Products|cooking product]]: [[Chewy Wires]]

They can be tamed with [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]], and drop [[Recovered Item (DATA)|recovered data]] upon death.

Like [[Mechanoceris]], descriptions for species in the Discoveries tab are entirely comprised of x86-64 assembly code, data transferral Instructions for real-life CPUs to execute.
