## Summary

**Thermoradix aquosus** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This mature, heat‑seeking flora stores significant water within its tissues and relies on aerobic digestion for nutrient processing.

### Appearance

A medium‑sized, multi‑stemmed flora consisting of two prominent, bulbous, mushroom‑like caps supported by thick, woody stems. Each cap is broadly hemispherical with a smooth, glossy surface that transitions from deep emerald green on the lower half to irregular patches of warm orange‑brown on the upper surface, giving a mottled, water‑laden appearance. The stems are robust, dark brown, and slightly twisted, showing a fibrous, bark‑like texture. The two main structures lean gently away from each other, creating a stable, paired growth habit. Overall morphology indicates a heat‑seeking, water‑rich organism adapted for aerobic digestion in its environment.
