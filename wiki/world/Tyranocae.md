# Tyranocae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Dinosaurs, specifically T-Rex
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Tyranocae** is a genus of fauna.

## Summary

**Tyranocae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky.

They are often referred to as just dinosaur or T-Rex. They appear to travel alone or together with a pup. They are often aggressive but are not always, and can be found on many types of planets.

## Appearance

They are generally small to medium in size.

## Major Characteristics

- Bipedal
- Typically have a tail
- T-rex like head and teeth

## Variant Types

- **T-Rex** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with a reptilian style head.
- **Xeno Rex** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with a moth-like head.
- **Dodo** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with a condor-like head.
- **Greater Mushroom Back** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with a a large mushroom appendage on its backside.

- **Noodles** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with noodle like appendages.
- **Unicorn** - A bipedal Tyrannosaurus dinosaur like creature with a unicorn-like head and horn.
