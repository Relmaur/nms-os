# Hexungulatis

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Six-legged cows
- **Added in:** Pathfinder

**Hexungulatis** is a species.

## Summary

**Hexungulatis** is a [[Scientific name|genus]] of [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky. Generally referred to just as "six legged cows" or "hexapod cows," this genus is closely related to [[Ungulatis]], the four-legged cow genus. Hexungulatis species resemble Ungulatis species in every way, generally with the only significant difference being an extra pair of legs.

## Major Variants & Characteristics

- Six legs.
- Feet are usually elephant-like (flat, relatively circular), but can also have different appearances, like hooves or many long toes.
- Two eyes and a distinct mouth (Except in those with "exotic" or "alien" heads)

## Additional information

This genus page is currently misspelled, with the proper spelling being Hexungulatus in the 2.42 game files.
