# Biome - Waterworld

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Biome
- **Description:** Planets with vast oceans.
- **Added in:** Cosmos

**Waterworld** is a type of [[Biome]].

## Summary

As its name implies, worlds with the **Waterworld** biome do not have a surface, but rather, waters. If viewed from space, the planet is covered entirely by a vast ocean. Without [[Aqua-Jets]] for aquatic landing, it is impossible to land on the planet, though there are a few waterworlds that do have small islands to land on. To use the [[Nautilon]], a [[Nautilon Platform]] must be built (this is different from [[Nautilon Chamber]] as the platform floats on the water's surface. If you wish to use the Nautilon when you get to the bottom, you can use the chamber).

On this planet, the water may be very deep, usually more than 1000u in depth. Swimming to this depth only using the [[Exosuit]] could be quite challenging and will take a long time, since if you swim to around 200u, your [[Life Support]] and [[Hazard Protection]] will become strained, and will drain faster. A Pressure Membrane will help mitigate this effect.

Using the Nautilon is more convenient than swimming by yourself, as it does not drain the Underwater Protection (the [[Oxygen]] reserve that are available when underwater) and [[Life Support]], and have a speed of ~101 ku/h (~28 u/s) when boosted. However, it does not provides protection from hazards (thermal/toxic/radiation), therefore the Hazard Protection may still deplete.

The resource [[Lithium]] is always and only found on Waterworlds.

## Weather

The following sequence is taken from the game data.

1. Coastal Storms

Unlike other biomes, Waterworlds always have this singular weather description. They can be calm, normal, or extreme.

## Additional Information

- Waterworlds can only be found in [[System colours|Purple coloured]] [[Star system|Star systems]].
  - Because of this, [[Lithium]] is exclusive to these systems.
- It looks like the main biome types (e.g. Lush, Frozen, Barren, etc.) can be a waterworld. Evidence of this is shown either by landing on a waterworld with islands, observing the "dust" trail while flying just above the water's surface (e.g. yellow-ish dust means irradiated), or waiting for a storm to occur, if there are storms.
