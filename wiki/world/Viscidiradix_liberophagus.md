## Summary

**Viscidiradix liberophagus** is found on [[Spalek]] in the [[Omen]] system. A mature, terrestrial flora that acquires nutrients from free radicals in the irradiated atmosphere, its surfaces are protected by a sticky coating.

## Appearance

A mature terrestrial flora consisting of a single thick, segmented dark gray stem with a wrinkled surface texture supporting a large terminal cup-shaped structure of vibrant cyan-blue coloration with broad, overlapping petals and a sticky coating. The cup encloses several upright, dark banded filaments tipped with pale blue. The overall morphology indicates an upright growth habit with grasping root structure specialized for free radical nutrient acquisition.

## Additional information

- The grasping root structure suggests an anchoring strategy that allows the plant to secure itself to the heavily metallic, rocky terrain of Spalek while maximizing surface contact for nutrient absorption.
- Free radicals as a nutrient source indicate an adaptation to the planet's high‑radiation environment, where unstable atmospheric molecules may be harvested directly.
- The sticky coating may serve multiple functions: trapping airborne particulates and moisture, deterring herbivores, or protecting against the corrosive effects of Radnox in the atmosphere.
