# Prionterrae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** varied forms, soil-diggers, non-aggressive
- **Added in:** Origins

**Prionterrae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Prionterrae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **PLOUGH** that appears in the game code.

These fauna are a varied form of fast-moving, non-aggressive creature. They can be found on nearly all types of planets.

## Characteristics

- They are generally medium sized and come in a variety of forms. Some are serpentine with bony exoskeletons. Some have snail-like shells with antennae. Some have central bodies with rotating legs and feet that end in spade-like diggers that churn up soil to move. Yet others appear like bony millipedes with angler-like lights.

## Variant Types

### Bone Ploughs

- **Sled** - A creature with a lower, relatively flat body that appears to skim or glide across and into surfaces, and features flowing tentacles from the rear half top of its torso.
- **Spine** /**Tendril Digger** - A creature with five blade-like segments that exudes glowing tendrils.
- **Squid** - A generally triangular head followed by a series of organic flowing tentacles, typically 3-8.
- **Osteo** / **Bone Snake** - A bony, serpentine creature with a large blade-like head that almost swims through soil.
- **Angler** - A long digger with about 20 legs that dig through the soil and has a glowing angler-like protrusion on its head.

### Caterpillar

- **Caterpillar** - A creature which resembles a large worm/caterpillar which moves through the soil and has no clearly discernible body segments aside from a non-descript head and tail.

### Living Wheel

- **Buzzsaw Snail** - A shell with a central churning blade and two large horns.

### Spinners

- **Spade** / **Roller Spade**- A spherical creature with about 10 legs that end in triangular spades that dig through the soil, allowing for faster movement.
- **Anvilsects** / **Blade Swarm** - A group of about a dozen blade-like organisms working in a pack to churn up and move quickly through soil.
