# Felidae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Cats, dogs, pigs, etc
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Felidae** is a genus of fauna.

## Summary

**Felidae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. The word *Felidae* directly references the Earth family of the same name and refers general to Earth cats. This genus in No Man's Sky is referred to by a wide variety of names due to its equally wide variety of appearances. Common names include "cats," "dogs," and "boars/pigs," all due to this genus's resemblance to Earth organisms. Species from this genus often look like Earth organisms with some exotic elements added. For example, a cat with a spiked shell, or a dog with "wings."

## Major Characteristics

Although the physical appearance is widely varied, Felidae only has two major variants: the bipedal and quadrupedal variants. These species are identical in most ways as far as their physical appearance goes, but the bipeds walk on two legs in a somewhat "clumsy" or unsure fashion, while the quadrupeds move like felines or canines from Earth biology.

- Four limbs. Quadrupeds walk/run on all four, bipeds walk on their hind legs while the forelimbs mostly just hang at their sides.
- Two eyes and a mouth, a tail, usually two ears.
- Usually four distinctly visible canine teeth when their mouths are open, except in the case of boar-like species.
- Usually canine/feline-like feet.

## Variant Types

- **Quadruped** - A four limbed feline/dog creature that walks on all four legs.
- **Bipedal** - A four limbed feline/dog creature that walks on its hind rear legs as a bipedal.
- **Unicorn** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features horns on its snout.
- **Winged** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features wings on its torso.
- **Canine** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features a dog style head.
- **Feline** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features a cat style head.
- **Reptilian** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features a reptilian style head.
- **Boar** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features a boar style head.
- **Xenobeast** - A four limbed felidae creature that walks on all four legs and features a alien style head.
