## Summary

**Clamorradix juvenis** is a flora species found on [[Aevum Michaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It is notable for its wailing roots, which emit mournful sounds when disturbed.

### Appearance

A globose, spherical flora with a compact central body exhibiting a uniformly cracked, desiccated integument of pale brownish-tan coloration marked by irregular polygonal fissures and a leathery, weathered texture. Encircling the sphere in multiple intersecting orthogonal planes is a rigid cage-like framework composed of thick, black, segmented tubular bands featuring prominent transverse ribbing and annular constrictions; several bands terminate in open circular loops or rings, while others form continuous meridional and equatorial reinforcements with additional circular framing around small white apertures. Small circular apertures with pale interiors are visible on the central mass beneath the lattice. Overall morphology indicates a heavily armored, sessile organism with pronounced external skeletal reinforcement.
