## Summary

**Vivohydrothermite** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This mineral is of hydrothermal origin and harbors faint life signs within its matrix.

### Appearance

A large, irregularly coiled or toroidal mineral formation resembling a twisted, doughnut-like structure. Its surface is densely covered in a bumpy, scaly, or vesicular texture composed of small rounded nodules and shallow pits, giving it a rough, organic appearance. The coloration is predominantly dark metallic gray to charcoal-black with subtle brownish undertones and occasional faint metallic sheen along raised edges. The overall form is compact yet asymmetrical, with the coil folding back on itself to create a central opening. No external lattice, rings, or protrusions are present beyond the inherent bumpy surface. Overall morphology indicates a dense, hydrothermal mineral with faint life signs and high metal content.
