## Summary

**Noctovolcanite** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It contains trace inclusions of dark matter within its structure.

### Appearance

A pair of large, jagged, crystalline mineral formations rising vertically from a shared base. The larger specimen on the right is sharply pointed and pyramidal, while the smaller one on the left is more rounded and irregular. Both exhibit a deep emerald‑green to teal coloration with a glossy, glassy surface and prominent internal banding. Dark, smoky inclusions and fractures run through the crystals, creating a layered, volcanic appearance. The overall morphology indicates a dense, volcanic mineral with high metal content and faint traces of dark matter.
