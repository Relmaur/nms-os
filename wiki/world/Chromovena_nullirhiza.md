## Summary

**Chromovena nullirhiza** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. A seasoned, rootless terrestrial flora, it fixes nitrogen directly from the irradiated atmosphere and exhibits a striking multi‑colored appearance.

## Appearance

A spherical terrestrial flora consisting of a central, textured, pale greenish body with a cracked, bark-like surface from which multiple thick, elongated, reddish-brown spikes radiate outward in all directions. The spikes are robust and slightly tapered with darker mottling. The overall morphology indicates a seasoned, rootless growth habit specialized for nitrogen fixation, displaying a striking multi-colored appearance with durable, spiny protection.
