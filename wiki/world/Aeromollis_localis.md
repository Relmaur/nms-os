## Summary

**Aeromollis localis** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. A fully grown terrestrial flora with a localized root structure, it emits a calming, soothing scent that pacifies local fauna, likely as a symbiotic adaptation.

## Appearance

A fully grown terrestrial flora consisting of a dense basal cluster of smooth, rounded, deep reddish-brown nodules from which multiple long, slender, ribbon-like tendrils emerge. The tendrils are dark reddish-brown at the base, transitioning to a vibrant pink-orange toward the tips, and curve gracefully upward and outward in an elegant, flowing arrangement. The overall morphology indicates a localized root structure specialized for aerobic digestion and emission of a calming scent.
