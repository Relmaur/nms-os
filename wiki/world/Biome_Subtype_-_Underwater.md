# Biome Subtype - Underwater

**Underwater** is biome subtype.

## Summary

**Underwater** is a [[biome]] subtype. It is not directly related to the actual biome of the planet, but inherits specific properties like hazards from the planet's biome.

Many planets harbor them, except for [[Biome - Dead|dead planets]] and [[Biome - Exotic|exotic planets]] which never harbor them. Nor is their presence systematic on planets that the biome is likely to possess. Underwater are non-existent on the moons except for moons within a purple star system.

## Underwater Environment

Generally, planetary dangers (extreme cold, toxicity and radiation) are more accentuated underwater than at the surface, so hiding in the oceans is not a solution to escape climate dangers. Only [[Biome - Lush|lush]], [[Biome - Barren|barren]] or [[Biome - Scorched|scorched planets]] are home to temperate oceans.

It is impossible to breathe underwater, once in the water your underwater protection will activate and goes down very quickly, so it is necessary to have appropriate [[Hazard Protection]] upgrades for the exosuit (purchasable in [[Space Station|Space Stations]]) to make extended dives.

In case of emergency, it is possible to build an [[Marine Shelter|underwater shelter]] or swim up from underwater to recharge Underwater Protection.

In addition, the oceans can be explored with the help of a submarine, the [[Nautilon]], which is faster and naturally provides [[Oxygen]]. To propel it, it must be recharged with [[Hydrothermal Fuel Cell|Hydrothermal Fuel Cells]].

## Fauna and Flora

The oceans are home to a specific [[Fauna]] and [[Flora]], fish and other aquatic creatures such as crabs, jellyfish and even more monstrous creatures such as [[Abyssal Horror|Abyssal Horrors]].

The flora is very variable depending on the planet, it is also likely to be scarce and find rock formations and even gigantic crystals instead. However, there is a bioluminescent plant in all conditions, which makes it possible to obtain bags of kelp and whose harvesting also allows you to fill your Underwater Protection in case of emergency.

## Resources

Specific resources can be found in the ocean.

Aquatic minerals usually contain [[Salt]] or [[Chlorine]] (instead of [[Ferrite Dust]]), flora contains mostly [[Cyto-Phosphate]] instead of [[Carbon]].

The seas are also full of volcanic vents from which it is possible to collect [[Crystal Sulphide|Crystal Sulphude]]. Other interesting items can be found, such as [[Armoured Clam|Armoured Clams]]. Worlds with deep waters will allow players to find [[Lithium]] and [[Sea Glass]].

## Deep Waters

As of The Worlds: Part II update, deep waters and water worlds were introduced in purple star systems. Starting with this update, players can now explore new aquatic flora and fauna. In deep water, there is bioluminescent flora and translucent fauna. These types of oceans may deepen from 600 to 1,200u, and in deep water, one may find [[Lithium]] and [[Sea Glass]]—the two new resources.

Exploring in low depth will render the environment dark and make the sound condensed. If the player cross the depth of 200u, "**DEEP WATER**" and "**HYPERPRESSURE**" warning will appears on the [[Life Support]] and the [[Hazard Protection]], and will drain them faster, through this is minimized by installing the [[Pressure Membrane]].

## Additional Information

- Beginning in Worlds Part I, players that have the [[Aqua-Jets]] installed on their Starship will be allowed to land on the water.
