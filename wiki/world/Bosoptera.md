# Bosoptera

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial / Aerial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Flying beetles
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Bosoptera** is a genus.

## Summary

**Bosoptera** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **FLYINGBEETLE** that appears in the game code.

These fauna resemble flying beetles such as the [Scarab Beetle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynastinae). They appear to travel in swarms of four to six individuals and are not aggressive. They are typically found on lush planets.

## Major Variants & Characteristics

- They are generally medium to large in size
- Insectoid with six legs and a hard carapace
- Winged and tend to land on trees and rocks
- Horns, antennae, and other exoskeletal protrusions

## Additional information

They produce the [[Cooking Products|cooking product]]: [[Crunchy Wings]].
