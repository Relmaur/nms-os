# Spiralis

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Spiral shaped, flies in and out of ground
- **Added in:** Origins

**Spiralis** is a genus.

## Summary

**Spiralis** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **DRILL** that appears in the game code.

Spiralis is always spiral shaped and flies in and out of the ground. The shape and type of spiral are varied.

## Characteristics

- Spiral shaped
- Can phase though terrain

## Variants

- **Glowdrill** -This creatures features several different colors in a regular glowing pattern.
- **Cordrill** - The creature strips appear to resemble cord, rope or twine and maintain a constant color.
- **Plantdrill** - The creature strips appear to resemble organic plant matter, and maintain a constant color.
- **Bonedrill** - The creature strips appear to resemble segmented bones and maintain a constant color.
- **Spiradrill** - This creatures features a light and dark coloration in a regular pattern.

## Additional information

They produce the [[Cooking Products|cooking product]]: [[Latticed Sinew]].
