# Talpidae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** mole or turtle appearance, always in dirt
- **Added in:** Origins

**Talpidae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Talpidae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **MOLE** that appears in the game code.

Talpidae can appear as a mole, turtle, or a squid like creature.

## Major Characteristics

- Partially submerged
- Scooches in a slow and jagged movement

## Variant Types

- **Spikey** - This creature is often partially submerged in the ground and moves in a slow and jagged movement. It features a series of protruding spikes on its torso that wave as it moves.
- **Turtle** - This creature is often partially submerged in the ground and moves in a slow and jagged movement. It features a shell, head and appendages that resemble turtles.
- **Squid** -	This creature is often partially submerged in the ground and moves in a slow and jagged movement. It features a shell, head and appendages that resemble crustaceans.
