## Summary

**Lunarapidus brevitas** is found on [[Spalek]] in the [[Omen]] system. A short-lived terrestrial flora with a lifespan of several days, it draws nutrients from moonlight and exhibits rapid reproduction.

## Appearance

A short, bulbous terrestrial flora consisting of a thick, irregular dark gray-purple body with a lumpy, fleshy surface texture. The central mass supports several upward-curving, arm-like branches terminating in large, fan-shaped leaves of yellowish-brown hue with serrated, toothed margins and occasional perforations. Numerous slender, tendril-like roots extend downward from the base in a grasping formation. The overall morphology indicates a very young growth habit specialized for rapid reproduction and moonlight nutrient absorption.
