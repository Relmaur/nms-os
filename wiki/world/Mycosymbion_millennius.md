## Summary

**Mycosymbion millennius** is found on [[Mk VI]] in the [[Omen]] system. An ancient, terrestrial fungus‑like flora with a symbiotic root structure, it draws atmospheric moisture and exhibits inconsistent blossoming cycles.

## Appearance

A multi-stemmed terrestrial flora consisting of three large, umbrella-shaped caps of warm orange-brown coloration with a smooth, slightly glossy surface and subtle textural pitting. The thick, fleshy stalks are yellowish-tan and exhibit a twisted, intertwined growth pattern at the base, supporting the caps at varying heights. The overall morphology indicates an ancient symbiotic growth habit specialized for atmospheric moisture absorption with inconsistent blossoming cycles.
