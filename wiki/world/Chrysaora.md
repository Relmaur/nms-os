# Chrysaora

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Aquatic
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Jellyfish, never predatory
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Chrysaora** is a genus.

## Summary

**Chrysaora** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. Generally referred to as jellyfish (the term used by the game code). Chrysaora species are found exclusively underwater, and are never predatory, although they do explode if touched by a player. Although they may be found at any depth of water, they will sometimes have issues spawning in very shallow pools, either not spawning at all or exhibiting glitchy behaviour.

## Major Variants & Characteristics

Chrysaora have no major variants, although they do have a wide variety of diversity in their appearance.

### Standard Chrysaora Appearance

- Propel themselves by jet propulsion: inflation and deflation of the upper portion of their body (the head in most species).
- Most species have either two eyes, appearing either as a pair of eyestalks or bulging eyes, or four front-facing eyes.
- All species have tentacles, typically appearing as either eight radially arranged, arrow-headed tentacles, or ten ribbon-shaped tentacles which appear in two bilaterally symmetrical rows

### Shared Characteristics

- Usually found in large schools (10+) of the same species.
- Causes damage to the player upon collision, killing itself in the process.

## Additional information

At one point jellyfish were a subtype of the [[Ictaloris]] genus, but were expanded into being their own separate genus with the [[Abyss]] update.
