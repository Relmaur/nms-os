# Biome - The Reliquary

**The Reliquary** is one of the Biome varieties.

## Summary

The surface of the planet can be covered with rocks, grasses, ices, etc., however they depends on the main biome of the planet. [[Ruined Structure|Ruined Structures]] can be found here, in which it seems to belongs to an ancient civilization that once lived there. [[Mining]] these "ruins" yields [[Polished Stone]] that are required in [[Base building]] for buildable ruined structure decorations.

[[Memory Stone]] can be occasionally found on these planets. These stones rewards the player base building ruins decoration.

## Fauna and Flora

Can be numerous and paradise like.
