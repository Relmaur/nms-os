## Summary

**Gasphagus protoskeletalis** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This old, invasive‑rooted flora draws its nutrients from gas clouds and is noted for its developing mineralized proto‑skeleton.

### Appearance

A cluster of three large, ovoid to egg‑shaped structures emerging from a shared, irregular bronze‑colored base. Each ovoid body has a smooth, marbled, pale blue‑gray to silvery surface with subtle internal veining and faint translucency, resembling polished stone or thick wax. The base is a rugged, cup‑like structure of dark bronze to brownish‑gold coloration with a heavily textured, cracked, and porous surface featuring numerous irregular holes and jagged, crown‑like protrusions that cradle the ovoid bodies. Small glowing teal spots are visible on the bronze base near the points of contact. Overall morphology indicates a sessile, symbiotic flora with multiple large, waxy fruiting bodies supported by an invasive, grasping root structure.
