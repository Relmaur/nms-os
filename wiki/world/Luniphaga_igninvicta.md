## Summary

**Luniphaga igninvicta** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This old, grasping-rooted flora draws its nutrients from moonlight and is notable for producing fireproof nuts.

### Appearance

A large, upright floral organism consisting of two distinct parts. The lower portion is a broad, cup-shaped or urn-like base of dark bronze to brownish-gold coloration with a heavily textured, cracked, and porous surface featuring numerous irregular holes and openings. The upper portion is a massive, ovoid to egg-shaped structure of pale blue-gray to silvery-white with a smooth, marbled, and slightly translucent appearance, resembling polished stone or thick wax. The egg-shaped structure sits nestled within the open, crown-like rim of the bronze base. Overall morphology indicates a sessile, specialized flora adapted for nutrient uptake via moonlight and protected by a fire-resistant outer casing.

## Additional information

### Notes

- The plant’s fireproof nuts are encased in the pale, stone-like upper structure; they resist combustion even in the planet’s most extreme conditions.
- Its grasping roots anchor it firmly to the soil, allowing it to withstand the toxic winds and caustic ground.
- Nutrient absorption occurs primarily at night, when moonlight is absorbed through the pale, translucent upper surface.
