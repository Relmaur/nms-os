**Adhesiophytom omnidirens** is a [[Flora]] species.

## Summary

**Adhesiophytom omnidirens** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. An ancient, parasitic terrestrial flora with an omni‑directional growth habit, it draws sustenance from surrounding plants using a sticky coating.

## Appearance

A multi-branched terrestrial flora consisting of numerous thick, elongated, worm-like stalks of dark brownish-black coloration with a deeply ribbed, segmented texture. The stalks transition to a pale yellowish-tan at the tips and radiate outward in all directions from a central base. The overall morphology indicates an ancient omni‑directional growth habit with a sticky coating, specialized for capturing and consuming other plants.
