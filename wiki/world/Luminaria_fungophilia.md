## Summary

**Luminaria fungophilia** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. An advanced terrestrial flora, it produces luminous bulbs through a deep symbiotic relationship with radiation‑tolerant fungi.

## Appearance

A tall, graceful terrestrial flora consisting of a thick, twisted turquoise trunk with a textured, bark-like surface that splits into multiple elegant, arching branches. The branches curve outward and downward, each terminating in a large, glowing, bell-shaped bulb of luminous white with a soft inner light. Dark, leaf-like fronds emerge from the upper portions, creating a striking contrast against the pale structure. The overall morphology indicates an advanced fungal symbiotic growth habit specialized for low-light environments with luminous bulbs.
