# Crustacea

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Aquatic

- **Added in:** Remnant

**Crustacea** is a genus.

## Summary

**Crustacea** is a [[genus]] of water [[fauna]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].

Their body is made of

- Head with two eyes and small pseudo-pods on the mouth sides
- Segmented body with 8 articulated legs. No separate thorax/abdomen like in earth shrimps.
- Tail
- various optional ornaments
