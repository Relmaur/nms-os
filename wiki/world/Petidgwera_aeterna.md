**Petidgwera aeterna** is a [[Flora]] species.

## Summary

**Petidgwera aeterna** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. A timeless, parasitic terrestrial flora, it sneakily taps into geothermal vents and the root networks of other plants to extract nutrients.

## Appearance

A slender, upright terrestrial flora consisting of a thin green stem supporting numerous elongated, curved, pod-like structures arranged in alternating pairs along its length. The pods display a vibrant gradient from deep reddish-orange at the base to bright yellow-green at the tips, with a smooth, slightly ribbed surface. The overall morphology indicates a timeless parasitic growth habit specialized for extracting nutrients from underground bubbles in a sneaky, inconspicuous manner.
