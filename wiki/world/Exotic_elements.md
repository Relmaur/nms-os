# Exotic elements

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Asteroids, Planets, Moons
- **Type:** Resource
- **Description:** Exotic Elements
- **Added in:** Frontiers

Exotic elements are a division of resources.

## Summary

**Exotic elements** are one of the eight divisions of the [[resource|resources]] in [[No Man's Sky]].

**Exotic elements** are mainly used as an ingredient for [[refining]] other resources or [[tradeable|tradeables]]. They are the main ingredients for crafting [[freighter]] related [[base building]] products and some planet/moon base building products.

## Members

##### Neutron-Rich Fuel Element

- [[Tritium]]

##### Valuable Asteroid Mineral

- [[Gold]]
- [[Platinum]]
- [[Silver]]

## Sources

Tritium can only be mined from [[asteroid|asteroids]] by using a [[Starship Weapons technology|starship weapon]] or by colliding with an asteroid.

The others can be found:
- By using starship weapons or a collision with an asteroid containing it
- In caves by mining some types of [[mineral|minerals]]
- Using a [[Terrain Manipulator]] to collect from surface [[Resource Deposit|Resource Deposits]]
- Building a [[Mineral Extractor]] to mine [[Deep-Level Mineral Deposit|Deep-Level Mineral Deposits]]
- Refined using certain [[Tradeable|tradeable alloy metals]] in a [[Refiner]]
- Refined by combining certain resources in [[Medium Refiner]] or [[Large Refiner|Large Refiners]].
