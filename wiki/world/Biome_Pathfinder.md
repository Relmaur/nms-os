# Biome (Pathfinder)

**Biomes** influence a [[planet]]'s general environmental hazard as well as the richness of animals and plants. Biomes **do not** influence the [[weather]] and therefore the intensity of the present environmental hazard. Each planet will only have a single biome to encourage exploration.

## Types of biomes

There are many types of biomes available for planets.<br> The name of the biome appears in front of "planet" when first discovering a planet.
