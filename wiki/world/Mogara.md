# Mogara

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** humanoid, similar to Gek or Teddy Bear
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Mogara** is a genus.

## Summary

**Mogara** is a [[genus]] of [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky. They are commonly referred to as "grunts" or "Proto Geks".

They are considered rare spawns, they were first demonstrated in the early gameplay demos, though they seem to have become more common as of the Atlas Rises update.

## Major Characteristics

Mogara species are easily recognizable.

- Bipedal.
- Humanoids with 2 arms.
- Usually seen in small groups/gatherings
- Some specimens bear a remarkable resemblance to the Gek.
- Some specimens bear a remarkable resemblance to a Teddy Bear.

## Variant Subtypes

- **Teddy Bear**/**Carebear** - A mogara which bears a strong resemblance to a bipedal walking teddy bear.
- **Gek-Like** - A mogara which bears a strong resemblance to a bipedal walking [[Gek]].
- **Flying Winged Mogara** - A mogara which has wings and occassionally floats slightly off the ground as it moves.
- **Masked Mogara** - A mogara which appears to have a shell based mask covering its face.
- **Teddy Antlers** - A mogara with antlers which bears a strong resemblance to a bi-pedal walking teddy bear.
- **Gek Antlers** - A mogara with antlers which bears a strong resemblance to a bipedal walking [[Gek]].
