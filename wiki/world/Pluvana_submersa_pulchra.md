## Summary

**Pluvana submersa pulchra** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. An ancient terrestrial flora with a localized root structure, it draws nutrients from heavy rain and possesses an inner beauty visible upon close inspection.

## Appearance

A striking terrestrial flora consisting of a tall, vertically stacked column of smooth, rounded, pebble-like segments in warm tan to golden-brown hues with a fine, cracked surface texture. The segments are tightly interlocked in an elegant, slightly spiraling formation that tapers gently toward the top. The overall morphology indicates an ancient growth habit with localized root structure, specialized for heavy rainfall environments and displaying a unique inner beauty when viewed closely.
