# Oxyacta

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Flying
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Wraiths or flying snakes.
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Oxyacta** is a genus.

## Summary

**Oxyacta** is a [[genus]] of [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky.

They are commonly referred to as "wraiths." This is because when they were visible in the early No Man's Sky trailers, they were seen around [[Portal|Portals]], and came to be called "Portal wraiths." Although they are not known to be associated with Portals in the final release, the "wraith" portion stuck. They are also commonly called "flying snakes," which is the 'common name' that the game code uses, and "flying worms," due to their appearance.

## Defining Characteristics

Oxyacta species are easily recognizable, as it is the largest flying genus in terms of size.

- Long, snake-like bodies with no significant limbs.
- Often segmented, as seen [here](http://68.media.tumblr.com/e750f18e9dfd44466d69e8afe180c5b0/tumblr_oipyobefDq1vd179lo1_1280.png), or more flexible, as seen [here](http://68.media.tumblr.com/7a54dbae8b538fbeff8f0a0a6194d9e8/tumblr_ok80i15v1q1vd179lo2_1280.png).
- Usually seen in pairs or in large schools/gatherings.
- Invincible - cannot be killed by any known means.
- Will never attack a player or any other fauna.
- Often appear with leechlike mouthparts.
