## Summary

**Conjuratus fragilibulbus** is found on [[Spalek]] in the [[Omen]] system. A youthful, terrestrial flora that appears to conjure its nutrients through an unknown biochemical process, producing delicate, fragile bulbs.

## Appearance

A slender, upright terrestrial flora consisting of a single contorted, sinuous stem of dark teal-green coloration with a deeply wrinkled, fibrous surface texture. The stem supports a terminal cluster of numerous elongated, finger-like bulbs of pale purple to lavender hue with distinct dark banding, arranged in a radiating, hand-like formation. The overall morphology indicates a youthful, semi-woody growth habit with an obtuse basal root structure specialized for vertical elongation and fragile bulb development.

## Additional information

- The obtuse root structure suggests a shallow, spreading anchoring system, which may allow the plant to rapidly absorb surface moisture and nutrients in the irradiated, heavy metallic terrain.
- "Conjuration" as a nutrient source likely refers to a biochemical or metabolic process that synthesizes organic compounds from inorganic precursors, possibly utilizing the planet's ambient radiation or electrical storms as an energy source.
- The fragile bulbs may be a reproductive adaptation, designed to detach and disperse easily in the planet's frequent irradiated thunderstorms.
