## Summary

**Stratofossilite** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This mineral is formed through fossilization and exhibits a distinct multi‑layered structure, preserving sequential records of ancient life.

### Appearance

A tall, vertically oriented, columnar mineral formation composed of four distinct stacked segments that gradually decrease in width toward the top, giving it a slightly tapered, tower‑like profile. Each segment has a rough, heavily fissured and layered texture with sharp horizontal banding and angular edges, resembling stratified rock or fossilized material. The coloration is a dark, metallic olive‑brown to bronze on the lower sections, transitioning upward to a vibrant, iridescent turquoise‑blue with a crystalline or mineralized crust on the upper segments. The overall morphology indicates a dense, multi‑layered fossilized structure formed through prolonged lithification processes.
