## Summary

**Flakiphyta nitrogena** is found on [[Spalek]] in the [[Omen]] system. A seasoned, terrestrial flora that draws nutrients from sublimated nitrogen in the irradiated atmosphere, its tissues are protected by a distinctive flaky coating.

## Appearance

A multi-stemmed terrestrial flora consisting of several thick, irregularly twisted and fused stems of dark brownish-gray coloration with vivid orange-red patches and a rugged, flaky, deeply fissured surface texture. The stems support sparse, slender secondary branches terminating in small to medium ovate-lanceolate leaves of muted dusty blue-green hue with slightly irregular margins and leathery texture. Foliage is concentrated apically on the contorted column, with thickened basal root-like projections anchoring an upright, semi-woody growth habit specialized for structural resilience and vertical elongation.

- The significant root structure suggests a robust anchoring system, allowing the plant to withstand the planet's irradiated thunderstorms and heavy metallic terrain.
- Sublimated nitrogen as a nutrient source indicates an adaptation to the thin, Radnox‑rich atmosphere, where nitrogen is deposited directly from the air onto the plant's surfaces.
- The flaky coating may serve as a protective barrier against the planet's persistent electrical storms and high background radiation, or as a means of shedding contaminants.
