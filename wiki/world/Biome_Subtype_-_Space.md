# Biome Subtype - Space

**Space** is a biome subtype.

## Summary

**Space** is a [[biome]] sub-type that is an extension of the general planetary info for every [[planet]] and [[moon]]. It is NOT related to the actual biome of the planet.

This page covers the dangers of traveling in Space without your Starship, and that it should never be attempted without the proper equipment or planning.

## Accessibility

This subtype is accessible by either exiting the [[corvette]] while in space, building on a planet into the **Outer Atmosphere** (where you no longer have access to building) and flying out of the **Outer Atmosphere** using your [[Jetpack]], jumping out of [[Freighter|Freighters]], or by glitching out of [[Space station|Space stations]].

## Outer Atmosphere

The Outer Atmosphere is a part of all planets/moons where space and the planet meet. The Outer Atmosphere is also the Build Limit for base building. Once you reach the Outer Atmosphere, you can no longer build. (However, you can stay below the Outer Atmosphere and build into it, as long as you're not in it during the building process.) The Outer Atmosphere also has rapid oxygen drain.

- Bringing [[Oxygen]], [[Life Support Gel]], or any similar Oxygen Replenishing item is recommended.
- Bring a fully upgraded [[Life Support]] system and Jetpack (with S and/or X class modules) as well as food that gives you Jetpack Boosts, such as [[Bread]].

## Space

You can tell you are in the Space region when your camera begins to malfunction. You will also notice that instead of just the oxygen rapidly depleting, your [[Thermal Protection Module (Heat)|Freeze Protection]] fails as well, and at a faster rate than your oxygen. This will result in eventual death unless you're using some sort of bot to spam refill both Life Support and Freeze Protection. There is nothing note-worthy in Space; it is similar to the Space in the Main Story after you get sent there, but you lose Freeze Protection.

## Additional Notes

- The best way to get to the main part of Space is by jumping off a [[frigate]] or freighter; or glitching out of a Space station, ignoring the main part of the Outer Atmosphere that planets have.
- One way to glitch out of a freighter or space station is to sit on a chair or a sofa, enter and exit photo mode, and this will remove all collision, life support penalties, and also hazard protection penalties until you leave the sitting position.
- Building a Tower into the Outer Atmosphere is really useful when going through it from the planet. Simply jetpack up the wall and you won't lose any Boost.
- Freighters and Space Stations have a slight Outer Atmosphere, which is why they are better to go through, since they are smaller.
- There are little to no ways to glitch out of Space Stations intentionally; most glitches are accidental and happen from loading errors or collision errors.
- It is unknown if you can enter a Space Station, [[Atlas Station]], or Freighter without a Starship. Further testing needs to be done.
- [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]] have similar properties to Space, as you also lose Freeze Protection, though not as fast. (The Outer Atmosphere has the Oxygen loss, losing it as fast as you would in Space.)
- The Outer Atmosphere has slight gravity, so if you were to run out of Jetpack Boost, you would fall back to the planet. This is unpleasant if you don't have the [[Airburst Engine|Jetpack Recharge]] module.
- Space has no gravity, so once you're there, you can let your Jetpack recharge before you keep going. (This assumes you have the Jetpack Recharge module.)
