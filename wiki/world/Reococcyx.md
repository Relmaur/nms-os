# Reococcyx

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** bipedal deer or antelope
- **Added in:** Companions

**Reococcyx** is a genus.

## Summary

**Reococcyx** is a [[genus]] of [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky. Commonly referred to as "bipedal antelopes," the common name the game uses, and "bipedal deer."

The name of this genus is likely a reference to , the Earth-biology genus to which roadrunner birds belong.

## Major Characteristics

Reococcyx only has one variant. They are very similar to [[Tetraceris]] species, with the major and obvious difference being that they're bipedal rather than quadrupedal.

- Bipedal, usually hooves or elephant-like feet.
- Often have some form of horns, ranging from small stubs to decently-sized antlers.
- Two distinct eyes, a mouth, and nose at the end of snout.
