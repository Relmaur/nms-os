# Osteofelidae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** stone/bone textured, aggressive
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Osteofelidae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Osteofelidae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **BONECAT** that appears in the game code.

Osteofelidae is immediately recognizable as it appears to be made of stone or bones.

## Major Variants & Characteristics

- Stone texture
- Often is covered in patches of jewels
- Often aggressive

## Additional information

They produce [[Cooking Products|cooking products]]: [[Crystal Flesh]] and [[Bone Nuggets]].
