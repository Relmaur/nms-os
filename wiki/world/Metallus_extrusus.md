**Metallus extrusus** is a [[Flora]] species.

## Summary

**Metallus extrusus** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. A fully developed terrestrial flora with a planet‑spanning global root network of metallic durability, it fixes nitrogen and produces exceptionally tough fruit.

## Appearance

A fully developed terrestrial flora consisting of a large, flat, triangular lattice-like structure of interwoven, gnarled branches in mottled gray-green and dark tones with a tough, bark-like texture. The intricate open-weave framework tapers to a sharp point at the base while spreading wide at the top, forming a delicate yet durable triangular sail-like form. The overall morphology indicates a global root system specialized for nitrogen fixation and production of durable fruit.
