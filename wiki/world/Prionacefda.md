# Prionacefda

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Aquatic

- **Added in:** ExoMech

**Prionacefda** is a genus.

## Summary

**Prionacefda** is a [[genus]] of water [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky, usually referred to as "swimming cows".

They look like land species of quadrupedal [[Ungulatis]], but are found underwater, and have swimming animations.

They are considered rare spawns.

## Defining Characteristics

**Prionacefda** species are easily recognizable.

- Quadrupedal "cows"
- Found underwater.
