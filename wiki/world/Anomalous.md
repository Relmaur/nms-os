# Anomalous

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** distinct appearance, specific to exotic biomes
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Anomalous** is a creature genus.

## Summary

**Anomalous** is a [[Scientific name|genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. This type of genus can only be found on planets with an [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] [[biome]].

Creatures belong to this genus can be easily identified by the [[Analysis Visor]], which shows them as **Anomalous Animals**.

## Loot information

Anomalous type creatures drop different kind of [[items]] when killed. It may change between planets but stays the same on any particular planet:
- 2 [[Hex Core|Hex Cores]]
- 16 [[Living Slime]]
- 25 [[Mordite]]
- 27 [[Oxygen]]
- ? [[Pugneum]]
- 14 [[Residual Goop]]

## Appearance

Anomalous creatures have a very distinct appearance, specific to each exotic biome.

## Additional information

The term **Anomalous** is derived from the description seen when scanning any of these species with a [[Analysis Visor|visor]] and is not their genus listed by the game code. According to an analysis of the 2.42 game files, the small fluttering variety found on Irri Shell Exotic worlds belongs to the butterfly genus [[Rhopalocera]], while all others belong to the blob genus [[Lok]].
