## Summary

**Photovirens robustus** is a flora species found on [[Aevum Michaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It is a robust photosynthesizer notable for its resistance to blights.

### Appearance

An upright floral organism consisting of a thick, vertically oriented stem with prominent horizontal segmentation and a smooth, grayish-green woody integument. Surmounting the stem is a single massive terminal bloom forming an elongated, trumpet-shaped corolla that flares outward into a broad, open cup composed of numerous overlapping, slightly undulate petals of luminous peach-pink to pale salmon coloration with subtle radial veining. Centrally projecting from within the corolla are several tall, dark teal-green, cylindrical staminal structures arranged in a tight fascicle, each terminating in a blunt, rounded apex. A whorl of broad, metallic blue bracts with prominent parallel venation and iridescent sheen radiates outward at the base of the bloom. Overall morphology indicates a robust, photosynthetically specialized floral structure adapted for elevated display.
