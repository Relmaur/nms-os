**Lunaphila oculirritans** is a [[Flora]] species.

## Summary

**Lunaphila oculirritans** is found on [[Sanctae Barbarae Venenum I]] in the [[Omen]] system. A dying terrestrial flora with an efficient root structure, it draws nutrients from moonlight and is known for its ocular irritant properties.

## Appearance

A tall terrestrial flora consisting of a thick, segmented green stem supporting a large, upright, cup-shaped bloom of vibrant luminous green with a scalloped rim and subtle vertical ribbing. The bloom sits atop a base of broad, dark purple leaves with prominent veining. The overall morphology indicates a dying growth habit specialized for moonlight nutrient absorption and known for its ocular irritant properties.
