# Floradae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Ground
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Plant/Animal Hybrids
- **Added in:** Breach

**Floradae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Floradae** is a [[genus]] of [[species|fauna]] in No Man's Sky. Their name is a combination of the words *flora* and *felidae*, and as such, they are a mixture of plants and animals.

## Major Characteristics

Because they share the same animations as [[Felidae]], Floradae appear in both bipedal and quadrupedal variants.

- Flowers for heads, which have bioluminescent stamens.
- The body consists of a root system roughly in the shape of a Felidae.
- A mane made of leaves that runs from the neck to the base of the tail.
  - The middle section's leaves are a different color and shape - having serrated leaf margins instead of smooth ones.
