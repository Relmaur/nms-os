# Protosphaeridae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Spherical, non-aggressive
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Protosphaeridae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Protosphaeridae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **PROTOROLLER** that appears in the game code.

These fauna are a varied form of spherical, non-aggressive creatures. They can be found on virtually any type of world except airless and exotic planets.

## Loot Information

Protosphaeridae produce [[Bone Nuggets]] when fed and [[Lumpy Brainstem]] when slain (in addition to usual [[Mordite]]).

## Major Variants & Characteristics

They are generally medium sized and come in a variety of forms as follows:
- **Armadillon** / **Rolly Polly** - An armored arthropod creature that appears like a woodlouse curled up in a shell
- **Bone Hoop** / **Armoured Rollers** - A bony hoop-like shape that rolls very fast.
- **Sauronosphere** / **Cat Eye** - A eyeball like spherical creature.
- **Cats Eye Swarm** - A swarm of 6 to 8 eyes that roll together in a tight formation appearing much like the eyes of cats
- **Eyeball Bubble** - A translucent sphere with 12 half-open eyeballs within it
- **Jellified Ball** - A jellified mass with fleshy buboes
- **Puffball** - A perforated sphere with specially-placed breathing holes
- **Rainbow Sphere** - A translucent sphere with 12 prismatic spheres within it
- **Snake Ball** - A armored reptilian creature that appears like a coiled ball of snakes
- **Futball** / **Soccer Ball** - A creature that resembles a football/soccer ball
- **Tendril Cluster** / **Nerve Bundle** / **Virus** - A creature that appears as a cluster of tendrils attached to a sphere
- **Kugelpanzer** - A creature with a shell like ball
- **Ladybug** - A ball-shaped creature with a carapace bearing large colorful dots
- **Unmentionable** - Somewhat similar to the Jellified Ball, a clump of bulbous body parts with thick pustules
