# Mechanoceris

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Terrestrial
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** Robotic Creature
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Mechanoceris** is a genus.

## Summary

**Mechanoceris** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **ROBOTANTELOPE** that appears in the game code.

Mechanoceris is a robotic creature and has a ten percent chance of manifesting in groups of two on planets in uncharted systems. In these systems, they can also exist on [[Biome - Exotic|Exotic]] planets in place of their usual Anomalous fauna.

## Major Characteristics

- Robotic

## Variant Types

- **Head Shape:** Disk ("Saucer"), Triangle ("Long"), or Torso-shaped ("Curved")
- **Shoulder Attachments:** Shields, Solar Cells, Spark Plugs, or Plain
- **Back Attachments:** 1 Shield, 3 Shields, Wheel, Batteries/Reactor ("Torus"), Magnet ("U-shaped"), or Plain

## Additional information

They produce the following [[Cooking Products|cooking product]]: [[Chewy Wires]]

They can be tamed with [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]].
