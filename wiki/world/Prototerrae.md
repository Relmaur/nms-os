# Prototerrae

## Quick Facts

- **Location:** Subterranean
- **Type:** Genus
- **Description:** tentacles, medium sized
- **Added in:** Origins

**Prototerrae** is a genus.

## Summary

**Prototerrae** is a [[genus]] of [[fauna]] in No Man's Sky. It is a player-created name for the creature ID **PROTODIGGER** that appears in the game code.

These fauna are subterranean, tentacular creatures. They are solitary and are not aggressive. They can be found on lush, scorched, arctic, radioactive, and toxic planets.

## Characteristics

- They are generally medium sized
- Six to eight tentacles
- Some tentacles end in blinking eyes
- Some tentacles have suckers and/or stinging barbs
- Some are gas erupting from the ground

## Variant Types

- ** Tentacle Cluster** - A series of tentacles protruding from the surface that move wildly and may rapidly move across the surface when the creature is in an aggressive mood.
- ** Sporestalk Cluster** - A series of stalks protruding from the surface that move wildly and may rapidly move across the surface when the creature is in an aggressive mood.
- ** Eyestalk Cluster** - Similar to Sporestalk Cluster, but with an eyeball. A series of eyestalks protruding from the surface that move wildly and may rapidly move across the surface when the creature is in an aggressive mood.
- ** Geothermal Vents** /**Smokestack ** - The creature appears only as a series of sequential gas vents erupting from the ground.
- ** Scrat** - The creature appears as a small mole-like creature that constantly dips and emerges from the surface at high speeds.
