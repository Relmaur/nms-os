## Summary

**Polyvalorite** is encountered on [[Sanctus Nathanaelis I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. This mineral formed through polymerization and possesses a unique structure of high industrial value.

### Appearance

A large, dense, teardrop‑shaped mineral mass with a smooth, rounded upper dome and a slightly flattened base. Its surface is dark olive‑green to brownish‑gray with a rough, layered texture marked by prominent horizontal striations, shallow grooves, and fine cracking patterns. Subtle metallic sheen is visible along some of the ridges and fracture lines, consistent with the high metal content. The overall form is compact and solid, with no external lattice, rings, or protrusions. Overall morphology indicates a recently formed, polymerized mineral with industrial value.
