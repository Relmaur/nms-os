## Summary

**Oculifer transferrus** is a flora species found on [[Terra Lorrainae I]] in the [[Ioseph]] system. It possesses partially developed ocular structures and transferrable roots that enable slow migration across the landscape.

## Appearance

A sessile, roughly spherical flora with a rigid, polyhedral central body displaying a prominent honeycomb-like integument composed of glossy, raised hexagonal plates in olive-green tones. Radiating outward in a dense, radial arrangement are approximately twenty to thirty elongate, tapering, horn-like projections of varying lengths and gentle curvature; these appendages exhibit a smooth, sclerotized texture and transition from greenish bases to deep reddish-brown to maroon coloration. Small recessed pores or ocular-like structures are visible between the surface facets. Overall morphology indicates a stationary, defensive autotrophic organism specialized for elevated sensory or protective function.
