**Community Research (week 7)** is a mission.

## Summary

**Community Research (week 7)** is the seventh mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is help the recovery of [[Specialist Polo]] with [[Priest Entity Nada]]'s guidance by taking images of various underwater [[fauna]].

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.71|Abyss 1.71]] until 9 November 2018. The first part of the mission takes place on a planet called Kofu 31/J2 in the Volandro system.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

## Walkthrough

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

With this mission set as the active mission, fly into space and Priest Entity Nada will contact the player through the communicator.

**Priest Entity Nada**
--<br>
--
- Accept
--

### Visit the marked planet

A portal will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

 A chill runs through my body as I face the silent gateway.
 It tugs at my mind with menacing persistence.<br>
 What 'dark place' is Nada sending me to?
- Input Nada's Glyphs
- Leave

### Photograph an underwater swarm

After arriving to the other side of the portal, the mission objective will be updated, but the destination will not be indicated with an icon.

The player can use [[Analysis Visor]] to check for their current location in order to reach the coordinates given by Nada.

Using the [[Photo Mode]] is required and after successfully taking a picture of the swarm, the mission objective will be updated again.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, after using the [[starship]]'s scanner.

**Priest Entity Nada**
--
- Upload photographs
--<br>
--
 As Nada mentions Polo's robotic invention, their visor lights flicker subtly, in what I can't help but interpret as a facial expression. It passes so quickly, however, I question whether it was a  just a glitch in the hologram.
- End transmission

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Photograph underwater creatures on specific worlds

The broadcast is amplified and the signal zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to take photographs of underwater creatures on any planets.

On the [[Galactic Map]] water is indicated per system, therefore the player is required to warp into the system and use visual checking which planets have water.

The planets will require the following biomes with water present and they need to be visited in the following order twice. It is advised to visit the same planets the second time.
- [[Biome - Lush|Tropical world]], where [[Star Bulb]] is present
- [[Biome - Toxic|Toxic world]], where [[Fungal Mould]] is present
- [[Biome - Scorched|Scorched world]], where [[Solanium]] is present
- [[Biome - Irradiated|Radioactive world]], where [[Gamma Root]] is present
- [[Biome - Frozen|Frozen world]], where [[Frostwort]] is present

If you turn in partial results, the following conversation takes place.

**Priest Entity Nada**
--
- Upload photographs
1. Nada thanks me, clapping their hands together in a convincing display of delight. They suggest that more data would further assist Polo's existential recovery.
- End transmission
1. --

After taking all 10 images, the data limit is reached and the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, after using the starship's scanner.

**Priest Entity Nada**
--
- Upload photographs
--

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The Space Anomaly will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

After speaking with the merchant NPC again, the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 2,000  Quicksilver after uploading the first image.
- 1,250  Quicksilver after uploading all 10 optional images.
