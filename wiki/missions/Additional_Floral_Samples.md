**Additional Floral Samples** is a secondary mission.

## Summary

**Additional Floral Samples** is a repeatable secondary mission. The mission, which can be completed once every 24 hours (real time, not game time), tasks the player with gathering resources commonly gained from [[farming]].

## Walkthrough

### Starting the Mission

The player may only initiate the mission once the following two conditions have both been met:
1. The player has finished the mission [[Agricultural Research]].
1. At least 24 hours have passed in real time since the mission was last completed (or the player has never completed this mission before).

Once the prerequisites have been met, the player must speak with the [[Farmer (NPC)]] at an [[Agricultural Terminal]] and accept their request for additional floral samples.

### Additional Floral Samples

The mission will ask the player to gather 50 units of a specific farming good: [[Solanium]], [[Frost Crystal]], [[Gamma Root]], [[Fungal Mould]], [[Cactus Flesh]], [[Star Bulb]], [[Faecium]] or [[Mordite]]. Though these can easily be obtained from the farms which have likely already been set up by this point, the player may instead elect to obtain the required resources via other means if they do not already have them on hand.

Once a sufficient quantity is in their possession, the player must return to the Farmer and hand over the goods in order to claim the mission's reward, a random Delicate Flora which was one of their experiments. If the player does not already know the [[blueprint]], they will also learn how to create NipNip seeds.

## Rewards

- A [[Delicate Flora (FARM)]] or [[Delicate Flora (PLNT)]] of random rarity
- [[NipNip]] blueprint (only if it has not yet been learned)
