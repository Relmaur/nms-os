# In Stellar Multitudes

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Added in:** Update 5.50

**In Stellar Multitudes** is a mission that starts at the end of the main storyline.

## Mission Requirements

In order to start mission, you need to:
- Complete [[They Who Returned]] [[storyline]] mission.
- Complete [[The Atlas Path]] storyline mission.

Engage your starship's pulse drive. The quest will start automatically after engaging pulse drive.

## Walkthrough

### Divergence Fragment

Before the mission is triggered, the starship's pulse engine will deactivate and a [[Divergence Fragment]] [[Space Encounter]] will appear in front of them. The fragment will attempt to communicate with you through the starship communicator.
When you open the communicator, the fragment will say hello to you and asks if you are a "Wearer-Entity" or a "Worn-Entity". After further conversation, ending the communications starts the mission.

### Memories of Atlantid

The mission will prompt you to enter a system with dissonant worlds if you are not already in one, then prompts you to land on a dissonant world.
Once landed on a dissonant world, the player is prompted to scan for [[Echo Seed|Echo Seeds]] which can be done using the [[Analysis Visor]]'s Target Sweep mode.
When reaching a [[Sentience Echo]], activate a [[Scan Harmoniser]] or [[Polyphonic Core]] to enable mining.

Once 3 [[Echo Seed|Echo Seeds]] have been gathered, the player is prompted to compile [[Memories Of Atlantid]]. After compiling, the player needs to activate the pulse drive to scan for the [[Divergence Fragment]]'s wavelength.
When the Divergence Fragment has been found, deactivate the pulse drive and open communications. After some dialogue, the fragment will leave and the player is prompted to find and enter an [[Atlas Interface]].

### The Scaffold

Once entering the [[Atlas Interface]], the player needs to sideload the [[Memories Of Atlantid]]. Once sideloading, various options are available:
- Payload α
  - When selecting Payload α, the following sub choices are available. When selecting one of these sub choices, you are given the further choice to manifest the memory or to select a different sub choice.
  - Witness memory: 'Gek'
  - Witness memory: 'Vy'keen'
  - Witness memory: 'Korvax'
  - Witness memory: 'Uncharted'
  - After selecting one of the above sub choices, the unselected options for sideloading are available again.
- Payload β
  - When selecting Payload β, the following sub choices are available. When selecting one of these sub choices, you are given the further choice to manifest the memory or to select a different sub choice.
  - Witness memory: 'Titan'
  - Witness memory: 'Flood'
  - Witness memory: 'Paradise'
  - After selecting one of the above sub choices, the unselected options for sideloading are available again.
- Payload γ
  - When selecting Payload γ, the following sub choices are available. When selecting one of these sub choices, you are given the further choice to manifest the memory or to select a different sub choice.
  - Witness memory: 'Storm'
  - Witness memory: 'Horror'
  - Witness memory: 'Hunted'
  - After selecting one of the above sub choices, the unselected options for sideloading are available again.
Once all payloads have been selected, you are allowed to examine the properties you've selected. You are further allowed to edit one of your previous choices above or lock in your choices, creating a new [[Star system]].<br>
Payload α chooses the primary lifeform of the system: Gek, Vy'keen, Korvax or Uncharted.<br>
Payload β chooses the properties of the primary celestial body: Gas giant, Water world or Paradise planet.<br>
Payload γ chooses the properties of the secondary celestial body: Extreme weather, Nest of horrors or Overrun by Sentinels.<br>
When locking in their choices, [[The Atlas]] then propagates a timeline to the local scaffold matrix, leading to a [[System colours|Purple coloured]] [[Star system]] being restored from deletion.

### A New Star

The [[Divergence Fragment]] appears in front of the [[Atlas Interface]] upon leaving to tell the player about a shift in the stars, that being the [[System colours|purple coloured]] [[Star system]]. They are prompted to open the [[Galactic Map]] where the player sees the [[Star system]] they have restored. Once leaving the [[Galactic Map]], the dialogue continues, with the Divergence Fragment going to follow you to the new [[Star system]]. Unfortunately, the player isn't able to go to the [[Star system]] at that moment, due to the lack of an [[Atlantid Drive]]. After more dialogue, the [[Divergence Fragment]] leaves and you are prompted to travel to the [[Space Anomaly]] and talk to the [[Assembled Construct]].

After some dialogue, the [[Assembled Construct]] will give you the blueprints for the [[Atlantid Drive]], followed by more dialogue.

The player now needs to make an [[Atlantid Drive]] to continue, at which point the player is prompted to travel to the restored system.

Once reaching the [[System colours|Purple coloured]] [[Star system]], the player must summon the [[Space Anomaly]] and notify the [[Assembled Construct]] of their safe arrival.

After more dialogue with the [[Assembled Construct]], the player then needs to talk with [[Specialist Polo]] and [[Priest Entity Nada]] about allowing the [[Autophage]] into the system, including the [[Divergence Fragment]], the player receives the [[Reality Anchor]] blueprint.

### Anchoring Reality

Once the player constructs the [[Reality Anchor]], they must go to the [[Space Anomaly]] and show the constructed [[Reality Anchor]] to [[Specialist Polo]] and [[Priest Entity Nada]].

After showing the [[Reality Anchor]] to [[Specialist Polo]] and [[Priest Entity Nada]], you'll need to go to the [[Korvax Simulation Terminal]] to bind the restored system to reality. Due to a failure, dialogue continues and you receive the blueprint for the [[Anomalous Scaffold]] in order to fix it.

After crafting the [[Anomalous Scaffold]], the player is prompted to go to the [[Space Anomaly]] and continue from where they left off previously.
Once done, you'll need to leave the [[Space Anomaly]] to tell the [[Divergence Fragment]] about the newly anchored system. This prompts the player to open the [[Galactic Map]], where every deleted [[Star system]] has been restored. When leaving the [[Galactic Map]], the dialogue continues and you get to say goodbye to the [[Divergence Fragment]] as they explore the many [[System colours|purple coloured]] [[star system|star systems]] in the galaxy.

## Additional Information

- It isn't possible to invite other players to visit the [[System colours|Purple coloured]] [[Star system]] that appears during the course of the mission until they've finished it themselves, unless that same system also appeared for them in the mission.

## Reward

- [[Atlantid Drive]] Blueprint
- [[Reality Anchor]] Blueprint
- [[Anomalous Scaffold]] Blueprint
- Access to [[System colours|purple coloured]] [[star system|star systems]].
- The "Manifestation of" title.
- A new [[System colours|purple coloured]] [[Star system]] with properties of your choosing.
