# Daily Research: Trade / Collection (variant 2)

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Series:** Daily Research
- **Requires:** Community Research (mission 9)
- **Added in:** Visions 1.77
- **Description:** Gather rare crystals for the Space Anomaly

**Daily Research: Trade / Collection** is a Daily Research mission.

## Summary

**Daily Research: Trade / Collection** is a [[Daily Research]] mission introduced as part of the [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help the research of [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Walkthrough (Community Research (mission 9)

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

This part is a continuation of the Community Research (mission 9) mission, but specific to this mission.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**
Input data to my storage banks, and I will synthesise reward output across all realities. A range of data is of utility to the Space Anomaly. Today, do you wish to contribute to TRADE / COLLECTION, EXPLORATION or DESTRUCTION?
- Select 'Trade / Collection'
Thank you! You have selected TRADE / COLLECTION.<br>
Today's directive is to collect rare storm crystals from planets with extreme climates. Scans of this natural phenomenon will greatly augment Master Polo's geological archives.
<br><br>
After this conversation select the Daily Research in the **Log** to view mission objectives.

## Walkthrough

### Gather storm crystals

Navigate to a [[planet]] with extreme [[weather]] conditions and gather [[Storm Crystal|Storm Crystals]].

During storms these can be located with the [[Analysis Visor]] by pressing // (PS4/PC/Xbox) and looking for the  icon.

After collecting three pieces of Storm Crystals the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, or after using the starship's scanner.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**
Greetings! I am pleased to see your safe return, Traveller!<br>
Did you successfully retrieve the requested crystal formations?
- Transmit
.scanning storm crystals...................<br>
.generating Quicksilver output...................<br>
Sincerest thanks, Traveller! I am accessing the Space Anomaly's hyperdrive. You are most welcome to dock and browse the database of exotic collectables.
<br><br>
After this conversation the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 250 Quicksilver for mission completion.
