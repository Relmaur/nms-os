# Under a Rebel Star

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Given by:** Space Encounter
- **Added in:** Outlaws
- **Description:** Against the red machine

**Under a Rebel Star** is a mission page.

## Summary

**Under a Rebel Star** is a mission introduced with [[Update 3.85]]. It leads players through the steps to introduce outlaws and receive **Shroud of Freedom** hood.

## Mission Steps

In order to trigger the mission's start, the player needs to have a [[Space Encounter]] with an [[Abandoned Starship]].

## Walkthrough

### Distress Beacon

In order to trigger the mission, use the [[Pulse Engine]] until you receive an emergency transmission. When received, disabling the Pulse Engine will cause an abandoned [[Solar Ship]] Space Encounter to appear in front of your starship. The ship will attempt to communicate with you. Opening the communicator begins the mission.

You will automatically receive coordinates located in a neighbouring [[star system]]. The coordinates are approximate and can only be located using the target sweep. Once located, the coordinates will lead you to a [[Crashed Ship]] with a [[Distress Beacon]]. Interacting with the beacon will cause a swarm of [[Corrupted Sentinel|Corrupted Sentinels]] to intercept you. These Sentinels must be destroyed before you can receive a [[Rendezvous Beacon]].

### The Promised Star

When the swarm of Corrupted Sentinels is defeated, you will receive a [[consumable]]: the Rendezvous Beacon. Activating the beacon is required to proceed to the next step of the mission. Once the beacon is activated, you will receive coordinates to a [[Derelict Freighter - Space Encounter|Derelict Freighter]] in space.

When you reach it, you will be attacked by several [[pirate|pirates]] guarding the freighter. You must defeat them to receive an invitation. When the pirates are defeated, you will receive a message directing you to an [[observatory]], which is the source of the invitation. Again, the coordinates are approximate and can only be located using the target sweep. Once located, enter the structure and interact with its [[Terminal]] to receive a [[Ticket to Freedom]], also a consumable.

Accepting the invitation (by activating the ticket) will take you back to a neighbouring star system. The system is controlled by pirates. When you enter the system, coordinates will lead you to an Outlaw Station. Once you have docked at this [[space station]], you will need to talk to several NPCs on the station. Most of the NPCs will tell you that Freedom violates the original rule (although they prefer Freedom).

Talk to them until they give you the Shroud of Freedom. This time, return to your spaceship to begin your departure. Before you can do so, you will be contacted by the Subverted Station Core, which will tell you that [[Atlas]] does not allow pirates to be free, and will give you a [[Forged Passport]].

You must take the passport to the marked coordinates in a neighbouring star system. Once in the system, dock with the space station and bring it to the [[Station Core]]. It will begin a traffic scan of the pirate-controlled system you visited. You have two choices: Disrupt the traffic scan or report the VoF. Either choice will mark the mission as completed and make it disappear from the log, except that you need to Disrupt the traffic scan to cover your tracks instead of reporting your tracks (most races have chosen to Disrupt the traffic scan). You'll also receive the "Reached the Promised Star" title.

## Bug

As of [[Update 6.06]], if the player use the Forged Passport in a different system than the marked one, they will still need to visit the marked system. Once they reach the system, they will receive the "Reached the Promised Star" title and the mission will disappear from the log without any story conclusion to the "Under a Rebel Star" arc. Using the passport in a different system doesn't trigger story-related lines.

## Additional Information

- If you choose to denounce the VoF, the Core will automatically accept your confession and report it to the local [[Sentinel|Sentinels]] to deal with the pirate-controlled system. If you launch your ship and make a Pulse Jump, you will encounter a ship (called The Choir of Freedom) that is calling pirates to attack you because you have denounced them.
