**Ghosts in the Machine** is a primary mission.

## Summary

**Ghosts in the Machine** is the third story mission of the [[Artemis Path]], starting immediately upon the conclusion of [[Alone Amidst the Stars]]. The mission tasks the player with contacting [[Apollo]] and working with them to figure out a way to reach [[Artemis]].

- Locate and activate a Holo-Terminus (objective associated with speaking to Apollo)
- Locate Apollo's contact
- Reach your base
- Expand and staff your new home
- Return to space and contact Apollo
- Install Apollo's Personal Forcefield
- Collect Nanites to buy Technology Upgrades
- Visit a multitool technology trader in a Vy'keen system
- Return to space and contact Apollo (objective associated with mysterious transmission --- see Walkthrough)
- Return to space and contact Apollo
- Attract Sentinel attention by infiltrating a Korvax facility

## Walkthrough

### First Contact

*Something terrible has happened to Artemis, their signal cut off as they were surrounded by strange figures they described as having 'seeds of glass' within their skin. Before they went, they managed to transmit the frequency for someone named 'Apollo'. I do not know if this stranger will prove friend or foe.*
First, the player must use their [[Starship]]'s scanner to locate a [[Holo-Terminus]], then use it to reach out to the lifeform called Apollo. During the conversation, the player will need to give Apollo their data logs, then agree to work with them to uncover the connections between the [[Portal]] system and [[Sentinel]] appearances.

### Advanced Base Building

*Apollo has suggested that the Sentinels may be linked to Artemis' disappearance. They suggest we cooperate. If the Sentinels use the portal network, then if we managed to follow them inside, who knows what secrets we might unlock on our way to rescuing Artemis...*
The conversation will end with Apollo referring the player to a lifeform in another star system's [[Space Station]]. The player must visit this lifeform and claim an environmental protection [[Upgrade Module]], then travel to one of their existing bases (or create a new base using another [[Base Computer]]).

The player should be aware that [[Space Fleets]] will begin to spawn in all inhabited [[Star system|Star systems]] upon initial arrival in the system containing the lifeform; as well, a [[Space battle]] will trigger in that system, allowing the player to obtain their first [[freighter]] for free and begin the mission [[Freighter Command]].

Once at a base, the player will be tasked with preparing for the dangers ahead by expanding their base and hiring staff. The lifeform that granted the player an Upgrade Module also gave them 15 [[Salvaged Data]] as well as the blueprints for the [[Cylindrical Room]], [[Door]] and [[Deepwater Chamber]] structures at that time; in addition, the player will have learned the blueprint for the [[Construction Terminal]] upon arriving at their base, starting the mission [[Expanding the Base]]. To advance Ghosts in the Machine, the player will need to progress Expanding the Base far enough to obtain the blueprint for the [[Science Terminal]], then work through [[Scientific Research]] until they receive blueprints for [[Lubricant]] and [[Acid]] from the now-hired [[Scientist]].

Once the player has built the two terminals and hired an [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]] and Scientist to staff them, they must return to space and make contact with Apollo to receive a blueprint for a [[Personal Forcefield]].

### Preparations

*Apollo has asked me to upgrade my equipment by obtaining blueprints from a multitool technology trader. It is dangerous work, hunting Sentinels. I need to be prepared for anything...*
The player must install a Personal Forcefield in their [[Multi-tool]] if it is not already present (a trade-in for a superior multi-tool may be required if there is not enough room for the technology in their current multi-tool). Apollo will then task the player with obtaining [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]] from an [[Abandoned Building]]'s terminal, then exchanging 60 nanites for a multi-tool upgrade module from the marked merchant in the local [[Space station]] (though the mission objective mentions a [[Vy'keen]] system, this part can actually be accomplished in a [[Korvax]] or [[Gek]] system as well). Once the module has been acquired, the player must return to space once more to converse with Apollo.

However, before Apollo has a chance to contact the player, the player will receive a transmission from a mysterious entity which claims to know all about the player's activity --- as it ends, the player will discover that the entity's name is [[-null-]] (that is, their name is missing).

### On the Offense

*Apollo wants to study the Sentinels. They hope to draw the drone's attention by attacking a Korvax facility. I appear to be the bait in this 'trap'...*
Immediately after the mysterious transmission, the player must answer Apollo's transmission and indicate their readiness for whatever Apollo has planned next. They will direct the player to break into a [[Korvax]] [[Manufacturing Facility]], not only to obtain any potential data from its terminal, but also to force Sentinel appearances so Apollo can figure out how they move around.

As with normal [[Manufacturing Facilities]], the player will need to destroy the reinforcement on the door with a dedicated multi-tool weapon in order to gain access. (If the player has no boltcaster installed and uses an exocraft mining beam instead, the quest will not advance)

The facility's terminal will need to be repaired with 20 [[Sodium]] ([[Security Alarm]]), 8 [[Chromatic Metal]] ([[Output Screen|Output screen]]), and 50 [[Pure Ferrite]] ([[Boiler Unit]]) before it can be operated by the player. Once it is operational, the player will be given blueprints for the [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]]. Players that already have the blueprints will instead get [[Nanite Cluster]]. Afterward, they will encounter a hairy creature which is doomed to die regardless of what happens. The player can choose to feed the creature (requires 15 [[Carbon]]), euthanise the creature or do nothing; the mission will conclude after this decision point, triggering a 3-star [[Wanted level]] and beginning [[A Leap in the Dark]].

## Rewards

- Access to [[Space Fleets]], [[Space battle|Space battles]] and [[freighter|freighters]]
- [[Exosuit]] Environmental Protection [[Upgrade Module]]
- 15 [[Salvaged Data]]
- Blueprints for [[Cylindrical Room]], [[Door]], [[Deepwater Chamber]] and [[Construction Terminal]]
- Access to advanced [[Base building]] missions
- [[Personal Forcefield]] blueprint (if not already known)
- [[Multi-tool]] Upgrade Module
- 135+ [[Nanite Cluster|Nanite Clusters]]

## Release history

- [[Atlas Rises]] - Added to the game as a primary mission.
- [[Beyond]] - Multiple changes to mission sections leading up to the facility raid
  - Warping to the system containing Apollo's contact now triggers space fleet spawns
    - Formerly, these spawns were triggered much earlier, near the end of the mission [[Awakenings]]
  - Apollo's contact now grants 15 Salvaged Data instead of the [[Paving]] blueprint
  - Construction Terminal blueprint is now learned only through this mission
    - Formerly, it was taught by building a Cylindrical Room for the first time
  - Added Personal Forcefield blueprint reward and installation objective
  - Players are now required to visit an Abandoned Building exactly once
    - Formerly, possessing 60+ nanites was the only requirement to complete the nanite collection objective
- [[Update 2.09|Beyond 2.09]] - *Fixed an issue where players were asked to collect nanites from an unusable terminal during the Ghost in the Machine  mission.*
