**Community Research (week 3)** is a mission.

## Summary

**Community Research (week 3)** is the third mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo]] determine how the universe changed recently.

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.60|NEXT 1.60]] until 27 September 2018.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

## Walkthrough

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

With this mission set as the active mission, fly into space and Polo will contact the player through the communicator.

**Specialist Polo**

--<br>
--
- Accept
--

### Visit the marked planet

A portal will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

 The portal yawns before me.
 I inhale deeply, awed by the imposing structure. The destination for Polo's request is at my fingertips. Are there others treading this interstellar path with me, I wonder?
- Input Polo's Glyphs
- Leave

### Reach the marked facility

After arriving to the other side of the portal, the mission objective will be updated showing the  icon for a [[Manufacturing Facility]].

It is locked by a reinforced steel door that the player will have to break through, which will alarm two [[Sentinel Drone|Sentinel Drones]] when attempted.

After successfully entering the building, a terminal can be found that requires the player's attention. Three [[Damaged Component|Damaged Components]] need to be repaired before it can be fully accessed.

 The terminal interface lists several databases related to Sentinels: manufacturing information as well as behavioural logs. The data is encrypted, but I can see an option to download for backup.
 Something prickles, tingles, at the base of my skull. A sense of impending danger as the crimson alarm light sweeps across the room...
- Download Sentinel Data
- Leave

### Destroy Advanced Sentinels

After the information is downloaded, the mission objective will be updated.

[[Sentinel Quad]] is considered as an Advanced Sentinel, which requires Security Level 3 to appear.

When one is destroyed, the mission will be updated again, head to space for contacting Polo.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, after using the [[starship]]'s scanner.

**Specialist Polo**

--
- Transmit data

 Polo reviews the logs, their holographic image nodding seriously. They tell me they have transferred a gift to my accounts, a small token for the risks I take on their behalf.
 They seem reluctant to ask, but hesitantly they let me know there is still work to be done.

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Destroy more Sentinels, anywhere

Return to space and contact Polo again by using the starship's scanner.

The broadcast is amplified and the research zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to kill Sentinels on any planets.

- 2,000  Quicksilver after killing the first Sentinel Quad.
