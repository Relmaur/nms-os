# Lost in Space

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Given by:** Derelict Freighter

**Lost in Space** is a secondary mission.

## Summary

**Lost in Space** is a secondary mission which starts after locating a [[Derelict Freighter]].

## Walkthrough

### Starting the Mission

In order to trigger the mission, the player must do one of the following steps:
  1. Buy an [[Emergency Signal Scanner]] from the [[Scrap Dealer]] for 5-30m  Units or get a free one from [[Iteration: Helios]] once per week.
  1. Engage your starship's [[Pulse Engine]] and wait ~10s for the space encounter to trigger.
- If you don't have an Emergency Signal Scanner or not enough money to buy one, you can pulse until a Derelict Freighter is located randomly. Note that the chance for this encounter to happen randomly is very small. [[Recurring Space Encounter]] will make it faster.

### Explore the Derelict Freighter

#### Airlock Control Unit

It will supply up to four [[Landing Pad|Landing Pads]] to land on. However, the game doesn't save when you leave your ship. Also, unlike other [[freighter|freighters]], [[Life Support]] and [[Hazard Protection|cold protection]] still drain.

You need to interact with the Docking Bay Door Control to unlock the door. Before unlocking the door, you can collect emergency supplies from the terminal. You cannot interact with the terminal again after unsealing the door. Emergency supplies include 4000-6000 [[Projectile Ammunition]], x3 [[Ion Battery]] and x3 [[Life Support Gel]].

#### The Records Terminal

On your journey to explore the Derelict Freighter, head to the [[Records Terminal]]. The terminal will provide the infomation about the Derelict Freighter and some crew onboard this Freighter. Access the terminal will provide that the
terminal is searching infomation about its mission destination, company charter detail and the [[Crew Manifest]]. Note that only Crew Manifest is accessible. When access the manifest, it will shows:

- Crew status as of last ship-wide biological scan
- Name of commanding officer and duty officers
- Crew status report data
- Supernumeraries infomation

After showed these infomation, you need to update a biological scan. The terminal will shows that it fail to update because there's no detected biological trace matches crew records. After that, it will automatically retrieving last know crew positions. The known positions can change depending on what Freighter you are in. After that, you will receive a [[Crew Manifest]].

#### The Secure Mainframe

After the Record Terminal, head to the [[Secure Mainframe]]. It will indicate that the biological scan detected a severe incident status and request you to indicate a Captain's fate: unknown, deceased and incapacitated. No matter what option you choice, it will accept your input. It request you to give it a [[Security Credentials]] and when you do so, it will allow you log in as a random crew (please note that you only choose one crew). After choose a crew, you can read the log and you will be given a [[Captain's Log]].

#### The Last Terminal: Engineering Control

After the Secure Mainframe, you need to interact with the [[Engineering Control]]. At this terminal, you need to fabricate [[Salvaged Fleet Unit|Salvaged Freighter Upgrade]], [[Cargo Bulkhead]] or extract  Nanites. The mission objective will switch when one of these 3 item is fabricated.

### The Buyer of the Salvage

The objective will updated and you need to travel to the [[Space Station]] and interact with the [[Scrap Dealer]] in order to exchange Crew Manifest for [[Tainted Metal]]. After that, you need to offer Captain's Log to the [[Guild Envoy]] for standing in which race in the system you are in.

## Additional Information

- This mission can be triggered again after complete this mission by encounter the Derelict Freighter again.

- Interacting with the Door Control and collecting emergency supplies when this mission is re-triggered will result in the Door Control reporting the same message as the initial message.

- The log of the Secure Mainframe can be change depending on the owner of the Freighter.

- Alternatively, you can use [[Anomaly Detector]] to reveal the Freighter instead of buying Emergency Signal Scanner, although it take a sightly long time to locate this Freighter when it locating other [[Space Encounter]].
- You can get a free Emergency Signal Scanner from [[Iteration: Helios]] every week if the mission has been completed once.
