# The Settlers

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Added in:** Frontiers
- **Description:** Defend the Planetary Settlement from Sentinel and become settlement's overseer

**The Settlers** is a secondary mission.

## Summary

**The Settlers** is a secondary mission introduced in the [[Update 3.60|Frontiers]] update.

## Walkthrough

### The Emergency Broadcast

The player will receive an Emergency Broadcast from an alien race in the [[Star system|system]] they are in, this can be answered from the [[starship]] communicator. After the emergency broadcast ends, the player will receive a set of coordinates, that lead to a Planetary Settlement.

### The Settlement's Survivor

The player needs to talk with the entity that sent an Emergency Broadcast to them. Turns out, the settlement has a debt and they are calling you to say that the Settlement was attacked by [[Corrupted Sentinel Drone|Corrupted Sentinel Drones]]. After the dialogue has ended, a corrupted swarm will come to attack the settlement. Your duty is to defeat seven Corrupted Drones. When all of the corrupted drones are defeated, speak to a citizen one more time in order to receive their thanks.

After that, go to the Settlement Interface to become this settlement's overseer. Then, build the Overseer's Office.

As of the [[Update 5.70|Beacons]] update, all buildings begin at C level which decrease happiness and increase debt because production is less than upkeep.

## Additional Information

- As of the [[Update 5.70|Beacons]] update, the player can retire as overseer at the Settlement Interface.
