# A Trace of Metal

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Given by:** Planetary Settlement
- **Added in:** Waypoint
- **Description:** Create sentinel companion and upgrade the Minotaur

**A Trace of Metal** is a mission page.

## Summary

The **A Trace of Metal** is a mission introduced with [[Update 3.80]] for players who own a [[Planetary Settlement]], and with [[Update 4.10|Update 4.1.0]] for Nintendo Switch. It leads players through the steps to create their own [[Sentinel]] companion and upgrade the [[Minotaur]] with Sentinel Technology.

## Mission Steps

Except on Nintendo Switch, in order to trigger the mission's start, the player must meet the following requirements:

- Own a Planetary Settlement.
- 'The Settlers' entry in the mission log says the player is 'free to explore', i.e. there are no outstanding settlement mission objectives or resources to collect.
- The player is not in the same system as their settlement. If the mission has not triggered, despite the above requirements being met, the player should use their ship's hyperdrive to warp to nearby systems twice and the mission will start.
On Nintendo Switch, the mission triggers in the middle of [[The First Traveller]] or after the player has warped 25 times, whichever comes first.

## Drone Reconstruction

Except on Nintendo Switch, a player receives a message from their Planetary Settlement informing them of an extreme Sentinel assault. They should return to their Settlement and defeat a significant Sentinel force including [[Sentinel Hardframe Battle Mech]]. After this, they will have communication with [[Iteration: Tethys]] who will ask to find something interesting on the battlefield. Searching the battlefield debris, the player discovers an **Undamaged Drone Shell** which they have to bring to Tethys for an inspection.

On Nintendo Switch, the player receives a communication from Iteration: Tethys asking them to check out unusual sentinel activity at a particular location. Sentinels immediately appear when the player lands at the location. After defeating the sentinels, inspecting the nearby traveler grave yields the Undamaged Drone Shell.

On the [[Space Anomaly]] Tethys asks the player to bring a few more components from other [[Traveller|Travellers]] inhabiting the Anomaly. After a few dialogs the player returns to Tethys with three items: **Portal Phylactery**, **Nanite Filament**, and **Beads of Glass**. With these components, Tethys reconstructs the drone, gives the player a **Sentinel Flare** for summoning a reconstructed drone and asks the player to do a field test at their Settlement.

## Hardframe Right Arm

After interacting with the Reconstructed Drone and restarting it, coordinates to a nearby [[Sentinel Pillar]] are received. After unlocking the **Sentinel Pillar Access Node** by destroying three **Pillar Control Nodes** and defeating sentinels guarding the pillar, the player will receive the [[Hardframe Right Arm]] blueprint. They need to install it on their Minotaur, and then return to their Settlement to speak to its citizens.

## Hardframe Legs

Settlement citizens express their concern with recent events and offer help by organising an expedition to either an [[Abandoned Building]] or [[Monolith]] in search of answers. The Sentry gives the player new coordinates leading to a [[Manufacturing Facility]] in the nearby [[star system]]. After breaking into the facility, the player will interact with the terminal, at which point the [[Exosuit]] takes over and extracts the [[Hardframe Legs]] blueprint and a Sentry upgrade packet. The player will have to install this blueprint as well.

## Hardframe Left Arm

The citizen expedition returns and has to be debriefed. Debrief will result in two choices:
- Release the explorers - adds feature "Creeping, Spreading Glass" (Raises Productivity)
- Quarantine the explorers - adds feature "Quarantined explorers" (Reduces Sentinel Alert Level by 4%)

A blueprint for [[Hardframe Left Arm]] is given and has to be installed.

## Hardframe Body

The Sentry gives itself a name: [[Laylaps]], gives the player a [[Paralysis Mortar]] blueprint, and coordinates leading the player to a [[Plaque]] on another planet. There the player needs to destroy a [[Sentinel Walker]] to recover a **Pristine Brain** needed to finish Minotaur transformation, and a [[Hardframe Body]] blueprint. This brain has to be presented to Tethys who will modify the brain and ask the player to contact [[Messenger of Atlas]] to finish the transformation into a [[Radiant Brain]]. Laylaps will then direct the player to another Sentinel Pillar where they will disconnect the Sentry from the sentinel network.
