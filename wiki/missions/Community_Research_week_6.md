## Summary

**Community Research (week 6)** is the sixth mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to learn the fate of [[Specialist Polo]]'s missing friend.

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.65|NEXT 1.65]] until 26 October 2018. The first part of the mission takes place on a planet called Rikasu XIII in the Lusalkai system.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

### Visit the marked planet

A portal will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Take photographs for Polo to analyse

The broadcast is amplified and the signal zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to take photographs of buildings on any planets. The player could visit other planets, but it can be done on the initial planet.

The [[Signal Booster]] can be used to reveal some of the required buildings, but with not exact results.

The following images are needed for completion, the [[planetary coordinates]] are given for Rikasu XIII.
- [[Monolith]] →  -35.56, -99.77
- [[Minor Settlement]] → -37.07, -97.71
- Abandoned Building → -39.62, -101.99
- [[Trading Post]] → -34.68, -101.80
- Portal → -35.78, -101.80
- [[Holographic Comms Tower]] (HoloTerminus) → -35.46, -103.57

If you turn in partial results, the following conversation takes place.

**Specialist Polo**
--
- Transmit images
 Thank you for this data, friend. Data always brings hope!
- Decline

After taking all 6 images, the data limit is reached and the mission objective will be updated.

Return to space and contact Polo again by using the starship's scanner.

**Specialist Polo**
--
- Transmit data
--

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The Space Anomaly will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

After speaking with the merchant NPC again, the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 3,000  Quicksilver after transmitting all 6 images.
