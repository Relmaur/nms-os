# Replacement Antimatter

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Requires:** Inability to warp after receiving Antimatter in Awakenings
- **Description:** A failsafe mission designed to get the player back on track during Awakenings

**Replacement Antimatter** is a secondary mission.

## Summary

**Replacement Antimatter** is a secondary mission which triggers if the game detects that the player is stuck in their starting [[star system]] in [[Awakenings]] due to a mistake. The mission tasks the player with finding another source of [[Antimatter]] so that they can fuel their [[starship]]'s [[Hyperdrive]] and leave their starting system.

## Mission Triggers

Replacement Antimatter will trigger if either of the following two conditions is fulfilled:
1. After receiving their first Antimatter from an [[Abandoned Building]], the player loses the Antimatter before they can craft it into a [[Warp Cell]].
  - *Possible Causes:* [[Death]] (Normal/Survival [[Game mode|Game modes]]); item destruction; [[repair]] of a [[Drop Pod]]; [[crafting]] of a [[Shield Lattice]], [[Teleport Receiver]], [[Barrel Fabricator]] or [[Crate Fabricator]]
1. After fueling their starship's Hyperdrive, the player somehow ends up without the fuel required to warp between systems.
  - *Possible Causes:* Hyperdrive was [[dismantle|dismantled]] after being fueled; player switched to a starship without a fueled Hyperdrive

## Initial Description

I received antimatter from a mysterious source. In my haste to reach the stars, I failed in my attempt to use the antimatter to fuel my hyperdrive. I need to acquire more if I'm to leave this star system.

## Walkthrough

The player must scan their surroundings, then travel to the marked lifeform and interact with it. After a brief conversation, the lifeform will give the player a replacement unit of Antimatter, concluding the mission.

## Rewards

- Antimatter (for use as Hyperdrive fuel)
