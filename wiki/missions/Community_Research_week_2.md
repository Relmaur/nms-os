**Community Research (week 2)** is a mission.

## Summary

**Community Research (week 2)** is the second mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo]] determine how the universe changed recently.

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.59|NEXT 1.59]] until 13 September 2018.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

## Walkthrough

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

With this mission set as the active mission, fly into space and Polo will contact the player through the communicator.

### Visit the marked planet

A [[portal]] will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

 The portal yawns before me.
 I inhale deeply, awed by the imposing structure. The destination for Polo's request is at my fingertips. Are there others treading this interstellar path with me, I wonder?
- Input Polo's Glyphs
- Leave

### Feed Creatures and detect Anomalies

After arriving to the other side of the portal, the mission objective will be updated showing the number of fed [[creature|creatures]].

The player can easily spot them by using their [[Analysis Visor]]. Feeding requires various resources, the type is indicated upon interacting with the creature.

After a successful feeding, the creature stays near the player and rewards them multiple times with its unique faeces called Coprite Crystal, which is indicated with  icon.

When these picked up, each yields 12-18 pcs of [[Hexite]]. After feeding 25 creatures the mission will be updated again, head to space for contacting Polo.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, after using the [[starship]]'s scanner.

**Specialist Polo**

--
- Transmit data

 Polo clicks their beak and gives a little sniff. They tell me they have transferred a gift to my accounts.
 And - they hasten to add - their research could always benefit from further samples!

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Feed more creatures, anywhere

Return to space and contact Polo again by using the starship's scanner.

The broadcast is amplified and the research zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to feed creatures on any planets.

**Specialist Polo**

- End transmission.

## Rewards

- 2,000  Quicksilver after feeding 25 creatures.
- 3,500  Quicksilver for feeding a total of 100 creatures, besides the initial 25.
- 45  Quicksilver for finishing the optional part of the mission.
