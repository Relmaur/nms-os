## Summary

**Community Research (week 8)** is the eighth mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is helping [[Specialist Polo]] in their research of the freshly-seeded [[flora|floras]] on the newly reformed [[biome|biomes]].

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 14 days, starting from [[Update 1.75|Visions 1.75]] until 6 December 2018. The first part of the mission takes place on a planet called Nitan III with [[Bone Spire]] exotic biome in the Hekhac-Kikar system.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

## Walkthrough

With this mission set as the active mission, fly into space and Specialist Polo will contact the player through the communicator.

### Visit the marked planet

A portal will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

 The portal yawns before me.
 I find I have no need to access my exosuit's memory banks: Polo's final glyph sequence is scorched bright in the forefront of my mind, commited to my own organic memory.
- Input Polo's Glyphs
- Leave

### Complete a botanical survey for Polo

After arriving to the other side of the portal, the mission objective will be updated, but the destination will not be indicated with an icon.

The player can use [[Analysis Visor]] to scan the local flora, and when seven discoveries are made, upload is not necessary, the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, or after using the [[starship]]'s scanner.

**Specialist Polo**

- Transmit data

--<br>
--
- End transmission

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Collect more flora scan data, anywhere

The broadcast is amplified and the signal zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to scan floras on any planets.

If you turn in partial results, the following conversation takes place.

**Specialist Polo**
--
- Transmit data
1. Polo thanks me repeatedly, transferring a gift to my accounts. They quickly assess the display of their tablet device, then confirm that the Synthesis Bot is still responding energetically to the accumulated data. More discoveries would power it further.
- End transmission
1. --

After scanning 200 floras, the data limit is reached and the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, or after using the starship's scanner.

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The Space Anomaly will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

After speaking with the merchant NPC again, the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 1,500  Quicksilver after transmitting the initial 7 flora scan data.
- 9,000  Quicksilver after transmitting all 200 optional flora scan data.

## Additional information

- Tier 1, tier 2 and tier 3 were completed during the mission for all platforms. The related rewards are available at Quicksilver Synthesis Companion.
