# They Who Returned

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Added in:** Update 4.40
- **Description:** Talk with Autophage robot and staff your Multi-Tool.

**They Who Returned** is a mission.

## Mission Requirements

In order to start mission, you need to:
- Complete [[The Purge]] [[storyline]] mission. If you have New Beginnings in your Log tab, you've done this part.
- Use an [[Echo Locator]] to locate a [[Harmonic Camp]], and lift the seal via the camp's terminal. This will also give you the Aeron Turbojet jetpack appearance. The camp must be found using an Echo Locator. If you already have the Aeron Turbojet (#5) jetpack appearance unlocked, you've done this part.

Guide, adapted and corrected from one posted on Reddit by [AmatsukiTsubaki](https://www.reddit.com/r/NoMansSkyTheGame/comments/1606oe1/comment/k28w1fc/):
1. Navigate to any "Dissonant" system, and then to a planet within the system with "Dissonance Detected".
1. Fight [[Dissonance Resonator|Dissonance Resonators]], the super tall pillar-like bots that are mining around the planet, until they drop an [[Echo Locator]]. (Tip: if you walk up close to the Resonator, it will show you if that one drops an Echo Locator or not.) Echo Locators can also be found as a random drop from Corrupted Sentinel Quadrupeds.
1. Use the [[Echo Locator]], which will identify the location of a [[Harmonic Camp]].
1. Navigate to the Harmonic Camp.  In the middle of the camp is a [[Harmonic Interface]] terminal, on which the player must solve a puzzle by first selecting "Scan memory registers" option to receive 3 simple arithmetic equations, and then input the runes that corresponds to the answers to those three equations, in any order, into the "Input override glyphs" option in the terminal.
1. Warp to another system in your starship (not your freighter).  If this warp triggers a freighter battle, the player will need to warp once more to receive the mission.
1. An interaction with the starship will automatically trigger after landing in the new system.  The answers to the conversation steps do not matter, the only possible outcome is receiving the mission.

The following are FALSE misconceptions often found in guides, but are not actually required to trigger the mission:
- The Echo Locator does not need to be used in a Korvax system.  It can be used in the same system it was acquired in, regardless of system race alignment.  None of the steps to trigger the mission require specifically Korvax star systems.
- The location of a sentinel ship does not need to be received from the Harmonic Camp terminal, nor does the player need to retrieve a sentinel ship prior to receiving the mission, nor do they need to be flying one to receive the mission.
- The camp's multi-tool cabinet does not need to be unlocked from the Harmonic Camp terminal.
- The system warped to in order to trigger the mission, after lifting the seal on the Harmonic camp, does not need to be either a Dissonant system or a Korvax system.  Any system will do.
- The player does not need to have completed [[The Settlers]] mission series (or otherwise have a [[Planetary Settlement]]), the [[A Trace of Metal]] mission, or the [[Atlas Path]] mission series.

## Walkthrough

### Meet the Autophage

When the mission is triggered upon warping into a star system, the player will receive a [[starship]] message which alerts the player to their starship's operating system having some error. The player is given the option to analyse an error in Block0, Block1 and Block2. All of the blocks will display several hexadecimal addresses, with one of the addresses in Block2 having an odd non-alphanumeric character in it.  Selecting Block2 will progress the conversation, but selecting one of the others does not cause any issues, it just indicates that the block selected does not have any errors before returning to the block selection.  The player should continue through the conversation (exact answers picked do not matter, there's no way to fail the conversation).  After several conversation sequences, a mechanical voice will interrupt the starship alert, saying that they believed they were hijacking a sentient starship, but were forced to abort the hijack due to the starship being controlled by a pilot. After the dialogue has ended, the player will receive a waypoint to the location the hijack signal was attempting to direct the starship's autopilot, which will lead the player to an Autophage inside a [[Harmonic Camp]].

After reaching it, the player will need to have a dialogue with a damaged Autophage. Unlike other aliens, it has its own unique [[language]] and its sounds are distorted. The player will have several sets of options for actions they can take.  Ultimately, the player is presented with 3 options for resources to give to the Autophage:
- [[Nanites]]
- [[Pugneum]]
- [[Atlantideum]]

Only Atlantideum will unlock the next step.

### Monolith

After the dialogue has ended, the player will receive coordinates to a nearby Korvax [[Monolith]]. The player will need to interact with a Discordant Interface found on the Monolith platform. It speaks the same language as the autophage and the player will need to offer a gift, and should once again offer Atlantideum. The player should then interact with a main Monolith pedestal, which will offer several options to explore Autophage lore.  After each option is selected, the player is prompted with the remaining options.  After all 3 options have been read, the player is granted knowledge of the Autophage words for the numbers 1-16, then directed to return to the original camp.

NOTE: Once this step is completed, the player can interact with the Discordant Interface on any Korvax Monolith and, provided they answered the Monolith's challenge properly, have the option to receive an [[Multi-Tool|Atlantid Multi-Tool]] from the Monolith.  This can be repeated with each Korvax Monolith located.

### Return to the Harmonic Camp

After the player returns to the camp, they must again interact with the damaged Autophage head.  The head's dialog will include three numbers.  When the player selects the option to touch the head, they are prompted to enter the runes corresponding to those same numbers, similar to the puzzle on the central terminal in the camp.

Entering the correct runes will unlock the robot's translation system, allowing it to communicate with the player.  The robot thanks the player and introduces itself. When confronted about the hijack attempt on the player's starship, the robot will apologize and explain further.  Ultimately, the player is granted the blueprint for a [[Scan Harmoniser]], an upgrade to their multitool's scanner.

Once installed, the player should press the scan key ((PC), (PS4), or (Xbox)) to activate Scan Harmoniser, which will decloak several nearby Autophage.  After speaking with each of them, the last one will gift the place some Atlantideum, and the player will be directed to the [[Space Anomaly|Nexus]] to continue.

### Tell Nada about the Autophage

Player will need to speak with [[Priest Entity Nada|Nada]] on board the [[Space Anomaly|Nexus]] about the Autophage.  The player informs Nada about the Harmonic Camp and the cloaked Autophage.  After a pause, Nada says that their information in insufficient to determine if the Autophage are familiar, and directs them to speak to a nearby [[Assembled Construct]], to which the player must present some Atlantideum.  When Atlantideum is shown to them, they will become slightly less coherent, and repeat VOID four times. The player can ask it about the Construct and the Autophage.  The Construct will grant the player the blueprint for a [[Polyphonic Core]], a replacement for the previously constructed Scan Harmoniser that also allows the player to locate Autophage gatherings.

The player is directed to speak with Nada once again, who will suggest two courses of action, giving the player two additional missions that act as parallel paths to continue:
- Prayers of the Machine
- Audience with the Autophage

Both sub-missions must be completed in order to continue with They Who Returned.

### Prayers of the Machine

In this mission, the player will present Atlantideum to a number of computerized systems and observe the results.  The player will be prompted to scan for and then present Atlantideum to 4 computers:

- The Terminal Prime in the [[Space Anomaly|Nexus]].
- The computer of a planetary manufacturing facility (being aware of the hostile sentinel response triggered by breaching the door).
- The Station Core of the system's space station.
- The computer of the player's personal [[starship]].

Each computer will reveal a small binary sequence. This translates to: *motherhome | she grows | she lives | she is near*

The starship will note that it has located a fragment of "New Atlantid".  The player is directed to enter space and activate their pulse drive to search for it.  After a short period of time in pulse drive, a special [[Space Encounter]] will trigger.  The player receives a starship communication, which includes logs stored in the fragment.  The final log references being saved by [[The Atlas]], and the player extracts some language data from the fragment.

### Audience with the Autophage

In this mission, the player has to interact with three Autophage in different systems to gain their favor.  They are first prompted to construct and install the [[Polyphonic Core]]. In each system visited, the player will needs to activate the Polyphonic Core to locate a camp. Once a camp is reached, they need to speak with the marked Autophage in the camp, using the scanner to decloak them. The Autophage will give the player a task to complete. When completed, the player will receive the blueprint for a component of a [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Voltaic Staff|Voltaic Staff]] as a reward.  They will need to speak with the Autophage again to discuss the staff component received, then construct the component in their inventory. When all three components are acquired and constructed, a Voltaic Staff need to be assembled at [[Autophage Synthesis Terminal]] in any Autophage camp to continue the questline. The Multi-tool class will be random, and a few slots will be damaged.

### Reach the hidden galaxy

The player will need to use their scanner to locate a [[Portal]]. They will then present Atlantideum to a portal, display their Voltaic Staff, and say the word "Atlas", in any order.  Once all 3 have been done, the portal will activate, teleporting the player into space, as in one of the sequences in the [[16 / 16]] story mission, before they are teleported to an [[Atlas Station]] after approximately 20 seconds.  The player's Exosuit life support will drain during this time, due to the cold of space, so the player should ensure their life support is topped up, and be ready to refill it if necessary during the short period in space.

The player should speak with the Atlas, and present Atlantideum to it.  This corrupts the Atlas with purple effects similar to the Atlantideum crystals and causes it to malfunction.  It reveals some backstory about the [[Korvax]] and Autophage races, and the player is given various options for how to respond. At the end of the dialogue, the player is teleported to a random planet, about 500u away from your ship, similar to other teleportations to Atlas stations, though without damage to the ship's launch thrusters this time.

### Completion

The player is prompted to leave the planet and consult Nada on the Nexus, giving them the data about the Atlas encounter and providing them a sample of Atlandideum, which appears to invoke an ancient memory in Nada. The player will receive a guide entry about the Autophage race and the mission they just completed.  In addition, the next time they engage their pulse drive, they'll have a [[Space Encounter]] that starts the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] mission.

## Additional Information

- In the Prayers of the Machine mission, when interacting with the Terminal Prime, the Operations Centre or the Factory, and the Station Core, the last message they say will always have a binary number, the following messages have been translated:
- **Terminal Prime**: "mother home"
- **Operations Centre**: "she grows"
- **Station Core**: "she lives"

Finally, the starship will send a message: "she is near", which is automatically translated.

- All blocks in the starship alert are the hexadecimal numbers that can be converted to normal text. However, these block translated are strings of non-printable and special characters that may not have any meaning in plain text. For example: 0xE832FA8EB48E in your starship alert's secondary block translated is: è2úëH.
