## Summary

**Community Research (week 1)** is the first mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo]] determine how the universe changed recently.

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.58|NEXT 1.58]] until 6 September 2018.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

## Walkthrough

With this mission active, you can use your [[Starship]] to go to the space. You will receive a communication from [[Specialist Polo]]. If answered, Polo will inform that the [[galaxy]] is roars, shifts, renewed and ask if you sense it too. After that, Polo will transmit a coordinate to a [[Portal]] which, when activated, will lead you to a planet. The Portal does not requires recharge.

After arrived at the planet, the mission objective will update, but will not give you any coordinates. You need to use your Analysis Visor to locate [[Buried Technology Module]], and dig it using [[Terrain Manipulator]]. Opening these modules will give you [[Hex Core]], and it will count towards the mission. You need collect x25 Hex Core to complete the objective. Once sufficient Hex Core are collected, you need to return to space to contact Polo which will inform that [[Priest Entity Nada]] have see your Starship signature first and will ask you to transport the core to the [[Space Anomaly]]. Ask how will say that they have deconstructed a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] to build a transporter. Accepting the request (which is the only choice rather than asking how) will cause the Space Anomaly to warp ahead your Starship.

You need to go to the Quicksilver Synthesis Companion to buy collectables. You can dig for more Hex Core then transport it to the Space Anomaly repeatedly.
