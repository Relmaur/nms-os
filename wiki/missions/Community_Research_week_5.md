## Summary

**Community Research (week 5)** is the fifth mission introduced as [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help [[Specialist Polo]] locate an old acquaintance of theirs.

This is a time-limited mission and it was available for 7 days, starting from [[Update 1.64|NEXT 1.64]] until 19 October 2018. The first part of the mission takes place on a planet called Elorence E28 in the Inkixig-Garac system.

WARNING: It makes use of [[portal]] travel. Please do not forget to go back through the portal when you finished, or you will encounter [[Portal Interference]] messages.

A portal will be marked in the system where the player currently located. Due to it is a mission-related travel, the [[Portal Terminal]] will not require any recharge.

### Visit Polo's robotic companion

The [[Space Anomaly]] will warp into the player's current system, allowing it to be boarded it.

The door behind the Control Panel is accessible, where [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] resides.

After speaking with the merchant NPC the mission objective will be updated.

### (Optional) Investigate more crashed starships

The broadcast is amplified and the signal zone extended throughout the galaxy, allowing to investigate crashed ships on any planets. The player could visit other planets, but it can be done on the initial planet.

The mission log suggests using the "Scan Distress Frequencies" option with a [[Navigation Data]] on the [[Signal Booster]] can reveal crashed ship locations.

However using the "Locate Nearby Structures" can also reveal locations with a [[Distress Beacon]], which also qualifies for the mission progress.
