# Daily Research: Exploration (variant 2)

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Series:** Daily Research
- **Requires:** Community Research (mission 9)
- **Added in:** Visions 1.77
- **Description:** Find undiscovered systems for the Space Anomaly

**Daily Research: Exploration** is a Daily Research mission.

## Summary

**Daily Research: Exploration** is a [[Daily Research]] mission introduced as part of the [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help the research of [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Walkthrough (Community Research (mission 9)

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

This part is a continuation of the Community Research (mission 9) mission, but specific to this mission.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**
Input data to my storage banks, and I will synthesise reward output across all realities. A range of data is of utility to the Space Anomaly. Today, do you wish to contribute to TRADE / COLLECTION, EXPLORATION or DESTRUCTION?
- Select 'Exploration'
Thank you! You have selected EXPLORATION.

Today's directive is to navigate to undiscovered solar systems. Overviews of the orbital makeup, conflict levels, and economic prosperity from an array of systems will greatly augment Master Polo's astronomical archives.

After this conversation select the Daily Research in the **Log** to view mission objectives.

## Walkthrough

### Warp to new systems

The player must warp to undiscovered [[star system|star systems]] with their [[starship]] or [[freighter]] by using the [[Galactic Map]], claiming them as [[discoveries]] is optional.

After visiting four star systems the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, or after using the starship's scanner.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**
Greetings! I am pleased to see your safe return, Traveller!<br>
Did you successfully collect the requested data from undiscovered systems?
- Transmit
.receiving discovery data...................<br>
.generating Quicksilver output...................<br>
Sincerest thanks, Traveller! I am accessing the Space Anomaly's hyperdrive. You are most welcome to dock and browse the database of exotic collectables.
<br><br>
After this conversation the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 250 Quicksilver for mission completion.
