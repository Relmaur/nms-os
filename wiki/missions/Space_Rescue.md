# Space Rescue

- **Type:** Secondary

**Space Rescue** is a secondary mission.

## Summary

**Space Rescue** is a secondary mission that involves rescue a [[freighter]] from [[pirate]] forces.

## Mission Description

### System conflict detected

Locate the distressed freighter and aid in its defense.<br>WARNING: Large numbers of enemy ships detected.

Use brake (S) to turn quickly and use Boost (L Shift) to evade. Change targets with , / . Your shield will regenerate if you avoid enemy fire for long enough.

## Starting the Mission

Player have two choice to start this mission:

1. Fly your starship for a while until a freighter's distress signal is revealed
1. Randomly warp your starship until a freighter under attack by pirates appears

## Walkthrough

Player must receive a distressed freighter coordinates and reach it, after that you need to destroy the pirate starship attacking it. You may see the freighter try to defend themselves by using a turret but it deals no damage (except for when the player flies through it). After defeating the pirate forces, you can own a freighter for free.
