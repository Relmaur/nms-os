# Collecting Fossils

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Given by:** Planetary
- **Requires:** Partial completion of Awakenings
- **Added in:** Relics
- **Description:** A mission focused on learning how to display fossilised remains. Travellers recover ancient bones to construct exhibits and create museum displays at their planetary base.

**Collecting Fossils** is a mission.

## Summary

**Collecting Fossils** is a secondary mission focused on the collection of ancient bones found across certain planets. Some worlds are rich in [[Fossil Bed|Fossil Beds]]. Travellers can exhume these remains and utilize [[Fossil Display|Fossil Displays]] to reassemble and exhibit long-lost creatures.

Fossils can be used to decorate the player's base as part of a personal museum.

## Acquisition

Collecting Fossils begins after discovering your first [[Ancient Bones]] on a planet. Use the [[Survey Device]] to scan for fossil sites. Excavating an ancient bone will trigger a Base Computer alert, initiating the mission.

## Objectives

- Learn a [[Fossil Display]] blueprint
  - Learn a Fossil Display blueprint to assemble [[Ancient Bones]] into an exhibit.
  - Visit the [[Construction Research Station]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].
  - In space, summon the Anomaly from the Quick Menu
- Gather more ancient bones. A complete fossil requires a skull, a ribcage, a tail, and limbs.
- Assemble and package a fossil exhibit
- Visit a Fossil Collector
