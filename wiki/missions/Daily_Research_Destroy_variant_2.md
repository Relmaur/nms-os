# Daily Research: Destroy (variant 2)

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Secondary
- **Series:** Daily Research
- **Requires:** Community Research (mission 9)
- **Added in:** Visions 1.77
- **Description:** Record destruction logs for the Space Anomaly

**Daily Research: Destroy** is a Daily Research mission.

## Summary

**Daily Research: Destroy** is a [[Daily Research]] mission introduced as part of the [[Community Research]] and its purpose is to help the research of [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]].

## Walkthrough (Community Research (mission 9)

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

This part is a continuation of the Community Research (mission 9) mission, but specific to this mission.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**

Input data to my storage banks, and I will synthesise reward output across all realities. A range of data is of utility to the Space Anomaly. Today, do you wish to contribute to TRADE / COLLECTION, EXPLORATION or DESTRUCTION?
- Select 'Exploration'
Thank you! You have selected DESTRUCTION.

Today's directive is to target and destroy resource depots. Data concerning the challenge of and sentinel response to this destructive process will greatly augment Master Polo's defensive and sentinel study archives.

After this conversation select the Daily Research in the **Log** to view mission objectives.

## Walkthrough

### Destroy a resource depot

Locate a [[Depot]] by using the [[exocraft]]'s [[Exocraft Signal Booster]]. The option to scan for it can be accessed through the [[Quick Menu]] by pressing  (PS4/PC/Xbox).

It can also be located with the [[Signal Booster]] by scanning for Secure Frequencies, but this option can provide locations for [[Manufacturing Facilities]] or [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]] as well.

After destroying one container with a [[Multi-tool]] weapon the mission objective will be updated.

### Make contact with the Space Anomaly

The player will be contacted again through the communicator, or after using the starship's scanner.

**Quicksilver Synthesis Bot**

Greetings! I am pleased to see your safe return, Traveller!<br>
Did you successfully record logs detailing the destruction of a resource depot?
- Transmit
.receiving destruction data...................<br>
.generating Quicksilver output...................<br>
Sincerest thanks, Traveller! I am accessing the Space Anomaly's hyperdrive. You are most welcome to dock and browse the database of exotic collectables.
<br><br>
After this conversation the mission will be completed.

## Rewards

- 250 Quicksilver for mission completion.
