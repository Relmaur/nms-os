# Factory Override Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Constructed Technology
- **Type:** Advanced Research Module
- **Value:** 50,000.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Factory Override Unit** is a Constructed Technology.

## Summary

**Factory Override Unit** is a [[Constructed Technology]] and one of the [[Constructed Technology|advanced research modules]].

## Game description

A portable data unit, compatible with most planetary fabricator facilities.

Once access has been gained to a central control terminal, the Override Unit can divert the facilities resources towards researching new product recipes.

## Source

There is a chance you get 1 or 2 if you successfully override a [[Manufacturing Facility]].

It can be gained as a reward from a mission from [[The Nexus]] or the Corvette Local Mission Radar board.

## Additional information

It is not recommended to sell these, as the crafted products researched with this technology can get you much more money.

As of [[Update 3.98|Endurance]], 44 Factory Override Units are required to learn all recipes; 13 for components and devices, and 31 for Valuable products.
