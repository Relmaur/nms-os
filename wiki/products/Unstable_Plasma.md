## Summary

**Unstable Plasma** is a [[consumable]] [[product]].

## Game description

Densely-packed fuel capsule for use in the [[Plasma Launcher]].

Contained highly-pressurised electro-thermal energy for use in [[Land Disruptor|land disruption]] grenade technology.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Oxygen]] + 1 × [[Metal Plating]] → 1 × Unstable Plasma

Also available from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

- **Refiner** · *Plasma Pressurisation* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Oxygen]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 20 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Unstable Plasma
- **Refiner** · *Plasma Pressurisation* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Oxygen]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 20 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Unstable Plasma
- **Refiner** · *Plasma Pressurisation* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Oxygen]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 20 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Unstable Plasma
- **Refiner** · *Plasma Pressurisation* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Oxygen]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 20 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Unstable Plasma
