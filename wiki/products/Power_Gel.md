# Power Gel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Energy Recharge Cell
- **Value:** 171.9
- **Description:** Convenient and optimised power storage unit
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Power Gel** is a product.

## Summary

**Power Gel** is a [[Product]] used to energize the [[Multi-tool]], [[Starship]] [[weapon|weapons]] and [[life support]].

## Game description

Convenient and optimised power storage unit. Used in a wide variety of weapon and life support systems. Easily constructed from common isotopes, and frequently found in cylindrical metal containers.

## Craft

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Plutonium]] + 100 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Power Gel

## Usage

It is used to recharge the following items:
- [[Life support]] system by 50%
- [[Mining Beam]] by 25%
- [[Boltcaster]] by 25%
- [[Phase Beam]] by 5%

## Additional information

- Can be bought and sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- Note that it is generally [[Power Canister|Power Canisters]] that are found in cylindrical metal containers, not Power Gels.

## Release history

- [[Release]] - Added as a product.
