# Echo Seed

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Crystalline Sentience Cage
- **Value:** 1,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Echo Seed** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Echo Seed** is a [[curiosity]] and is collected for [[Priest Entity Nada]] and the [[Assembled Construct]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].

## Game Description

The Seed is an echo of life, the last reverberations of a living being.

Nada believes that planting this seed within Nada's Prime Terminal may allow their simulation to develop a convergence of its own sort, providing the spark that brings life to the Construct.

## Source

Echo seeds are mined from sentience echo sites on [[Dissonant Planet|Dissonant Planets]], appearing as a bigger version of the large, purple [[Atlantideum]] and [[Radiant Shard]] crystals. They are revealed using the [[Polyphonic Core]] and [[Scan Harmoniser]] technologies and are marked by a purple crystal icon similar to that of radiant shards, but with an exclamation point.

## Use

Echo seeds are used for a small step in the [[They Who Returned]] questline, and previously used during [[Expedition 10: Singularity]].
