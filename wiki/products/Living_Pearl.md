# Living Pearl

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Semi-Conscious Gemstone
- **Value:** 4,797.5
- **Added in:** Origins

**Living Pearl** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Living Pearl** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

Born in the hearts of enormous glowing molluscs found deep underwater.

Unlike most pearls, this sample is no unliving calcite. Some trace of the mother-clam's consciousness remains within the gem, giving it a lingering, haunting beauty.

## Source

It can be found in [[Armoured Clam|Armoured Clams]]. Damage will force them to open their shells for a short time, revealing the Living Pearl inside. If it is not taken in the allotted time, the shells will close and the player needs to damage them again. It can also be taken without doing damage by interacting and not getting too close.
