# Non-Stick Piston

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Industrial)
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Non-Stick Piston** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Non-Stick Piston** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Wrapped and greased pistons, ready for shipping.

High-tech components frequently require rapidly moving parts, or high-endurance motors. Without specially coated pistons, the failure rate of such parts would be significantly higher.

## Source

Non-Stick Piston can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Manufacturing Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Non-Stick Piston can be sold for a profit in any [[Technology Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
