# Ion Capacitor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Value:** 15,000.0
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Technology)
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Ion Capacitor** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Ion Capacitor** is a 3rd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A reinforced crate of ion capacitors.

These supercapacitors have an incredibly high energy density. In great demand in power generation economies.

## Economic Demand

The base value for Ion Capacitor is 15,000 Units.

Ion Capacitor is in high availability (lower buy value) in Technology (Light Blue) economy systems that are marked with a Nanites Symbol :
- Engineering
- High Tech
- Nano-Construction
- Technology

Ion Capacitor is in high demand (higher sell value) in Power Generation (Red) economy systems that are marked with a battery Symbol :
- High Voltage
- Fuel generation
- Energy Supply
- Power Generation

## Source

Ion Capacitor can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Technology Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Ion Capacitor can be sold for a profit in any [[Power Generation Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
