# Soul Engine

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Advanced Crafted Product
- **Value:** 1,000.0

**Soul Engine** is a curiosity product.

## Summary

**Soul Engine** is a [[curiosity]] and used in crafting the [[Mind Ark]] during [[The First Traveller]] mission.

## Game description

A powerful organic computing unit.

A glitching beat drives this miracle circuit, performing cycle after cycle in an endless, furious loop of calculation.

## Source
