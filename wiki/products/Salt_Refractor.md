# Salt Refractor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 30,500.0
- **Added in:** Origins

**Salt Refractor** is a component.

## Summary

**Salt Refractor** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

A specialist technology component, vital to the complex internals of modern laser systems.

This light-scattering device takes advantage of the unique optical properties of stabilised and polished salt, to both slow and refract passing photons.

## Source

Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
