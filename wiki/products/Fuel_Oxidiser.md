# Fuel Oxidiser

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Consumable Frigate Upgrade
- **Value:** 75,000.0
- **Added in:** Origins

**Fuel Oxidiser** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Fuel Oxidiser** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] and one of the upgrades available for [[frigate|frigates]]. It is a temporary upgrade which reduces the expedition's duration of a frigate fleet for a single expedition by 10 points.

The [[British spelling]] of this phrase is used in the wiki.

## Game description

A temporary frigate upgrade that lasts for one expedition.

This powerful fuel upgrade overloads frigate engines, allowing for faster-than-usual travel.

## Sources

- The product's blueprint is a possible reward from a [[Manufacturing Facility]] or [[Operations Centre]].
- The blueprint can be researched at the [[Upgrade Control]] terminal for one [[Salvaged Frigate Module]].

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Quad Servo]] + 50 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Fuel Oxidiser
