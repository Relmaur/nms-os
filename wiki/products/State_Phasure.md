# State Phasure

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Unique valuable curiosity

**State Phasure** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**State Phasure** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 7th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

For unknown reasons, this unique Atlas Seed has been phased out of alignment with this quantum reality. Contents cannot be determined.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Curiosity]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Photic Jade]] + 100 × [[Cadmium]] → 1 × State Phasure
