# Sentinel Flare

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Sentinel Communicator
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Sentinel Flare** is a curiosity

## Summary

**Sentinel Flare** is a [[curiosity]]. It is used to summon your [[Sentinel]] companion named [[Laylaps]].

## Game Description

### Inactive

A specialist device, created by Iteration Tethys. Its primary function is to hail your reconstructed Sentinel drone.

Select the device and use Activate Flare (//) to signal [The Drone].

While this flare is active in your Exosuit inventory, the drone will stay by your side while planetside.

### Active

A specialist device, created by Iteration Tethys. Its primary function is to hail your reconstructed Sentinel drone.

Select the device and use Deactivate Broadcast (//) to stop signalling [ The Sentry ].

While this flare is active in your Exosuit inventory, the drone will stay by your side while planetside.

## Acquirement

The Sentinel Flare is handed out by [[Iteration: Tethys]]. They give out a new one should the old one be dismantled.

## Use

The drone can be summoned and dismissed by pressing the // (PC/PS/XB) key.
