# Suspension Fluid

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Value:** 1,100
- **Description:** Non-reactive and pressure-resistant liquid.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Suspension Fluid** is a product.

## Summary

**Suspension Fluid** is a [[Product]] used in the [[crafting]] of various technologies.

Crafting Suspension fluid is the first step of crafting a [[Warp Cell]]. It can be crafted in your inventory or found commonly in [[Cargo Drop|"Search" boxes]].

## Game description

Non-reactive and pressure-resistant liquid. Vital to the manufacture of [[starship]] and [[exosuit]] technologies.

## Source

When you talk to [[Artemis]] in the Hologram for the first time, Artemis rewards you with the crafting recipe for Suspension Fluid at the end of the dialogue.

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Suspension Fluid

## Use

Suspension Fluid is an obsolete item and no longer has a purpose in the game.
