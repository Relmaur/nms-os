# Health Station

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** Healing
- **Power:** 10
- **Added in:** Origins

A **Health Station** is a base building component.

## Summary

A **Health Station** is a piece of equipment that allows the player to restore their [[health]].

## Game description

A wall mounted module that can be used to recharge [[health]].

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Metal Plating]] + 20 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 50 × [[Uranium]] → 1 × Health Station

## Additional information

- The Health Station is only usable by the player who built it.
- [[Power]] consumption is 10kP. As the Health Station has no points for [[Electrical Wiring]] to attach to, its power supply can only come from being attached to an electrically powered, prefabricated base-building structure, such as a [[Cylindrical Room]]. Alternatively, it can be placed on a [[Storage Container]] and be powered that way.
