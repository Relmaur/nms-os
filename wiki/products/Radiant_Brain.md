# Radiant Brain

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Anomalous Material
- **Used for:** Upgrading
- **Updated:** SentinelUp

**Radiant Brain** is a component.

## Summary

**Radiant Brain** is a component that is used for upgrading.

## Game Description

Shifting nanite clusters sewn together with a pugneum filament, this circuit is painfully hot to the touch. The purple light that leaks from deep within its wiring has softened, as if it has become less hostile.

Previously embedded within a Sentinel Walker, this semi-organic computer has been altered by both Iteration: Tethys and a Messenger of the Atlas.

## Sources

- Loot a Walker Brain from a Sentinel Walker and take it to Tethys near the Portal on the Space Anomaly.

## Use

**Radiant Brain** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Hardframe Body -- Radiant Brain x1 + Antimatter x1 + Life Support Gel x1 → Hardframe Body
