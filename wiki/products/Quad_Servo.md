# Quad Servo

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Anomalous Material
- **Value:** 20,000.0
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Quad Servo** is a component.

## Summary

**Quad Servo** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game Description

A highly sprung motor arm salvaged from inside of a Sentinel patrol unit. It occasionally twitches, as if experiencing the echo of combat.

## Source

It can be obtained by the following methods:
- destroying the [[Combat Supplies]] containers dropped by [[Sentinel Quad|Sentinel Quads]]
- [[Suspicious Packet (Goods)]] from the [[Scrap Dealer]]
- a gift from [[Guild Envoy|Guild Envoys]] of the [[Mercenaries Guild]]
