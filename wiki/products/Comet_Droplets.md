# Comet Droplets

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods
- **Value:** 15,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Comet Droplets** are a tradeable.

## Summary

**Comet Droplets** are a 3rd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game description

Several canisters of comet droplets.

An elaborate trading name for chunks of meteorite, these rocks are nonetheless extremely valuable to the advanced material economies that can process them.

## Source

Comet Droplets can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Trading Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. They are also offered as a reward for doing missions at the [[Mission Board]].

## Usage

Comet Droplets can be sold for a profit in any [[Advanced Materials Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
