# Hydrothermal Fuel Cell

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Submarine Reactor Fuel
- **Value:** 7,200.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Hydrothermal Fuel Cell** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Hydrothermal Fuel Cell** is a  [[consumable]] [[product]] which is used to power the [[Nautilon|Nautilon's]] [[Humboldt Drive]].

## Game Description

A high thermal energy fuel, contained within a standardised fuel cell. Used to fuel the Humboldt Drive that powers the Nautilon submarine.

Created with a mix of organic compounds (Carbon and cyto-phosphates) and localised mineral salts.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Salt]] + 40 × [[Cyto-Phosphate]] + 40 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Hydrothermal Fuel Cell

## Additional information

- One Hydrothermal Fuel Cell will fully recharge the Humboldt Drive.
- All the required resources can be collected underwater, eliminating the need to resurface.

## Release history

- [[Abyss]] - Added as a product.
