# Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Stabilised Di-hydrogen Fuel
- **Used for:** Charging
- **Value:** 80,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)** is a fuel source.

## Summary

**Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] which is used as fuel for [[Frigate|Frigates]]. They can be sold for 80,000  Units each.

## Game Description

A huge barrel of frigate fuel.

This viscous gel is highly toxic to the touch. A refined fuel, it powers the interstellar engines of large trading ships.

## Source

The blueprint is a reward given during the [[Freighter Command]] mission.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 200 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 200 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Frigate Fuel (200 Tonnes)
