# Historical Document

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Historical Document
- **Value:** 2,296,996.0
- **Used for:** Trading, Trading (High Value)
- **Added in:** Relics

**Historical Document** is a type of Artifact.

## Summary

**Historical Document** is one of several types of [[Artifact|Artifacts]] and it is a reward for opening [[Large Artifact Crate|Large Artifact Crates]] found in Ancient [[Ruin|Ruins]], completing [[Mission Board|missions]], from containers found on [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]], or completing exploration type expeditions with [[frigate|frigates]].

## Game description

These are the possible game descriptions for Historical Document. One of the following is randomly chosen when an artifact is procedurally generated.

The sequence is taken from the game file.

### Common

1. Approximately **XXX** years old.<br>The paper is crumbling, the ink is fading. This may be the final opportunity anyone has to read this manuscript before time claims it for good.
1. Approximately **XXX** years old.<br>Written in an otherworldly script, this manuscript is covered with strange marginalia. Generations have added notes and diagrams to the original text, some of which are highly disturbing.
1. Approximately **XXX** years old.<br>The lettering is harsh and deeply carved. Whoever wrote this document left their emotions clearly engraved into the surface, regardless of the story the words are trying to tell.
1. Approximately **XXX** years old.<br>This document is handwritten, its lettering shaky and weak. Whoever wrote this spent a great deal of energy - perhaps their last - to commit their words to history.
1. Approximately **XXX** years old.<br>Though the cover has been competently printed, the internal lettering is crude and juvenile. Perhaps this was merely a practise sheet, an exercise to improve their writing?

### Uncommon

1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>Several of these pages appear stained, though there have been some clumsy attempts to clean them, and the second half has clearly been written in a different hand to the first...
1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>The original text of this document has been greatly altered. Entries have been crossed through, written over and even torn out, apparently by several different writers. Whatever was written here was hotly contested.
1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>This document was carefully packed in its container, wrapped in paper to keep it free of stains. There is a small bundle of hair clipped to the inside cover.

### Rare

1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>This manuscript is beautifully illustrated, with flowing calligraphy and dramatic, gilded illuminations. It is a work of exceptional artistry.
1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>The text in this document does not seem to concern anything out of the ordinary. However, the letters themselves do appear to be written in blood.
1. Approximately **XXXX** years old.<br>This manuscript is full of small, delicate blemishes. Tiny crinkled circles litter every page, as if the paper has been stained by tears.

The names are procedurally generated with certain terms using one of the following options: 1. Adjective + Noun; 2. Adjective + Noun.
