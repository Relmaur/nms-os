# Atlas Stone

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Very high value curiosity
- **Value:** 68,750.0
- **Added in:** Abyss

**Atlas Stone** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Atlas Stone** is a [[curiosity]]. It is currently unobtainable, but if the player held onto it since the Atlas Rises update, it can still be found in their inventory.

## Game description

A spherical gem that seems to pulsate with red light from its centre.

An extremely rare item often associated with ancient monolithic sites.

## Trivia

One of the interactions with the Atlas Interfaces still mentions the Atlas Stones as of Version 5.0.

## Release history

- [[Release]] - Added as a curiosity.
- [[Atlas Rises]]
  - [[The Atlas Path]] primary mission was completely reworked and Atlas Stone was replaced by [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]], also it cannot be obtained from [[Atlas Interface|Atlas Interfaces]].
  - It has the same icon as [[Remembrance]].
  - *Hidden changes:* It is no longer available for purchase at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
