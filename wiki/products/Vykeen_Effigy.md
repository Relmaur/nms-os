# Vy'keen Effigy

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Medium value curiosity
- **Value:** 24,750.0
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Vy'keen Effigy** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Vy'keen Effigy** is a [[curiosity]] of [[Vy'keen]] origin.

## Game Description

A small wooden icon carved into the shape of a [[Sentinel Drone|Sentinel drone]].

Used by warriors to enter a temporary trance-like state that eliminates their kind's need for sleep.

## Sources

- It can be found in [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Containers]] or as [[monolith]] rewards.
- It can also be bought from NPCs in [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]].
- It is a possible reward from [[frigate]] expeditions.
- It is also a possible trade from wandering traders you encounter while using the [[Pulse Engine]].
- It is also a possible reward from Salvaged Glass dropped by Sentinels.

## Additional information

- It can be given to Vy'keen NPCs to increase the player's standing with them.
