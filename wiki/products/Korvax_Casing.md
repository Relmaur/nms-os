# Korvax Casing

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** High value curiosity
- **Value:** 22,000.0
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Korvax Casing** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Korvax Casing** is a [[curiosity]] of [[Korvax]] origin.

## Game Description

The metallic shell of a disconnected electronic lifeform that was too distant from its kind to be refreshed with a new entity.

Highly prized by all species, not least its own.

## Sources

- The simplest way to get Korvax Casings in large amounts is to buy it from pilot NPCs in any [[Trading Post]] found in Korvax systems.
- Can be found in [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Containers]] or as [[monolith]] rewards.
- A possible reward from [[frigate]] expeditions.
- A possible trade from wandering traders you encounter while using the [[Pulse Engine]].
- With a 10% chance from crew lockers in [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]].

## Use

- Can be given to Korvax NPCs to increase the player's [[Faction|standing]] with Korvax faction.
- Can be used when interacting with a [[monolith]] in Korvax systems to reveal the location of the [[portal]] for the planet.
