# Organic Piping

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Scientific)
- **Value:** 30,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Organic Piping** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Organic Piping** is a 4th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A vat of preserved organic piping. Still warm.

To avoid difficult ethical problems, many labs grow their test subjects on a component level. The leftovers can be profitable in trading economies with relaxed medical regulations.

## Source

Organic Piping can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Scientific Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, medium-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Organic Piping can be sold for a profit in any [[Trading Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
