# Organic Catalyst

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Manufactured Gas Product
- **Value:** 320,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Organic Catalyst** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Organic Catalyst** is a [[tradeable]].

## Game description

A catalytic material used in the creation of many organic fertilisers.

Crafted from [[Thermic Condensate]] and [[Enriched Carbon]].

## Source

- Blueprint can be obtained in two ways:
  - Purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites on the [[Space Anomaly]]
  - In the [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]] through either a random drop or the Components blueprint unlock panel thanks to the use of [[Factory Override Unit]] (which is the same as in the Synthesis Laboratory).
- Individual units of the Organic Catalyst can be obtained:
  - As a mission reward.
  - From the [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Thermic Condensate]] + 1 × [[Enriched Carbon]] → 1 × Organic Catalyst

## Use

3 units of the Organic Catalyst are also used to upgrade the Terrarium Unit, Agri-Module and Biological Facility within the [[Planetary Settlement - Construction Opportunities|Settlement construction]].

## Additional information

Trying to obtain the Organic Catalyst through the Blueprints at the [[Planetary Chart (secure site)|secure sites]] (Manufacturing/Ops Centres) is very, very time-inefficient. By far easier it is to fly into space, call the Space Anomaly and buy the blueprint at the Synthesis Laboratory in the middle part of the upper floor. Drop rate of the Organic Catalyst at the frigate expeditions is extremely low, while the ingredients for the recipe - Enriched Carbon and Thermic Condensate - are more likely to drop (and they are easier to craft if you have unlocked either of the [[Compressed Atmospheric Gas|buildings extracting gases]]).
