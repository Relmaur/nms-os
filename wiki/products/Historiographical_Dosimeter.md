# Historiographical Dosimeter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Personal Reality Flux Monitor
- **Value:** 3,200.0
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Historiographical Dosimeter** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Historiographical Dosimeter** is a [[curiosity]] acquired and used during [[Expedition 2: Beachhead]].

## Game Description

[[Nada]] has detected a new pattern emerging in the stories of the [[Gek]], [[Korvax]], and [[Vy'keen]].

The dosimeter measures exposure to historiographical flux around [[Point of Interest|key artifact sites]]. Recordings of unusual historical shifts will enable Nada to trace their signal.

Assist Nada and Polo in their research by using this device to [[Expedition 2: Beachhead|transmit historical data]] from alien artifact sites.

## Sources

Awarded upon reaching the Rendezvous 5 during the Beachhead expedition.

## Additional Information

- Each flux reading yields 15 Quicksilver.
