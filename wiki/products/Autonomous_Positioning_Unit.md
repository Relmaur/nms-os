# Autonomous Positioning Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Technology)
- **Value:** 30,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Autonomous Positioning Unit** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Autonomous Positioning Unit** is a 4th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A container of positioning units and associated wiring.

These APS units can be used to turn virtually any technology into a self-navigating system. Highly valuable in power generation economies, where they're used to remove conductive organic hands from the manufacturing process.

## Source

Autonomous Positioning Unit can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Technology Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, medium-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Autonomous Positioning Unit can be sold for a profit in any [[Power Generation Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
