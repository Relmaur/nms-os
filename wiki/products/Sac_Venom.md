# Sac Venom

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Value:** 60,800
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Sac Venom** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Sac Venom** is a [[tradeable]] with a high sell value and one of the [[Tradeable|trade commodities]].

It can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with  icon.

## Game description

Stabilized poison sample initially produced by aquatic urchin creatures. A rare and expensive item on the Galactic Trade Network.

## Sources

Sac Venom can be found by destroying urchin-like creatures found on planetary surfaces and under the water. Like other high value non-stackable trading commodities, some planets have an abundance of these.

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Sac Venom also be grown as a product of the [[Venom Urchin]] plant, with a harvest time of 3 hours 20 minutes.

## Additional Information

If found in the wild, picking one up will immediately give the player a three-star [[Sentinel|wanted level]]. Once a player begins mining Sac Venom, two drones and a [[Sentinel Quad]] are dispatched to interfere.
