# Memory Imprint

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Expedition tool
- **Type:** Loop Artifact
- **Value:** 16
- **Used for:** Expedition
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Memory Imprint** is an Expedition 7 reward.

## Summary

**Memory Imprint** is one of the rewards in [[Expedition 7: Leviathan]]. It does not have any active uses.

## Game description

A memory is burned into this strange material, an echo of another lifetime...

### Memory Imprint 1

"The leviathan was like nothing I've ever seen.<br>
So vast as to blot out the sun.<br>
I wanted it. I deserve to have it! It will be mine.<br>
But it resisted, would not come quietly.<br>
It wailed and it wailed as the infra-knife carved into its meat.<br>
It almost took me down with it. Perhaps it has. Perhaps..."

### Memory Imprint 2

"The same nightmare chases me: a wail fills my cockpit, the dashboard controls gummed with blood.<br>
I have given up sleep. I pulsed past blurred stars.<br>
And then there it was:Majestic, drifting in the ocean of space.<br>
Something inside me burns for it. I must have it.<br>
Its great fin flexed toward my ship, and my hull was gone..."

### Memory Imprint 3

"Today should have been the day. Their hour was at hand.<br>
It must be mine. They are like nothing I've ever seen.<br>
Everything was prepared. But it moved faster than I thought possible. As though it knew where I would fly, before I knew it myself.<br>
A great tentacle hugged my ship, my cockpit - pressing my suit down to the bone. This suit will be my grave."

### Memory Imprint 4

"The chase is over, and I have lost.<br>
The giants will endure, and I will end.<br>
As I plummeted from the sky, I saw another ship falling alongside mine. Then another, and another. <br>
I may have imagined it. The misfiring of a dying mind...<br>
Those creatures were so beaautiful. But it si all ended now.<br>
They will haunt my thoughts no longer."

### Memory Imprint 5

"I came face-to-face with a creature more beautiful and bizarre than anything I have ever seen.<br>
Despite no discernible eyes, I do not believe it is blind.<br>
It was huge, glorious, serene. But then - with a flick of its tentacles, it was gone.<br>
I have thought of nothing else since. I can only think of the whale. whale. whale. wail."

## Source

Memory Imprint is obtained as a reward for reaching loops in each iteration of the Expedition 7: Leviathan.
