## Summary

**Amino Chamber** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

A specialist biological catalyzation device.

This unit takes advantage of naturally occurring enzymes in order to facilitate increased energy yields and faster processing in a wide range of advanced technologies.

## Source

- Available from some [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] for 5000+ units
- From some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
