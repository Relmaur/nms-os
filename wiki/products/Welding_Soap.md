# Welding Soap

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Technology)
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Welding Soap** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Welding Soap** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Several large bars of welding soap.

Used in power generation systems to heat-clean metal diodes before they're fused into batteries.

## Source

Welding Soap can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Technology Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Welding Soap can be sold for a profit in any [[Power Generation Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].

Welding Soap can be used as certain (Synthesis Plant C->B) Settlement buildings upgrades.
