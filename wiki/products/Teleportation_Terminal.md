# Teleportation Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** Enables direct teleportation to known locations
- **Base complexity:** 10
- **Added in:** Echoes

A **Teleportation Terminal** enables direct fast travel to previously visited or registered locations.

## Summary

A **Teleportation Terminal** allows players to instantly travel between registered teleport locations, including bases, space stations, freighters, settlements, and planetary teleporters.

[[Terminal|Terminals]] of this type are found in player bases, freighters, settlements, and Space Anomaly locations. They provide an efficient method of navigating across the galaxy without requiring starship fuel or warp capabilities.

Players can build these terminals in their base or freighter, enabling long-distance travel from custom locations.

## Game description

A teleportation interface providing direct access to known locations, including bases, stations, and freighter systems.

## Usage

Upon activation, the terminal displays a list of accessible teleport locations. These include:
- Player bases with active base computers
- Visited space stations in each star system
- Active teleporter-equipped freighters
- Anomaly-based destinations
- Settlements where the player is Overseer

Teleportation is instantaneous and does not consume any resources. Location lists are filtered by name and recency of visit.

Using a Teleportation Terminal to leave the current system will cancel any active [[Nexus]] mission, even if the objective is partially completed.

## Destinations

Teleportation Terminals allow connection to:
- All previously visited [[Space station|Space stations]]
- Player-built [[Base Computer|bases]] with teleporters
- Player's [[Freighter]] if it includes a Teleportation Terminal
- [[Settlement|Settlements]] under the player’s control, once the Overseer's Office is constructed and visited
- [[Space Anomaly]] destinations related to active missions, when using the terminal inside the Anomaly

Note: The Space Anomaly cannot be teleported to and must be summoned manually. Destinations only appear once physically visited. Bases and settlements must have active teleport-capable structures. Deleted bases or settlements will no longer appear.

### Interface Options

The Teleportation Terminal menu includes several filters to organize destinations:

- **All** – Displays all valid teleportation destinations, including bases, stations, freighters, and settlements.
- **Recent** – Prioritizes destinations recently visited or teleported from.
- **Featured** – Highlights mission-critical or currently relevant locations, such as Nexus targets or active quest areas.
- **Space Anomaly** – A category filter that only appears when using a terminal inside the [[Space Anomaly]]. It lists destinations relevant to Nexus missions or Anomaly-based objectives. It does not allow teleportation to the Anomaly itself.
- **Your Bases** – Displays only player-built bases with active teleporters.
- **Your Settlements** – Lists settlements where the player is Overseer and has built the Overseer's Office.
- **Space Stations** – Displays all previously visited stations with functioning teleporters.
- **Group** – Shows destinations shared with the current multiplayer group.
- **Favorites** – Custom tab where players can pin preferred locations by selecting the diamond icon in the top-right corner of any destination card.

Selecting a category reorganizes the list dynamically. Favorite locations are marked with a diamond symbol and persist across sessions.

## Build

For construction and blueprint details, see: [[Base Teleport Module]].

The Base Teleport Module serves as the functional Teleportation Terminal in bases and freighters. It must be powered in player bases and provides access to all qualified destinations via the teleportation network.

## Additional information

### General

- The Teleportation Terminal functions as a personal fast-travel node.
- No fuel or charge is required for activation.
- The terminal synchronizes with all other teleporters across the player’s network, provided the destination has been previously visited and the base computer is online.

### Player-Constructed

- A limit of 10 teleportation terminals exists per base.
- Short-range teleporters will not replace this terminal's long-distance travel function.
- Can be used in freighters and settlements to return to bases or visited stations without needing to warp.
- Terminals placed in player bases require a power supply to function.

### Teleportation Limitations

- Some distant systems or outlaw stations may not appear unless manually visited.
- Bases that have been deleted or moved will no longer appear in the terminal's list.
- Connection to systems under story-lock progression may be temporarily restricted.
- Leaving the system via Teleportation Terminal will cancel any active [[Nexus]] missions.
- Systems with no visited space station or teleporter will not appear on the destination list.
- Bases without an active teleporter or power supply will not be reachable.
- [[Freighter|Freighters]] must be summoned in a system at least once before they become teleport destinations.
- [[Outlaw System|Outlaw Systems]] or systems with abandoned space stations may prevent inbound or outbound teleportation.
- Some story missions and quest objectives may override teleportation access, requiring manual travel.
