# Di-hydrogen Jelly

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Portable Life Support Power
- **Value:** 200.0
- **Added in:** Fractal

**Di-hydrogen Jelly** is a component.

## Summary

**Di-hydrogen Jelly** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game Description

A faintly-glowing jelly. Its gentle light and soft wobble belie the fact that this sticky gel is both highly toxic and highly flammable. Skin contact is to be avoided at all costs.

Synthesised from Di-hydrogen.

## Source

Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Di-hydrogen Jelly

- **Refiner** · *Condense Di-hydrogen* · 60 s per unit: 30 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Di-hydrogen Jelly

## Additional Information

- It is required to repair the [[starship]]'s [[Launch Thruster]].
- Exploit - Because Di-hydrogen Jelly can be *refined* from only 30 [[Di-hydrogen]] using a refiner (compared to 40 Di-hydrogen from direct crafting), you can multiply your reserves of both Di-hydrogen and Di-hydrogen Jelly infinitely. It should be noted however that this method is rather slow and tedious compared to most other methods. For further information, see the [[Di-hydrogen|"Exploit"]] section on the Di-hydrogen page.
