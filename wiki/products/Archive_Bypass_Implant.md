# Archive Bypass Implant

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Subcutaneous Access Circuit
- **Used for:** Colossal Archive
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Archive Bypass Implant** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Archive Bypass Implant** is a [[consumable]] product specifically used to provide administrative access to ancient texts at a [[Colossal Archive|Planetary Archive]].

## Game description

A biotechnical firewall, developed as a safeguard against textual cognitohazards.  It is coated in something slippery, and has started to bubble along the surface.

## Source

- Blueprint is received as a reward from Hunt 3, Milestone 4 *They Pushed too Far* as part of [[Expedition 4: Emergence]].
- Must be constructed using the blueprint.

## Crafting

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 2 × [[Wiring Loom]] + 90 × [[Cyto-Phosphate]] + 3 × [[Hadal Core]] → 1 × Archive Bypass Implant

## Release history

- [[Frontiers]] - This item was added to the game as part of the Expedition 4.
- [[Waypoint]] - Renamed, formerly "Archive Override Implant".
