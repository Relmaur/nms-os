## Summary

**Englobed Shade** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 2nd of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].

It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

This Atlas Shell holds the remains of a parasitic space-borne lifeform, most likely of extra-dimensional origins.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Juno]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Captured Nanode]] + 100 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Englobed Shade
