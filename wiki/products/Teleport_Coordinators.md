# Teleport Coordinators

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods
- **Value:** 50,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Teleport Coordinators** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Teleport Coordinators** are a 5th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A bundle of precisely calibrated teleport coordinators.

These pin-prick sized circuits are vital to the delicate process of inserting reinforcing material into advanced alloys. They can also aid long-distance sales efforts.

## Source

Teleport Coordinators can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Trading Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Teleport Coordinators can be sold for a profit in any [[Advanced Materials Economy]] star system.

It is also used as an upgrade material for some [[Planetary Settlement]] buildings.

| Cost | Structure | Class |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9 Teleport Coordinators | Trading centre | S |

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
