# Bromide Salt

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Minerals)
- **Value:** 15,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Bromide Salt** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Bromide Salt** is a 3rd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A crate of bromide salt.

The result of an extensive distillation and evaporation process, the bromide salt vats feed hungry factories in manufacturing systems.

## Source

Bromide Salt can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Mining Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Bromide Salt can be sold for a profit in any [[Manufacturing Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
