# Poly Fibre

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 130,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Poly Fibre** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Poly Fibre** is a [[tradeable]] that can be crafted or purchased.

## Game description

A connective fibre for electronics, made from an advanced synthetic polymer and capable of serving as a conduit for power and data.

Crafted from Cactus Flesh and Star Bulbs.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Cactus Flesh]] + 200 × [[Star Bulb]] → 1 × Poly Fibre

## Additional information

The blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and continue the [[Scientific Research]] quest line.
