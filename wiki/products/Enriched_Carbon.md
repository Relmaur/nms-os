# Enriched Carbon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Enhanced Gas Product
- **Value:** 50,000
- **Added in:** Beacon

**Enriched Carbon** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Enriched Carbon** is a [[tradeable]] that can also be used to craft certain products.

## Game description

A refined product, taking ordinary carbon and boosting it with radioactive energy.

Crafted from Radon and Condensed Carbon.

## Sources

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- It can be found at [[Resource Depot|Resource Depots]].
- It can also be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Radon]] + 50 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 1 × Enriched Carbon

- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Radon]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 5 × [[Chlorine]] → 1 × Enriched Carbon
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Radon]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 10 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Enriched Carbon
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Radon]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 5 × [[Chlorine]] → 1 × Enriched Carbon
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Radon]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 10 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Enriched Carbon
