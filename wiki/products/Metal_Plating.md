# Metal Plating

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 800.0
- **Added in:** Companions

**Metal Plating** is a component.

## Summary

**Metal Plating** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting and repairing.

## Game description

A lightweight metal product, heavily used in the manufacture of starship parts and deployable technology. A mainstay of the construction industries of all known primary species.

Crafted from Ferrite Dust.

## Source

- **Crafting**: 50 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Metal Plating

Also available from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Additional information

While used for general construction, these platings can also be used to repair certain ship equipment.
