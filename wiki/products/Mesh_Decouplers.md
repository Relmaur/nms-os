# Mesh Decouplers

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Industrial)
- **Value:** 15,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Mesh Decouplers** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Mesh Decouplers** is a 3rd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A crate of mesh decouplers.

The creation of high-tech components often involves accidental mesh-coupling. These decouplers are particularly powerful, being able to deploy six prongs simultaneously.

## Source

Six-Pronged Mesh Decoupler can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Manufacturing Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Six-Pronged Mesh Decoupler can be sold for a profit in any [[Technology Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
