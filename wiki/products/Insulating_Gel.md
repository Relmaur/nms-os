# Insulating Gel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Component
- **Description:** Shields circuitry from degredation
- **Value:** 44,000
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Insulating Gel** is an item used in electronics.

## Game description

An all-purpose insulating gel to shield circuitry from environmental weathering, radiation and chemical degradation.

## Source

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 20 × [[Cactus Flesh]] + 20 × [[Fungal Mould]] → 1 × Insulating Gel

Blueprint obtained by following the [[Science Terminal]] quest line.

## Use

Insulating Gel is an obsolete item and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Dismantle

Cannot be dismantled.

## Additional information

Insulating Gel is used to acquire the blueprint for the [[Galactic trade terminal]].

As of the [[Atlas Rises]] update, this item has been removed and has been reclassified as an **Obsolete Item** in-game. It's value has not changed.
