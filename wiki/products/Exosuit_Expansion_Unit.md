# Exosuit Expansion Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Upgrade Unit
- **Value:** 155,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Exosuit Expansion Unit** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Exosuit Expansion Unit** is a [[curiosity]].

It increases a [[Inventory|general inventory or technology slot]] by one up to a limit.

## Game description

An advanced survival technology module that allows expansion of a user's Exosuit Storage.

Pre-packed nano-expanders create stablised sub-warp voids within the Exosuit, increasing its inventory capacity. Apply this augmentation at the Exosuit upgrade terminal on board a Space Station.

## Usage

Locate an Exosuit Upgrade Console near the [[Technology Merchant]] that sells Exosuit Upgrades on the left-hand side of any [[Space Station]]. If you have already bought the Exosuit Slot Upgrade from this specific console and you have an Exosuit Expansion Unit in your/your ship's inventory, open the console again and you'll be able to add a new slot, consuming the expansion unit.
