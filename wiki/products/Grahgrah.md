# Grahgrah

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Trade Commodity
- **Value:** 13,750.0
- **Description:** Assists the Vy'keen in creating battle screams
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Grahgrah** is a trade commodity.

## Summary

**Grahgrah** is a relatively expensive [[trade commodity]] valued by the [[Vy'keen]].

## Game description

Gas inhaled by Vy'keen warriors to temporarily increase lung capacity and allow high quality battle screams. Outlawed by many peace agreements throughout the galaxy, many of which are now broken.

## Location

It can be found in [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Containers]] on a [[planet]].

It can also be found occurring in a planet's mantle, as on planet [[Fiham XI]].

## Use

Grahgrah is an obsolete product and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

- It may also be offered to a Vy'keen to pledge allegiance and gain 3 reputation.
- A new version of this product was brought back in the [[Outlaws]] era, but spelled as [[GrahGrah]].
