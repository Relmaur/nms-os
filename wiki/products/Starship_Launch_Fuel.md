# Starship Launch Fuel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Stabilised Di-hydrogen Fuel
- **Value:** 450
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Starship Launch Fuel** is a [[consumable]] [[product]].

## Summary

**Starship Launch Fuel** is a consumable product which is used to provide fuel for a [[starship]]'s [[Launch Thruster|Launch Thrusters]]. It provides 100% Launch Thruster fuel when consumed.

It can be crafted or refined, with [[Di-hydrogen]] being the primary ingredient in both cases. Once obtained, a single stack can hold up to 40 units in a [[Storage Container]] or [[Freighter]] inventory and 20 units in other inventories.

## Game Description

Highly reactive solid-state fuel, required to fuel Starship Launch Thrusters.

Created from crystallized Di-hydrogen, found in blue deposits on all worlds.

## Source

- **Crafting**: 40 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 1 × [[Metal Plating]] → 1 × Starship Launch Fuel

- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Capture* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 20 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 5 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 1 × Starship Launch Fuel
- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Capture* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 20 × [[Pure Ferrite]] + 10 × [[Sodium]] → 1 × Starship Launch Fuel
- **Refiner** · *Di-hydrogen Capture* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 10 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 5 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 1 × Starship Launch Fuel

Starship Launch Fuel is also available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]], though in some star systems, the price markup for it can be over 90%, resulting in prices in the ten-thousands for one canister.
