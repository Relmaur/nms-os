# Component

**Components** are a type of product.

## Summary

**Components** are a type of product that used as ingredients in various blueprints and most of them can be crafted.

## Sources

- Can be purchased from Galactic Trade Terminals and Technology Merchants.
- Blueprints can be acquired from Manufacturing Facilities and Operations Centres.
- Crafted Technology Component blueprints can be purchased from Synthesis Laboratory for 250 each.

## List of Components

Crafted Technology Component

- Antimatter (blueprint from Awakenings)
- Antimatter Housing (blueprint from Awakenings)
- Carbon Nanotubes (always craftable)
- Cobalt Mirror (obsolete)
- Hermetic Seal (blueprint from Awakenings)
- Metal Plating (always craftable)
- Microprocessor (blueprint from Scientific Research)
- Oxygen Filter (obsolete)
- Salt Refractor (obsolete)
- Sodium Diode (obsolete)
- Amino Chamber
- Hydraulic Wiring
- Magnetic Resonator
- Quantum Computer
- Solar Mirror

Portable Life Support Power

- Di-hydrogen Jelly (always craftable)

Anomalous Material

- Quad Servo (acquired by killing Sentinel Quad)
- Walker Brain (acquired by killing Sentinel Walker)

## Additional information

- Components can be sold, but one should consider holding onto some as they are required for certain blueprints.
- Quad Servo and Walker Brain cannot be crafted, purchased and do not have blueprints. They can be acquired by killing certain type of Sentinels.
