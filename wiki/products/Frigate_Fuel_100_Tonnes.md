# Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Stabilised Di-hydrogen Fuel
- **Value:** 40,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes)** is a fuel source.

## Summary

**Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes)** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] which is used as fuel for [[Frigate|Frigates]]. They can be sold for 40,000  Units each.

## Game Description

A large barrel of frigate fuel.

This viscous gel is highly toxic to the touch. A refined fuel, it powers the interstellar engines of large trading ships.

## Source

The blueprint is a reward given during the [[Freighter Command]] mission.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Di-hydrogen]] + 100 × [[Tritium]] → 1 × Frigate Fuel (100 Tonnes)
