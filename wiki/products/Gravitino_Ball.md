# Gravitino Ball

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Total Value:** 40,023.5
- **Updated:** Echoes

**Gravitino Ball** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Gravitino Ball** is a tradeable and one of the trade commodities

As the player walks closer to one, it will shine brighter. It can be found in small, medium, and large varieties.

It can be revealed with Analysis Visor and will be indicated with the icon.

When a Gravitino Ball is picked up in the wild, the player receives a three-star wanted level. There is no risk gathering them from your base or freighter hydroponics, and are a great cash crop when grown en masse.

## Game Description

A supersymmetric particle cluster created by divergent super-gravitational forces. A micro-rift is created as they form, and extraction of the orb prompts a localised reality weakening. Sentinel forces will respond to stablise any such anomaly.

Can be sold at a Galactic Trade Terminal.

## Source

The Gravitino Ball in a natural habitat. It only shines brightly when the player is near.

Sentient Plants on a frozen world

- Gravitino Balls will always spawn on planets with hostile or corrupted sentinels.
- They can also be found from a rock-like creature with a stalk holding a Gravitino Ball. These are called Sentient Plant in-game. The stalk will only appear when you're near it. This type has a low chance of appearing on planets without extreme weather, and is similar to the Light Stalk found around Xen in the game Half-Life.
- Several can also be found at certain Ancient Ruins.
- With assistance from a Farmer, Gravitino Balls can also be grown as a product of the Gravitino Host plant, with a harvest time of 120 minutes.
- Can be found on Frigate expeditions

## Use

### Crafting

**Gravitino Ball** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Gravitino Coil -- Magnetic Resonator x3 + Gravitino Ball x1 + Chromatic Metal x50 → Gravitino Coil
- Precision Minotaur Laser -- Gravitino Ball x2 + Solar Mirror x1 → Precision Minotaur Laser
- Resonance Amplifier -- Di-hydrogen x160 + Gravitino Ball x3 + Radiant Shard x1 → Resonance Amplifier

### Refining

**Gravitino Ball** is not used as an ingredient for refining using a Refiner.

### Cooking

**Gravitino Ball** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

The value can be calculated as being 40,023.5 units, due to the fact that a stack of 10 gravitino balls is worth 400,235 units. When stacked, the value is displayed as "40,024". When not stacked, the value is displayed as "40,023". Only the 10x stack has enough data to provide the real accurate value.
