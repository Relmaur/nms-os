# Gravitino Ball

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Value:** 40,023.5
- **Description:** Extremely rare and valuable.
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Gravitino Ball** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Gravitino Ball** is a [[tradeable]] and one of the [[Tradeable|trade commodities]]

As the player walks closer to one, it will shine brighter. It can be found in small, medium, and large varieties.

It can be revealed with [[Analysis Visor]] and will be indicated with the  icon.

When a Gravitino Ball is picked up in the wild, the player receives a three-star [[Sentinel|wanted level]]. There is no risk gathering them from your base or freighter hydroponics, and are a great cash crop when grown en masse.

## Game Description

A supersymmetric particle cluster created by divergent super-gravitational forces. A micro-rift is created as they form, and extraction of the orb prompts a localised reality weakening. Sentinel forces will respond to stablise any such anomaly.

Can be sold at a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Source

- Gravitino Balls will always spawn on planets with hostile or corrupted sentinels.
- They can also be found from a rock-like creature with a stalk holding a Gravitino Ball. These are called [[Sentient Plant]] in-game. The stalk will only appear when you're near it. This type has a low chance of appearing on planets without extreme weather, and is similar to the [[wikia:c:half-life:Light Stalk|Light Stalk]] found around Xen in the game Half-Life.
- Several can also be found at certain Ancient Ruins.
- With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Gravitino Balls can also be grown as a product of the [[Gravitino Host]] plant, with a harvest time of 120 minutes.
- Can be found on Frigate expeditions

## Additional Information

The value can be calculated as being 40,023.5 units, due to the fact that a stack of 10 gravitino balls is worth 400,235 units. When stacked, the value is displayed as "40,024". When not stacked, the value is displayed as "40,023". Only the 10x stack has enough data to provide the real accurate value.
