# Albumen Pearl

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Value:** 10,640
- **Description:** Valuable green gem stone found in shells
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Albumen Pearl** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Albumen Pearl** is a [[tradeable]] and one of the [[Tradeable|trade commodities]].

It is a valuable gemstone most frequently found enclosed inside of [[Humming Sac|Humming Sacs]], which are shells that resemble plants. It currently has no use other than trading.

## Game description

Gemstone formed over the course of centuries within the egg of a super-incubatory species. Highly ornamental, these gems are themselves living seeds, lucent grains of potential life.

Can be sold at a Galactic Trade Terminal.

## Sources

- Albumen Pearls are most reliably sourced in [[cave]] systems beneath a planet's surface, inside red shells called Humming Sacs. Taking these will not alarm the [[Sentinel|Sentinels]].
- On certain planets, they can be found everywhere on the surface inside large pea pod-shaped plants similar to Albumen Pearl Orbs but labelled as [[Sentient Plant|Sentient Plants]] by the Analysis Visor. While these planets usually have a high environmental hazard, this is not always the case. Taking Albumen Pearls from these plants will alarm the Sentinels.

With assistance from a [[Farmer (NPC)|Farmer]], Albumen Pearls can also be grown as a product of the [[Albumen Pearl Orb]] plant, with a harvest time of 2 hours.

## Additional information

- If it stored in the inventory, scanning [[pirate|pirates]] will target the player during spaceflight. Many players have reported getting involved in large scale space battles when having large quantities of Albumen Pearls.

## Speculation

- A possible reference to the xenomorphs of the "Alien" franchise, as hinted at by their in-game description.
