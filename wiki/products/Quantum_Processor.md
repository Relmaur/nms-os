# Quantum Processor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Crafted Product
- **Value:** 4,400,000.0
- **Added in:** Remnant

**Quantum Processor** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Quantum Processor** is a [[tradeable]] that can be crafted or purchased.

## Game description

A highly advanced computational device, essential for the complex calculations involved in warp travel.

Crafted from Circuit Boards and Superconductors.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Circuit Board]] + 1 × [[Superconductor]] → 1 × Quantum Processor

Can also be obtained as an industrial product of a Korvax settlement when an S-Class industrial facility is present

## Additional Information

If a player is attempting to farm the highest-level [[Tradeable]] items, they should account for the fact that one quantum processor is required for both recipes when designing their farm.
