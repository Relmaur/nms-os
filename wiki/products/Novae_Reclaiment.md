# Novae Reclaiment

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Unique valuable curiosity

**Novae Reclaiment** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Novae Reclaiment** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 8th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

Atlas Seed contains neutronium elements from a catastrophic supernova event that obliterated a sentient civilization in an earlier era.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Hubble]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[State Phasure]] + 100 × [[Emeril]] → 1 × Novae Reclaiment
