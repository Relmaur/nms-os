# Shielding Shard

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Defensive Energy Battery
- **Value:** 344.0
- **Description:** Hazard Protection and Shield energy storage unit.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Shielding Shard** is a [[Product]] used to energize Deflector Shield, Exosuit Hazard Protection or Hazard Protection Units.

## Summary

**Shielding Shard** is used to store energy for various types of equipment. They do not stack in your inventory.

Since they are built with 70% [[Iron]], the only [[Oxide elements|oxide]] element that cannot be used to recharge devices, the Shielding line is a relatively cheap and reliable method to recharge your [[Hazard Protection|Exosuit Hazard Protection]], [[Hazard Protection Unit|Hazard Protection Units]] and [[Deflector Shield]].

## Game description

Hazard Protection and Shield energy storage unit. Easily constructed from common oxides, and often stored in yellow crates.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Iron]] + 20 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Shielding Shard

## Usage

Shielding Shards can be used to recharge the following items:
- Exosuit Hazard Protection by 62%
- Hazard Protection Units by 62%
- Deflector Shield by 25%

## Additional information

- Can be bought and sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
