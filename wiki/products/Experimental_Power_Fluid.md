# Experimental Power Fluid

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Energy Source)
- **Value:** 30,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Experimental Power Fluid** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Experimental Power Fluid** is a 4th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Corrosion-resistant barrels of an experimental power fluid.

One barrel of this highly volatile concoction is enough to power a space station for weeks on end. Particularly valued by mining economies.

## Source

Experimental Power Fluid can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Power Generation Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, medium-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Experimental Power Fluid can be sold for a profit in any [[Mining Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
