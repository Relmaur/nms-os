## Summary

## Game Description

A unique ID transponder, ripped from the internal signal array of an outlaw starship.

Possesion[sic] of such a device implies the destruction of the vessel in question. Turn it over to an envoy of the Mercenaries Guild to collect any associated bounties.

## Source

- Occasionally received on destroying pirate Starships.

## Additional Information

It can be given to Mercenaries Guild Envoys to earn standing.
