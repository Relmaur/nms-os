# Pirate Transponder

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Pirate Transponder** is a [[component]].

## Game Description

A unique ID transponder, ripped from the internal signal array of an outlaw starship.

Possesion of such a device implies the destruction of the vessel in question. Turn it over to an envoy of the Mercenaries Guild to collect any associated bounties.

## Source

- Occasionally received on destroying pirate [[Starship|Starships]].

## Additional Information

It can be given to [[Mercenaries Guild|Mercenaries]] [[Guild Envoy|Guild Envoys]] to earn standing.
