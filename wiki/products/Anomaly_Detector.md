# Anomaly Detector

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Anomalous Homing Device
- **Value:** 3,280
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Anomaly Detector** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Anomaly Detector** is a [[curiosity]] and one of the [[Curiosity|anomalous homing devices]].

## Game Description

A single-use geometric beacon that probes the surrounding area for near-space anomalies. Such objects are highly unpredictable.

Select the Detector and use Begin Scan (E) to begin the location process.

## Source

- Occasionally received on shooting [[Asteroid|Asteroids]]. They are most commonly dropped from "rare" asteroids. These are most common in red star systems (12%). Anomaly Detectors have a 0.5% chance of dropping from these rare asteroids.
- Can be received for free from a [[Explorers Guild]] [[Guild Envoy|Envoy]] for standing rank "Master" and above.
- Can be found from Abandoned Wreckages that occasionally spawn on planets with [[Salvageable Scrap]].

## Additional Information

It can be used to force detection of [[Space Encounter|Space Encounters]], including a [[Rogue Black Hole]]. As of the The Endurance Update (July 20, 2022), they can be used to find Living Frigates. Ignoring the detection notification and keep pulsing further for a few seconds will reroll another encounter to be detected until exiting pulse drive.
