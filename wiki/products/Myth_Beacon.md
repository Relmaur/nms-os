# Myth Beacon

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Experimental Summoning Technology
- **Value:** 80.0
- **Added in:** Remnant

**Myth Beacon** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Myth Beacon** is a [[curiosity]] acquired and used during [[Expedition 2: Beachhead]].

## Game Description

The result of [[Nada]]'s research into unusual patterns detected in the stories of the [[Gek]], [[Korvax]], and [[Vy'keen]].

The beacon broadcasts a signal echo attuned to the shift historiographical flux mapped by Nada's traveller companions.

Specialist [[Polo]] is unwilling to predict the results of the broadcast.

## Sources

Awarded after reaching Rendezvous 5 during the Beachhead expedition. it is necessary to complete the Historiography Lesson.  After acquiring the Historiographical Dosimeter, interact with three plaques or monoliths, and select "Take dosimeter reading for Nada". Once three readings are collected and the award is unlocked, then proceed to the [[Space Anomaly]] and it will be possible to redeem 80 [[Quicksilver]] from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] to obtain a Myth Beacon.

## Usage

The Myth Beacon must be used to complete *Echo Through the Ages* task in the Beachhead expedition.  This is done by reaching the [[Boundary Failure]] at latitude/longitude +60.78, +119.42 on [[Nessie Q8]] and deploying the Myth Beacon.

## Additional Information

No longer functions in any use as of the [[Remnant]] release.
