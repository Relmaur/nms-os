# Hyaline Brain

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Sentience Vessel
- **Total Value:** 35,000.0
- **Updated:** Orbital

**Hyaline Brain** is a component.

## Summary

**Hyaline Brain** is a component which, when purified into a Harmonic Brain at a Monolith, is used to make a crashed Sentinel Interceptor claimable.
When the player has 3 Radiant Shards and 1 Inverted Mirror (collected from a Dissonance Resonator) in their inventory the Hyaline Brain can be probed in the inventory (by pressing E on PC) revealing the location of the closest Monolith.

## Game Description

The central processing unit of a Sentinel interceptor ship. The circuitry writhes microscopically, constantly rearranging itself as it scans for anomalies.

A vital component for any entity wishing to construct their own Pilot Interface for a Sentinel Interceptor.

Status: VOLATILE

## Sources

- Loot from crashed Sentinel Interceptors (100%).
- Loot from destroying enemy sentinel interceptors in space (15%)

## Use

Hyaline Brain is used in the repair of Sentinel Interceptors.

### Crafting

**Hyaline Brain** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Hyaline Brain** is used as an ingredient for refining the following products using a Refiner:

- Nanite Cluster -- Hyaline Brain x1 → Nanite Cluster x230 (*"Sentience Extraction"*, 12.8 sec./unit output)

### Cooking

**Hyaline Brain** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Hyaline Brain can be transformed into a Harmonic Brain at a Monolith.
