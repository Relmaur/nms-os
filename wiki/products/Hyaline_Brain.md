# Hyaline Brain

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Sentience Vessel
- **Category:** Component
- **Value:** 35,000.0
- **Added in:** Orbital

**Hyaline Brain** is a component.

## Summary

**Hyaline Brain** is a [[component]] which, when purified into a Harmonic Brain at a Monolith, is used to make a crashed Sentinel Interceptor claimable.
When the player has 3 [[Radiant Shard|Radiant Shards]] and 1 [[Inverted Mirror]] (collected from a [[Dissonance Resonator]]) in their inventory the Hyaline Brain can be probed in the inventory (by pressing E on PC) revealing the location of the closest Monolith.

## Game Description

The central processing unit of a Sentinel interceptor ship. The circuitry writhes microscopically, constantly rearranging itself as it scans for anomalies.

A vital component for any entity wishing to construct their own Pilot Interface for a Sentinel Interceptor.

Status: VOLATILE

## Sources

- Loot from crashed [[Sentinel Interceptor|Sentinel Interceptors]] (100%).
- Loot from destroying enemy sentinel interceptors in space (15%)

## Use

Hyaline Brain is used in the repair of [[Interceptor (Starship)|Sentinel Interceptors]].

## Additional Information

Hyaline Brain can be transformed into a [[Harmonic Brain]] at a [[Monolith]].
