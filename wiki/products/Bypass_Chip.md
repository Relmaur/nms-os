# Bypass Chip

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** System Hacking Device
- **Value:** 3575
- **Description:** used to gain access to encrypted terminals and facilities.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Bypass Chip** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] for hacking terminals.

## Summary

The **Bypass Chip** is used to hack encrypted terminals. It is a single-use device, so it must be crafted when needed. Commonly used to recall a [[starship]].

## Game description

A single-use device that can be used to gain access to encrypted terminals and facilities. Commonly used to hijack [[Landing Pad]] facilities at alien outposts. Perhaps illegal.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 10 × [[Iron]] + 10 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Bypass Chip

## Additional information

- It was first seen on pre-release planet [[Balari V]] where the player hacks a Landing Pad on a [[Korvax]] building, summoning his ship to the building without having to search for it.
- Despite the alleged illegality there are no adverse consequences to using this item.
