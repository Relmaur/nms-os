# AtlasPass V4

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Device
- **Type:** Key
- **Description:** physical cardboard device
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **AtlasPass V4** is a physical cardboard device.

## Summary

The **AtlasPass V4** is an actual physical cardboard device created by Hello Games as part of the Waking Titan ARG.

Several thousand were shipped to selected members that participated in Waking Titan.

There are a variety of versions (see gallery below) with 16-A and L-15 appearing to be the most common.

There was also an easter egg item called AtlasPass V4 during [[Expedition 23: Our Journey Continues]] in 2026. It appeared as a regular AtlasPass item but it said V4. It was used during the Waking Titan milestone as a callback.

## Acquiring

Shipped from [[Hello Games]] to selected members of the public worldwide.
