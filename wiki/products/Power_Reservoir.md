# Power Reservoir

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Energy Recharge Cell
- **Description:** High capacity power gel source
- **Value:** 515.6
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Power Reservoir** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Power Reservoir** is a [[Product]] used to energize the [[Multi-tool]], [[Starship]] [[weapon|weapons]] and [[life support]].

## Game Description

High capacity power gel source. A hugely efficient device that optimises power storage within exosuit and starship inventories. Used in a wide variety of weapon and life support systems.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Plutonium]] + 200 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Power Reservoir

## Usage

It is used to recharge the following items:
- [[Life support]] system by 100%
- [[Mining Beam]] by 50%
- [[Boltcaster]] by 50%
- [[Phase Beam]] by 10%

## Additional information

- Can be bought and sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
