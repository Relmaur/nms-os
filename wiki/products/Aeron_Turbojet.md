# Aeron Turbojet

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Exosuit Visual Enhancement
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Aeron Turbojet** is a customisation item.

## Summary

**Aeron Turbojet** is a type of appearance that can be modify at a [[Appearance Modifier]]. This type of appearance is a replacement of the [[Jetpack]] but sightly unique. It has a [[Sentinel]] [[scanner]] at the back of the appearance, although the original scanner icon is normal.

## Game Description

An exclusive Exosuit Appearance Override.

A titanium flightpack, assembled from [[technology]] stolen from the Sentinel horde. Its engines are precooled with a constant flow of [[pugneum]] and [[nitrogen]], ensuring fuel-efficient and deadly silent operation.

Warning: do not unseal pugneum containment without protective gear.

Transform the appearance of your [[Exosuit]] at an Appearance Modifier.

## Source

Can be unlocked at a [[Harmonic Camp]].
