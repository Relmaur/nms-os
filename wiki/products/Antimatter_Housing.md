# Antimatter Housing

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 6,500.0
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Antimatter Housing** is a component.

## Summary

**Antimatter Housing** is a [[component]] needed to craft a [[Warp Cell]].

## In-game description

Secure antimatter containment device, required before Antimatter can be safely secured as Warp Cells.

Rebound Oxygen forms a protective gas pocket, preventing the antimatter from reacting with the surrounding casing.

## Source

**Antimatter Housing** can be purchased at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and Planetary [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]].

This item has a 10% chance to drop out of barrel-type cargo.   The [[AtlasPass v1]] access card is required to open these.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 30 × [[Oxygen]] + 50 × [[Ferrite Dust]] → 1 × Antimatter Housing
