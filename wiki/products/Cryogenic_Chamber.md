# Cryogenic Chamber

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Crafted Product
- **Value:** 3,800,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Cryogenic Chamber** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Cryogenic Chamber** is a [[tradeable]] that can be crafted or purchased.

## Game description

A low temperature chamber, designed to prevent the natural decay of organic matter.

Crafted from Living Glass and a Cryo-Pump.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Living Glass]] + 1 × [[Cryo-Pump]] → 1 × Cryogenic Chamber

Cryogenic Chamber can be purchased and sold at [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] and NPCs for a profit.
