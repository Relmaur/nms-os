# Salvaged Scrap

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Salvaged Scrap
- **Value:** 2,759,013.0
- **Used for:** Trading, Trading (High Value)
- **Added in:** Omega

**Salvaged Scrap** is a type of [[Artifact]].

## Summary

**Salvaged Scrap** is one of several types of [[Artifact|Artifacts]] and it is a reward for destroying the [[Salvage Container]].

The number of [[Protective Casing|Protective Casings]] interconnected with a Salvage Container defines the rarity of the Salvaged Scrap: 2 → Common, 3 → Uncommon, 5 → Rare.
