# AtlasPass v3

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Constructed Technology
- **Type:** Access Card
- **Value:** 0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**AtlasPass v3** is a Constructed Technology.

## Summary

**AtlasPass v3** is a [[Constructed Technology]] and it works as a key, the player crafts it once and it allows infinite unlocks. It unlocks doors located in [[space station|space stations]].

It can also unlock objects that require AtlasPass v1 or AtlasPass v2, which can then be discarded by the player.

## Game description

Security and permissions pass granted to specific travellers by many primary galactic species.

Provides access to high level restricted areas and outpost systems.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 200 × [[Emeril]] + 1 × [[Microprocessor]] → 1 × AtlasPass v3
