# Polychromatic Zirconium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Minerals)
- **Value:** 30,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Polychromatic Zirconium** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Polychromatic Zirconium** is a 4th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A sack full of polychromatic zirconium crystals.

These crystals produce lenses of dazzling colour and near-indestructible hardness. Highly valuable in manufacturing economies.

## Source

Polychromatic Zirconium can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Mining Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, medium-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Polychromatic Zirconium can be sold for a profit in any [[Manufacturing Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
