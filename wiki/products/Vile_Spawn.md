# Vile Spawn

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Hungering Embryo
- **Value:** 16.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Vile Spawn** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Vile Spawn** is a curiosity that can be obtained by harvesting the drops from hungering tendrils after beating Titan Worms.

## Game Description

The fragile embryo of a Hungering Tendril, still dripping with mucal butter. It wails and recoils when touched.

May be extracted from embryotic sacs with the aid of a [[Visceral Synthesiser]].

## Additional Information

Even though the description states that a visceral synthesiser is needed, this is not the case anymore.
