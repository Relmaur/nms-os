# Geode (land)

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Valuable Mineral Deposit
- **Value:** 3,280
- **Added in:** Companions

**Geode** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Geode** is a [[Curiosity|curiosity]] and one of the [[Curiosity|valuable mineral deposits]]. It can be cracked open to gain varying amounts of ferrite elements with a rare chance for Glowing Minerals.

| Material | Prob | Avg | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [[Ferrite Dust]] | 65.5% | 94.7 | 30-79 |
| [[Pure Ferrite]] | 27.2% | 139.6 | 103-171 |
| [[Magnetised Ferrite]] | 6.9% | 136.1 | 116-176 |
| [[Glowing Mineral]] | 0.4% | 1 | 1 |

## Additional information

Geodes are worth 3,280  Units, as is. Opening them averages a value of 2,710.4  Units. Even though each Geode could potentially hold a Glowing Mineral which could in turn hold a valuable material, over time opening Geodes will actually be worth less than just selling each Geode.
