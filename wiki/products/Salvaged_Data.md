# Salvaged Data

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Constructed Technology
- **Type:** Salvaged Technology
- **Value:** 52,000.0
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Salvaged Data** is a Constructed Technology.

## Summary

**Salvaged Data** is a [[Constructed Technology]] that can be exchanged at the [[Space Anomaly]] or [[Construction Research Unit]] for various [[base building]] [[blueprint|blueprints]].

## Game description

The salvageable heart of some ancient technology. Unreadable in its current form.

These databanks can be spent at a Construction Research Unit and used to research new Construction Blueprints.

## Sources

- Found in [[Buried Technology Module|Buried Technology Modules]] and each of them rewards the player 2-4 Salvaged Data1.
- Can be purchased as a component from some [[Minor Settlement|Minor Settlements]].
- Storage containers in [[Crashed Freighter|Crashed Freighters]]. (19%)
- Companions can dig it up. (8%)
- Gift from the [[Merchants Guild]] [[Guild Envoy|envoy]]. (14%)
- [[Nexus]] missions (8%)
- [[Mission Board]] missions (~4%)
- Missions from the bounty master in outlaw systems. (2%)
- From the [[Base Computer Archives]] mission.

1 It can't be found on dead planets or in uncharted systems.
