# Life Support Gel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Portable Life Support Power
- **Value:** 200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Life Support Gel** is a consumable [[product]].

## Game Description

Emergency [[life support]] fuel.

Synthesised from rocket fuel crystals and [[carbon]], this gel offers a temporary boost to life support systems. Long-term impact on user's lungs is currently unknown.

## Source

- **Crafting**: 1 × [[Di-hydrogen Jelly]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Life Support Gel

- **Refiner** · *Advanced Oxygen Capture* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Oxygen]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 10 × [[Di-hydrogen]] → 1 × Life Support Gel

- Can always be bought from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] or from pilot NPCs on [[Space Station|Space Stations]] (only on "Goods Availability->Abundant" setting).

## Additional information

- Provides 100% charge to Life Support when consumed. Despite the tooltip, it does not seem to damage the user's lungs in the long term.
