# Solar Mirror

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 6,150.0
- **Added in:** Expeditions

**Solar Mirror** is a component.

## Summary

**Solar Mirror** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

A specialist optical component. Employing an impossibly polished surface, this mirror is able to selectively filter different wavelengths of light, changing its reflective pattern based on nearby magnetic fields.

Used in the creation of a wide range of advanced technologies.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Available from some [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] for 5000+ units.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Gold]] + 30 × [[Silver]] + 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Solar Mirror
