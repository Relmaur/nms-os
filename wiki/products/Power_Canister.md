# Power Canister

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Energy Recharge Cell
- **Value:** 343.8
- **Description:** Increased capacity power gel source
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Power Canister** is a product.

## Summary

**Power Canister** is a [[Product]] used to energize the [[Multi-tool]], [[Starship]] [[weapon|weapons]] and [[life support]].

## Game desscription

Increased capacity power gel source.
Promotes efficient power storage within [[exosuit]] and starship inventories.
Used in a wide variety of weapon and life support systems.

## Source

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 75 × [[Plutonium]] + 150 × [[Carbon]] → 1 × Power Canister

## Usage

It is used to recharge the following items:
- [[Life support]] system by 75%
- [[Mining Beam]] by 37%
- [[Boltcaster]] by 37%
- [[Phase Beam]] by 7%

## Additional information

- Can be bought and sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].

## Release history

- [[Release]] - Added as a product.
- [[Foundation (Update)]] - Blueprint changed by adding [[plutonium]] and tripled the amount of [[carbon]], making energy source significantly more expensive to build.
- [[NEXT]] - Removed from the game.
