# Vigilant Stone

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Unblinking Eye
- **Value:** 102,000
- **Added in:** Relics

## Summary

**Vigilant Stone** is a [[tradeable]].

## Game description

An eye of living stone: waiting, watching. A relic left by a slumbering guardian, keeping sentry over ancient creature burial grounds.

Warning: Disturbing the eye may disturb the slumber of living stone guardians. Ensure re-animated guardians are defeated before storing this relic in the home.

## Sources

- On planets with Ancient Bones, near fossils. Shows up on analysis visor.

## Additional information

- When you find a vigilant stone, be prepared to fight, run, or have your ship near you, as the Stone Guardian can be a difficult fight.
- One or two Stone Guardians will appear when disturbing the Vigilant Stones.
- Defeating each Stone Guardian will grant 20 [[Polished Stone]].
