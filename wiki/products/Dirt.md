# Dirt

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Minerals)
- **Value:** 1,000.0
- **Description:** Several tonnes of dirt
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Dirt** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Dirt** is a 1st tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Several tonnes of dirt.

Leftover dirt from mining operations. Residual metals can be extracted by specialists in manufacturing economies with the time and equipment to sift through it.

## Source

Dirt can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all [[Mining Economy]] [[star system|star systems]].

## Usage

Dirt can be sold for a profit in any [[Manufacturing Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]], but can be used in [[Planetary Settlement|upgrading agricultural buildings]] in their Settlements.
