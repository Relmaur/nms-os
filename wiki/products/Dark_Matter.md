# Dark Matter

## Quick Facts

**Dark Matter** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Dark Matter** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 4th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

An Atlas Seed shell encompassing a void element harvested from the collapse state of the last universe. Toxic if ingested.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Cassini]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Noospheric Orb]] + 5 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 1 × Dark Matter
