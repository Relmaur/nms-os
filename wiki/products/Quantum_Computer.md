# Quantum Computer

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 4,200.0
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Quantum Computer** is a component.

## Summary

**Quantum Computer** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

An extremely fast advanced processing unit. Allows real-time solving of otherwise impossible calculations. However, protection against decoherence is not guaranteed.

Used in the creation of a wide range of advanced technologies.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Available from some [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] for 5000+ units
- Can be produced from Salvaged Glass.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Microprocessor]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Quantum Computer

## Additional information

This component is used for [[Planetary Settlement|Planetary Settlements]].
