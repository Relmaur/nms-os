# Geodesite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Enriched Alloy Metal
- **Value:** 150,000.0
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Geodesite** is a tradeable.

## Game description

**Geodesite** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

Advanced and durable alloy primarily used in starship hull construction and deep-space exploration drone technology.

Crafted from Herox, Dirty Bronze and Lemmium.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Dirty Bronze]] + 1 × [[Herox]] + 1 × [[Lemmium]] → 1 × Geodesite
