# Microprocessor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 2,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Microprocessor** is a component.

## Summary

**Microprocessor** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

A compact, low-heat microchip. Assembled from processed Chromatic Metal, created from one of several specialised terrain metals.

Used in the creation of a wide range of advanced technologies.

## Source

- Blueprint can be awarded in the [[Scientific Research]] mission, found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 1 × [[Carbon Nanotubes]] → 1 × Microprocessor

Also available from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
