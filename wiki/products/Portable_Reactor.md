# Portable Reactor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable

- **Value:** 4,200,000.0

## Summary

**Portable Reactor** is a [[tradeable]] that can be crafted or purchased.

## Game description

A miniature self-controlled nuclear reactor. Generates sub-stellar levels of energy in a hassle-free, widely compatible fuel module.

Crafted from Liquid Explosives and Fusion Accelerant.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Liquid Explosive]] + 1 × [[Fusion Accelerant]] → 1 × Portable Reactor
