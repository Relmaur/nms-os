# Science Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** Research station for science projects
- **Added in:** Origins

The **Science Terminal** is an item used for research.

## Summary

[[Terminal|Terminals]] of various types can be crafted, each with their own special purposes. The Science Terminal is used for specialized research, and for the creation of science-related [[blueprint|blueprints]]. The blueprint for building the Science Terminal is received as part of the [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]] questline.

The [[blueprint|blueprints]] for the following items can be acquired at a Science Terminal based on specific quests from the [[Scientist]].

## Game description

A research station tailored to the needs of Scientists, and equipped for research and development of scientific blueprints.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] → 1 × Science Terminal

- **Dismantle** returns: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]]

## Additional information

- There appears to be a restriction that requires the terminal to be built on the top floor of a structure.  Once the terminal is built, another floor can be put on top of it.
