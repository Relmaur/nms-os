# Magnetic Resonator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Crafted Technology Component
- **Value:** 6,150.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Magnetic Resonator** is a component.

## Summary

**Magnetic Resonator** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

An electromagnetic frequency oscillator, able to function in a wide-range of conditions thanks to both ferrous and non-ferrous magnetic properties.

Used in the creation of a wide range of advanced technologies.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Available from some [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] for 5000+ units

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Magnetised Ferrite]] + 40 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Magnetic Resonator
