## Summary

**Noospheric Orb** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 3rd of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

An Atlas Seed capable of resonating on humanoid telepathic frequencies; may attempt to subsume any subordinate intellect in close proximity.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Rosetta]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Englobed Shade]] + 100 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Noospheric Orb
