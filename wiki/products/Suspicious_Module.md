# Suspicious Module

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Unknown Upgrade
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Suspicious Modules** are consumable products.

## Summary

**Suspicious Modules** appear as an Extremely Powerful (purple) icon with an X-like banner. They are black-market modifications of regular [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]].

They can greatly improve properties of the installed [[Technology|technologies]] in your [[exosuit]], [[multi-tool]], or [[starship]].

Found in [[Buried Cache|Buried Caches]], bought using [[Tainted Metal]] from [[Scrap Dealer|Scrap Dealers]], or from random starships encountered on planets.

- The stats for these types of modules are typically of a regular C-A class module.
- Some upgrades may exceed the stats of an S-class module.
- Some upgrades may have no stats at all. If so, it displays the following text: "UPGRADE_0_STAT_ILLEGAL_DESC"

## Usage

When applied, all Suspicious Modules share almost the same description.

"A suspicious **black-market modification** for the %SYSTEM%.

Bypassing nearly every galactic standard for workmanship and safety, this upgrade module affects %STATS%."

## Additional information

Suspicious Modules were initially priced at the Scrap Dealer for around **200-400** Tainted Metal. They can be sold to [[Technology Merchant|Technology Merchants]] for more than 500  Nanites which makes them a great source of nanites at all stages of the game.

With the [[NextGen]] update, this price dropped to a range of 50-170  Nanites.

Occasionally, a trader's ship may land near you on a planet, selling a variety of these modules with varying, undisplayed quantities.
