# AtlasPass v4

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Constructed Technology
- **Type:** Access Card
- **Value:** 2613
- **Added in:** Cosmos

**AtlasPass v4** is a Constructed Technology.

## Summary

**AtlasPass v4** is a [[Constructed Technology]] that allows the player to decode the Loop16 transmission. It is consumed upon decoding.

It cannot act as the other three AtlasPasses.

## Game description

Security and permissions pass granted to specific travellers by many primary galactic species.

Provides access to high level restricted areas and outpost systems.

## Source

- Found on a Monolith inside of Our Journey Continues.
