# Electron Vapour

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Technology component
- **Description:** Captured cloud of ionised electrons.
- **Value:** 4,950
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Electron Vapour** is a product.

## Summary

**Electron Vapour** is a [[Product]] used in the [[Crafting]] of various [[Technology|Technologies]].

It is the second-to-last ingredient needed to craft a [[Warp Cell]], and is needed to craft [[Antimatter]]. It can be crafted in your inventory or found in [[Cargo Drop|"Search" boxes]].

The [[British spelling]] of this phrase is used in the wiki.

## In-game description

Captured cloud of ionised electrons. A fundamental component of many technologies, and often used in the creation of antimatter.

## Craft

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 1 × [[Suspension Fluid]] + 100 × [[Plutonium]] → 1 × Electron Vapour

## Use

Electron Vapour is an obsolete item and no longer has a purpose in the game.
