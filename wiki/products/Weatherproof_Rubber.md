# Weatherproof Rubber

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Updated:** Atlas Rises

**Weatherproof Rubber** is an advanced agricultural product. The product recipe is new as of the Atlas Rises update.

## Game description

An advanced organic material, this rubber takes advantage of the natural properties of its parent plant. It is highly water resistant, is not distorted by extremes of temperature, and smells only slightly of salt.

## Source

Weatherproof Rubber cannot be crafted.

Original formula:

Kelp Sac x50 → Weatherproof Rubber

## Use

### Crafting

**Weatherproof Rubber** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Weatherproof Rubber** is not used as an ingredient for refining using a Refiner.

### Cooking

**Weatherproof Rubber** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Dismantle

Weatherproof Rubber cannot be dismantled.

## Additional information

The Weatherproof Rubber blueprint is rewarded when you construct the Science Terminal and continue the Scientist quest line.

## Release history

- Atlas Rises - Added as a product.
- NEXT - Removed from game.
