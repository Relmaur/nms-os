# Weatherproof Rubber

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Weatherproof Rubber** is an advanced agricultural product. The product recipe is new as of the [[Atlas Rises]] update.

## Game description

An advanced organic material, this rubber takes advantage of the natural properties of its parent plant. It is highly water resistant, is not distorted by extremes of temperature, and smells only slightly of salt.

## Source

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Kelp Sac]] → 1 × Weatherproof Rubber

## Dismantle

Weatherproof Rubber cannot be dismantled.

## Additional information

The Weatherproof Rubber blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and continue the [[Scientist]] quest line.
