# Flesh Rope

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Intestinal Fragment
- **Value:** 16,016
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Flesh Rope** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Flesh Rope** is a curiosity that can be harvested from Titan Worms.

## Game Description

The coiled remains of a Hungering Tendril's digestive tract. Though its meaty walls still convulse, there is surely no way for a fresh tendril to regrow from these meagre scraps... Such specialist offal is rarely traded on the Galactic Exchange and is thus highly valuable.

## Consume

- Can not be consumed without a [[Nutrient Processor]].
-

## Source

Flesh rope can be obtained from worms in a Titan Worm Burrow, found on [[Biome Subtype - Infested|infested]] planets.

## Additional information

Flesh Rope is a rare material gathered by killing giant Titan Worms. Because these massive creatures are rarely spotted, and only for a few seconds when they do appear, this item is nearly impossible to gather outside of specific events, such as the Halloween Event in 2021, where the worms are far more common and can sometimes react to the player. It can be classified as an event material.

This Material is easily acquired on Terrorsphere planets, Titan Worm Burrows appear commonly on this planet type.
