# Herox

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Alloy Metal
- **Value:** 25,000.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Upgrading, Trading
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Herox** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Herox** is a [[tradeable]] that can be [[Crafting|crafted]], found in containers or purchased.

## Game Description

Combined neutral [[alloy]] with uses that range from pharmacology all the way to heavy industry. Crafted from [[Ionised Cobalt]] and [[Ammonia]].

Forms the backbone of galactic alloy trading.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]
- Can be salvaged from destroyed [[pirate]] ships.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Ammonia]] + 50 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Herox

- **Refiner** · *Alloy Latticing* · 45 s per unit: 30 × [[Ammonia]] + 20 × [[Tritium]] + 60 × [[Cobalt]] → 1 × Herox
