# Electrical Cloaking Unit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Power Distribution Module
- **Description:** Power Distribution Visibility Toggle System
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Electrical Cloaking Unit** is a base building constructed technology.

## Summary

**Electrical Cloaking Unit** is a [[base building]] [[product]] that toggles the visibility of connecting lines which indicate electrical wiring when turned on/off.

## Game Description

Device to enable a wireless aesthetic in habitable bases.

Constructing the  Electrical Cloaking Unit within a powered base will reduce the visual prominence of all local [[Electrical Wiring]]. Hidden wires will continue to supply electricty, provided they are sufficiently powered.

## Build

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Metal Plating]] + 30 × [[Gold]] + 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Electrical Cloaking Unit

Blueprint cost is 1 [[Salvaged Data]].
