# AquaSphere

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Trade Commodity
- **Value:** 23,375.0
- **Description:** Used in crafting the Homingbolt Adaptor
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**AquaSphere** is a trade commodity.

## Summary

**AquaSphere** is a rare [[trade commodity]] item.

## Game description

Extremely valuable curiosity can sometimes be found in the seas and lakes of some planetary systems.

Their initial origin is unknown.

## Source

AquaSpheres can be found among some underwater plants, or hidden in the centre of some underwater boulders. When they are present and the scanner is used, they are marked with a purple icon like other [[Neutral/Exotic elements|exotic]] resources.

## Use

AquaSpheres are an obsolete product and no longer have a purpose in the game.
