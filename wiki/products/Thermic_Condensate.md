# Thermic Condensate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Enhanced Gas Product
- **Value:** 50,000.0
- **Description:** Refined gas product
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Thermic Condensate** is a tradeable item.

## Summary

**Thermic Condensate** is a [[tradeable]] that can also be used to craft certain products.

## Game description

A refined product, holding the energy potential of heated atmospheric gas in a stable state until required.

Crafted from [[Sulphurine]] and [[Condensed Carbon]].

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be found in planetary [[depot|depots]].
- Can be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 250 × [[Sulphurine]] + 50 × [[Condensed Carbon]] → 1 × Thermic Condensate

- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Sulphurine]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 5 × [[Chlorine]] → 1 × Thermic Condensate
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Sulphurine]] + 20 × [[Carbon]] + 10 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Thermic Condensate
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Sulphurine]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 5 × [[Chlorine]] → 1 × Thermic Condensate
- **Refiner** · *High-Speed Sublimation* · 45 s per unit: 100 × [[Sulphurine]] + 10 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 10 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Thermic Condensate

## Additional information

**Thermic Condensate** can no longer be purchased from [[Galactic trade terminal|Galactic trade terminals]] and NPCs.
