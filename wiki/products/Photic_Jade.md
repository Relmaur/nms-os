# Photic Jade

## Summary

**Photic Jade** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 6th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

This irreplaceable Atlas Seed is a form of matter-adapted photons coalesced into physical solidity by unknown means.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Dawn]] or above is required.
