# Shielding Plate

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Defensive Energy Source
- **Value:** 687.5
- **Description:** Increased capacity energy storage unit. Promotes efficient energy storage within exosuit and starship inventories. Used in Hazard Protection and Shield systems.
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Shielding Plate** is a [[Product]] used to energize [[Deflector Shield]], Exosuit Hazard Protection or Hazard Protection Units.

## Summary

Efficient storage units for various power uses. They do not stack. Since it's partially built with [[Iron]], which is the only oxide element that cannot be used to recharge devices, the Shielding line is a fairly cheap and reliable method to recharge your [[Hazard Protection|Exosuit Hazard Protection]], [[Hazard Protection Unit|Hazard Protection Units]] and [[Deflector Shield]].

## Game description

Increased capacity energy storage unit. Promotes efficient energy storage within exosuit and starship inventories. Used in Hazard Protection and Shield systems.

## Build

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 100 × [[Iron]] + 20 × [[Platinum]] → 1 × Shielding Plate

## Usage

Shielding Plates can be used to recharge the following items:
- Exosuit Hazard Protection by ??%
- Hazard Protection Units by 93%
- Deflector Shield by 37%

- Can be bought and sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
