# Glass

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 200.0
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Glass** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Glass** is a [[tradeable]] item that can be used in construction and [[base building]]. It can also be used for decoration and room parts other than windows.

## Game description

Shatter-proof, scratch-resistant glass with a self-cleaning, UV-cured coating, for general construction use.

Refined from Silicate powder. Extract silicate from the earth with a [[Terrain Manipulator]].

Can be crafted from Frost Crystals extracted from plants on frozen worlds.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Glass

- **Refiner** · *Salt Crystal Polishing* · 10 s per unit: 50 × [[Cyto-Phosphate]] + 50 × [[Salt]] → 1 × Glass
- **Refiner** · *Silicate Forging* · 5 s per unit: 40 × [[Silicate Powder]] → 1 × Glass
- **Refiner** · *Polish Crystals* · 30 s per unit: 40 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Glass

## Addtional information

The [[blueprint]] for glass is obtained from the [[Overseer (NPC)]] at the beginning of their quest line.
