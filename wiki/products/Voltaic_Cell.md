# Voltaic Cell

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Component
- **Description:** Compact power source
- **Value:** 1547.0
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Voltaic Cell** is a tech item used as a power source.

## Game description

A compact but powerful battery. A vital component for the construction of technologies that require an internal power source.

## Source

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 50 × [[Marrow Bulb]] → 1 × Voltaic Cell

Voltaic Cells cannot be dismantled.

## Additional information

- The Voltaic Cell blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and begin the [[Scientist]] quest line. The Voltaic Cell is also used to acquire the blueprint for the [[Weapons Terminal]].
- To turn in the Marrow Bulbs for the quest, make sure it is the active quest or the scientist will not accept your Marrow Bulbs
