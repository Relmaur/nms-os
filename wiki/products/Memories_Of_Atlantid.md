# Memories Of Atlantid

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Forgotten Void Data
- **Value:** 1,919
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Memories Of Atlantid** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Memories Of Atlantid** is a curiosity that is used within the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] questline.

## Game description

The howling of an advanced intelligence banished to oblivion, a reverberation in sentience and crystal.

Each memory in this data packet is an entire reality in itself, bearing the fingerprint of countless lives. The Atlas will recognise this seed.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 3 × [[Echo Seed]] + 1 × [[Quantum Computer]] → 1 × Memories Of Atlantid

## Use

First used to initiate a scan for the [[Divergence Fragment]]'s wavelength when using the [[Pulse Drive]]. Then inserted into the [[Atlas Interface]] during the [[In Stellar Multitudes]] questline.
