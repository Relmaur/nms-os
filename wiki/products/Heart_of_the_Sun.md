# Heart of the Sun

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Unique valuable curiosity
- **Value:** 1,000.0
- **Added in:** Visions

**Heart of the Sun** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Heart of the Sun** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the last of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission.

## Game description

An Atlas Seed containing a time-locked quantum cilia suspended in a photonic shell. No others are known to exist.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Magellan]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Modified Quanta]] + 100 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Heart of the Sun

## Use

Required to complete [[The Atlas Path|the Final Atlas Interface]] of the The Atlas Path story line of missions.
