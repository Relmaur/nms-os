# Hex Core

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Contained Reality Glitch
- **Value:** 16.0
- **Added in:** Interceptor

**Hex Core** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Hex Core** is a [[curiosity]].

## Game description

This strange energy orb constantly vibrates, emitting a low-frequency drone. Slight variations in the pattern repeat over and over, almost as if it is attempting to relay a message.

## Sources

- 1-3 Hex Cores rewarded for killing [[Anomalous]] [[fauna]] on specific planets with [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] biome. (14%)
- Can be received from an Anomaly Iteration as a gift
- Randomly from Black Market Vendor
- Randomly from [[Suspicious Packet (Goods)]] (3%)

## Additional information

- During the [[Community Research (week 1)]] mission it could be obtained from [[Buried Technology Module|Buried Technology Modules]] and could be turned in for [[Quicksilver]] by contacting [[Specialist Polo]].
- Other than for resale, there are no uses for Hex Cores.
