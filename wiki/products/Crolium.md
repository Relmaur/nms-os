# Crolium

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Neutral Metal Alloy
- **Total Value:** 35,378.8
- **Updated:** Pathfinder

**Crolium** is an alloy.

## Summary

**Crolium** is a product alloy that is used for construction and trading.

## Game description

Crolium is a highly resistant and durable alloy primarily used in starship hull construction and deep-space exploration drone technology. The crolium trading market is a strong force within the overall Galactic Trading Network.

## Source

Crolium cannot be crafted.

Original formula:

Iridium x100 + Chrysonite x80 → Crolium

Before being removed from the game it could be salvaged from destroyed pirate ships and chests on planets.
