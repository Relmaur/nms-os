# Crystallised Heart

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Corrupted Aeronic Core
- **Category:** Component
- **Used for:** Crafting, Upgrading
- **Value:** 37,000.0
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Crystallised Heart** is a component.

## Summary

**Crystallised Heart** is a [[component]] that is used for crafting.

## Game Description

The functional core of a Sentinel quadruped unit, but warped beyond all recognition. The entire machine is infested with a crystalline material that pulses wildy upon inspection.

A close analysis reveals the machinery itself appears to have been reconfigured, stretching and warping into a whole new form, writhing in the presence of these radiant shards of glass.

## Sources

Blue [[Combat Supplies]] loot from a [[Corrupted Sentinel Quadruped]] spider on planets with corrupted Sentinels.

## Use

**Crystallised Heart** is used in the repair of [[Multi-Tool Catalogue - Sentinel|Sentinel multi-tools]] (alternatively a [[Repair Kit]] may be applied).
