# Warp Hypercore

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Charging Unit
- **Value:** 46.750
- **Added in:** Endurance

The **Warp Hypercore** is a [[consumable]] in *[[No Man's Sky]]*.

## Summary

Warp Hypercores are a fuel source for a [[starship|Starship's]] [[Hyperdrive]]. However, unlike [[Warp Cell|Warp Cells]], a single Warp Hypercore can fully charge a Hyperdrive by itself. However, as they require [[Storm Crystal|Storm Crystals]] to craft, they can be difficult to obtain en-masse.

## Game description

A high-capacity antimatter-based fuel, this cell harnesses an internal black hole generator to power starship hyperdrives across multiple warp jumps.

Cell walls are specially designed to reflect any reality paradoxes back towards the heart of the unit, where they generate additional power.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Occasionally shows up as a reward for a [[The Nexus|Nexus]] mission.
- Can be awarded by [[Guild Envoy|Guild Envoys]].

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Antimatter]] + 1 × [[Storm Crystal]] → 1 × Warp Hypercore
