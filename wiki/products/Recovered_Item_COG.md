## Summary

**Recovered Item (COG)** is a procedural item, specifically a **Recovered Item**.

## Game Description

A damaged technology component, ejected from its machine and discarded by its owner.

Worthless, except as scrap. Select in the Inventory () and use  to break it down into useful materials.

## Source

Can be obtained by opening a [[Buried Cache]].
