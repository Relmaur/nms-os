# Hot Ice

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Manufactured Gas Product
- **Value:** 320,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Hot Ice** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Hot Ice** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

A paradoxical material, made less mysterious by its relative ease of manufacture.

Crafted from Enriched Carbon and Nitrogen Salt.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Enriched Carbon]] + 1 × [[Nitrogen Salt]] → 1 × Hot Ice

## Release history

- [[Atlas Rises]] - Added as a product.
- [[NEXT]] - Blueprint changed, formerly used Enriched Carbon and [[Ammonium Salt]], value was 400,000. Hot Ice can no longer be purchased from [[Galactic trade terminal|Galactic trade terminals]] and NPCs.
- [[Prisms]] - Added Synthesis Laboratory as a blueprint source.
