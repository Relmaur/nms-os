# Humming Sac

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Container
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Updated:** Frontiers

Albumen Pearls are found in Humming Sacs

**Humming Sac** is a container.

## Summary

**Humming Sac** can be found in caves, it has the appearance of an egg and it contains an Albumen Pearl. It is unknown if the sac is of plant, animal, or mineral origin.

Gathering pearls from this source will not alarm sentinels, as opposed to gathering pearls from the pea-shaped sentient plants.

The Humming sac has been confused with Whispering eggs, But these are completely different. If you see a Humming sac, collect the contents; don't pass it up!

The Analysis Visor will reveal these, and their location will be indicated with icon.
