# Weapons Terminal

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Terminal
- **Description:** Research station for armourers
- **Added in:** Orbital

The **Weapons Terminal** is a terminal.

## Summary

The **Weapons Terminal** is used for weaponry and explosives. It is used by a hired NPC known as an [[Armourer]]. These NPCs can be recruited in [[space station|space stations]] within [[Vy'keen]] controlled systems.

The [[blueprint|blueprints]] for the following items can be acquired at a Weapons Terminal based on specific quests from the Armourer.

- [[Haz-Mat Gauntlet]]
- [[Cyclotron Ballista]]
- [[Advanced Mining Laser]]
- [[Positron Ejector]]
- [[Infra-Knife Accelerator]]

## Game description

A research station tailored to the needs of Armourers, and equipped for the development of munitions and explosives as well as selected civilian tech.

## Sources

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 40 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 25 × [[Pugneum]] → 1 × Weapons Terminal

- **Dismantle** returns: 20 × [[Chromatic Metal]] + 12 × [[Pugneum]]

## Additional information

- It can be built in a [[Cylindrical Room]] or a [[Square Room]].
- The multitool can be up to S class
- You can re-roll which multitool you get by changing systems. Can be done quickly with a freighter that has the Weapons Terminal built on it. By changing systems, you can improve the class by one level, as some systems will give you a slightly better multitool than others.
