## Game description

The final sputtering flame of a Traveller soul, bound in the remains of living creatures.

This identifying signature belonged to an intact, unfragmented Traveller, with equal parts of Royal, Sage, and Weaver. It will unlock the Launch Thrusters of the starship PlayerStarship.

## Sources

- Crafted using a [[Pilot Echo]] and 32 [[Mordite]] after crafting the three soul echoes. Only obtainable in Expedition 22.

## Use

Used for accessing the Launch Thrusters of the ship in Expedition 22.
