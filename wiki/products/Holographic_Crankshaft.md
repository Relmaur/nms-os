# Holographic Crankshaft

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Industrial)
- **Used for:** Trading
- **Value:** 30,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Holographic Crankshaft** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Holographic Crankshaft** is a 4th tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Packed and separated data containers for holographic crankshafts.

Real crankshafts are frequently too large, noisy or hard-to-maintain for use in delicate machines. When faced with such a situation, high-tech manufacturers turn to these holographic substitutes.

## Source

Holographic Crankshaft can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all high-wealth [[Manufacturing Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, medium-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Holographic Crankshaft can be sold for a profit in any [[Technology Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
