# Gek Charm

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Trade Commodity
- **Description:** Gek good luck charm
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Gek Charm** is a trade commodity.

## Summary

**Gek Charm** is a [[trade commodity]] that according to game lore is a good luck charm.

## Game Description

Good luck charm carried by many galactic traders.

## Crafting

**NOTE**: In the early eras of the game, a Gek Charm was used to acquire the [[blueprint]] for the [[Agricultural Terminal]]. Once this was in hand, Iron was used to complete construction.

## Use

Gek Charm is an obsolete product and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

- Can be purchased at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]].
- Can be found from [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Containers]] on planets.
- Can be offered to the Gek as a gift and in return, you gain 1 reputation point.

If you are in good standing with the [[Gek]] race this item can be traded for a "gift" while talking to traders. Gifts seem to be various upgrades.
