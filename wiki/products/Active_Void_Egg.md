# Active Void Egg

- **Value:** 1616.0

## Summary

**Active Void Egg** is a [[curiosity]].

## Game description

An anomalous egg that has been activated as the first step in the [[Starbirth]] mission. Since the encounter with the bizarre deep-space 'creature', its strange music has grown more active, more agitated.

## Sources

The Active Void Egg can only be activated once a [[Void Egg]] is in the players inventory, and the player leaves a [[Space Station]] or [[Anomaly]] and uses the pulse drive within a [[Star system]]. The player will be notified to drop out of Pulse and then will encounter a deep-space creature which then activates the egg.

## Additional Information

The activated void egg remains in the player's inventory and should remain there through the Starbirth mission.
