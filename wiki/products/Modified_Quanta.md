## Summary

**Modified Quanta** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 9th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

This one-of-a-kind Atlas Seed holds an aesthetically pleasing mathematical construct salvaged from the simulation of an alternate reality.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]].  Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Viking]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Novae Reclaiment]] + 100 × [[Indium]] → 1 × Modified Quanta
