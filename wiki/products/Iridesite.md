# Iridesite

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Enriched Alloy Metal
- **Value:** 150,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Iridesite** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Iridesite** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

Advanced light metal alloy traded throughout the galactic Outer Edge. Often used in the interiors of trading [[Starship Catalogue - Shuttle|shuttles]] to reduce weight as they ascend from planets with high gravitational fields.

Crafted from Aronium, Magno-Gold and Grantine.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Aronium]] + 1 × [[Magno-Gold]] + 1 × [[Grantine]] → 1 × Iridesite
