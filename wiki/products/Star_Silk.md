# Star Silk

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Beacon

**Star Silk** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Star Silk** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game description

Several spools of star silk.

Star silk is woven on enormous solar looms in orbit around trading planets. Cosmic ray impact triggers the pattern of the weave, which can then be used in advanced material processing.

## Economy

Star Silk can be purchased in Trading (Green) economy systems, marked by a Stopwatch icon:
- Mercantile
- Shipping

It is in high demand (higher sell value) in Advanced Materials (Purple) economy systems that are marked with a DNA Symbol :
- Material Fusion
- Metal Processing
- Ore Processing
- Alchemical

## Source

Star Silk can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Trading Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Use

Star Silk can be sold for a profit in any [[Advanced Materials Economy]] star system.

Star Silk is used to craft the Classic Armchair which is available from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] aboard the [[Space Anomaly]].

It is used as an upgrade material for some [[Planetary Settlement]] buildings.
