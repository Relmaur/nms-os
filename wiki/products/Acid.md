# Acid

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 188,000.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Acid** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Acid** is a [[tradeable]] and one of the [[Tradeable|advanced agricultural products]].

## Game description

Dilute acid for high current generation in diverse power cells.

Crafted from Mordite and Fungal Mould.

## Sources

- Blueprint can be awarded by completing a certain stage of [[Scientific Research]] mission.
- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 25 × [[Mordite]] + 600 × [[Fungal Mould]] → 1 × Acid

## Additional information

- Acid is tied with [[Dirt]] and [[Iron]] for the shortest item name in the current version of No Man’s Sky, with 4 characters.
