# Ammonium Salt

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Trade Commodity

**Ammonium Salt** is a [[trade commodity]] item.

## Game description

A refined product, this is a powerful organic fertiliser in an easily distributed form. Crafted from [[Nitrogen]] and [[Carbon]].

Ammonium Salt can also be purchased and sold at [[Galactic trade terminal|Galactic trade terminals]] and NPCs for a profit.
