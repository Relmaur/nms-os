# Superconductor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Manufactured Gas Product
- **Value:** 1,500,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Superconductor** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Superconductor** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## In-game description

An advanced conductive material, the superconductor further enriches the field-warping power of the Semiconductor.

Crafted from Enriched Carbon and Semiconductors.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Enriched Carbon]] + 1 × [[Semiconductor]] → 1 × Superconductor
