# Grantine

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Grantine** is a [[tradeable]].

## Game description

Soft workable [[alloy]] favored by artisans throughout the known galaxy. Crafted from Ionised Cobalt and Dioxite.

A popular trading good, with many galactic shipping paths devoted to its sale and transport.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] + 50 × [[Dioxite]] → 1 × Grantine
