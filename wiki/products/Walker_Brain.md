# Walker Brain

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Anomalous Material
- **Total Value:** 35,000.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Upgrading
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Walker Brain** is a component.

## Summary

**Walker Brain** is a component that is used for crafting.

## Game Description

Shifting nanite clusters sewn together with a pugneum filament, this circuit is painfully hot to the touch.

A sinister purple light leaks from deep within its wiring, changing in intensity as it watches its holder.

## Sources

- Loot from a Sentinel Walker
- Mercenaries Guild Envoy
- Suspicious Packet (Goods)
- Mission Board missions
- Nexus missions
- Can be found on a robot at the Harmonic Scrap point of interest

## Use

**Walker Brain** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Conflict Scanner -- Walker Brain x1 + Wiring Loom x1 → Conflict Scanner
- Explosive Drones -- Walker Brain x1 + Gold x50 → Explosive Drones
- Liquidator Body -- Walker Brain x1 + Quantum Computer x1 + Wiring Loom x2 → Liquidator Body
