# Dawn's End

## Summary

**Dawn's End** is a [[curiosity]], also known as the 5th of 10 unique [[Atlas Seed|Atlas Seeds]].
It has an important part in [[The Atlas Path]] mission and it is worth 1,000  Units.

## Game description

Sculpted tau neutrino-boson matrix fixed within an irreplaceable Atlas Seed shell. May be semi-sentient.

## Source

The blueprint is given after talking to an [[Atlas Interface]]. Milestone [[Journey Milestones|Kepler]] or above is required.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Dark Matter]] + 25 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Dawn's End
