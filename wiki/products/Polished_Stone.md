# Polished Stone

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Recycled Minerals
- **Used for:** Building, Charging
- **Symbol:** R
- **Value:** 82
- **Added in:** Breach

**Polished Stone** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Polished Stone** is a [[curiosity]] that can be harvested from ruined structures found on ruined worlds.

## Game Description

The powdery remains of a highly polished mineral surface. It glitters as it catches the light, like fiery grains of sand.

Acquired by applying the Mining Laser to the structural remnants located on ruined worlds.

## Source

Can be harvested from [[Ruined Structure]] found on ruined worlds using a [[Mining Beam]]. These structures are exclusive to the [[Biome - The Reliquary|Reliquary]] planetary biome, and its 22 name variants.

Small amounts can also be gained from defeating Vigilant Stone guardians.

## Use

***Upgrades***

**Polished Stone** is used to upgrade buildings in your [[Planetary Settlement]].
