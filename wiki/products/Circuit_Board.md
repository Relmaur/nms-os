# Circuit Board

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 916,250.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Trading
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Circuit Board** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Circuit Board** is a [[tradeable]] and it is worth 916,250.0  Units each. Used for advanced crafting of Quantum Processors.

## Game description

Stackable, semi-flexible circuit board made from a highly durable synthetic polymer.

Crafted from Heat Capacitors and Poly Fibre.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Heat Capacitor]] + 1 × [[Poly Fibre]] → 1 × Circuit Board

## Additional information

- The blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and continue the [[Scientist]]'s [[Scientific Research]] quest line, you will receive it after completion of the Monolith Visit.  *Note*: The Heat Capacitor required to build the Circuit Board is rewarded after completing Scientific Research's **The Korvax Convergence** section.
- A Circuit Board is required to obtain the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] blueprint from the [[Overseer (NPC)|Overseer]]
- Since you only need to grow four different plants ([[Frost Crystal]], [[Solanium]], [[Cactus Flesh]], and [[Star Bulb]]), these can be an effective way of making money.
