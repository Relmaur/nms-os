# Terumin

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Neutral Metal Alloy
- **Total Value:** 39,105
- **Updated:** Foundation

**Terumin** is an alloy.

## Summary

**Terumin** is a product alloy that is used in construction and trading.

## Game description

Light metal alloy traded throughout the galactic Outer Edge. Often used in the interiors of trading shuttles to reduce weight as they ascend from planets with high gravitational fields.

## Source

Terumin cannot be crafted.

Original formula:

Emeril x40 + Gold x40 + Herox x1 → Terumin

It can also be salvaged from destroyed pirate ships and Cargo Drops on planets.
