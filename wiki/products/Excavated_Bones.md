# Excavated Bones

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Excavated Bones
- **Value:** 1,952,544.8
- **Used for:** Trading, Trading (High Value)
- **Added in:** Singularity

**Excavated Bones** is a type of [[Artifact]].

## Summary

**Excavated Bones** is one of several types of [[Artifact|Artifacts]] and it is a reward for interacting with [[Ancient Bones]] found at [[Natural Burial Site‎‎|Natural Burial Site‎‎s]].
