# Dangling Orb

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Fishing Bait
- **Value:** 550.0
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Dangling Orb** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Dangling Orb** is a [[Fishing Bait]] that can be crafted by the player and used with [[Fishing Rig]] or [[Lost Angler's Rig]].

## Game Description

A piece of specialist bait, designed to improve the size and rarity of your catch.

Locate your Fishing Rig and begin bait application with .

## Source

- **Crafting**: 1 × [[Solar Mirror]] + 1 × [[Metal Plating]] + 65 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] → 20 × Dangling Orb

## Usage

Select your fishing/angler's rig from your [[Multi-tool]] inventory, press  to access your [[Fishing|Bait Box]] and then select the fishing bait to apply it to your rig.

This will apply a bonus when catching your next fish. When a fish is caught, or the line is reeled in, you will lose one piece of [[bait]] from your rig.
