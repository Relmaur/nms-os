# Ion Battery

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Portable Energy Storage
- **Value:** 200.0
- **Used for:** Building, Charging, Upgrading
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Ion Battery** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Ion Battery** is a [[consumable]] [[product]].

## Game description

Convenient and optimised power storage unit. Used in a wide variety of defensive and hazard protection systems.

Constructed by fusing Ferrite Dust with underground Cobalt. Frequently found ready-made in cylindrical metal containers.

## Source

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 5 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 10 × [[Cobalt]] → 1 × Ion Battery

- **Refiner** · *Ion Capture* · 45 s per unit: 10 × [[Ferrite Dust]] + 10 × [[Silver]] + 10 × [[Sodium]] → 1 × Ion Battery

- Can always be bought from any [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] or from pilot NPCs on [[Space Station|Space Stations]] (only on "Goods Availability->Abundant" setting).

## Use

Ion Batteries can be used to recharge the following items:
- [[Hazard Protection]]
- [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Heat)]]
- [[Thermal Protection Upgrade (Cold)]]
- [[Radiation Protection Upgrade]]
- [[Toxic Protection Upgrade]]

Ion Batteries can be used to tame any mechanical fauna from the genus [[Mechanoceris]] or [[Structurae]].
