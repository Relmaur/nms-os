# Starshield Battery

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Portable Energy Storage
- **Value:** 500
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Starshield Battery** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Starshield Battery** is a [[Consumable|consumable]] and one of the [[Consumable|portable energy storages]].

## Game description

A power storage unit optimised for use in starship defensive systems.

[[Tritium]]-fuelled capacitors provide quick-release charge for the [[Deflector Shield]], but would overload smaller-scale equipment.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Tritium]] + 20 × [[Gold]] → 1 × Starshield Battery
