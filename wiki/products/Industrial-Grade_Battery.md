# Industrial-Grade Battery

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Energy Source)
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Industrial-Grade Battery** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Industrial-Grade Battery** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A packet of well-isolated industrial batteries.

Heavy-duty batteries are required to power deep level miningoperations. These highly durable sources can last for weeks, even in the extremes of heat and pressure found kilometres underground.

## Source

An Industrial-Grade Battery can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Power Generation Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]].

## Usage

Industrial-Grade Battery can be sold for a profit in any [[Mining Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
