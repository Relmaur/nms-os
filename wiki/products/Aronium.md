# Aronium

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Aronium** is a [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

A lightweight and highly malleable alloy. Crafted from [[Paraffinium]] and [[Ionised Cobalt]].

In high demand on the Galactic Trade Network due to its utility in the construction of planetside outposts and facilities.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Paraffinium]] + 50 × [[Ionised Cobalt]] → 1 × Aronium
