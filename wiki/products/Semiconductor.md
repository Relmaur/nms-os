# Semiconductor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Manufactured Gas Product
- **Value:** 320,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Semiconductor** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Semiconductor** is a [[tradeable]] that is used for crafting.

## Game description

An advanced material useful in many electronic devices, with its own in-built temperature regulator.

Crafted from [[Thermic Condensate]] and [[Nitrogen Salt]].

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be purchased from NPCs in some [[Minor Settlement|Minor Settlements]].

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Thermic Condensate]] + 1 × [[Nitrogen Salt]] → 1 × Semiconductor
