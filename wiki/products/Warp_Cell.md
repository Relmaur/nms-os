# Warp Cell

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Hyperdrive Charging Unit
- **Used for:** Building, Charging, Upgrading
- **Value:** 46,750.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Warp Cell** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Warp Cell** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] used to fuel the [[starship]]'s [[hyperdrive]].

It charges the hyperdrive for one jump, but 4 cells will charge the hyperdrive for 5 jumps.

## Game Description

This antimatter-based fuel is one of the few power sources capable of meeting the incredible energy requirements of a starship hyperdrive.

Secured within a robust housing, the cell is guaranteed paradox-free during FTL travel.

## Source

- **Crafting**: 1 × [[Antimatter Housing]] + 1 × [[Antimatter]] → 1 × Warp Cell

The total [[resource|resources]] needed to build a Warp Cell from scratch are:

|   |   |   |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To build... | You need... | Qty |
| [[Antimatter]] | [[Chromatic Metal]] | 25 |
|   | [[Condensed Carbon]] | 20 |
| [[Antimatter Housing]] | [[Oxygen]] | 30 |
|   | [[Ferrite Dust]] | 50 |

- **Refiner** · *Antimatter Bypass* · 300 s per unit: 50 × [[Carbon]] + 5 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Warp Cell
- **Refiner** · *Antimatter Bypass* · 300 s per unit: 25 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 10 × [[Sodium]] + 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Warp Cell
- **Refiner** · *Antimatter Bypass* · 300 s per unit: 25 × [[Condensed Carbon]] + 5 × [[Sodium Nitrate]] + 250 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Warp Cell

Warp Cells can also be found by locating an [[Atlas Interface]] or [[The Atlas]]; inside you'll sometimes find two Warp Cells near to the interaction area.

## Additional information

- You cannot sell Warp Cells.
