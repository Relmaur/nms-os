# Heat Capacitor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 180,000.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Trading
- **Added in:** Echoes

**Heat Capacitor** is an advanced crafting component

## Summary

**Heat Capacitor** is a [[component]] used in crafting.

## Game description

A thermal regulator produced from refined organic material. It is unique in its ability to produce, dissipate and distribute heat as required.

Crafted from [[Solanium]] and [[Frost Crystal|Frost Crystals]].

## Source

- Blueprint can be awarded by completing a certain stage of [[Scientific Research]] mission.
- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- This can be found in a [[Cargo Drop]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 100 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 200 × [[Solanium]] → 1 × Heat Capacitor

## Use

- Used as a construction component in [[Planetary Settlement|Planetary Settlements]].

## Additional information

It resembles the lamps seen in [[space station|space stations]] and [[Trading Post|Trading Posts]].
