# Heat Capacitor

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Total Value:** 180,000.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Trading
- **Updated:** Echoes

**Heat Capacitor** is an advanced crafting component

## Summary

**Heat Capacitor** is a component used in crafting.

## Game description

A thermal regulator produced from refined organic material. It is unique in its ability to produce, dissipate and distribute heat as required.

Crafted from Solanium and Frost Crystals.

## Source

- Blueprint can be awarded by completing a certain stage of Scientific Research mission.
- Blueprint can be found in Manufacturing Facilities and Operations Centres, or purchased from Synthesis Laboratory for 250.
- This can be found in a Cargo Drop

Heat Capacitor can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Frost Crystal ×100 + Solanium ×200 → Heat Capacitor

## Use

- Used as a construction component in Planetary Settlements.

**Heat Capacitor** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Circuit Board -- Heat Capacitor x1 + Poly Fibre x1 → Circuit Board

## Additional information

It resembles the lamps seen in space stations and Trading Posts.
