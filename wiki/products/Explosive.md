# Explosive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Technology
- **Type:** Component
- **Description:** General purpose explosive
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Explosives** are an item used in construction.

## Game description

A general purpose explosive charge with an advanced propellant that an be rendered inert until needed, for safe transport and handling.

## Sources

- **Crafting (obsolete)**: 40 × [[Coprite]] + 40 × [[Solanium]] → 1 × Explosive

## Additional information

- An initial investment of 50 [[Rubeum]] is required for producing this item.
- As of the [[Atlas Rises]] update, this item has been removed and has been reclassified as an **Obsolete Item** in-game. It's value has not changed.
- This item has been replaced by the [[Liquid Explosive]] item.
