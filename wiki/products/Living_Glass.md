# Living Glass

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 566,000.0
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Living Glass** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Living Glass** is a [[tradeable]] item.

## Game description

An advanced building material, this glass has been imbued with organic compounds that allow it to react to the environment. Microbes within the glass filter harmful light of all wavelengths and balance the atmosphere as required.

Crafted from Lubricant and Glass.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Lubricant]] + 5 × [[Glass]] → 1 × Living Glass

## Additional information

The blueprint is rewarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and complete the [[Scientist]] quest line.
