# Unrefined Pyrite Grease

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Trade Goods (Minerals)
- **Value:** 6,000.0
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Unrefined Pyrite Grease** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Unrefined Pyrite Grease** is a 2nd tier [[tradeable]].

## Game Description

Large jars full of pyrite grease.

A fat solution with a high mineral content. This grease is highly valued by manufacturing economies, where it's used to keep large machinery oiled and glistening.

## Source

Unrefined Pyrite Grease can be purchased from [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]] in all medium-wealth and high-wealth [[Mining Economy]] [[star system|star systems]]. It is also obtainable in some, but not all, low-wealth systems with this [[Economy]], this item can also be found by destroying cargo bays in a space frigate or freighates.

## Usage

Unrefined Pyrite Grease can be sold for a profit in any [[Manufacturing Economy]] star system.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].
