# Wiring Loom

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Universal Technology Platform
- **Value:** 25,000.0
- **Used for:** Crafting, Upgrading
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Wiring Loom** is a consumable product.

## Summary

**Wiring Loom** is a [[consumable]] [[product]] and used in crafting and repairing various technology components.

## Game description

A prefabricated circuit board, designed for flexible use in a large range of technology components.

The module features an integrated nanite processor, allowing it to be quickly configured for a wide variety of uses.

## Sources

- Can be purchased at a [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] in any star base for about double of the announced value (50-55K units).
- Can be dismantled from [[Obsolete Technology]] or most [[Upgrade Module|Upgrade Modules]].
- Can be purchased from technologists in outposts for around 75000 units.
