# Terrifying Sample

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Artifact
- **Type:** Terrifying Sample
- **Value:** 1,379,958.6
- **Used for:** Trading, Trading (High Value)
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Terrifying Sample** is a type of Artifact.

## Summary

**Terrifying Sample** is one of several types of [[Artifact|Artifacts]]. The DARK variant is a reward for opening [[Large Artifact Crate|Large Artifact Crates]] found in Ancient [[Ruin|Ruins]] and containers in [[Derelict Freighter|Derelict Freighters]]. This artifact type is also available by offering a [[Hypnotic Eye]] to [[Submerged Ruins]]. However, this option will only become available after listening to enough story dialogues from the ruins.

A second variety identified as FEAR can be obtained from several varieties of [[Space Encounter|Space Encounters]]. Keeping this item when obtained in this way can cause the space encounter that awarded it to keep spawning without warning, dropping you out of pulse drive involuntarily.

## Game description

An appalling relic, the haunted remains of some [[Abyssal Horror (stationary)|abyssal horror]]. It has lived at least **X**0 lifetimes, and will live many more again.

Will stalk the bearer across the galaxy and the next, unless it is sold before the curse can take effect.
