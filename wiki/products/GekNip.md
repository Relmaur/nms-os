# GekNip

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Trade commodity
- **Total Value:** 20,625.0
- **Updated:** Voyagers

**GekNip** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**GekNip** is a curiosity of Gek origin and one of the trade commodities.

## Game description

Edible product much beloved by the Gek.

Allows beaked traders to release delightfully scented packets of gas for a prolonged period of time.

GekNip gas packets are considered an art form, and the truest representation of the Gek soul.

## Use

- Can not be consumed normally through inventory.
- Fueling a Nutrient Ingestor with **GekNip** will grant -100% Unspecified for 0:06 per unit consumed.

## Source

- It can be found in Damaged Containers or as monolith rewards.
- It can also be bought from NPCs in Trading Posts.
- It is a possible reward from frigate expeditions.
- It is also a possible trade from wandering traders you encounter while using the Pulse Engine.
- It is a possible reward from Practice Language with Geks

## Recipes

**GekNip** is used as an ingredient for cooking the following products using a Nutrient Processor:

- Herbal Crunchie -- Sugar Dough x1 + GekNip x1 → Herbal Crunchie x1 (*"Dough Augmentation"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, GekNip gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%
- Other notes: N/A

## Additional information

- They can be used to invest in Gek Planetary Settlements.
