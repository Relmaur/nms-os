# Carite Sheet

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Technology component
- **Total Value:** 825.0
- **Updated:** Atlas Rises

**Carite Sheet** is a product.

## Summary

A **Carite Sheet** is a product used in building and repairing technology.

## Game description

A lightweight metal product heavily used in ship part manufacture. Also used in the construction of planetside outposts and facilities of all known primary species.

## Source

Carite Sheet cannot be crafted.

Original formula:

Iron x50 → Carite Sheet

## Use

**Carite Sheet** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

## Usage

Damaged ship components

While used for general construction, these sheets can also be used to repair certain ship equipment.
