# Trident Key

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Aquatic Relic
- **Value:** 1,000.0
- **Added in:** Abyss

**Trident Key** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Trident Key** is a [[curiosity]] required to open a [[Trident Key (Damaged Component)]] on [[Sealed Sea Chest|Sealed Sea Chests]] that can be found at [[Submerged Ruins]].

## Game Description

A strange forked key, glowing with an ultraviolet light.

Despite its primitive appearance, Exosuit scanning systems reveal traces of nanite foam upon its teeth.

## Sources

One Trident Key can be received from the Refracted Light located at the Submerged Ruin, after listening to The Voice of the Shell dialogue.

## Release history

- [[Abyss]] - Added as a curiosity.
