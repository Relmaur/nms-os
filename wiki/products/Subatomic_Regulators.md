# Subatomic Regulators

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Value:** 940,000.0
- **Type:** Starship Subcomponent
- **Added in:** Adrift

**Subatomic Regulators** is a tradeable item.

## Summary

**Subatomic Regulators** is a [[tradeable]] item of scrap.

## Game Description

A valuable piece of broken down [[starship]], created by a [[Starship Outfitting Terminal]] at a [[Space Station]].

Component is useful only as scrap to be sold at a [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Trade Terminal]].

## Source

Sometimes created by a Starship Outfitting Terminal at a Space Station when the player chooses "Claim Scrap" menu option to salvage their starship into [[Units]].

## Usage

Component is useful only as scrap to be sold at a Trade Terminal.

It is not usable by the player in [[Crafting]] or [[Base building]].

## Additional Information

- 0-1 piece is awarded when salvaging a starship.
- Maximum stack size is 10.
- The same icon is used by [[Decrypted User Data]]
