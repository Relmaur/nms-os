# Unstable Gel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Value:** 50,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Unstable Gel** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Unstable Gel** is a [[tradeable]] that is used in crafting.

## Game description

A highly reactive gel, this substance is both energy-rich and dangerous to touch.

Crafted from [[Cactus Flesh]].

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 200 × [[Cactus Flesh]] → 1 × Unstable Gel

Sometimes awarded for opening [[Crashed Freighter]]'s Cargo Pods.

## Additional information

The Unstable Gel blueprint is awarded when you construct the [[Science Terminal]] and continue the [[Scientist]] quest line.

## Release history

- [[Atlas Rises]] - Added as a component.
- [[Prisms]] - Added Synthesis Laboratory as a blueprint source.
