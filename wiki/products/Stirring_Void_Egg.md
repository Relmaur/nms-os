# Stirring Void Egg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Agitated Curiosity
- **Value:** 1616.0
- **Added in:** Living Ship

**Stirring Void Egg** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Stirring Void Egg** is a [[curiosity]].

## Game description

An anomalous egg that has been activated as the second step in the [[Starbirth]] mission. Since the encounter with the bizarre deep-space 'creature', its strange music has grown more active, more agitated.

## Sources

The Stirring Void Egg can only occur once a [[Void Egg]] has been activated to become an [[Active Void Egg]] and is in the players inventory. Once the player leaves a [[Space Station]] or [[Anomaly]] and uses the pulse drive within a [[Star system]]. The player will be notified to drop out of Pulse and then will encounter a deep-space creature which then stirs the egg.

## Additional information

The stirring void egg remains in the players inventory and should remain there through the Starbirth mission.
