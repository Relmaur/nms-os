# Liquid Explosive

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Advanced Agricultural Product
- **Total Value:** 800,500.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Liquid Explosive** is a product.

## Summary

**Liquid Explosive** is an advanced agricultural product.

## Game description

A general purpose high explosive, offered in liquid form for convenience of transport and deployment. Do not drink.

Crafted from Acid and Unstable Gel.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in Manufacturing Facilities and Operations Centres, or purchased from Synthesis Laboratory for 250.

Liquid Explosive can be built using a blueprint and the following ingredients:

- Acid ×1 + Unstable Gel ×1 → Liquid Explosive

## Use

A Liquid Explosives Farm

**Liquid Explosive** is used as an ingredient for crafting the following products:

- Portable Reactor -- Liquid Explosive x1 + Fusion Accelerant x1 → Portable Reactor

## Additional information

The Liquid Explosive blueprint is rewarded when you construct the Science Terminal and complete the Scientist quest line.

- Atlas Rises - added as an agricultural product
