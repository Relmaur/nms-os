# Void Egg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Curiosity
- **Type:** Living Curiosity
- **Value:** 3200.0
- **Added in:** Outlaws

**Void Egg** is a curiosity.

## Summary

**Void Egg** is a [[curiosity]].

## Game description

An anomalous egg. The shell is tough and leathery, and twitches when touched.

A faint tapping can be heard coming from within. From time to time, it appears to sing.

## Sources

The Void Egg can be purchased from the [[Quicksilver Synthesis Companion]] for 3200  Quicksilver.

A decorative only version can also be found randomly on Infested Planets.

## Additional information

Having the Egg in inventory may cause the player to receive a transmission after using the [[Pulse Engine|pulse drive]], triggering the [[Starbirth]] mission.
