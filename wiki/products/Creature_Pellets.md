# Creature Pellets

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Compressed Nutrients
- **Value:** 20
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Creature Pellets** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Creature Pellets** is a [[consumable]] and it is one of the [[Consumable|Compressed Nutrients]].

## Game Description

Basic creature bait. Can be placed directly or accessed via the Quick Menu ().

This bait will attract creatures and cause them to yield [[Faecium]]. Once a creature has been fed, they may be tamed or farmed for their produce.

Dropping bait may calm angry or aggressive creatures.

## Use

- Can be added to a [[Fishing|Bait Box]].
- Can be added to an [[Automated Feeder]].
- Can be fed to [[Fauna|terrestrial fauna]] to [[Companion Pets|tame]] them, or harvest [[Animal Byproduct|Animal Byproducts]] from them.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will provide **+1% Life Support Tanks**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Creature Pellets** provides **+1% Life Support Tanks** for 0:06 minutes.
