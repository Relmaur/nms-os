## Summary

**Cryo-Pump** is a [[tradeable]].

## Game description

An advanced, self-regulating hybrid material, the Cryo-Pump distributes heat across itself with near perfect efficiency, making it vital in the creation of thermal regulators.

Crafted from Hot Ice and Thermic Condensate

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Can be obtained as a mission reward.
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 1 × [[Hot Ice]] + 1 × [[Thermic Condensate]] → 1 × Cryo-Pump
