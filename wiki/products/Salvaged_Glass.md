# Salvaged Glass

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Component
- **Type:** Recovered Sentinel Components
- **Total Value:** 3,000
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Salvaged Glass** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Salvaged Glass** is a component, specifically **Recovered Sentinel Components**. It can be shattered to yield various components or Sentinel Upgrade Modules.

## Game Description

A sinister shard of purple glass, salvaged from the destroyed remains of Sentinel machinery. Its walls are thin, and it may be broken down to access valuable Sentinel components.

Select in the Inventory (//) and shatter with //.

## Source

- May be recovered from Combat Supplies dropped by defeated Sentinels.
- Occasionally found on Autophage heads and crashed Sentinel Interceptors on dissonant planets.

## Use

Shattering Salvaged Glass will yield one of the following:

- Alloy (Magno-Gold, Lemmium, Dirty Bronze, Geodesite)
- Sentinel Weapons Shard
- Sentinel Exosuit Fragment
- Sentinel Boundary Map
- Antimatter
- Crafting Components (Magnetic Resonator, Hydraulic Wiring, Quantum Computer)
- Vy'keen Effigy
- Nanites

## Additional Information

Community testing has resulted in the following drop chances from Salvaged Glass:
