# Fascination Bead

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Trade Commodity
- **Value:** 12,375
- **Description:** Increases the processing power of electronic lifeforms
- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

**Fascination Bead** is a trade commodity.

## Summary

**Fascination Beads** are a relatively expensive [[trade commodity]].

It can be found in [[Cargo Drop|Damaged Containers]].

## Game Description

Golden metal sphere used to temporarily increase the processing power of electronic lifeforms.

Conductivity within an entity's casing is dramatically increased, resulting in faster analysis and research.

## Use

Fascination Bead is an obsolete product and no longer has a purpose in the game.

## Additional information

- Can be sold at the [[Galactic Trade Terminal]] (approximate value 13,000 units)
- It may also be offered to a [[Korvax]] to pledge allegiance and gain 3 reputation.
