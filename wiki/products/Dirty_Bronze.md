# Dirty Bronze

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Tradeable
- **Type:** Alloy Metal
- **Value:** 25,000.0
- **Added in:** Endurance

**Dirty Bronze** is a tradeable.

## Summary

**Dirty Bronze** is a [[tradeable]] that can be crafted or purchased.

## Game description

A popular metal [[alloy]] with a wide variety of uses. 'Dirty Bronze' has shed its scientific name in favor of the popular term for its colour.

Crafted from Pure Ferrite and Pyrite.

## Source

- Blueprint can be found in [[Manufacturing Facility|Manufacturing Facilities]] and [[Operations Centre|Operations Centres]], or purchased from [[Synthesis Laboratory]] for 250  Nanites.
- Available from some [[Galactic Trade Terminal|Galactic Trade Terminals]].
- Can be obtained from [[Frigate]] expeditions.
- Can be received after analysing [[Glowing Mineral]]

- **Crafting** · needs the blueprint: 50 × [[Pyrite]] + 100 × [[Pure Ferrite]] → 1 × Dirty Bronze
