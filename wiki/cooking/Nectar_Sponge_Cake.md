# Nectar Sponge Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 79,700
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Nectar Sponge Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Nectar Sponge Cake** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Proto-Batter.

Flavour profile: manifold, vibrant, otherwordly.

Recipe Complexity: 93%

## Source

Nectar Sponge Cake can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Proto-Batter x1 + Syrupy Nectar x1 → Nectar Sponge Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Batter x1 + Butter Syrup x1 → Nectar Sponge Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Nectar Sponge Cake** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Nectar Sponge Cake gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 7%
- Catch Size Improvement: 39%

## Additional Information

Restores 2% health once consumed.

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +6,440% Fauna Analysis Rewards, Duration 10 minutes
