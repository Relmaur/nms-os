# Horrifying Mush

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 90,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Horrifying Mush** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Horrifying Mush** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Hypnotic Eye]] → 1 × Horrifying Mush

## Recipes

Note that three of the below recipes are incorrect as of Xeno-Arena. They should be: Horrifying Mush + Sweetroot = Abyssal Stew; Horrifying Mush + Pulpy Roots = Abyssal Stew; and Horrifying Mush + Kelp Rice is not a valid recipe.

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.
