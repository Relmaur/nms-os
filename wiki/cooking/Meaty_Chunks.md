# Meaty Chunks

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Meaty Chunks** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Meaty Chunks** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by killing many types of fauna. Each creature killed will yield between 5 and 25 **Meaty Chunks**.
- Antelopes ([[Tetraceris]] and [[Reococcyx]])
- Rodents ([[Procavya]])
- Diplos ([[Rangifae]])
- Triceratops ([[Theroma]])
- Jellyfish ([[Chrysaora]])
- Sea snakes ([[Prionace]])
- Swimming cows ([[Prionacefda]])
- Swimming rodents ([[Procavaquatica]])

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Incinerate Flesh* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Meat Flakes]] → 1 × Meaty Chunks

## Additional information

- Restores one (1) [[Health|Core Health]] square when consumed.
- Despite possibly sounding like a lower-grade ingredient than [[Raw Steak]], the two ingredients are completely interchangeable in all recipes except for the [[Proteinous Doughnut]] which requires Chunks specifically.
- [[Processed Meat]] is also interchangeable with Meaty Chunks in almost all recipes, except for the [[Creature Pellets]] and [[Ground Meat]] bait-recipes which specifically require Steak or Meaty Chunks.
