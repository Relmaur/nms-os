# Caramel Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 62,000.0
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Caramel Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Caramel Tart** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.  It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Crunchy Caramel]] → 1 × Caramel Tart

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 15 second sprint overload when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
- +7% Sprint Recovery Time *(10:00)*
