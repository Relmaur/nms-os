# Jammy Burster

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 81,900
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Jammy Burster** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Jammy Burster** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.
