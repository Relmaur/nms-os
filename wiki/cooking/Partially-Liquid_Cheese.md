# Partially-Liquid Cheese

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 24,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Partially-Liquid Cheese** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Partially-Liquid Cheese** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: nutty, fermented, tangy.

Recipe Complexity: 66%

## Source

Partially-Liquid Cheese can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cream x1 + Tangy Cheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Cream x1 + ProtoCheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Cream x1 + Bone Cream (Cheese) x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Cream x1 + Tangy Cheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Cream x1 + ProtoCheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Cream x1 + Bone Cream (Cheese) x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Bone Cream x1 + Tangy Cheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Bone Cream x1 + ProtoCheese x1 → Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
