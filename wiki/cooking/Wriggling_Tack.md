# Wriggling Tack

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 55,000.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Wriggling Tack** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Wriggling Tack** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully-chosen ingredients.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Flesh Rope]] → 1 × Wriggling Tack
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Nightmare Sausage]] → 1 × Wriggling Tack

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 40% life support power when consumed.
