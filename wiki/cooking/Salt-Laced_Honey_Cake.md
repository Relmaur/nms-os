# Salt-Laced Honey Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 92,300.0

**Salt-Laced Honey Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Salt-Laced Honey Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

+6214% Mineral Analysis Rewards for 10:00 when consumed in Nutrient Ingestor.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Thick, Sweet Batter]] + 1 × [[Salty Custard]] → 1 × Salt-Laced Honey Cake
