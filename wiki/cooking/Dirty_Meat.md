# Dirty Meat

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,400.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Dirty Meat** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Dirty Meat** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by killing ploughs ([[Prionterrae]]) or protodiggers ([[Prototerrae]]).
