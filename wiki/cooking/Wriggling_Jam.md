# Wriggling Jam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 9,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Wriggling Jam** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Wriggling Jam** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: ephemeral, tropical, sublime.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 87%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Preserve Fruit* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] + 1 × [[Processed Sugar]] → 1 × Wriggling Jam

## Additional information

Restores 25% [[hazard protection]] once consumed.

+26% Hazard Protection Battery when consumed through the [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
