# Silicon Egg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 500
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Silicon Egg** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Silicon Egg** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: musty, ripe, coarse.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 44%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Chromatic Yolk Formation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Chromatic Metal]] → 1 × Silicon Egg

## Additional Information

Restores 5% [[life support]] power once consumed.
