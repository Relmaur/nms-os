# Fishy Slab

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 39,000
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Fishy Slab** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fishy Slab** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A huge slab of seafood, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **50% [[hazard protection]]**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Fishy Slab** provides **+49% Breathing Efficiency** for 6 minutes.
