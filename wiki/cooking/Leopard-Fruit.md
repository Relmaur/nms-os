# Leopard-Fruit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200.0
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Leopard-Fruit** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Leopard-Fruit** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be gathered from cat-like creatures with predatory features ([[Felidae]] and [[Felihex]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

- The creatures where this is gathered from can be hostile or not.
- They will have predatory features even if they are not predators.
- When gathering for this ingredient, creatures will have a 'Berry' Pouch.
