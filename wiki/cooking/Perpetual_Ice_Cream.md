# Perpetual Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 44,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Perpetual Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Perpetual Ice Cream** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

Each ice cream provides a 320% jetpack boost for 10 minutes when used in the Nutrient Ingestor.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.
