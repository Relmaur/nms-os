# Gooey Honey Puff

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 38,400.0
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Gooey Honey Puff** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gooey Honey Puff** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Gooey Honey Puff
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Gooey Butter]] → 1 × Gooey Honey Puff

## Additional Information

Consuming **Gooey Honey Puff** gives a short boost to the [[Jetpack]].
