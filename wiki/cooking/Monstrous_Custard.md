# Monstrous Custard

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 100,000.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Monstrous Custard** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Monstrous Custard** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Source

Monstrous Custard can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Larval Core x1 + Cream x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Monstrous Custard x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
