# Delicate Legs

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable

## Summary

**Delicate Legs** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A piece of seafood, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **20% [[life support]] power**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Delicate Legs** provides **+42% Breathing Efficiency** for 5:30 minutes.
