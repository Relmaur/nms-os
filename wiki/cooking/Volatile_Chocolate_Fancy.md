# Volatile Chocolate Fancy

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product

## Summary

**Volatile Chocolate Fancy** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Writhing, Roiling Batter. Some caution is advised.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Writhing, Roiling Batter]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Volatile Chocolate Fancy

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
