# Gooey Butter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 32,000.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Gooey Butter** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gooey Butter** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Gooey Butter gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 10%
- Catch Size Improvement: 32%

## Source

Gooey Butter can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Churned Butter x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Gooey Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Bone Butter x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Gooey Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
