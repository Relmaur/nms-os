# Grahberry

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Value:** 800
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Grahberry** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Grahberry** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

Believed to have once native to the [[Vy'keen]] homeworld of [[Dryn'dargh]], the Grahberry Plant can found on [[Biome - Irradiated|irradiated]] planets throughout the galaxy.

Grahberries are used in traditional Vy'keen foods such as [[Grahj'am]] and [[Vy'ice Cream]].

## Game description

An edible plant, harvested from planetary flora.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

Wild plant, harvested on [[Biome - Irradiated|irradiated]] planets.

## Additional information

Restores 5% [[life support]] power once consumed.
