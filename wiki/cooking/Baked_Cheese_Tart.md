# Baked Cheese Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 62,000.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Baked Cheese Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Baked Cheese Tart** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Baked Cheese Tart can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Tangy Cheese x1 → Baked Cheese Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + ProtoCheese x1 → Baked Cheese Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Bone Cream (Cheese) x1 → Baked Cheese Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Baked Cheese Tart** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional information

+30% Hazard Protection Battery for 10min, when consumed in a Nutrition Ingestor.

15 seconds sprint overload, when consumed through Exosuit.
