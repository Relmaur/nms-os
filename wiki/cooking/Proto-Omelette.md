# Proto-Omelette

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 24,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Proto-Omelette** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Proto-Omelette** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Creature Egg]] + 1 × [[ProtoCheese]] → 1 × Proto-Omelette
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Tall Eggs]] + 1 × [[ProtoCheese]] → 1 × Proto-Omelette
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Giant Egg]] + 1 × [[ProtoCheese]] → 1 × Proto-Omelette

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.
