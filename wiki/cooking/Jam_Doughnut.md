# Jam Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 70,000.0
- **Added in:** Frontiers

**Jam Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Jam Doughnut** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Ever-burning Jam]] → 1 × Jam Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Grahj'am]] → 1 × Jam Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Cactus Jelly]] → 1 × Jam Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Furball Jelly]] → 1 × Jam Doughnut

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
