# Rancid Flesh

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 6,200
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Rancid Flesh** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Rancid Flesh** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[raw Ingredient|raw Ingredients]].

## Game Description

A writhing slab of meat, harvested from the corpse of the biological horror that once contained it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

Rewarded by killing [[Biological Horror|Biological Horrors]].

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 1% health when consumed.
