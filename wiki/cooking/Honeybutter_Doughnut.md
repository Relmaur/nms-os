# Honeybutter Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 105,000
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Honeybutter Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Honeybutter Doughnut** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Butter]] → 1 × Honeybutter Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Proto-Butter]] → 1 × Honeybutter Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Honey Butter]] → 1 × Honeybutter Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Honied Proto-Butter]] → 1 × Honeybutter Doughnut
