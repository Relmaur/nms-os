# Assorted Roe

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Assorted Roe** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A small morsel of seafood, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **50% health**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Assorted Roe** provides **+56% Breathing Efficiency** for 7:30 minutes.
