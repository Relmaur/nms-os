# Gooey Screamer

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 167,200
- **Added in:** Fractal

**Gooey Screamer** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gooey Screamer** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Thick, Sweet Batter]] + 1 × [[Monstrous Custard]] → 1 × Gooey Screamer
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Gooey Screamer
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Gooey Butter]] → 1 × Gooey Screamer

## Additional information

- Provides a Jetpack Supercharge similar to the [[Jetpack|Deuterium plant boost]] when it is consumed.
- If fed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], provides a 6,129% bonus to [[Fauna]] Analysis Rewards.
