# Crab 'Apple'

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Crab 'Apple**' is a consumable.

## Summary

**Crab 'Apple**' is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced by gathering from spider- or crab-like creatures ([[Bos]], [[Conokinis]], [[Bosaquatica]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

- Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
- Flying and ground dwelling crab-like creatures will have them.
- The creatures can be hostile or not.
- When given the tool-tip for gathering, the creature will have Growths.

- [[Beyond]] - Added as a consumable.
