# Smokey Meat Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 42,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Smokey Meat Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Smokey Meat Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Smoked Meat]] → 1 × Smokey Meat Pie

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, **Smokey Meat Pie** gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

    Catch Rarity Improvement: 54%

    Catch Size Improvement: 4%

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

Process with [[Nutrient Ingestor]] for 8:00 minutes of +25% Hazard Protection Battery.
