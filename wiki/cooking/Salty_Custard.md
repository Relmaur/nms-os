# Salty Custard

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 28,000.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Salty Custard** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Salty Custard** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

Salty Custard can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Salt x1 + Cream x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Salty Custard x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
