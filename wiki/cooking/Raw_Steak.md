# Raw Steak

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,400
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Raw Steak** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Raw Steak** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by killing terrestrial cow-like creatures ([[Ungulatis]] and [[Hexungulatis]]).

## Additional information

- Restores 1% [[health]] once consumed.
- Despite possibly sounding like a higher-grade ingredient than [[Meaty Chunks]], the two ingredients are completely interchangeable in all recipes except for the [[Proteinous Doughnut]] which requires Chunks specifically.
- Raw Steak is also interchangeable with [[Processed Meat]] in almost all recipes, except for the [[Creature Pellets]] and [[Ground Meat]] bait-recipes which specifically require Steak or Meaty Chunks.

## Release history

- [[Beyond]] - Added as a consumable.
- [[Aquarius]] - Added fishing bait bonuses.
