# Fiendish Roe

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,400.0
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Fiendish Roe** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fiendish Roe** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by gathering from roller-type creatures found on Exotic planets ([[Anomalous]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

Restores 1-3% of your health. This percentage is calculated from your max health number, which is only accessible with a save editor. It is only a very small value change and results in mostly just one full health point being restored.
