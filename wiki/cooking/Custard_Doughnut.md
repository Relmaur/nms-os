# Custard Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 105,000
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Custard Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Custard Doughnut** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable.**

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Viscous Custard]] → 1 × Custard Doughnut
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Very Thick Custard]] → 1 × Custard Doughnut

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
