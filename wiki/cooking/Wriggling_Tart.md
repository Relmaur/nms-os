# Wriggling Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 54,000.0
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Wriggling Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Wriggling Tart** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] → 1 × Wriggling Tart
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] → 1 × Wriggling Tart

## Additional information

15 sec sprint overload, when consumed in Exosuit.
