# Fungal Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 40,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Fungal Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fungal Tart** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: synesthetic, astonishing, chromatic.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Fungal Tart can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Non-Toxic Mushroom x1 → Fungal Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Fungal Tart** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Fungal Tart gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 13%
- Catch Size Improvement: 9%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish

## Additional Information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload once consumed.
