# Haunted Fillet

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 39,000.0
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Haunted Fillet** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Haunted Fillet** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A small morsel of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate **8 seconds of jetpack overload**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Haunted Fillet** provides **+50% Breathing Efficiency** for 7:30 minutes.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Seafood Assembly* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[The Angler]] → 1 × Haunted Fillet

## Additional Information

- Probably the rarest food product in the game.
- This multiplies the value of The Angler by over 24 times.
