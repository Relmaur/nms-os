# Salty Juice

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 1,800.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Salty Juice** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Salty Juice** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate **8 seconds of jetpack overload**.

- When consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor, **Salty Juice** provides **+58% Breathing Efficiency** for 9 minutes.

## Source

Salty Juice can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cactus Flesh x1 + Salt x1 + Fire Water x1 → Salty Juice x1 (*"Liquidise"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Salty Juice** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
