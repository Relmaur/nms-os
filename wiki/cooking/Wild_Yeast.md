# Wild Yeast

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Wild Yeast** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Wild Yeast** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An edible plant, harvested from planetary flora.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Fermentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wild Yeast]] → 1 × Wild Yeast
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Fermentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Faecium]] → 1 × Wild Yeast

## Additional information

- Restores 5% [[life support]] power once consumed.
- Wild Yeast is the only cooking product in No Man's Sky that has a redundant recipe with the same output as the input: Wild Yeast makes Wild Yeast. Hence, fermentation can mean you are bluffing the cooking action.
