# Delicious Vegetable Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 18,800.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Delicious Vegetable Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Delicious Vegetable Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Delicious Vegetable Stew can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fibrous Stew x1 + Flavoursome Sauce x1 → Delicious Vegetable Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Delicious Vegetable Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 20% life support power when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a Nutrient Ingestor

- +42% Shield Strength *(10:00)*

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Delicious Vegetable Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 25%
- Catch Size Improvement: 15%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish

## Additional Information

Sold to Iteration: Cronus in The Anomaly for 40-129 nanites.

When giving 100 Delicious Vegetable Stew to Iteration: Cronus, they will give an average of 94.69 Nanites per Delicious Vegetable Stew.
