# Chewy Biscuit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 56,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Chewy Biscuit** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Chewy Biscuit** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully-chosen ingredients.

Flavour profile: tangy, fruity, roasted.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 68%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Nourishing Slime]] → 1 × Chewy Biscuit
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Mucous]] → 1 × Chewy Biscuit
