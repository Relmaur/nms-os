# Softened Marrow

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 79,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Softened Marrow** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Softened Marrow** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: rich, smooth, velvety.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 68%

## Source
