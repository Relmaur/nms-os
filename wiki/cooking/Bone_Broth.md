# Bone Broth

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 172,000
- **Updated:** Relics

**Bone Broth** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bone Broth** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: earthy, fresh, tangy.

Recipe Complexity: 72%

## Source

## Recipes

**Bone Broth** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Bone Broth gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 27%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%

## Additional Information

Restores 50% Hazard Protection once consumed.

Gives resources mined +42% for 6:36 if placed in the Nutrient Ingestor.
