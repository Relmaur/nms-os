# Nectar Islands

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 94,800
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Nectar Islands** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Nectar Islands** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Syrupy Nectar]] → 1 × Nectar Islands
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Butter Syrup]] → 1 × Nectar Islands

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
- +6,532% Fauna Analysis Rewards *(10:00)*
