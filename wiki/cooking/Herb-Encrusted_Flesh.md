# Herb-Encrusted Flesh

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 18,800
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Herb-Encrusted Flesh** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Herb-Encrusted Flesh** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: intense, synesthetic, aromatic.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Herb-Encrusted Flesh can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Mystery Meat Stew x1 + Flavoursome Sauce x1 → Herb-Encrusted Flesh x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Well-Stirred Stew x1 + Flavoursome Sauce x1 → Herb-Encrusted Flesh x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Soiled Soup x1 + Flavoursome Sauce x1 → Herb-Encrusted Flesh x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Herb-Encrusted Flesh** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Herb-Encrusted Flesh gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 18%
- Catch Size Improvement: -%

## Additional Information

- Restores 20% life support power once consumed.
- It appears to be one of the better sources of Nanites when food trading with Iteration: Cronus at the Space Anomaly.
- When giving 100 Herb-Encrusted Flesh to Iteration: Cronus, they will give an average of 96.18 Nanites per Herb-Encrusted Flesh.
