# Herb-Encrusted Flesh

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 18,800
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Herb-Encrusted Flesh** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Herb-Encrusted Flesh** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: intense, synesthetic, aromatic.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Mystery Meat Stew]] + 1 × [[Flavoursome Sauce]] → 1 × Herb-Encrusted Flesh
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Well-Stirred Stew]] + 1 × [[Flavoursome Sauce]] → 1 × Herb-Encrusted Flesh
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Soiled Soup]] + 1 × [[Flavoursome Sauce]] → 1 × Herb-Encrusted Flesh

## Additional Information

- Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.

- It appears to be one of the better sources of [[Nanites]] when food trading with [[Iteration: Cronus]] at the [[Space Anomaly]].

- When giving 100 Herb-Encrusted Flesh to Iteration: Cronus, they will give an average of 96.18 Nanites per Herb-Encrusted Flesh.
