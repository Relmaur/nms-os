# The Worst Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 96,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**The Worst Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**The Worst Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

The Worst Stew can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Juicy Grub x1 + Rancid Flesh x1 → The Worst Stew x1 (*"Purge by Fire"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**The Worst Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, The Worst Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 13%

## Additional Information

Exosuit receives an 8 second Jetpack Overload once consumed.

+50% Toxic Resistance, for 6,30min, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor.
