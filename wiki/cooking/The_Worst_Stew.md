# The Worst Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 96,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**The Worst Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**The Worst Stew** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Purge by Fire* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Juicy Grub]] + 1 × [[Rancid Flesh]] → 1 × The Worst Stew

## Additional Information

Exosuit receives an 8 second Jetpack Overload once consumed.

+50% Toxic Resistance, for 6,30min, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor.
