# Syrupy Nectar

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Syrupy Nectar** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Syrupy Nectar** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game Description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by gathering from [[Floradae]] when fed [[Creature Pellets]] or by culling.

## Additional Information

Restores 25% [[Hazard Protection]] once consumed.
