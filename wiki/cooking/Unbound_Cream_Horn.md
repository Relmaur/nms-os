## Summary

**Unbound Cream Horn** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Writhing, Roiling Batter. Some caution is advised.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Writhing, Roiling Batter]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Unbound Cream Horn
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Writhing, Roiling Batter]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Unbound Cream Horn
