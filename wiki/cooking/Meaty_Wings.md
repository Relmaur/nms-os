# Meaty Wings

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Value:** 1,400.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Meaty Wings** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Meaty Wings** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Use

Restores one (1) [[Health|Core Health]] square when consumed.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by killing bird-like creatures ([[Agnelis]]), flying lizards ([[Cycromys]]) and large butterflies ([[Rhopalocera]]).

- [[Beyond]] - Added as a consumable.
