# Refined Flour

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 1,800
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Refined Flour** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Refined Flour** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: intense, smooth, explosive.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Refining* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Heptaploid Wheat]] → 1 × Refined Flour
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Refining* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Glass Grains]] → 1 × Refined Flour
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Extract Nutrients* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Kelp Rice]] → 1 × Refined Flour

## Additional Information

Restores 20% [[Life Support]] power once consumed.
