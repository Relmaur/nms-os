# Refined Flour

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 1,800
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Refined Flour** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Refined Flour** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: intense, smooth, explosive.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Refined Flour can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Heptaploid Wheat x1 → Refined Flour x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Glass Grains x1 → Refined Flour x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Kelp Rice x1 → Refined Flour x1 (*"Extract Nutrients"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
