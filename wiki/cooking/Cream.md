## Summary

**Cream** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: bitter, dry, harsh.

Recipe Complexity: 58%

## Source

Cream can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fresh Milk x1 → Cream x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Wild Milk x1 → Cream x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
