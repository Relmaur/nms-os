# Deathly-Cold Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 128,000.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Use

- When consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor, **Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** provides **+64% Breathing Efficiency** for 10 minutes.

## Source

Deathly-Cold Ice Cream can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Monstrous Custard x1 + Frozen Tubers x1 → Deathly-Cold Ice Cream x1 (*"Rapid Chilling"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Monstrous Custard x1 + Frost Crystal x1 → Deathly-Cold Ice Cream x1 (*"Rapid Chilling"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
