# Deathly-Cold Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 128,000.0
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Use

- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Deathly-Cold Ice Cream** provides **+64% Breathing Efficiency** for 10 minutes.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Monstrous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] → 1 × Deathly-Cold Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Monstrous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Deathly-Cold Ice Cream
