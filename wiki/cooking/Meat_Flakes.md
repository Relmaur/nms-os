# Meat Flakes

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 800.0
- **Added in:** Waypoint

**Meat Flakes** is a cooking ingredient.

## Summary

**Meat Flakes** is a [[Raw Ingredient|raw]] [[cooking]] ingredient.

## Game Description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Use

Restores 5% [[Life Support]] when consumed.

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Extract Nutrients* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Mordite]] → 1 × Meat Flakes
