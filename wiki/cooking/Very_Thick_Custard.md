# Very Thick Custard

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 32,000.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**Very Thick Custard** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Very Thick Custard** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Source

Very Thick Custard can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Bone Cream x1 + Processed Sugar x1 + Creature Egg x1 → Very Thick Custard x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
