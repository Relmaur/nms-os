# Enriched Biscuit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 56,000.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Enriched Biscuit** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Enriched Biscuit** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully chosen ingredients.
