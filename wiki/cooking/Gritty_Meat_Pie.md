# Gritty Meat Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 62,000.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Gritty Meat Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gritty Meat Pie** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Use

Gives 15% Stamina Boost when consumed.

## Source

Gritty Meat Pie can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Lumpy Brainstem x1 → Gritty Meat Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Gritty Meat Pie** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
