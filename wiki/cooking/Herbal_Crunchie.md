# Herbal Crunchie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 60,000
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Herbal Crunchie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Herbal Crunchie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully-chosen ingredients.

## Use

- Replenishes 5% [[Life Support]] when consumed.
-

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[NipNip Buds]] → 1 × Herbal Crunchie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[GekNip]] → 1 × Herbal Crunchie

## Additional Information

The in-game display text previously incorrectly stated that consumption will activate 5 seconds jetpack overload. This has sense been patched.
