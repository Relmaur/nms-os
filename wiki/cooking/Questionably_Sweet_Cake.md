# Questionably Sweet Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 73,600.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Questionably Sweet Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Questionably Sweet Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Questionably Sweet Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Gooey Butter]] → 1 × Questionably Sweet Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Thick, Sweet Batter]] + 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Questionably Sweet Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Thick, Sweet Batter]] + 1 × [[Gooey Butter]] → 1 × Questionably Sweet Cake

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 50% hazard protection when consumed.

+64% Resources mined, for 10 min, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor
