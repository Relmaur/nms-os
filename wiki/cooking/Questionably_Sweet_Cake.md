# Questionably Sweet Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 73,600.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Questionably Sweet Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Questionably Sweet Cake** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

Questionably Sweet Cake can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cake Batter x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Questionably Sweet Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Cake Batter x1 + Gooey Butter x1 → Questionably Sweet Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Thick, Sweet Batter x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Questionably Sweet Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Thick, Sweet Batter x1 + Gooey Butter x1 → Questionably Sweet Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Questionably Sweet Cake** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 50% hazard protection when consumed.

+64% Resources mined, for 10 min, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor
