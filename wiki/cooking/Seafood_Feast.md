# Seafood 'Feast'

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 39,000
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Seafood 'Feast**' is a consumable.

## Summary

**Seafood 'Feast**' is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A huge slab of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **25% [[hazard protection]]**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Seafood 'Feast**' provides **+39% Breathing Efficiency** for 6 minutes.
