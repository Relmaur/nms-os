# Proto-Sausage Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Proto-Sausage Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Proto-Sausage Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: prismatic, ephemeral, manifold.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[ProtoSausage]] → 1 × Proto-Sausage Pie

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.
