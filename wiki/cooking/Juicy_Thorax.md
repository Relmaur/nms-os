# Juicy Thorax

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,400
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Juicy Thorax** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Juicy Thorax** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

A nutritious meat product, harvested from the corpse of the creature that once contained it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by killing [[Arthropodae]].

## Additional Information

Restores 1% [[health]] once consumed.
