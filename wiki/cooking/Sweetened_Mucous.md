# Sweetened Mucous

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 9,000
- **Used for:** Cooking
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Sweetened Mucous** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Sweetened Mucous** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

Sweetened Mucous can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Nourishing Slime x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Sweetened Mucous x1 (*"Preserve Fruit"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Usage

### Crafting

**Sweetened Mucous** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Sweetened Mucous** is not used as an ingredient for refining using a Refiner.

## Consume

- Replenishes 25% Hazard Protection when consumed.
- Fueling a Nutrient Ingestor with **Sweetened Mucous** will grant +27% Hazard Protection Battery for 4:00:00 per unit consumed.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Sweetened Mucous gives the following bonus towards your next catch:
