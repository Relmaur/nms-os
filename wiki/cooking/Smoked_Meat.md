# Smoked Meat

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 2,200
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Smoked Meat** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Smoked Meat** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Replenishes 20% Life Support when consumed.

33% Heat Resistance for 5 minutes when placed in a Nutrient Ingestor

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Smoked Meat gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish
