# Jellied Fur Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 54,000.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Jellied Fur Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Jellied Fur Tart** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Jellied Fur Tart can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Leopard-Fruit x1 → Jellied Fur Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Furball Jelly x1 → Jellied Fur Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Jellied Fur Tart** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, **Jellied Fur Tart** gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

Catch Rarity Improvement: 24%

Catch Size Improvement: 14%

Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish

## Additional information

Consume directly for 15 seconds sprint overload.

+16% bonus to Sprint Recovery Time (10 minutes each) when used in the Nutrient Ingestor.
