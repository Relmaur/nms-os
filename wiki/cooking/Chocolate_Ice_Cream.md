# Chocolate Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 44,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Chocolate Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Chocolate Ice Cream** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Viscous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Chocolate Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Viscous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Chocolate Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Very Thick Custard]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Chocolate Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Very Thick Custard]] + 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Chocolate Ice Cream
