# Chewy 'Dumpling' Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 6,400
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Chewy 'Dumpling' Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Chewy 'Dumpling' Stew** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]]. It is made by combining [[Lumpy Brainstem]] and any other meat ingredient.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.
