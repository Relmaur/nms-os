# Cake of the Lost

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 199,200.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Cake of the Lost** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cake of the Lost** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Writhing, Roiling Batter. Some caution is advised.
