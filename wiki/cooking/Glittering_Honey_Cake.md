# Glittering Honey Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 50,100
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Glittering Honey Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Glittering Honey Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Synthetic Honey]] → 1 × Glittering Honey Cake

## Additional Information

Restores 50% [[hazard protection]] once consumed. <br>
