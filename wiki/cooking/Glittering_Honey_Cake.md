# Glittering Honey Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 50,100
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Glittering Honey Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Glittering Honey Cake** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

Glittering Honey Cake can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cake Batter x1 + Synthetic Honey x1 → Glittering Honey Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Glittering Honey Cake** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Glittering Honey Cake gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 15%
- Catch Size Improvement: 29%

## Additional Information

Restores 50% hazard protection once consumed.

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +52% Resources Mined, Duration 10 minutes
