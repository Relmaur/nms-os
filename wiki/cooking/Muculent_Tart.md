# Muculent Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 62,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Muculent Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Muculent Tart** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: synesthetic, astonishing, ephemeral.

Recipe Complexity: 93%

## Source

Muculent Tart can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Sweetened Mucous x1 → Muculent Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Muculent Tart** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Muculent Tart gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 20%
- Catch Size Improvement: 33%

## Additional Information

Grants 15 seconds of Sprint Overload once consumed.

- Worlds Part I - Added as a consumable.
