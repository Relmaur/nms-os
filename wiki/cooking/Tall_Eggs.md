# Tall Eggs

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200.0
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Tall Eggs** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Tall Eggs** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced by gathering from striders ([[Anastomus]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

When consumed it recharges 20% of standard [[Life Support]].
