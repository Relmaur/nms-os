# Cake of Glass

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 179,600.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Cake of Glass** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cake of Glass** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Use

- Exosuit nutrient system 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
- Fueling a Nutrient Ingestor with **Cake of Glass** will grant +325% Jetpack Tanks for 10:00 per unit consumed.

## Source

Cake of Glass can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Wailing Batter x1 + Anomalous Jam x1 + Cream x1 → Cake of Glass x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Wailing Batter x1 + Anomalous Jam x1 + Bone Cream x1 → Cake of Glass x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Cake of Glass** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Cake of Glass gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 57%
- Catch Size Improvement: 10%
