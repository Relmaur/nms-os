# Brined Flesh

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 35,400
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Brined Flesh** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Brined Flesh** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A slab of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **25% [[hazard protection]]**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Brined Flesh** provides **+48% Breathing Efficiency** for 5:30 minutes.
