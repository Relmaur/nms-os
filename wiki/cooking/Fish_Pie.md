# Fish Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 40,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Fish Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fish Pie** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: polyphonic, intense, manifold.

Recipe Complexity: 94%

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will power **15 seconds sprint overload**.

- When consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor, **Fish Pie** provides **+47% Breathing Efficiency** for 8 minutes.

## Source

Fish Pie can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Salty Chunks x1 → Fish Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Fish Pie** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Fish Pie gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 38%
- Catch Size Improvement: 4%

## Additional Information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload once consumed.
