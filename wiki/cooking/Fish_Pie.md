# Fish Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Fish Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fish Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: polyphonic, intense, manifold.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 94%

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will power **15 seconds sprint overload**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Fish Pie** provides **+47% Breathing Efficiency** for 8 minutes.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Salty Chunks]] → 1 × Fish Pie

## Additional Information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload once consumed.
