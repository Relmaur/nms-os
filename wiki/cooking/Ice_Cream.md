# Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 48,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Ice Cream** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Viscous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] → 1 × Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Viscous Custard]] + 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Ice Cream

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.
