# Tangy Cheese

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 9,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Tangy Cheese** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Tangy Cheese** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: pleasant, balanced, fresh.

Recipe Complexity: 65%

## Source

Tangy Cheese can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cream x1 + Wild Yeast x1 → Tangy Cheese x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
