# Bread

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 5,200
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Bread** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bread** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly nutty.

Flavour profile: floral, tangy, complex.

Recipe Complexity: 71%

## Source

Bread can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Dough x1 → Bread x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Bread** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Bread gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 31%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%

## Additional information

Grants an 8 second jetpack overload once consumed.
