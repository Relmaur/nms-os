# Fiery Vegetable Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 18,800.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Fiery Vegetable Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fiery Vegetable Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Fiery Vegetable Stew can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fibrous Stew x1 + Scorching Sauce x1 → Fiery Vegetable Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Use

Restores 20% Life Support power on consumption.

## Recipes

**Fiery Vegetable Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
