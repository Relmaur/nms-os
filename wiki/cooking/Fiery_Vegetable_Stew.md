# Fiery Vegetable Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 18,800.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Fiery Vegetable Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fiery Vegetable Stew** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Fibrous Stew]] + 1 × [[Scorching Sauce]] → 1 × Fiery Vegetable Stew

## Use

Restores 20% [[Life Support]] power on consumption.
