# Pollen Puffball

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 120,000
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Pollen Puffball** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pollen Puffball** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Butter Syrup]] → 1 × Pollen Puffball

## Additional Information

Restores 20% Life Support power once consumed.
