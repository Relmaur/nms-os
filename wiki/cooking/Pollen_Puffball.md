# Pollen Puffball

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 120,000
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Pollen Puffball** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pollen Puffball** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

Pollen Puffball can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Lumpen Doughnut x1 + Butter Syrup x1 → Pollen Puffball x1 (*"Nutrient Injection"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Pollen Puffball** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Restores 20% Life Support power once consumed.

## Release History

- Worlds Part I - Added as a consumable.
