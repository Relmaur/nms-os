# Spiced 'Apple' Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 64,300.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Spiced 'Apple' Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Spiced 'Apple' Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] → 1 × Spiced 'Apple' Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Spiced 'Apple' Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Spiced 'Apple' Cake

## Additional information

52% Resources mined for 10 min, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor

Will restore 50% Hazard protection when consumed through Exosuit.
