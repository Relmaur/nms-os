# Root Juice

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 1,800.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Root Juice** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Root Juice** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Liquidise* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pulpy Roots]] → 1 × Root Juice

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.

## Additional Information

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +50% Solar Panel Power, Duration 6 minutes
