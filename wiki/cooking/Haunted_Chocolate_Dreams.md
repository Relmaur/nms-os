## Summary

**Haunted Chocolate Dreams** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Bittersweet Cocoa]] → 1 × Haunted Chocolate Dreams

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
