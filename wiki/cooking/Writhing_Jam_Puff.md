## Summary

**Writhing Jam Puff** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Writhing, Roiling Batter. Some caution is advised.
