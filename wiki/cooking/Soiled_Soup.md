# Soiled Soup

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 6,400
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Soiled Soup** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Soiled Soup** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: fruity, nutty, balanced.

Recipe Complexity: 71%

**Soiled Soup** is used as an ingredient for cooking the following products using a Nutrient Processor:

- Cheese-and-Flesh Stew -- Soiled Soup x1 + Partially-Liquid Cheese x1 → Cheese-and-Flesh Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Soiled Soup gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 7%
- Catch Size Improvement: 21%

## Additional Information
