# Syrupy Proto-Butter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 36,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Syrupy Proto-Butter** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Syrupy Proto-Butter** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: balanced, mellow, bracing.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 61%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Component Churn* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Butter]] + 1 × [[Syrupy Nectar]] → 1 × Syrupy Proto-Butter

## Additional Information

Restores 40% Life Support power once consumed.
