# Honied Angel Cake

## Summary

**Honied Angel Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Synthetic Honey]] → 1 × Honied Angel Cake

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
