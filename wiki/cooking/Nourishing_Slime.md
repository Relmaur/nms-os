# Nourishing Slime

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Category:** Consumable
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Nourishing Slime** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Nourishing Slime** is a [[consumable]] and it is one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game Description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

Can be obtained by feeding an [[Arthropodae]] some [[Creature Pellets]] and then harvesting it.

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Liquidate* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Juicy Grub]] → 1 × Nourishing Slime

## Consume

- Replenishes **20% [[Life Support]] power** when consumed without a [[Nutrient Processor]].
-

## Additional Information

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
