# Crystalline Soup

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 6,400
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Crystalline Soup** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Crystalline Soup** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

It is made by combining [[Crystal Flesh]] and any other meat ingredient.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Use

Restores 20% [[Life Support]] power once consumed.

+39% Resources Mined for 6:30 when ingested through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]].
