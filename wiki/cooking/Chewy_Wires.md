# Chewy Wires

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Chewy Wires** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Chewy Wires** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game Description

This odd collection of wires pulses with a faint, almost organic energy. It seems to remember the whole from which it was parted...

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate 'edible' products.

## Use

- Provides 20% Energy Boost when consumed.
- Rewards between 5 to 10 nanites when consumed.
-

## Source

This ingredient can be sourced by gathering from robotic creatures ([[Mechanoceris]] and [[Structurae]]) when fed [[Ion Battery|Ion Batteries]].

## Release History

- [[Origins]] - Added as a consumable.
- [[Worlds Part I]] - Can be harvested from Walking Buildings
