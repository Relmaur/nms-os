# Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 23,600.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Extra-Fluffy Batter x1 + Cream x1 → Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Extra-Fluffy Batter x1 + Bone Cream x1 → Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Extra-Fluffy Cream Cake** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional information

Consume directly for 8 seconds jetpack overload.

Process with Nutrient Ingestor for 10 minutes +6,422% fauna analysis rewards.
