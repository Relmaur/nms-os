# Pilgrim's Tonic

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 800.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**Pilgrim's Tonic** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pilgrim's Tonic** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

Pilgrim's Tonic can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pilgrimberry x1 → Pilgrim's Tonic x1 (*"Liquidise"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Use

When consumed, increases thermal protection(cold) gauge by 25% per instance.

## Recipes

**Pilgrim's Tonic** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional information

At the moment it appears Pilgrim's Tonic is a final product.

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +77% Life Support Tanks, Duration 7 minutes
