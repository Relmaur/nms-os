# High-Fibre Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**High-Fibre Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**High-Fibre Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: manifold, polyphonic, tropical.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 77%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Steamed Vegetables]] → 1 × High-Fibre Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Jade Peas]] → 1 × High-Fibre Pie

## Additional Information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload once consumed.
