# Fresh Milk

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Fresh Milk** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fresh Milk** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced by gathering from cow-like creatures ([[Ungulatis]] and [[Hexungulatis]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

These creatures can be told apart from rodents ([[Procavya]]) by the lack of fat, round bottoms.

## Additional information

When consumed it recharges 20% of standard [[Life Support]].
