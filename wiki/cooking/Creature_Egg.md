# Creature Egg

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Creature Egg** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Creature Egg** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced by gathering from triceratops ([[Theroma]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.

Process with [[Nutrient Ingestor|Nutrient Ingesto]]r for 1:30 minutes of +13% Sprint distance.
