# Abyssal Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 90,000.0
- **Updated:** Aquarius

**Abyssal Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Abyssal Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Usage

### Crafting

**Abyssal Stew** is not used as an ingredient for crafting.

### Refining

**Abyssal Stew** is not used as an ingredient for refining using a Refiner.

### Cooking

**Abyssal Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Consume

- Replenishes **25% Hazard Protection** when consumed without a Nutrient Processor.
- Fueling a Nutrient Ingestor with **Abyssal Stew** will grant +2 Words Translated for Unkown per unit consumed.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Abyssal Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 16%
- Catch Size Improvement: 38%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish
