# Sweet and Salty Puff

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 92,300
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Sweet and Salty Puff** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Sweet and Salty Puff** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Salty Custard]] → 1 × Sweet and Salty Puff

## Additional Information

Restores 50% health once consumed.
