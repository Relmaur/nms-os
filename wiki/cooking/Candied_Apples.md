# Candied 'Apples'

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 78,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Candied 'Apples**' is a consumable.

## Summary

**Candied 'Apples**' is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

Flavour profile: piquant, polyphonic, prismatic.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] → 1 × Candied 'Apples'
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Candied 'Apples'
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Candied 'Apples'
