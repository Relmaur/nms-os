## Summary

**Caramel Curiosity** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Proto-Batter.

Flavour profile: vivid, piquant, vibrant.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Batter]] + 1 × [[Crunchy Caramel]] → 1 × Caramel Curiosity

## Additional information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload once consumed.
