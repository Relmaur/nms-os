# Sponge of Ambrosia

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 97,200
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Sponge Of Ambrosia** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Sponge Of Ambrosia** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

Flavour profile: complex, delicate, colourful.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Sponge of Ambrosia can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Syrupy Batter x1 + Viscous Custard x1 → Sponge of Ambrosia x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Syrupy Batter x1 + Very Thick Custard x1 → Sponge of Ambrosia x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Sponge of Ambrosia** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Sponge of Ambrosia gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 21%
- Catch Size Improvement: -%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish

## Additional Information

Restores 50% health once consumed.
