# Gelatinous Membrane

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Used for:** Cooking
- **Value:** 8,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Gelatinous Membrane** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gelatinous Membrane** is a [[Consumable]] and it is an [[Edible Product]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Incinerate Flesh* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Flesh Rope]] → 1 × Gelatinous Membrane
