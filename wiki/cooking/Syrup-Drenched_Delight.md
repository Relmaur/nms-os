# Syrup-Drenched Delight

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 73,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Syrup-Drenched Delight** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Syrup-Drenched Delight** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

Flavour profile: explosive, astonishing, delicate.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%
