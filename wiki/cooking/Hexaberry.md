# Hexaberry

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Used for:** Cooking
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Hexaberry** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Hexaberry** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game Description

An edible plant, harvested from planetary flora.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

Flavour profile: bland, homely, malted.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 42%

## Source

Wild plant, harvested on [[Biome - Exotic|exotic]] planets.

Can also be sourced by gathering from some fauna on exotic planets.

## Additional Information

- Restores 5% [life support] once consumed.
- Hexaberry can be harvested from anomalous creatures.
