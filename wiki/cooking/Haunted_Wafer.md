# Haunted Wafer

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 80,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Haunted Wafer** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Haunted Wafer** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully-chosen ingredients.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Larval Core]] → 1 × Haunted Wafer
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Hypnotic Eye]] → 1 × Haunted Wafer
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Fiendish Roe]] → 1 × Haunted Wafer

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 50% hazard protection when consumed.
