# Anomalous Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 70,000
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Anomalous Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Anomalous Doughnut** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Anomalous Jam]] → 1 × Anomalous Doughnut

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
- +6443% Fauna Analysis Rewards *(10:00)*

## Additional information

When using anomalous doughnuts in the nutrient ingestor, you get a massive buff to fauna analysis. If you just consume them, you only get a jet pack boost.
