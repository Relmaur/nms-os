# Interstellar Curiosity

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 92,300.0

**Interstellar Curiosity** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Interstellar Curiosity** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Proto-Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Batter]] + 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] → 1 × Interstellar Curiosity

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.
