# The Pie Of Knowledge

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 62,000.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**The Pie Of Knowledge** is a consumable.

## Summary

**The Pie Of Knowledge** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

When consumed, it provides a temporary sprint speed boost.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable.**

## Source

The Pie Of Knowledge can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Dirty Meat x1 → The Pie Of Knowledge x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**The Pie Of Knowledge** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
