# Wild Milk

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Wild Milk** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Wild Milk** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced by gathering from antelopes ([[Tetraceris]] and [[Reococcyx]]) and rodents ([[Procavya]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]].

## Additional information

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
