# Fluffy Caramel Delight

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 23,600.0

**Fluffy Caramel Delight** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fluffy Caramel Delight** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Crunchy Caramel]] → 1 × Fluffy Caramel Delight

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.
