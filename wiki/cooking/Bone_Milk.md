# Bone Milk

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 32,000.0
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Updated:** SentinelUp

**Bone Milk** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bone Milk** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

Bone Milk can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Bone Nuggets x1 → Bone Milk x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Ancient Bones x1 + Ancient Bones x1 → Bone Milk x1 (*"Stew Bones"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Bone Milk** is used as an ingredient for cooking the following products using a Nutrient Processor:

- Bone Cream -- Bone Milk x1 → Bone Cream x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Enriched Biscuit -- Sugar Dough x1 + Bone Milk x1 → Enriched Biscuit x1 (*"Dough Augmentation"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Additional information

When consumed it recharges 20% of standard Life Support.
