# Bone Milk

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Used for:** Refining
- **Value:** 32,000.0
- **Added in:** SentinelUp

**Bone Milk** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bone Milk** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Component Churn* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Bone Nuggets]] → 1 × Bone Milk
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Stew Bones* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Ancient Bones]] + 1 × [[Ancient Bones]] → 1 × Bone Milk

## Additional information

When consumed it recharges 20% of standard [[Life Support]].
