# Gooey Caramel Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 64,900.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Gooey Caramel Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gooey Caramel Cake** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

## Source

Gooey Caramel Cake can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Thick, Sweet Batter x1 + Crunchy Caramel x1 → Gooey Caramel Cake x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Gooey Caramel Cake** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +6,208% Mineral Analysis Rewards, Duration 10 minutes
