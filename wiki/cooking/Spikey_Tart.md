# Spikey Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Spikey Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Spikey Tart** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Aloe Flesh]] → 1 × Spikey Tart
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Cactus Nectar]] → 1 × Spikey Tart

## Additional Information

Bestows +62% heat resistance for 10 minutes when consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.
