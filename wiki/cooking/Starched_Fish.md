# Starched Fish

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 52,500
- **Updated:** Aquarius

**Starched Fish** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Starched Fish** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A slab of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will power **15 seconds sprint overload**.

- When consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor, **Starched Fish** provides **+45% Breathing Efficiency** for 7:30 minutes.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Starched Fish gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 3%
- Other notes: Attracts nocturnal fish
