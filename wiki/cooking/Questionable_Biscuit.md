# Questionable Biscuit

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 35,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Questionable Biscuit** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Questionable Biscuit** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully-chosen ingredients.

Flavour profile: nutty, fresh, sharp.

Recipe Complexity: 75%

## Recipes

**Questionable Biscuit** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 20% life support power when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a Nutrient Ingestor

- +14% Sprint Recovery Time *(6:36)*

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Questionable Biscuit gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%
- Other notes: Attracts nocturnal fish
