# Churned Butter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 7,200.0
- **Updated:** SentinelUp

**Churned Butter** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Churned Butter** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Churned Butter gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 0%

## Source

Churned Butter can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cream x1 → Churned Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Churned Butter** is used as an ingredient for cooking the following products using a Nutrient Processor:

- Butter Syrup -- Churned Butter x1 + Syrupy Nectar x1 → Butter Syrup x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Clarified Oil -- Churned Butter x1 + Bone Butter x1 → Clarified Oil x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Clarified Oil -- Churned Butter x1 + Churned Butter x1 → Clarified Oil x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Clarified Oil -- Churned Butter x1 + Proto-Butter x1 → Clarified Oil x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Gooey Butter -- Churned Butter x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Gooey Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Honey Butter -- Churned Butter x1 + Synthetic Honey x1 → Honey Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pastry -- Refined Flour x1 + Churned Butter x1 → Pastry x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Sweetened Butter -- Churned Butter x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Sweetened Butter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
