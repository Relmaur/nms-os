# Cake Batter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 38,000.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Cake Batter** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cake Batter** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Source

Cake Batter can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Sweetened Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Creature Egg x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Honey Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Creature Egg x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Sweetened Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Tall Eggs x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Honey Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Tall Eggs x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Sweetened Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Giant Egg x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Honey Butter x1 + Refined Flour x1 + Giant Egg x1 → Cake Batter x1 (*"Component Churn"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
