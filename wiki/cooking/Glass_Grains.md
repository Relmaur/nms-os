# Glass Grains

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 300.0
- **Added in:** Origins

**Glass Grains** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Glass Grains** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]]. Glass Grains can found inside [[Frost Crystal]], which can be harvested from [[Frostwort]].

## Game Description

An edible plant, harvested from planetary flora.

Process in a [[Nutrient Processor]] to generate edible products.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Extract Nutrients* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Glass Grains
