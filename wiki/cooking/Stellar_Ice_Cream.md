# Stellar Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 36,000.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Stellar Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Stellar Ice Cream** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

Stellar Ice Cream can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Stellar Custard x1 + Frozen Tubers x1 → Stellar Ice Cream x1 (*"Rapid Chilling"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Stellar Custard x1 + Frost Crystal x1 → Stellar Ice Cream x1 (*"Rapid Chilling"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Stellar Ice Cream** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.
