# Stellar Ice Cream

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 36,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Stellar Ice Cream** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Stellar Ice Cream** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] → 1 × Stellar Ice Cream
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] + 1 × [[Frost Crystal]] → 1 × Stellar Ice Cream

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.
