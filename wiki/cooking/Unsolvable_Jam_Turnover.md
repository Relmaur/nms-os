# Unsolvable Jam Turnover

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 80,800.0

**Unsolvable Jam Turnover** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Unsolvable Jam Turnover** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Proto-Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Batter]] + 1 × [[Anomalous Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Unsolvable Jam Turnover
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Batter]] + 1 × [[Anomalous Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Unsolvable Jam Turnover

## Additional information

Will activate 15 seconds Sprint Overload when consumed.

+307% Jetpack Tanks, for 10:00, when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor.
