# Grahj'am

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 3,400.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**Grahj'am** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Grahj'am** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable**. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Grahj'am gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 15%
- Catch Size Improvement: 19%

## Source

Grahj'am can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Grahberry x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Grahj'am x1 (*"Preserve Fruit"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
