# Ever-burning Jam

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 3,400.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**Ever-burning Jam** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Ever-burning Jam** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable**. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Source

Ever-burning Jam can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fireberry x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Ever-burning Jam x1 (*"Preserve Fruit"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
