# Gristle Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 62,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Gristle Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gristle Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Use

Gives 15% Stamina Boost when consumed.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Latticed Sinew]] → 1 × Gristle Pie
