# Gristle Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 62,000.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Gristle Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Gristle Pie** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Use

Gives 15% Stamina Boost when consumed.

## Source

Gristle Pie can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Latticed Sinew x1 → Gristle Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Gristle Pie** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.
