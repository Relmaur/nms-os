# Haunted Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 52,000
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Haunted Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Haunted Pie** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

Haunted Pie can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Fiendish Roe x1 → Haunted Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Haunted Pie** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

## Release History

- Beyond - Added as a consumable.
- Prisms - Can now be cooked as Fiendish Roe is actually available in-game.
