# Gelatinous Sponge

## Quick Facts

## Summary

**Gelatinous Sponge** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]].

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

Flavour profile: complex, smooth, tropical.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%
