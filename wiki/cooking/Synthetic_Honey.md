# Synthetic Honey

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 3,400
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Synthetic Honey** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Synthetic Honey** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: mediocre, homely, harsh.

Recipe Complexity: 51%

## Source

Synthetic Honey can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Synthetic Honey x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Syrupy Nectar x1 → Synthetic Honey x1 (*"Nutrient Refining"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
