# Synthetic Honey

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 3,400
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Synthetic Honey** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Synthetic Honey** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: mediocre, homely, harsh.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 51%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Refining* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Synthetic Honey
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Refining* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Nectar]] → 1 × Synthetic Honey

## Additional Information

Restores 25% [[Hazard Protection]] once consumed.
