# Splicer's Delight

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 81,900
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Splicer's Delight** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Splicer's Delight** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Thick, Sweet Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Mucous]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Splicer's Delight
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Syrupy Batter]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Mucous]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Splicer's Delight

Restores 50% health once consumed.
