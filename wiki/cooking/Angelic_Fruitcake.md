# Angelic Fruitcake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 31,300
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Angelic Fruitcake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Angelic Fruitcake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] → 1 × Angelic Fruitcake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Angelic Fruitcake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Angelic Fruitcake

## Additional information

Consume directly for 8 seconds jetpack overload.

Process with [[Nutrient Ingestor]] for 10 minutes +6,205% mineral analysis rewards.
