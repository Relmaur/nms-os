# Perpetual Cake

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 67,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Perpetual Cake** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Perpetual Cake** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]].

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Anomalous Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Perpetual Cake
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Anomalous Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Perpetual Cake

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 50% hazard protection when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
- +329% Jetpack Tanks *(10:00)*
