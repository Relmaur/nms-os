# Scrambled Marrow

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 24,000
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Scrambled Marrow** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Scrambled Marrow** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Creature Egg]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream (Cheese)]] → 1 × Scrambled Marrow
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Tall Eggs]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream (Cheese)]] → 1 × Scrambled Marrow
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Bind Proteins* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Giant Egg]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream (Cheese)]] → 1 × Scrambled Marrow

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload if consumed.
