# 'Apple' Roll

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 42,000
- **Added in:** Swarm

**'Apple' Roll** is a consumable.

## Summary

**'Apple' Roll** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A well-baked biscuit, created in the Nutrient Processor.

What was once a thin sheet of vaguely-sweetend nutrient paste has been elevated with the addition of carefully chosen ingredients.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Crab 'Apple']] → 1 × 'Apple' Roll
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Dough Augmentation* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Sugar Dough]] + 1 × [[Wriggling Jam]] → 1 × 'Apple' Roll
