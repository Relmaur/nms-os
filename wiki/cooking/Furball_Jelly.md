# Furball Jelly

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 9,000.0
- **Updated:** Origins

**Furball Jelly** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Furball Jelly** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Furball Jelly gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 8%
- Catch Size Improvement: 3%
- Other notes: Attracts nocturnal fish

## Source

Furball Jelly can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Leopard-Fruit x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Furball Jelly x1 (*"Preserve Fruit"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
