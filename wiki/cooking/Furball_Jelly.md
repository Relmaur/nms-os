# Furball Jelly

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 9,000.0
- **Added in:** Origins

**Furball Jelly** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Furball Jelly** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Preserve Fruit* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Leopard-Fruit]] + 1 × [[Processed Sugar]] → 1 × Furball Jelly

## Additional Information

+27% Hazard Protection Battery (8 minutes each) when used in the Nutrient Injestor.
