# Honey Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 48,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Honey Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Honey Tart** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: polyphonic, prismatic, manifold.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Sticky 'Honey']] → 1 × Honey Tart
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Synthetic Honey]] → 1 × Honey Tart
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Syrupy Nectar]] → 1 × Honey Tart

## Additional Information

Grants 15 seconds of Sprint Overload once consumed.
