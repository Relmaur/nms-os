# Honey Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 48,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Honey Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Honey Tart** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: polyphonic, prismatic, manifold.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Honey Tart can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Sticky 'Honey' x1 → Honey Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Synthetic Honey x1 → Honey Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Syrupy Nectar x1 → Honey Tart x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Honey Tart** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Honey Tart gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 10%
- Catch Size Improvement: 13%
- Other notes: Attracts storm-loving fish

## Additional Information

Grants 15 seconds of Sprint Overload once consumed.
