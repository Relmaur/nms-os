# Mystery Meat Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 6,400.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Mystery Meat Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Mystery Meat Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Use

When consumed, provides a sprinting power surge for 15 seconds

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Mystery Meat Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 12%
- Catch Size Improvement: 0%
