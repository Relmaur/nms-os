# Syrupy Viscera

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 32,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Syrupy Viscera** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Syrupy Viscera** is a [[consumable]] and it is an [[Edible Product]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Component Churn* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Nightmare Sausage]] + 1 × [[Processed Sugar]] → 1 × Syrupy Viscera

## Consume

- Can not be consumed without a [[Nutrient Processor]].
