# Pie Case

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 30,000
- **Updated:** Swarm

**Pie Case** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pie Case** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: musty, unrefined, nostalgic.

Recipe Complexity: 50%

## Source

Pie Case can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pastry x1 → Pie Case x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
