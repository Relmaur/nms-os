# Iced Screams

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 90,000.0
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Iced Screams** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Iced Screams** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Hypnotic Eye]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] → 1 × Iced Screams
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Rapid Chilling* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Horrifying Mush]] + 1 × [[Frozen Tubers]] → 1 × Iced Screams
