# Bugs-in-a-Blanket

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 24,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Bugs-in-a-Blanket** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bugs-in-a-Blanket** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: tangy, pleasingly sweet, mellow.

Recipe Complexity: 68%

## Source

Bugs-in-a-Blanket can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Scrambled Marrow x1 + Juicy Thorax x1 → Bugs-in-a-Blanket x1 (*"Bind Proteins"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Bugs-in-a-Blanket** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Bugs-in-a-Blanket gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 7%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%
- Other notes: Attracts storm-loving fish

## Additional Information

Grants 15 seconds of Sprint Overload once consumed in Exosuit.

+41% Toxic Resistance for 6.30 Min when consumed through a Nutrient Ingestor.
