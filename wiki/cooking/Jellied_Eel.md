# Jellied Eel

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 52,500
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Jellied Eel** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Jellied Eel** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A slab of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **50% [[hazard protection]]**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Jellied Eel** provides **+46% Breathing Efficiency** for 7:30 minutes.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Seafood Assembly* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Jellymeat]] + 1 × [[Fleshy Cylinder]] → 1 × Jellied Eel
