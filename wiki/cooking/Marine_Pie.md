# Marine Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 52,500
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Marine Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Marine Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A slab of seafood, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **40% [[life support]] power**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Marine Pie** provides **+51% Breathing Efficiency** for 8:30 minutes.
