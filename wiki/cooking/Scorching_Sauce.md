# Scorching Sauce

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 7,200.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Scorching Sauce** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Scorching Sauce** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Replenishes 20% Life Support when consumed.

## Source

Scorching Sauce can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fire Water x1 + Fire Water x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Fire Water x1 + Fireberry x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Fire Water x1 + Solartillo x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Fire Water x1 + Sievert Beans x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Fire Water x1 + Grahberry x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Root Juice x1 + Fireberry x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Root Juice x1 + Solartillo x1 → Scorching Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
