# Foraged Mushrooms

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 3,200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Foraged Mushrooms** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Foraged Mushrooms** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

## Game description

An animal byproduct, this nutritious morsel can be easily farmed without harm to the animal that produces it.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

This ingredient is an [[Animal Byproduct]] that can be sourced from turtle- or mole-like creatures ([[Talpidae]]) when fed [[Creature Pellets]]. Such creatures cannot be fed and harvested from manually, so an [[Automated Feeder|automated feeding]]/[[Livestock Unit|harvesting]] setup is recommended to acquire **Foraged Mushrooms**.

## Additional information

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
