# Sweetened Butter

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 26,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Sweetened Butter** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Sweetened Butter** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: crisp, roasted, pleasingly sweet.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 57%

## Source
