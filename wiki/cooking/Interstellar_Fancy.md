# Interstellar Fancy

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 80,200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Interstellar Fancy** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Interstellar Fancy** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Cake Batter.

Flavour profile: astonishing, vibrant, tropical.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Cake Batter]] + 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] → 1 × Interstellar Fancy

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 50% hazard protection when consumed.
