# Bittersweet Cocoa

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 1,800
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Bittersweet Cocoa** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bittersweet Cocoa** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable**. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: fruity, bracing, mellow.

Recipe Complexity: 72%

## Source

Bittersweet Cocoa can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Impulse Beans x1 → Bittersweet Cocoa x1 (*"Extract Nutrients"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
