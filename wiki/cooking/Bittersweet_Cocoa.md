# Bittersweet Cocoa

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 1,800
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Bittersweet Cocoa** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Bittersweet Cocoa** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable**. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: fruity, bracing, mellow.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 72%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Extract Nutrients* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Impulse Beans]] → 1 × Bittersweet Cocoa

## Additional Information

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
