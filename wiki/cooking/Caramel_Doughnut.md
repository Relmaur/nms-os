# Caramel Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 65,000.0
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Caramel Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Caramel Doughnut** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]].  It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Crunchy Caramel]] → 1 × Caramel Doughnut
