# Parasitic Omelette

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 102,000.0
- **Updated:** NextGen

**Parasitic Omelette** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Parasitic Omelette** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Use

Grants 15 sec Sprint Overload when consumed.

+54% Solar Panel power when ingested through a Nutrient Ingestor.

## Source

Parasitic Omelette can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Larval Core x1 + ProtoCheese x1 → Parasitic Omelette x1 (*"Bind Proteins"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Parasitic Omelette** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Parasitic Omelette gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 6%
- Catch Size Improvement: 2%
- Other notes: Attracts diurnal fish

## Additional information

Gives sprint boost when consumed.
