# Cream of Vegetable Soup

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 26,000.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Cream of Vegetable Soup** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cream of Vegetable Soup** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition past or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Cream of Vegetable Soup can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Fibrous Stew x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Cream of Vegetable Soup x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Cream of Vegetable Soup** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 20% life support power when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a Nutrient Ingestor

- +9% Sprint Recovery Time *(10:00)*

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Cream of Vegetable Soup gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 8%
- Catch Size Improvement: 0%
