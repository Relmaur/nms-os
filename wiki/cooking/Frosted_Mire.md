# Frosted Mire

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 35,200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Frosted Mire** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Frosted Mire** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

Flavour profile: ephemeral, smooth, vibrant.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%
