# Chewy Organ Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Chewy Organ Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Chewy Organ Pie** is a [[Consumable|consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|edible products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Offal Sac]] → 1 × Chewy Organ Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Feline Liver]] → 1 × Chewy Organ Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Scooped Innards]] → 1 × Chewy Organ Pie

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.
