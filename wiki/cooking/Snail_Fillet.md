# Snail Fillet

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 33,100
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Snail Fillet** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Snail Fillet** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A small morsel of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **20% [[life support]] power**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Snail Fillet** provides **+51% Breathing Efficiency** for 6 minutes.
