# Lumpen Doughnut

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 48,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Lumpen Doughnut** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Lumpen Doughnut** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable**. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

Flavour profile: fruity, juicy, spicy.

Recipe Complexity: 74%

## Source

Lumpen Doughnut can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Clarified Oil x1 + Dough x1 + Processed Sugar x1 → Lumpen Doughnut x1 (*"Accelerated Fry"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
