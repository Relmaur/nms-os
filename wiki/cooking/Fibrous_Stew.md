# Fibrous Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 6,400.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Fibrous Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fibrous Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products. It is made by combining two vegetable products.

## Game description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be **highly valuable.** It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subject to further processing.

## Use

Provides a 15 second **Sprint Boost** when consumed.
