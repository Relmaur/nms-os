# The Stellarator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 100,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**The Stellarator** is a consumable.

## Summary

**The Stellarator** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: vivid, polyphonic, aromatic.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

The Stellarator can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Lumpen Doughnut x1 + Stellar Custard x1 → The Stellarator x1 (*"Nutrient Injection"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**The Stellarator** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, The Stellarator gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 49%
- Catch Size Improvement: 9%
- Other notes: Attracts storm-loving fish

## Additional information

Consume directly for 8 seconds jetpack overload.

Process with Nutrient Ingestor for 10 minutes +64% resources mined. (NOTE: This effect compounds with mining beam upgrades. Yields approaching 9X are possible.)
