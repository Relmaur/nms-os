# The Stellarator

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 100,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**The Stellarator** is a consumable.

## Summary

**The Stellarator** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.  It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: vivid, polyphonic, aromatic.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Nutrient Injection* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Lumpen Doughnut]] + 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] → 1 × The Stellarator

## Additional information

Consume directly for 8 seconds jetpack overload.

Process with [[Nutrient Ingestor]] for 10 minutes +64% resources mined. (NOTE: This effect compounds with mining beam upgrades. Yields approaching 9X are possible.)
