# Fruity Pudding

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 40,000.0
- **Added in:** NextGen

**Fruity Pudding** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Fruity Pudding** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Pilgrimberry]] → 1 × Fruity Pudding
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Grahberry]] → 1 × Fruity Pudding
