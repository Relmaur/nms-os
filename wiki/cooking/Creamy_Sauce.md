# Creamy Sauce

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 16,000.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Creamy Sauce** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Creamy Sauce** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

Creamy Sauce can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Cream x1 + Cream x1 → Creamy Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Cream x1 + Proto-Cream x1 → Creamy Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Cream x1 + Proto-Cream x1 → Creamy Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Proto-Cream x1 + Bone Cream x1 → Creamy Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Bone Cream x1 + Bone Cream x1 → Creamy Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

**Creamy Sauce** is used as an ingredient for cooking the following products using a Nutrient Processor:

- Creamed Organ Soup -- Chewy 'Dumpling' Stew x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Creamed Organ Soup x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Creamed Organ Soup -- Crystalline Soup x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Creamed Organ Soup x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Creamed Organ Soup -- Gelatinous Goop x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Creamed Organ Soup x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Creamed Organ Soup -- Stewed Organs x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Creamed Organ Soup x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Creamy Sauce gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 46%
- Catch Size Improvement: 5%

## Additional information

Replenishes 20% Life Support when consumed.
