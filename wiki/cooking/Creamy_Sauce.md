# Creamy Sauce

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 16,000.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Creamy Sauce** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Creamy Sauce** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.
