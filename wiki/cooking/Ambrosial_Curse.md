# Ambrosial Curse

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 199,200
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Ambrosial Curse** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Ambrosial Curse** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Writhing, Roiling Batter. Some caution is advised.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Writhing, Roiling Batter]] + 1 × [[Syrupy Nectar]] → 1 × Ambrosial Curse
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Writhing, Roiling Batter]] + 1 × [[Butter Syrup]] → 1 × Ambrosial Curse

## Additional Information

Grants an 8 second jetpack overload once consumed.

Grants +6523% Fauna Analysis Results for 10 minutes when consumed in a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
