# Cake of Burning Dread

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 176,900.0
- **Updated:** Prisms

**Cake of Burning Dread** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cake of Burning Dread** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Source

Cake of Burning Dread can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Wailing Batter x1 + Ever-burning Jam x1 + Cream x1 → Cake of Burning Dread x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Wailing Batter x1 + Ever-burning Jam x1 + Bone Cream x1 → Cake of Burning Dread x1 (*"Assemble Baked Product"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Cake of Burning Dread** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a Nutrient Ingestor

- +54% Heat Resistance *(10:00)*

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Cake of Burning Dread gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 13%
- Catch Size Improvement: 38%
