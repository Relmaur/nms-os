# Cake of Burning Dread

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 176,900.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Cake of Burning Dread** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Cake of Burning Dread** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Ever-burning Jam]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Cake of Burning Dread
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Ever-burning Jam]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Cake of Burning Dread

## Use

Exosuit nutrient system will activate 8 seconds jetpack overload when consumed.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Provides the following effects when automatically consumed using a [[Nutrient Ingestor]]
- +54% Heat Resistance *(10:00)*
