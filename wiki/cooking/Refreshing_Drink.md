# Refreshing Drink

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 1,800.0
- **Updated:** WorldsOne

**Refreshing Drink** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Refreshing Drink** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Use

Replenishes 25% Hazard Protection when consumed.

## Source

Refreshing Drink can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Aloe Flesh x1 → Refreshing Drink x1 (*"Liquidise"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Refreshing Drink** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Additional Information

Nutrient Ingestor Effect +79% Life Support Tanks, Duration 7 minutes
