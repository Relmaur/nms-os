# Leathery Tart

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 62,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Leathery Tart** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Leathery Tart** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: tropical, piquant, colourful.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Crunchy Wings]] → 1 × Leathery Tart

## Additional information

Grants a 15 second sprint overload.
