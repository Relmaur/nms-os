# Pickled Fish

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 28,000
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Pickled Fish** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pickled Fish** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A small morsel of seafood, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Nutrient Ingestor

Exosuit nutrient system will restore **1% health**.
- When consumed through a [[Nutrient Ingestor]], **Pickled Fish** provides **+37% Breathing Efficiency** for 4:30 minutes.
