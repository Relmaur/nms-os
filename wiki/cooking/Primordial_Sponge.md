# Primordial Sponge

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 180,700
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**Primordial Sponge** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Primordial Sponge** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]].

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Wailing Batter.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Mucous]] + 1 × [[Cream]] → 1 × Primordial Sponge
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Wailing Batter]] + 1 × [[Sweetened Mucous]] + 1 × [[Bone Cream]] → 1 × Primordial Sponge

## Additional Information

Restores 1% health once consumed.
