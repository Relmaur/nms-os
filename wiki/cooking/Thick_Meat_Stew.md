# Thick Meat Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 26,000.0
- **Added in:** Omega

**Thick Meat Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Thick Meat Stew** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the [[Nutrient Processor]]. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition past or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Mystery Meat Stew]] + 1 × [[Creamy Sauce]] → 1 × Thick Meat Stew
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Well-Stirred Stew]] + 1 × [[Creamy Sauce]] → 1 × Thick Meat Stew
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Soiled Soup]] + 1 × [[Creamy Sauce]] → 1 × Thick Meat Stew

Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.
