# Thick Meat Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 26,000.0
- **Updated:** Omega

**Thick Meat Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Thick Meat Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition past or pellets.

Food such as this can be **highly valuable**.

## Source

Thick Meat Stew can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Mystery Meat Stew x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Thick Meat Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Well-Stirred Stew x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Thick Meat Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Soiled Soup x1 + Creamy Sauce x1 → Thick Meat Stew x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Thick Meat Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Thick Meat Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 64%
- Catch Size Improvement: 17%

## Additional information

Restores 20% life support power once consumed.
