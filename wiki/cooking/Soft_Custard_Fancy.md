# Soft Custard Fancy

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 94,800.0
- **Added in:** Prisms

**Soft Custard Fancy** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Soft Custard Fancy** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor.

These cake-like nutrients are processed from a base of Extra-Fluffy Batter.

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Stellar Custard]] → 1 × Soft Custard Fancy
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Baked Product* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Extra-Fluffy Batter]] + 1 × [[Very Thick Custard]] → 1 × Soft Custard Fancy

## Additional information

Effect when consumed in the Nutrient Ingestor: +6,355% Fauna analysis rewards.

Exosuit nutrient system will activate +8 seconds Jetpack overload.
