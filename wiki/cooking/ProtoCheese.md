# ProtoCheese

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 12,000.0
- **Added in:** WorldsOne

**ProtoCheese** is a consumable.

## Summary

**ProtoCheese** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. Food such as this can be highly valuable. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Can be consumed as it stands or subjected to further processing.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Component Churn* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Proto-Cream]] + 1 × [[Wild Yeast]] → 1 × ProtoCheese

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will restore 25% hazard protection when consumed.
