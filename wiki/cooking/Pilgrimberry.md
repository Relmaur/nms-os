# Pilgrimberry

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Raw Ingredient
- **Value:** 300
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Pilgrimberry** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Pilgrimberry** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Raw Ingredient|Raw Ingredients]].

Pilgrimberry is the fruit of the [[Star Bramble]], which can be extracted from the [[Star Bulb]], the flowers of Star Bramble.

## Game Description

An edible plant, harvested from planetary flora.

Process in a Nutrient Processor to generate edible products.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Extract Nutrients* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Star Bulb]] → 1 × Pilgrimberry

## Additional Information

Restores 5% [[life support]] power once consumed.
