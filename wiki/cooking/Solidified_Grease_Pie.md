# Solidified Grease Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 54,000
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Solidified Grease Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Solidified Grease Pie** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: manifold, chromatic, aromatic.

Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

Solidified Grease Pie can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Pie Case x1 + Regis Grease x1 → Solidified Grease Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Clarified Oil x1 → Solidified Grease Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Proto-Oil x1 → Solidified Grease Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Pie Case x1 + Nourishing Slime x1 → Solidified Grease Pie x1 (*"Assemble Pie"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)

## Recipes

**Solidified Grease Pie** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Solidified Grease Pie gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 37%
- Catch Size Improvement: 8%

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

Jetpack Tanks capacity is increased by +317% for 10 minutes when Solidified Grease Pie is processed by the Nutrient Ingestor.
