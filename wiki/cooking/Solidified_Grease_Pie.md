# Solidified Grease Pie

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 54,000
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Solidified Grease Pie** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Solidified Grease Pie** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: manifold, chromatic, aromatic.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 100%

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Regis Grease]] → 1 × Solidified Grease Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Clarified Oil]] → 1 × Solidified Grease Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Proto-Oil]] → 1 × Solidified Grease Pie
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Assemble Pie* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Pie Case]] + 1 × [[Nourishing Slime]] → 1 × Solidified Grease Pie

## Additional Information

Exosuit nutrient system will power 15 seconds sprint overload when consumed.

Jetpack Tanks capacity is increased by +317% for 10 minutes when Solidified Grease Pie is processed by the [[Nutrient Ingestor]].
