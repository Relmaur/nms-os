# Popping Stew

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 6,400
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Popping Stew** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Popping Stew** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game description

A highly refined food product, created in the Nutrient Processor. It has both the identifying shapes and aromas of an actual food item, in stark contrast with conventional nutrition paste or pellets.

Food such as this can be highly valuable.

Flavour profile: earthy, fresh, juicy.

Recipe Complexity: 66%

## Recipes

**Popping Stew** is not used as an ingredient for cooking using a Nutrient Processor.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Popping Stew gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 10%
- Catch Size Improvement: 6%
- Other notes: Attracts storm-loving fish

## Additional Information

Restores 20% Life Support power once consumed.
