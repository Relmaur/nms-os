# Flavoursome Sauce

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Value:** 7,200
- **Added in:** WorldsTwo

**Flavoursome Sauce** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Flavoursome Sauce** is a [[consumable]] and one of the [[Edible Product|Edible Products]].

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: crisp, zesty, floral.
<br>Recipe Complexity: 69%

## Nutrient Ingestor

- Restores 20% [[life support]] power once consumed.

## Source

- **Nutrient Processor** · *Combine and Reduce* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Root Juice]] + 1 × [[Steamed Vegetables]] → 1 × Flavoursome Sauce
- **Nutrient Processor** · *Liquidise* · 2.5 s per unit: 1 × [[Steamed Vegetables]] + 1 × [[Steamed Vegetables]] → 1 × Flavoursome Sauce

## Additional Information

- When giving 100 Flavoursome Sauce to [[Iteration: Cronus]], they will give an average of 53.86  Nanites per Flavoursome Sauce.
