# Flavoursome Sauce

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Consumable
- **Type:** Edible Product
- **Total Value:** 7,200
- **Updated:** WorldsTwo

**Flavoursome Sauce** is a consumable.

## Summary

**Flavoursome Sauce** is a consumable and one of the Edible Products.

## Game Description

A consumable liquid, created in the Nutrient Processor. Prized across the galaxy for its pleasing mouth-feel and authentically natural flavours.

Can be ingested as it stands or subjected to further processing.

Flavour profile: crisp, zesty, floral.

Recipe Complexity: 69%

## Nutrient Ingestor

- Restores 20% life support power once consumed.

## Fishing Bait

When applied as bait, Flavoursome Sauce gives the following bonus towards your next catch:

- Catch Rarity Improvement: 11%
- Catch Size Improvement: 29%

## Source

Flavoursome Sauce can be cooked using a Nutrient Processor with the following ingredients:

- Root Juice x1 + Steamed Vegetables x1 → Flavoursome Sauce x1 (*"Combine and Reduce"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
- Steamed Vegetables x1 + Steamed Vegetables x1 → Flavoursome Sauce x1 (*"Liquidise"*, 2.5 sec./unit output)
