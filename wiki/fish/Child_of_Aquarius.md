# Child of Aquarius

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fish
- **Type:** Precious Spawn
- **Value:** 500
- **Added in:** Xeno Arena

**Child of Aquarius** is a precious spawn.

## Summary

**Child of Aquarius** is a [[fish]] that can be caught as an item by the player.

## Game Description

An impossibly delicate jellyfish. Its tentacles are as fine as strands of hair, its entire being glassy, dancing on the edge of what is possible.

A gift from the sea, almost too precious to keep...

## Use

Can be used to summon the [[Deepwater Guardian]].
