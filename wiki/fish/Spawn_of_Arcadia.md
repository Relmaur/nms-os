# Spawn of Arcadia

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fish
- **Type:** Fragile Medusoid
- **Value:** 500
- **Added in:** Xeno Arena

**Spawn of Arcadia** is a fish.

## Summary

**Spawn of Arcadia** is a [[fish]] that can be caught as an item by the player.

## Game Description

A glassy jellyfish, lights pulsing beneath its skin. Its thin tentacles pluck at the air, their vibrations manipulating the space around its body.

As it writhes, a faint music drifts from its tendrils, worming its way into your ears. The melody seems to never end, worming its way into your ears, never ending, worming its way into your ears, never ending, never...

## Use

Can be used to summon the [[Deepwater Guardian]]

## Source

Can be acquired via [[fishing]] in deep bodies of water.

## Additional Information

- As of [[Update 6.30]] this item can no longer be obtained as it was part of [[Expedition 20: Breach]]. Rather, its non-expedition variant [[Child of Aquarius]] can still be caught and used to spawn the [[Deepwater Guardian]].
