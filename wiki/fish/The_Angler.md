# The Angler

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fish
- **Type:** Legendary Fish
- **Value:** 1,600
- **Used for:** Cooking
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**The Angler** is a legendary fish.

## Summary

**The Angler** is a [[fish]] that can be caught as an item, by the player.

## Game Description

Fire dances across the scales of this impossible marine being. Its starlit eyes stare back at you with a meaning far deeper than any fish should know. As they stare, the world around you fades away.

The sound of water fills your ears. It wants to be free. You want to be free...

## Use

Can be used as [[bait]], used for [[cooking]], sold, or released into [[Biome Subtype - Underwater|water]].

Needed to fully complete the [[Expedition 15: Aquarius|Aquarius Expedition]].

## Source

Obtained via [[fishing]].

## Fishing Conditions

Can be caught during the questline of the Aquarius Expedition.

## Additional Information

Caught as a part of the Aquarius Expedition. It doesn't seem to be possible to catch this fish in any other way.
- The Angler can be caught in an automated trap. Check your traps if you are not catching it with your fishing rig.

It is not a very effective bait choice, especially considering how rare it is.

It seems to be unique in not having a weight description, or catalogue entry.
