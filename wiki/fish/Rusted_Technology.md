# Rusted Technology

## Quick Facts

- **Category:** Fish
- **Type:** Collected Flotsam
- **Added in:** Aquarius

**Rusted Technology** is a type of fish.

## Summary

**Rusted Technology**  is one of the [[Collected Flotsam]] that can be caught by the player when fishing.

## Game Description

A technology upgrade that has been permanently damaged by prolonged exposure to water.

Use  to begin upgrade installation process.

## Use

- Will deploy a "permanently damaged" !-class [[Upgrade Module]] to the inventory when dismantled. Effectively technology at a tier lower than C-class.

## Source

Obtained via [[fishing]].

## Additional Information

- Has a value of 10 nanites Nanites rather than units Units.
