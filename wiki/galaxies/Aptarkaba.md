# Aptarkaba

**Aptarkaba** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- [[Pathfinder]]: this galaxy was accessible only by reaching the centre of the previous eleven galaxies

**Aptarkaba** appears to have fewer lush planets and a higher proportion of Desolate/Forsaken [[biome|biomes]] than a regular galaxy. As a result, exotic [[planet|planets]] may be more common here.
