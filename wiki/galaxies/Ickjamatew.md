- **Type:** Norm

**Ickjamatew** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- **Simulation reset** - Players in Hyades galaxy, who choose to reset the simulation into a [[Norm galaxy]] during the [[New Beginnings]] primary mission, will start again in this galaxy.
