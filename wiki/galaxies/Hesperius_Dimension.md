# Hesperius Dimension

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Norm
- **Galaxy number:** 4
- **Previous:** Calypso
- **Next:** Hyades
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Hesperius Dimension** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- **Simulation reset** - Players in [[Hilbert Dimension]] and Calypso galaxies, who choose to reset the simulation into a [[Norm galaxy]] during the [[New Beginnings]] primary mission, will start again in this galaxy.

## Known Regions

- The [[AGT Hesperius Embassy]] has been established in the Voyagers era as a navigation way-point and outpost by the [[Alliance of Galactic Travellers]]
- The original [[AGT Hesperius Embassy (Yuprineskips Adjunct)]] was established in the [[Pathfinder]] era and is currently declared by the AGT as lost/missing.
- [[GenBra Space Corp]] has a small base at the core.
- [[InExCor]] has outposts in [[InExCor Hesperius Dimension Discoveries|several regions]]
- [[Oxalis]] has an outpost in the **Owicarita Adjunct** region.
- [[Multiverse Expeditionary Corps]] has a base at the core.
- The [[Tugarv Compendium]] established a Galaxy Jump Point Outpost in the **Ogswellia** region.
-

## Additional Information

This galaxy has a procedurally generated name of Jagaimyarba, which is overridden. This name was exposed in the Teleportation Terminal by a bug in [[Update 3.03|Origins 3.03]], then fixed in [[Update 3.05|Origins 3.05]].
