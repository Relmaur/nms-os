# Odyalutai

## Quick Facts

- **Galaxy number:** 256
- **Previous:** N/A
- **Next:** Hilbert Dimension
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Odyalutai** is a galaxy.

## Summary

**Odyalutai** is the 256th [[galaxy]] in the [[No Man's Sky]] [[universe]].
Odyalutai cannot be reached through normal gameplay since the [[Atlas Rises]] update, the only ways to reach this galaxy are if you join a player who is already there (via Multiplayer), visit a player base on **Odyalutai** through the [[Nexus]], or using a save editor. It is confirmed that the next galaxy is the [[Hilbert Dimension]].

The galactic core of Odyalutai appears to be Magenta in color.

## Known Regions

- [[GenBra Space Corp]] set up vacation, exploration, and research bases at the core of this galaxy, directed by [[GenBra Defense Systems]].
