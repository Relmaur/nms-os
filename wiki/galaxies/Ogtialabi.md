# Ogtialabi

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Harsh
- **Galaxy number:** 15
- **Previous:** Odiwagiri
- **Next:** Muhacksonto
- **Added in:** Voyagers

**Ogtialabi** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- [[Atlas Rises]] - players in the [[Calypso]] galaxy who choose to reboot the Universe into a [[Harsh galaxy]] at the end of the [[storyline]] will start again in this galaxy.

This galaxy appears to have a larger number of extreme hazard and extreme sentinel [[planet|planets]] than other galaxies.

## Known Regions

- [[InExCor]] has outpost in [[InExCor Ogtialabi Discoveries|several regions]].
- [[Resoom'em t'Yedal'em-Negud]] is headquartered in, and has extensively colonized, the [[Xiones Band]], a region along the Ogtialabian [[The Fade|Fade]].
- The [[Alliance of Galactic Travellers]] is colonizing a region in this galaxy as of the [[Voyagers]] era.
- The [[Tugarv Compendium]] has established a Galaxy Jump Point Outpost in the **Kuberelawd** region.
