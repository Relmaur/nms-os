# Hilbert Dimension

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Norm
- **Galaxy number:** 2
- **Previous:** Euclid
- **Next:** Calypso
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Hilbert Dimension** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- **Simulation reset** - Players in [[Iousongola]] and Euclid galaxies, who choose to reset the simulation into a [[Norm galaxy]] during the [[New Beginnings]] primary mission, will start again in this galaxy.

## Civilized space

- The [[AGT Hilbert Embassy]] has been established as a navigation waypoint and outpost by the [[Alliance of Galactic Travellers]] along with four [[AGT Cardinal Outposts]] and an [[AGT Operations Center]] located just outside the galaxy core. The AGT have also colonised the [[Sea of Ajukuna]] and have their largest civilization outside Euclid in the [[Louyen]] region of space, approximately 3,800 light-years outside the galactic core.
- It is rumored that the [[Galactic Network of Systems]] has a presence here, though yet to be confirmed by the GNS themselves.
- [[GenBra Space Corp]] maintains a small base at the core.
- [[InExCor]] has outposts in [[InExCor Hilbert Dimension Discoveries|several regions]].
- [[Oxalis]] has an outpost in the **Aijaoi Instability** region.
- The **Galactic Federation of Space** has a base here in the **Lumbava Nebula**.
- [[No Man's High Hub]]'s first capital was in the [[Ningugu]] region. Since the [[Update 3.00|Origins]] update, the capital was moved to [[Omins XI]] in [[Lahanhar Conflux]] region of [[Euclid]].
- The [[Tugarv Compendium]] established a Galaxy Jump Point Outpost in the **Edquaayba Cluster** region.
-

## Additional information

This galaxy has a procedurally generated name of Goquaornsax, which is overridden. This name was exposed in the Teleportation Terminal by a bug in [[Update 3.03|Origins 3.03]], then fixed in [[Update 3.05|Origins 3.05]].
