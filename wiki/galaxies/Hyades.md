# Hyades

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Norm
- **Galaxy number:** 5
- **Previous:** Hesperius Dimension
- **Next:** Ickjamatew
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Hyades** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- **Simulation reset** - Players in Hesperius Dimension galaxy, who choose to reset the simulation into a [[Norm galaxy]] during the [[New Beginnings]] primary mission, will start again in this galaxy.

## Known Regions

- The [[Hyades Travellers Foundation]] is highly active in several galactic core regions with multiple bases in some densely populated HTF systems.
- [[GenBra Space Corp]] has a small base at the core.
- [[InExCor]] has an outpost in the **Behrei** region.
- [[Maya Technology Corporation]] has established a branch office in the **Inleia Expanse** region.
- [[Oxalis]] has an outpost in the **Bujunete Boundary** region.
- [[The Qitanian Empire]] has established a Navigation outpost and a Waypoint.
- [[Multiverse Expeditionary Corps]] has a base at the core.
- The [[AGT Hyades Embassy]] was established as a navigation waypoint and outpost by the [[Alliance of Galactic Travellers]] in the [[Pathfinder]] era but is now deemed as lost by the AGT SLT as coordinates cannot be confirmed.
- The [[Tugarv Compendium]] established a Galaxy Jump Point Outpost in the **Tahuitts** region.
-

## Additional Information

This galaxy is commonly referred to as 'Hyades', but it does have alternate names.

- **Galaxy map** - 'Hyades Galaxy' as opposed to most other galaxies which don't have this term attached.
- This galaxy has a procedurally generated name of Yombesagru, which is overridden. This name was exposed in the Teleportation Terminal by a bug in [[Update 3.03|Origins 3.03]], then fixed in [[Update 3.05|Origins 3.05]].
- The glitched center system is [[Evskyv]]
