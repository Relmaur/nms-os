# Elder Galaxy

## Quick Facts

- **Added in:** Atlas Rises

The **Elder Galaxy** was one of the galaxies of the **First Universe**. The Galaxy was destroyed by the [[Atlas]] when it enacted the [[Sentinel]] purge across the universe. Only the [[Anunaki]] race and their planet [[Anuk Valyros]] survived with Priest Entity [[Nada]].
