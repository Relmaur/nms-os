# Calypso

## Quick Facts

- **Type:** Harsh
- **Galaxy number:** 3
- **Previous:** Hilbert Dimension
- **Next:** Hesperius Dimension
- **Added in:** Swarm

**Calypso** is a galaxy.

## Summary

- **Simulation reset** - Players in [[Iousongola]] galaxy and in all the subsequent galaxies up to Hilbert Dimension, who choose to reset the simulation into a [[Harsh galaxy]] during the [[New Beginnings]] primary mission, will start again in this galaxy.

## Specialty Stars

## Civilized Space

- [[GenBra Space Corp]] has several bases near the core, in collaboration with *MrBlack87* (YHUB)
- [[InExCor]] has outposts in [[InExCor Calypso Discoveries|several regions]].
- [[Oxalis]] has an outpost in the **Gologarf Nebula** region.
- [[Multiverse Expeditionary Corps]] has a base at the core.
- The [[Tugarv Compendium]] established a Galaxy Jump Point Outpost in the **Riviola** region.
-

## Additional Information

This galaxy is commonly referred to as 'Calypso', but it does have alternate names.
- **Galaxy map** - 'Calypso Galaxy' as opposed to most other galaxies which don't have this term attached.
- The 'harsh' label of Calypso has garnered it a reputation as an 'Outlaw's Galaxy.'
- This galaxy has a procedurally generated name of Luhuangatis, which is overridden. This name was exposed in the Teleportation Terminal by a bug in [[Update 3.03|Origins 3.03]], then fixed in [[Update 3.05|Origins 3.05]].
