## Summary

**Update 7.03.1** is the 294th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on September 16, 2026 on PC, Mac and PS5.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Cosmos, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 7.03.1, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that caused a white screen hang.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,

Hello Games
