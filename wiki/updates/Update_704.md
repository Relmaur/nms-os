# Update 7.04

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 21 September, 2026
- **Platforms:** PC, Mac
- **Added in:** Cosmos
- **Previous:** 7.03.1
- **Next:** 7.05

**Update 7.04** is the 295th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 7.04** is the 295th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on September 21, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Cosmos, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 7.04, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause the first Atlas Path mission to repeatedly select itself.
- Fixed an issue that caused untranslated text IDs to appear in a conversation with Iterations Gemini and Hesperus.
- Fixed a minor text issue during fishing missions if the Fishing Rig is not yet known.
- Resolved a number of issues with collection and completion with deep-space salvage missions that could occur when having multiple of the same mission type active.
- Fixed an issue that could prevent In Stellar Multitudes from recognising the installation of the Atlantid Drive if playing with Crafting set to Free in difficulty settings.
- Fixed an issue that could cause mission markers on the Space Anomaly to fail to load if the game was saved on the Anomaly itself.
- Fixed an issue that caused platform-specific text to be missing from the developer notes gathered from the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Fixed an issue that caused the new reward ships claimed during the Our Journey Continues expedition to charge nanites.
- Fixed a text issue in the Worlds II milestone of the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Improved the visual effects of the vintage Sentinel Interceptor ship awarded by the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Fixed an issue that prevented legacy space stations from appearing on the Star System Map during the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Fixed an issue in the Voyagers milestone of the Our Journey Continues expedition that prevented the location of a Corvette Workshop from being indicated with a marker.
- Fixed an issue that could cause Nexus missions related to creature taming and fishing to be offered to the player during the Beyond milestone in the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Fixed an issue that allowed Corvettes to teleport to and get stuck in legacy space stations during the Our Journey Continues expedition.
- Fixed a text issue that caused the Weekend Missions milestone of the Our Journey Continues expedition to suggest looking for Knowledge Stones in the Analysis Visor on planets with no Knowledge Stones.
- Fixed an issue that could block players on the Our Journey Continues expedition if they traded away their Multi-Tool before reaching the Beyond milestone. The Terrain Manipulator blueprint is now known by default.

- Fixed an issue that limited the number of space stations which could be claimed by one player.
- Improved corvette spawn location when teleporting to a space base.
- Fixed an issue that could cause the hint message about refunded corvette modules to persist after it was no longer relevant.
- Improved the collision for external Space Station decorations, fixing an issue that could cause ships to bump into invisible collision on very large parts.
- Fixed an issue that caused corvette landing docks to appear on civilian frigates.
- Fixed an issue which caused incorrect input processing on some VR controllers.
- Fixed an issue that caused some points of interest in deep space to have a disproportionately huge explosion when destroyed.
- Fixed an issue that caused Oceanus' creature training battles to open the freighter inventory transfer page.
- Fixed an issue that could cause frigates around deep-space points of interest to spawn either too far away or intersecting with an object.
- Fixed an issue that caused incorrect scaling of UI elements in the Analysis Visor when playing with HUD disabled.
