# Update 4.12.1

- **Platforms:** PC

**Update 4.12.1** is the 165th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 4.12.1** is the 165th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 3 March, 2023 on PC.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Fractal Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.12.1, which is live on Steam.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug fixes

- Fixed a number of network-related crashes.
- Fixed an issue that could cause controllers to disconnect when playing via Steam.
