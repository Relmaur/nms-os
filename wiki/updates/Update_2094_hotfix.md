# Update 2.09.4 hotfix

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 4 September 2019
- **Platforms:** PC
- **Added in:** Beyond
- **Previous:** 2.09.4
- **Next:** 2.11

**Update 2.09.4 hotfix** is the 62nd official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 2.09.4 hotfix** is the 62nd official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]] and was released on 4 September 2019 to PC.

It addresses mod incompatibilities. The previous update is [[Update 2.09.4]].

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you again to everyone playing Beyond, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk.

We have become aware of a number of mod incompatibilities causing a crash on startup with release 2.09.4. As a result, we have pushed a hotfix that will disable mods by default.

Mods are not officially supported and we cannot guarantee they will not cause instability or damage saves. However, if your mods have been updated and are compatible with the new release, they can be re-enabled by following the steps listed here:

[How should I install Steam mods?](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001916589-How-should-I-install-Steam-mods-)

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,<br>
Hello Games
