# Update 7.06

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 5 October, 2026
- **Platforms:** PC, Mac
- **Added in:** Cosmos
- **Previous:** 7.05

**Update 7.06** is the 297th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 7.06** is the 297th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on October 5, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Cosmos, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 7.06, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which caused the camera facing to change when rolling while space walking.
- Fixed an issue affecting Gravitino Coil targeting when collecting objects from hulks.
- Fixed a rare issue affecting players who transferred a corvette from their expedition save to their main save and then immediately redeemed an expedition reward ship, which resulted in many copies of a broken Radiant Pillar appearing in their ship collection. These broken ships will be automatically cleaned up in any affected saves.
- Fixed a crash on PC which occurred on some Nvidia GPUs.
- Fixed a rare crash on PS5 that could occur on a loading screen.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
