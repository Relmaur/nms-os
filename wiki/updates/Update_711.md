## Summary

**Update 7.11** is the 299th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on October 29, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Forsaken, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 7.11, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a crash that could occur when using the teleporter in the Anomaly.
- Fixed a crash that could occur while warping to another system.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,

Hello Games
