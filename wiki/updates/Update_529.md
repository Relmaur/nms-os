**Update 5.29** is the 232nd official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 5.29** is the 232nd official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 11 December, 2024 on PC.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Holiday 2024 Expeditions, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 5.29, which is now live on Steam and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which caused ship inventories to become merged.
- Wind vertex optimisations.
- Fixed a rare crash on PS4.
- Fixed an issue that could cause multiplayer connections to fail on PS4.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when running the PS4 version on PS5.
- Fixed an issue which caused the application to lose focus on Mac which resulted in poor performance.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
