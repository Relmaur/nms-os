# Update 2.06E

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 19 August 2019
- **Platforms:** PC
- **Added in:** Beyond
- **Previous:** 2.06D
- **Next:** 2.06F

**Update 2.06E** is the 51st official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 2.06E** is the 51st official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]] and was released on 19 August 2019 to PC.

It addresses bugfixes. The previous update is [[Update 2.06D]].

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you again to everyone playing Beyond, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of stability issues. Fixes for these were released on Steam experimental earlier today, and this patch, 2.06e, is now live on PC. We’ll be rolling out these fixes to other platforms as soon as possible.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed a networking issue that could cause bad data to be synced between ambient players.
- Fixed a compatibility issue with a specific revision of nVidia drivers.
We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,<br>
Hello Games
