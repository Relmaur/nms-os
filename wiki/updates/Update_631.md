**Update 6.31** is the 278th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.31** is the 278th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on April 9, 2026 on PC.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Xeno Arena, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.31, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Network optimisation.
- Fixed a crash that could be caused when moving tech while the charge popup is active.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
