# Update 3.93

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 17 June 2022
- **Platforms:** All platforms
- **Added in:** Outlaws
- **Previous:** 3.92

**Update 3.93** is the 147th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 3.93** is the 147th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 17 June, 2022 on all platforms.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Leviathan Expedition, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 3.93, which will be live on all platforms soon.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause custom discovery names to be reset on load.
- Fixed a number of issues that could allow players to take damage in protected locations.
- Fixed a multiplayer issue that could cause players to incorrectly take damage that ought have been applied to a different player.

## Additional information

- The second bug listed here, which is related to taking damage in protected locations, was fixed due to there being a very nasty exploit where players could cheat an exocraft into the space anomaly and shoot people in Permadeath to build their killing sprees, wiping their hard-earned saves in the process. This has been one of the most insidious bugs to have been patched in No Man's Sky.
