# Update 2.61.1

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 24 July 2020
- **Platforms:** All
- **Added in:** Desolation
- **Previous:** 2.61
- **Next:** 2.62

**Update 2.61.1** is the 94th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 2.61.1** is the 94th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 24 July to all platforms.

It improves performance on integrated Intel GPUs. The previous update is [[Update 2.61]].

## Actual text

**DESOLATION PATCH 2.61.1**

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Desolation Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a performance issue for PCs with integrated GPUs.

## Patch Notes

- Improved performance on integrated Intel GPUs by disabling anamorphic lensflare.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
