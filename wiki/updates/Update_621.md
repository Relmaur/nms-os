# Update 6.21

- **Platforms:** PC, Mac, PS

**Update 6.21** is the 272nd official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.21** is the 272nd official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on February 12, 2026 on PC and Mac, and on February 13, 2026 on PS.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Remnant, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.21, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which caused Corvette collision to be incorrect when landed on planets.
- Fixed an issue which caused some PCVR setups to have lower than intended graphical quality.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
