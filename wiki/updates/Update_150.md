# Update 1.50

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 24 July 2018
- **Platforms:** PS4/PC/Xbox
- **Added in:** NEXT
- **Previous:** 1.38
- **Next:** 1.51

**Update 1.50** is the 23rd update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 1.5**, also known as No Man's Sky [[NEXT]], is the 23rd [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]], released on 24 July, 2018 to Playstation 4, Xbox One, Wegame, and PC. It expanded [[multiplayer]] features and player interactions.

### Hidden Changes

- The blueprints of [[Freighter Warp Reactor Sigma]], [[Freighter Warp Reactor Tau]] and [[Freighter Warp Reactor Theta]] are no longer available for purchase.
- [[Crashed Freighter]] requires a [[Damaged Component]] to be repaired, formerly various resources and products were required. Multiple models added for the crashed freighter, previously there was only one.
- The **temperament** renamed to **behaviour** on the information panel of the [[creature|creatures]].

## New Features

From Hello Games: "You’ll be able to explore the universe with your friends, or bump into random travelers. You can help friends to stay alive, or prey on others to survive. Tiny shelters or complex colonies that you [[Base building|build]] as a team are shared for all players. Fight as a [[pirate]] or a wingman in epic [[space battle|space battles]] with friends and enemies. Race [[exocraft]] across weird alien terrains, creating [[racetrack|racetracks]] and trails to share online." ([[NEXT Update|Full text]])

Sean Murray has compared the new multiplayer experience to [Battlestar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battlestar_Galactica) and [Star Trek Away Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Trek:_Away_Team).

## Xbox Booster Pack

A [[Xbox Booster Pack|booster pack]] specific to the Xbox release is available for pre-orders. It contains a special [[exo-suit]] with the X-visor, a unique [[multi-tool]], and 10,000  Units. This is similar to the pre-game package available when No Man's Sky was first released.
