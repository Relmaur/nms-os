# Update 4.61

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 27 March, 2024
- **Platforms:** PC, Mac, PS
- **Added in:** Orbital
- **Previous:** 4.60
- **Next:** 4.62

**Update 4.61** is the 205th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 4.61** is the 205th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 27 March, 2024 on PC, Mac and PS.

## Actual Text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the No Man’s Sky Orbital update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.61, which is now live on Steam and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug Fixes

- Fixed a rare crash relating to very large bases.
- Fixed an issue that could cause missing text when playing in US English.
