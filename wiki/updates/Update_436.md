# Update 4.36

## Quick Facts

- **Platforms:** PC

**Update 4.36** is the 184th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 4.36** is the 184th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 19 June, 2023 on PC and on 10 July, 2023 on Nintendo Switch.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the No Man’s Sky Singularity Expedition, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.36, which is now live.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug fixes

- Fixed a crash that could occur while aboard The Anomaly.
