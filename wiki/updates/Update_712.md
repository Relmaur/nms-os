# Update 7.12

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 30 October, 2026
- **Platforms:** PS5
- **Added in:** Forsaken
- **Previous:** 7.11

**Update 7.12** is the 300th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 7.12** is the 300th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on October 30, 2026 on PS5.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Forsaken, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 7.12, which is now live on PS5, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a crash that could occur when using the teleporter in the Anomaly.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,

Hello Games
