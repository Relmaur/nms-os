# Update 6.01

- **Platforms:** PC, Mac, PS, XB

**Update 6.01** is the 255th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.01** is the 255th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on August 28, 2025 on PC, Mac and PS5 and on August 29, 2025 on XB and PS4.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Voyagers, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.01, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause your Corvette ship to appear at the wrong scale.
- Fixed an issue that could cause some difficulty when trying to interact with your Corvette.
- Fixed an issue that could cause a Corvette ship to land on a regular landing pad in the Space Anomaly.
- Fixed Looping Grass not appearing when built on a base.
- Fixed an issue that caused a black screen to appear when playing with HDR enabled on Xbox consoles.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when in the Anomaly.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when warping aboard a Corvette in multiplayer.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,<br>
