## Summary

**Update (2019.01.09)** is an unofficial [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]] and was released on 9 January 2019 to PS4, PC, and Xbox.

It addresses the [[Community Research]] progress. The previous update is [[Update 1.77]].

### Hidden Changes

- The duration of the [[Community Research (mission 9)]] is extended by 14 days.

## Additional Information

It was probably done to let the players unlock the tier 3 reward, as the mission would have ended a day later.
