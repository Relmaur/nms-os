## Summary

**Update 6.17.2** is the 269th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on November 20, 2025 on PC.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Holiday Expeditions 2025, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.17.2, which is now live on Steam.

## Bug Fixes

- Memory optimisations.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
