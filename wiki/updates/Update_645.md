## Summary

**Update 6.45** is the 288th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on June 16, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – The Swarm, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.45, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a number of issues with the Hive of Glass milestone.
- Fixed an issue which caused players to lose resources when editing player bases they did not own themselves.
- Fixed an issue which could cause ship trails to flicker.
- Fixed an issue with clouds on PSVR2.
- Fixed a hang that occurred when accessing photo mode on Switch 1.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
