**Update 6.41** is the 284th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.41** is the 284th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on May 28, 2026 on PC, PS and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – The Swarm, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.41, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause player characters to have incorrect colour customisation during The Swarm expedition.
- Fixed a crash that could occur when joining a multiplayer session.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
