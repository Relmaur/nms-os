# Update 2.06C

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 16 August 2019
- **Platforms:** PC
- **Added in:** Beyond
- **Previous:** 2.06B
- **Next:** 2.06D

**Update 2.06C** is the 49th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 2.06C** is the 49th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]] and was released on 16 August 2019 to PC.

It addresses bugfixes. The previous update is [[Update 2.06B]].

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you again to everyone playing Beyond, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of stability issues. Fixes for these were released on Steam experimental earlier today, and this patch, 2.06c, is now live on PC. We’ll be rolling out these fixes to other platforms as soon as possible.

### Bug fixes

- Reduce texture quality if it is causing an out of memory error.
- Don’t let the VR player punch in places they aren’t allow to use weapons.
- Revert to the non-device-local heap in low memory situations.
- Removed misleading version number from main menu.
We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,<br>
Hello Games
