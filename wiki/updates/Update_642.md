## Summary

**Update 6.42** is the 285th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on May 28, 2026 on PC and Mac, on May 29, 2026 on PS and on June 01, 2026 on XB.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – The Swarm, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.42, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which caused gamertags to be displayed incorrectly.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
