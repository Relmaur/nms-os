# Update 6.05.2

- **Platforms:** PC, Mac

**Update 6.05.2** is the 261th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.05.2** is the 261st official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on September 22, 2025 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Voyagers, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.05.2, which is now live on Steam.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a Steam input issue.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games
