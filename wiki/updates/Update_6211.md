## Summary

**Update 6.21.1** is the 273rd official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on February 13, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Remnant, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.21.1, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause invisible collision in Atlas Stations.
- Fixed an issue that caused networked player markers to always show their distance label, even when not aiming the reticle at the marker.
- Fixed an issue that caused networked player markers to be missing their "Busy" label when the player was unavailable for interaction.
- Fixed an issue that caused networked player markers to be missing their platform icon.
- Fixed a timing-specific crash that could occur when destroying obstacles with the Colossus Excavation Blade.
- Fixed a memory-related crash on Nintendo Switch 1 and 2.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,
Hello Games
