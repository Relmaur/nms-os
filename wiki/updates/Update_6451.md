**Update 6.45.1** is the 289th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.45.1** is the 289th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on June 22, 2026 on PC and Mac.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – The Swarm, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.45.1, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue which could cause freighters to disappear under some circumstances.
- Fixed an issue that prevented freighter captains from calling the player after completing a freighter battle.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
