# Update 4.14

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 10 March, 2023
- **Platforms:** All
- **Added in:** Fractal
- **Previous:** 4.13
- **Next:** 4.15

**Update 4.14** is the 167th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 4.14** is the 167th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 10 March, 2023 on PC, on 14 March, 2023 on PS and XB, and on 17 March, 2023 on Nintendo Switch.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Fractal Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.14, which is live on Steam.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue that could cause some players to lose discoveries.

## Additional Information

The patch notes changed on 14 March, 2023, after the patch was released for PS and XB. The Steam version was re-released as well.

The following point was removed from the patch notes:
- Fixed a crash related to rendering.

The [release log](https://www.nomanssky.com/release-log/) lists Switch as having received the 4.14 update, but the Switch version number is still 4.1.3.
