## Summary

**Update 4.20.1** is the 170th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 5 April, 2023 on PC.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Interceptor Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.20.1, which is now live on Steam.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games

### Bug fixes

- Fixed a crash on Steam Deck.
