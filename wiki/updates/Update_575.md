**Update 5.75** is the 253th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 5.75** is the 253th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on July 23, 2025 on PC, Mac, PS and XB and on July 25, 2025 on NS.

## Actual Text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Beacon, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 5.75, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed a visual issue with the tail of the Wraith ship.
- Fixed an issue which caused Alloy base parts to appear in the wrong colour.
- Fixed a crash on PS5 when using the Terrain Manipulator.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,<br>
Hello Games
