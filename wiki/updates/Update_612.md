# Update 6.12

- **Platforms:** PC, PS, XB, Mac

**Update 6.12** is the 265th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 6.12** is the 265th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on October 28, 2025 on PC, PS and Mac and on October 30, 2025 on XB.

## Actual text

Hello Everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing No Man’s Sky – Expedition Twenty: Breach, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 6.12, which is now live on Steam, and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

## Bug Fixes

- Fixed an issue that can cause players to warp unexpectedly while in a system with a black hole.
- Enabled constructing the planetary research stations during the Breach expedition.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,
Hello Games
