# Update 4.23

- **Platforms:** PC, PS, XB

**Update 4.23** is the 174th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 4.23** is the 174th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 17 April, 2023 on PC and on 18 April, 2023 on PS and XB.

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Interceptor Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a number of issues. These fixes are included in patch 4.23, which is now live on Steam and will be coming to other platforms as soon as possible.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a [bug report](https://hellogames.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=167605).

Thank you,

Hello Games
