# Update 2.34

## Quick Facts

- **Release date:** 31 March 2020
- **Platforms:** PC
- **Added in:** Living Ship
- **Previous:** 2.33
- **Next:** 2.40

**Update 2.34** is the 79th official update to No Man's Sky.

## Summary

**Update 2.34** is the 79th official [[Patch notes|update]] to [[No Man's Sky]]. It was released on 17 March 2020 to PC.

It fixed a PC issues and crashes. The previous update is [[Update 2.33]].

## Actual text

Hello everyone,

Thank you to everyone playing the Living Ship Update, especially those taking the time to report any issues they encounter via Zendesk or console crash reporting.

We are listening closely to your feedback, and have identified and resolved a stability issue. This patch was released earlier on Steam experimental, is now live on Steam.

We will continue to release patches as issues are identified and resolved. If you experience any issues, let us know by submitting a bug report.

Thank you,

Hello Games

## Patch Notes

- This hotfix addresses a PC issue which began to occur recently, and fixes a crash seen most often in the Space Anomaly.
